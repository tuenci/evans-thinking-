active immunity /ˈæk.tɪv ɪˈmjuː.nə.ti/ 主动免疫 p. 743
Natural acquisition of an illness, resulting in the body developing the antibodies to protect a person from getting it again.
主动免疫：机体自然罹患某种感染性疾病或接种疫苗抗原后，刺激自身免疫系统主动生成特异性抗体与致敏淋巴细胞，从而在日后获得长期或终生免受该病原体再次感染侵害的保护性免疫力。
anatomical position /ˌæn.əˈtɒm.ɪ.kəl pəˈzɪʃ.ən/ 标准解剖学姿势 p. 720
The body standing erect, with the feet shoulder-width apart, face forward, arms at the sides, and palms of the hands facing forward.
标准解剖学姿势：人体直立，两眼平视正前方，双足并拢或与肩同宽向前，双上肢下垂置于躯干两侧，手掌向前（掌心朝前）的基准身体体位。
arteries /ˈɑː.tər.iz/ 动脉 p. 739
Blood vessels that carry blood away from the heart.
动脉：将血液由心室射出、运送运离心脏至全身各组织器官的弹性厚壁血管管腔管道。
arteriole /ɑːˈtɪə.ri.əʊl/ 小动脉/微动脉 p. 739
The smallest branch of an artery.
小动脉/微动脉：动脉系统分支中最细小的血管末梢，直接连接毛细血管网，管壁富含平滑肌，是调节全身外周血管阻力和血压的核心枢纽。
ball-and-socket joints /ˌbɔːl.ənˈsɒk.ɪt dʒɔɪnts/ 球窝关节 p. 727
Joints that allow movement in all directions. They are made up of the rounded end of one bone and the hollow end of another bone. The rounded end of one fits into the hollow end of the other.
球窝关节：允许在所有空间平面和轴向进行多轴自由运动的滑膜关节。由一根骨头圆球形的球状头嵌入另一根骨头的凹形杯状窝中构成（如肩关节与髋关节）。
capillaries /kəˈpɪl.ər.iz/ 毛细血管 p. 739
The smallest form of blood vessels and the site of nutrient, gas, and waste exchange between cells and the bloodstream.
毛细血管：人体中最微细薄壁的微血管，仅由单层内皮细胞构成，是血液与周围组织细胞之间进行营养物质、氧气、二氧化碳与代谢废物弥散交换的唯一生理场所。
cartilage /ˈkɑː.təl.ɪdʒ/ 软骨 p. 725
Connective tissue that cushions the joint so that bone ends do not rub together.
软骨：一种半透明、致密坚韧且富有弹性的特殊结缔组织，被覆于关节骨端表面形成缓冲软垫，防止骨端在运动过程中相互摩擦磨损。
cell /sel/ 细胞 p. 720
The basic functional unit of body structure and function.
细胞：构成人体生命形态解剖结构与代谢生理功能的最基本生命单位。
chemical digestion /ˈkem.ɪ.kəl daɪˈdʒes.tʃən/ 化学性消化 p. 745
The chemical breakdown of food into small nutrient parts that the body can absorb and utilize.
化学性消化：通过消化道分泌的各种水解消化酶的作用，将食物中复杂的生物大分子（淀粉、蛋白质、脂肪）化学水解分解为机体可直接吸收利用的小分子营养素（单糖、氨基酸、脂肪酸）的生化反应过程。
condyloid joint /ˈkɒn.dɪ.lɔɪd dʒɔɪnt/ 髁状关节/椭圆关节 p. 727
A joint formed when the oval articular surface of one bone fits into the oval depression of the second articulating bone, such as at the wrist and the knuckles at the base of the fingers.
髁状关节/椭圆关节：由一根骨头的卵圆形凸面关节头嵌入另一根骨头的卵圆形凹陷关节窝形成的双轴关节，允许屈伸与内收外展运动，例如腕关节及手指根部的掌指关节。
deep /diːp/ 深 p. 721
Away from the surface or further into the body.
深：解剖方位术语，指远离身体表面、位于机体或器官内部较深层次的方位。
digestion /daɪˈdʒes.tʃən/ 消化 p. 744
The process of physically and chemically breaking down food into nutrients that can be absorbed for use by the cells.
消化：在消化道内通过机械物理研磨磨碎与化学酶促水解两种方式，将摄入食物分解为能够被肠道黏膜上皮细胞吸收并供全身细胞利用的营养素的全过程。
gliding joint /ˈɡlaɪ.dɪŋ dʒɔɪnt/ 平面关节/滑动关节 p. 727
A joint between two corresponding flat bones held in place by ligaments that glide past one another in any direction along the plane of the joint—up and down, left and right, or diagonally. Gliding joints are found in the wrist, ankle joints, and the vertebral column.
平面关节/滑动关节：由韧带固定的两个相对应的平坦或微凸凹的骨面构成，两骨面可在关节平面上沿上下、左右或对角线方向相互微小滑动，见于腕骨间关节、跗骨间关节及椎骨关节突关节。
hemoglobin /ˌhiː.məˈɡləʊ.bɪn/ 血红蛋白 p. 737
The substance in red blood cells that carries oxygen and gives blood its colour.
血红蛋白：红细胞内富含铁元素的结合蛋白质，负责在肺部与氧气结合并在全身毛细血管释放氧气供组织利用，并赋予血液特有的红色。
hinge joints /hɪndʒ dʒɔɪnts/ 屈戍关节/铰链关节 p. 727
Joints that allow movement in one direction around a single axis, like the hinge of a door.
屈戍关节/铰链关节：只能围绕单一水平额状轴在一个平面内进行屈曲和伸展单轴运动的滑膜关节，类似于门窗合页，例如肘关节、膝关节及指间关节。
homeostasis /ˌhəʊ.mi.əʊˈsteɪ.sɪs/ 内环境稳态 p. 720
A steady state or stable internal environment maintained when all of the body's organ systems work together properly.
内环境稳态：机体所有器官系统在神经和体液调节下紧密协调运作，从而使细胞外液的理化性质（温度、pH值、渗透压、电解质浓度等）保持动态相对恒定平衡的稳定状态。
hormone /ˈhɔː.məʊn/ 激素 p. 735
A chemical substance produced by the cells of specialized glands and secreted into the bloodstream.
激素：由内分泌腺或特化内分泌细胞合成并直接分泌入血液循环中的高效能生物活性化学信使分子，调节机体新陈代谢、生长发育与应激反应。
immunity /ɪˈmjuː.nə.ti/ 免疫力 p. 742
Protection against a disease or condition.
免疫力：机体免疫系统识别“自我”与“非己”，并特异性抵御、中和或清除病原体（细菌、病毒、毒素）及异常突变细胞的保护性防御生理机能。
inferior /ɪnˈfɪə.ri.ər/ 下 p. 721
Situated below another structure or toward the lower part of the body.
下：解剖方位术语，指位于另一解剖结构下方，或朝向足部/躯干低位的方位。
isotonic solution /ˌaɪ.səˈtɒn.ɪk səˈluː.ʃən/ 等渗溶液 p. 721
The solution of 0.09% NaCl (sodium chloride or salt) and water that is equal in concentration to intracellular and extracellular fluid.
等渗溶液：渗透压与正常人体细胞内液及细胞外液完全相等相等的溶液（教材特指 0.9% 生理盐水氯化钠溶液），红细胞置于其中既不膨胀破裂也不皱缩。
mastication /ˌmæs.tɪˈkeɪ.ʃən/ 咀嚼 p. 745
The first step of digestion in which food is bitten, chewed, and crushed by the teeth.
咀嚼：消化的起始物理步骤，通过上下颌牙齿在咬肌收缩下的咬合、切割、研磨将大块固体食物破碎成易于吞咽的食团。
mechanical digestion /mɪˈkæn.ɪ.kəl daɪˈdʒes.tʃən/ 机械性消化 p. 744
Physically breaking down large food particles into smaller ones.
机械性消化：通过牙齿咀嚼、胃壁平滑肌研磨搅拌以及小肠分节运动，将食物物理粉碎为微小碎块并与消化液充分混匀的过程。
menstruation /ˌmen.struˈeɪ.ʃən/ 月经 p. 749
The process in which the lining of the uterus breaks up and is discharged from the body through the vagina.
月经：在雌孕激素周期性撤退作用下，子宫内膜功能层坏死脱落并伴随血管破裂出血，经阴道排出体外的规律性周期生理现象。
metabolism /məˈtæb.əl.ɪ.zəm/ 新陈代谢 p. 721
The chemical processes carried out by the cells of the body to produce energy and utilize food.
新陈代谢：机体细胞内发生的一切化学反应的总称，包括合成代谢（利用营养物质构建细胞组织）与分解代谢（氧化分解有机物释放生物能量 ATP）。
midline /ˈmɪd.laɪn/ 正中线 p. 720
An imaginary line that divides the body into right and left halves.
正中线：贯穿人体前后、将身体纵向平分为左右完全对称两半的假想中轴垂线。
nonspecific immunity /ˌnɒn.spəˈsɪf.ɪk ɪˈmjuː.nə.ti/ 非特异性免疫/先天性免疫 p. 742
The body’s reaction to anything it does not recognize as a normal body substance.
非特异性免疫/先天性免疫：机体先天固有、针对一切外来异物或病原微生物均能迅速发挥作用的非特异性防御屏障（包括完整皮肤黏膜、吞噬细胞、补体系统及炎症反应）。
organs /ˈɔː.ɡənz/ 器官 p. 722
Structures made of different tissues that are arranged to perform a specialized function.
器官：由几种不同类型的组织按照特定构筑模式有机组合而成、能够独立行使特定生理功能的解剖结构（如心脏、肺、胃、肾脏）。
organ systems /ˈɔː.ɡən ˈsɪs.təmz/ 器官系统 p. 722
Organs that work together to perform special functions.
器官系统：在结构和生理功能上密切相关的一系列器官联合构成的综合系统（如消化系统、心血管系统、呼吸系统），共同完成复杂的机体生命活动。
passive immunity /ˈpæs.ɪv ɪˈmjuː.nə.ti/ 被动免疫 p. 743
A short-lived immunity passed from mother to fetus through the placenta and from mother to infant through breast milk, or from receiving antibodies produced by another person or animal.
被动免疫：机体被动接受外源性现成抗体（如母体 IgG 经胎盘传递给胎儿、初乳 IgA 喂养婴儿、或临床注射免疫球蛋白/抗毒素血清）而暂时获得的即刻免疫保护，其体内不产生记忆细胞，保护时效相对短暂。
peripheral /pəˈrɪf.ər.əl/ 外周/周围 p. 721
Away from the centre of the body.
外周/周围：解剖学方位术语，指远离身体中轴中线或神经中枢、朝向四肢及体表周缘的区域。
peristalsis /ˌper.ɪˈstæl.sɪs/ 蠕动 p. 744
The smooth muscle contraction that involuntarily pushes food forward in the digestive tract in one direction in a wavelike motion.
蠕动：消化道管壁平滑肌发生的不受主观意志控制的波浪形节律性收缩舒张运动，推动食糜沿胃肠道单向持续前行。
pivot joints /ˈpɪv.ət dʒɔɪnts/ 车轴关节/环枢关节 p. 727
Joints that allow turning from side to side.
车轴关节/环枢关节：由一根圆骨突围绕纵向单轴在另一根骨头的骨-韧带环内进行旋转运动的滑膜关节，允许左右侧向旋转转动，例如寰枢正中关节（摇头）及近端桡尺关节（前臂旋前旋后）。
saddle joint /ˈsæd.əl dʒɔɪnt/ 鞍状关节 p. 727
Joints that allow for a wide range of motion between two articulating saddle-shaped bones, such as at the base of the thumb.
鞍状关节：由两个互为马鞍状相互嵌合的关节面构成，允许在两个相互垂直的轴上进行屈伸、展收及环转等多范围大幅度运动，最典型的代表是人类大拇指基底部的大多角骨-第一掌骨关节（对掌运动基础）。
secretion /sɪˈkriː.ʃən/ 分泌 p. 721
The release of a substance either into or out of the body after normal body functions.
分泌：腺上皮细胞等特化细胞将合成的特殊化学物质（如消化酶、黏液、激素、汗液）释放至血液或排出至上皮表面的生理过程。
specific immunity /spəˈsɪf.ɪk ɪˈmjuː.nə.ti/ 特异性免疫/获得性免疫 p. 743
The ability of an organism to resist or overcome an infection or illness against a certain disease or infection caused by a pathogen or antigenic substance.
特异性免疫/获得性免疫：由 T/B 淋巴细胞介导、仅针对特定外来抗原决定簇精准识别并特异性攻击杀伤的后天防御反应，具有免疫记忆特征。
superficial /ˌsuː.pəˈfɪʃ.əl/ 浅 p. 721
Near the surface of the body.
浅：解剖方位术语，指靠近身体外表面或某一器官表层的相对位置。
superior /suːˈpɪə.ri.ər/ 上 p. 721
Situated above another structure or toward the upper part of the body.
上：解剖方位术语，指位于另一解剖结构上方，或朝向头顶/身体高位的方位。
tissue /ˈtɪʃ.uː/ 组织 p. 721
A group of similar cells with a common function.
组织：由结构形态相似的细胞及其细胞间质组合而成、共同承担特定生理机能的细胞群体。
veins /veɪnz/ 静脉 p. 739
Blood vessels that carry blood back to the heart.
静脉：引导全身各处微循环血液回流运送回心脏的低压薄壁血管管腔管道，内多具备防止血液倒流的静脉瓣。
venules /ˈven.juːlz/ 微静脉/小静脉 p. 739
The smallest form of veins. The venules drain blood from the capillaries and come together to form the veins.
微静脉/小静脉：静脉系统分支中最细小的血管末梢，直接汇集引流毛细血管网中的静脉血，随后逐级汇合成中静脉和大静脉。

Cells

细胞（微观生命基石）
The basic functional unit of body structure is the cell. Each cell has the same basic structure, but the function, size, and shape of cells may be different. Cells are so small that you need a microscope to see them. Cells need nutrients, water, and oxygen to live and to perform their functions.
细胞是构成人体形态解剖结构与代谢功能的最基本生命单位。人体内每一个细胞都遵循相同的基本结构蓝图，但在具体生理功能、体积大小和微观形态上存在显著分化。细胞微小至极，必须借助高倍显微镜方能肉眼观察。细胞为了维系自身存活并高效行使其代谢功能，必须时刻获得营养素、水和氧气的充足供应。
The solution of 0.09% NaCl (sodium chloride or salt) and water which makes up all the entire body’s cells and bodily fluids is an isotonic solution and is compatible with all cells and their surrounding bodily fluids (see Chapter 28).
在人体中，构成全身细胞内液与细胞外液的 0.9% 氯化钠（NaCl，盐水）水溶液属于等渗溶液（isotonic solution），其渗透压与所有组织细胞及其周围体液完全相等兼容（参见第 28 章静脉输液规程）。

Each cell has 46 chromosomes that contain genes, which are passed on to new cells during reproduction. Genes determine the physical and chemical traits inherited by children from their parents. Inherited traits include height, eye colour, skin colour, and many other characteristics.
染色体（chromosomes）是位于细胞核内呈丝状或棒状的核酸蛋白复合结构，承载着指导细胞所有生化反应与发育程序的“遗传基因指令”。人体每一个正常体细胞均含有 46 条染色体（23 对），其上密布着成千上万个基因（genes），并在细胞分裂增殖时忠实传递给子代新细胞。基因决定了子女从亲代父母处遗传获得的一切生理与外貌特征。这些遗传表型特征包括身材身高、眼睛虹膜颜色、皮肤肤色以及众多体质特征。
Besides controlling the cell’s activities, the nucleus is responsible for cell reproduction. Cells reproduce by dividing in half and each half becoming a new cell. The process of cell division, called mitosis, is needed for the growth and repair of body tissues. The chromosomes replicate (or duplicate) and are pulled apart, and then the cell divides so that each new cell is identical, each with 46 chromosomes (FIGURE 32.3).
除了指挥调控细胞的日常代谢活动外，细胞核还主导细胞的繁殖与自我复制。细胞通过一分为二的均等分裂进行繁殖，分裂形成的两个子细胞各自成长为独立的全新细胞。这一细胞分裂的生理全过程被称为“有丝分裂（mitosis）”，是人体组织生长发育、表皮脱落更新以及创伤修复愈合的绝对物质前提。在有丝分裂过程中，母细胞核内的染色体首先进行高保真自我复制，随后被纺锤丝牵引向细胞两极精准分离，最终细胞膜与胞质在中央凹陷缢裂，形成两个与母细胞完全一致、各自均拥有 46 条完整染色体的崭新子细胞（见图 32.3）。

Tissues

组织（四大基本组织分类）
The cells are the body’s building blocks. Tissue is a group of cells that are similar to each other in structure and function. The body has four basic types of tissue:
细胞是构筑人体的生命积木。组织（Tissue）是由形态结构相似、具有共同起源且协同承担特定生理功能的细胞群及其细胞间质构成的结构复合体。人体拥有四大基本组织类型：
Epithelial tissue covers internal and external body surfaces. Epithelial tissue is classified according to the shape of the cells and the number of layers within each. The shape of epithelial cells is described as squamous (thin and flat, like fish scales), cuboidal (cube-shaped, like dice), or columnar (tall and narrow, like columns). Epithelial tissue forms the thin outer layer of the skin that lines the cavities that open to the outside in the nose, mouth, respiratory tract, stomach, urethra, vagina, and intestines. Many glands are also epithelial tissue. The function of epithelial tissue is to aid in protection, absorption, secretion, and filtration. Secretion is the release of a substance either into or out of the body after normal body functions.
1. 上皮组织（Epithelial tissue）：被覆于人体外表面以及体内各管、腔、囊的内表面。上皮组织根据其细胞形态以及细胞排列的层数进行严密分类。上皮细胞的几何形态可分为鳞状上皮（squamous，薄而扁平，如鱼鳞状）、立方上皮（cuboidal，呈立方体，如骰子状）以及柱状上皮（columnar，细高直立，如圆柱状）。上皮组织构成皮肤的表皮层，并衬覆于与外界相通的各种天然腔道内壁，如鼻腔、口腔、呼吸道、胃黏膜、尿道、阴道及肠道。机体内的许多外分泌与内分泌腺体本质上也由特化的腺上皮细胞构成。上皮组织的核心生理机能涵盖四大方面：物理保护、营养吸收、物质分泌以及液体滤过。分泌（Secretion）是指特化腺体细胞将机体合成的生物活性物质向内释放入血液或向外排出至上皮表面的生理过程。

Epithelial tissue has no blood supply of its own, so it depends on the blood supply of the underlying connective tissue for nourishment. Because it is composed almost entirely of cells and because it is so well nourished from the underlying connective tissue, it can repair or replace itself quickly. Epithelial tissue does contain sensory cells, so it is able to perceive sensations such as pain and pressure.
上皮组织自身没有任何血管分布（无血管性），因此它必须绝对依赖其深层基底膜下方结缔组织中毛细血管网的物质弥散浸润来摄取营养和氧气。由于上皮组织几乎全部由紧密相连的活细胞组成，且获得来自深层结缔组织的充分血液滋养，因此它具备极其强大的自我更新与快速分裂修复再造能力。上皮组织内密布着丰富的感觉神经末梢与感觉上皮细胞，因而能够极其灵敏地感知外界刺痛、触压及温热等各种感觉刺激。
Connective tissue anchors, connects, and supports other body tissues. Connective tissue is found in every part of the body. The types of connective tissue are connective tissue proper, made up of loose irregular cells in a viscous (thickened) fluid base or of dense irregular tissues, such as in joint capsules, and specialized connective tissue, which includes the following: (1) Adipose tissue (fat), which supplies energy to surrounding cells, aids in body temperature regulation, and acts both as a cushion and a support to surrounding structures. (2) Dense fibrous connective tissue, such as tendons (which attach muscles to bones) and ligaments (which connect bones and support joints). (3) Cartilage connective tissue, which supports, protects, and cushions joints and provides framework. (4) Bone tissue, which can protect organs such as the brain and supports the weight of the body for moving. (5) Blood, which transports nutrients, hormones, and respiratory gases such as oxygen and carbon dioxide and waste materials. (6) Lymph, which drains interstitial fluids and is involved in the body’s immune response.
2. 结缔组织（Connective tissue）：负责锚定、连接、充填、营养和支持人体的其他各类组织器官。结缔组织遍布全身各个角落。结缔组织分为两大类：固有结缔组织（由疏松不规则排列的细胞悬浮于黏稠液体基质中构成，或如关节囊般呈致密不规则纤维构筑）以及特殊结缔组织，后者包括以下六大核心成员：（1）脂肪组织（Adipose tissue，脂肪），为周围细胞储存并供应能量，参与体温保温隔热调节，并在重要器官周围发挥机械缓冲避震与稳固支撑功能；（2）致密纤维结缔组织，例如肌腱（tendons，将肌肉牢固连接固定于骨骼上）与韧带（ligaments，连接相邻骨骼并加固稳定关节）；（3）软骨结缔组织（Cartilage），支撑器官形态、保护并缓冲关节面磨损并构建弹性支架；（4）骨组织（Bone tissue），坚硬坚固，全力护卫脑、心、肺等脆弱生命脏器，并支撑人体体重负荷实现自由行动；（5）血液（Blood，流动结缔组织），在血管网络中高效转运营养物质、激素、呼吸气体（氧气与二氧化碳）以及代谢废物；（6）淋巴液（Lymph），引流细胞间隙组织液并深度参与全身免疫防御反应。
Muscle tissue is responsible for almost all body movements through contraction. There are three types of muscle tissue (see page 728). Nerve tissue is the major tissue of the nervous system. It is the main component of the brain, spinal cord, and all the nerves in the body. It is responsible for relaying information to and from the brain and throughout the body.
3. 肌组织（Muscle tissue）：通过肌纤维的主动收缩与舒张，驱动机体几乎所有的机械运动与内脏节律性搏动。人体内分布有三大类肌组织（详见第 728 页骨骼肌、平滑肌与心肌）。
4. 神经组织（Nerve tissue）：是构成神经系统的主要组织成分。它是大脑、脊髓以及全身所有周围神经干的主体结构，司职在神经中枢与外周效应器之间极其迅速精准地转导、整合与传递电化学神经冲动。

Organs and Body Cavities

器官与主要体腔分布
Organs are structures made of different tissues that are arranged to perform a specialized function. An organ performs one or more functions. Examples of organs are the heart, brain, liver, lungs, and kidneys. Organs are usually located within body cavities, but skin is also considered an organ. The two major cavities where organs are located are the dorsal cavity and the ventral cavity. The dorsal cavity has two cavities—the cranial cavity and the spinal cavity. The ventral cavity consists of the thoracic cavity, the abdominal cavity, and the pelvic cavity (FIGURE 32.4).
器官（Organs）是由几种不同类型的基本组织按照特定的立体解剖模式有机排列组合而成、能够独立行使特定生理机能的结构复合体。一个器官可以承担一项或多项复杂的生命功能。典型器官包括心脏、大脑、肝脏、肺脏与肾脏等。绝大多数脏器均安全容纳于人体的天然体腔之内，但被覆于体表的皮肤在解剖学上也属于一个完整的器官。人体内部容纳内脏的两大核心主腔隙为背侧腔（dorsal cavity）与腹侧腔（ventral cavity）。背侧腔由坚硬骨骼围成，细分为颅腔（cranial cavity，容纳脑）与椎管脊髓腔（spinal cavity，容纳脊髓）。腹侧腔则包含胸腔（thoracic cavity，容纳心肺）、腹腔（abdominal cavity，容纳消化吸收脏器）以及盆腔（pelvic cavity，容纳膀胱与生殖内器官）（见图 32.4）。

Organ Systems

器官系统与内环境稳态
Organ systems are formed by organs that work together to perform special functions (FIGURE 32.5). Homeostasis is a steady state or stable internal environment maintained when all of the organ systems in the human body work together. For example, if the external temperature drops, our bodies start to shiver, and this action creates warmth and helps keep our internal temperature at the normal level. We will discuss the major organ systems in this chapter: integumentary, musculoskeletal, nervous (including the senses), endocrine, circulatory (containing the cardiovascular and lymphatic), respiratory, immune, digestive, urinary, and reproductive.
器官系统（Organ systems）是由一系列在结构与功能上密切配合的器官协同组装而成，共同执行重大的综合生理任务（见图 32.5）。内环境稳态（Homeostasis）是指当人体所有器官系统和谐紧密配合运作时，机体所维持的一种动态平衡、理化性质相对恒定的稳定内部微环境。例如，当外界气温骤降时，机体骨骼肌会不自主发生寒战战栗（shiver），这种肌肉微颤收缩能够产生大量物理热量，从而有力协助核心体温维系在 37°C 的正常稳态水准。本章将对人体各大核心系统展开逐一系统阐述：皮膜系统、肌骨骼系统、神经系统（含特殊感官）、内分泌系统、循环系统（包含心血管与淋巴循环）、呼吸系统、免疫系统、消化系统、泌尿系统以及生殖系统。

THE INTEGUMENTARY SYSTEM

皮膜系统（人体第一道防御屏障）
The integumentary system consists of skin and its appendages—hair, nails, and sweat and oil glands. (A gland is a group of cells that produces and secretes a substance. The glands in skin produce and secrete sweat and oil.) The integumentary system is the largest system of the body since the skin is considered to be the largest organ by mass.
皮膜系统（The integumentary system）由皮肤及其附属器——毛发、指/趾甲以及汗腺和皮脂腺共同组成。（腺体是一群高度特化的细胞团，专门负责合成并分泌特定物质；皮肤内的腺体负责合成并分泌汗液与皮脂油。）由于皮肤在人体器官中按总质量和体表面积计算均属最大，因此皮膜系统被公认为人体最大的解剖系统。
Integument means “covering.” Skin is the body’s natural covering, and it consists of all four types of tissue working together. There are two skin layers—the epidermis and the dermis (FIGURE 32.6). The dermis is the inner layer of skin and is made up of connective tissue. Blood vessels, nerves, sweat and oil glands, and hair roots are found in the dermis. The epidermis is the outer layer and contains living and dead cells arranged in specific layers. As the cells divide, creating newer cells, the older cells are pushed toward the surface, where they eventually die. Dead cells constantly flake off and are replaced by living cells. The living cells of the epidermis contain pigment, which gives skin its colour. The epidermis has no blood vessels and few nerve endings.
“Integument”在解剖学拉丁语中意为“外被被膜”。皮肤是人体的天然保护外被，由四大基本组织紧密协作构筑而成。解剖上皮肤由两层主要结构组成——表皮层（epidermis）与真皮层（dermis）（见图 32.6）。真皮层是皮肤的内层结构，由坚韧且富有弹性的致密结缔组织交织而成；血管、感觉神经末梢、汗腺、皮脂腺以及毛根（毛囊）均深嵌于真皮层内。表皮层是皮肤的最外层，由特定层级排列的活细胞与死亡角质细胞构成。随着基底细胞不断进行有丝分裂产生全新子细胞，衰老的细胞被逐步推向体表表面，在此过程中细胞角质化并最终走向死亡。表层死亡的角化扁平上皮细胞呈皮屑状不断自然脱落（flake off），并时刻由深层上推的新生细胞补充更替。表皮深层的活细胞富含黑色素颗粒（pigment/melanin），决定并赋予了人体皮肤的自然肤色。表皮层内部完全没有毛细血管分布，且仅分布极少数游离神经末梢。
The skin rests on a layer of subcutaneous tissue, also called the subcutaneous layer or fatty layer (sub means “under,” and cutaneous means “skin”). Subcutaneous tissue is not technically part of the skin, but it connects the skin to the underlying muscles and bones. It is made up of adipose tissue, which insulates the body against cold and helps protect the body against injury by cushioning internal organs.
皮肤紧密附着并铺展在皮下组织（subcutaneous tissue，亦称皮下层或脂肪层；“sub-”意为“在……之下”，“cutaneous”意为“皮肤”）之上。从解剖学严格界定，皮下组织并不属于皮肤本身的真皮或表皮层，但它起着至关重要的锚定作用，将皮肤牢固连接固定于其下方的骨骼与肌肉筋膜上。皮下组织主要由富含脂滴的脂肪细胞（adipose tissue）构成，发挥绝佳的保温隔热屏障作用防止体内热量散失，并通过柔软饱满的脂肪软垫缓冲外力撞击，全面保护深层内脏免受钝性外伤。

The skin appendages provide specific functions for the body:
皮肤的四大附属器各自为人体行使特定的生理防御功能：
  • • Hair—The entire body, except the palms of the hands and the soles of the feet, is covered with hair. Hair in the nose, ears, and around the eyes protects these organs from dust, bugs, and other foreign objects.
    毛发（Hair）：除手掌和足底等无毛皮肤外，人体全身几乎全被毛发覆盖。生长在鼻腔内部的鼻毛、外耳道的耳毛以及眼睑边缘的睫毛，能够机械性拦截灰尘杂质、小飞虫及各种异物，从而对重要感觉器官提供有效防护。
  • • Nails—Nails protect the tips of fingers and toes from injury. The root of the nail lies beneath a fold of skin. Nails grow constantly as new cells form at the nail root.
    指/趾甲（Nails）：甲板由坚硬致密的角蛋白构成，坚固保护手指与脚趾末端娇嫩的远端指腹免受挤压外伤。甲根（nail root）深埋于近端甲褶皮肤下方，随着甲母质生发层不断分裂形成新细胞，指甲终身持续向外生长推移。
  • • Sweat glands—Sweat glands are located in the dermis and secrete perspiration through sweat ducts that open onto the skin’s surface at sweat pores. Sweat helps cool the body as it evaporates.
    汗腺（Sweat glands）：广泛盘曲分布于真皮深层，通过汗腺导管向上开口于体表汗孔，向外排泄汗液。汗液主要由水分、微量无机盐（氯化钠）及尿素等少量代谢废物构成；当体表汗液蒸发时带走大量汽化潜热，是人体在高温环境下维持核心体温平衡最核心的散热生理机制。
  • • Oil glands (sebaceous glands)—These glands are located in the dermis near hair roots and secrete an oily substance called sebum into the hair follicle and onto the skin’s surface. Sebum keeps hair and skin soft, shiny, and waterproof, and also inhibits bacterial growth on the skin’s surface.
    皮脂腺（Oil/Sebaceous glands）：位于真皮层内紧邻毛囊根部，其导管开口于毛囊内，合成分泌富含脂类的皮脂（sebum）。皮脂顺着毛干流向皮肤表面，形成一层弱酸性皮脂膜，不仅保持毛发与角质层柔软润泽富有光泽，形成天然防水防脱水屏障，而且皮脂中的游离脂肪酸能够显著抑制表皮致病细菌与真菌的异常滋生。

THE MUSCULOSKELETAL SYSTEM

肌骨骼系统（运动、支撑与保护框架）
The musculoskeletal system provides the framework for the body and allows the body to move. This system also protects the body and gives it shape. Besides bones and muscles, the system consists of ligaments, tendons, and cartilage.
肌骨骼系统（The musculoskeletal system）构筑了支撑整个人体直立的刚性骨架，并赋予机体在三维空间中自由协调运动的能力。该系统还承担着保护内部生命脏器以及赋予人体独特体型轮廓的重任。除坚硬的骨骼与动力肌群外，肌骨骼系统还囊括了韧带、肌腱以及软骨等极其关键的结缔组织运动辅助构件。

Bones

骨骼（微细结构与 206 块骨骼架构）
Bones are hard, rigid structures that are made up of living cells surrounded by minerals that have been deposited by the living cells. A thin but strong membrane, called the periosteum, covers the entire surface of every bone in the body and merges with the ligaments and tendons that attach to the bones. Since the bones are living tissue, they require blood vessels to provide oxygen and nutrients; these vessels are found within the structure of the bones. The hard, dense layer of bone that forms the surface of every bone is called compact bone. It surrounds a softer, less dense form of bone called spongy bone (trabecular bone), which has many tiny hollow spaces (like a sponge). Inside these hollow spaces is a substance called bone marrow. Blood cells are manufactured in the bone marrow.
骨骼（Bones）是坚硬致密、高度坚固的活体器官，由活的骨细胞（成骨细胞、骨细胞、破骨细胞）及其分泌沉淀的大量无机矿物质晶体（羟基磷酸钙）与胶原纤维交织而成。一层薄而极其坚韧的结缔组织纤维膜，称为骨膜（periosteum），紧密包被在全身除关节面外每一块骨骼的外表面，并与附着其上的肌腱和韧带致密融合无间。由于骨骼是代谢活跃的活体结缔组织，需要密集的骨内血管滋养孔穿透进入骨质深层，时刻输送氧气与营养素。构成每块骨骼外层的坚硬、致密实心骨板被称为密质骨/密质骨（compact bone）；密质骨内部则包裹着较为疏松、呈网状海绵结构的多孔骨质，称为松质骨（spongy/trabecular bone），其内部密布无数微小骨小梁孔隙（宛如海绵）。在这些松质骨孔隙及长骨骨髓腔内充填着骨髓（bone marrow）；全身所有的红细胞、绝大部分白细胞及血小板等血细胞，全部由红骨髓造血干细胞在此源源不断制造生成。
The human body has 206 bones (FIGURE 32.7), consisting of five types: (1) Long bones are hard and dense bones that provide strength, structure, and mobility. They are longer than they are wide. Long bone structure is most recognizable in the bones of the arms (humerus, radius, and ulna) and legs (femur, tibia, and fibula) but also includes the metacarpals of the palms, the metatarsals of the feet, and the phalanges of the fingers and toes. (2) Short bones allow skill and ease in movement. The bones in the wrists and ankles are short bones. Short bones are cube-shaped.
成人的骨骼系统由 206 块骨骼严密组装而成（见图 32.7），解剖学上根据其形态比例划分为五大类型：（1）长骨（Long bones）：质地坚固致密，长大于宽，呈管状结构，专门提供机械强度、负重支架与运动杠杆力矩。长骨在人体四肢最为典型醒目，如上肢的肱骨、桡骨与尺骨，下肢的股骨（人体最长最结实的骨骼）、胫骨与腓骨，同时也包括掌骨、跖骨以及手指和脚趾的各节指/趾骨。（2）短骨（Short bones）：立方形或多角形，赋予关节灵巧精细、多轴协同运动的自由度；手腕处的腕骨（8 块）与足踝处的跗骨（7 块）均属此类。

The remaining three types of bones are as follows:
其余三种骨骼分类的形态学与力学特征如下：
  • • Flat bones are thin, flat, and curved bones that protect the organs. Such bones include the ribs and sternum (breastbone), the skull, pelvic bones, and scapulae (shoulder blades).
    扁骨（Flat bones）：薄、扁平且往往呈现弧形弯曲的板状骨骼，专职大面积机械性护卫深层核心脏器并提供宽阔的肌肉附着面。典型代表包括肋骨、胸骨（护卫心肺）、颅骨穹窿板（护卫大脑）、骨盆髋骨板以及肩胛骨（肩胛扁板）。
  • • Irregular bones are irregularly shaped bones with various bony projections and features that interact with other bones. These bones provide sites for muscle attachment and allow various degrees of movement and flexibility, along with some support. They include the hip bones, vertebrae in the spinal column, and various bones in the skull.
    不规则骨（Irregular bones）：形态复杂多样、不属于上述任何标准几何规则的骨骼，表面具备突起的骨性棘突、横突与关节面，与其他骨骼精细咬合。它们为深层强力肌群提供牢固锚定附着点，赋予身体不同维度的活动度与柔韧性并承载中轴重力负荷。典型代表包括构成脊柱的 26 块椎骨、复杂的髋骨以及颅底部分不规则骨。
  • • Sesamoid bones are usually short or irregular bones that are imbedded in a tendon and protect the tendon. These bones include the patella (knee cap), which is connected to the quadriceps tendon.
    籽骨（Sesamoid bones）：通常是发育包埋在强力肌腱深面或穿经关节处的扁小或椭圆形骨骼，其生理使命是保护肌腱免受过度磨损与机械性剪切挤压，并改变肌腱牵引受力角度以增大肌肉做功杠杆效应。全身最大最著名的籽骨是膝关节正前方的髌骨（patella，膝盖骨），它牢牢嵌入包绕在股四头肌肌腱与髌韧带之中。

Joints

关节（骨骼连接与运动枢纽）
A joint is the point at which two or more bones meet (FIGURE 32.8). Joints allow movement (see Chapter 14). Cartilage is connective tissue that cushions the joint so that bone ends do not rub together. The synovial membrane lines the joint and secretes synovial fluid, which acts as a lubricant so that the joint can move smoothly. Bones are held together at the joint by strong bands of connective tissue called ligaments.
关节（Joint，骨连结）是两块或两块以上骨骼相互咬合相遇的解剖连接位点（见图 32.8）。关节是骨骼杠杆运动的机械枢纽枢轴（参见第 14 章）。覆盖在骨端表面的关节软骨属于特殊的无血管致密结缔组织，起到极其平滑的缓冲防震软垫作用，防止在承重或剧烈撞击下两端坚硬骨面发生直接骨-骨摩擦破坏。关节囊内层的滑膜（synovial membrane）分泌清亮黏稠的滑液（synovial fluid），充当天然生物高分子润滑剂，使关节面在相对滑动时摩擦阻力降至几乎为零。相邻骨骼在关节周围由极其坚韧的致密结缔组织纤维索带——韧带（ligaments）紧密维系缠绕在一起，防止关节发生异常脱位与过度异常伸展扭伤。

Joints can be classified into three groups according to the amount of movement:
解剖学上根据关节允许发生的机械活动度大小，将全身关节划分为三大功能类别：
  • • Fibrous, or immovable joints—The bones are held tightly together by dense fibrous connective tissue and allow no movement, such as the sutures connecting the bones of the skull.
    纤维关节/不动关节（Fibrous/Immovable joints）：骨与骨之间由极短而致密的结缔组织纤维紧密固连，彼此之间绝对不允许发生任何滑动位移，最典型的代表是紧密咬合颅骨各块骨板的颅骨缝（sutures）。
  • • Cartilaginous, or slightly movable joints—The bones are connected by cartilage, allowing limited movement, such as the movement achieved in the spinal column between the cartilaginous intervertebral discs and the joints connecting ribs to the sternum.
    软骨关节/微动关节（Cartilaginous/Slightly movable joints）：骨与骨之间通过纤维软骨或透明软骨直接牢固相连，只允许发生微小有限的形变与缓冲摆动，典型代表包括脊柱相邻椎骨之间富含弹性的椎间盘软骨，以及胸廓中连接真肋与胸骨的肋软骨关节。
  • • Synovial, or freely movable joints—These joints possess a fluid-filled joint cavity surrounded by an articular capsule, providing much more flexibility and wide range of movement than the other two types of joints.
    滑膜关节/活动关节（Synovial/Freely movable joints）：两骨端之间完全分离并形成一个密闭充盈滑液的关节腔，外部被关节囊紧密包绕，赋予机体远超前两种关节类型的极大灵活性与多轴宽广活动范围（如肩、肘、髋、膝等）。
A typical synovial joint is more complex than the other types of joints and includes the following structures: (1) Articular cartilage—which lines the articulating surface of each of the two bones, forming a smooth, frictionless surface within the joint. (2) Joint capsule—made up of fibrous connective tissue, enclosing the joint cavity and maintaining structural integrity. (3) Synovial membrane—which lines the inner surface of the joint capsule and secretes lubricating synovial fluid. (4) Synovial fluid—a thick, slippery fluid that lubricates the joint and nourishes the articular cartilage. (5) Ligaments—strong, flexible bands of fibrous tissue that bind bones together and prevent undesirable or excessive movements.
一个典型的滑膜活动关节具有比不动关节复杂得多的立体解剖构筑，必须完整具备以下核心结构：（1）关节软骨（Articular cartilage）：被覆于两块骨骼相对的咬合关节面表面，形成极其光滑、无血管神经且弹性极高的低摩擦缓冲镜面；（2）关节囊（Joint capsule）：由坚韧的纤维结缔组织构成囊套，将两骨端完整封闭包裹，维系关节腔结构的密闭性与机械抗张强度；（3）滑膜（Synovial membrane）：衬覆于关节囊内层的薄层疏松血管神经网，专司源源不断合成并分泌滑液；（4）滑液（Synovial fluid）：充盈在关节腔内的蛋清样无色黏稠液体，充当高效流体润滑剂并为缺乏血管的关节软骨提供必需的营养弥散代谢；（5）韧带（Ligaments）：横跨关节周围或深藏于关节腔内的强韧纤维束带，牢牢牵系加固两端骨骼，限定关节运动轴向并严防过度异常脱位。

Synovial joint components continued: Joint capsule—made up of fibrous connective tissue, enclosing the joint in a strong sleevelike covering. Synovial membrane—lines the joint capsule and secretes lubricating synovial fluid into the joint cavity. Synovial fluid—lubricates the bones in the joint and decreases the friction within the joint. Bursae (singular: bursa)—small sacs of synovial fluid that function as a cushion and gliding surface between the joint and the tendons that cross over the joint (excessive use of the joint may cause a painful inflammation of the bursae, known as bursitis).
滑膜关节核心结构续篇：关节囊（Joint capsule）——由强韧的纤维结缔组织构成袖套状结构紧密封闭关节；滑膜（Synovial membrane）——衬覆于关节囊内壁并向密闭关节腔内源源不断分泌润滑滑液；滑液（Synovial fluid）——在骨端间形成流体润滑层，将关节内骨面相对运动时的摩擦系数降至极限；滑囊/滑液囊（Bursae，单数形式为 bursa）——衬有滑膜、内充滑液的封闭性扁平囊袋，垫衬于跨越关节的肌腱、韧带与骨面之间发挥缓冲防磨和减压滑动功能（关节长期过度劳损或反复剧烈摩擦可诱发滑囊无菌性炎性水肿，临床上称为滑囊炎/bursitis）。

Moving Synovial Joints

活动滑膜关节六大运动力学类型
The body contains many types of freely movable synovial joints. The types of motion and the degree of flexibility vary with each type of joint. There are six types of movable synovial joints, classified by the type of movement allowed by the joint (FIGURE 32.9):
人体内分布着多种类型各异的自由活动滑膜关节。不同关节所允许产生的空间运动轨迹、转动轴数以及活动度范围存在显著解剖差异。根据关节咬合面的几何构型及所允许发生的力学运动类型，人体活动滑膜关节被精确细分为六大经典类型（见图 32.9）：
  • • Ball-and-socket joint—allows movement in all directions. It is made up of the rounded end of one bone and a hollow cavity or depression in another bone. The rounded end of one fits into the cavity of the other. The joints of the hips and shoulders are ball-and-socket joints. Although the ball-and-socket joint allows for a wide range of movement, it also predisposes the joint to easy displacement. For example, a fall can cause the shoulder to dislocate.
    球窝关节（Ball-and-socket joint）：具备三个互相垂直的运动轴，允许在三维空间中进行屈伸、内收外展、旋内旋外及环转等多轴全方位大幅度自由活动。由一根骨头圆球形的骨头头端（球）嵌入另一根骨头杯状的凹陷关节窝（窝）中组装而成。人体典型的球窝关节包括肩关节（肱骨头与肩胛盂）与髋关节（股骨头与髋臼）。尽管球窝关节赋予了肢体极其宽广广阔的活动度，但这也使其在解剖力学上更容易发生脱位错缝；例如老年案主跌倒外伤极易引发肩关节前脱位。
  • • Hinge joint—allows movement in one direction around a single axis. The structure of these joints is very similar to a hinge that you might find on a door. Hinge joints include elbows, knees, and fingers.
    屈戍关节/铰链关节（Hinge joint）：仅围绕单一体内水平额状轴在一个平面内进行屈曲（flexion）与伸展（extension）运动。其咬合机械构造与日常门窗上的金属合页完全如出一辙。人体典型的铰链关节包括肘关节（肱尺关节）、膝关节（屈伸主运动）以及各指/趾骨间的指间关节。
  • • Pivot joint—consists of a rounded projection on one bone fitting into a depression on another bone, which allows turning from side to side. The skull is connected to the spine by a pivot joint.
    车轴关节/环枢关节（Pivot joint）：由一根骨骼圆柱状的骨性突起嵌入另一根骨骼及环状韧带围成的骨-纤维环内，仅允许围绕自身纵向长轴进行侧向左右旋转转动。头颅骨通过第一颈椎（寰椎）与第二颈椎（枢椎）构成的寰枢正中车轴关节牢牢连接在脊柱顶端，使头部能够自由左右摆动摇头。
  • • Saddle joint—allows for a wide range of motion between two articulating bones that are saddle-shaped. The base of the thumb has a saddle joint known as a carpometacarpal joint, which gives the thumb a wide range of motion (opposable thumb) and gives us the ability to firmly grasp objects.
    鞍状关节（Saddle joint）：两块相咬合骨骼的关节面均互为凹凸的马鞍形态，允许在两个相互垂直的轴向上进行多轴屈伸、内收外展以及圆锥环转运动。人类双手大拇指根部的大多角骨-第一掌骨关节（掌指腕关节）属于典型的鞍状关节，正是这一独特的进化构造赋予了人类大拇指无可比拟的“对掌运动（opposable thumb）”灵活性，使人类能够精准灵巧地捏拿抓握细小工具。
  • • Gliding joint—a joint between two corresponding flat bones held in place by ligaments that glide past one another in any direction along the plane of the joint—up and down, left and right, or diagonally. This movement ensures the bones have limited but complex gliding movements. Gliding joints are found in the wrist (intercarpal joints), ankles (intertarsal joints), and the vertebral column.
    平面关节/滑动关节（Gliding joint）：由韧带固定维系的两个相对应的扁平微曲骨面构成，两骨面可在关节咬合平面上向任意方向相互发生微小错动微滑——上下、左右或沿对角线滑动。这种微动确保了骨骼群能够产生受限但协同极其精细的微量缓冲减震；广泛分布于腕骨间的腕骨间关节、足踝处的跗骨间关节以及脊柱相邻椎骨间的关节突关节。
  • • Condyloid joint—is formed when the oval articular surface of one bone fits into the oval depression of the second articulating bone. The wrist (radiocarpal joint) and the knuckles at the base of the fingers (metacarpophalangeal joints) are examples.
    髁状关节/椭圆关节（Condyloid joint）：由一根骨头的卵圆形凸面关节头嵌合于另一根骨头的卵圆形凹陷关节窝中构筑而成，允许屈伸与内收外展两轴双向运动。典型代表包括桡腕关节（手腕）以及手指根部掌骨头与近节指骨底连接的掌指关节（手打指关节拳峰）。

Muscles

肌肉系统（肌组织三大分类与生理功能）
There are more than 600 muscles in the human body (FIGURES 32.10 through 32.12). Muscle tissue is categorized as forming skeletal (striated) muscles, smooth muscles, or cardiac muscles.
人体骨骼肌肉系统由超过 600 块肌肉严密分布而成（见图 32.10 至 32.12）。肌组织在组织学与生理功能上被系统归为三大基本类型：骨骼肌（横纹随意肌）、平滑肌（非横纹不随意肌）以及心肌（横纹不随意肌）。
Skeletal muscles, the muscles attached to bones, are striated—that is, they look striped or streaked under a microscope. They are also voluntary, meaning they can be consciously controlled. Arm muscles or leg muscles do not work unless you deliberately move your arm or your leg. Smooth muscles can be identified by their smooth appearance (they are not streaked or striped). Smooth muscles are involuntary, which means they work automatically and cannot be consciously controlled. Smooth muscles are present in the stomach, intestines, blood vessels, and other body organs. Cardiac muscle is found only in the heart. Although it appears striated like skeletal muscle, it is involuntary muscle with tight junctions between branching cells.
骨骼肌（Skeletal muscles）是绝大多数通过肌腱附着固定在骨骼上的动力肌群。在光学显微镜下，其肌原纤维呈现出特征性的明暗交替横纹条纹（striped/streaked），因而又称横纹肌。骨骼肌同时属于随意肌（voluntary），即其收缩与舒张完全受大脑皮层意识的主观意志支配；除非你主动指令支配活动手臂或腿部，否则肢体骨骼肌绝不会自发随意做功。平滑肌（Smooth muscles）以其肌纤维表面平滑、缺乏横纹条带而得名。平滑肌属于不随意肌（involuntary），即由自主神经系统（植物神经）自动调节其节律性舒缩，完全不受主观意识随意控制。平滑肌广泛构筑于胃壁、肠道管壁、血管壁以及支气管、膀胱、子宫等内脏器官肌层之中。心肌（Cardiac muscle）是唯独存在于心脏心肌壁的特化肌组织；尽管心肌在显微镜下具有类似骨骼肌的明暗横纹，但其肌纤维分支吻合、细胞间具备紧密缝隙连接（闰盘），完全不受主观意志支配，属于高度自律的不随意肌。

Muscles can do only two things: contract and relax. Most muscles contract and relax quickly. Muscles perform three important body functions: (1) Movement of body parts. (2) Maintenance of posture. (3) Production of body heat.
从生物力学本质而言，肌肉所能执行的基本动作唯有两件事：主动收缩（变短发力）与被动舒张（复原放松）。绝大多数肌肉能够以毫秒级的极高速度迅速收缩与舒张。骨骼肌肉系统在人体中发挥着三大不可或缺的生理核心机能：（1）驱动身体各解剖部位的空间位移与杠杆机械运动；（2）维持直立挺拔的抗重力身体姿态与平衡构型；（3）通过营养物质氧化代谢持续源源不断生产体热以维系恒定体温。
Strong, tough connective tissues called tendons connect muscles to bones. When muscles contract (shorten), tendons at each end of the muscle cause the bone to move. The body has numerous tendons; the Achilles tendon is shown in FIGURE 32.12. Some muscles constantly contract to maintain the body’s posture. When muscles contract, they must metabolize nutrients to generate energy, resulting in the production of heat.
强韧坚实、抗拉伸强度极高的白纤维结缔组织束带——肌腱（tendons），将每块肌肉两端牢牢铆定连接在骨骼表面。当肌纤维接受神经冲动而主动收缩（缩短）时，拉力经由两端肌腱传递至骨骼杠杆，从而驱动骨骼绕关节转动位移。人体内遍布无数长短肌腱；其中人体最强壮粗大的肌腱——跟腱（Achilles tendon，阿喀琉斯之踵，连接小腿三头肌与跟骨）详见图 32.12。部分核心姿态肌群（如椎旁肌、腹直肌、臀大肌）时刻保持持续微弱的张力性等长收缩，以维系身体抗拒地心引力的稳定姿势。在肌肉收缩做功的过程中，肌细胞必须加速氧化代谢葡萄糖等营养物质生成 ATP 能量，此过程约有 70% 以上的能量以热能形式释放，构成了机体最主要的产热源泉。

THE NERVOUS SYSTEM

神经系统（人体最高司令与调控中枢）
The nervous system controls, directs, and coordinates body functions. It is the body’s communication network. The nervous system has two main divisions: (1) The central nervous system (CNS), which consists of the brain and the spinal cord (FIGURE 32.13), and (2) The peripheral nervous system (PNS) (FIGURE 32.14), which carry messages or impulses to and from the central nervous system.
神经系统（The nervous system）统驭、指挥并高度协调机体所有的生理功能与脏器代谢活动。它是贯穿整个人体的超高速神经信息通讯网络。解剖学上神经系统被划分为两大核心中枢与外周分支：（1）中枢神经系统（Central Nervous System，CNS），由颅腔内的脑与椎管内的脊髓两大最高调控中枢组成（见图 32.13）；（2）周围神经系统（Peripheral Nervous System，PNS）（见图 32.14），由遍布全身的脑神经与脊神经组成，专职在中枢神经系统与全身各种感受器及外周效应器之间往返传导神经冲动与生理指令。
The nervous system is stimulated by a stimulus. A stimulus (plural: stimuli) is anything that excites or causes an organ or tissue to react. For example, a stimulus can be a hot surface, bright light, or sound.
神经系统的全部电生理反应均由“刺激（stimulus）”所触发始动。刺激（复数形式为 stimuli）是指能使机体感受器或组织器官兴奋并激发生理应答的任何内外环境理化变化。例如，体表接触到滚烫灼热的茶杯壁、瞳孔遭遇耀眼刺目的强光直射、或耳膜接收到突如其来的剧烈爆炸声响，均属于典型的神经刺激源。

Neurons and Nerve Impulses

神经元微细构筑与突触冲动传导
The basic unit of the nervous system is the neuron, or nerve cell (FIGURE 32.15). A neuron consists of a cell body and two types of threadlike processes called dendrites and an axon. Dendrites carry messages to the cell body, whereas the axon carries messages away from the cell body.
神经系统最基本的解剖形态与神经传导功能单位是神经元（neuron），即神经细胞（见图 32.15）。一个典型的神经元由一个细胞体（cell body，胞体）以及从胞体向外延伸出的两类长短粗细不同的细胞突起——树突（dendrites）与轴突（axon）构成。树突通常较短且呈树枝状分支，专职接收来自其他神经元的化学信息并向细胞体传入神经电信号；而轴突通常细长且独一无二，专职将胞体整合后的神经冲动向远端轴突末梢高速运离传出。
A nerve impulse is a signal that is transmitted along the membrane of the neuron. Many axons are covered with a protective layer called the myelin sheath (see FIGURE 32.15). The myelin sheath insulates the axon and helps the nerve impulse travel faster along the axon. Nerve cells are unable to reproduce by mitosis; once destroyed, they cannot be replaced.
神经冲动（nerve impulse）是一种沿着神经元质膜高速跳跃扩布的生物电动作电位信号。许多神经元的长轴突外层紧密包裹着一层富含类脂质的特化绝缘保护套层，称为髓鞘（myelin sheath，见图 32.15）。髓鞘发挥高度绝缘防漏电功能，使动作电位只能在没有髓鞘包裹的朗飞氏结（Ranvier's nodes）之间展开跳跃式高速传导，从而将神经冲动的传导速率提升数十倍。成熟的神经元高度特化分化，终身彻底丧失通过有丝分裂进行再生的潜能；脑与脊髓中枢神经元一旦遭受不可逆坏死破坏，将永久无法原位增殖替代。
Between neurons, there is a microscopic gap called a synapse (FIGURE 32.16). The electrical impulse travels along the axon until it reaches the axon terminal, where it triggers the release of chemical substances called neurotransmitters into the synapse. The neurotransmitter molecules diffuse across the synapse and bind to receptors on the dendrite of the receiving neuron, initiating a new electrical impulse. This alternating electrical and chemical transmission allows information to be relayed rapidly throughout the entire body.
在相邻两个神经元相接的微观部位，存在一个极微细的细胞外间隙，称为突触（synapse，见图 32.16）。当动作电位电信号沿轴突高速传导抵达轴突末梢的突触前膜时，会电触发突触小泡破裂，将特异性的化学信使分子——神经递质（neurotransmitters，如乙酰胆碱、去甲肾上腺素、多巴胺）释放入突触间隙中。神经递质迅速弥散穿过突触间隙，与突触后膜树突表面的特异性受体蛋白高度亲和结合，从而在受体神经元膜上重新激发崭新的电动作电位。这种“电信号—化学信号—电信号”的交替转换机制，使复杂的感知、运动与思维信息得以毫秒级地在全身神经回路中精准传递。

The Central Nervous System (CNS)

中枢神经系统（脑与脊髓）
The central nervous system consists of the brain and the spinal cord (see FIGURE 32.13). The brain is housed inside the cranial cavity of the skull, and the spinal cord is housed inside the spinal canal of the vertebral column. The brain is the control centre of the entire nervous system and consists of three major parts: (1) The cerebrum, (2) The cerebellum, and (3) The brain stem.
中枢神经系统（CNS）由脑（brain）和脊髓（spinal cord）两大核心解剖结构组合而成（见图 32.13）。脑重达约 1.4 千克，严密安居并受坚硬颅骨围成的颅腔保护；脊髓则呈长圆柱状向下延伸，安居在脊柱骨骼围成的椎管之中。脑是整个人体一切生命活动、自主行为、感觉意识与思维智慧的最高调控总指挥部，解剖学上将其划分为三大主力解剖区域：（1）大脑（cerebrum）；（2）小脑（cerebellum）；（3）脑干（brain stem）。

The Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)

周围神经系统（脑神经、脊神经与自主神经）
The peripheral nervous system consists of the nerves throughout the body outside the brain and spinal cord (see FIGURE 32.14). The PNS includes: (1) 12 pairs of cranial nerves, which originate in the brain and connect directly to receptors and muscles in the head, neck, chest, and abdomen. Cranial nerves conduct impulses for sight, hearing, taste, smell, eye and facial movements, and swallowing. (2) 31 pairs of spinal nerves, which branch off the spinal cord and carry impulses to and from the skin, muscles, and other parts of the chest, abdomen, and limbs.
周围神经系统（PNS）由分布于脑与脊髓以外全身各处的广泛神经干及神经节组成（见图 32.14）。PNS 在解剖上主要由两大对偶神经束网构成：（1）12 对脑神经（cranial nerves）：从脑干及间脑直接发出，穿过颅底骨孔分布至头部、颈部、胸部及腹部脏器；脑神经负责掌管视觉、听觉、味觉、嗅觉、眼球转动、面部肌肉表情表达、咀嚼吞咽以及舌咽喉部的感觉运动调控；（2）31 对脊神经（spinal nerves）：成对从脊髓各个节段对称发出（8对颈神经、12对胸神经、5对腰神经、5对骶神经、1对尾神经），穿过椎间孔向外周辐射，负责将躯干与四肢皮肤的感觉神经冲动传入脊髓，并将运动中枢指令高效下达传导至骨骼肌肉与内脏。
The peripheral nervous system also includes the autonomic nervous system (ANS), which controls involuntary functions such as heartbeat, blood pressure, intestinal contractions, and glandular secretions. The ANS is divided into two divisions: the sympathetic nervous system, which stimulates organs and speeds up body functions during emergency, fear, exercise, or stress ('fight-or-flight' response), and the parasympathetic nervous system, which slows functions down to maintain normal baseline states, conserves energy, and promotes digestion ('rest-and-digest').
周围神经系统在功能机制上还包含了自主神经系统（Autonomic Nervous System，ANS，植物神经系统），专门调节不受主观意识支配的内脏不随意机能，如心肌搏动节律、血管口径舒缩阻力、胃肠平滑肌蠕动排空以及各类外分泌与内分泌腺的分泌活动。自主神经系统细分为两大彼此拮抗又精密协同的双向分支：交感神经系统（sympathetic system）——在机体遭遇应激、恐惧、愤怒、剧烈运动等紧急状态下高度兴奋被激活，释放去甲肾上腺素加速心率、升高血压、扩张气管支气管、抑制消化，调动全身储备进入“战斗或逃跑（fight-or-flight）”应激反应；副交感神经系统（parasympathetic system）——在非应激常态或休息时占据主导优势，使心跳节律减缓、血压回落平稳、促进胃肠道蠕动吸收消化、积蓄体能储备，维系“休养与消化（rest-and-digest）”的内环境基线平衡。

The Cerebrum and Cerebral Cortex

大脑半球、皮层灰质与脑叶功能定位
The cerebrum is the largest part of the brain and is the centre of thought and voluntary action. The cerebrum is divided into two halves called the right and left hemispheres. The right hemisphere controls movement and activities on the body’s left side, whereas the left hemisphere controls the body’s right side (contralateral control). The outermost layer of the cerebrum is called the cerebral cortex. The cerebral cortex is composed of grey matter (billions of unmyelinated neuron cell bodies), and its surface is deeply folded into ridges (gyri) and grooves (sulci) to increase its surface area.
大脑（Cerebrum）占据脑总体积的 80% 以上，是人类意识思维活动、逻辑推理、语言表达与自主运动的最高神经枢纽。大脑在纵向被大脑纵裂完全分隔为左右两半，分别称为右大脑半球与左大脑半球。左右半球通过强大的神经纤维桥梁——胼胝体（corpus callosum）紧密相连。人类神经系统存在显著的“交叉对侧支配（contralateral control）”法则：右侧大脑半球指挥支配人体左侧半身的运动与感觉功能；反之，左侧大脑半球精确统辖调控人体右侧半身。大脑两半球最表层被覆着厚度仅约 2 至 4 毫米的脑组织层，称为大脑皮层（cerebral cortex）。大脑皮层全部由密集的神经元胞体和树突（无髓鞘神经组织）交织而成，呈现出特征性的灰褐色（灰质/grey matter）；其表面形成深浅不一的起伏折叠回旋（脑回/gyri）与沟裂（脑沟/sulci），以极其紧凑的几何构型极大地扩展了皮层的有效神经功能表面积。
The cerebral cortex is divided into four functional lobes, each named after the cranial bone overlying it: (1) Frontal lobe—controls voluntary muscle movements, personality, moral reasoning, emotional expression, and motor speech (Broca’s area). (2) Parietal lobe—interprets somatic sensory information from the skin and body, including touch, temperature, pressure, and pain. (3) Temporal lobe—contains auditory and olfactory association areas responsible for hearing, balance, smell, and memory comprehension. (4) Occipital lobe—interprets visual impulses received from the optic nerves, allowing us to recognize and interpret visual images.
大脑皮层在解剖上根据覆盖其表面的颅骨位置，系统划分为四大脑叶，各自承担极其明确的功能定位：（1）额叶（Frontal lobe）：司职骨骼肌的自主运动策划、道德伦理判断、长远计划、个性人格以及运动性语言表达（布罗卡区/Broca's area）；（2）顶叶（Parietal lobe）：是全身躯体感觉中枢，专门整合与破译来自皮肤与深层组织的触觉、温度觉、压觉、空间本体感觉及疼痛刺激；（3）颞叶（Temporal lobe）：负责听觉中枢与嗅觉中枢的信号分析，深度参与声音理解、音乐感知、嗅觉辨别以及语言记忆整合；（4）枕叶（Occipital lobe）：位于大脑后极，是全身视觉投射最高中枢，负责解析来自视神经的视觉光影冲动，使我们能够看清、识别并理解所观察到的复杂三维物体形象。

Cerebellum, Brain Stem, Meninges, and CSF

小脑、脑干、三层脑膜与脑脊液循环系统
The cerebellum regulates and coordinates body movements. It coordinates muscle actions so that movements are smooth and steady, and it is responsible for maintaining balance and controlling voluntary muscles. Injury to the cerebellum results in jerky, uncoordinated movements and loss of balance.
小脑（Cerebellum）位于大脑枕叶后下方，紧邻脑干背侧。它的核心生理机能是调节和精细协调骨骼肌的随意运动。小脑精准协同不同对抗肌群的收缩与舒张节律，使身体各项肢体动作能够平稳、圆润、精准而不产生震颤抖动，并且时刻司职维持机体的动态平衡与抗重力姿态。小脑一旦遭遇外伤、卒中缺血或退行性病变，案主将立刻表现出肢体共济失调（ataxia）、步态踉跄醉酒样、动作不稳与剧烈失衡。
The brain stem connects the cerebrum to the spinal cord. It contains three major structures: the midbrain, the pons, and the medulla. The midbrain relays messages between the cerebrum and the spinal cord. The pons relays messages between the cerebrum and the cerebellum. The medulla, located at the bottom of the brain stem, contains the vital autonomic centres that control heart rate, breathing, blood vessel diameter (blood pressure), swallowing, coughing, and vomiting. Damage to the medulla typically results in immediate respiratory or cardiovascular arrest and death.
脑干（Brain stem）是向下连接大脑与脊髓的中枢生命管道，由上至下依次由中脑（midbrain）、脑桥（pons）与延髓（medulla oblongata）三大结构组成。中脑负责在上级大脑皮层与深部脊髓之间向上向下中继传导运动感觉冲动并调控瞳孔光反射；脑桥充当连接大脑与小脑之间的神经信息交叉中继桥梁；延髓位于脑干的最底端、向下直接延续为颈段脊髓。延髓在医学界被称为无可替代的“生命中枢（vital centres）”，其内密集分布着调控心跳节律与心肌收缩力的心血管中枢、调控自主呼吸节律的呼吸中枢、调节全身小动脉舒缩的外周阻力血管中枢，以及吞咽反射、咳嗽反射与呕吐中枢。延髓一旦遭受重创、压迫或脑疝形成，往往会在数秒之内迅速导致心跳呼吸骤停而发生不可逆临床死亡。
The brain and spinal cord are covered and protected by three layers of connective tissue membranes called the meninges: (1) The dura mater is the tough, outer layer lining the skull and vertebral canal. (2) The arachnoid mater is the delicate, web-like middle layer. (3) The pia mater is the thin, vascular inner layer adhering tightly to the surface of the brain and spinal cord. The subarachnoid space between the arachnoid and pia mater is filled with cerebrospinal fluid (CSF). Cerebrospinal fluid circulates around the brain and spinal cord, acting as a watery shock absorber that protects the central nervous system against physical trauma.
脑与脊髓除坚硬骨骼骨盆防护外，其表面还完整包被着三层致密保护性结缔组织被膜，解剖学上通称为脑膜与脊膜（meninges）：（1）硬脑膜/硬脊膜（Dura mater）：紧贴于颅骨内板和椎管骨膜内面，是一层极厚坚韧、富含胶原纤维的白色外层硬膜；（2）蛛网膜（Arachnoid mater）：半透明、菲薄如蛛网般的中间结缔组织膜；（3）软脑膜/软脊膜（Pia mater）：富含丰富毛细血管网的薄层内膜，极其紧密地贴附包覆在大脑每一个脑回沟底以及脊髓表面。在蛛网膜与软脑膜之间的宽广腔隙称为蛛网膜下隙（subarachnoid space），其内充盈流动着清亮无色的脑脊液（Cerebrospinal fluid，CSF）。脑脊液由脑室内脉络丛源源不断分泌生成并在中枢周围循环流动，充当液体水力缓冲防震吸震垫，使重达千克以上的大脑宛如“悬浮”于液体护甲之中，强力缓冲任何来自外界剧烈颠簸撞击对神经脑组织的机械损伤。

The Sense Organs: The Eye and Sight

特殊感觉器官：眼球精细解剖与视觉机制
The five senses are sight, hearing, taste, smell, and touch. Receptors for taste are located in the tongue and are called taste buds. Receptors for smell are located in the nose. Touch receptors are found in the dermis of skin throughout the body, especially in fingertips, toes, and lips. Receptors for vision are located in the eyes. The eye is protected by the bones of the skull, eyelids, eyelashes, and tears. Eye structures are shown in FIGURE 32.18. The eye has three layers: (1) The sclera—the tough white outer layer, which anteriorly forms the transparent cornea. (2) The choroid—the vascular middle layer, which includes the ciliary body and the iris (which contains the pupil). (3) The retina—the innermost layer containing photoreceptors (rods and cones) and the fibres of the optic nerve.
人体的五大特殊感觉为视觉、听觉、味觉、嗅觉与触觉。味觉感受器广泛嵌合分布于舌乳头内，称为味蕾（taste buds）；嗅觉感受器密集分布于鼻腔上鼻甲黏膜内；触压觉感受器广泛分布于全身真皮层内，在指腹、脚趾及口唇等部位分布密度最高。视力感受器完全集中于眼球壁视网膜内。眼球作为极其娇嫩脆弱的感觉器官，受到颅骨眼眶骨板、眼睑、睫毛与泪液的多重严密防御保护。眼球解剖切面如图 32.18 所示，由同心圆排列的三层眼球壁构成：（1）外层（纤维膜）：后 5/6 为瓷白色坚韧抗张的巩膜（sclera，眼白），前 1/6 为完全透明、微向前凸的角膜（cornea）；（2）中层（血管膜/色素膜）：包含富含血管滋养眼球的脉络膜（choroid）、具备调节晶状体凸度睫状肌的睫状体（ciliary body），以及赋予眼球特异颜色的虹膜（iris，中央的透光圆孔为瞳孔/pupil）；（3）内层（视网膜/retina）：薄而娇嫩的神经感光层，密布视杆细胞与视锥细胞两大感光神经元，并在视乳头处汇聚成粗大的视神经（optic nerve）穿出眼眶直通大脑枕叶。

Light enters the eye through the cornea, the transparent part of the outer layer that lies over the front of the eye. Light rays pass through the pupil to the lens, which lies behind the pupil. The lens changes its curvature to focus light rays onto the retina. The retina contains two types of specialized photoreceptors: rods, which respond to dim light and allow night and peripheral black-and-white vision, and cones, which operate in bright light and enable sharp central vision and colour perception. The photoreceptors convert light energy into electrical nerve impulses, which are carried to the occipital lobe of the brain by the optic nerve for interpretation.
外界光线首先穿透眼球前表面晶莹剔透的角膜。角膜曲率对光线产生强烈的初步屈折聚焦。光线穿过瞳孔后，精准射入位于瞳孔后方的双凸透镜状晶状体（lens）。晶状体在睫状肌牵引调节下能够灵活变凸变扁，从而将不同距离的目标物象精准聚焦呈像于视网膜感光表面。视网膜上分布着两大具有高度功能分工的感光细胞：视杆细胞（rods）——对微弱光线极度敏感，专职暗光夜视与周边黑白轮廓感知；视锥细胞（cones）——仅在充足光照下工作，专职高清晰度的中央精细视觉以及红、绿、蓝全彩视觉感知。这两类光敏细胞通过光化学反应将光量子转化为电神经冲动，由视神经高速传导至大脑枕叶视觉皮层，经大脑神经中枢最终解析整合为我们所“看到”的清晰生动的视觉世界。
The interior of the eyeball contains two fluid chambers: The anterior aqueous chamber, between the cornea and the lens, is filled with aqueous humor, a clear fluid that maintains intraocular pressure and nourishes the avascular cornea and lens. Behind the lens lies the large posterior cavity filled with vitreous humor (the vitreous body), a clear, gelatinous substance that prevents the eyeball from collapsing and firmly holds the fragile retina against the choroid.
眼球内部被晶状体和睫状体分隔为两个流体腔隙：位于角膜与晶状体之间的前房与后房，其内充盈着清亮水样的房水（aqueous humor），维持恒定的眼内压并为无血管的角膜和晶状体供给必需代谢营养；在晶状体后方则为庞大的眼球后腔，其内充满无色透明蛋清样凝胶基质——玻璃体（vitreous humor/body），其流体静水压力支撑着眼球饱满圆润的立体形态，并温和而坚定地将娇嫩的视网膜平整压覆于脉络膜血管床上，防止发生视网膜脱离。

The Ear and the Sense of Hearing and Balance

听觉与前庭平衡器官：耳的解剖架构（外耳、中耳与内耳）
The ear is a sense organ that functions for both hearing and balance. The ear is divided into the external ear, middle ear, and inner ear. Ear structures are shown in FIGURE 32.19. The external ear consists of the pinna (or auricle) and the external auditory canal. Sound waves are gathered by the pinna and guided through the canal to the eardrum (tympanic membrane), which separates the external ear from the middle ear. Glands in the canal secrete cerumen (earwax), which traps foreign particles.
耳（The ear）兼具听觉换能与空间前庭平衡感知两大至关重要的感觉机能。解剖学上耳被划分为三大空间区域：外耳、中耳与内耳（解剖立体构型见图 32.19）。外耳由突出于体表的耳郭（pinna/auricle）与长约 2.5 厘米的外耳道（external auditory canal）组成。耳郭宛如天然声波收集漏斗，将空气振动声波高效汇集并导入外耳道，直达鼓膜（tympanic membrane/eardrum）。鼓膜是一层极其薄脆且富有弹性的半透明椭圆纤维薄膜，横向密封隔绝了外耳道与中耳鼓室。外耳道软骨段皮下分布着特化的耵聍腺，分泌淡黄色黏稠的耵聍（cerumen，耳屎），协同外耳道纤毛阻截外界灰尘、昆虫及微小异物侵入耳道深层。
The middle ear is a small, air-filled cavity inside the temporal bone containing three tiny bones called ossicles: (1) The malleus (hammer), (2) The incus (anvil), and (3) The stapes (stirrup). Sound waves hitting the eardrum cause it to vibrate, and the ossicles amplify these vibrations and transmit them to the oval window of the inner ear. The middle ear connects to the throat via the Eustachian tube (auditory tube), which equalizes air pressure on both sides of the eardrum.
中耳（鼓室）是深藏于颅骨颞骨内部一个充满空气的微小骨性腔隙，内部悬挂着人体骨骼系统中最小巧玲珑的三块听小骨（ossicles）：（1）锤骨（malleus，形似铁锤）；（2）砧骨（incus，宛如铁砧）；（3）镫骨（stapes，形似马鞍脚镫）。当声波撞击鼓膜引起高频物理机械振动时，三块听小骨通过精巧的微型关节联动，将鼓膜微弱的机械振动大幅放大数十倍，并将其强有力地传递推压至内耳前庭窗（卵圆窗）。此外，中耳鼓室通过一条狭长的管道——咽鼓管（Eustachian tube）与鼻咽部相连通；吞咽或打哈欠时咽鼓管被动张开，使外界气压平衡地导入鼓室，确保鼓膜内外两侧气压始终保持绝对平衡，防止鼓膜因气压骤变而发生破裂或严重内陷。
The inner ear contains the cochlea and the three semicircular canals. The cochlea is shaped like a snail shell and contains fluid and specialized hair-cell receptors (the organ of Corti). Fluid vibrations stimulate these hair cells, generating nerve impulses that travel along the acoustic (cochlear) nerve to the brain for auditory interpretation. The three fluid-filled semicircular canals lie at right angles to one another and contain receptors that detect changes in head position and movement, sending signals via the vestibular nerve to help maintain equilibrium and balance.
内耳（迷路）深嵌于颞骨岩部极其坚硬的骨质内，由螺旋状的耳蜗（cochlea）与呈三维空间直角排列的三个半规管（semicircular canals）组成。耳蜗形态宛如一个微型蜗牛壳，其内部充满内淋巴与外淋巴液体，基底膜上密布着精巧非凡的听觉感受器（柯蒂氏器/organ of Corti）毛细胞。当听小骨将振动推向卵圆窗时，内耳淋巴液产生机械流体行波，直接弯折纤毛摆动，从而将机械水动力转化为电神经冲动，由听神经（耳蜗神经）迅速传导至大脑颞叶听觉中枢，最终在大脑中被破译为优美动听的语言与声响。而三个互成 90 度直角分布的半规管与前庭器官，内部液体流动能够极其敏锐地感知头部在空间中的角加速度、旋转位移以及重力倾斜，经由前庭神经将姿态位置信息毫秒级反馈给小脑与脑干，使机体在运动或跌撞瞬间能够自发调动肌肉张力，稳稳维持身体前庭姿态平衡。

The Tongue and the Sense of Taste

舌与味觉感知（味蕾分布与脑神经支配）
The taste buds contain the receptors for taste. They are located around the small structures on the upper surface of the tongue, the soft palate, the upper esophagus, and the epiglottis. They relay sensations of different tastes to the brain for interpretation. The four basic taste sensations are salty, sweet, sour, and bitter. The receptors for each sensation are located all over the tongue. The anterior two-thirds of the tongue is controlled by the facial nerve (cranial nerve [CN] VII). The posterior third of the tongue is controlled by the glossopharyngeal nerve (CN IX).
味蕾（taste buds）是感知味觉刺激的特化化学感受器。它们主要嵌合分布于舌背表面的蕈状与轮廓乳头周围，亦有少量散布于软腭、食管上段及会厌黏膜表面。味蕾将不同化学呈味物质激发的神经冲动传导至大脑顶叶与岛叶皮层进行味觉整合破译。人类的四大经典基本味觉为咸、甜、酸、苦（现代生理学亦证实鲜味 umami）。虽然全舌各处均能感知这些味觉，但敏感度在局部存在微小差异。舌前 2/3 的味觉神经传入由面神经（第 VII 对脑神经，鼓索支）特异性支配；舌后 1/3 的味觉与一般躯体感觉则由舌咽神经（第 IX 对脑神经）统一统辖支配。

The Nose and the Sense of Smell

鼻腔与嗅觉感知（嗅神经与气味辨识）
Receptors for smell are located on the roof of the nasal cavity. These are called olfactory receptors, and the sense of smell is controlled by the olfactory nerve (CN I). These receptors are stimulated by molecules dissolved in the fluid of the mucous membrane in the nose. The impulses are sent to the brain, where they are interpreted as smell. Smell and taste are very connected. When we have a cold and our nose is congested (“stuffed”), we find that foods often do not taste the same.
嗅觉感受器密集分布于鼻腔顶部上鼻甲及鼻中隔上部的特化嗅上皮中，称为嗅感受器（olfactory receptors），嗅觉由嗅神经（第 I 对脑神经）司职支配。空气中挥发性的气味微粒分子首先溶解于鼻黏膜表面的黏液膜中，进而化学刺激嗅感觉神经元的嗅纤毛。激发的电神经冲动经筛板筛孔直通嗅球并投射至大脑边缘系统与颞叶嗅觉皮层，最终被破译为特异的气味记忆。嗅觉与味觉在神经整合上高度交织协同；当我们罹患普通感冒导致鼻腔黏膜水肿充血、严重鼻塞（“stuffed”）时，食物挥发气味无法刺激嗅感受器，我们常会感到进食时食物“食不知味”、风味大打折扣。

THE ENDOCRINE SYSTEM

内分泌系统（体液调节与化学激素网络）
The endocrine system is made up of glands called endocrine glands (FIGURE 32.20). The endocrine glands secrete chemical substances called hormones into the bloodstream. Hormones regulate the activities of other organs and glands in the body by binding to the receptor sites of their “target” tissue, similar to a key fitting in its corresponding lock. Hormones influence metabolic processes involving fat, proteins, and carbohydrates; water and electrolyte balance; cell growth and development; and regulation of the numerous chemical components in our cells and body fluids. Hormones play an important role in growth and reproduction.
内分泌系统（The endocrine system）由一系列无导管的内分泌腺（endocrine glands）及散在的内分泌细胞群组合而成（见图 32.20）。内分泌腺将其合成的高效能生物活性化学分子——激素（hormones），直接释放分泌进入紧邻的毛细血管网络与血液循环之中。激素随血流巡行全身，通过与特定靶细胞（target cells）或靶器官表面极其特异的蛋白质受体紧密结合，从而精确调节靶器官的生理代谢功能，其特异亲和机制宛如“钥匙精确匹配对应的锁孔”。激素全面调控三大营养物质（脂肪、蛋白质、碳水化合物）的中间代谢、体内水和电解质酸碱平衡、细胞分化分裂与形态发生，并维持体液化学成分的相对恒定。激素在机体的体格生长发育、性征成熟与繁衍后代中扮演着主导中枢核心角色。

The pituitary gland is also called the master gland. About the size of a cherry, it is located at the base of the brain behind the eyes. The pituitary gland is divided into the anterior pituitary lobe and the posterior pituitary lobe. The anterior pituitary lobe secretes: (1) Growth hormone (GH)—needed for the growth of muscles, bones, and other organs throughout life. (2) Thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH)—needed for thyroid gland function. (3) Adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH)—stimulates the adrenal glands. (4) Reproductive hormones (FSH and LH)—regulate growth, development, and function of the male and female reproductive systems. The posterior pituitary lobe secretes antidiuretic hormone (ADH) and oxytocin, which are produced in the hypothalamus. ADH prevents the kidneys from excreting excessive amounts of water, and oxytocin causes uterine contractions during childbirth.
脑下垂体（Pituitary gland）在内分泌学界被尊称为“主腺（master gland）”。它体积仅如一颗樱桃大小，悬垂于大脑底部的蝶骨垂体窝内。脑垂体在解剖与胚胎学上分为两大叶：垂体前叶（腺垂体）与垂体后叶（神经垂体）。垂体前叶合成分泌一系列极其关键的促激素：（1）生长激素（Growth hormone，GH）：终身促进骨骼、肌肉及内脏器官的蛋白质合成与体积增大；若婴儿先天缺乏生长激素将导致侏儒症，而过量分泌则诱发巨人症或肢端肥大症；（2）促甲状腺激素（TSH）：刺激甲状腺滤泡上皮细胞合成释放甲状腺素；（3）促肾上腺皮质激素（ACTH）：调控刺激肾上腺皮质生长与糖皮质激素分泌；（4）促性腺生殖激素（FSH 与 LH）：调节男女两性生殖器官的发育成熟、配子生成及性激素分泌。垂体后叶则负责储存并释放由下丘脑视上核与室旁核神经元合成的两种重要九肽激素：抗利尿激素（ADH，加压素，强力促进肾集合管重吸收水分、抑制水分过度随尿排出）与催产素（oxytocin，强力诱发分娩时子宫平滑肌阵发性收缩并促进产后射乳）。

The thyroid gland, shaped like a butterfly, is situated in the neck in front of the larynx and secretes two types of thyroid hormones—thyroxine (T4) and tri-iodothyronine (T3)—as well as a hormone called calcitonin. Thyroid hormones regulate metabolism, which is the burning of nutrients by the cells to produce heat and energy. The thyroid hormones T3 and T4 are responsible for the proper functioning of all other hormones, the maturation of the nervous system, and normal growth and development. Calcitonin helps to regulate calcium levels in the body by lowering the amount of calcium in the blood plasma and increasing bone calcium content. Too little of the thyroid hormones T3 and T4 results in slowed body processes, slowed movements, and weight gain (hypothyroidism). Too much of them causes increased metabolism, excess energy, and weight loss (hyperthyroidism). If a baby is born with deficient amounts of thyroid hormone, physical and mental growth will be stunted (cretinism).
甲状腺（Thyroid gland）形似一只展翅的蝴蝶，横跨位于颈前部喉与气管上段两侧。它合成分泌两类至关重要的含碘甲状腺激素——甲状腺素（T4）与三碘甲状腺原氨酸（T3），同时由滤泡旁 C 细胞分泌降钙素（calcitonin）。甲状腺激素是机体基础代谢率的“总油门”，加速全身细胞对营养素的氧化燃烧以产生热量和 ATP 能量。T3 与 T4 对胎儿及婴儿期神经系统的髓鞘化成熟、骨骼生长发育以及维持各靶器官敏感性起着决定性作用。降钙素则主要抑制破骨细胞活性、促进血钙沉淀入骨基质，从而降低血浆游离钙浓度。若体内 T3/T4 分泌严重不足（甲状腺功能减退症/甲减），案主将出现代谢迟缓、体温偏低、动作迟钝嗜睡及黏液性水肿体重暴增；反之若分泌过剩（甲亢），则导致基础代谢亢进、心悸消瘦、情绪易激惹。婴儿若先天性缺乏甲状腺素，将导致身材矮小且智力不可逆严重残疾的“呆小症（cretinism）”。
The parathyroid glands are four small collections of cells, two on each side of the thyroid gland, and they secrete parathormone (PTH). Parathormone also regulates the body’s use of calcium. Calcium is needed for proper nerve, heart, and muscle function and for the building of strong bones and teeth. Parathormone increases the level of calcium in the blood by stimulating bone breakdown and increasing calcium absorption by the kidneys and intestines. Too little parathormone causes calcium levels to drop, leading to tetany (severe muscle spasms). If untreated, tetany can cause death.
甲状旁腺（Parathyroid glands）是四个黄豆大小的微小内分泌腺体，对称嵌附在甲状腺左右两叶的后缘，专职分泌甲状旁腺激素（Parathormone，PTH）。PTH 是全身血钙浓度的“升钙总指挥”，与降钙素形成精密的负反馈拮抗平衡。钙离子对于神经元动作电位发放、心肌电生理稳定、骨骼肌兴奋-收缩偶联以及骨骼牙齿矿化坚固至关重要。当血钙偏低时，PTH 分泌激增，强力激活破骨细胞溶骨脱钙入血，并促进肾小管对钙的重吸收以及激活维生素 D 促进肠道钙吸收，从而迅速提升血钙水平。若甲状旁腺功能低下导致 PTH 严重匮乏，血清游离钙将大幅暴跌，引发全身神经肌肉兴奋性异常增高，诱发严重手足抽搐、喉痉挛窒息（tetany）；若不紧急静脉补钙纠正，喉痉挛可迅速致死。
There are two adrenal glands, one located on top of each kidney. Each adrenal gland has two parts: the adrenal cortex (outer portion) and the adrenal medulla (inner portion). The adrenal cortex secretes: (1) Glucocorticoids (such as cortisol), which regulate carbohydrate metabolism, control the inflammatory response, and help the body cope with stress. (2) Mineralocorticoids (such as aldosterone), which regulate salt and water balance by causing the kidneys to retain sodium and excrete potassium. (3) Small amounts of sex hormones. The adrenal medulla secretes epinephrine (adrenaline) and norepinephrine. These hormones stimulate the body to produce energy quickly during emergencies, increasing heart rate, blood pressure, muscle power, and alertness in the 'fight-or-flight' response.
人体拥有两个肾上腺（Adrenal glands），各自宛如一顶小帽子骑跨在左右两肾的顶端。每个肾上腺在解剖与内分泌学上截然分为表层的肾上腺皮质（adrenal cortex）与深层的肾上腺髓质（adrenal medulla）。肾上腺皮质合成分泌三大类固醇激素：（1）糖皮质激素（以皮质醇/cortisol 为主）：调节糖、脂肪与蛋白质中间代谢，抗炎抗过敏抗毒素，是机体耐受一切创伤应激的生命守护激素；（2）盐皮质激素（以醛固酮/aldosterone 为主）：作用于肾远曲小管与集合管，强力促进“保钠排钾保水”，调节细胞外液容量与长期血压稳定；（3）微量性激素（以脱氢表雄酮为主）。肾上腺髓质则属于特化的交感神经节后组织，专职合成分泌儿茶酚胺类激素——肾上腺素（epinephrine/adrenaline）与去甲肾上腺素（norepinephrine）。在遭遇急性危机应激时，交感神经电刺激促使髓质瞬时爆发性向血液释放大量肾上腺素，瞬间加快心率、强力收缩血管推高血压、扩张气管、调动肝糖原分解暴增血糖，使全身肌肉爆发力达到巅峰，完美驱动机体展开生死攸关的战斗或逃跑。
The pancreas secretes insulin and glucagon from specialized clusters of cells called the islets of Langerhans. Insulin regulates the amount of sugar in the blood by facilitating the transport of glucose from the bloodstream into body cells for energy production, thereby lowering blood sugar. Glucagon has the opposite effect, raising blood sugar by stimulating the liver to convert glycogen into glucose. If the pancreas produces too little insulin, sugar cannot enter cells and accumulates in the blood, resulting in diabetes mellitus.
胰腺（Pancreas）是兼具外分泌消化酶与内分泌激素功能的特殊混合腺体。其散布在腺泡之间的特化微型细胞岛被称为“胰岛（islets of Langerhans）”。胰岛 B 细胞（β细胞）分泌胰岛素（insulin），它是全身唯一的降血糖激素，强力促进血液中的葡萄糖跨膜转运进入骨骼肌、脂肪等组织细胞内氧化供能或合成为糖原储存，从而降低血糖水准。胰岛 A 细胞（α细胞）分泌胰高血糖素（glucagon），与胰岛素形成完美的阴阳拮抗；在禁食低血糖状态下，胰高血糖素强力激活肝糖原分解和糖异生，将糖迅速释放入血以推高血糖。若胰岛 B 细胞遭受自身免疫破坏或功能耗竭导致胰岛素分泌绝对或相对匮乏，血液中的葡萄糖无法被细胞摄取利用而大量滞留蓄积于血管中，随尿排出，即引发典型的糖尿病（diabetes mellitus）。

THE CIRCULATORY SYSTEM

循环系统（血液成分与心血管网络总论）
The circulatory system is made up of the blood, heart, and blood vessels. It is also called the cardiovascular system (cardio means 'heart', and vascular means 'blood vessel'). The circulatory system has several essential functions: (1) Transports oxygen, nutrients, and hormones to all cells. (2) Removes carbon dioxide and metabolic waste products from cells. (3) Helps regulate body temperature by distributing heat. (4) Defends the body against infection through white blood cells and antibodies. (5) Maintains normal fluid and electrolyte balance.
循环系统（The circulatory system）由流动的血液、动力泵心脏以及密闭的血管管道网络共同组装而成。它通常又被称为心血管系统（cardiovascular system，“cardio-”指心脏，“vascular”指血管）。循环系统承担着维系整个人体生命运转的五大核心使命：（1）高效将氧气、微营养素与内分泌激素运送递呈至全身每一个活细胞；（2）及时将细胞代谢产生的二氧化碳、尿素等代谢废物转运至肺、肾及皮肤排泄器官予以清除；（3）通过调节皮肤与深层血流灌注的重新分配，协助机体散发或保留热量以维系核心体温稳态；（4）通过血液中的白细胞、免疫球蛋白与补体系统，时刻抵御病原微生物的感染侵害；（5）调节并维持血管内外及各体腔之间的体液与电解质容量平衡。

Blood Composition: Formed Elements and Plasma

血液组成：有形成分（红细胞、白细胞、血小板）与血浆
Blood consists of blood cells (formed elements) suspended in a liquid called plasma. An average adult has about 5 litres (approx. 5 quarts) of blood. Plasma is about 90% water and carries blood cells, nutrients, proteins (albumin, globulins, fibrinogen), electrolytes, hormones, and waste products. The formed elements consist of three types of cells: (1) Red blood cells (RBCs, or erythrocytes)—contain hemoglobin, which binds to oxygen in the lungs and releases it to body cells. Hemoglobin gives blood its red colour. RBCs live about 120 days and are continuously replaced by new cells formed in red bone marrow. (2) White blood cells (WBCs, or leukocytes)—protect the body against infections by destroying pathogens through phagocytosis and antibody production. WBC numbers increase dramatically during an active infection. (3) Platelets (thrombocytes)—essential for blood clotting (coagulation), adhering to damaged blood vessel walls to form a plug and prevent fatal blood loss.
血液（Blood）是一种特殊的液体结缔组织，由悬浮于淡黄色清亮液体基质——血浆（plasma）中的血细胞有形成分（formed elements）共同构成。正常成年人体内全血总量约为 5 升（约占体重的 7% 至 8%）。血浆中 90% 以上为水分，其余 10% 溶解着血浆白蛋白、球蛋白、纤维蛋白原、电解质、无机盐、营养素（葡萄糖、氨基酸）、激素以及尿素等代谢废物。血液的有形成分细分为三大特化细胞家族：（1）红细胞（Red blood cells，RBCs，红细胞/erythrocytes）：双凹圆盘状，无细胞核，胞内充满高浓度的血红蛋白（hemoglobin）。血红蛋白在肺泡毛细血管与氧气特异性结合，并运送至全身微循环释放供组织细胞利用；血红蛋白与氧结合后呈现鲜红色，缺氧时则呈暗紫色。红细胞平均生命周期约为 120 天，衰老红细胞在脾脏和肝脏被巨噬细胞破坏降解，同时由红骨髓造血干细胞每日源源不断合成补充。（2）白细胞（White blood cells，WBCs，白细胞/leukocytes）：具有完整细胞核，是人体巡逻卫士与免疫部队；通过阿米巴样运动穿出毛细血管壁，通过吞噬消化入侵病原菌（吞噬细胞）以及合成特异性抗体来彻底消灭外来感染源。当机体发生急性细菌或病毒感染时，外周血白细胞总数会迅速激增。（3）血小板（Platelets，血小板/thrombocytes）：是由骨髓巨核细胞胞质脱落形成的微小双凸圆盘状无核胞质碎片；当血管内皮破损受创时，血小板迅速黏附聚集于创口形成临时止血栓，并释放促凝血因子启动凝血瀑布反应使血液凝固结痂，杜绝致命性大出血失血。

The Heart: Structure, Chambers, and Valves

心脏解剖：三层心壁结构、四腔室与四对单向瓣膜
The heart is a hollow, muscular organ that pumps blood throughout the circulatory system. It is about the size of a fist and lies in the chest cavity between the lungs, tilted slightly toward the left side behind the sternum (FIGURE 32.22). The heart wall has three distinct layers: (1) The pericardium—the outer protective fibroserous sac that surrounds the heart. It contains a small amount of lubricating pericardial fluid that prevents friction during heartbeats. (2) The myocardium—the thick, middle layer made of involuntary, branching cardiac muscle that contracts forcefully to pump blood. (3) The endocardium—the smooth, thin inner epithelial lining of the heart's chambers and valves, allowing blood to flow smoothly without resistance or clot formation.
心脏（The heart）是一个中空的强力肌肉泵，昼夜不息地通过节律性舒缩驱动血液在整个心血管网络中周而复始流动。成年人心脏体积约如本人紧握的拳头大小，坐落于胸腔中部两侧肺脏之间的纵隔内，正后方靠近胸骨，心尖微向左下方倾斜（见图 32.22）。心脏心壁在解剖上由三层致密结构精细构筑而成：（1）心包（Pericardium）：包裹在心脏外面的双层浆膜纤维囊套，内含微量澄清的心包润滑液，在心脏每分钟数十上百次的剧烈搏动中彻底消解摩擦阻力；（2）心肌层（Myocardium）：构成心壁主体的极厚中层心肌组织，由特化分支互联的心肌纤维编织而成，其收缩产生的强大静水压是推动血液循环的根本动力；（3）心内膜（Endocardium）：衬覆于心脏各个心室内表面及心瓣膜表面的极其光滑平整的薄层内皮结缔组织，使心腔内血液能够高速顺畅滑行而不发生涡流湍流或异常血栓形成。

The heart contains four hollow chambers (see FIGURE 32.23): The upper two receiving chambers are the right atrium and the left atrium. The lower two powerful pumping chambers are the right ventricle and the left ventricle. Deoxygenated blood from the body enters the right atrium via the superior and inferior vena cava. The right ventricle pumps this venous blood to the lungs through the pulmonary artery. Oxygenated blood from the lungs returns to the left atrium through the four pulmonary veins. The left ventricle, having the thickest myocardium, pumps this oxygen-rich blood into the aorta to supply the entire body.
心脏内部被室间隔与房间隔严密分隔为四个完全不相通的中空腔室（见图 32.23）：上方两个收集静脉回流血液的薄壁腔室为右心房（right atrium）与左心房（left atrium）；下方两个负责强力射血的厚壁肌肉腔室为右心室（right ventricle）与左心室（left ventricle）。来自全身外周各组织的缺氧静脉血经由上腔静脉与下腔静脉汇流注入右心房；随后右心室收缩将这些暗红色的静脉血泵入肺动脉干，送达肺脏进行气体交换；在肺泡微循环完成氧合的鲜红动脉血，经由 4 根肺静脉回流注入左心房；左心室拥有全心脏最肥厚（约为右室三倍）的心肌壁，收缩时产生高达 120 mmHg 以上的强大射血压，将富氧血液射入主动脉弓，推动体循环源源不断灌注滋养全身每一个器官。
Four one-way valves keep blood flowing in only one direction and prevent backflow: (1) Tricuspid valve—between the right atrium and right ventricle. (2) Mitral valve (bicuspid valve)—between the left atrium and left ventricle. (3) Pulmonary valve—between the right ventricle and pulmonary artery. (4) Aortic valve—between the left ventricle and aorta.
心脏内部装置有四对单向开闭的结缔组织心瓣膜（valves），确保血液只能单向单行流动而绝对杜绝发生病理性逆流：（1）三尖瓣（Tricuspid valve）：位于右心房与右心室之间的房室口；（2）二尖瓣（Mitral/bicuspid valve）：位于左心房与左心室之间的房室口；（3）肺动脉瓣（Pulmonary semilunar valve）：位于右心室与肺动脉干开口处；（4）主动脉瓣（Aortic semilunar valve）：位于左心室与主动脉主干开口处。

The Cardiac Cycle: Diastole and Systole

心动周期动力学：舒张期充盈与收缩期射血
There are two phases of heart action. During diastole, the resting and filling phase, the heart muscle relaxes, allowing the chambers to fill with blood. During systole, the contracting and pumping phase, the heart muscle contracts forcefully, expelling blood out of the ventricles into the pulmonary artery and the aorta. The valves open and close with precise timing to produce the familiar 'lub-dub' heart sounds.
心脏每次搏动所经历的机械活动周期称为心动周期，精细分为两大交替时相：舒张期（diastole）为静息充盈期，此时心室肌完全松弛舒张，心室内压骤降，房室瓣顺畅敞开，左右心室被动与主动大量充盈来自心房的血液；收缩期（systole）为主动射血期，心室肌发生强有力的爆发性等容与快速收缩，室内压瞬间暴增迫使房室瓣严密关闭、半月瓣强力冲开，将血液喷射涌入肺动脉干与主动脉中。四对瓣膜伴随心室收缩与舒张的交替关闭，撞击血流产生临床听诊器中标志性的“怦-哒（lub-dub）”第一心音与第二心音。

Blood Vessels: Arteries, Capillaries, and Veins

血管解剖网络：动脉、小动脉、毛细血管、微静脉与大静脉
Blood flows through three main types of blood vessels: (1) Arteries—carry blood away from the heart. They have thick, muscular, elastic walls that expand and contract as blood surges through under high pressure. The largest artery in the body is the aorta. Arteries branch into smaller vessels called arterioles. (2) Capillaries—the microscopic, thin-walled vessels connecting arterioles and venules. Formed of a single layer of endothelial cells, capillaries allow oxygen and nutrients to diffuse out of the blood into tissues and carbon dioxide and waste to diffuse in. (3) Veins—carry deoxygenated blood back toward the heart. Veins have thinner walls and less muscle than arteries. Because venous pressure is very low, veins contain one-way valves that prevent blood from pooling or flowing backward against gravity. Small venules unite to form larger veins, culminating in the superior vena cava and inferior vena cava (FIGURE 32.24).
血液在由三大类功能血管连缀构成的完全封闭管网中循环流动：（1）动脉（Arteries）：将血液由心室射出运离心脏。动脉管壁极厚，由平滑肌与多层弹性纤维致密编织而成，能够耐受并传导心脏射血时的高压脉冲脉搏波。全身口径最大、承受压力最高的主干动脉是主动脉（aorta）。动脉在进入外周器官前反复分支为管径微小的阻力血管——小动脉（arterioles）。（2）毛细血管（Capillaries）：连接微动脉与微静脉的超微细血管网，管径微小仅容红细胞单列通过，管壁由极其薄脆的单层血管内皮细胞构成。它是血液与周围组织细胞进行物质交换的绝对中枢枢纽：富集的氧气与营养素顺浓度梯度跨壁弥散入组织间隙，而细胞排出的二氧化碳与代谢废物则高效弥散吸收入血。（3）静脉（Veins）：负责将完成物质交换后的暗红色静脉血逆重力回流引导回心脏。由于静脉血压极低，静脉管壁较动脉菲薄且缺乏强力弹性平滑肌；为了克服地心引力防止血液下坠淤滞，中大静脉管腔内密布成对的半月状单向静脉瓣（valves）。微细的微静脉（venules）引流毛细血管网血液，逐级汇聚成粗大静脉，最终汇入直通右心房的上腔静脉（收集头颈上肢血液）与下腔静脉（收集下肢及腹盆腔血液）（见图 32.24）。

Pulse and Blood Pressure

外周动脉脉搏与动脉血压生理机制
When the left ventricle contracts, it pumps a surge of blood into the aorta, producing a wave of expansion that travels along the elastic walls of all arteries. This rhythmic wave of pressure can be felt at certain points where an artery lies close to the skin and over a bone, such as at the radial artery in the wrist or the carotid artery in the neck; this is the pulse. Blood pressure is the force exerted by the circulating blood against the inner walls of the arteries. It is measured in millimetres of mercury (mm Hg) and expressed as two numbers: systolic pressure (the peak pressure during ventricular contraction) over diastolic pressure (the minimum baseline pressure during ventricular relaxation). Normal adult blood pressure is typically less than 120/80 mm Hg.
当左心室强力收缩射血时，一股高压血流涌入主动脉，沿着全身动脉弹性管壁传导出一阵波浪形扩张搏动波。在浅表动脉跨越骨骼隆起的特定解剖部位（如手腕外侧的桡动脉、颈侧部的颈总动脉、腹股沟韧带下方的股动脉），将手指指腹轻置其上即可清晰感知到规律跳动的机械节律，即为脉搏（pulse）。血压（Blood pressure）是指血液在动脉血管管腔内流动时对单位面积血管内壁产生的侧压力。临床上以毫米汞柱（mm Hg）为统一计量标准，记录为收缩压（高压，心室收缩泵血时的巅峰峰值压力）除以舒张压（低压，心室舒张期血管回缩维持的基线最低压力）。健康成人在安静静息状态下的典型理想血压通常低于 120/80 mm Hg。

THE LYMPHATIC SYSTEM

淋巴系统（组织液回流与外周免疫滤过）
The lymphatic system is a specialized subsystem of the circulatory and immune systems. It consists of lymph fluid, lymphatic vessels, lymph nodes, and lymphoid organs (the spleen, thymus gland, and tonsils). As blood circulates through tissue capillaries, fluid and small proteins continuously filter out into the interstitial spaces between cells to nourish them. Most of this fluid returns directly to blood capillaries, but the excess fluid (interstitial fluid) enters microscopic, blind-ended lymphatic capillaries, where it is called lymph.
淋巴系统（The lymphatic system）是循环系统极其重要的辅助回流通路，亦是人体特异性免疫防御网络的核心构架。它由澄清透亮的淋巴液（lymph）、单向引流的淋巴管道、串珠样排列的淋巴结（lymph nodes）以及重要的淋巴器官（脾脏、胸腺、扁桃体）共同构筑。当动脉血流经毛细血管床时，在毛细血管流体静水压驱动下，血浆中的水分与小分子溶质不断滤过渗透进入细胞间隙，形成组织间隙液（interstitial fluid）以滋养细胞。约 90% 的组织液通过微静脉端毛细血管重新吸收入血，而剩余约 10% 富含大分子蛋白质与异物的组织液，则被毛细淋巴管盲端单向吸收引流，进入淋巴管内即改称为淋巴液（淋巴）。
Lymphatic vessels transport lymph through successively larger ducts until it empties back into the venous bloodstream near the heart via the thoracic duct and right lymphatic duct. Along the path of lymphatic vessels are bean-shaped structures called lymph nodes (FIGURE 32.25). Lymph nodes filter lymph as it passes through, trapping bacteria, viruses, cancer cells, and other harmful debris. Inside the nodes, specialized white blood cells (macrophages and lymphocytes) destroy these trapped foreign substances. Lymph nodes are clustered in areas such as the neck (cervical), armpits (axillary), and groin (inguinal). During an infection, lymph nodes often become swollen and tender as lymphocytes multiply rapidly to fight pathogens.
淋巴管道由毛细淋巴管、淋巴管逐级汇集成淋巴干，最终通过粗大的胸导管（收纳全身 3/4 淋巴）与右淋巴导管，将净化后的淋巴液完全倾倒入锁骨下静脉与颈内静脉交汇的静脉角，重返全身血液循环。在淋巴管循行必经通路上，星罗棋布着数以百计蚕豆大小的微型结缔组织滤器——淋巴结（见图 32.25）。淋巴液流经淋巴窦时流速放缓，淋巴结宛如多层精密滤网，机械性拦截细菌、病毒、恶性肿瘤转移细胞及细胞碎片碎屑。淋巴结小梁间密布着巨噬细胞与淋巴细胞，通过活跃的吞噬作用与抗体攻击将截留的致病抗原当场消灭。全身淋巴结在颈部（颈深淋巴结）、腋窝（腋窝淋巴结）、腹股沟（腹股沟淋巴结）以及纵隔与肠系膜呈群聚分布。当邻近组织遭受细菌或病毒急性感染时，相应引流区域的淋巴细胞迅速受刺激爆发性增殖抗敌，常导致淋巴结出现特征性的炎性肿大、坚硬与压痛。
The spleen is the largest lymphatic organ, located in the upper left quadrant of the abdomen behind the stomach. The spleen filters blood rather than lymph, destroying old or damaged red blood cells, recycling iron from hemoglobin, storing reserves of platelets and blood, and producing lymphocytes. The thymus gland, located behind the sternum in the upper chest, produces hormones that promote the maturation and differentiation of T lymphocytes (T cells).
脾脏（Spleen）是人体最大的外周淋巴器官，安居在左上腹部胃后方的左季肋区深处。与淋巴结不同，脾脏是一个专门滤过“血液”的巨大血库和免疫滤器：其红髓巨噬细胞极其敏锐地吞噬清除循环中衰老、破损或畸形的红细胞，高效回收利用血红蛋白中的铁离子，并在平素储存大量储备血液与血小板；其白髓富含淋巴小结，源源不断产生致敏淋巴细胞与特异性抗体。胸腺（Thymus gland）位于胸骨后方、心脏大血管前方的上纵隔内；它是机体极其核心的初级中枢淋巴器官，合成分泌胸腺肽等多种激素，指导诱导骨髓来源的原始前体 T 细胞在此分化、成熟与筛选为具备特异免疫应答能力的成熟 T 淋巴细胞（T cells）。

THE IMMUNE SYSTEM

免疫系统（特异与非特异性防御三重防线）
The immune system defends the body against internal and external threats, including bacteria, viruses, fungi, parasites, toxins, and our own cells that have become abnormal (cancer). Immunity means that a person has protection against a disease or condition—the person will not contract or be affected by the disease. There are two types of defence mechanisms, or immunity, classified as nonspecific immunity and specific immunity.
免疫系统（The immune system）是保卫机体生命安全的国防军，时刻严密抵御来自外部与内部的一切生物与病理威胁，包括病原性细菌、病毒、致病真菌、寄生虫、外毒素，以及机体内部自身发生衰老畸变或突变恶变的肿瘤癌细胞。免疫力（Immunity）意味着个体获得了抵御特定疾病或致病因子的强固保护屏障——在遭遇病原体侵袭时不会罹患该病或不致产生病理损害。人体的全部免疫防御体系在机制上精准划分为两大防御范式：非特异性免疫（天然先天性免疫）与特异性免疫（获得后天性免疫）。

Nonspecific Immunity: First and Second Lines of Defence

非特异性免疫：机体第一与第二道防御防线
Nonspecific immunity is the body’s innate reaction to anything it does not recognize as a normal body substance, acting against all foreign invaders indiscriminately without requiring prior exposure. Nonspecific immunity consists of two lines of defence: The first line of defence includes mechanical barriers (intact skin and mucous membranes), chemical barriers (tears, stomach acids, saliva, perspiration), and protective reflexes (coughing, sneezing, vomiting, and diarrhea). The second line of defence includes phagocytes (white blood cells, like neutrophils and macrophages, that ingest and digest invaders), inflammation (the release of histamine that dilates blood vessels, causing redness, heat, swelling, and recruitment of immune cells), and fever (which stimulates phagocytosis and inhibits bacterial reproduction).
非特异性免疫（Nonspecific/Innate immunity）是机体先天固有的泛化防御反应，对一切未被机体识别为正常自身成分的外来异物均迅速发起无差别攻击，既无需事先致敏接触，也不针对特定抗原决定簇。它构建起坚固的两重天然防线：
机体第一道防线为物理与化学屏障构成的体表防线：机械屏障（完好无损的表皮角质层与湿润的黏膜上皮）、化学屏障（泪液中的溶菌酶、胃液中强酸性的胃酸、唾液、汗液中的弱酸与抑菌肽）以及物理排异防御反射（剧烈咳嗽、打喷嚏、呕吐及保护性腹泻排毒）。
机体第二道防线为体液与细胞级防御：吞噬细胞（中性粒细胞与单核-巨噬细胞，通过趋化游走吞噬并消化病原体与异物碎屑）、炎症反应（受损或受刺激肥大细胞释放组胺，诱发局部微血管强烈扩张、血流剧增充血发红发热，并增加毛细血管通透性渗出富含抗体和补体的炎性渗出液导致局部水肿并招募吞噬部队）以及保护性发热（发热致热原刺激下丘脑调定点上移，适度发热不仅大幅激活吞噬细胞酶活性，而且直接恶化抑制多种致病菌与病毒的复制繁殖条件）。

Specific Immunity: The Third Line of Defence

特异性免疫：第三道防线（体液免疫与细胞免疫）
Specific immunity (adaptive immunity) is the body's third line of defence, capable of recognizing, attacking, and remembering specific antigens. An antigen is a foreign substance (usually a foreign protein on a virus, bacterium, or pollen) that stimulates an immune response. An antibody is an immunoglobulin protein synthesized by plasma cells in response to a specific antigen, designed to bind to that antigen and neutralize or destroy it. Specific immunity is mediated by two types of lymphocytes: (1) B lymphocytes (B cells)—responsible for humoral immunity. When exposed to an antigen, B cells proliferate into plasma cells that secrete circulating antibodies into the blood and lymph. Some B cells become memory B cells. (2) T lymphocytes (T cells)—responsible for cell-mediated immunity. Cytotoxic (killer) T cells directly destroy virus-infected or cancerous cells by releasing perforins and cytotoxins. Helper T cells release cytokines that coordinate the entire immune response, and regulatory T cells suppress the response once the threat is resolved.
特异性免疫（Specific/Adaptive immunity）构筑了机体最高阶的第三道国防防线，具备精准识别抗原分子表位、特异性定向歼敌以及长久终身免疫记忆三大绝佳特征。抗原（Antigen）是指凡能被 T/B 淋巴细胞表面受体特异性识别、并激发机体产生免疫应答的任何外来异物大分子（通常是病原微生物表面的特化外被蛋白质或多糖）。抗体（Antibody）是由成熟 B 细胞转化的浆细胞专职合成分泌的免疫球蛋白（IgG、IgA、IgM、IgD、IgE），其立体构象与激发它的特异性抗原决定簇精准契合嵌合，通过中和毒素、凝集沉淀或激活补体瀑布反应彻底消灭抗原。特异性免疫由两大特化淋巴细胞部队统辖协同：（1）B 淋巴细胞（B cells）：介导体液免疫（humoral immunity）；在抗原刺激与辅助 T 细胞协助下活化增殖为浆细胞，向血液与组织液释放数以亿计的游离抗体，并分化出长寿记忆 B 细胞；（2）T 淋巴细胞（T cells）：介导细胞免疫（cell-mediated immunity）；细胞毒性 T 细胞（杀手 T 细胞）能够紧贴受病毒感染的靶细胞或恶性癌细胞，释放穿孔素与颗粒酶直接溶解炸毁靶细胞；辅助性 T 细胞（Helper T cells）释放大量细胞因子指挥调动全身免疫部队；调节性 T 细胞则在抗原彻底清除后负责下调免疫反应，严防自身免疫组织破坏。

THE RESPIRATORY SYSTEM

呼吸系统（通气通道与肺换气总论）
The respiratory system brings oxygen into the lungs and eliminates carbon dioxide from the body (see FIGURE 32.26). Air enters the body through the nose and passes into the pharynx (throat), which serves as a common passageway for both air and food. Air then enters the larynx (voice box), which lies between the pharynx and the trachea. The larynx contains the vocal cords and the thyroid cartilage (Adam’s apple). During swallowing, a cartilaginous flap called the epiglottis drops down like a lid over the larynx to prevent food and liquids from entering the airway. From the larynx, air passes into the trachea (windpipe), a tube about 11 cm (4.5 in.) long kept open by C-shaped rings of cartilage that prevent it from collapsing.
呼吸系统（The respiratory system）负责将富含氧气的新鲜外界空气吸入肺泡，并通过排气将机体代谢产生的废气二氧化碳彻底排出体外（解剖全景见图 32.26）。外界空气首先经由鼻孔吸入鼻腔；鼻腔黏膜内密布毛细血管网与黏液纤毛，对吸入空气进行充分加温、加湿与除尘过滤。随后气流进入咽部（pharynx，咽喉）；咽是空气与食物共同穿经的解剖交叉通道。气流穿过喉口进入喉（larynx，声门喉部）；喉由软骨支架包绕，内部架设着声带（vocal cords），呼出气流震动声带产生人类语言声响，其前方突起的甲状软骨在男性体表显现为明显的“喉结（Adam's apple）”。在每一次吞咽食物瞬间，一片形似树叶的弹性软骨薄片——会厌（epiglottis），会如阀门盖板般自发向下严密盖扣在喉口上方，绝对杜绝食物或水误吸滑入气道深层。气流由喉向下涌入气管（trachea，风管）；气管长约 11 厘米，其管壁由 16 至 20 个呈“C”字形的半环状透明软骨环紧密支撑，确保气管无论在任何体位和负压下始终保持管腔畅通无阻、绝不发生塌陷闭合。

Classification of Acquired Immunity

后天获得性免疫四大分类机制
Immunity to diseases is classified as genetic immunity and acquired immunity (see FIGURE 32.27). Genetic immunity (species immunity) is innate and protects a member of one species from diseases that afflict other species. Acquired immunity is obtained either naturally or artificially: (1) Naturally acquired active immunity—results when a person contracts a disease and the body's immune system actively produces its own antibodies and memory cells, often providing life-long protection (e.g., catching chickenpox). (2) Naturally acquired passive immunity—occurs when preformed maternal antibodies pass across the placenta to the fetus or through breast milk (colostrum) to the infant, providing temporary immunity lasting about 6 months. (3) Artificially acquired active immunity—results from immunization with a vaccine, which contains killed or weakened pathogens or antigens that stimulate antibody production without causing the full-blown disease. (4) Artificially acquired passive immunity—results from the injection of preformed antibodies (immune globulin or antitoxin) from an immune donor, providing immediate but short-lived protection.
机体抵御感染性疾病的免疫力系统划分为种属遗传免疫与后天获得性免疫两大类（见图 32.27）。种属遗传免疫是机体天生具备的物种天然屏障，使某物种个体完全免疫其他物种特有的传染病（例如人类不会感染某些禽类或犬科特异性病毒）。后天获得性免疫则根据抗原接触与抗体来源细分为四种形式：（1）自然主动获得性免疫：个体在日常生活中自然罹患某种疾病（如水痘、麻疹），体内免疫系统被强力激活，主动产生大量高亲和力特异性抗体与长寿记忆细胞，通常赋予个体数十年甚至终身的免疫力；（2）自然被动获得性免疫：母体血清中的 IgG 抗体经胎盘主动转运入胎儿体内，以及新生儿摄入初乳中富含的高浓度分泌型 IgA 抗体，婴儿由此被动获得来自母亲的即刻保护，时效通常维持在出生后最初约 6 个月；（3）人工主动获得性免疫：通过人工接种灭活疫苗、减毒活疫苗或重组亚单位抗原，模拟感染过程激发机体自身免疫系统主动生成抗体与记忆细胞，而不引发严重临床疾病（如接种脊髓灰质炎、乙肝、流感疫苗）；（4）人工被动获得性免疫：对遭遇破伤风感染、狂犬病毒暴露或严重免疫缺陷案主，直接静脉或肌内注射从免疫供体血清中分离提纯的外源性现成抗体（丙种免疫球蛋白、抗毒素血清），从而使机体在数小时内获得即刻的紧急中和抗体保护，但由于体内未产生自身记忆细胞，其保护期通常仅能维持数周。

Lower Airways and Alveolar Gas Exchange

下呼吸道支气管树与肺泡毛细血管网气体交换
The trachea divides into the right main bronchus and left main bronchus at its lower end. The right main bronchus is shorter, wider, and more vertical than the left bronchus, making it the more common site for inhaled foreign objects. Each bronchus enters its lung and branches repeatedly into secondary bronchi, tertiary bronchi, and smaller bronchioles, forming the bronchial tree. Bronchioles terminate in microscopic alveolar ducts leading into alveolar sacs, which are clusters of tiny air sacs called alveoli.
气管在胸骨角水平向下分叉为左、右两大主支气管（main bronchi）。在解剖构型上，右主支气管比左主支气管更为粗短、管径更大且走向更陡直（几乎是气管纵轴的直接自然延伸）；因此当老人或儿童发生误吸时，异物落入右侧肺脏主支气管的概率远高于左侧。两大主支气管分别经肺门穿入左、右肺实质，随后如同茂密树冠般逐级反复分支为叶支气管、段支气管以及管径小于 1 毫米的细支气管（bronchioles），构成高度复杂的支气管树（bronchial tree）。细支气管最末端向下通达微观的肺泡管与肺泡囊，最终汇入成串葡萄样紧密镶嵌的微小肺泡（alveoli）。正常人体两肺拥有超过 3 亿至 5 亿个微小肺泡，其总表面积高达近 100 平方米（约如网球场大小），为肺泡气体与血液毛细血管网之间的气体分子弥散交换提供了极其浩瀚的微观反应接触面。

THE DIGESTIVE SYSTEM

消化系统（消化道解剖构架与机械蠕动）
The digestive system, also called the gastrointestinal system (GI system), breaks down nutrients physically and chemically so that they can be absorbed for use by cells—a process called digestion—and also eliminates solid wastes from the body. The digestive system consists of the alimentary canal (GI tract) and the accessory organs of digestion (FIGURE 32.28). The alimentary canal is a long tube extending from the mouth to the anus. Its major parts are the mouth, the pharynx, the esophagus, the stomach, the small intestine, and the large intestine. The accessory organs of digestion are the teeth, tongue, salivary glands, liver, gallbladder, and pancreas.
消化系统（The digestive system，亦称胃肠系统/GI系统）司职通过机械物理磨碎与化学酶促水解双重方式，将食物分解为微小分子营养素，使之能够穿透肠黏膜被机体细胞吸收利用（此全过程称为消化/digestion），同时将未消化的食物残渣与固体废物形成粪便排出体外。消化系统在解剖上由两大核心部分组成：消化道管腔（alimentary canal/GI tract）与消化辅助器官（见图 32.28）。消化道是一条长达约 9 米（30 英尺）、从口腔一直向下贯穿延伸至肛门的连续中空肌肉管道，其主要组成段落包括口腔、咽、食管、胃、小肠与大肠。消化辅助器官则包括牙齿、舌、唾液腺、肝脏、胆囊以及胰腺。

Structure of the Digestive Tract Wall

消化管壁三层微观组织构架
Although modified for specific functions in different organs, the wall of the digestive tract has a similar structure throughout its length: (1) The innermost layer is the mucosa, which contains glands (which secrete mucus, digestive enzymes, and hormones) and ducts. (2) The middle layer is the submucosa, composed of loose connective tissue containing blood vessels, nerves, glands, and lymphatic vessels. (3) The third and most superficial layer is the muscularis (muscle layer), consisting of two layers of smooth muscle (inner circular and outer longitudinal layers). These muscles perform two types of movements: rhythmic churning in the stomach to mix food with juices mechanically, and peristalsis—involuntary smooth-muscle contractions that push food forward in the digestive tract in one direction in a wavelike motion, similar to squeezing toothpaste from a tube.
尽管在不同解剖器官部位为了适应特定功能而有所特化，但整条消化管壁在全长范围内均遵循高度一致的三层同心圆组织学分层构筑：（1）内层（黏膜层/mucosa）：衬覆于管腔内表面，内含大量分泌保护性黏液、消化酶及局部胃肠激素的黏膜腺体及排泄导管；（2）中层（黏膜下层/submucosa）：由疏松结缔组织构成，密布着为管壁提供血液循环滋养的毛细血管网、淋巴管及调节黏膜分泌与血流的黏膜下神经丛（Meissner's丛）；（3）外层（肌层/muscularis）：主体由内环行、外纵行两层平滑肌紧密排列而成。平滑肌层司职执行两类协助消化的关键机械动力运动：第一种是胃壁平滑肌反复交替的节律性收缩舒张研磨，以类似“晃荡/回荡（swishing）”的往返机械剪切力将食糜与胃酸胃蛋白酶强力混匀磨碎；第二种称为“蠕动（peristalsis）”，即平滑肌发生不受意志支配的自发波浪形推进收缩，宛如挤压牙膏管一般，将食团或食糜坚定地沿着消化道单向向前推进。

The Process of Digestion: Mouth to Stomach

消化分解流程：口腔咀嚼、唾液酶解与胃内食糜研磨
Food enters the mouth (oral cavity), where digestion begins with mastication (chewing). Food is bitten, positioned by the tongue and cheeks, and crushed by the teeth to increase its surface area, which is mechanical digestion. Taste and smell stimulate the salivary glands to secrete saliva, which is 99% water plus digestive enzymes, mucus, electrolytes, and antibacterial substances. Saliva moistens food into a slippery ball called a bolus. Salivary amylase begins the chemical digestion of carbohydrates. During swallowing (deglutition), the tongue pushes the bolus into the pharynx, which contracts to push it into the esophagus. The esophagus, a muscular tube about 25 cm (10 in.) long, uses peristalsis to move the bolus down into the stomach through the cardiac sphincter.
食物首先摄入口腔（oral cavity）。消化的第一步始于咀嚼（mastication）。在咀嚼过程中，食物被牙齿切断咬碎，经舌与颊肌协同搅拌，被臼齿充分碾压研磨成微小碎末，从而数十倍增大其有效表面积，此即机械性消化。味觉与嗅觉反射性刺激三大对唾液腺（腮腺、下颌下腺、舌下腺）大量分泌唾液（saliva）。唾液 99% 为水，其余 1% 溶解着黏液、电解质、溶菌酶以及消化酶。唾液浸润湿润食物并将其揉捏包裹成易于吞咽的润滑食团（bolus）；唾液淀粉酶（salivary amylase）迅速启动对淀粉等复杂多糖的初步化学性水解消化。在吞咽反射中，舌根向后上方推挤食团滑入咽腔，咽壁缩肌收缩将其挤入食管。食管（esophagus）是一条长约 25 厘米（10 英寸）的光滑肌肉管道，其强有力的蠕动波在数秒之内将食团向下推送，经由食管下括约肌（贲门括约肌/cardiac sphincter）单向松弛排入胃内。
The stomach is a muscular, J-shaped pouch in the upper left portion of the abdominal cavity. Strong stomach muscles churn food into smaller particles. Gastric glands secrete gastric juice containing hydrochloric acid (which kills microbes and activates enzymes) and pepsin (which begins protein digestion). The partially digested food churns into a semi-liquid mixture called chyme. Mucus and bicarbonate secreted by epithelial cells protect the stomach wall from self-digestion. Chyme is released in small squirts through the pyloric sphincter into the small intestine.
胃（The stomach）是位于左上腹部的一个呈“J”字形的可膨胀肌性囊袋。胃壁强大的三层平滑肌（纵行、环行与斜行肌）展开剧烈的研磨搅拌与翻转运动。胃黏膜胃腺合成分泌强酸性的胃液（gastric juice），内含盐酸（强酸杀灭食物中绝大多数病原微生物，并激活胃蛋白酶原）与胃蛋白酶（pepsin，启动对食物中大分子蛋白质的肽键水解）。经过胃内数小时充分研磨与消化酶作用，食团完全被分解转化为呈均质半流体糊状的食糜（chyme）。同时，胃黏膜表皮细胞分泌富含重碳酸盐的浓稠黏液胶冻层，紧密贴附在胃壁上，构筑起不可逾越的“胃黏膜屏障”，严防强酸与胃蛋白酶对自身胃壁造成化学性“自我消化”溃疡。食糜成熟后，幽门括约肌（pyloric sphincter）节律性阵发性松弛开放，将少量食糜分批喷射喷入十二指肠。

The Small Intestine and Nutrient Absorption

小肠：十二指肠、空肠、回肠与绒毛吸收中枢
The small intestine is about 6 metres (20 feet) long and consists of three parts: the duodenum, the jejunum, and the ileum. In the duodenum, chyme mixes with bile (produced by the liver and stored in the gallbladder, which emulsifies fats) and pancreatic juice (containing digestive enzymes for proteins, carbohydrates, and fats). The small intestine lining is covered with millions of tiny, finger-like projections called villi, which increase the absorptive surface area tremendously. Each villus contains blood capillaries and a lymphatic vessel called a lacteal. Nutrients (amino acids, simple sugars, vitamins, minerals, water) are absorbed through the villi into the bloodstream, while digested fats enter the lacteals.
小肠（Small intestine）全长约 6 米（20 英尺），是消化吸收全过程中最为至关重要的核心战场，在解剖上顺次分为三段：十二指肠（duodenum）、空肠（jejunum）与回肠（ileum）。在十二指肠降段，食糜与肝脏合成、胆囊浓缩储存排出的碱性胆汁（bile，强力乳化脂肪使微滴化），以及胰腺外分泌的高浓度胰液（pancreatic juice，内含胰淀粉酶、胰蛋白酶、胰脂肪酶）充分混匀，将食糜中的各类营养大分子彻底水解为可吸收的终末产物。小肠内黏膜表面密布数以百万计的微观指状突起——小肠绒毛（villi），使小肠内吸收表面积暴增数百倍。每一根绒毛内部均穿行着丰富的微血管网和一条特化的中央乳糜管（lacteal，毛细淋巴管）。水溶性微营养素（单糖葡萄糖、氨基酸、水溶性维生素、无机盐电解质及水分）直接跨膜吸收入毛细血管汇入肝门静脉系统；而脂溶性长链脂肪酸与甘油三酯则被包装成乳糜微粒，主动吸收入中央乳糜淋巴管回流全身。

The Large Intestine (Colon)

大肠（结肠水分回收与粪便排泄）
Undigested chyme passes from the small intestine through the ileocecal valve into the large intestine (colon). The large intestine is about 1.5 metres (5 feet) long and consists of the cecum (with the appendix), ascending colon, transverse colon, descending colon, sigmoid colon, rectum, and anus. The large intestine does not secrete digestive enzymes. Its primary functions are the absorption of water, sodium, and electrolytes, and the compacting of indigestible food residues into solid feces. Normal gut bacteria synthesize vitamin K and certain B vitamins, which are absorbed into the blood. Feces pass into the rectum and are eliminated from the body through the anus via defecation.
未被消化吸收的食糜残渣经回盲瓣单向排入大肠（large intestine/colon）。大肠管径粗大，全长约 1.5 米（5 英尺），解剖上由盲肠（附着盲管状阑尾/appendix）、升结肠、横结肠、降结肠、乙状结肠、直肠及肛管肛门顺次组成。大肠黏膜本身绝不分泌任何消化酶；其核心生理功能是高效重吸收水分、钠盐与电解质，将原本稀薄的肠道残留物脱水压榨浓缩成成型的固体粪便（feces）。大肠内寄居着海量的正常肠道共生菌群，通过酵解发酵合成人体必需的维生素 K（凝血所必需）与部分 B 族维生素并吸收入血。粪便在大肠结肠带囊状推进运动下蓄积于直肠壶腹，当达到一定容量阈值时引发排便反射，经由肛门内外括约肌松弛排出体外（排便/defecation）。

THE URINARY SYSTEM

泌尿系统（排泄代谢废物与水盐酸碱稳态）
The urinary system removes waste products from the blood, maintains water balance, and maintains electrolyte and acid-base balance in the body. The urinary system consists of two kidneys, two ureters, the urinary bladder, and the urethra (FIGURE 32.29).
泌尿系统（The urinary system）是人体最核心的液体排泄与内环境稳态维系器官系统。它时刻清除血液中一切含氮代谢终末废物（尿素、肌酐、尿酸），维持机体水容量平衡，并极其精细地调控电解质（钠、钾、钙）浓度与血液 pH 酸碱稳态。泌尿系统在解剖上由成对的肾脏、成对的输尿管、单一的膀胱以及一条尿道共同组装而成（见图 32.29）。

The kidneys are two bean-shaped organs in the upper posterior abdominal cavity on either side of the spine, protected by the lower ribs and a cushion of fat. The basic functional unit of the kidney is the nephron (FIGURE 32.30). Each kidney contains about one million nephrons. Each nephron consists of a glomerulus (a cluster of capillaries), a Bowman’s capsule (a bulb that surrounds the glomerulus), and a coiled renal tubule surrounded by peritubular capillaries. Urine is formed through three processes: (1) Glomerular filtration—blood pressure forces water and dissolved solutes out of the glomerulus into Bowman’s capsule. (2) Tubular reabsorption—as filtrate travels through the tubule, about 99% of the water, glucose, and essential electrolytes are reabsorbed back into the blood capillaries. (3) Tubular secretion—waste products (such as excess potassium, hydrogen ions, and drugs) are actively secreted from capillaries into the tubule. The remaining fluid, called urine, flows into collecting ducts.
肾脏（Kidneys）是两个蚕豆状的实质性器官，对称分布于腹后壁脊柱两侧的腹膜后隙内，受到下段肋骨与厚实肾周脂肪囊的严密机械缓冲保护。肾脏行使滤过机能的最基本结构与功能微观单位是“肾单位（nephron）”（见图 32.30）。每一个正常人肾脏内均精密集成着约 100 万个肾单位。每个肾单位由肾小体（包含毛细血管缠绕而成的肾小球/glomerulus 与包绕其外的双层杯状肾小囊/Bowman's capsule）以及与其相连的盘曲细长肾小管（近曲小管、髓袢、远曲小管）构成，外周缠绕着密集的管周毛细血管网。尿液的生成必须经历极其严密的三步生理级联：（1）肾小球滤过（Glomerular filtration）：心脏射血产生的高有效滤过压，迫使血液中除血细胞和大分子蛋白质外的大量水分、葡萄糖、电解质及代谢废物透过滤过膜进入肾小囊腔，形成原尿（每日原尿高达 180 升）；（2）肾小管与集合管重吸收（Tubular reabsorption）：原尿流经盘曲的肾小管时，管壁上皮细胞将原尿中 99% 的水分、全部葡萄糖以及大部分必需无机盐离子主动重新逆浓度差吸收入管周毛细血管网重返血液；（3）肾小管分泌（Tubular secretion）：管周毛细血管将血液中多余的氢离子、钾离子、肌酐及药物代谢产物主动逆转运分泌排入管腔滤液中。最终流出集合管进入肾盂的极浓缩液体即为终尿（每日终尿仅约 1.5 升）。

Urine travels from the kidneys to the bladder through the ureters, which are tubes about 25 to 30 cm (10 to 12 in.) long. Smooth muscle contractions of the ureters push urine into the bladder. The bladder is a hollow, muscular sac in the pelvic cavity that stores urine. The bladder can hold about 500 mL of urine. When the bladder fills with about 150 to 250 mL of urine, stretch receptors stimulate the urge to void (urinate). Urine leaves the bladder through the urethra, passing out of the body through the meatus.
终尿由肾盂漏斗引流而出，经由两条长约 25 至 30 厘米（10 至 12 英寸）的细长管道——输尿管（ureters）运送至膀胱。输尿管壁平滑肌发生自发蠕动波，克服重力阻力将尿液间歇性挤压喷入膀胱内部。膀胱（urinary bladder）是安居在骨盆腔底部的中空高弹性肌性贮尿囊袋。健康成人的膀胱最大生理容量可达 500 毫升以上。当膀胱内蓄积尿液达到约 150 至 250 毫升时，膀胱逼尿肌肌层内的牵张感受器受到刺激兴奋，将传入冲动上传至脊髓排尿反射初级中枢与大脑皮层，产生明确的主观排尿欲念（尿意）。当大脑皮层允许排尿时，尿道括约肌松弛、逼尿肌强烈收缩，尿液经由尿道（urethra）冲刷排出体外（尿道外口称为 meatus）。正常成人每日平均尿量约为 1,000 至 1,500 毫升。

生殖系统（配子发生、性激素与繁衍机制）

The Male Reproductive System

男性生殖系统（睾丸、附睾、前列腺与精液生成）
The male gonads (sex glands) are two testes (testicles), suspended outside the pelvic cavity in a sac of skin called the scrotum (FIGURE 32.31). The scrotum keeps the testes about 2°C to 3°C cooler than core body temperature, which is essential for normal sperm production (spermatogenesis). The testes produce sperm cells and the male sex hormone testosterone, which is responsible for male secondary sex characteristics (deep voice, facial and body hair, muscle mass).
男性主要的生殖腺（性腺）是成对的睾丸（testes/testicles），悬垂容纳在盆腔体外由皮肤和筋膜构成的阴囊（scrotum）之中（见图 32.31）。阴囊通过平滑肌提睾肌的舒缩调节位置，使睾丸局部温度始终比腹腔核心体温稳定低约 2°C 至 3°C，而这一低温环境是睾丸曲细精管生精上皮正常进行精子发生（spermatogenesis）与维持精子存活力的绝对生理必要条件。睾丸在垂体促性腺激素调节下承担两大核心生殖机能：每日产生数以千万计的雄性生殖配子——精子；其间质细胞合成分泌主要雄激素——睾酮（testosterone）。睾酮在青春期强力促使男性第二性征全面显现（嗓音低沉粗犷、喉结突起、面部胡须与体毛旺盛生长、骨骼粗壮骨架宽大与骨骼肌体积显著增大）。
Sperm travel from the testes into the epididymis, a coiled tube lying on top of each testis, where sperm mature and gain motility. From the epididymis, sperm enter the vas deferens, which carries sperm into the pelvic cavity. The vas deferens joins with ducts from the seminal vesicles to form the ejaculatory duct, which empties into the urethra. The accessory glands—the seminal vesicles, prostate gland, and bulbourethral (Cowper’s) glands—secrete fluids that nourish and protect sperm and enhance their motility. This mixture of sperm and glandular secretions is called semen. The penis is the male external organ of copulation, through which the urethra passes. The penis contains erectile tissue that engorges with blood during sexual excitation, causing an erection.
新生成的未成熟精子首先从睾丸输出小管移行进入紧贴在睾丸后外侧弯曲盘绕的长管——附睾（epididymis）；精子在附睾液中停留并历经生化成熟，最终获得自主摆动鞭毛向前游动的运动活力与受精能力。射精时，精子离开附睾穿入强韧的肌肉导管——输精管（vas deferens）。输精管伴随精索穿经腹股沟管进入盆腔，与成对的精囊腺排泄管汇合形成射精管，随后穿入前列腺实质开口于前列腺部尿道。男性三大附属腺体——精囊腺（分泌富含果糖供能的弱碱性黏液）、前列腺（prostate gland，围绕膀胱颈与尿道起始部，分泌乳白色碱性液体，稀释精液并强力激活精子运动）以及尿道球腺（Cowper's腺，分泌润滑黏液中和尿道残留酸性尿液），共同贡献了精液的液体基质。精子与这些腺体分泌物的混合浆液被称为精液（semen）。阴茎（penis）是男性外生殖器与交配器官，尿道自其全长贯穿穿行（兼具排尿与射精双重功能）。阴茎内部由两条阴茎海绵体与一条尿道海绵体构成高度特化的勃起血管网组织；在性兴奋副交感神经冲动下，小动脉剧烈扩张，海绵体血窦急剧充盈高压动脉血而静脉回流受阻，使阴茎迅速变硬变长直立，发生勃起（erection）。

The Female Reproductive System: Internal Organs

女性内生殖器官：卵巢、输卵管、子宫与阴道
The female gonads are two ovaries, almond-shaped glands situated on each side of the uterus in the pelvic cavity (FIGURE 32.32). The ovaries produce the female sex cells (ova, or eggs) and secrete the female sex hormones estrogen and progesterone. Estrogen is responsible for female secondary sex characteristics (breast development, wider hips, fat deposition, pubic and underarm hair). Progesterone prepares the uterus for pregnancy. Each month during the reproductive years, one ovary releases a mature ovum in a process called ovulation.
女性核心的生殖腺（性腺）是成对的卵巢（ovaries），呈扁椭圆形杏仁状，对称悬挂固定于骨盆腔内子宫的两侧（见图 32.32）。卵巢承担着双重核心内分泌与生殖机能：周期性生长、成熟并排泄雌性生殖配子——卵子（ova/eggs）；合成分泌两大类固醇女性激素——雌激素（estrogen）与孕激素（progesterone）。雌激素在青春期强力促使女性第二性征显现（乳房发育丰满、骨盆横径显著增宽呈圆盆形、特异性皮下脂肪在臀部与大腿蓄积、阴毛与腋毛萌出）；孕激素则专门促使子宫内膜发生分泌期转化，全力为受精卵着床和维系妊娠做好微生态准备。在育龄女性的每一个正常月经周期中，两侧卵巢通常交替成熟并向腹膜腔排出一个成熟卵细胞，这一生理过程称为排卵（ovulation）。
When an ovum is released from an ovary, it is swept into the Fallopian tube (oviduct) by the motion of finger-like projections called fimbriae. There are two Fallopian tubes, each extending about 10 cm (4 in.) from near an ovary to the upper uterus. The Fallopian tube is the normal site of fertilization (the union of a sperm and an ovum). Cilia and peristaltic contractions of the tube move the ovum toward the uterus.
当成熟卵子从卵巢表面破泡排出后，立刻被靠近卵巢的输卵管伞端（fimbriae）指状突起的微颤节律性摆动犹如吸尘器般捕获并卷吸入输卵管管腔内。左右两侧输卵管（Fallopian tubes/oviducts）长约 10 至 12 厘米，横向平展架设于卵巢与子宫角之间。输卵管壶腹部是人类生命起源的天然圣殿——精子与卵子在此精准相遇并发生受精（fertilization）。受精后形成的单细胞受精卵，在输卵管黏膜纤毛的有节律协同摆动与管壁平滑肌微弱蠕动下，在数天之内向子宫腔平稳运送推进。
The uterus is a hollow, muscular, pear-shaped organ located in the centre of the pelvic cavity behind the bladder and in front of the rectum. The uterus consists of the fundus (upper rounded portion), the body (middle main part), and the cervix (the narrow lower neck that projects into the vagina). The wall of the uterus has three layers: (1) The endometrium—the inner mucous lining that thickens each month in response to estrogen and progesterone to prepare for an embryo; if fertilization does not occur, the functional layer of the endometrium breaks down and is shed during menstruation. (2) The myometrium—the thick middle layer of smooth muscle that contracts powerfully during labour to expel the fetus. (3) The perimetrium—the outer serous membrane covering. The vagina is a muscular, elastic canal about 7 to 10 cm (3 to 4 in.) long extending from the cervix to the outside of the body. It serves as the birth canal, the exit for menstrual flow, and the organ for sexual intercourse.
子宫（The uterus）是一个由厚实平滑肌构筑的中空倒置梨形器官，坐落于骨盆腔中央、正前方紧贴膀胱而后方毗邻直肠。子宫在解剖上由宫底（fundus，输卵管入口水平以上的隆起圆顶）、宫体（body，中部主干肌肉腔）以及宫颈（cervix，下端狭窄圆柱状、突入阴道内的颈管口）三部分组成。子宫壁由三层组织紧密复合构成：（1）子宫内膜（Endometrium）：富含血管与腺体的黏膜内层，在每个月经周期雌孕激素调控下呈周期性增厚增殖充血，为胚胎着床构筑温床；若本周期未受孕，黄体退化导致内膜功能层缺血坏死剥脱并破裂出血，形成月经排出；（2）子宫肌层（Myometrium）：构成子宫壁主体的极厚平滑肌层，平滑肌纤维呈环形、纵行及斜形交织网状排列，在分娩发动时催产素诱发其产生毁天灭地的阵发性强烈收缩，强力推动胎儿穿过产道降生；（3）子宫外膜（Perimetrium）：被覆于子宫表面的浆膜腹膜外被。阴道（Vagina）是一条长约 7 至 10 厘米、富有极大伸展弹性的平滑肌纤维管道，上端环抱宫颈而下端开口于阴道前庭；它兼任胎儿分娩自然娩出的产道、月经经血排出的通道以及男女进行性交接纳精液的器官。

女性外生殖器在医学上统称为外阴（vulva，见图 32.33）。阴阜（mons pubis）是覆盖在耻骨联合正前方隆起的皮下脂肪软垫，青春期后表面被覆阴毛。大阴唇（labia majora）是向后延伸的两条饱满隆起的纵行皮肤皱襞，保护着内部脆弱的尿道和阴道开口。小阴唇（labia minora）是位于大阴唇内侧的两条较小、无毛且富含皮脂腺与血管神经末梢的薄皮肤皱襞。阴蒂（clitoris）位于小阴唇前端顶部的交界处，由微型的海绵体勃起组织构成，表面密布极其敏感丰富的感觉神经感觉小体，专职司职女性性唤起与性快感。阴道口后缘与肛门前缘之间的软组织肌肉区域被称为会阴（perineum/会阴体），在自然阴道分娩时承受巨大的组织张力扩展。

The Mammary Glands (Breasts)

乳房与乳腺腺体（腺叶导管与产后泌乳机制）
The mammary glands (breasts) are accessory organs of the female reproductive system that secrete milk (lactation) to nourish an infant (FIGURE 32.34). Each breast consists of 15 to 20 glandular lobes separated by adipose (fat) tissue and fibrous suspensory ligaments. Adipose tissue determines the size and shape of the breasts. Within each lobe are lobules containing clusters of milk-producing glands called alveoli. During lactation, milk flows from the alveoli through lactiferous ducts into lactiferous sinuses (reservoirs) behind the nipple, and finally exits through tiny openings on the surface of the nipple. The pigmented area surrounding the nipple is called the areola.
乳腺（Mammary glands，乳房）属于特化的外胚层外分泌腺，在生殖生理学上是女性生殖系统的附属器官，专职在产后合成分泌乳汁（lactation/哺乳）以孕育抚养新生婴儿（解剖切面见图 32.34）。每个乳房在解剖上由 15 至 20 个放射状排列的乳腺叶（lobes）构成，各腺叶之间被大量的脂肪结缔组织（adipose tissue）与库珀氏悬韧带（Cooper's ligaments）相互分隔充填；乳房体积的大小主要由其中脂肪沉积的厚度决定，与实际腺体组织的多寡及产后泌乳能力并无直接关联。每个乳腺叶内部包含众多乳小叶，其微观由成簇的泌乳腺泡（alveoli）组成。分娩后在催乳素（prolactin）驱动下腺泡上皮细胞大量分泌乳汁，乳汁汇入各级输乳管（lactiferous ducts），在乳晕深面膨大形成输乳窦（lactiferous sinuses/储乳囊），并在催产素介导的射乳反射下经由乳头（nipple）表面的微孔向外射出。包绕在乳头周围呈粉红或深褐色的环状色素沉着皮区称为乳晕（areola）。

Menstruation and Fertilization

月经周期调控与新生命受精着床生理
Menstruation is the cyclic discharge of the uterine lining that occurs approximately every 28 days (ranging from 21 to 35 days) from menarche (the onset of menstruation during puberty) until menopause (the permanent cessation of menstruation in middle adulthood). The menstrual cycle is controlled by pituitary hormones (FSH and LH) and ovarian hormones (estrogen and progesterone). In the first half of the cycle, FSH stimulates an ovarian follicle to mature, and estrogen causes the endometrium to proliferate. At approximately day 14 of a 28-day cycle, a surge of LH triggers ovulation—the release of the mature egg into the pelvic cavity. After ovulation, the ruptured follicle transforms into the corpus luteum, which secretes large quantities of progesterone to prepare the endometrium for embryo implantation.
月经（Menstruation）是指子宫内膜功能层周期性坏死脱落并伴随血管出血排出体外的生理现象。月经周期平均约为 28 天（临床正常波动范围在 21 至 35 天），其周期始自青春期的月经初潮（menarche），持续至中年期绝经（menopause）彻底终止。月经周期完全受下丘脑-垂体-卵巢轴（HPO轴）激素的精密级联负反馈网络调控。在月经周期的前半段（卵泡期/增殖期），垂体分泌的促卵泡激素（FSH）刺激卵巢中的原始卵泡生长发育并合成分泌大量雌激素，使子宫内膜迅速增殖修复加厚。在典型 28 天周期的第 14 天左右，垂体爆发性释放黄体生成素峰（LH surge），诱发成熟卵泡破裂释放卵子（排卵）。排卵后，残留在卵巢内的卵泡壁塌陷黄素化转变为富含毛细血管网的黄体（corpus luteum）；黄体分泌大量孕激素与雌激素，促使子宫内膜腺体弯曲充满糖原、间质水肿充血，进入分泌期，全力迎接胚胎着床。
If the egg is fertilized by a sperm within 12 to 24 hours after ovulation in the Fallopian tube, the resulting zygote begins cell division (cleavage) as it travels to the uterus over several days, where it implants into the nutrient-rich endometrium. The developing placenta then secretes human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG), which keeps the corpus luteum active to maintain the endometrium throughout pregnancy. If fertilization does not occur, the unfertilized egg disintegrates, the corpus luteum degenerates, and levels of estrogen and progesterone drop precipitously. Deprived of hormonal support, the endometrial blood vessels constrict, causing the lining to slough off and flow out through the vagina as the menstrual period, which typically lasts 3 to 7 days.
若排卵后 12 至 24 小时内卵子在输卵管壶腹部与获能精子相遇受精，精卵核融合形成的二倍体受精卵（zygote）便在向子宫游动的过程中迅速展开有丝分裂卵裂，在受精后约第 6 至 7 天囊胚精准植入（着床）营养丰盈的子宫内膜深层。随后早期胚胎滋养层细胞分泌人绒毛膜促性腺激素（hCG），维持卵巢黄体转变为妊娠黄体，持续供应高浓度孕激素以严密维持子宫内膜处于静止蜕膜状态，彻底杜绝子宫内膜剥脱出血并保护胎儿足月孕育。若本次排卵未发生受精，未受精卵细胞在 24 小时内自行凋亡解体，卵巢黄体在排卵后约 14 天自发萎缩退化为白体，导致体内雌激素与孕激素水平发生断崖式急剧暴跌。子宫内膜深层螺旋小动脉因失去性激素支持而发生痉挛性强烈收缩缺血坏死，内膜表层大面积缺血剥脱破裂出血，经阴道流出体外，即形成月经经血（通常持续 3 至 7 天，经血中混有脱落的内膜组织碎片、宫颈黏液及阴道脱落上皮细胞），并随即开启崭新的下一轮月经周期循环。

KEY POINTS

全章核心考点总结（九大系统精要）
  • • The anatomical position is the body standing erect, with the feet shoulder-width apart, face forward, arms at the sides, and palms of the hands facing forward. It serves as the single universal baseline for all directional and anatomical terminology.
    标准解剖学姿势是身体直立、两足并拢或与肩同宽向前、两眼平视正前方、双上肢下垂置于躯干两侧、双掌掌心向前的基准姿态，是所有解剖方位术语与临床病灶定位的唯一通用国际基准。
  • • Cells are the basic unit of body structure. Groups of cells with similar functions combine to form tissues (epithelial, connective, muscle, and nerve). Organs are groups of tissues that work together to perform a special function. Organ systems are formed by organs working in coordination to maintain homeostasis.
    细胞是构成人体生命形态解剖结构与代谢功能的最基本生命单位。结构形态相似的细胞群及其间质构成人体四大基本组织（上皮组织、结缔组织、肌组织与神经组织）。几种组织有机组合构成行使特定功能的器官；器官之间协同组装构成器官系统，共同维持人体内环境动态相对恒定的稳态（homeostasis）。
  • • The major organ systems in the body include: (1) Integumentary system (skin, hair, nails, sweat and oil glands)—provides protection, sensation, and temperature regulation. (2) Musculoskeletal system (206 bones, joints, and over 600 skeletal, smooth, and cardiac muscles)—provides framework, movement, organ protection, and heat production. Scenario: A patient has a disorder that affects the immune system (e.g., severe immunosuppression, leukemia, or advanced HIV/AIDS). How does such a disorder affect body function? How will you provide care in a way that protects the person?

Clinical Analysis & Safe Care Protocol:
1. Impact on Body Function:
- The immune system serves as the body's military defence against pathogenic microbes and abnormal cancerous cells. When the immune system is compromised, both nonspecific and specific defence mechanisms fail. The body cannot mount an effective inflammatory response, produce adequate antibodies, or deploy cytotoxic T cells.
- Consequently, opportunistic infections caused by harmless environmental bacteria, viruses, or fungi can easily invade the bloodstream (sepsis), proliferate uncontrollably, and cause fatal multi-organ failure. Minor skin tears or colds can rapidly escalate into life-threatening pneumonia.
2. Protective Care & Standard Infection Control:
- Strict Hand Hygiene: Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water or use alcohol-based rub before and immediately after entering the client's room and touching any client equipment (the single most effective measure).
- Protective (Reverse) Precautions: The support worker and visitors must wear a clean medical mask, gloves, and gown if assigned or if presenting with any mild respiratory symptoms. Never enter an immunocompromised client's room when sick.
- Environmental Cleanliness: Ensure client surroundings are sanitized regularly using hospital-grade disinfectants. Avoid fresh flowers, standing water, or raw/unpasteurized food (which harbour Pseudomonas and Listeria).
- DIPPS Dignity & Compassion: Immunocompromised clients often feel isolated and frightened by isolation precautions. Practice compassionate listening, explain every barrier step, and provide uninterrupted gentle personal care.
临床情境：一名案主罹患严重影响免疫系统的重度疾患（例如骨髓移植后重度免疫抑制状态、白血病化疗后骨髓抑制或晚期 HIV/AIDS）。此类免疫功能障碍会如何深刻破坏机体的正常生理运转？在日常支持照护中，你应当如何实施专业防护照护以全力保护该案主免受感染威胁？

临床深度剖析与标准防护照护规程：
1. 对机体生理运转的破坏机制：
- 免疫系统是人体抵御外来致病微生物与清除恶变肿瘤细胞的核心国防部队。当免疫系统遭受严重破坏时，非特异性与特异性免疫防线全线溃败：机体丧失正常的局限性炎性反应能力，无法及时合成足够的保护性特异抗体，细胞毒性 T 细胞也无从抵御胞内感染。
- 其直接后果是：在健康人群中完全无害的环境共生微生物（条件致病菌/机会感染菌，如真菌念珠菌、卡氏肺孢子菌、表皮葡萄球菌）会轻而易举侵入案主深部血流引发致命性败血症（sepsis）与感染性休克；体表微小的皮肤抓伤擦伤或一次普通轻微感冒，便能在 24 小时内迅速恶化进展为致死性重症重度肺炎与多器官功能衰竭。
2. 保护性照护与感控硬门禁实施策略：
- 严苛执行手卫生（第一核心门禁）：在踏入案主病房前后、接触案主身体及接触其床单位辅助器具前后，必须严格执行七步洗手法或使用含醇速干手消剂揉搓至少 15 秒（这是切断外源性交叉感染传播链条最廉价也是最决定性的举措）。
- 实施保护性隔离（反向隔离/Reverse Precautions）：支持工作者在照护该案主时必须穿戴清洁医用口罩、手套与防护隔离衣。任何工作人员或探视家属若出现咳嗽、流涕、咽痛或发热等轻微呼吸道前驱症状，严禁踏入案主病房！
- 严控照护环境洁净度：每日使用医用级消毒湿巾对接头、床栏、呼叫铃及卫浴把手进行彻底表面消毒消杀；病房内绝对禁止摆放生花鲜花、潮湿花瓶积水（极易孳生铜绿假单胞菌等耐药菌），严禁向案主提供未经巴氏杀菌的乳制品或生冷未熟透瓜果蔬菜。
- 践行 DIPPS 尊严与心理支持：长期处于保护性隔离下的免疫受损案主往往承受巨大的孤独隔绝感与恐惧焦虑。工作者切勿将案主视为“危险污染源”，而应在穿戴防护装备的同时，以温和坚定的眼神与语言开诚布公向案主解释每一项防护举措的保护意义，倾注无限的人文关怀与体温。

10. (B) 肺换气（外呼吸气体交换）专门发生在肺泡壁与紧密贴附的肺毛细血管内皮构成的薄薄气-血屏障上，氧气扩散入血，二氧化碳扩散入肺泡排出体外。
11. (A) 消化过程始于口腔（Mouth）。牙齿咀嚼研磨食物展开机械性消化，同时唾液腺分泌的唾液淀粉酶开始对碳水化合物进行初步化学性水解消化。
12. (B) 小肠（Small intestine，包括空肠与回肠）黏膜表面密布数以百万计的环形皱襞、小肠绒毛与微绒毛，构成巨大的吸收表面积，机体绝大部分单糖、氨基酸、脂肪酸与维生素均在此被主动吸收。
13. (B) 肾脏（Kidneys）实质内的上百万个肾单位通过肾小球滤过、肾小管重吸收和肾小管分泌三大生理过程生成尿液；膀胱仅起暂时储存尿液的作用。
14. (C) 睾丸（Testis，复数 testes）是男性的主要性腺（生殖腺），容纳于阴囊内，其曲细精管负责产生精子，间质细胞负责合成分泌雄激素（睾酮）。
15. (A) 内分泌腺（无导管腺）分泌的特异性化学信使物质通称为激素（Hormones）。胰岛素（Insulin）仅属于胰腺分泌的众多激素中的一种，因此最通用规范的答案是 A。
16. (B) 免疫系统由免疫器官、免疫细胞及免疫活性分子组成，核心使命是识别并消灭入侵的致病微生物（细菌、病毒等）以及清除机体内衰老损伤或恶变的肿瘤细胞，全力护卫机体免受感染与疾病侵害。
