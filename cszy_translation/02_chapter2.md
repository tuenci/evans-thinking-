# 《重生真意》（中英双语对照版）
## 二、为什么人必须重生？ / Chapter 2: Why Must Man Be Born Again?

---

### 【中文】
二、为什么人必须重生？

### 【English】
Chapter 2: Why Must Man Be Born Again?

---

### 【中文】
「我说：『你们必须重生』，你不要以为希奇。」(约三7)

### 【English】
"Do not marvel that I said to you, 'You must be born again.'" (John 3:7)

---

### 【中文】
我们当注意主耶稣在这里所说的一句话：「你们必须重生」。重生不是一件可有可无的事，也不是一件无关紧要的事。它是「必须」的事──要见神的国「必须重生」；要进神的国「必须重生」；要到神面前来「必须重生」；要作神的儿子「必须重生」。

### 【English】
We must pay earnest attention to the words uttered here by the Lord Jesus: "You must be born again." Regeneration is not an optional luxury, nor is it a matter of minor consequence. It is an absolute necessity—to see the kingdom of God, one *must* be born again; to enter the kingdom of God, one *must* be born again; to come before the presence of God, one *must* be born again; to become a child of God, one *must* be born again.

---

### 【中文】
可惜多少称为基督徒的人轻忽了这事！

### 【English】
Alas, how many who bear the name of Christian treat this matter with utter negligence!

---

### 【中文】
多少称为传福音的人也轻忽了这事！他们看重「考道」，看重「记名」，看重「受洗」，看重「坚振」，以为这些都是「必须」的。以为一个人经过这些步骤以后便可以蒙神的悦纳、进神的国，却将耶稣所看为重要，并且要我们也看为重要的事看为无足轻重。传道的人不讲「重生」的道理，信道的人也不追求「重生」的经验。这是何等可叹的事啊！

### 【English】
How many who profess to preach the Gospel likewise neglect this vital truth! They place immense weight upon doctrinal examinations, church roll enrollment, water baptism, and confirmation, deeming these outward rites to be "essential." They imagine that once a person has undergone these procedural steps, he is assured of God's acceptance and entrance into the kingdom of God; yet that which Jesus deemed of paramount importance—and which He commanded us to regard as indispensable—they treat as trivial. Preachers fail to preach the doctrine of regeneration, and churchgoers make no effort to seek the experience of being born again. What a lamentable tragedy this is!

---

### 【中文】
然而「重生」的道理和经验不会因人的轻看、忽略便减轻它的价值。传道的人可以不讲重生的道理，也可以告诉人说重生不重生无关紧要，信道的人也可以把它当作一个不急需讨论的神学问题；但主耶稣的话──「你们必须重生」──却永不能改变。除非一个人不要神的儿子，不要进神的国，若不然，我就要对他说：「你必须重生。」

### 【English】
Yet the truth and reality of regeneration lose none of their supreme value merely because men despise or neglect them. Preachers may remain silent on the new birth, or even tell people that being born again is of little consequence; churchgoers may dismiss it as an unessential theological debate. But the word of the Lord Jesus—"You must be born again"—remains forever unalterable. Unless a person desires to forfeit the Son of God and renounce all hope of entering the kingdom of God, I must declare unto him with all urgency: "You must be born again!"

---

### 【中文】
为什么人必须重生呢？我们要明白这个问题，必须先彻底认清人类在神面前的真面目。但这件事却是我们最难明白的，若不是有神的话清楚将这件事指示我们，恐怕我们至死也不认识自己的真面目。没有镜子我们总不能看见自己的脸，没有神的话我们也是照样总不能看见自己在神面前实在的情形。

### 【English】
Why must man be born again? To understand this question, we must first recognize clearly humanity's true condition in the sight of God. Yet this is precisely what is most difficult for us to see. Were it not for the Word of God unmistakably revealing this reality to us, I fear we would go to our graves without ever discerning our own true state. Without a mirror, we can never behold our own face; likewise, without the Word of God, we can never perceive our true spiritual plight before God.

---

### 【中文】
按我们自己的性情，不但看不见自己的真相，而且也不愿意看见。我们愿意奉承自己，谄媚自己，歌自己的功，颂自己的德，同时还愿意别人也这样待我们，「人同此心，心同此理」，因此人类更没有指望能认识自己了。

### 【English】
According to our fallen human nature, not only are we blind to our true condition, but we are also entirely unwilling to see it. We love to flatter ourselves, sing our own praises, and extol our own virtues, while yearning for others to do the same toward us. Since this self-congratulatory impulse is universal among men, humanity is left with even less hope of ever arriving at true self-knowledge.

---

### 【中文】
感谢神，祂不欺骗我们。祂爱我们。祂爱我们远胜于我们爱自己。祂毫不隐讳的把我们的真相向我们显明。听祂的话说：

### 【English】
Thanks be to God, He never deceives us! He loves us far more deeply than we could ever love ourselves. Without concealing anything, He lays bare our true condition before our eyes. Listen to what His Word declares:

---

### 【中文】
「耶和华从天上垂看世人，要看有明白的没有，有寻求神的没有。他们都偏离正路，一同变为污秽；并没有行善的，连一个也没有。」(诗一四2-3)

### 【English】
"The LORD looks down from heaven on the children of man, to see if there are any who understand, who seek after God. They have all turned aside; together they have become corrupt; there is none who does good, not even one." (Psalm 14:2–3)

---

### 【中文】
「在你面前，凡活着的人没有一个是义的。」(诗一四三2)

### 【English】
"For no one living is righteous before You." (Psalm 143:2)

---

### 【中文】
「时常行善而不犯罪的义人，世上实在没有。」(传七20)

### 【English】
"Surely there is not a righteous man on earth who does good and never sins." (Ecclesiastes 7:20)

---

### 【中文】
「我们已经证明：犹太人和希利尼人都在罪恶之下。就如经上所记：没有义人，连一个也没有。没有明白的；没有寻求神的；都是偏离正路，一同变为无用。没有行善的，连一个也没有。他们的喉咙是敞开的坟墓；他们用舌头弄诡诈；嘴唇里有虺蛇的毒气，满口是咒骂苦毒。杀人流血，他们的脚飞跑，所经过的路便行残害暴虐的事。平安的路，他们末曾知道；他们眼中不怕神。」(罗三9-18)

### 【English】
"For we have already charged that all, both Jews and Greeks, are under sin, as it is written: 'None is righteous, no, not one; no one understands; no one seeks for God. All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; no one does good, not even one.' 'Their throat is an open grave; they use their tongues to deceive.' 'The venom of asps is under their lips.' 'Their mouth is full of curses and bitterness.' 'Their feet are swift to shed blood; in their paths are ruin and misery, and the way of peace they have not known.' 'There is no fear of God before their eyes.'" (Romans 3:9–18)

---

### 【中文】
这就是我们的真相，这就是全体人类的真相。「都是偏离正路，一同变为无用。并没有行善的，连一个也没有。」「没有一个是义的。」「犹太人和希利尼人都在罪恶之下。」这些话是神说的。这是「耶和华从天上垂看世人」所得的结论。祂从天上垂看世人几千年之久，看世人在外面有种种的不同——老少的不同、男女的不同、贫富的不同、贵贱的不同、好坏的不同、智愚的不同、文野的不同、美丑的不同——但在一件事上他们却毫无分别——「都偏离正路，一同变为污秽。」

### 【English】
This is our true state; this is the reality of all humanity! "All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; no one does good, not even one." "None is righteous." "Both Jews and Greeks are under sin." These are not the speculations of men; they are the solemn pronouncements of God. This is the divine verdict arrived at when "the LORD looks down from heaven on the children of man." For thousands of years He has looked down upon the human race from on high. Outwardly, men display endless differences—differences of age and youth, male and female, rich and poor, noble and base, good and bad, wise and foolish, civilized and barbarian, beautiful and plain. Yet in one fundamental reality, there is not the slightest distinction: "All have turned aside; together they have become corrupt."

---

### 【中文】
「都是偏离正路，一同变为无用。并没有行善的，连一个也没有。」这几句话如果是出于人的口，我就不敢确信。因为世界上没有一个人能知道世界上所有人实在的状况。全世界的人多得不可胜数，我们所认识的能有多少人呢？普通人所认识的不过只有自己一家的人，自己的亲族、朋友、同人、邻舍，和一些与自己常相接触往来的人。有些人因为职业和环境的关系，认识的人比较普通人所认识的更多一些；但无论如何，我们决不信世上有一个人能认识全世界人类的百分之一，不用说百分之一，就是能认识全世界人类千分之一、万分之一的人，也不会寻出一个来。

### 【English】
"All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; no one does good, not even one." Had these words originated from mere mortal lips, I would not dare to accept them with certainty. For no human being possesses the capacity to know the true internal condition of every person on earth. The inhabitants of this world are countless; how many can any one of us ever personally know? The ordinary individual knows merely the members of his own household, his relatives, his personal friends, his colleagues, his neighbors, and those with whom he regularly crosses paths. Even those whose professions or circumstances afford them a wider circle of acquaintances can never know even one percent of the global population; indeed, you could search in vain for a single human being who has known a thousandth or a ten-thousandth of the human race.

---

### 【中文】
在我们所认识的那少数的人里面，我们能彻底认识的又有多少呢？许多人是我们所认识的，但我们不过认识他们的面貌、知道他们的姓名。有些人我们认识比较深，但也不过认识他们生活的一部分──他们在街市中怎样行走、遇见友人怎样谈话、在办公室里怎样办事、在宴会中怎样应酬──这一部分是显露在人面前的，至于他们自己家庭中的生活究竟怎样、他们自己独处的时候所作的是什么事，我们就都不知道了。

### 【English】
Furthermore, among the few individuals we do know, how many do we truly and thoroughly comprehend? Many are mere acquaintances whose faces we recognize and whose names we can recall. With others our acquaintance runs somewhat deeper, yet we still know only an outward fragment of their lives—how they conduct themselves on the street, how they converse when meeting friends, how they discharge their duties in the office, how they socialize at banquets. All this is merely what is publicly displayed before human eyes. As for how they truly live within the privacy of their homes, or what deeds they commit when completely alone in secret, of this we know nothing at all.

---

### 【中文】
许多人是这样诡诈，在别人面前作出很诚实清洁、公义端正的样子来，别人看见他们以为他们真是品行端正的人，其实在别人看不见的时侯，他们的生活和他们所作的事真是卑鄙龌龊到极点。我们若只看他们显露在人前的那一部分生活，怎么能认识他们呢？有些人虽然不至坏到这种地步，可是我们也不能单看他们显露在人前的那一部分生活，就断定他们究竟是怎样的人。或者有些人我们认识得更加清楚，我们和他们在一处同住过几日，我们可以看见他们生活的大部分，但他们的心究竟如何，还不是我们所能知道的。

### 【English】
Many individuals are exceedingly deceitful: before onlookers they put on an appearance of honesty, purity, uprightness, and righteousness, leading others to regard them as persons of sterling virtue; yet when hidden from human view, their private lives and hidden deeds are vile and abominable to the uttermost degree. If we judge them solely by the outward persona they exhibit in public, how could we ever know their true character? Even where people are not corrupt to such an extreme, we can never determine what a person truly is merely by observing the visible slice of life he displays before society. Perhaps there are some whom we know more intimately, having lived under the same roof with them for days so that we observe the greater part of their conduct; nevertheless, what truly resides within the depths of their hearts remains entirely beyond our scrutiny.

---

### 【中文】
「画虎画皮难画骨，知人知面不知心。」我们对于世上的人所认识的既是这样有限，自然不能断定人类实在的状况是怎样了；但神却不是这样，祂是鉴察人心、试验人肺腑的(耶一七10)。祂知道人一切的言语行为，祂也知道人心中所存一切的意念(诗一三九1-4)。世界上没有一种隐藏的事是祂所不能看见的。

### 【English】
As the ancient proverb says: "In painting a tiger, one can draw the skin but not the bones; in knowing a person, one can know his face but never his heart." Since our knowledge of fellow men is so pitifully limited, we naturally possess no authority to pronounce upon the true condition of humanity. But it is not so with God! He is the One who searches the heart and tests the innermost mind (Jer 17:10). He knows every word on human tongues, every deed done in secret, and every fleeting thought harbored within the heart (Psalm 139:1–4). In the entire universe, there is no hidden thing that can escape His piercing gaze.

---

### 【中文】
祂从天上垂看世人几千年之久，留意世界上每一个人；祂细心寻找一个行善的人，结果竟使祂大失所望，因为他们「都是偏离正路，一同变为无用。并没有行善的，连一个也没有。」「他们的喉咙是敞开的坟墓；他们用舌头弄诡诈；嘴唇里有虺蛇的毒气，满口是咒骂苦毒。杀人流血，他们的脚飞跑，所经过的路便行残害暴虐的事。平安的路，他们末曾知道；他们眼中不怕神。」祂不但告诉我们世人都是罪人，祂也把世人的罪状一一的宣布出来，使世人再没有为自己辩护的余地。真的，我们若能毫不自欺，容让神用祂的光照亮我们，我们一定会看出自己在神面前真是「从脚掌到头顶没有一处是完全的」。神并不欺骗我们。祂说世上的人都是罪人，因为他们实在都是有罪的。因为神是全知的，所以我信祂所说的话；因为神是信实的，所以我对祂的话更毫无怀疑。

### 【English】
For thousands of years He has looked down from heaven upon the sons of men, fixing His attentive eye upon every single soul across the globe. He carefully searched for one who does good, and the outcome was an utter, heartbreaking desolation: "All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; no one does good, not even one." "Their throat is an open grave; they use their tongues to deceive; the venom of asps is under their lips; their mouth is full of curses and bitterness. Their feet are swift to shed blood; in their paths are ruin and misery, and the way of peace they have not known; there is no fear of God before their eyes." God not only declares that all men are sinners, but He also catalogues the indictment against them point by point, leaving humanity without the slightest ground for self-justification. Truly, if we refuse to deceive ourselves and instead allow God's light to illuminate our souls, we will instantly see that before Him, "from the sole of the foot even to the head, there is no soundness in us." God does not deceive us. He proclaims that all men are sinners because all men are indeed guilty of sin. Because God is omniscient, I believe what He speaks; because God is faithful, I harbor not the shadow of a doubt concerning His Word.

---

### 【中文】
世人都有罪，这事我们已经看清楚了，再进一步的问题就是「罪是起源于人的外面呢，还是起源于人的里面呢？」我们若是向人征求这个问题的答案，一定有许多人要回答说：「罪是起源于人的外面。人本来是好的，只因社会不良，所以那本来良好的人便被恶劣的社会变坏了。」请详细想一想这种说法通不通呢？人是好的，社会是坏的，因此坏社会把好人变坏了。世界上能不能有这种不合理、不可能的事呢？社会是什么？社会不是一所房屋，不是一座城市，也不是一块地土；社会是人群的集合。若每一个人是好的，他们所集合而成的社会怎能会是坏的呢？因为人是坏的，所以他们所集合而成的社会才是坏的。不是社会把人变坏，乃是坏人组成了坏的社会。这是合理的解释，也是百跌不破的事实。

### 【English】
That all men are sinners is now abundantly clear to us. The next question that arises is this: Does sin originate from outside a person, or from within? If we were to poll the opinions of the world on this question, many would undoubtedly answer: "Sin originates from the outside. Man is fundamentally good, but because society is corrupt, originally virtuous individuals are corrupted by their wicked environment." Consider this carefully: does such an argument hold water? That man is good while society is bad, and that a bad society turns good men into bad—could such an illogical and impossible contradiction ever exist? What, after all, is society? Society is not a building, nor a city, nor a plot of land; society is the aggregate of human individuals. If every individual were inherently good, how could the society formed by their assembly ever be corrupt? It is precisely because individual men are wicked that the society they compose is wicked! It is not society that corrupts man; rather, corrupt men form a corrupt society. This is the only rational explanation, and it is an unshakeable, universal fact.

---

### 【中文】
罪恶不是从外面来的，我们已经证明了。那样它是从哪里来的呢？神的话清楚回答了这个问题：「从人里面出来的，那才能污秽人；因为从里面，就是从人心里，发出恶念、苟合、偷盗、凶杀、奸淫、贪婪、邪恶、诡诈、淫荡、嫉妒、谤讟、骄傲、狂妄。这一切的恶都是从里面出来，且能污秽人。」(可七20-23)

### 【English】
Having proven that sin does not originate from the outside, from where then does it come? The Word of God answers this question with crystalline clarity: "What comes out of a person is what defiles him. For from within, out of the heart of man, come evil thoughts, sexual immorality, theft, murder, adultery, coveting, wickedness, deceit, sensuality, envy, slander, pride, foolishness. All these evil things come from within, and they defile a person." (Mark 7:20–23)

---

### 【中文】
啊！这真是十分清楚、十分彻底的答案！世界上的罪虽有千百种，但无一不是从人的心中发源。因为人的心充满了自私、诡诈、贪婪、邪淫、嫉妒、恼恨，以及种种的恶念，所以他们的口才吐出各种的恶言，他们的手才作出各样的恶事，因此社会中才充满了争权夺利、损人利己、营私舞弊、贪脏受贿、欺诈偷盗、抢夺拐骗、绑票勒索、拆白渔利、家庭悖逆、兄弟哄抢、结党纷争、排挤倾轧、嫉妒恼恨、流血杀人、奸淫苟合、纵欲乱伦、挑拨争端、布散谣言、假公济私、倚势凌人、卖主卖友、负义忘恩、倚大欺小、恃强凌弱；争地以战，杀人盈野；争城以战，杀人盈城；以及种种述不胜述的各样罪恶。真的，世界上再没有别的地方比人心更污秽，再没有别的东西比人心更邪恶。

### 【English】
Ah, what an unsparing, exhaustive, and definitive answer! Though the world contains thousands of variations of sin, every single one of them originates in the human heart. Because the human heart is saturated with selfishness, deceit, greed, lust, jealousy, hatred, and all manner of depraved thoughts, human lips pour forth vile words, and human hands perpetrate wicked deeds. Hence human society is rife with struggles for power and wealth, exploiting others for self-interest, embezzlement and graft, bribery, fraud, theft, robbery and extortion, kidnapping, swindling and racketeering, domestic rebellion, brotherly strife, factional discord, rivalry and intrigue, malice, bloodshed, murder, fornication, incestuous lust, stirring up quarrels, spreading slanderous rumors, abusing public office for private gain, bullying the weak through influence, betraying masters and friends, ungrateful treachery, the strong oppressing the vulnerable; wars waged for territory with corpses blanketing the fields; sieges fought for cities with slaughter filling the streets—and innumerable other iniquities beyond description. Truly, in all creation there is no place filthier than the human heart, nor is there anything more wicked!

---

### 【中文】
「人心比万物都诡诈，坏到极处，谁能识透呢？」(耶一七9)神所说的这两句话真是确切不移的了。

### 【English】
"The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately sick; who can understand it?" (Jeremiah 17:9). These words spoken by God stand forever indisputable.

---

### 【中文】
我们再往下讨论一个问题──「人从多大年岁才有罪？」对于这个问题有人要回答说：「从十几岁。」也有人要回答说：「从七、八岁或四、五岁。」圣经怎样回答这个问题呢？「恶人一出母胎就与神疏远，一离母腹便走错路，说谎话。」(诗五八3)「我是在罪孽里生的，在我母亲怀胎的时侯就有了罪。」(诗五一5)

### 【English】
Let us press our inquiry one step further: At what age does a person begin to have sin? To this question, some might answer: "From their teenage years." Others might reply: "From seven or eight, or perhaps four or five." How do the Scriptures answer this question? "The wicked are estranged from the womb; they go astray from birth, speaking lies." (Psalm 58:3). "Behold, I was brought forth in iniquity, and in sin did my mother conceive me." (Psalm 51:5).

---

### 【中文】
神的话告诉我们，人不是长大以后才变坏的，乃是本来就是坏的。人犯罪不是学习的，他们本来就有罪，本来就会犯罪。

### 【English】
The Word of God reveals to us that man does not become corrupt merely upon growing up; he is corrupt by nature from his very inception. Man does not need to be taught how to sin; he possesses sin innately and sins instinctively.

### 【中文】
从前我不明白这理，我以为小孩子是清洁无罪的，及至他们长大以后，常看他们的父母、家人、邻舍犯罪，他们才渐渐学会犯罪。后来我留意观察儿童的生活动作，我才明白事实并不是这样。我看见小孩子本来就有犯罪的倾向，还不很会说话的小孩子就知道自私；就知道夺取别的儿童的玩具；就知道动手打人；就知道贪得无厌；就知道向别人索取东西，却不肯把东西给别人。我们多少次看见小孩子向他们的父母索取食物或玩具，是那样的贪得无厌，要了还要，及至他们的父母向他们索要一点，或是叫他们给他们的弟弟妹妹一些的时侯，他们总是不肯给，就是给也不过极少的一点，或是拣选不好的、很小的拿出来给他们，自己却把好的、大的存留起来。这种贪心，这种自私，这种忘恩负义的罪恶，并不是从人学来的，乃是他们生来就有的。这也不足为奇，父母既是有罪的，他们所生的儿女又岂能没有罪呢？黄种人所生的孩子总是黄种人，白种人所生的孩子总是白种人；孩子生出来一定像他的父母，有罪的人所生的孩子又岂能没有罪呢？

### 【English】
In former years I did not comprehend this truth. I naively imagined that young children were pure and innocent, and that only as they grew older, observing their parents, family members, and neighbors commit sins, did they gradually learn to imitate their sinful ways. Later on, however, when I closely observed the behavior of young children, I came to realize that the reality is entirely otherwise. I saw that little children possess an inherent propensity toward sin from their earliest infancy. Even before they can speak fluently, toddlers know how to be thoroughly selfish; they know how to snatch toys from other children; they strike out with their little hands; they display insatiable greed; they demand things from others while stubbornly refusing to share what they have. How often have we watched a toddler clamor for sweets or toys from his parents with boundless greed, asking for more and more; yet when his parents gently ask him to share a piece, or tell him to give some to his younger brother or sister, he adamantly refuses. Even if he does yield, he parts only with the tiniest scrap, or picks out the bruised, inferior portion to give away, while jealously keeping the best and largest for himself. This greed, this self-centeredness, this ungrateful selfishness was never taught to him by an instructor; it was born in him! Nor should this surprise us in the least. Since parents are sinners, how could the offspring they bring forth be free from sin? Yellow parents give birth to yellow children; white parents give birth to white children. Offspring inevitably resemble their progenitors; how then could children born of fallen sinners ever be without sin?

### 【中文】
我们现在认清楚人类的真面目了吗？都是罪人！都是从里面坏到外面的！都是生下来就有罪，而且一生常常犯罪的！人类在神面前完全败坏了！他们败坏到不可收拾的地步！有些人要借着「普及教育」去挽救这种败坏的状况；有些人要借着「提倡道德」去挽救这种败坏的状况；又有些人要借着「改良社会」的工作去挽救这种败坏的状况。但是结果怎样呢？力气用了不少，结果竟使人大失所望。他们很惊奇的说：「这是怎么一回事呢？我们想教育的力量能改善人的思想品行，怎么现在竟没有多少功效呢？我们想一般人那样败坏，是因为少有人提倡道德的缘故，如今我们这样努力提倡，怎么人类仍是照常的败坏呢？我们想人犯罪作恶是因为社会不良的缘故，若是能将社会改好，人一定可以不再犯罪作恶了，怎么我们费了九牛二虎的力气，竟不能将社会改得比从前好一点呢？」其实这有什么希奇呢？人是从里面败坏的，从外面下手修理一下，怎么能希望有功效呢？

### 【English】
Do we now perceive humanity's true condition? All are sinners! All are corrupt from the inside out! All are born in sin and spend their entire lives continually committing sin! Before a holy God, mankind is totally depraved—ruined beyond human remedy! Some seek to remedy this wretched condition through the spread of universal education; others attempt to salvage it by promoting ethical and moral crusades; still others labor through social reform programs to alleviate this corruption. But what is the ultimate outcome? Immense labor has been expended, yet the results end only in bitter disappointment. Reformers cry out in bewilderment: "How can this be? We assumed that the power of education would elevate human thought and conduct; why has it borne so little fruit? We thought the masses were corrupt merely because ethical values had not been vigorously promoted; yet now that we champion morality with all our might, why does humanity remain as corrupt as ever? We imagined people sinned because of a defective social environment; if only society could be reformed, surely people would cease from evil; why then, after exhausting all our Herculean efforts, have we failed to make society one whit better than before?" Why should this surprise anyone? Man is corrupt from within; how could patching him up from the outside ever produce genuine cure?

### 【中文】
树根若是干了，修理树叶有什么用处呢？泉源若是浊了，要帮助河水变清岂不是徒然的吗？神不作这愚笨的事，祂所采用的是「正本清源」的办法。祂知道若叫一个从里面到外面完全败坏了的人去学作好、学行善，是一件不可能的事。祂知道一个人的行为败坏，是因为他的生命先败坏了。祂知道人不能作好事是里面的问题，不是外面的问题；是生命的问题，不是行为的问题。因此祂不教训我们借着人的方法去改良我们这已经败坏的旧生命，祂只是对我们说：

### 【English】
If the root of a tree has withered, what profit is there in trimming its leaves? If the fountainhead is foul and polluted, is it not an exercise in futility to try to purify the river downstream? God never engages in such foolish vanity. The method He employs is to deal with the fountainhead and cure the root. He knows that to demand that a human being—thoroughly depraved from inside to outside—should learn to do good and practice virtue on his own is an absolute impossibility. He knows that a man's conduct is corrupt because his inner life is fundamentally ruined. He knows that man's inability to do good is an internal problem, not an external one; a problem of life, not of outward manners. Therefore, He does not instruct us to employ human stratagems to patch up our ruined old life; instead, He looks upon us and declares:

### 【中文】
「你们必须重生。」

### 【English】
"You must be born again."

---

### 【中文】
祂的意思是说：「你们的旧生命就是一切罪恶的起源；你们的旧生命永远不能修理得好，永远不能行善，永远不能使我满意。我现今给你们一个彻底的治疗方法，我要你们得着一种新生命以后，你们必定喜爱善良，恨恶罪恶；有了这个新的生命以后，你们方能行善，方能作好，方能得我的喜悦，方能进入我的国；因此我对你们说，你们必须重生。」

### 【English】
His meaning is this: "Your old life is the fountainhead of all iniquity; your old life can never be patched up, can never do good, and can never satisfy My holy demands. I now offer you a radical, total remedy: I will grant you a new life, and once you possess this new life, you will love righteousness and hate iniquity; endowed with this new life, you will be able to do good, to live uprightly, to be pleasing in My sight, and to enter My kingdom. Therefore, I say unto you: You must be born again!"
