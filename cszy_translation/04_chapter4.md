# 《重生真意》（中英双语对照版）
## 四、怎样方能得重生？ / Chapter 4: How Can One Be Born Again?

---

### 【中文】
四、怎样方能得重生？

### 【English】
Chapter 4: How Can One Be Born Again?

---

### 【中文】
「摩西在旷野怎样举蛇，人子也必照样被举起来，叫一切信祂的都得永生。」(约三14——15)

### 【English】
"And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in Him may have eternal life." (John 3:14–15)

---

### 【中文】
在这一段话中主耶稣指示尼哥底母得重生的方法。祂引用了一件古时以色列人所经历的事实，来阐明重生的要道。我们要清楚知道这一件事实，所以容我们先读这一段旧约的记载:

### 【English】
In these verses, the Lord Jesus points out to Nicodemus the way to receive the new birth. He cites a historical event experienced by ancient Israel to illuminate the fundamental truth of regeneration. In order to grasp this event with clarity, let us first read this account from the Old Testament:

---

### 【中文】
「他们从何珥山起行，往红海那条路走，要绕过以东地。百姓因这路难行，心中甚是烦躁，就怨讟神和摩西说:『你们为什么把我们从埃及领出来、使我们死在旷野呢？这里没有粮，没有水，我们的心厌恶这淡薄的食物。』于是耶和华使火蛇进入百姓中间，蛇就咬他们。以色列人中死了许多。百姓到摩西那里，说：『我们怨讟耶和华和你，有罪了。求你祷告耶和华，叫这些蛇离开我们。』于是摩西为百姓祷告。耶和华对摩西说:『你制造一条火蛇，挂在杆子上，凡被咬的，一望这蛇，就必得活。』摩西便制造一条铜蛇，挂在杆子上，凡被蛇咬的，一望这铜蛇就活了。」(民二一4～9)

### 【English】
"From Mount Hor they set out by the way to the Red Sea, to go around the land of Edom. And the people became impatient on the way. And the people spoke against God and against Moses, 'Why have you brought us up out of Egypt to die in the wilderness? For there is no food and no water, and we loathe this worthless food.' Then the LORD sent fiery serpents among the people, and they bit the people, so that many people of Israel died. And the people came to Moses and said, 'We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD and against you. Pray to the LORD, that He take away the serpents from us.' So Moses prayed for the people. And the LORD said to Moses, 'Make a fiery serpent and set it on a pole, and everyone who is bitten, when he sees it, shall live.' So Moses made a bronze serpent and set it on a pole. And if a serpent bit anyone, he would look at the bronze serpent and live." (Numbers 21:4–9)

---

### 【中文】
我们要注意这一段事迹的起源。以色列人被火蛇所咬，乃是因为他们埋怨神。他们所以埋怨神，乃是因为他们走过一段难走的路。神使他们走过这一段路不是害他们，也不是苦待他们，乃是引领他们经过旷野，进入迦南。但他们却因为这路难走，心中甚是烦躁，就埋怨神，攻击摩西。因此，耶和华打发火蛇进入他们中间，咬他们，致使许多人死亡。

### 【English】
We must observe how this crisis originated. The Israelites were bitten by fiery serpents because they grumbled against God. They grumbled against God because they were traveling along an arduous, difficult road. God did not lead them along this path to harm or mistreat them; He was guiding them through the wilderness in order to bring them into the Promised Land of Canaan. Yet because the journey was hard, their souls grew exceedingly impatient; they murmured against God and attacked Moses. Therefore, the LORD sent fiery serpents among them, which bit them so that many of the people died.

---

### 【中文】
请注意：每一个被火蛇所咬的人的生命已经断绝了。虽然被蛇咬以后的几小时或是一两天以内他还活着，照常动作，像常人一样，但蛇的毒已经进入他的血脉，他已经列入死人之数。若没有特别的方法救他，他必定要死，毫无幸免的希望。在这样危险绝望的时候，神命令摩西造了一条铜蛇，挂在杆子上，又叫他向百姓宣布说，凡被火蛇所咬的人一望这铜蛇就必得活。在这件事上，神显明了祂的公义和严厉，但同时也显明了祂的仁爱和慈悲。

### 【English】
Please take careful note: the moment an Israelite was bitten by a fiery serpent, his earthly life was virtually forfeit. Even though for several hours, or perhaps a day or two after being bitten, he remained alive, walked about, and functioned like any other person, the serpent's lethal venom had already penetrated his bloodstream; he was already numbered among the dead. Unless a miraculous deliverance intervened to save him, he was doomed to perish without the slightest hope of escape. At this moment of supreme peril and desolation, God commanded Moses to forge a bronze serpent and set it upon a pole, and instructed him to proclaim to the congregation: whoever is bitten by a fiery serpent, when he looks upon this bronze serpent, shall live! In this event, God manifested His righteous severity, yet simultaneously displayed His boundlessly tender mercy and compassionate grace.

---

### 【中文】
主耶稣就是用这件事来教导尼哥底母，将一个人得重生的方法指示他。祂告诉尼哥底母说:「摩西在旷野怎样举蛇，人子也必照样被举起来，叫一切信祂的都得永生。」祂告诉尼哥底母说，正如那些被火蛇所咬、将要死亡的以色列人仰望铜蛇便得了医治、得了生命一样，在罪恶中将要灭亡的世人信靠那挂在木头上为他们舍命的人子，也必得拯救、得永生。

### 【English】
The Lord Jesus employed this very historical narrative to instruct Nicodemus, revealing to him the exact manner by which a person is born again. He said to Nicodemus: "As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in Him may have eternal life." He showed Nicodemus that just as those bitten, dying Israelites looked upon the bronze serpent and were healed and preserved in life, so dying sinners perishing in their iniquities, when they look in faith to the Son of Man lifted up on the tree to lay down His life for them, are delivered and receive eternal life.

---

### 【中文】
因此我们的问题——怎样方能得重生？——在这里便得了一个清楚的答案——信耶稣基督。

### 【English】
Thus our question—How can one be born again?—receives right here a crystalline, definitive answer: By believing on the Lord Jesus Christ.

---

### 【中文】
必有人说:「得重生的方法既是这样筒单，那么所有基督徒必是都已经得重生了。但照前一章所提的一个人得重生以后所有的改变看来，却证明在基督徒当中有许多人是未得重生的，这究竟是怎么一回事呢？」

### 【English】
Some will surely say: "If the way to receive the new birth is so exceedingly simple, then surely everyone who is called a Christian must already be born again! Yet according to the life-transforming evidences set forth in the preceding chapter, it is evident that vast multitudes among professing Christians have never been born again at all. How do you account for this?"

---

### 【中文】
我们要明白这事，须先清楚明白「信耶稣」究竟是怎么一回事。我们以为一切称为基督徒的都已经信了耶稣吗？不，在那些人里，实在有一部分已经信了耶稣，但其中还有许多人离信耶稣的地步相差很远。

### 【English】
To understand this discrepancy, we must first comprehend with absolute clarity what "believing on Jesus" truly entails. Do we imagine that every person bearing the name of Christian has truly believed on Jesus? Far from it! Among their ranks, a genuine remnant has indeed believed on Jesus, but vast numbers remain miles away from genuine faith in Christ.

---

### 【中文】
一个被火蛇所咬的以色列人来望铜蛇是十分容易的事，然而这件十分容易的事却包含了几个重要的步骤：

### 【English】
For a snake-bitten Israelite to look upon the bronze serpent was in itself a very simple act; yet this simple act involved four vital, indispensable steps:

---

### 【中文】
第一，那个被蛇所咬的人必须知道他是被蛇咬了，然后他方肯来望铜蛇。假使有一个以色列人在睡觉的时侯被蛇咬了，他睡醒以后并不知道有一条火蛇曾咬了他，并且也没有别人看见这事来告诉他，他绝不会跑来望铜蛇。

### 【English】
First, the victim had to know that he had actually been bitten by a serpent before he would ever look upon the bronze serpent. Suppose an Israelite was bitten while fast asleep; when he awoke, he was totally unaware that a fiery serpent had struck him, and no bystander was there to inform him. Under such circumstances, he would never dream of running to look at the bronze serpent.

---

### 【中文】
第二，那个被蛇所咬的人必须知道他被蛇咬的结果是何等可怕。因为被蛇所咬不是疼痛几日，也不是红肿多少时候，或是受一些别的苦楚就完了。被蛇所咬的人是必定要死的。假使有人知道他被蛇咬了，但他以为只要用手摩擦几下，或是洗上一把凉水，便可以平安无事，他也绝不会去望铜蛇。

### 【English】
Second, the bitten man had to know how utterly terrifying the consequence of that bite was. For to be bitten by a fiery serpent was not merely to suffer a few days of localized pain, nor to endure transient swelling, nor to pass through some temporary discomfort; to be bitten by that venomous serpent meant certain, inexorable death! Suppose a man knew he had been bitten, but lightly assumed that rubbing the wound with his hand or washing it with cold water would make him fine—he, too, would never bother to look upon the bronze serpent.

---

### 【中文】
第三，那个被蛇所咬的人必须知道他自己没有一点方法能救自己。假使他想他手中有一种善治毒虫咬伤的良药，或是他想他精通医术，可以治疗自已的创伤，或是他想起他朋友曾介绍过一种解毒的偏方，他决不会跑去望杆子上的铜蛇。必等到他用尽了一切的方法，受尽了一切的痛苦，发见自己实在绝望的时候，他方肯来望铜蛇。

### 【English】
Third, the stricken man had to know that he possessed no earthly remedy within himself to effect his own cure. If he imagined he held in his hands some potent antidote for snake venom, or flattered himself that his medical skill could dress the wound, or remembered some folk remedy recommended by a friend, he would never run to look at the bronze serpent on the pole. Only when he had exhausted every human remedy, tasted the agony of despair, and discovered himself utterly helpless would he turn in earnest to look upon the bronze serpent.

---

### 【中文】
第四，那个被蛇所咬的人必须深信神的话。他信神藉摩西所传达的消息，信他照神所指示的去望铜蛇就必得医治，必得存活。这样他方会去望铜蛇。假使他不信神的话，我想他也必不肯去望铜蛇。

### 【English】
Fourth, the bitten Israelite had to believe God's Word with unwavering conviction. He had to believe the message communicated by God through Moses, trusting that if he looked upon the bronze serpent as God had commanded, he would surely be healed and live. Only with such faith would he look. If he refused to believe God's promise, he would never direct his gaze toward the bronze serpent.

---

### 【中文】
一个因罪而失去生命的罪人来信耶稣也是一件很容易的事，但这件容易的事也包含了相同的四个步骤：

### 【English】
For a sinner who has forfeited his life through sin to come and believe on Jesus is likewise a very simple matter; yet this simple act of saving faith encompasses these exact same four steps:

---

### 【中文】
第一，一个罪人必须知道自己真是犯了罪。第二，他必须知道犯罪的必要死亡。第三，他必须知道他毫无方法可以救自己。第四，他必须信神的话，仰望那挂在木头上的耶稣基督。

### 【English】
First, the sinner must know that he has truly committed sin against a holy God. Second, he must know that the wages of sin is eternal death. Third, he must know that he is totally powerless to save himself. Fourth, he must believe the Word of God and look in faith to Jesus Christ who was lifted up upon the cross!

---

### 【中文】
一个被毒蛇咬了的以色列人有了以上四步经验怎样立刻得了医治，照样一个罪人有了以上四步经验也就立刻得了拯救，得了重生。

### 【English】
Just as a venom-stricken Israelite who passed through these four steps was instantly healed, so any sinner who passes through these four steps of spiritual experience is immediately saved and born again!

---

### 【中文】
现今各地称为基督徒的数目诚然不少，但实在有过以上所提的四步经验的人究竟有多少呢？许多称为基督徒的不用说信神的话、仰望耶稣，甚至他们从来就末曾有一次真实彻底的知道自己是个罪人。

### 【English】
Today, those called Christians across the world are indeed legion; but how many have truly passed through these four steps of spiritual experience? Far from believing God's Word and looking unto Jesus, many who bear the Christian name have never once in their entire lives had a genuine, shattering realization that they are lost sinners before God!

---

### 【中文】
不是有许多人加入教会是因为生计困难，找事不易，想在教会或教会附属的机关里谋一个吃饭的地方，所以作了基督徒吗？不是有许多人因为在教会学校里教授，在教会的医院里服务，或是同基督徒作生意，为了饭碗的缘故，所以作了基督徒吗？不是有许多人因为想与某一个基督徒结婚，所以受洗作了基督徒吗？

### 【English】
Are there not multitudes who joined the church simply because life was hard, jobs were scarce, and they sought a livelihood within the church or its affiliated institutions—and so became "Christians"? Are there not many who, teaching in mission schools, serving in church hospitals, or doing business with Christians, took up the Christian label purely for the sake of their daily bread? Are there not many who submitted to baptism and became "Christians" merely because they wished to marry a Christian spouse?

---

### 【中文】
比这些较好一点的，有些人是因为想耶稣的教训可以造成伟大的人格，所以作了基督徒。有些人以为人必须信宗教，但各宗教中最好的首推基督教，所以作了基督徒。有些人想耶稣的博爱精神可以改良社会，可以促进世界大同，所以作了基督徒。

### 【English】
Somewhat higher than these, there are those who became Christians because they admired the moral teachings of Jesus as an inspiring ideal for cultivating a noble personality. Others became Christians believing that humanity needs some religion, and concluding that Christianity is the most enlightened among world faiths. Still others became Christians supposing that Jesus' spirit of universal love could reform society and advance world peace and brotherhood.

---

### 【中文】
以上所说的这些作基督徒的动机虽然各不相同，但在几样最要紧的事上却毫无差别，就是：他们都未曾知道自己有罪；都末曾知道罪人的结局；都未曾知道自己不能救自己；都未曾信靠那位在十字架上为他们舍命的主耶稣。

### 【English】
Though the motivations of these professing Christians differ widely, in the most essential matters they are all identical: Not one of them has ever realized his personal guilt as a sinner; not one has ever perceived the horrifying eternal damnation awaiting the unrepentant; not one has recognized his absolute inability to save himself; and not one has placed his wholehearted faith in the Lord Jesus Christ who shed His blood on the cross for sinners!

---

### 【中文】
这些人自己说他们是基督徒，其他人也有承认他们是基督徒，称呼他们为基督徒的。这些人中甚至有一些还自称是为神作工的，是传道士，是牧师，是长老，是执事。别人也这样称呼他们。但事实上他们却完全在基督以外，未曾得着重生。

### 【English】
These individuals profess themselves to be Christians, and others acknowledge and address them as Christians. Among their number are even some who claim to be workers for God—preachers, pastors, elders, and deacons—and others address them with these reverend titles. Yet in sober spiritual reality, they remain completely outside of Christ, utterly destitute of the new birth!

---

### 【中文】
我们切不要以为每一个加入教会自称为基督徒的人便是真信耶稣的人，便是真得了重生的人。唯独那些真有过上面所提的四步经验的人才是真信了耶稣、真得了重生的人。许多称为基督徒的人没有重生的表现，就是因为他们根本未曾信耶稣，未曾得重生。

### 【English】
We must never naively imagine that every person who joins a church and calls himself a Christian is a true believer in Jesus and truly born again! Only those who have genuinely passed through the four steps outlined above are true believers in Jesus and genuinely born of the Spirit. The reason so many professing Christians display no evidences of the new birth is simply that they have never truly believed in Jesus at all, and have never been born again!

---

### 【中文】
称为基督徒的人中有许多已经得了重生，这是毫无可疑的事实，但不是所有称为基督徒的都是得了重生的人。一个人得重生并不在乎他在哪个教会记名，在哪个教会受洗，到哪个礼拜堂做礼拜。

### 【English】
That many among those called Christians have indeed been born again is an indisputable truth; but by no means are all who bear the name of Christian regenerate. A person's new birth depends not in the least on which church register holds his name, in which denomination he was baptized, or which sanctuary he frequents for worship.

---

### 【中文】
一个人得重生唯一的方法就是知道自己有罪，知道自己的危险，知道自己无法拯救自己，听见神的福音就信从，就来仰望被钉十字架的耶稣，未曾得重生的人走这条道路立刻就能得重生。

### 【English】
The one and only way for a person to be born again is this: to know that he is a guilty sinner; to know his imminent eternal peril; to know that he is utterly helpless to save himself; and upon hearing God's Gospel, to believe it with the heart and look in faith to the crucified Jesus. The unregenerate who walks this path will immediately be born again!

---

### 【中文】
在这里我们又看出得重生是很快的一件事。那个被蛇所咬的以色列人「一望铜蛇就活了」。注意这个「一望铜蛇就活了」。那个望铜蛇的人在很短的时间内就因信得了生命。他来的时候是一个被蛇咬伤、满身是毒、快要死的人；他回转去的时候，却成了一个毒气全消、满有生机的人。

### 【English】
Here we discover another glorious truth: the new birth takes place instantaneously! When the snake-bitten Israelite looked upon the bronze serpent, the Scripture records: "when he sees it, he shall live"—he looked and lived! Observe that phrase: "he looked and lived!" In a split second, through faith, that man received life. When he first dragged himself toward the pole, he was a dying man, riddled with deadly venom, on the brink of the grave; yet the moment he turned his gaze upon the serpent of bronze, the venom was neutralized, and he returned whole, vibrant with life!

---

### 【中文】
一个罪人因信耶稣基督而得重生也是这样。只要这个人真认识了自己，真知道了自己的污秽、不义、痛苦、绝望，愿意笃信神的话，信靠那为罪人舍命的基督，什么时侯他这样作，神便在什么时候赦免他的罪，称他为义，赐给他永生，使他从水和圣灵而生。

### 【English】
It is precisely so when a sinner is born again through faith in Jesus Christ. The moment a person truly comes to the end of himself, truly acknowledges his inward filth, unrighteousness, misery, and despair, and wholeheartedly embraces the Word of God, placing his faith in Christ who laid down His life for sinners—in that very instant, God forgives his sins, justifies him freely, imparts unto him eternal life, and causes him to be born of water and the Holy Spirit!

---

### 【中文】
昨天他还是死人，今日他已经得了生命；昨天他还遍体污秽，今日他已经像雪一样的洁白；昨天他还是神的仇敌，今日他已经成为神的儿子。第一次生产是很快的事，一个小孩未出生以前是在母腹中，但一落草便成了一个活人，重生的事也是这样。

### 【English】
Yesterday he was dead in trespasses and sins; today he is alive forevermore! Yesterday he was covered in vile defilement; today he is washed whiter than snow! Yesterday he was an alien and an enemy of God; today he is a beloved son of God! Physical birth is a sudden, decisive event: before birth, the child is concealed within the womb; but the moment he is delivered, he is a living human being. So it is with the new birth!

---

### 【中文】
按圣经所讲的道理来说，一个人的罪无论怎样大，他的境况无论怎样可怜，只要他真知道自己有罪，真有诚恳悔改求救的心，笃信神的话来投靠耶稣基督，他立时便得赦罪，便被称义，便得重生，便有永生。

### 【English】
According to the truth revealed in Holy Scripture, no matter how monstrous a person's sins may be, and no matter how wretched his condition, if only he acknowledges his guilt, genuinely repents, and flees for refuge to Jesus Christ in reliance upon God's promise, he receives that very instant the full pardon of his sins, is justified before God, is born again, and possesses eternal life!

---

### 【中文】
不知道什么缘故，这宝贵的福音竟被人讲得乱七八糟，以致多少人以为得救是极困难的事，重生是最不易得的经验，永生总须等到死后才能得着。因此许多已经真实悔改的信徒仍是常常疑惑自己的得救，更不用提那些软弱未曾重生的信徒了。

### 【English】
For reasons hard to comprehend, this precious, glorious Gospel has been so terribly garbled and confused by human preaching that multitudes have been misled into imagining that salvation is an almost impossible mountain to climb, that regeneration is a mystical experience beyond ordinary reach, and that eternal life cannot be known until after physical death. Consequently, countless believers who have genuinely repented are tormented by perpetual doubts concerning their salvation—to say nothing of those weak souls who have never been born again at all.

---

### 【中文】
要挽救这种可怕的现象，最要紧的就是使人明白真诚悔改信靠耶稣，立时就得重生，就有永生，就蒙拯救。讲明这种根本的要道，真是今日急不容缓的事啊！

### 【English】
To remedy this disastrous state of affairs, the supreme necessity is to proclaim with all clarity that whoever repents and believes on Jesus receives at once the new birth, possesses eternal life, and is delivered from wrath! To declare this foundational truth plainly is indeed the most urgent task confronting the church today!

---

### 【中文】
是的，主耶稣在十字架上已经完成了救工，不用我们再设什么方法去救自己，也不用我们再在这种救工上加添什么。只要真诚悔改，承认自己的罪，全心信靠那为我们舍命的主耶稣，立时就得拯救，立时就得重生，立时就有永生。

### 【English】
Yes! The Lord Jesus has already finished the work of salvation upon the cross! There is no need for us to devise human schemes to save ourselves, nor is there any possibility of adding our puny works to His finished redemption. If only you sincerely repent, confess your sins, and trust with your whole heart in the Lord Jesus who died in your stead, you are instantly saved, instantly born again, and instantly possessed of eternal life!

---

### 【中文】
你还未这样信吗？现在就信，现在你就能得着重生，得着永生。你已经这样信了吗？你当知道你已经得了重生，有了永生了，因为主耶稣绝不欺骗我们。祂的话是可信的。祂说:「摩西在旷野怎样举蛇，人子也必照样被举起来，叫一切信祂的都有永生。」

### 【English】
Have you not yet believed in this manner? Believe right now! In this very moment you can receive the new birth and possess eternal life! Have you already believed in this manner? Then know with unshakeable certainty that you have indeed been born again and already possess eternal life! For the Lord Jesus will never deceive us. His Word is faithful and true. He Himself declared: "As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in Him may have eternal life."
