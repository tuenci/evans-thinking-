# 《重生真意》（中英双语对照版）
## 一、重生是什么意思？ / Chapter 1: What Does It Mean to Be Born Again?

---

### 【中文】
一、 重生是什么意思？

### 【English】
Chapter 1: What Does It Mean to Be Born Again? (What Is Regeneration?)

---

### 【中文】
「耶稣回答说：『我实实在在地告诉你，人若不重生，就不能见神的国。』」(约三3)

### 【English】
"Jesus answered him, 'Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born again he cannot see the kingdom of God.'" (John 3:3)

---

### 【中文】
「耶稣说：『我实实在在地告诉你，人若不是从水和圣灵生的，就不能进神的国。』」 (约三5)

### 【English】
"Jesus answered, 'Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God.'" (John 3:5)

---

### 【中文】
「重生」是什么意思呢？「重」就是「再」的意思。一个人以前作过一件事，现在他再作，我们就说重作。比方说一个人在若干年前曾到过一个地方，现在他又来到这个地方，我们就说他「旧地重游」。「生」这一个字在中文有许多的意思，就如「生产」、「生长」、「生命」、「生存」等等，但这里所说的「生」，乃是「生产」的意思。那样，我们把「重生」这两个字放在一处，就是「再生产一次」。每一个人都曾经过一次的生产。当他乍出母腹，呱呱落地的时侯，那是第一次的生产。每一个人都必须经过这第一次的生产，然后方能进入这个世界，方能成为一个人。但他要进入神的国，要成为神的孩子，却非经过第二次的生产不可。因为主耶稣的话明明说：「我实实在在地告诉你，人若不重生，就不能见神的国。」

### 【English】
What is the meaning of being "born again" (regeneration)? In Chinese, the character *chóng* (重) means "again" or "anew." When someone did something in the past and does it a second time, we say it is done "over again." For instance, if a person visited a place years ago and now returns, we say he is "revisiting an old haunt." The character *shēng* (生) carries many meanings in Chinese—such as "to give birth / be born," "to grow," "life," or "to exist"—but as used here, it specifically means "to be born" or "birth." Thus, when we combine these two characters into *chóng-shēng* (重生), it literally signifies "to be born once again." Every human being has already undergone one birth. When we emerged from our mother's womb and cried our first cry upon this earth, that was our first birth. Every individual must pass through this first birth in order to enter this world and become a human being. But if a person is to enter the kingdom of God and become a child of God, he must without exception undergo a second birth. For the Lord Jesus explicitly declared: "Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born again he cannot see the kingdom of God."

---

### 【中文】
必有人在听见这话以后要十分惊异的问道：「人怎能再生产一次呢？这样大的一个人还能回到母腹中再生一次吗？」人问这样的问题是不足为奇的，当日与耶稣谈话的尼哥底母听见耶稣讲论重生的道理，也曾问过这个问题说：「人已经老了，如何能重生呢？岂能再进母腹生出来吗？」「重生」的道理本是我们从来未曾想到的，乃是神的一个大奥秘，若非主耶稣清清楚楚的解明，无论什么人听见「重生」这句话都不能不感觉落在五里雾中一样。

### 【English】
Hearing these words, some will undoubtedly ask in utter bewilderment: "How can a person be born a second time? Can a grown adult re-enter his mother's womb and be born all over again?" It is hardly surprising that people should raise such a question. On that night long ago, when Nicodemus conversed with Jesus and heard Him expound the truth of regeneration, he asked that very question: "How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother's womb and be born?" The doctrine of the new birth is something that never would have entered the human mind; it is a profound mystery of God. Unless the Lord Jesus had explained it clearly, anyone hearing the phrase "born again" would inevitably feel lost in a dense fog.

---

### 【中文】
主耶稣怎样回答尼哥底母这个问题呢？祂说：「我实实在在地告诉你，人若不是从水和圣灵生的，就不能进神的国。从肉身生的就是肉身；从灵生的就是灵。我说『你们必须重生』，你不要以为希奇。风随着意思吹，你听见风的响声，却不晓得从哪里来，往哪里去；凡从圣灵生的，也是如此。」(约三5-8)

### 【English】
How did the Lord Jesus answer Nicodemus' perplexity? He said: "Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God. That which is born of the flesh is flesh, and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit. Do not marvel that I said to you, 'You must be born again.' The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit." (John 3:5–8)

---

### 【中文】
祂叫尼哥底母知道，重生并不是进到母腹中，然后从母腹中再生产一次。这样的生产纵使再有几十次、几百次，仍是毫无补益，因为「从肉身生的就是肉身」。重生不是从肉身生，乃是从「水和圣灵」生。

### 【English】
He made Nicodemus realize that being born again does not mean entering back into a mother's womb to undergo physical birth once more. Even if such a physical birth could be repeated tens or hundreds of times, it would still profit nothing, for "that which is born of the flesh is flesh." Regeneration is not a birth from the flesh; it is a birth from "water and the Spirit."

---

### 【中文】
这里所说的水不是物质的水，乃是指着神的道。我们都知道一切有生命的物都不能离了水；照这样，属灵的生命也不能离了神的道。我们又知道，水能洁净一切污秽的东西；照这样，神的道也能洁净我们心身一切的污秽。圣经中用水代表神的道真是最合宜的(参弗五25-27；约一五3)。圣经中又有两段说话清清楚楚的告诉我们，这里所说的水是指着神的道：

### 【English】
The "water" spoken of here is not physical water, but refers to the Word of God. We all know that no living creature can survive without water; in the same manner, spiritual life cannot exist apart from the Word of God. Furthermore, we know that water cleanses all filth and defilement; likewise, the Word of God cleanses all impurities from our hearts and lives. Throughout the Scriptures, using water as an emblem of the Word of God is supremely fitting (cf. Eph 5:25–27; John 15:3). Moreover, two other passages in Scripture explicitly demonstrate to us that the "water" mentioned here signifies the Word of God:

---

### 【中文】
「我亲爱的弟兄们，不要看错了。各样美善的恩赐和各样全备的赏赐都是从上头来的，从众光之父那里降下来的；在祂并没有改变，也没有转动的影儿。祂按自己的旨意，用真道生了我们，叫我们在祂所造的万物中好象初熟的果子。」(雅一16-18)

### 【English】
"Do not be deceived, my beloved brothers. Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom there is no variation or shadow due to change. Of His own will He brought us forth by the word of truth, that we should be a kind of firstfruits of His creatures." (James 1:16–18)

---

### 【中文】
「你们蒙了重生，不是由于能坏的种子，乃是由于不能坏的种子，是借着神活泼常存的道。因为凡有血气的，尽都如草；他的美荣都像草上的花。草必枯干，花必凋谢；惟有主的道是永存的。所传给你们的福音就是这道。」(彼前一23-25)

### 【English】
"Since you have been born again, not of perishable seed but of imperishable, through the living and abiding word of God; for 'All flesh is like grass and all its glory like the flower of grass. The grass withers, and the flower falls, but the word of the Lord remains forever.' And this word is the good news that was preached to you." (1 Peter 1:23–25)

---

### 【中文】
第一次的生产是从肉身生的。肉身是污秽的、必朽坏的，所以凡从肉身生的也是污秽的、必朽坏的。第二次的生产是从神的道和神的灵生的。神的道和神的灵是圣洁的、是不朽坏的，所以凡从水和圣灵生的也是圣洁的、不朽坏的。「从肉身生的就是肉身；从灵生的就是灵。」

### 【English】
The first birth is of the flesh. The flesh is corrupt, polluted, and perishable; therefore, whatever is born of the flesh is likewise corrupt, polluted, and perishable. The second birth is of the Word of God and the Spirit of God. The Word and the Spirit of God are holy and imperishable; therefore, whoever is born of water and the Spirit is likewise holy and imperishable. "That which is born of the flesh is flesh, and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit."

---

### 【中文】
有人说：「重生这种说法并不是说真有一次生产的事实，不过是一个比喻，表明一个人悔悟以前的罪恶，决定心志要作一个好人，并且他真能洗心革面，痛改前非，这种心志和行为正好象一个人又重新生产了一次一样。先哲所说：『从前种种譬如昨日死，以后种种譬如今日生』，也就是这个意思。」

### 【English】
Some say: "The expression 'born again' does not denote an actual, factual birth; it is merely a metaphor indicating that an individual has repented of his past sins, resolved to become a good person, and genuinely turned over a new leaf. Such a resolve and moral reform are compared to a person being born afresh. This is just what the ancient sages meant when they said: 'Let all things past die as of yesterday, and all things to come be born as of today.'"

---

### 【中文】
说这种话的人明显有两个极大的错误：第一，他们擅自更改了主耶稣所讲的话。主耶稣未曾说重生是个比喻。祂以第二次的生产为一件事实，如同第一次的生产是一件事实一样。如果我们以第二次的生产为一个比喻，那样第一次的生产也必是一个比喻了。请问这样的说法通不通呢？

### 【English】
Those who make such assertions commit two glaring, fundamental errors: First, they take the liberty of distorting what the Lord Jesus explicitly taught. The Lord Jesus never said that regeneration is a metaphor. He treated the second birth as an objective reality, just as the first birth is an objective reality. If we reduce the second birth to a mere metaphor, then by the same logic, the first birth must also be regarded as a metaphor. Does such an assertion make any sense at all?

---

### 【中文】
假如有人告诉你说，你的一个朋友在昨天晚间生了一个儿子，你便问她说：「这是一个比喻呢，还是一件事实呢？」你想这个问题是何等没有理性、何等可笑呢？！再比方说有一次你向人述说你的履历，你说你在某年某月某日生在某某地方。听见的人便向你发问说：「那是一个比喻呢，还是一件事实呢？」请问你要怎样回答这个问题呢？你一定要哂笑那个发问题的人，说他是患精神病的。好了，你既承认第一次的生产是一件事实，怎么能说第二次的生产是一个比喻呢？

### 【English】
Suppose someone comes and tells you that your friend gave birth to a son last night, and you were to ask her: "Is this a metaphor, or is it an actual fact?" Can you imagine how absurd and utterly irrational such a question would sound?! Or suppose you were reciting your biographical resume to someone, stating that you were born on such-and-such a date in such-and-such a place. If the listener were to ask you: "Is that a metaphor, or is it an actual fact?" how would you respond? You would surely laugh him to scorn and conclude that he was mentally deranged. Well then, since you acknowledge that the first birth is a literal fact, how dare you claim that the second birth is a mere metaphor?

---

### 【中文】
说这种话的人的第二个错误，就是他们完全弄错了「生」的意思。一个小孩子生出来，乃是被他的父母所生。他不能生他自己。这样，一个人被重生也是如此，他是被神所生，他完全是处于被动的地位。若照上面所提的那种说法，说一个人洗心革面、痛改前非就是重生，那便是说他重生了自己。我们走遍天涯地角也不会找出「一个人生他自己」这种怪事来。不错，一个人必须痛心悔改，信靠耶稣，才能得重生。但我们当知道那是得重生的条件。一个人履行这个条件，神方能借着道和圣灵生他。痛心悔改这件事是我们作的，生我们这件事乃是神作的。若说痛心悔改方能得重生，这话是对的。若说痛心悔改就是重生，这话便全盘错误了。

### 【English】
The second error of those who hold this view is that they completely misunderstand the very meaning of the word "birth." When an infant is born, he is brought forth by his parents; he cannot give birth to himself. In exactly the same way, when a person is regenerated, he is born of God—he is entirely in the passive position. If, according to the aforesaid human view, a person's moral reformation and turning over a new leaf constituted regeneration, that would mean a person gives birth to himself! You could search to the ends of the earth and never find so bizarre a monstrosity as "a person giving birth to himself." To be sure, a person must genuinely repent of his sins and believe on the Lord Jesus in order to be born again. But we must understand that repentance and faith are the conditions for receiving the new birth. When a person meets this condition, God then brings him forth through His Word and His Spirit. Repenting with a broken heart is our part; bringing us to new birth is God's sovereign act. To say that "one must repent in order to be born again" is entirely true; but to claim that "repentance itself is regeneration" is utterly false.

---

### 【中文】
必有人说，我们承认第一次的生产是一件事实，因为我们看见了那件事实。我们看见一个产妇在产房里生育，又看见一个几磅重的婴儿呱呱落地；但我们从来末曾看见过一个人怎样得重生，也末曾看见过从水和圣灵生的那个人。没有看见过的事怎能说是事实呢？

### 【English】
Some will object: "We acknowledge that the first birth is a fact because we can see it with our eyes. We see a mother laboring in the delivery room, and we see an infant of several pounds crying as it enters the world. But we have never seen how a person is born again, nor have we ever laid eyes on that person who is born of water and the Spirit. How can something we have never seen be called a fact?"

---

### 【中文】
真的吗？没有看见过的事便不能是事实吗？我们中间有谁看见过神呢？有谁看见过主耶稣呢？有谁看见过圣灵呢？有谁看见过天使呢？又有谁看见过魔鬼呢？我们虽然末曾看见过神，但仍确信有神；虽然末曾看见过主耶稣，却仍信有耶稣；虽然末曾看见过圣灵，却仍信有圣灵；虽然末曾看见过天使与魔鬼，却仍信有天使与魔鬼；就是因为这些都是属于灵界的。物质界有许多的事实，灵界也有许多的事实。不过因为我们现今处在物质界内，我们的身体和眼睛也都是属于物质界的，所以不能看见灵界的事实。圣灵是确实有的，一个人从水和圣灵生也是一件真确的事实，不过这都是我们属物质的眼睛所不能看见的罢了。

### 【English】
Is that really so? Can something not be a fact simply because it has not been seen by mortal eyes? Who among us has ever seen God? Who has seen the Lord Jesus? Who has seen the Holy Spirit? Who has seen angels? And who has seen the devil? Though we have never seen God with physical eyes, we firmly believe God exists; though we have not seen the Lord Jesus, we believe in Jesus; though we have not seen the Holy Spirit, we believe in the Spirit; though we have not seen angels or demons, we believe in their reality—precisely because all these belong to the spiritual realm. Just as there are innumerable facts in the physical realm, so there are innumerable facts in the spiritual realm. But because we currently dwell in the physical realm, and our physical bodies and optical eyes belong to the material order, we cannot visually behold the realities of the spiritual realm. The Holy Spirit is absolute reality, and a person being born of water and the Spirit is an indisputable, objective fact; it is simply invisible to our mortal eyes.

---

### 【中文】
又有人要问说，重生既是一件事实，那样必定有一个新生出来的人了，但是那个人在哪里呢？我们要回答这个问题：「是，实在有一个新生出来的人；圣经中称这个人为『里面的人』，同时称那第一次所生的人为『外面的人』。」容我们读经上的话：「所以，我们不丧胆。外体虽然毁坏，内心却一天新似一天。」(林后四16)

### 【English】
Others may ask: "If regeneration is indeed a literal fact, then there must be a newly born person! Where is this person?" To this question we answer: "Yes, there is indeed a newly born person! The Scriptures call this person 'the inner man' (or inner self), while designating the person produced by the first birth as 'the outer man' (or outward self)." Let us read the words of Scripture: "So we do not lose heart. Though our outer self is wasting away, our inner self is being renewed day by day." (2 Corinthians 4:16)

---

### 【中文】
这一节经文在中文的译本里有两个错误：第一，这一节经文的希腊原文有一个「人」字，中文的译本却把它译作「体」字；第二，中文的译本加了一个「心」字，是原文里所没有的。这一节经文在原文是说：「所以，我们不丧胆。我们外面的人虽然毁坏，里面却一天新似一天。」前一句说「外面的人」，后一句是说「里面的」。后一句虽然没有「人」字，但对照上面的意思，也可以清楚看出它的意思是说「里面的人」。所以英文的译本就在后一句当中「里面的」以后加上一个「人」字，在这一节中我们清楚看出信徒的两个人：一个是「外面的人」，是从肉体生的，是显于外的、看得见的；一个是「里面的人」，他是从灵生的，是隐于内的、看不见的。可惜在中文的圣经译本里竟把这「外面的人」译作「外体」；又把这「里面的人」译作「内心」，以致这一节中很重要的一样真理竟被掩藏起来。

### 【English】
In this verse, the traditional Chinese translation contains two shortcomings: First, the original Greek text explicitly contains the word "man" (ἄνθρωπος), but the Chinese version rendered it as "body" (体); second, the Chinese version inserted the word "heart" (心), which is entirely absent from the Greek original. In the original text, the verse literally says: "Therefore we do not lose heart. Though our outer man is decaying, yet the inward is being renewed day by day." The first clause speaks of the "outer man," and the second speaks of "the inward." Although the word "man" is not repeated in the second clause, the parallel context clearly demonstrates that it means "the inner man." For this reason, English translations rightly render it as "the inward man" or "the inner self." In this verse, we plainly perceive the two distinct beings within a believer: one is the "outer man," born of the flesh, visible and external; the other is the "inner man," born of the Spirit, hidden and unseen. It is regrettable that the Chinese translation rendered "outer man" merely as "outer body" and "inner man" merely as "inner heart," thereby obscuring a vital biblical truth contained within this passage.

---

### 【中文】
除去以上所提的一节经文以外，还有两处经文提到这「里面的人」，可惜这两处经文在中文的译本里也都没有按着原文的意思译出来。其中一段经文是罗马书七章二十二节，这一节中「里面的人」在中文译本译作「里面的意思」；另一段是以弗所书三章十六节，这一节中「里面的人」在中文译本译作「心里的力量」。其实「内心」、「里面的意思」和「心里的力量」这几个名词在原文都是「里面的人」。这「里面的人」是神所生的，所以他「喜欢神的律」(罗七22)。他与那「外面的人」是绝不相同的，所以「外面的人」虽然因为经过种种苦楚患难而渐渐毁坏，「里面的人」却因此一天新似一天(林后四16)。

### 【English】
In addition to the verse cited above, two other passages mention this "inner man," both of which, unfortunately, were similarly obscured in the Chinese version without reflecting the literal Greek meaning. One passage is Romans 7:22, where "inner man" was translated into Chinese as "my inner intention" (里面的意思); the other is Ephesians 3:16, where "inner man" was rendered as "power in the heart" (心里的力量). In the original Greek, however, each of these expressions—rendered in Chinese variously as "inner heart," "inner intention," and "inner strength"—is literally "the inner man" (τὸν ἔσω ἄνθρωπον). This "inner man" is born of God, and therefore he "delights in the law of God" (Rom 7:22). He is radically different from the "outer man"; hence, though the "outer man" gradually decays through diverse afflictions and trials, the "inner man" is thereby renewed day by day (2 Cor 4:16).

---

### 【中文】
但这「里面的人」也能因着种种原因变为刚强或软弱，所以使徒求神「按着祂丰盛的荣耀，藉看祂的灵」，使信徒「里面的人刚强起来」。(弗三16)

### 【English】
Yet this "inner man" can also become either strong or weak due to various conditions. Therefore, the Apostle prayed that God "according to the riches of His glory may grant you to be strengthened with power through His Spirit in the inner man." (Eph 3:16)
