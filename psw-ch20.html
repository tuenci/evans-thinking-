LEARNING OBJECTIVES · 执业胜任力标准

OBJECTIVES

本章学习与执业考核目标 (14 大核心能力指标)
After reading this chapter, the learner should be able to do the following:
研读并掌握本章知识后，学习者应当能够全面达成以下临床核心技能与理论目标：
  • • List the risk factors for accidents.
    系统列举引发各类意外事故与身体伤害的危险因素（年龄生理变化、意识障碍、视听感官减退及药物副作用）。
  • • Describe how identifying the client, providing call bells, and correctly using bed rails promote client safety.
    详述严格核对案主身份、确保呼叫铃触手可及以及合规使用床栏在保障案主生命安全中的核心价值。
  • • Identify the safety measures to prevent falls.
    熟练掌握预防案主跌倒摔伤的综合性临床防护措施（环境清理、防滑鞋具、离床报警器及辅助用具）。
  • • Identify how a client’s quality of life can suffer as a result of being restrained.
    深刻阐明保护性约束对案主生活质量、躯体机能、心理尊严与自主权利造成的严重负面损害。
  • • Identify causes of early childhood deaths from injury.
    识别导致婴幼儿及儿童发生意外创伤死亡的常见原因（窒息、溺水、中毒、跌落与烧烫伤）。
  • • Explain how the developmental stage a child is in can influence their risk for injury.
    阐明儿童所处的各生理认知发育阶段如何显著影响其面临的意外伤害风险特征。
  • • Identify the safety measures to prevent falls, poisoning, suffocation, and burns for all age groups.
    全面掌握适用于各年龄段人群的防跌倒、防意外中毒、防窒息与防烧烫伤的标准化防护措施。
  • • Describe the causes of and emergency care for burns.
    详述烧烫伤的常见病理诱因及发生烫伤时的现场紧急急救处置流程（冷水冲洗、严禁涂抹油脂）。
  • • Identify fire prevention measures.
    识别机构与居家环境中的火灾隐患及关键防火安全预防措施（安全用氧、严禁吸烟、电器检查）。
  • • Describe what to do during a fire.
    熟练掌握火灾发生时的标准应急响应程序（RACE 救援警报隔离灭火撤离法与 PASS 灭火器四步法）。
  • • Describe how to prevent equipment accidents.
    阐明预防医疗护理仪器设备意外的标准化操作规范（三孔接地插头、杜绝破损电线、异味停机报告）。
  • • Describe how restraints can cause injury to clients and why they should be avoided.
    深入阐述约束可能引发的致命身体伤害（胸部压迫窒息、绞勒死亡、皮肤破溃、骨折）及坚决践行约束替代方案的必要性。
  • • List the steps to reporting a workplace injury, as identified by the Workplace Safety and Insurance Board.
    明确安大略省工作场所安全与保险委员会 (WSIB) 规定的工伤事故与职业伤害申报法定闭环步骤。
  • • Apply the information provided in this chapter to protect yourself in the workplace.
    将本章所学的职业防护与安全知识全面应用于临床与社区执业实务中，切实防范职场暴力与人身伤害。

SAFETY: A BASIC NEED AND A RIGHT

安全：全人类的基本生理需求与法定权利

Safety is a basic need and a right for all people. People of all ages need to both be safe and feel safe, in all circumstances, including on social media, regardless of their circumstances.

安全对所有人而言都是一项最基本的生理需求和不可剥夺的人身权利。无论处于何种境遇（包括在日常社交媒体活动中），所有年龄段的人都既需要置身于安全之中，也需要在心理上切实感受到安全。

You, as the support worker, your clients, and your co-workers have the right to safety, whether in a client’s home or in a facility where you are employed. Promoting client safety is one of the priorities of support work.

作为支持工作者，您、您的案主以及您的同事都享有法定的安全权利——无论是在案主的私人居所中，还是在您受雇的照护机构内。积极促进并维护案主的安全是支持工作中最崇高的优先要务之一。

Because of age or a physical or mental health condition, many clients are at great risk for accidents and injuries, so facilities and community care agencies try to create and maintain a safe environment for their clients. While this chapter’s main focus is on a number of aspects involving everyday safety, there are many other types of safety that are also addressed in other chapters throughout the book, including psychological safety, cultural safety, safety in the workplace, and freedom from bullying and prejudice.

由于高龄衰老、身体伤残或心理健康受损，许多受助案主面临发生各类意外事故与身体伤害的巨大风险，因此各类照护机构与社区服务机构都竭力为案主营造并维持一个安全的物理与人文环境。尽管本章的核心聚焦于日常生活照护中的各项安全维度，但在全书的其他章节中也系统探讨了心理安全、文化安全、执业场所安全以及免受霸凌与歧视的安全环境。

In a safe environment, clients face little risk of accidents or injuries, and they feel safe and secure, physically and emotionally. You and your employer share the responsibility of ensuring client and worker safety. Your employer must ensure that workplace hazards are identified and eliminated or controlled, and you must follow safe work practices and report hazards promptly.

在一个安全的环境中，案主面临极低的事故与损伤风险，在躯体和情绪层面都能获得充分的安全感与踏实感。您与您的雇主共同承担着确保案主与医护人员生命安全的根本责任。雇主必须确保执业环境中的各类危险源被精准识别、根除或严格受控；而作为支持工作者，您必须时刻恪守安全操作规程，并第一时间上报任何潜在隐患。

ACCIDENT RISK FACTORS

意外事故与伤害的高危风险诱因剖析

Older persons, children, and people living with illnesses or disabilities are especially vulnerable to experiencing accidents. Factors that increase their risk for accidental injury include impaired awareness of surroundings, sensory deficits, mobility impairments, medication side effects, and age.

老年人、年幼儿童以及罹患急慢性疾病或身体伤残的人群尤其容易发生各类意外事故。显著增大其意外伤害风险的关键诱因包括对周围环境认知警觉性下降、感官知觉减退、肢体活动受限、药物不良反应以及年龄生理变化。

The Client With Awareness Challenges

意识状态受损与认知警觉性障碍案主

To protect themselves from injury, people need to be aware of their surroundings. Children are not old enough to be aware of the potential harm that many hazards can cause.

为了保护自身免受伤害，人们必须时刻清晰感知周围环境。儿童由于心智发育尚不成熟，往往无法预见诸多日常危险源的潜在破坏力。

As well, people who live with intellectual disabilities, some physical disabilities, or who are disoriented might not be able to detect and avoid dangers and could, therefore, be harmed unintentionally. For example, a disoriented client may wander outside in the middle of winter without wearing a coat, gloves, or boots. Children and people who are unaware of their surroundings (such as an unconscious person) must, therefore, rely on others for protection.

同样，患有智力障碍、特定躯体残疾或处于定向力障碍（谵妄、认知症）状态的案主，难以敏锐察觉并躲避危险，因而极易遭受非预期的伤害。例如，定向力障碍案主可能在寒冬腊月未穿大衣、手套或棉靴便擅自走出室外游荡。儿童以及完全丧失环境感知能力的案主（如昏迷患者），必须完全依赖他人的严密监护与保护。

Distracted or Fatigued Caregivers

注意力分散或处于深度疲劳状态的照护人员

People (including health care workers!) who are preoccupied with things other than the task they are doing are more likely to have accidents because of a decreased awareness of their surroundings. Some may just be tired; according to research, fatigue is a factor in many motor vehicle accidents along Canada’s highways. Caregivers who are exhausted, hurried, or multi-tasking can make serious errors in client identification, medication handling, and transfers.

包括医护人员在内的任何人，若在执行操作时心不在焉、被与手头任务无关的琐事牵绊，都会因周围环境警觉性骤降而极易酿成事故。部分人员可能仅仅是因为过度疲劳；根据加拿大交通研究，疲劳是导致全国高速公路大量机动车恶性交通事故的核心诱因。身心力竭、慌忙赶工或在多任务间频繁切换的照护者，在核对案主身份、转运移位以及给药辅助中极易发生致命性差错。

The Client With Vision Challenges

存在视力减退、视野缺损与深度知觉障碍的案主

Poor vision can lead to falls. People may not be able to see the obstacles in their path (such as toys, small rugs, furniture, or electrical cords), or they may not be able to judge distances or depth correctly.

视力受损是导致跌倒摔伤的核心诱因。案主可能无法看清行走路径上的障碍物（如散落的玩具、小地毯、低矮家具或拖曳的电线），或者丧失正确判断距离与地面落差台阶深度的能力。

They may also have difficulty reading labels on medicines, cleaners, and other containers, which can result in their ingesting poisonous agents. Ensure clients wear prescribed, clean eyeglasses and maintain adequate glare-free lighting.

此外，视力模糊会导致案主难以辨认药瓶、清洁剂及其他容器上的警示说明，从而误服剧毒化学品。支持工作者必须确保案主佩戴度数合适且擦拭明亮的眼镜，并保持居室内充足、均匀且无眩光的照明。

The Client With Hearing Challenges

存在听力损伤、耳鸣或听觉剥夺的案主

People with hearing challenges may not hear warning sounds—such as sirens, smoke detectors, car horns, or approaching traffic. They may also fail to hear a caregiver's verbal warnings. Make sure hearing aids are clean, turned on, with fresh batteries, and that you face the client directly when speaking in a normal, clear tone.

听力障碍案主可能无法听见警报汽笛、烟雾火警警报器、汽车喇叭或临近车辆的轰鸣声，甚至漏听照护人员发出的即刻危险口头警示。务必确保助听器清洁干燥、开关开启且电池电量充足；在沟通时应面朝案主，以正常语速和清晰声调表达，配合眼神与温和肢体示意。

The Paralyzed Client & Impaired Mobility

截瘫、偏瘫、四肢瘫痪与重度肢体活动受限案主

Paralysis is the loss of muscle function, sensation, or both. People with paraplegia (paralysis from the waist down) or quadriplegia (paralysis from the neck down) cannot move or feel parts of their body, making them completely vulnerable to burns, pressure injuries, and falls during transfers. Support workers must reposition paralyzed clients regularly, test bath water temperature meticulously, and never leave them unattended in unstable seating.

瘫痪是指骨骼肌肉自主运动功能丧失、浅深感觉丧失或二者兼有的严重神经病理状态。截瘫（腰部以下截瘫）或四肢瘫痪（颈髓损伤导致颈部以下全身瘫痪）案主无法自主活动患侧肢体，且对冷热刺痛毫无知觉，极易在移位过程中发生跌落、在温水接触中发生重度烫伤或因局部长期受压而发生深度压疮。支持工作者必须定时协助其翻身减压，极其严格地双人核测水温，且绝不可将瘫痪案主单独留在缺乏防护的座椅上。

作为支持工作者，您在日常执业中需要照护多位案主，每位案主均有其独特的医疗处置、康复疗程、饮食禁忌以及活动限制等级。在执行任何一项护理操作或生活照料规程之前，必须百分之百确保是将“正确的照护施加于正确的案主”。

Safety is something we take for granted until someone is injured. Common sense and simple safety measures can prevent most accidents.

安全往往是一种在未发生流血创伤前极易被人们忽视的平静状态。然而，运用基本的审慎常识与系统化的安全防范措施，绝大多数意外事故完全可以被杜绝于萌芽之中。

PREVENTING FALLS

预防案主跌倒与坠床：守护高龄脆弱群体的核心实务

Falls are the most common accidents in all care settings. Older persons are particularly vulnerable; according to Statistics Canada, falls are the leading cause of injury-related hospitalizations and injury-related deaths among Canadian seniors. Fall-related injuries range from bruises and sprains to catastrophic hip fractures and traumatic brain injuries (TBIs).

跌倒（Falls）是所有医疗机构、照护院所与居家环境中发生率最高的多发意外事故。高龄长者尤其脆弱；根据加拿大统计局权威数据，跌倒已成为导致加拿大 65 岁以上老年人创伤住院和创伤性死亡的首要元凶。跌倒造成的后果极其严重，涵盖了软组织挫伤、关节扭伤，直至灾难性的髋部股骨颈骨折、硬膜下血肿及重度脑外伤（TBIs）。

Causes and Risk Factors for Falls

引发跌倒的多元诱因：机体内在衰竭与外在环境危险的交织

Most falls result from a complex interaction between intrinsic factors (characteristics of the person) and extrinsic factors (hazards in the physical environment). Intrinsic risk factors include poor balance, gait instability, muscle weakness, visual impairment, cognitive disorientation, urinary urgency, and postural hypotension.

绝大多数跌倒事故都是案主自身机体内在脆弱因素（Intrinsic factors）与物理环境外在危害因素（Extrinsic factors）复杂交织作用的恶果。内在危险因素涵盖前庭平衡功能减退、步态摇晃蹒跚、下肢肌肉衰减萎缩、视野受损、认知症定向力混乱、突发尿急便急以及体位性直立低血压。

Extrinsic risk factors include dim lighting, throw rugs, slippery wet floors, uneven steps, lack of handrails, cluttered pathways, poorly fitting non-supportive shoes, and ill-maintained mobility equipment. Polypharmacy—taking multiple medications simultaneously—multiplies fall risk dramatically.

外在环境危险源包括昏暗无光的通道、松散滑动的小地毯、湿滑地面、高低不平的台阶踏板、缺乏走廊防摔扶手、堆积杂物的过道、不合脚且缺乏支撑的软拖鞋，以及未定期检修维护的松垮助行车。此外，多重用药（Polypharmacy，同时服用多种降压、镇静或利尿药物）更会成倍放大中枢神经反应迟钝与离床跌倒概率。

Fall Prevention Programs in Facilities

照护机构防跌倒综合干预项目与多学科联动

Many facilities have structured fall prevention programs. Personalized care plans outline targeted fall mitigation strategies.

现代专业照护机构均建立了结构严密的防跌倒综合干预项目。由主管护士、物理治疗师（PTs）、作业治疗师（OTs）以及一线支持工作者组成的多学科团队，在案主初次入住、发生任何偶发跌倒事件后以及健康状况出现微小波动时，都会立即实施多维跌倒风险重估。个性化照护计划会清晰列明量身定制的防跌倒保护规程。

  • • Implementing scheduled toileting programs to minimize unassisted rushing to the commode.
    建立规律性定时协助如厕计划，最大程度消除案主因急于如厕而冒险独自离床冲动的诱因。

    推行主动式白班与夜间每小时巡视制（Hourly Rounding），主动满足案主饮水、体位调整与陪伴诉求。
  • • Adjusting bed height to its lowest possible position whenever care is complete.
    在完成各项护理操作离开床旁时，坚决将电动病床高度降至贴近地面的最低水平。
  • • Ensuring assistive mobility aids (canes, walkers) are placed directly beside the client.
    确保案主匹配的助行器械（拐杖、带轮助行架）始终处于其优势手顺手可抓扶的黄金位置。

PREVENTING ACCIDENTAL POISONING

预防意外中毒：高危毒物识别与危险源阻断

Poisoning is a serious risk for both young children and older adults. In children, exploratory oral behaviours and an inability to recognize chemical hazards lead to unintentional ingestion of medications, cosmetics, and cleaning chemicals. In older adults, cognitive disorientation, dementia, poor vision, and confusion regarding pill organizers cause accidental medication overdoses and toxic ingestions.

意外中毒是严重威胁婴幼儿与高龄长者生命安全的高发意外事故。在儿童群体中，探索性的“抓入口中”本能以及对化学危险品的无知，常导致误服散落药品、成人化妆品及家用洗涤剂。在老年人群体中，认知症定向力障碍、视力严重衰退难以辨认微小药名标签，以及多重药物分装盒错服，极易引发致命性的蓄积性药物过量或误饮有毒日化品。

Common Poisonous Household Substances

居家与机构中常见的强毒性化学产品排查

Everyday products found in kitchens, bathrooms, and laundry rooms can be lethal if ingested, inhaled, or absorbed through the skin. Common poisons include liquid laundry detergent pods, bleach, ammonia, drain cleaners, disinfectants, furniture polish, mouthwash containing alcohol, nail polish remover, hair dyes, prescription and over-the-counter medications, vitamins with iron, rubbing alcohol, hand sanitizers, bug sprays, and antifreeze.

存放在厨房、卫浴间及洗衣房中的日常日用品，一旦被误服、吸入呼吸道或大面积接触皮肤，均可造成严重内脏衰竭乃至死亡。常见剧毒物涵盖浓缩洗衣凝珠、含氯漂白水、工业氨水、强碱管道疏通剂、高浓度消毒液、家具光亮剂、含酒精漱口水、洗甲水、染发剂、各类处方与非处方药、含铁维生素软糖、医用消毒酒精、含醇免洗手消、杀虫气雾剂及汽车防冻液。

Ways to Prevent Accidental Poisoning

防范意外中毒的标准化日常照护法则
  • • Keep all hazardous products in their original containers with intact manufacturer warning labels.

    坚决绝对严禁将有毒清洁剂或药水倒进矿泉水瓶、饮料罐或食品碗碟中分装！

Emergency Protocol for Suspected Poisoning

疑似急性误服中毒的现场急救闭环流程

If a poisoning is suspected: Act immediately! Call your local Poison Control Centre or 9-1-1 immediately.

一旦怀疑案主发生误服中毒：必须分秒必争即刻行动！立即拨打当地毒物控制中心热线或 9-1-1 呼救。

Have the chemical container or medication bottle in hand so you can read the exact chemical name, active ingredients, and approximate volume consumed to the toxicologist. NEVER induce vomiting or administer water, milk, or raw eggs unless explicitly commanded to do so by Poison Control specialists; vomiting caustic acids or alkalis causes devastating esophageal perforation and aspiration.

务必将涉事药瓶或清洁剂容器拿在手中，以便向毒理专家准确朗读化学品法定全称、有效成分含量及估计摄入量。除非毒物控制中心专职医生明确指令，否则坚决严禁擅自催吐、灌水或喂食牛奶生鸡蛋！强行催吐强酸强碱会导致食管黏膜遭受二次撕裂穿孔，并诱发致命性误吸性窒息！

PREVENTING BURNS

预防烧烫伤：水温严格控制、取暖辅具与化学灼伤防护

Burns can be caused by dry heat (fire, stoves, space heaters), moist heat (hot tap water, boiling soup, steam), electricity, caustic chemicals, and radiation. Older adults have thinned epidermal layers, reduced subcutaneous fat, and sluggish capillary circulation; hot water that causes mild erythema in a young adult can cause full-thickness third-degree burns and skin sloughing in an older person within two seconds.

烧烫伤可由干热（明火、炉灶、电暖气）、湿热（过烫自来水、沸腾热汤、蒸汽）、电流、强酸强碱腐蚀以及热辐射所引发。老年人表皮角质层严重菲薄、皮下脂肪萎缩且毛细血管舒缩迟钝；在青壮年身上仅引起轻微发红的过热水温，若接触高龄长者娇嫩皮肤，只需短短两秒钟即可造成全层表皮脱落剥脱性三度重度烫伤！

  • • Always measure bath and shower water temperature using a bath thermometer (must be 38°C to 40.5°C / 100°F to 105°F).
    在协助沐浴前，必须使用专用洗澡水温计实测水温（标准温度严格控制在 38°C 至 40.5°C 之间）。
  • • Test water temperature on the inner aspect of your wrist before the client enters the tub.
    在案主肢体接触水源之前，照护人员必须先用前臂内侧敏感皮肤亲自试水复核。
  • • Turn pot handles inward toward the back of the stove to prevent accidental knocking.
    在厨房烹饪时，必须将锅柄向内朝向炉灶内侧转动，防止经过时意外带翻热油热汤。
  • • Never use electric heating pads, hot water bottles, or heat lamps unless specifically authorized in the care plan.
    未经照护计划明确医嘱授权，坚决严禁为案主使用电热毯、热水袋或烤灯取暖。

PREVENTING SUFFOCATION

预防意外窒息：进食噎食防范与气道异物完全梗阻

Suffocation (asphyxiation) occurs when oxygen is cut off from the body, causing breathing to stop and brain death within four to six minutes. Major causes include choking on food or foreign objects, mechanical chest compression, suffocation in bed, and environmental entrapment.

窒息（Asphyxiation）是指呼吸道受阻导致氧气无法进入肺泡与血液循环的危急状态，持续 4 至 6 分钟即可诱发不可逆性脑细胞缺血性死亡。临床常见原因包括进食大块食物或异物卡塞咽喉（噎食）、胸部外力机械性压迫、睡床床品堵塞口鼻，以及床栏夹困绞勒。

Choking Hazards in Adults & Older Persons

成年长者发生进食噎食致死的高危临床诱因

Choking frequently occurs during meals. Older persons face heightened risk due to ill-fitting or loose dentures, missing teeth, dysphagia (neurological swallowing difficulties following stroke or dementia), and altered consciousness from sedatives. To prevent choking: Ensure dentures fit securely, position the client in an upright High-Fowler’s position (90 degrees) during all meals and for at least 30 minutes afterward, serve foods chopped or pureed as specified in the care plan, and encourage slow chewing without talking while food is in the mouth.

噎食大多在案主就餐时突发。高龄长者因假牙松动脱落、全口无牙咀嚼无力、脑卒中或认知症引发的吞咽障碍（Dysphagia），以及镇静药物引起的嗜睡，极易发生食物误入气管。预防噎食核心准则：就餐前确保假牙稳固佩戴；就餐全程及饭后至少 30 分钟内必须维持 90 度端坐高卧位（High-Fowler’s position）；严格遵照医嘱提供细切碎软食或均质糊状吞咽流质；反复提示案主小口进食、细嚼慢咽，口中有食物时绝对严禁说笑交谈。

Preventing Infant Suffocation and SIDS

婴幼儿睡眠安全：婴儿猝死综合征（SIDS）防范准则

Infants are unable to lift their heads or clear airway obstructions if face-down. The Public Health Agency of Canada emphasizes the 'Back to Sleep' protocol: Infants must always be placed on their backs to sleep on a firm, flat mattress designed for their crib.

新生儿与婴儿在趴睡口鼻受压时缺乏抬头挣扎与推开障碍物的肌力。加拿大公共卫生局严格推行“仰卧入眠”（Back to Sleep）金律：婴儿睡眠时必须始终保持仰卧位，平卧于贴合紧密的硬质专用床垫上。

The crib must meet current Canadian safety standards with no missing or loose slats. NEVER place pillows, thick quilts, plush bumper pads, comforters, sheepskins, or stuffed toys in an infant's crib. Avoid bed-sharing (co-sleeping) with adults or older siblings, as accidental overlying and rolling onto the baby can cause fatal smothering.

婴儿床必须符合加拿大最新安全标准，无松动脱落栏杆。坚决严禁在婴儿床内放置枕头、厚重棉被、毛绒防撞护垫、羊毛褥垫或长毛填充玩具！坚决避免成人与婴儿同床挤睡（Co-sleeping），成人翻身压迫极易在熟睡中导致婴儿机械性窒息死亡。

Plastic Bags and Cord Entanglement Hazards

超薄塑料袋与窗帘绳索缠绕窒息隐患

Thin plastic films (such as dry-cleaning garment bags, garbage bags, and plastic grocery bags) create strong electrostatic attraction and cling tightly to a child’s or confused adult's moist nose and mouth, instantly sealing off breathing. Keep all plastic packaging away from clients; always knot bags before disposal. Window blind cords must be kept tied high out of reach to eliminate strangulation loops.

干洗衣服防尘袋、超薄塑料购物袋及大垃圾袋具有极强的静电吸附性，一旦贴覆在儿童或认知症长者潮湿的口鼻面部，会瞬间如真空薄膜般严密封锁气道，数分钟内致死。所有塑料薄膜包装必须彻底清理并打死结后扔入带盖垃圾桶；窗帘拉绳必须加装安全收纳器固定在高处，消除形成上吊式绳套的危险。

FIRE SAFETY AND PREVENTION

火灾安全防范与引燃三要素综合控制

Fire is one of the most terrifying, fast-moving, and devastating threats to human life in any residential or healthcare setting. Smoke inhalation, hot toxic gas suffocation, and panic cause more deaths than actual thermal burns.

火灾是任何养老机构、综合医院及家庭住宅中破坏力最强、蔓延最快且最具毁灭性的致命威胁。在火灾伤亡统计中，浓烟毒气吸入窒息、高温灼伤呼吸道以及惊恐踩踏造成的死亡人数远超单纯火焰灼烧。

Fire requires three elements to exist, known as the Fire Triangle: (1) Spark or flame (heat), (2) Combustible material (fuel), and (3) Oxygen. Eliminating any one of these three elements prevents or extinguishes a fire.

火灾的发生必须同时具备三项先决要素，即“燃烧三要素（火三角）”：(1) 火种与引燃热源（Heat）、(2) 可燃物与助燃基质（Fuel）、以及 (3) 氧气（Oxygen）。成功阻断或剥离这三要素中的任何一环，即可彻底预防火灾或熄灭火势。

Major Causes of Domestic and Institutional Fires

照护机构与居家环境中四大常见火灾根源
  • • Careless smoking: Falling asleep with lit cigarettes in bed or dropping live ashes onto upholstered furniture.
    吸烟疏忽大意：卧床吸烟熟睡、未熄灭的烟头滚落至布艺沙发或地毯缝隙中阴燃。
  • • Defective electrical equipment, overloaded extension cords, and frayed wiring producing electrical arcing.
    劣质或故障电气设备、插线板超负荷串联过载以及破损电线短路爆出电火花。
  • • Unattended cooking on kitchen stoves, overheated cooking oil, and grease accumulation in exhaust hoods.
    厨房烹饪无人看管、过热食油起火自燃以及抽油烟机管道油脂长期蓄积引燃。
  • • Improper storage and handling of flammable solvents, paints, gasoline, and rubbing alcohol.
    易燃有机溶剂、油漆稀释剂、汽油及大桶消毒酒精违规存放于热源附近。

Oxygen Therapy: Extreme Flammability Risks

医用氧气治疗实务：爆燃隐患与绝对禁忌

Oxygen is a vital medical gas prescribed for hypoxemic clients. While pure oxygen does NOT burn by itself, it is a ferocious combustion accelerant.

医用氧气是纠正案主低氧血症的处方医疗气体。虽然纯氧本身不会自燃，但它是一种极具破坏力的剧烈助燃催化剂！

Any fire in an oxygen-enriched atmosphere burns explosively, igniting materials that normally resist fire (such as hair, skin oils, bed sheets, and plastics). Whenever oxygen cylinders or wall concentrators are operating in a room, you must enforce absolute safety discipline.

在富氧微环境中，火势会呈爆炸式瞬间蔓延，使原本不易引燃的织物、头发皮脂、病床被单甚至塑料制品瞬间化为火海。只要居室内正在使用氧气钢瓶或墙壁管道供氧，支持工作者必须坚决贯彻最高规格的安全铁律。

  • • Prominently post 'OXYGEN IN USE - NO SMOKING' signs on the entry door and above the bed (FIGURE 20.17).
    在房间入口门扉及床头墙面显眼处张贴醒目的“正在用氧·严禁吸烟”黄黑警示标志（图 20.17）。
  • • Enforce a zero-tolerance ban on cigarettes, matches, lighters, candles, and incense anywhere on the premises.
    全面严禁室内出现香烟、火柴、打火机、蜡烛及宗教熏香等任何点火器具。
  • • Remove all electrical equipment that could produce sparks (e.g. electric razors, heating pads, hair dryers).
    彻底移走所有可能产生微弱电气火花的设备（如普通电动剃须刀、电热毯、吹风机）。
  • • Never apply petroleum-based moisturizers (such as Vaseline) to the client's lips or nostrils; use water-soluble gels.
    坚决严禁使用凡士林等矿物油基唇膏涂抹案主口唇或鼻孔，遇高浓度氧气可自燃爆炸；必须选用医用水溶性润滑凝胶。

THE RACE PROTOCOL: EMERGENCY FIRE ACTION

火灾紧急应急响应：RACE 黄金救援与疏散四步法则

When a fire alarm chimes, or if you encounter flames, smoke, or a burning odor in a care facility, you must instantly execute the internationally recognized four-stage RACE protocol. Memorize this sequence so your actions are swift, calm, and automatic during a crisis:

当机构内火警警报骤然长鸣，或者当您在日常巡视中目击明火、闻到浓烟焦煳味时，必须不假思索地立即启动国际通用的 RACE 火灾应急四步规程。必须将该流程深植于肌肉记忆中，确保在突发危机面前镇定自若、分秒必争地挽救生命：

  • • R - RESCUE: Immediately move any clients in immediate, direct danger away from the fire room to a safe refuge zone behind self-closing fire barrier doors.
    R - RESCUE (紧急施救)：第一要务是立即将处于起火房间及直接生命危险区域内的所有案主快速撤离，转移至防火隔断门后方的安全避难区。
  • • A - ALARM: Activate the nearest manual pull station on the wall and shout the facility code (e.g., 'Code Red!'). Call 9-1-1 or alert the switchboard operator.
    A - ALARM (拉响警报)：迅速拉下走廊墙壁上的手动火警警报按钮，大声呼叫机构应急代号（如‘Code Red！’），并通知总机或直拨 9-1-1。
  • • C - CONFINE: Close all client bedroom doors, corridor fire doors, and windows tightly to contain smoke, starve the flames of fresh oxygen, and buy evacuation time.
    C - CONFINE (隔离烟火)：在撤出房间后随手紧紧关闭所有病房房门、窗户及走廊防火卷帘门，彻底阻断浓烟横向扩散，切断新鲜空气对火势的补给。
  • • E - EXTINGUISH / EVACUATE: Extinguish small, contained trash can fires using the correct fire extinguisher; otherwise, evacuate everyone calmly via designated stairwells.
    E - EXTINGUISH / EVACUATE (扑灭火源 / 全员撤离)：若火势尚处于微小初起阶段且退路无阻，使用灭火器扑灭；若火势蔓延，坚决组织全员走防火楼梯疏散。

Operating a Fire Extinguisher: The PASS Technique

手提式干粉/水基灭火器标准化操作规程：PASS 四步法

Support workers should only attempt to extinguish a fire if: (1) The fire is small and contained to an object like a wastebasket, (2) The alarm has been sounded and the room is cleared, (3) You have a clear, unblocked emergency escape exit at your back, and (4) You are properly trained in extinguisher use. Follow the PASS technique:

支持工作者只有在严格满足以下四项先决条件时，方可尝试自行扑救火灾：(1) 火情处于初起阶段且局限于废纸篓等单一封闭容器内，(2) 警报已拉响且房间内人员已全部撤空，(3) 你的身后始终保留着畅通无阻的安全撤退通道，且 (4) 你已熟练掌握灭火器操作手法。严格遵照 PASS 四字诀执行：

  • • P - PULL: Pull the safety locking pin out of the handle by breaking the plastic tamper seal.
    P - PULL (拔除插销)：用力扭断塑料铅封，拔出压把上的金属安全锁定保险销。
  • • A - AIM: Aim the nozzle, horn, or hose low, pointing directly at the BASE of the fire (where the fuel is burning, not at the leaping flames).
    A - AIM (对准根部)：手握喷管前端，将喷嘴水平对准火源的最底部根部（燃烧基质处，切忌对准半空中的火苗乱喷）。
  • • S - SQUEEZE: Squeeze the two operating lever handles together firmly to release the pressurized extinguishing chemical.
    S - SQUEEZE (压下压把)：稳妥握紧提把，用力向下压紧启闭压把，释放内部高压灭火药剂。
  • • S - SWEEP: Sweep the nozzle smoothly from side to side across the base of the fire until the fire is completely extinguished; back away carefully watching for flare-ups.
    S - SWEEP (左右扫射)：以左右平扫的动作覆盖火源整个根部，直至火苗彻底熄灭；灭火后面对火场缓慢后退，严防死灰复燃。

预防医疗器械与用电设备事故：接地与日常排查

HAZARDOUS MATERIALS AND WHMIS

工作场所危险化学品与加拿大 WHMIS 法定安全系统

A hazardous material is any substance that presents a physical hazard (e.g. fire, explosion, reactivity) or a health hazard (e.g. skin corrosion, toxicity, respiratory damage, cancer) in the workplace. Common hazardous materials in healthcare include high-level disinfectants, floor strippers, sterilizing gases, and chemotherapy agents. In Canada, worker safety regarding chemicals is governed by the Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS).

危险化学品是指在工作场所中对人身构成物理危害（如燃烧、爆炸、化学自反应）或健康危害（如皮肤强腐蚀、急性中毒、呼吸系统水肿、致突变与致癌）的任何物质。照护行业中常见的危险品包括戊二醛高水平消毒剂、地面脱蜡强碱剂、环氧乙烷灭菌气体及抗癌化疗药物。在加拿大，所有工作场所化学品的安全管理统一受联邦及省级的“工作场所危险化学品信息系统”（WHMIS）法定强制监管。

The Three Pillars of WHMIS Protection

加拿大 WHMIS 体系保护一线员工的三大核心支柱
  • • 1. Cautionary Labels: Standardized supplier and workplace labels affixed to all chemical containers.

  • • 2. Safety Data Sheets (SDSs): Detailed technical documents describing exact hazards, first aid, and safe handling.
    2. 安全数据表（SDSs）：由生产商提供的详尽技术文件，深度剖析毒性理化特性、急救措施与防护装备。
  • • 3. Worker Education and Training: Mandatory annual training programs educating workers how to read labels and protect themselves.
    3. 员工带薪安全教育与实操培训：法律规定雇主必须为所有员工提供强制培训，确保其知晓如何查对标签并保护自我。

RESTRAINTS: DEFINITION AND ETHICAL ISSUES

保护性约束：分类界定、伦理考量与法律底线

A restraint is any device, garment, barrier, furniture, or medication that limits or restricts freedom of movement or access to one's own body. Historically, restraints were commonly applied under the mistaken belief that they prevented falls. Decades of rigorous clinical evidence have proven the opposite: Restraints DO NOT prevent falls; instead, restrained clients suffer significantly more catastrophic head injuries, fractures, and fatal asphyxiations from struggling against straps.

保护性约束是指限制或剥夺个体自主身体运动能力、或限制其触及自身身体任何部位的任何装置、特制衣物、物理屏障、家具或精神药物统称。在过去的传统照护观念中，约束曾被误认为能够有效防止坠床跌倒。然而，数十年的严肃现代临床循证医学彻底推翻了这一谬误：约束根本不能防止跌倒！相反，遭受约束的案主因剧烈挣扎往往承受着更致命的脑部重创、肢体粉碎性骨折以及被约束带绞勒窒息致死的恶果。

The Three Broad Categories of Restraints

临床执业中保护性约束的三大形式
  • • 1. Physical Restraints: Garments or mechanical devices attached to or near the body (e.g. wrist restraints, vest restraints, mitts, lap belts).
    1. 物理约束：直接捆绑或紧贴身体的特制机械装置（如手腕约束带、背心式约束带、防抓手套、轮椅腰部安全带）。
  • • 2. Environmental Restraints: Furniture and architectural barriers that confine movement (e.g. fixed geriatric chair lap trays, locked wandering units, raised bed rails).
    2. 环境约束：限制其自由通行的家具或物理屏障（如锁定不可拆卸的老人椅托盘、全封闭带密码锁病区、双侧升起的床栏）。
  • • 3. Chemical Restraints: Psychoactive drugs administered specifically to suppress behaviour or restrict movement rather than treat medical illness.
    3. 化学约束：使用强效镇静剂或抗精神病药物，其目的专用于压制躁动行为或限制其身体行动，而非对症治疗精神疾病。

Physical and Psychological Complications of Restraints

保护性约束引发的致命躯体创伤与心理毁灭性恶果

Restraints cause profound physical and emotional trauma. The most catastrophic physical complication is death by strangulation or positional asphyxiation, which occurs when a client slips or tries to climb out of bed and becomes caught in the restraint vest, jacket, or straps. The chest is compressed, preventing respiratory expansion.

保护性约束会造成深重的身体器质性创伤与毁灭性的心理打击。最具有灾难性的身体并发症是约束带绞勒致死或体位性机械窒息——这常发生在案主试图翻身或攀爬下床时，身体滑脱被困在背心式约束带、马甲或绑带之中。胸廓受到沉重机械压迫而无法舒张，导致案主在数分钟内活活窒息身亡。

Other severe physical complications include pressure injuries from continuous friction and immobility, accelerated muscle atrophy and irreversible joint contractures, severe constipation and fecal impaction, urinary and fecal incontinence, loss of bone mass, deep vein thrombosis (DVT), and nerve damage. Psychologically, restrained clients suffer extreme humiliation, terror, agitation, increased aggression, severe depression, delirium, and a profound loss of dignity and self-esteem.

其他严重的躯体并发症包括局部受压导致的深度难愈性压疮、长期制动诱发的骨骼肌急速萎缩与不可逆关节挛缩畸形、顽固性便秘与粪便嵌顿、大小便失禁失控、骨质疏松加速、下肢深静脉血栓形成（DVT）以及周围神经受压麻痹。在心理精神层面，遭受约束的案主会经历极度的屈辱、恐慌、躁狂愤怒、敌对攻击性加剧、重度抑郁嗜睡、谵妄发作，以及自尊心与人类尊严的彻底丧失。

ALTERNATIVES TO RESTRAINTS

保护性约束的以人为本替代方案：全面践行免约束照护

Because of the grave dangers associated with restraints, Canadian healthcare facilities and community agencies have adopted 'least restraint' or 'restraint-free' policies. Restraints are never considered first-line interventions; every single alternative must be implemented and documented as ineffective before any restraint is contemplated. Identifying and addressing the root cause of confusion or restlessness eliminates the need for restraints in the vast majority of cases.

鉴于约束可能引发的致命危险，加拿大所有正规医疗与长期照护机构均已立法并全面推行“最低限度约束”（Least Restraint）乃至“无约束”（Restraint-Free）的人本照护哲学。约束绝不是一线应对措施；只有在团队彻底尝试了所有非限制性替代方案且详实记录证实无效后，方可谨慎评估。通过精准探寻案主烦躁、游荡或躁动背后的根本生理与心理需求并予以满足，绝大多数场景下的约束需求完全可以被彻底化解。

  • • Comfortable seating: Provide customized ergonomic chairs, positioning cushions, and wedge pillows that prevent sliding.
    舒适化座椅体位：配备人体工学轮椅、防滑定位凝胶坐垫与防前滑楔形海绵，消除长者下滑滑坠隐患。
  • • Meaningful activities: Engage the client in individualized calming tasks (folding warm towels, listening to favorite music, sorting yarn, pet therapy).
    个性化赋能活动：引导案主参与温和熟悉的日常活动（折叠温热毛巾、聆听经典老歌、整理毛线团、治疗犬抚触陪伴）。
  • • Frequent companionship and rounding: Encourage family visits, volunteer companions, and proactive 15-minute check-ins.
    强化陪伴与高频巡视：鼓励家属亲情陪伴、义工温和交谈，并由工作人员执行每 15 至 30 分钟的高频主动巡视。
  • • Meeting physiological needs: Implement strict toileting schedules, offer warm drinks, snacks, and pain management proactively.
    前置满足生理需求：建立定时协助如厕作息，主动提供温热饮品与小吃点心，及时评估并缓解隐匿性身体慢性疼痛。
  • • Safe environmental modifications: Utilize ultra-low floor beds with soft landing mats, soothing lighting, and gentle bed/chair sensor pads.
    环境安全无害化改造：采用贴地超低病床并在床侧平铺缓冲地垫，维持柔和光线，使用非侵入性的床椅感应报警器。

LEGAL AND ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS FOR RESTRAINT USE

约束使用的法律红线、知情同意与最低限度约束法案

In Canada, restraint use is strictly governed by provincial and territorial legislation (such as Ontario’s Patient Restraints Minimization Act). The law mandates that healthcare providers must minimize the use of restraints on clients.

在加拿大，保护性约束的使用受到各省与地区法律的严格刑律与民律管辖（例如安大略省《患者约束最小化法案》）。法律明确规定，医护人员必须最大程度减少对服务对象的任何身体约束。

Applying a restraint without legal authority, without physician orders, or without informed consent constitutes criminal battery and civil false imprisonment. Support workers face severe legal liability and professional dismissal if they apply unauthorized restraints.

无合法法定权限、缺乏正式执业医师医嘱或未经知情同意便强加约束，直接构成刑事蓄意人身侵害（Battery）与民事非法拘禁（False Imprisonment）罪行。支持工作者私自施加未获批准的约束将面临严重的法律追责与从业执照彻底吊销。

  • • A physician's written order is legally mandatory: Restraints can NEVER be applied as an as-needed (PRN) measure.
    必须具有执业医生签名的书面有效医嘱：约束绝对严禁作为“必要时”（PRN）备用医嘱开具或执行！
  • • Informed consent must be obtained from the client or their legal Substitute Decision-Maker (SDM) prior to application.
    在实施前，必须向案主本人或其法定代理决策人（SDM）充分告知约束缘由、潜在风险与时限，并取得书面知情同意。
  • • Restraints must be released at least every 2 hours for skin care, range-of-motion (ROM) exercises, hydration, and toileting.
    每隔最多 2 小时必须彻底松解约束带，提供全面的受压皮肤擦洗、关节活动度训练、饮水及如厕协助。
  • • Check the client's circulation, breathing, and comfort every 15 minutes while restrained; document meticulously.
    约束期间必须每 15 分钟巡视检查一次肢端血液循环（肤色、皮温、脉搏）、呼吸频率及心理情绪，并详实记录。

INCIDENT REPORTS (OCCURRENCE REPORTS)

不良事件报告（意外事故报告书）的法定填报规范

An incident report (also known as an occurrence report) is an official agency form completed whenever an accident, injury, medical error, near miss, or unexpected adverse event arises in the workplace. Examples requiring an incident report include: a client falling (even if uninjured!), giving wrong food or care, a client or staff sustaining a burn or skin tear, a lost personal belonging, malfunctioning equipment, and any incident of workplace violence or verbal threats.

不良事件报告（Incident Report，在许多机构亦称 Occurrence Report）是一份严肃的行政法律文书，凡是在工作场所中发生任何意外事故、人员身体损伤、给药或照护差错、险兆事件（Near Miss）或异常非预期事件时，都必须法定强制填报。需要立即申报的情形包括：案主发生跌倒（即便案主自称未受伤！）、执行了错误的饮食餐标或护理规程、案主或员工发生烫伤或皮肤撕裂伤、贵重物品遗失、设备故障爆火花，以及任何形式的职场暴力或言语威胁。

  • • Complete the report as soon as the client's immediate medical safety is ensured and the nurse has performed assessments.
    在案主的即刻生命安全得到抢救保障且主管护士完成专业医学评估后，必须尽快即刻完成填报。
  • • State only objective, observable facts: Record exactly what you saw, heard, and did; specify the exact date, time, and location.
    全篇必须仅记录客观、可观察的事实：真实叙述所见、所听、所执行的动作；精准注明具体发生年月日、分秒时刻与房间方位。
  • • Never assign blame, make assumptions, or record subjective opinions or theories about how the accident happened.
    绝对严禁在报告中指责他人、揣测推卸责任，或记录任何主观猜想、臆测与个人情绪色彩之词。
  • • Do NOT record in the client’s medical chart that an incident report was completed; the report is an internal administrative quality document.
    切忌在案主的正式病历与护理记录单中写上“已填写不良事件报告”；该报告属于机构内部风险管理与质量改进机密文书。

BED RAILS: RISKS, BENEFITS, AND ENTRAPMENT

床栏利弊权衡与床垫夹困致命风险

Hospital bed rails (bed side rails) are attached to the bed frame to prevent clients from accidentally rolling out of bed and to assist clients with turning and repositioning in bed. However, bed rails carry immense hazards.

医院与照护病床床栏（Bed rails）安装于床架两侧，其初衷在于防范卧床案主在熟睡或翻身时意外滚落，并便于案主抓握床栏自主翻身坐起。然而，现代医学研究揭示床栏潜藏着巨大危险。

A confused or agitated client may attempt to climb over raised rails, falling from a much greater height and sustaining catastrophic skull fractures or spinal injuries. Even more dangerous is entrapment, where the client’s head, neck, or chest becomes wedged in the rail openings or between the rail and the mattress.

处于定向力混乱或躁动的案主往往本能地试图翻越升起的床栏，导致其从更高的坠落高度硬性着地，造成极其惨烈的颅骨粉碎性骨折与高位截瘫。更为隐匿且致命的是“床栏夹困”（Entrapment）——案主的头颈、胸部或四肢卡死在床栏缝隙或床垫侧缘之间，酿成惨剧。

The Seven Hospital Bed Safety Entrapment Zones

加拿大卫生部与FDA医院病床七大夹困高危区域医学剖析

Health Canada and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) have established strict hospital bed dimensional guidelines identifying seven distinct entrapment zones where clients have suffered fatal asphyxiation, chest compression, and neck fractures:

加拿大卫生部（Health Canada）与美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）联合确立了极其严苛的医院病床安全几何尺寸国家标准，明确界定了七大容易造成案主致命性勒颈、胸廓受压窒息与颈椎骨折的危险夹困区域：

  • • Zone 1: Within the rail itself (between the individual vertical or horizontal bars).

  • • Zone 5: Between split bed rails (between the head-end rail and the foot-end rail).
    第五区域 (Zone 5)：分段式前后两截床栏之间的中间过渡通道缝隙。
  • • Zone 6: Between the end of the bed rail and the headboard or footboard panel.
    第六区域 (Zone 6)：床栏最外端边缘与床头板或床尾板之间的垂直间隙。
  • • Zone 7: Between the headboard or footboard panel and the end of the mattress.
    第七区域 (Zone 7)：床头板或床尾板内壁与床垫两端边缘之间的横向深缝。

WORKPLACE SAFETY AND OH&S LEGISLATION

职业健康安全法规与支持工作者三大法定权利

Healthcare is one of the most physically and emotionally demanding, injury-prone sectors in Canada. Support workers frequently experience back injuries from lifting, needle-sticks, slips, chemical exposures, and client aggression. Occupational Health and Safety (OH&S) legislation in every Canadian province and territory mandates that employers must provide a safe working environment. OH&S laws grant every healthcare worker three fundamental, non-negotiable legal rights:

医疗照护行业是全加拿大体力消耗最大、职业创伤发生率极高的行业之一。一线支持工作者频繁面临搬运案主导致的腰脊劳损骨折、针刺伤、湿滑跌倒摔伤、化学品飞溅灼伤以及来自案主的言语肢体攻击。加拿大各省与地区颁布的《职业健康与安全法》（OH&S）强制规定雇主必须提供安全健康的执业环境。法律赋予了每一位支持工作者三项神圣不可侵犯的法定权利：

  • • 1. The Right to Know: The legal right to be informed about all existing and potential hazards in your workplace and to receive comprehensive training and PPE to handle them.
    1. 知情权 (The Right to Know)：依法知悉执业场所中所有现存及潜在危险源、有害化学品及感染风险，并接受充分带薪安全培训与免费防护装备的权利。
  • • 2. The Right to Participate: The legal right to take part in workplace safety decisions and serve on the Joint Health and Safety Committee (JHSC).
    2. 参与权 (The Right to Participate)：依法参与用人单位各项安全政策制定、参与事故调查并在劳资联合健康与安全委员会（JHSC）中担任代表的权利。
  • • 3. The Right to Refuse Unsafe Work: The legal right to decline any task that you have reasonable cause to believe is dangerous to your safety or the safety of others without fear of retaliation.
    3. 拒绝不安全工作权 (The Right to Refuse Unsafe Work)：当有合理理由确信某项工作对您自身或他人的生命健康构成直接威胁时，依法坚决拒绝执行且免受雇主报复处分的权利。

WORKPLACE VIOLENCE AND HARASSMENT

防范职场暴力与侵犯：攻击前兆识别与言语降温技巧

Workplace violence includes physical assaults, attempted assaults, verbal threats, and aggressive posturing in healthcare and community environments. Support workers must recognize early warning signs of escalating agitation: pacing, clenched fists, flushed face, rapid shallow breathing, swearing, shouting, and erratic eye contact. Always maintain an arm's-length distance, speak in a calm non-threatening tone, validate the person's feelings, never argue, and ensure you position yourself with an unblocked path to the door.

工作场所暴力包括身体殴打、蓄意肢体袭击、言语恐吓威胁以及敌对侵犯姿态。支持工作者必须敏锐捕捉案主或家属情绪失控升级的早期前兆：烦躁踱步、双手握拳、面色潮红、呼吸急促粗重、辱骂咆哮、眼神闪烁凶狠。在介入时必须始终保持至少一臂以上的安全距离，以平稳低沉、不带评判色彩的温和语调沟通，共情接纳案主情绪，切忌激烈争辩，且身体站位必须始终确保身后通往大门的逃生通路绝对畅通。
