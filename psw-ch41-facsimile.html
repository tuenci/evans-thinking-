Caring for New Birth Parents
产妇照护要点与支持人员专业职责边界

Support workers are sometimes required to care for new birth parents and infants. Caring for an infant provides a valuable service to the birth parent because it enables them to have more time for themselves and their family. It is important that, as the support worker, you respect the family’s routines, schedules, and ways of doing things. Remember to follow the parent’s standards and preferences whenever possible. As with all of the duties that you perform, it is your responsibility to follow your clients’ care plan, know the limits of your scope of practice, and know who to turn to if you have any questions about the client’s care (see the Supporting Leslie and Milton: Knowing Who to Turn to for Answers box). If you have any concerns about the infant’s or birth parent’s health, you need to report your concerns to either the nurse in charge (if in a hospital or acute-care setting) or to the supervisor or public health nurse (if working in a home care or community setting).
支持工作人员有时需要在临床急性照护机构或居家社区环境中协助照护新产妇与新生儿。协助料理新生儿照料事务能够为产妇提供极为宝贵的支持，使其能够拥有更充裕的休息调养时间以恢复元气并顾及家庭其他成员。作为支持人员，极为关键的一点是高度尊重受照护家庭既有的日常生活习惯、作息时间表以及特定的育儿处事方式。只要不违背基本医疗安全原则，应始终尽可能遵循产妇家长的养育标准与个人偏好。正如履行所有照护职责一样，支持人员必须严格执行个性化照护计划，清晰明确自身法定执业范围（Scope of Practice）的红线边界，并在对照护产生疑问时明确知晓该向谁请示与咨询。一旦发现婴儿或产妇出现任何潜在健康隐患或异常体征，必须第一时间向责任护士（医院或急性照护机构）或主管护士/公共卫生护士（居家或社区照护环境）详尽汇报。
Support workers employed in acute-care settings, such as hospitals, may be assigned to units where babies and new birth parents are admitted. In community settings, a new birth parent may require home care services in the following situations: (1) They experienced complications before or after the birth. (2) The client needs help caring for other young children at home. (3) The client had a multiple birth (twins, triplets, or more). (4) The client has an infant with special needs who requires extra time and attention. (5) The client has a physical or mental disability. (6) The client has difficulty adjusting to new responsibilities.
在医院等急性医疗机构中，支持人员可能会被分配至产科病房或母婴同室病区工作。而在社区居家照护场景中，新产妇通常在以下特定情况下需要由支持人员提供居家上门照护支持：① 产妇在产前或产后经历过严重的产科并发症（如先兆子痫、产后出血等）；② 产妇家中尚有其他年幼子女需要分心照顾；③ 产妇分娩了多胞胎（双胞胎、三胞胎或更多）；④ 新生儿具有特殊医疗健康照护需求（如早产儿、先天性发育迟缓等），需要额外的专业看护精力；⑤ 产妇自身患有躯体残疾或精神心理障碍；⑥ 产妇在向母亲角色的心理转变中面临巨大情绪阻力与适应障碍。

The Postpartum Period
产褥期生理适应与子宫复旧过程

When assisting a new birth parent, you usually do one or more of the following: (1) Provide physical and emotional care for the birth parent. (2) Provide care for the newborn. (3) Help with child care when other young children are in the home. (4) Help with home management tasks such as meal preparation and housekeeping.
在协助新产妇时，支持人员的日常照护任务通常涵盖以下核心范畴：① 为产妇提供身心舒适护理与情绪疏导；② 协助实施新生儿的基础日常照护（喂养、换尿布、洗澡、测体重等）；③ 在家中有其他幼儿时协助看护与陪伴；④ 协助处理家庭事务，如准备营养月子膳食与基础居室保洁。
The postpartum—after (post) childbirth (partum)—period starts with the birth of the baby and ends 6 weeks later. During this time, the birth parent begins to adjust physically and emotionally to the effects of childbirth, and the uterus and the rest of the client’s body begin to return to their normal (prepregnant) state. Because of the many changes experienced during the postpartum period, the client needs to rest and recover. Birth parents need to be encouraged to rest or nap when the baby is sleeping and take time for themselves and their partner. During this time, the client also has important nutritional needs (see Chapter 27).
产褥期（Postpartum Period，post-意为“在……之后”，partum意为“分娩”）是指从胎儿娩出开始，至产妇全身器官（除乳腺外）恢复至孕前非孕状态的一段时期，临床上通常历时 6 周。在此期间，产妇无论在躯体生理还是心理情感上都经历着极其剧烈的调整与重塑：扩张如排球大小的子宫必须逐步收缩复旧至骨盆深部，内分泌激素水平急剧跌宕，各系统循环血量重新分配。由于面临如此巨大的生理消耗与组织创伤修复，产妇极度需要充足的休息与睡眠。支持人员应积极鼓励产妇“婴儿睡，自己就睡（Sleep when the baby sleeps）”，见缝插针地打盹补觉，并为自己和伴侣留出调适空间。同时，产褥期的高消耗与哺乳需求对膳食营养提出了极高要求（详见第 27 章营养支持）。

Lochia
恶露排泄机制与临床观察

In the first few weeks to months after giving birth, the birth parent’s hormone levels will change dramatically, and the uterus will begin to contract to its normal size (a process known as involution). The lining of the uterus is shed, resulting in a postpartum vaginal discharge called lochia. Lochia consists of blood, tissue, white blood cells (leukocytes), cervical mucus, and bacteria. It starts immediately after birth and continues for 4 to 6 weeks.
在分娩后的数周至数月内，产妇体内的雌激素与孕激素水平呈现断崖式骤降，子宫在催产素等作用下通过持续节律性肌阵缩逐步缩减体积并恢复至常态，该解剖复旧过程特称为“子宫复旧（Involution）”。在此期间，坏死脱落的子宫内膜基蜕膜、胎盘剥离创面渗出液随宫缩经阴道持续排出体外，形成所谓“恶露（Lochia）”。恶露由血液、坏死脱落蜕膜组织、白细胞（Leukocytes）、宫颈黏液以及宫腔脱落上皮与共生细菌混合组成。恶露在胎盘娩出后立即开始排出，通常持续 4 至 6 周之久。

Stages of Lochia & Perineal Care
恶露三阶段演变与会阴部伤口护理

The discharge changes colour and decreases in amount as the uterus heals. Three stages (FIGURE 41.1) describe the changes in the appearance of lochia: (1) Lochia rubra (rubra means “red”): Dark red or bloody discharge seen during the first 3 to 4 days after childbirth. It contains mostly blood and placental tissue. (2) Lochia serosa (serosa means “watery”): Pinkish-brown or watery discharge that begins on about the 4th day and lasts until about the 10th to 14th day. It contains blood, leukocytes, and cervical mucus. (3) Lochia alba (alba means “white”): White, cream-coloured, or pale yellow discharge that begins around the 10th to 14th day and lasts for 2 to 6 weeks. It consists mostly of leukocytes, mucus, and epithelial cells.
随着子宫创面的逐步上皮化愈合，恶露的颜色会发生特征性的演变，其排泄总量亦呈进行性减少。临床上将恶露的演进划分为三大经典病理生理阶段（图 41.1）：
① 红恶露（Lochia rubra）：“rubra”在拉丁语中意为“红色”，发生于分娩后最初的 3 至 4 天内，呈暗红色或鲜红色血液样，内含大量红细胞、胎盘蜕膜碎片及少量黏液；
② 浆液恶露（Lochia serosa）：“serosa”意为“浆液/水样”，通常于产后第 4 天开始显现，持续至第 10 至 14 天左右，呈现淡粉褐色或浆液水样，含少量血液、大量白细胞及宫颈黏液；
③ 白恶露（Lochia alba）：“alba”意为“白色”，约于产后第 10 至 14 天开始出现，可持续 2 至 6 周直至完全干净，呈白色、黏稠乳白色或淡黄色，主要由大量坏死白细胞、黏液组织及表皮细胞构成。

Perineal Care and Episiotomy
会阴部冲洗消毒与会阴切开创口护理

Perineal care is given to clean the perineum, promote comfort, and prevent infection. If the client had an episiotomy (an incision in the perineum to enlarge the vaginal opening) or a tear during delivery, the area will be tender, swollen, and bruised. A squirt bottle filled with warm tap water (perineal wash) is used to rinse the perineum after each voiding or bowel movement. Always cleanse or pat dry from front to back (urethra to anus) to avoid contaminating the vagina or incision with rectal bacteria. Change sanitary pads frequently. Tampons should NEVER be used postpartum because they introduce bacteria into the raw uterine wound and dramatically increase the risk of serious infection and toxic shock syndrome (TSS)!
产妇实施会阴护理（Perineal Care）旨在保持生殖道外口清洁干燥、减轻局部肿痛痉挛并严密阻断病原菌上行感染。若产妇在经阴道分娩时接受了会阴切开术（Episiotomy，切开会阴以扩大产道出口）或发生了会阴裂伤，缝合创面往往高度水肿、充血淤青并伴剧烈触痛。在每次排尿或排便完毕后，应指导并协助产妇使用装满温水的冲洗壶（Peri-bottle）温和冲洗会阴部。擦拭或蘸干手法必须严格遵循“由前向后（从尿道口向肛门方向）”的单向原则，绝对禁止由后向前来回涂抹，以防将肛周粪便大肠杆菌带入阴道创口与宫颈管。同时须勤换高吸收性产妇卫生巾。产褥期绝对严禁使用内置式卫生棉条（Tampons）：棉条会将外源性细菌直接推入尚未闭合的生殖道深部，阻碍创面引流，极易诱发致命的化脓性产褥感染与中毒性休克综合征（TSS）！

Caesarean Care & Breast Care
剖宫产腹壁切口护理与乳房胀痛管理

A Caesarean section (C-section) involves surgical incisions through the abdomen and uterus to deliver the baby. The birth parent is recovering from both major abdominal surgery and childbirth. The abdominal incision may be closed with staples, stitches, or skin glue, and the area around the incision will be bruised, swollen, and tender. As a support worker, you should: (1) Observe the incision dressing for drainage, redness, or separation of edges. (2) Support the incision with a small pillow when the client coughs, sneezes, moves, or laughs (splinting). (3) Help the client get in and out of bed gently. (4) Report any fever, excessive pain, or foul-smelling drainage immediately.
剖宫产术（Caesarean Section / C-section）是一种产科开腹大手术，外科医生通过切开下腹壁各层组织及子宫壁切口将胎儿娩出。产妇在术后不仅要经历分娩后的激素内分泌剧变，更要承受腹部大手术的组织创伤愈合。腹部外科切口通常采用医用手术缝线、金属吻合钉（Staples）或医用皮肤胶闭合，切口边缘通常呈现淤青、轻度水肿与明显活动痛。作为支持工作人员，应严格落实以下四项照护要领：① 定时检视切口敷料，严密观察有无活动性鲜血渗透、脓性分泌物、创缘红肿隆起或切口裂开；② 在协助产妇咳嗽、深呼吸、打喷嚏、翻身或大笑时，指导并协助其用小软枕按压紧贴切口（夹板固定支撑法 Splinting），以有效缓冲腹压剧增对缝合线的撕扯与剧痛；③ 协助产妇以侧卧屈膝支撑法平稳轻柔地上下床，避免腹直肌过度牵拉；④ 一旦发现产妇体温升高（>38.0°C）、切口搏动性剧痛或分泌物散发恶臭，必须立即汇报责任护士。

Breast Care and Lactation
乳房护理、泌乳机制与乳房过度充盈（乳胀）

Lactation is the process of producing and secreting milk from the breast. For the first few days after birth, the breasts produce colostrum—a thick, yellowish fluid rich in antibodies, protein, and calories that provides the newborn with vital immunity. Around the 2nd to 4th day postpartum, mature breast milk begins to “come in.” At this time, the breasts can become overfilled with milk, blood, and body fluids—a condition known as engorged breasts. Engorged breasts become swollen, hard, warm, shiny, and very painful. The nipples may become flattened, making it difficult for the infant to latch on.
泌乳（Lactation）是指乳腺腺泡合成、分泌并排出乳汁的生理过程。在产后最初的 2 至 3 天内，乳房并不分泌大量成熟乳，而是分泌质地黏稠、色泽微黄的初乳（Colostrum）。初乳富含极高浓度的分泌型免疫球蛋白 A（sIgA）、优质乳清蛋白及丰富电解质，是新生儿抵御呼吸道及肠道病原体感染的最坚实“天然免疫防护衣”。约在产后第 2 至 4 天，体内的泌乳素激增，成熟乳汁开始大量涌出（俗称“下奶”）。在此阶段，由于乳腺腺泡与乳导管内乳汁快速充盈，加之局部毛细血管充血与间质淋巴水肿，极易诱发生理性乳房过度充盈（乳胀 Engorged Breasts）。发生乳胀时，双侧乳房显著肿胀、质地坚如磐石、皮温升高、表面皮肤发亮紧绷伴剧烈触痛；乳晕甚至肿胀隆起使乳头凹陷平坦，导致婴儿极其难以张口含接（Latch-on）。
To relieve engorgement in breastfeeding parents: (1) Apply warm compresses or take a warm shower immediately before nursing to stimulate the let-down reflex. (2) Nurse frequently (every 1.5 to 3 hours). (3) Express a small amount of milk manually or with a pump before feeding to soften the areola. (4) Apply cold compresses between feedings to reduce swelling. (5) Wear a well-fitting, supportive bra without underwires.
对于母乳喂养的产妇，缓解乳房过度充盈（乳胀）的循证照护对策包括：① 哺乳前即刻实施温热敷或温水淋浴数分钟，以扩张乳腺导管并诱发喷乳反射（排乳反射 Let-down reflex）；② 增加亲喂频次（每 1.5 至 3 小时有效吸吮一次），利用婴儿的强力吸吮彻底排空乳汁；③ 若乳晕过于坚硬导致婴儿无法含乳，哺乳前先用双手或吸奶器人工挤出微量乳汁，使乳晕变软易捏，利于深部含接；④ 在两次哺乳间歇期施加局部冷敷，以收缩微血管并减轻间质水肿与灼痛感；⑤ 穿戴松紧适度、承托力良好且无硬质钢圈的纯棉全罩杯哺乳文胸。

Mastitis & Postpartum Mental Health
乳腺炎防治与产后情绪障碍三级演进

A plugged milk duct occurs when milk cannot flow through a milk duct. It feels like a small, tender lump in the breast. If left untreated, a plugged duct can quickly progress to mastitis—an infection of the breast tissue. Bacteria, usually from the baby’s mouth or the mother’s skin, enter through cracked nipples. Symptoms of mastitis include: a firm, red, painful wedge-shaped area on one breast; high fever (often above 38.5°C); severe flu-like chills and body aches; and exhaustion. Mastitis requires prompt medical treatment with antibiotics. The mother should continue breastfeeding or pumping from both breasts (including the affected breast) to keep the breast empty and promote healing.
乳腺导管阻塞（Plugged Duct）是指部分乳汁在导管内淤积凝固形成结节，触诊时表现为乳房局部质硬、伴压痛的小肿块。若不及时排空疏通，乳汁淤积往往极易继发细菌入侵，迅速演变为急性乳腺炎（Mastitis）。病原菌（通常为金黄色葡萄球菌）常由婴儿口腔黏膜破损或母亲乳头破裂皲裂处侵入。乳腺炎的特征性临床表现包括：患侧乳房出现边界清晰的楔形红肿热痛硬块、突发高热（常达 38.5°C 以上）、重度寒战发抖、全身肌肉酸痛与极度乏力虚脱。乳腺炎必须由执业医师及时开具抗菌药物治疗。值得特别强调的是：患有乳腺炎的母亲应当继续哺乳或按时用吸奶器排空患侧乳房，彻底排空乳汁是消除感染病灶与加速炎症消退的关键基石，且规范抗生素治疗期间母乳喂养对足月健康婴儿完全安全。

Postpartum Mental Health: Three Stages
产后心理健康三级演进：从轻度低落到危重精神病

Postpartum emotional changes range from mild sadness to life-threatening psychosis: (1) Postpartum blues (“baby blues”): Experienced by up to 80% of new birth parents during the first 2 weeks. Symptoms include mood swings, crying spells, anxiety, irritability, and insomnia. It is caused by rapid hormonal shifts and fatigue. It is self-limiting and resolves on its own with rest and emotional support. (2) Postpartum depression (PPD): A major depressive disorder that can occur anytime in the first year after birth, affecting about 10% to 15% of birth parents. Symptoms are deeper and last longer: intense sadness, overwhelming despair, lack of bonding with the infant, uncontrolled crying, panic attacks, and feelings of inadequacy. Professional counseling and medical treatment are necessary. (3) Postpartum psychosis: A rare (1 in 1,000) but catastrophic psychiatric emergency. Symptoms include delusions, hallucinations, extreme agitation, paranoia, confusion, and thoughts of suicide or harming the infant. The birth parent must NEVER be left alone with the baby, and emergency psychiatric care must be summoned immediately!
产妇在分娩后的情绪障碍呈现出由轻至重、病理机制截然不同的三级演进谱系：
① 产后情绪低落（Postpartum Blues / Baby Blues）：高发于高达 70% 至 80% 的新产妇，通常在产后第 3 至 5 天出现，于 2 周内自行消退。主要表现为情绪不稳定、无故阵发性哭泣、轻度焦虑、易怒、脆弱及睡眠障碍。这主要由胎盘娩出后雌孕激素断崖式剧降及照顾婴儿的睡眠剥夺引起，属于生理性自限状态，只需充足休息与家人情感支持即可自愈。
② 产后抑郁症（Postpartum Depression, PPD）：发生在产后一年内任何时期的重度抑郁发作，约波及 10% 至 15% 的产妇。其症状显著深重且迁延不愈：极度持久的悲伤、深重绝望感、对婴儿缺乏母爱连接、不可自控的痛哭、恐慌发作以及自责无能感。产妇失去日常生活能力，必须寻求专科心理咨询与药物治疗干预。
③ 产后精神病（Postpartum Psychosis）：一种极为罕见（发病率约 1/1000）但极度危凶险的精神科急症。通常在产后数天内急性暴发，典型症状包括逻辑荒谬的妄想、命令性幻听（如听到神明指令伤害孩子）、极度动作激惹、严重偏执与意识错乱。此时产妇具有极高危的自杀与杀婴（Infanticide）倾向，绝对禁止让产妇与婴儿单独共处！必须立即启动紧急精神科住院干预。

Holding the Infant
抱持婴儿科学手法与头颈部解剖学保护

Holding an infant requires knowledge of infant anatomy. An infant’s neck muscles are weak and undeveloped. In addition, an infant’s head is large and heavy in proportion to the rest of the body. Because infants cannot hold their heads up on their own, you must ALWAYS support the infant’s head and neck whenever lifting, holding, carrying, or laying down the baby. Failure to support the head can cause hyperextension of the neck, severe spinal cord injury, or brain trauma.
科学安全地抱持婴儿必须以深刻理解新生儿的解剖生理特征为前提。新生儿的颈部肌肉与韧带极其薄弱稚嫩，尚未发育出足够的抗重力肌张力。更为特殊的是，婴儿的头部在全身比例中占据极大比重（头部重量几乎占其总体重的 1/4），且缺乏骨骼支撑平衡。由于婴儿完全无法自主直立或稳住自己的头部，支持工作人员在抱起、托持、搬运或放下婴儿的每一个动作瞬间，都必须始终用手掌稳固托住其头枕部与颈椎。一旦忽视头颈部保护，婴儿头部会因重力突然后仰过伸，极易引发致命的颈椎脱位、高位脊髓损伤甚至闭合性颅脑损伤。
Infants have “soft spots” on their heads called fontanelles. The anterior fontanelle (front soft spot) is diamond-shaped and located on the top front of the head; it closes between 12 and 18 months of age. The posterior fontanelle (back soft spot) is triangular and located at the back of the head; it closes by about 2 to 3 months of age. Fontanelles allow the infant’s head to compress slightly during delivery and accommodate rapid brain growth during infancy. Although the soft spots are covered by a tough fibrous membrane, you must never press on them.
新生儿的头颅骨之间尚未完全骨化愈合，遗留有未闭合的纤维膜间隙，临床上特称为“囟门（Fontanelles）”。位于头顶前方的称为前囟门（Anterior fontanelle），呈菱形，通常在婴儿 12 至 18 个月时完全骨性闭合；位于枕骨后方的称为后囟门（Posterior fontanelle），呈三角形，通常在生后 2 至 3 个月时闭合。囟门的存在具有至关重要的生理意义：在分娩时允许颅骨轻度重叠塑形以顺利通过产道狭窄骨盆，并在婴儿期为脑实质的爆炸式迅猛生长提供充足的颅腔容积。虽然囟门表面覆盖有坚韧的纤维结缔组织膜与头皮，但在日常洗护时绝对不可用手指用力按压或指甲搔抓该区域。

Comforting the Infant & Swaddling
安抚啼哭婴儿科学技巧与襁褓裹包操作

Crying is an infant’s only way to communicate. When a baby cries, it indicates an unmet need. The infant may be hungry, wet or soiled, too hot or cold, in pain, lonely, or overstimulated. Never let an infant “cry it out”—infants cry because they need comfort and care. Responding promptly to crying builds trust and emotional security. In addition, NEVER shake a baby! Shaking a baby causes “Shaken Baby Syndrome”—violent whiplash forces cause the brain to slam against the skull, resulting in subdural hematomas, permanent blindness, severe brain damage, or death. If you feel overwhelmed, put the baby in a safe place (such as the crib) and seek immediate assistance.

⚠️ THINK ABOUT SAFETY: SIDS Prevention Guidelines
安全警示思考：预防婴儿猝死综合征（SIDS）国家临床金标准

Sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS) is the sudden, unexplained death of an apparently healthy infant under 1 year of age, usually during sleep. To minimize the risk of SIDS, health authorities (including Health Canada and the Canadian Paediatric Society) recommend the following strict safety guidelines:
婴儿猝死综合征（Sudden Infant Death Syndrome, SIDS）是指一岁以内表面看似完全健康正常的婴儿在睡眠中发生的突发、不可预测且原因不明的死亡事件。为了将 SIDS 的发生风险降至最低，加拿大卫生部（Health Canada）与加拿大儿科学会（CPS）联合制定了以下不可动摇的睡眠安全硬性规范：
  • Back to Sleep for Every Sleep: Always place the infant on their back to sleep, for naps and at night. Never place an infant on their stomach (prone) or side. Prone sleeping obstructs airways and causes carbon dioxide rebreathing.
    每次入睡严格采取仰卧位：无论白天小睡还是夜间长睡，婴儿必须始终采取仰卧姿势。严禁趴睡（俯卧）或侧卧，俯卧会压迫呼吸道并诱发二氧化碳再吸入与窒息。
  • Firm, Flat Sleep Surface: The baby should sleep on a firm, flat mattress covered only by a tightly fitted sheet in a crib, cradle, or bassinet that meets current Canadian safety standards.
    硬质平整床垫：婴儿必须睡在平整坚实的床垫上，且床垫仅铺有一层紧绷床笠；必须使用符合加拿大现行安全法规的合规婴儿床或摇篮。
  • Keep the Sleep Area Bare: Keep soft objects, loose bedding, pillows, bumper pads, quilts, sheepskins, and stuffed toys out of the crib! These soft items can cover the baby’s face and cause suffocation.
    婴儿床内绝对禁止杂物：床内绝不可放置松软毛毯、枕头、防撞床围垫、羽绒被、羊毛皮垫或毛绒玩具！任何松软蓬松物件覆盖口鼻都可在数分钟内导致机械性窒息。
  • Room-Sharing Without Bed-Sharing: The safest place for an infant to sleep is in their own crib in the parents’ bedroom for the first 6 months. Never practice bed-sharing (co-sleeping in an adult bed), which greatly increases the risk of entrapment, suffocation, and SIDS.
    同室不同床原则：生后前 6 个月最安全的睡眠方案是将婴儿床安放在父母卧室床旁。严禁与父母同睡一张成人床（Bed-sharing），成人床的厚重被褥、床垫夹缝及成年人翻身极易造成压迫窒息死亡。
  • Prevent Overheating: Make sure the baby is warm but never hot. Overheating significantly increases SIDS risk. Keep room temperature comfortable (around 20°C to 22°C). Do not overdress the baby. Check the back of the neck: if it is sweaty, the baby is too warm!
    严防体温过热：过度包裹与室内温度过高是 SIDS 的重要触发诱因。穿衣厚度比成年人多一层为宜；用手触摸婴儿后颈部：若出汗湿热则说明穿戴过暖，必须立即减衣降温。
  • Smoke-Free Environment: Maintain a 100% smoke-free and vape-free home and car. Exposure to second-hand tobacco smoke severely compromises the infant’s respiratory control centre.
    完全无烟环境：居家与车辆内必须百分之百绝对戒烟及远离电子烟二手烟，烟草毒素会直接麻痹破坏婴儿脑干自主呼吸中枢。

Common Infant Disorders
婴儿常见生理性紊乱：肠绞痛、乳痂与尿布疹

Infant colic is a condition characterized by bouts of intense, unexplained crying and inconsolable fussiness in an otherwise healthy, well-fed baby. Colic typically starts at 2 to 3 weeks of age, peaks around 6 weeks, and usually subsides by 3 to 4 months of age. Pediatricians often diagnose colic using the “Rule of Threes”: crying for more than 3 hours a day, for more than 3 days a week, for more than 3 weeks in a row. During a colic episode, the baby may clench their fists, draw their knees up to their tense, bloated abdomen, arch their back, and pass flatus (gas). The exact cause is unknown but is thought to involve immature digestive nerves, gut spasms, or food sensitivities. Colic is distressing for parents; assure them that it is not their fault and will resolve.
婴儿肠绞痛（Infant Colic）是一种以外表健康、营养摄入正常的婴儿出现阵发性剧烈、无法安抚且不可预测的持续性啼哭与烦躁不安为特征的发育性生理紊乱。肠绞痛通常在出生后 2 至 3 周始发，于 6 周左右达到顶峰，多在婴儿 3 至 4 个月大时自然彻底消退。儿科学界常采用著名的“三三法则（Rule of Threes）”作为临床诊断依据：即婴儿每天哭吵超过 3 小时，每周出现超过 3 天，且该状态持续超过 3 周以上。绞痛发作时，婴儿常双手紧握成拳、双腿紧紧蜷缩向胀满坚韧的腹部、躯干后仰反弓并伴频繁排气放屁。其确切发病机理尚未完全明确，普遍认为与肠道神经发育不成熟导致的胃肠平滑肌痉挛、积气或对配方奶/母乳饮食过敏相关。肠绞痛会使父母产生巨大挫败感与身心耗竭，支持人员应给予坚定安慰，说明这并非照护失误所致且属于良性暂态。

Cradle Cap (Seborrheic Dermatitis)
乳痂（婴儿脂溢性皮炎）病理特征与清洁护理

Cradle cap (seborrheic dermatitis) is a harmless, non-contagious skin condition in which yellowish, greasy, scaly, or crusty patches appear on the baby’s scalp. It is caused by overactive sebaceous (oil) glands in the skin, stimulated by maternal hormones still circulating in the baby’s bloodstream. The most common location is over the anterior fontanelle (soft spot), but it can spread to the forehead, eyebrows, and behind the ears. Cradle cap does not bother the baby (it does not itch) and resolves with simple care: massage a small amount of baby oil or mineral oil into the crusts to soften them overnight, then wash the scalp the next morning with mild baby shampoo and gently brush off the loosened scales with a soft baby brush or rough facecloth. Never pick or scrape scales forcibly with fingernails, as this can tear the skin and cause bacterial infection!
乳痂（Cradle Cap，医学名为婴儿脂溢性皮炎）是一种良性、无传染性且普遍存在的表皮皮脂代谢异常，表现为婴儿头皮表面形成一片片油腻微黄、呈鳞片状附着或结成硬壳的痂块。其主要发病机理为：残留在婴儿体内的母体胎盘激素过度刺激婴儿头皮皮脂腺分泌，大量旺盛油脂与脱落的表皮角质死皮细胞堆积粘连形成油腻性斑块。最常见的好发部位是头顶前囟门区域，亦可蔓延至前额、眉弓及耳后皱褶。乳痂通常既不瘙痒亦不引起婴儿疼痛。日常温和处理规范是：在前一晚睡前将适量温和婴儿润肤油或矿物油轻柔涂抹于头皮痂块上，使其充分浸润软化一整夜；次日早晨使用温和婴儿洗发水清洁头皮，并用柔软的婴儿发刷或粗面小方巾轻柔打圈刷除已松脱的黄色鳞屑。绝对禁止用手指甲直接用力抠剥硬痂，这会造成娇嫩头皮破损出血，极易引发严重创面化脓性细菌感染！

Diaper Rash Introduction
尿布疹分类与观察要点

Diaper rash is an inflammatory reaction of the skin covered by diapers. Common types include: (1) Simple diaper rash: Red, slightly rough skin caused by contact with moisture, friction, or chemicals in detergents or wipes. (2) Ammonia rash: Caused when bacteria on the skin break down urine into ammonia, producing chemical burns on the skin accompanied by a strong ammonia odor. Always wash hands before and after changing diapers, keep the area clean and dry, expose the buttocks to open air when possible, and report any rash that spreads or persists for longer than 24 hours.
尿布疹（Diaper Rash）是尿布覆盖区域发生的非特异性接触性皮炎。临床常见两大基本类型：① 单纯性尿布疹（Simple Diaper Rash）：受摩擦、潮湿浸渍或洗涤剂、湿纸巾防腐剂化学刺激引起的红斑与粗糙脱皮；② 氨皮炎（Ammonia Rash）：正常皮肤表面的细菌将尿液中的尿素分解产生强碱性氨，对娇嫩表皮造成化学灼伤，更换尿布时能闻到浓烈刺鼻的氨气味。照护人员在换尿布前后必须严格洗手，保持臀部洁净干燥，并在条件允许时多让婴儿光屁股暴露于空气中（Air drying）；若皮疹持续扩散或超过 24 小时未见好转，必须立即汇报主管护士。

Thrush & Diaper Rash Care

If one of these patches is accidentally or intentionally scraped away, you will find underneath a raw, inflamed, red area that may bleed. Thrush is not life-threatening, but it causes significant oral soreness, making feeding very painful; the baby may cry during nursing, refuse the breast or bottle, and become dehydrated. You may first suspect thrush if the baby cries while feeding or sucking on a pacifier. It is most common in infants 2 months of age and younger.

Thrush Treatment & Serious Illness Signs
鹅口疮防治闭环与婴儿潜在重症预警指征

If you suspect the baby has thrush, you should report it immediately to your supervisor. A doctor typically prescribes oral antifungal drops (such as nystatin) that the parent places directly on the white patches inside the baby’s mouth before feedings. If the parent is breastfeeding, the doctor may also prescribe an antifungal cream for the mother’s nipples; this simultaneous treatment is essential to prevent “ping-pong” re-infection between the mother’s breasts and the baby’s mouth. Soothers (pacifiers), bottle nipples, and breast pump parts harbour fungal spores and must be thoroughly sterilized in boiling water or replaced.
一旦怀疑婴儿患有鹅口疮，支持人员必须第一时间通报主管护士。执业医师通常会开具制霉菌素（Nystatin）口服抗真菌混悬滴液，由家长在哺乳喂食前直接滴涂于婴儿口腔白斑黏膜处。若产妇正在进行母乳亲喂，医生通常会同时为母亲开具抗真菌乳膏涂抹于乳头及乳晕表面；母婴双向同步抗真菌治疗是彻底阻断“乒乓球式”母婴反复交叉重叠感染的核心关键。此外，婴儿使用的安抚奶嘴、奶瓶奶嘴以及吸奶器喇叭罩极易藏匿真菌孢子，必须通过沸水煮沸彻底灭菌或直接弃旧换新。

Signs and Symptoms of Potentially Serious Illness
婴儿潜在危重疾病的早期特征性体征与急症识别

Infants can become critically ill very quickly! A newborn’s immune system is immature, and infections can spread rapidly throughout the body (sepsis). Signs and symptoms of serious illness in infants may be subtle at first and develop suddenly. You must be extremely observant and alert. Report the exact time when symptoms began. If an infant exhibits any of the red-flag signs in BOX 41.6, inform your supervisor or call emergency medical services immediately! Axillary temperatures are taken on infants (normal: 36.5°C to 37.5°C). Any fever above 38.0°C in an infant under 3 months is a medical emergency requiring immediate hospital evaluation.
婴儿的病情变化极其迅猛，往往数小时之内即可由轻症恶化为危重状态！新生儿的非特异性免疫功能尚未健全，局部感染极易突破血脑屏障或血流屏障发展为暴发性脓毒症（Sepsis）。婴儿严重全身性疾病的前驱症状起初往往极其隐匿、微弱且具有突发性。支持人员必须保持极高的临床警惕性，准确记录任何细微异常初发的时间点。一旦发现婴儿出现专栏 41.6 中列举的任何一条红色危险警讯，必须分秒必争地汇报主管护士或即刻拨打急救电话！婴儿常规采用腋温测量（正常腋温为 36.5°C 至 37.5°C）。在 3 个月以内的婴儿中，任何体温超过 38.0°C（100.4°F）的发热均属于急诊就医红线，必须立即送医进行全面的脓毒症排查。

🚨 BOX 41.6: Signs and Symptoms of Serious Illness in an Infant

  • Temperature Instability: Fever of 38.0°C (100.4°F) or higher, or low body temperature below 36.0°C (96.8°F). (Hypothermia in newborns is often a more ominous sign of sepsis than fever!)

Feeding the Infant & Breastfeeding Positions
婴儿喂养方式、母乳喂养体位与配方奶分类

Breast milk is the ideal and complete food for human infants, providing perfectly balanced nutrients, growth factors, and active immunoglobulins that protect against infections and chronic illnesses. Health Canada and the Canadian Paediatric Society recommend exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months of life, with continued breastfeeding alongside complementary foods for up to 2 years or beyond. However, for personal, cultural, or medical reasons, some parents choose or require formula feeding. As a support worker, you must support and respect the family’s chosen feeding method without judgment.

Infant Formula Types
市售婴儿配方奶三大类剂型特性

Infant formulas are commercially produced to mimic human breast milk. Three common forms are available: (1) Ready-to-feed formula: Liquid formula ready to pour straight from the can into clean bottles. It requires NO dilution with water. It is the most convenient but most expensive. (2) Liquid concentrate formula: Concentrated liquid that must be diluted with an equal amount of boiled, cooled water before feeding. (3) Powdered formula: Dry powder that must be mixed with boiled, cooled water according to exact package instructions. It is the least expensive and has the longest shelf life, but careful measuring is critical—too much powder causes kidney stress and severe dehydration, while too little causes malnutrition!
商业婴儿配方奶通过精密的工业配方模拟母乳成分，市面常见三大类制剂：
① 即开即用型配方奶（Ready-to-feed）：无需加水稀释，开罐/开瓶后摇匀即可直接倒入消过毒的奶瓶中喂哺。最便捷卫生，但价格最为昂贵；
② 浓缩液体配方奶（Liquid concentrate）：高浓度液体，使用前必须严格按照 1:1 的体积比例加入经煮沸冷却的无菌饮用水彻底调匀方可饮用；
③ 粉末状配方奶（Powdered formula）：粉剂罐装，必须严格依照产品罐体说明书的平勺刻度与指定水温水量充分溶解混合。其经济实惠且开罐后保质期较长，但水与奶粉的调配比例必须绝对精准：若冲调过浓，高渗透压会极大加重婴儿娇嫩肾脏排泄负担并诱发高钠脱水坏死；若冲调过稀，热量摄入不足则会导致营养不良与水中毒。

Safe Bottle Warming Protocol
安全温奶操作规程与严禁微波加热铁律

Many babies prefer formula at room temperature or slightly warmed. To warm a chilled bottle: place the bottle in a bowl or pot of warm (not boiling) tap water for about 5 to 10 minutes, or use a certified electric bottle warmer. NEVER warm a baby bottle in a microwave oven! Microwaves do not heat liquids evenly; they produce hidden, extremely hot pockets of liquid (“hot spots”) while the outside of the bottle feels cool. These hot spots can cause horrific, full-thickness burns to the infant’s mouth, tongue, and esophagus. After warming, swirl the bottle gently to mix, then shake a few drops onto the inner aspect of your wrist. It should feel pleasantly warm, never hot!
大多数婴儿偏好室温或略微温润的配方奶。温热冷藏奶瓶的标准规范是：将奶瓶置入盛有温热自来水（非滚开沸水）的水盆或水杯中温水水浴浸泡 5 至 10 分钟，或使用合规的恒温电动暖奶器。最严正的安全禁忌是：绝对严禁使用微波炉加热婴儿奶瓶！微波辐射穿透液体极不均匀，会引发严重的局部液体过热，形成隐藏在奶液深处的极高温滚烫“热点（Hot spots）”，而奶瓶外壁此时摸起来甚至依然温凉。这种隐蔽的高温热点在婴儿大口吞咽时会瞬间造成口腔黏膜、舌体以及食道黏膜的重度热坏死与三度热灼伤！水浴加温后，先轻轻旋转瓶身晃匀奶液，随后将奶嘴倒转挤出数滴奶汁滴于自己敏感的手腕内侧，体感应为舒适温润，绝不能有丝毫烫手感！

Burping the Infant
协助婴儿拍嗝排气机制与安全喂养姿势

Babies swallow some air during feeding, especially when bottle-fed. Swallowed air accumulates in the baby’s stomach, causing uncomfortable bloating, fullness, colic, and vomiting. Burping expels this trapped air. When bottle-feeding, tilt the bottle so that the neck of the bottle and nipple are ALWAYS full of formula (FIGURE 41.11). NEVER prop a bottle on a pillow or rolled blanket to feed the baby (FIGURE 41.12)! Bottle propping is an extremely dangerous hazard: the infant cannot push the heavy bottle away, leading to fatal choking, liquid inhalation, aspiration pneumonia, and severe ear infections. Always hold the infant in your arms when bottle-feeding!
婴儿在吸吮进食过程中不可避免地会吞入大量空气，在奶瓶喂养中尤为明显。积聚在胃部的气体不仅会产生腹胀饱胀感，更会诱发腹部剧烈绞痛、吐奶与喷射性溢奶。拍嗝（Burping）是通过物理手法协助婴儿排出胃内积气。瓶喂时必须保持奶瓶适当倾斜角度，使奶液始终完全充满奶嘴及瓶颈部（图 41.11），杜绝吸入空泡。绝对禁止用枕头或卷起的毛毯垫高奶瓶让婴儿平躺自主吸吮（即架空奶瓶 Bottle Propping，图 41.12）！架空奶瓶是极其凶险的致死隐患：婴儿无力推开重物奶瓶，一旦溢奶会导致无法躲避的呛咳窒息、误吸性吸入性肺炎以及奶水倒灌咽鼓管引发重度化脓性中耳炎。瓶喂时必须始终将婴儿温柔环抱在自己手臂怀中！

Diapering Basics: Safety & Sanitation

Newborns need diaper changes frequently—typically 8 to 12 times in 24 hours. Prompt diaper changes prevent skin breakdown, painful diaper rashes, and ascending urinary tract infections. Care of the Umbilical Cord
新生儿脐带残端干燥与抗感染照护规范

FIGURE 41.17 / 图 41.17: The umbilical cord connects the pregnant client and the fetus, transporting oxygen, nutrients, and waste products.
脐带在子宫内连接孕妇胎盘与胎儿，负责运输富氧血液、葡萄糖营养素并排出胎儿代谢废物。分娩后即行钳夹剪断。
The umbilical cord connects the pregnant client and the fetus (unborn baby) and carries blood, oxygen, and nutrients from the birth parent to the fetus (FIGURE 41.17). The umbilical cord is not needed after birth, so shortly after delivery, the physician or midwife clamps and cuts the cord. The stump of cord that is left on the baby dries up, turns blackish-brown, and naturally separates and falls off in 7 to 14 days. Slight oozing of a drop or two of blood may occur when the cord stump detaches. NEVER pull or twist the cord stump off, even if it looks ready to fall off hanging by a thread! Pulling the cord can cause massive hemorrhage and tear raw tissue.
脐带在母体子宫内连接胎盘与胎儿（图 41.17），是胎儿获取母体氧气与血流养分的生命线。胎儿娩出后肺循环建立，脐带便完成了其历史使命，产科医生或助产士会在距离婴儿脐轮约 2 至 3 厘米处放置无菌塑料脐夹并剪断脐带。遗留在婴儿腹壁上的残端逐渐缺血坏死、萎缩风化、变黑变硬，并在出生后 7 至 14 天内自然干燥脱落。在残端脱落的瞬间，创面上可能会有微量一两滴陈旧血迹渗出，属于正常生理现象。绝对禁止用手用力牵拉、扯拽或旋转脐带残端，即便残端看似仅连着一丝表皮！强行拉扯会撕裂深层腹壁动脉创面，诱发无法止血的脐带大出血甚至腹壁疝。
The cord stump provides a rich breeding ground for bacteria, so it must be kept clean and dry at all times: (1) Keep the cord dry. (2) Wash hands before and after touching the umbilical area. (3) Clean around the base of the cord stump with plain warm water and a clean, soft cloth or cotton swab (FIGURE 41.18). Note: Alcohol swabs are NO longer recommended because alcohol delays natural cord separation and irritates delicate skin. (4) Fold the top of the diaper below the cord stump. (5) Give sponge baths until the cord falls off and the navel heals. (6) Report any foul odor, purulent drainage, or spreading redness around the navel immediately!
湿润坏死的脐带残端是细菌定植滋生的绝佳温床，因此照护核心原则是“绝对保持清洁与充分干燥（Dry Cord Care）”：① 接触脐部前后严格洗手消毒；② 每次海绵擦浴或换尿布时，用温水浸湿的纯棉小方巾或无菌医用棉签，由脐窝中央向外周由内向外轻柔擦除基底分泌物（图 41.18）。特别注意：现代儿科指南已明确废除使用医用酒精涂抹脐带的陈旧做法，酒精会杀灭抑制局部有益菌群、硬化残端并显著推迟脐带的自然脱落时间；③ 尿布上缘必须向下翻折至脐带残端以下，防尿液浸湿；④ 脐带脱落前仅实施温水海绵擦浴，严禁整身浸入深盆浴浸泡脐带；⑤ 一旦发现脐周腹壁皮肤发红肿胀、触痛发硬、脐窝有黄白色脓性恶臭分泌物，必须立即通报主管护士，此乃新生儿急性脐炎（Omphalitis）的危险前兆！

Circumcision is the surgical removal of the foreskin (prepuce) from the penis. Parents decide whether to have their male infants circumcised based on personal, cultural, or religious beliefs (e.g., in Judaism, it is performed on the 8th day as a religious covenant by a mohel). In Canadian healthcare, circumcision is an elective procedure. Caring for an uncircumcised penis is very simple: gently wash the outside of the penis and scrotum with warm water and mild soap during bathing. NEVER force or retract the foreskin in an uncircumcised infant! In newborns, the foreskin is naturally adherent to the glans penis by embryological adhesions; forcing it back tears delicate tissue, causing severe pain, bleeding, and scarring that leads to pathological phimosis (constriction of the opening). The foreskin will separate and retract naturally over several years (often by age 3 to 5).

Care of the circumcised penis: (1) Clean the penis gently with warm water at each diaper change. Avoid commercial wipes with alcohol or fragrance on the raw wound. (2) Apply a generous dab of petroleum jelly (Vaseline) or an antibiotic ointment on the glans or onto a sterile gauze pad (FIGURE 41.19) at every diaper change. Petroleum jelly prevents the raw, sensitive glans from painfully sticking to the diaper fabric! (3) Fasten the diaper loosely over the front of the penis. (4) The penis will appear red, swollen, and crusty for the first 2 to 3 days; a yellowish film (granulation tissue/fibrin) will form over the head around days 2 to 4—this is normal healing tissue and must NOT be scrubbed or washed off. The wound heals fully in 10 to 14 days. Report active bleeding (more than a quarter-sized stain on diaper), swelling that prevents urination, foul-smelling drainage, or absence of voiding for 8 hours.

婴儿沐浴安全守则、水温监测与溺水防范红线

FIGURE 41.20 / 图 41.20: Use the inside of your wrist to test the bath water temperature before bathing.

Infants do not need a full bath every day; bathing 2 to 3 times a week is sufficient to keep them clean while preventing their delicate skin from drying out. However, the face, neck folds, hands, and diaper area should be cleansed daily. Sponge baths are given until the umbilical cord stump falls off and heals, and until a circumcision heals. After the cord and circumcision have healed, tub baths can be introduced (FIGURE 41.21).
新生婴儿并不需要每日进行全身水浸沐浴；通常每周进行 2 至 3 次温和沐浴即可满足清洁卫生的生理需求，过于频繁的清洗反而会破坏表皮天然皮脂保护屏障导致皮肤粗糙皲裂。但婴儿的面部、下颌颈部皮肤深褶、双手以及被尿便污染的会阴臀部则必须每日进行局部细致清洗。在新生儿脐带残端尚未脱落愈合、以及男婴包皮环切伤口尚未完全上皮化之前，常规必须采取“海绵温水擦浴（Sponge bath）”。待脐带残端完全干瘪脱落且脐窝上皮完全平整愈合后，方可正式开启“婴儿浴盆盆浴（Tub bath，图 41.21）”。
Bath safety rules are absolute: (1) NEVER leave a baby unattended in or near water, not even for a fraction of a second! A baby can silently drown in as little as 2.5 cm (1 inch) of water in seconds. Never step away to answer a phone, open a door, or retrieve a towel. If you must leave the room, wrap the baby in a towel and take the baby with you! (2) The room must be warm (approx. 24°C / 75°F) and completely free of drafts. (3) Gather all supplies beforehand. (4) Test bath water with a bath thermometer (37.8°C to 40.5°C / 100°F to 105°F) or by dipping your inner wrist into the water (FIGURE 41.20). (5) Fill the tub with only 5 to 8 cm (2 to 3 inches) of warm water. (6) Hold the baby securely at all times with a non-slip grip.
婴儿沐浴最高安全法则与铁律：① 绝对禁止将婴儿单独留在水中或靠近水盆之处，哪怕只有短短一秒钟！婴儿的运动反射极不成熟，身体失去平衡倒伏在水深仅 2.5 厘米（1 英寸）的水面中即可在数秒内发生静默性无声溺水死亡。严禁在沐浴中途抽身去接听电话、开门或拿取遗忘的毛巾；若遇不可抗力的紧急情况必须离开房间，必须将婴儿从水中抱出用大毛巾妥善包裹，随身抱离！② 沐浴房间必须提前加温（室温维持在约 24°C / 75°F），紧闭门窗彻底消除穿堂冷风；③ 沐浴前备齐全部洗护用品；④ 用水温计精准测量水温（标准温度区间为 37.8°C 至 40.5°C / 100°F 至 105°F），或将自身手腕内侧深入盆底水流中亲自试温（图 41.20），确保绝不烫手；⑤ 婴儿浴盆内水深仅需蓄入 5 至 8 厘米（2 至 3 英寸）浅水即可；⑥ 沐浴全程用前臂和手指牢固扣握婴儿腋下，实施防滑握持保护。

PRE-PROCEDURE / 准备阶段

  1. Ensure room is warm (24°C) and draft-free. Wash hands thoroughly.
    确保室温维持在 24°C，无对流冷风。严格执行手卫生洗手。
  2. Collect supplies: Baby tub / washbasin; Bath thermometer; Large hooded towel; 2 to 3 soft washcloths; Mild baby soap and shampoo; Cotton balls; Clean diaper and clean clothes; Waterproof pad.
    备齐物品：婴儿浴盆/洗脸盆、水温计、带帽保暖大浴巾、2至3块纯棉软毛巾、温和婴儿无泪洗发沐浴露、医用脱脂棉球、干净尿布及全套替换衣物、防水垫。提前按使用顺序排列在操作台旁。
  3. Fill tub with 5 to 8 cm (2 to 3 inches) of warm water. Measure water temperature (37.8°C to 40.5°C) or test with inner wrist.
    在盆内注入 5 至 8 厘米温水。用水温计精准测温（37.8°C–40.5°C）或将手腕内侧伸入水中试温。

PROCEDURE / 操作步骤（眼部与面部）

  1. Undress the baby, leaving the diaper on. Wrap the baby securely in a warm towel. (Keeping the diaper on prevents the baby from urinating or soiling the wash area during facial care.)
    脱去婴儿衣物，暂留纸尿裤不脱。用大毛巾包裹身体保暖。（保留尿布可防范清洁面部时婴儿排尿排便污染操作台。）
  2. Hold the baby in the cradle position on the changing table or your lap.
    将婴儿以摇篮抱姿势平稳安放在换布台或施术者大腿上。
  3. Dip a clean cotton ball or washcloth corner in plain warm water (NO soap!). Squeeze out excess water.
    取脱脂棉球或软毛巾一角浸入纯净温水中（绝对不加任何肥皂沐浴露！），挤出多余水分。
  4. Gently wipe one eyelid from the inner canthus (corner near nose) to the outer canthus (FIGURE 41.22). Discard cotton ball. (Wiping from inner to outer canthus prevents dirt and eye secretions from entering and blocking the nasolacrimal tear duct.)
    轻柔擦拭一侧眼睑：严格由内眦（靠鼻梁侧）向外眦（靠外耳侧）单向轻拭（图 41.22），随后弃去棉球。（由内向外擦拭能彻底防止眼部分泌物倒灌进入鼻泪管引发鼻泪管炎性堵塞。）
  5. Use a fresh cotton ball or different washcloth corner for the other eye. Wipe from inner to outer canthus. (Using a fresh surface for each eye prevents transferring bacteria from an infected eye to an uninfected eye.)
    换用一个全新无菌棉球或毛巾的另一洁净角，以相同手法擦拭另一侧眼睛。（严禁一只棉球两眼混用，以杜绝眼结膜感染性眼炎的双眼交叉污染。）
FIGURE 41.22 / 图 41.22: Wash the baby’s eyelids from the inner canthus to the outer canthus with plain warm water. Use a separate cotton ball or cloth corner for each eye.
使用温水单向擦拭婴儿眼睑：由内眦擦向外眦。每只眼睛必须使用独立的新棉球，防交叉感染。

PROCEDURE: BATHING THE BABY (CONT’D) / 操作规程：婴儿全流程规范沐浴法（面部、洗头与盆浴）

  1. Wash the baby’s face with a mitted washcloth using plain warm water (FIGURE 41.23). Do not use soap on the face. Wash ears, behind ears, under chin, and neck folds. Pat completely dry.
    将毛巾折叠成手套状浸温水（面部不使用任何肥皂，图 41.23），轻柔清洗前额、面颊、外耳廓及耳后深褶，翻开下颌吸干颈部皮褶奶渍污垢，彻底轻拍蘸干。
  2. Wash the head: Hold the baby in the “football hold” (FIGURE 41.24) with the head extended over the water basin. Support the head and neck firmly with your palm. Wet the hair with a small cup or wet washcloth.
    清洗头部：采取“橄榄球抱式（Football hold，图 41.24）”将婴儿夹持在腋下侧腰部，使其头部悬空伸出至水盆上方；用手掌与示指稳固托住枕颈部。用小水杯或湿毛巾将头皮打湿。
  3. Apply a drop of baby shampoo. Lather gently using circular motions over the entire scalp, including the fontanelle area.
    滴入微量婴儿洗发露，用指腹轻柔打圈揉出泡沫清洗整个头皮（含前囟门区域，动作须温和）。
  4. Rinse the head thoroughly by cupping water with your hand or a clean wet cloth, wiping backward to keep soapy water out of the baby’s eyes. Pat dry immediately with a hooded towel.
    用手舀温水或用干净湿毛巾由前向后彻底冲洗头皮，防止肥皂水流入眼眶。冲净后立即用大毛巾角吸干头皮水分保暖。
  5. Remove the diaper and clean the genital/buttocks area if soiled.
    解开尿布，若有粪便污染先行擦净。
  6. Tub Bath Entry: Place your non-dominant arm behind the baby’s shoulders, securely gripping the baby’s far axilla (armpit) with your fingers. Support the buttocks with your dominant hand. Lower the baby gently feet-first into the tub (FIGURE 41.25).
    入盆手法：非优势手前臂横贯婴儿肩背部，手指紧紧扣握住婴儿对侧腋窝（防滑脱安全锁扣）；优势手稳托臀部。双脚朝前平稳柔和地将婴儿下半身没入温水中（图 41.25）。
  7. Keep one hand securely gripping the baby's axilla at ALL times during the bath! Use your free hand to wash the chest, abdomen, arms, legs, and back with mild soap.
    在整个盆浴过程中，扣握婴儿腋窝的手臂绝对不可松开！腾出的另一只手打上温和皂液，依次清洗前胸、腹部、双上肢、双腿深褶及背部。
  8. Wash the genital area last, strictly wiping from front to back.
    最后清洗外阴会阴区，严格遵循由前向后清洗原则。

将软毛巾折成手套状，用纯温水轻拭面部与下颌皮褶。

以橄榄球夹腋抱姿在水盆上方冲洗洗发，手指托稳头颈。

POST-PROCEDURE: BATHING THE BABY / 婴儿沐浴后置复原处置规范

  1. Lift the baby safely out of the tub onto a large dry hooded towel on the changing table. (Hold baby securely; soapy skin is very slippery!)
    将婴儿自水中平稳托起抱出，立即转移至台面上摊开的干燥带帽大浴巾上。（肥皂水浸泡后表皮极滑，抓握必须稳固防脱手跌落！）
  2. Wrap the baby immediately in the warm towel, covering the head with the hood to prevent rapid heat loss.
    立即用大浴巾包裹全身，扣上帽子遮盖头顶（头部是新生儿热量散失最迅猛的部位）。
  3. Gently pat the skin dry, ensuring that all skin folds (neck, armpits, groin, behind knees) are completely dry. (Leaving folds damp causes maceration and intertrigo!)
    用浴巾轻拍吸干皮肤，重点翻开颈部、腋窝、腹股沟及腘窝等全部生理皱褶彻底吸干水分。（褶皱潮湿极易引起表皮浸渍与摩擦红斑擦烂！）
  4. Provide cord care and circumcision care as ordered. Put on a clean diaper, apply protective cream if ordered, and dress in clean clothes.
    依医嘱进行脐带残端及包皮环切伤口护理。穿上洁净尿布、薄涂护臀膏并换上干净婴儿服。
  5. Place the baby safely in the crib.
    将婴儿安全抱回婴儿床内。
  6. Empty tub, clean and disinfect equipment, wash hands, and Report and Record observations (behavior, skin condition, tolerance).
    排空浴盆积水，清洁消毒器材用具，洗手消毒。详尽记录沐浴过程、婴儿精神反应、皮肤色泽及伤口状况。

Weighing Infants
婴儿体重测量临床意义与生长发育监测原则

Infants are weighed at regular intervals (at birth, at hospital discharge, and during regular community well-baby visits) to monitor their growth and development. Weight is the most sensitive indicator of an infant’s overall health, nutritional adequacy, and hydration status. Normal full-term newborns typically lose up to 7% to 10% of their birth weight in the first 3 to 4 days of life due to fluid loss and passage of meconium. However, they should regain their birth weight by 10 to 14 days of age. Thereafter, healthy infants gain approximately 20 to 30 grams per day during the first few months. Failure to gain weight or sudden weight loss signals serious feeding difficulties, malabsorption, metabolic disorders, or dehydration.
婴儿在出生时、出院时以及随后的社区定期儿童保健随访中，都需要定期精密测量体重。体重是评估婴儿机体整体健康状况、营养摄入充分性以及体内水合状态最敏感、最核心的临床客观指标。正常足月新生儿在生后最初 3 至 4 天内，由于体内多余水分经呼吸道蒸发、排尿以及排泄胎便，通常会出现生理性体重下降（Physiological weight loss），正常下降幅度为出生体重的 7% 至 10%。但在生后 10 至 14 天左右，婴儿体重应当恢复至出生时的基准线。在此之后，健康足月儿在最初数月内平均每天增长约 20 至 30 克。若体重持续不增、甚至发生进行性下降，往往是喂养困难、乳汁摄入严重不足、吸收不良综合征、先天性代谢病或急性脱水的严重病理警讯。
Infants can be weighed on digital electronic scales (FIGURE 41.26) or balance-beam scales. Digital scales are widely used because they calculate weight quickly and feature an electronic dampening function that compensates for the baby’s movements. To ensure absolute clinical accuracy: (1) Weigh the baby at the same time of day. (2) Weigh the baby completely naked (undiapered), as a wet or soiled diaper can add up to 200 grams of false weight! (3) Always place a clean disposable paper towel or liner on the scale tray to prevent chilling and cross-contamination. (4) Balance (tare/zero) the scale with the liner in place BEFORE placing the baby on the tray.
测量婴儿体重通常使用高精度数字电子婴儿秤（图 41.26）或经典机械杠杆秤。数字电子秤因读数迅速且具备抗身体晃动数字平滑滤波功能而广泛应用于母婴病房与社区诊所。为了保证测量数据的绝对可信与标准化对比，必须严格遵循四大临床准则：① 固定测量时机（如每次均在早晨喂奶前同一时间段称重）；② 必须彻底脱光婴儿衣物并取下尿布称裸重（Nude weight），一片吸饱尿液的纸尿裤重量可达 100 至 200 克以上，会造成极其严重的虚假增重误判；③ 在冰冷的金属秤盘上必须预先铺设一张洁净干燥的一次性纸巾或薄垫单，既防止婴儿皮肤受寒发生低温惊厥，又阻断交叉感染；④ 在放置衬纸后、放入婴儿之前，必须按下“清零/去皮（Tare/Zero）”键，确保起始读数绝对归零。

PROCEDURE / 临床规范操作规程

Weighing the Infant
操作规程：使用婴儿秤测量体重（防跌落手势规范）

Advocate on behalf of the client’s quality of life by promoting: Dignity • Independence • Individualized Care • Preferences • Privacy • Safety (see BOX 1.4, on page 17)
践行品质照护核心价值：尊严守护 • 自立支持 • 个性化护理 • 意愿尊重 • 隐私保护 • 安全第一

PRE-PROCEDURE / 准备阶段

  1. Wash hands. Place the digital scale on a flat, stable table away from drafts.
    洗净双手。将数字电子婴儿秤平稳安放在水平、牢固无晃动的操作台面上，避开风口。
  2. Place a clean paper towel or disposable pad on the scale tray.
    在秤盘上铺设洁净一次性衬纸防寒隔离。
  3. Turn on the scale and press the “Tare / Zero” button. Ensure the digital readout shows 0.00 kg (or 0 lb 0 oz).
    接通电源，按下“去皮/清零”键，确认显示屏精准归零（0.00 kg）。

PROCEDURE / 操作步骤

  1. Undress the baby completely. Remove the diaper.
    解开全部衣服并取下尿布，实施裸重测量。
  2. Hold the baby securely and gently place the baby on the centre of the scale tray.
    双手稳妥托住婴儿，将其轻柔平放在秤盘正中央位置。
  3. Crucial Safety Action: Hold your hands poised closely over the baby without touching the infant or scale (FIGURE 41.26). NEVER look away or step back! (Keeping hands poised allows you to instantly catch the baby if the infant rolls, kicks, or startles, while avoiding putting pressure on the scale tray which would distort the reading.)
    核心安全手势：双手呈虚抱悬空状紧贴在婴儿上方约 2 至 3 厘米处（图 41.26），手掌不接触婴儿身体亦不触碰秤盘，目不转睛紧盯婴儿！（悬空双手可在婴儿突发蹬腿侧翻的瞬间瞬间拦截接住，防止发生跌落骨折；同时双手不施力以确保称重读数精确无干扰。）
  4. Wait for the digital display to stabilize and lock in the weight reading. Read the weight in kilograms and grams (or pounds and ounces according to agency policy).
    等待读数锁定。精准读取显示屏上的千克（克）或磅数数值。
  5. Immediately lift the baby from the scale tray, wrap in a warm blanket, and dress in a clean diaper and clothes.
    读数完毕立即双手抱起婴儿，用温暖抱被裹住保暖，并重新穿好干净尿布与衣物。
  6. Clean and disinfect the scale tray according to employer policy. Discard the paper liner.
    弃置一次性衬纸，用消毒湿巾彻底擦拭秤盘，关机收纳。
  7. Practise hand hygiene. Promptly Record the weight in the client's medical record or growth chart, and Report any sudden weight change to your supervisor.
    洗手消毒。立即将体重数值记录于婴儿生长发育曲线图上，若发现异常骤增骤减第一时间向主管护士汇报。

数字式高精度婴儿电子秤。请特别注意照护人员双手悬空虚扣在婴儿上方，既严防突发翻滚坠落，又杜绝触碰秤盘造成读数失真。

📌 KEY POINTS / 本章临床核心考点精要

  • Support Worker Role in Postpartum Care: Support workers assist new birth parents and infants, predominantly in home care settings, enabling parents to rest, recover, and care for their families while adhering strictly to individual care plans and scope of practice.
    产后支持照护角色：支持人员在居家社区协助料理产妇与新生儿日常照护，支持产妇休养复原，严格遵从照护计划与执业范围边界。
  • Family Cultural Respect: Respect the family’s established routines, cultural schedules, and parenting standards whenever safe.
    尊重家庭育儿文化：在确保安全的前提下，最大程度尊重并遵循受照护家庭的生活作息与喂养习惯。
  • Postpartum Timeline: The postpartum period begins with birth and concludes 6 weeks later, encompassing massive hormonal, reproductive, and emotional adaptations.
    产褥期时间跨度：从胎儿娩出起至产后 6 周止，母体经历剧烈的生理、内分泌及心理重塑。
  • Comprehensive Care Competencies: Support workers must master 14 core areas: (1) lochia assessment, (2) perineal care, (3) abdominal incision care, (4) breast care, (5) infant holding/handling, (6) soothing/comforting, (7) breastfeeding support, (8) formula preparation, (9) bottle-feeding safety, (10) diapering protocol, (11) umbilical cord care, (12) circumcised/uncircumcised penis care, (13) bathing, and (14) infant weighing.
    母婴照护 14 项全核心能力：恶露评估、会阴冲洗、剖宫产切口护理、乳房胀痛管理、头颈部抱姿支撑、安抚技巧、母乳喂养协助、配方奶调配、奶瓶喂养安全、更换尿布、脐带残端干燥护理、男婴生殖器护理、婴儿沐浴及体重监测。
  • Rapid Escalation of Red Flags: Support workers must immediately recognize and report abnormal postpartum complications (foul lochia, hemorrhage, mastitis, severe depression/psychosis) and infant emergency signs (fever >38.0°C, extreme lethargy, respiratory distress, dehydration).
    危急征象迅捷上报：敏锐捕捉产后大出血、严重感染、产后精神病以及新生儿发热（>38.0°C）、软瘫嗜睡、呼吸窘迫等危象，立即汇报并启动急救。
