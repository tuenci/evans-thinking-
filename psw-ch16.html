REHABILITATIVE CARE VERSUS RESTORATIVE CARE

康复护理（Rehabilitative Care）与恢复性照护（Restorative Care）辨析

Injury or health challenges can affect a person's ability to lead a productive life. For example, after a stroke, some people are unable to walk or to communicate like they once could. Those clients may benefit from rehabilitation care and restorative care services.

严重的躯体创伤或重大疾病冲击，会深刻剥夺一个个体过上富有成效与自主尊严生活的能力。例如，脑卒中（中风）发作后，许多案主会瞬间丧失独立行走或清晰言语交流的机能。这部分群体将极大受益于专业的康复护理（Rehabilitation care）与恢复性照护（Restorative care）服务。

People may require rehabilitative and restorative care following an acute injury or illness, or it may be part of the treatment for a lifelong disability, chronic condition or illness, or degenerative condition. Rehabilitation refers to a broad range of interventions that address the client's medical care needs, therapeutic needs, psychosocial needs, or a combination of these needs and would be part of a client's care plan. It is delivered in homes, community-based locations, long-term care homes, and hospitals.

人们既可能在急性创伤或重症发作后需要集约化康复，康复亦是应对终身残疾、慢性进展性疾病或退行性病变长期管理的核心支柱。康复涵盖广泛的临床干预矩阵，精准回应案主的医疗需求、物理与作业治疗需求及社会心理需求。康复服务普遍开展于案主私人家居、社区日间中心、长期照护院舍及综合医院康复科病房中。

Rehabilitative care is the process of restoring a person to the highest possible level of functioning through the use of therapy, exercise, and other methods. The aim of such care is to slow the decline of functioning or support clients in maintaining or functioning at a higher capacity to maintain their quality of life and independence. For some clients, the goal is to return to work, while some others may work toward resuming the self-care needs and perform the activities of daily living (ADLs) that they were able to perform in the past.

康复护理（Rehabilitative care）是指借助专业物理疗法、针对性机能锻炼及辅助技术，促使案主恢复至最高可能生理与生活机能水平的过程。其核心宗旨在于延缓机能衰退进程，或支持案主维持在更高的机能储备状态，以最大化捍卫其生活品质与自立尊严。对于年轻伤患，终极目标往往是重返工作岗位（Return to work）；而对众多失能长者而言，目标则是重新掌握洗漱穿衣等日常生活自理活动（ADLs）。

This type of care is usually provided by nurses, physiotherapists, physiotherapy assistants, and support workers. Restorative care is also aimed at preventing health deterioration and maintaining or improving a client's quality of life, but it is also aimed at restoring the client to their previous functional level whenever possible. Whereas more health care providers are on the team for a client in rehabilitative care, restorative care usually involves only the client and family, nurses, and support workers. Therapists and other professionals may be consulted as needed.

该类照护由注册护士、物理治疗师（PT）、物理治疗助理（PTA）及支持工作者（PSW）紧密协同提供。恢复性照护（Restorative care）同样聚焦于防止健康发生继发性滑坡、维持并提升生命质量，更力求在最大限度内协助案主找回并巩固既往已恢复的自理机能。两者的核心团队配置存在清晰主从分工：康复护理通常由大型多学科医疗专家团队深度介入；而恢复性照护则高度聚焦于日常生活场景，主要由案主本人、家属、责任护士及基层支持工作者共同承担，物理治疗师等专家则按需提供顾问指导。

GOALS OF REHABILITATIVE AND RESTORATIVE CARE

康复与恢复性照护的四大核心临床医疗与自立目标

Most clients largely want to reduce their reliance on others and to become more independent. The goals of rehabilitation and restorative care for any client are based on the client's condition, and may include the following:

绝大多数服务对象内心深处都渴望摆脱对他人无休止的依赖，重获身心独立。针对任何具体个案，康复与恢复性照护方案的拟定均基于案主的病理伤情与残存机能，系统涵盖以下四大核心支柱目标：

  • • To restore function to former levels. A full restoration of function is the goal for most clients with acute or temporary conditions. For example, a healthy client with a simple leg fracture aims for full recovery.
    促使机能完全恢复至病前水平（To restore function to former levels）：对于遭遇急性暂时性创伤的大多数健康个体而言，彻底恢复伤前全部机能是最直接的目标。例如一位骨质健康的年轻长者发生单纯性下肢闭合性骨折，康复目标就是百分之百回归无痛行走与负重。
  • • To improve functional abilities. A return to former levels of functioning is not possible for some clients. Instead, they aim to improve their abilities to the best extent possible. For example, they may want to work on mobility skills or carrying out ADLs independently.
    最大化改善现有功能潜力（To improve functional abilities）：对于某些重症中风或严重截瘫案主，彻底回到病前状态在医学上已无可能；此时的目标转变为在当前解剖局限下，将残存的代偿潜能挖掘至极致。例如通过强化健侧肢体代偿，掌握轮椅独立位移或单手进食洗漱穿衣等 ADL 技能。
  • • To learn new skills. Some clients need to learn new skills to adjust to their disability or limitations. For example, Mr. Liptak's larynx was surgically removed because of cancer, and he is not able to speak anymore. His goal is to learn to communicate using, for example, sign language or electronic speech devices.
    习得全新适应性代偿技能（To learn new skills）：部分案主需要掌握前所未有的生活方式以适应终身性身体缺损。例如利普塔克先生（Mr. Liptak）因喉癌晚期接受了全喉切除手术而永久丧失发声功能，其康复核心目标就是系统研习手势手语、食管发音法或使用电子人工喉进行有效人际交流。
  • • To prevent further disability and illness. Complications that cause further disability and illness must be prevented. For example, immobility can lead to pressure ulcers or contractures, so proper skin care, exercise and activity, good alignment, and frequent repositioning are essential.
    阻断继发性二次残疾与合并症（To prevent further disability and illness）：必须动用一切临床手段掐断由制动引发的恶性循环！例如长期卧床极易并发压疮、不可逆关节挛缩与深静脉血栓，必须通过定时翻身垫枕、被动 ROM 训练与皮肤减压予以彻底防范。

Individualized Rehabilitation Goals: Five Client Profiles

个性化康复目标规划：五位不同年龄与病损案主案例分析

Consider the rehabilitation goals of the following clients across different lifespans and clinical challenges:

  • • Chantal, 17, was in a car accident, which left her with hemiplegia (paralysis of one side of the body). She needs to maintain her current range of motion (ROM), adapt to a wheelchair, and perform self-care.

  • • Mr. Egri, 68, had a stroke that resulted in hemiplegia and severe speech impairment. He needs to learn communication methods and to restore function.     埃格里先生（Mr. Egri，68 岁）：罹患急性脑卒中并发右侧偏瘫与严重运动性失语症。其核心在于重建功能性交流通道、恢复患肢肌力，并通过规律服药与抗栓管理防范脑卒中复发；全家人同步受训掌握家庭协助技能。
  • • Mrs. Boucher, 82, fractured her hip in a fall. Her goal is to remain in her own home. She needs to restore her ability to function at former levels. She also needs to take measures to prevent another fall.
    布歇太太（Mrs. Boucher，82 岁）：跌倒导致股骨颈骨折手术。她的最高人生诉求是“继续住在自己熟悉的家中安享晚年（Aging in place）”。她需要重塑步态平衡能力以找回伤前自理水准，并全面排查居家跌倒隐患。

FOCUS ON OLDER PERSONS: Rehabilitation Challenges

长者照护聚焦：高龄案主康复进程迟缓机制与节奏调控法则
Rehabilitation often takes longer for older clients than for others. Age-related changes affect healing, mobility, vision, hearing, and other functions. Older clients often have chronic health problems that slow down their recovery. They are also at risk for injuries. Because older clients do not tolerate long or fast-paced programs, their rehabilitation is usually slow.
For clients who are too weak to perform ADLs, restorative care may involve measures that promote:
• Self-care (adaptive feeding, grooming)
• Elimination (bladder/bowel retraining)
• Positioning (alignment, pressure injury prevention)
• Mobility (assisted transfers, dangling)
• Communication (hearing aid checks, speech pacing)
• Cognitive function (reality orientation, memory cues)
• Preventing unnecessary decline in function.
相较于年轻群体，高龄长者的康复周期呈现出显著的漫长与曲折。衰老带来的生理退行性衰变波及成纤维细胞增殖、骨痂愈合速率、步态力学平衡、视力敏锐度及听觉辨析力。长者普遍伴有高血压、冠心病、糖尿病等多系统慢性合并症，显著拉长机体机能修复窗口。高龄长者骨质疏松严重，极其脆弱易伤。老年心肺脏器无法承受高强度或快节奏的高压训练，因此长者的康复进程必须遵循“小步慢跑、匀速耐受、极度耐心”的基本原则。
对于极度衰弱长者，恢复性照护的重点在于保全基础生命尊严：• 基础自理；• 排泄机能重塑；• 防畸形体位管理；• 安全位移；• 言语沟通支持；• 认知机能维持；• 坚决阻断非必要的功能滑坡。

Emphasis on the Whole Person

身心社智灵：全人康复（The Whole Person）五大生命维度

Illness and disability can affect a client's physical, emotional, social, intellectual, and spiritual health, so restorative care is aimed at the whole person. It addresses all dimensions of health, not just the physical one: • Physical health: Monitored continuously; ROM exercises prevent contractures; pain management avoids opioid misuse. • Emotional and social health: Family counselling; community re-entry. • Intellectual health: Cognition, memory, speech, organization. • Spiritual health: Pastoral counselling respecting faith.

The Rehabilitation Team

多学科康复团队（Multidisciplinary Team）运作与案主核心地位

Rehabilitation requires a multidisciplinary team effort. Members of the team can vary, depending on the client's needs and the setting where the rehabilitation is being conducted. The team usually includes the client, family members, physicians, nurses, occupational therapists, physiotherapists, physiotherapy assistants, support workers, and others—who all can help the client regain function and independence. The team meets often to discuss the client's progress, and changes are made in the rehabilitation care plan, as needed.

现代康复医疗绝非任何单一专业人员能够独力完成，必须依托高度紧密协作的多学科团队（Multidisciplinary Team）。团队架构依案主伤情动态组合，其最核心成员由案主本人及家庭直系亲属奠基，吸纳神经/骨科专科医师、康复医学专家（Physiatrist）、注册护士、物理治疗师（PT）、作业治疗师（OT）、理疗助理（PTA）、言语病理学家（SLP）、心理学家、社工及一线支持工作者（PSW）。团队定期召开多学科个案研讨会（Care conferences），依功能恢复曲线动态迭代护理计划。

The client and family attend the meetings whenever possible. The importance of family involvement and support throughout the client's rehabilitation process cannot be overemphasized. All aspects of rehabilitation are interconnected. For example, following a stroke, Mr. Silva learns how to care for himself (physical health) and how to follow instructions (intellectual health), which increases his self-esteem (emotional health); family and friends visit often (social health), and his spiritual advisor visits every week (spiritual health). The rehabilitation team encourages the client to control as many aspects of care as possible—being a key member of the rehabilitation team gives the client some control.

案主与家属全程受邀列席研讨会。家庭的深度介入与情感支柱作用，构成了康复能否最终成功的生命线！全人健康的五个维度在康复中紧密互锁：例如中风案主席尔瓦先生（Mr. Silva），通过重新掌握单手穿衣自理（躯体健康）与理解康复指令（智力健康），极大重塑了前额叶尊严与自我效能感（情绪健康）；亲朋好友的频繁探视与鼓励（社交健康）以及每周神职人员的心灵陪伴祈祷（精神健康），共同汇聚成了促使神经突触重塑代偿的强大内驱力。康复团队始终鼓励案主成为自身医疗计划的最高决策者，这种自主掌控感是战胜绝望的灵魂解药。

CASE STUDY: Rehabilitation Following a Brain Injury (Jeffrey Than, 34)

实战案例研习：杰弗里·谭（Jeffrey Than，34 岁）严重创伤性脑损伤（TBI）全周期多学科康复全景纪实
Jeffrey Than, a 34-year-old high school teacher, was cycling to school when he was struck by a van. He sustained a severe blow to the cranium, traumatic brain injury (TBI), and multiple fractures. After emergency neurosurgery and orthopedic repair, he was admitted to the ICU. Because he had suffered a serious brain injury, he could not speak or recognize his family.
After 10 days in the ICU, he was moved to the acute care unit for 2 months. Eventually, Mr. Than was transferred to the hospital's specialized rehabilitation unit, and 3 months later, he was discharged home. He received home-based and community rehabilitation for the next 6 months.
During his long rehabilitation, Mr. Than's multidisciplinary rehabilitation team worked collaboratively with him and his family:

• Mr. Than and his family: Made decisions regarding rehabilitation goals; actively involved in every aspect of care; learned new skills and relearned old skills.
• Neurosurgeon & Orthopedic surgeon: Led surgical repair and managed post-surgical medical stability.
• Nurses: Coordinated and provided nursing care at every clinical stage.
• Specialist in rehabilitation medicine (Physiatrist): Led and coordinated the comprehensive rehabilitation program.
• Neuropsychologists: Evaluated cognitive processing, memory, emotions, and personality changes; planned behaviour-management therapy.

Role of the Family

家庭在康复历程中的支柱性作用与家庭辅导

Rehabilitation of clients also involves their family members, who need to learn about the illness, injury, or disability and how to care for their loved ones. Family support and involvement in the client's care can play a crucial part of the client's rehabilitation success. Care often involves helping the client practise new skills.

服务对象的康复绝非个体的孤立事件，它深深卷入并重构着整个家庭系统的运转。家庭成员必须系统研习疾病病理、创伤机制与残疾特征，掌握科学照料所爱之人的专业技能。亲属深沉的情感支柱与全程参与，往往是决定康复成败的最关键生理心理催化剂。在日常照料中，家属需要每日督促并陪伴案主反复操练新习得的代偿技能。

The family also learns new skills—for example, how to communicate with a spouse who has lost the ability to speak, how to cope with the behaviour of a teenager with a mental health disorder, or how to dress a child with physical disabilities. Counselling is provided, as needed, to help the family cope with changes affecting them. Families dealing with the rehabilitation of a member with a chronic health problem will need constant support from team members because the challenges to a normal family life can be overwhelming, and family members need to know that the team is listening to their concerns.

家属自身亦必须攻克前所未有的相处技巧——例如学习如何利用手势图卡与罹患失语症的伴侣展开有效交流、如何理性应对出现冲动行为障碍的青春期精神创伤患儿、或如何以符合人体工效学的体位为脑瘫残疾儿童穿戴衣裤。多学科团队按需向家属提供危机心理疏导。长期照料慢性严重伤残亲属对传统家庭节奏构成了毁灭性重创与极度疲劳，家属极需来自康复团队的持续性赋能与喘息倾听，让家属真切感受到自身的焦虑与呼声始终被团队庄严重视。

Therapy and Training

功能疗法、技能重塑与适应性辅具训练

The professionals on the rehabilitation team choose the therapy and training needed to meet the client's goals. The client is taught how to improve a skill or how to perform a task and practises what was learned either independently or with a caregiver or a family member.

多学科康复团队中的注册专家根据案主的个性化目标，开具精准的功能疗法与动作重构处方。案主在专业指导下学习如何改善特定机能或完成复杂的 ADL 自理动作，并在随后的日常生活中由支持工作者或亲属陪伴反复强化巩固。

助行与位移装备系统涵盖拐杖、手杖、助行架、下肢矫形支具以及高定制轮椅（详见第 15 章）。对于双下肢截瘫或严重无力而上肢残存推撑肌力的轮椅案主，必须系统研习移位滑板（Transfer board，图 16.4）的过桥转移技术！移位板是一块表面光滑平整的坚硬板桥，两端分别搭扣于轮椅座垫与目标平面（如病床、马桶坐圈、汽车座椅或浴缸凳）之间。案主无需双下肢承重立起，只需依靠双臂支撑和臀部水平顺滑挪移，即可独立在不同座椅之间实现无缝平行位移。

PROVIDING COMPASSIONATE CARE: Assisting With Rehabilitation and Restorative Care

专栏：以同理心提供慈爱康复照护——DIPPS 人文关怀六大维度的临床深度践行
• Dignity: Treat clients with unconditional positive respect. Clients do not need pity; they need encouragement, positive reinforcement, and celebration of small gains. Never show impatience when explanations yield slow results. Having little control over one's body is deeply agonizing. Acknowledge the six classic stages of grief and adaptation to disability: Shock, Denial, Anger, Depression, Reaction against independence, and Acceptance. Family and client may not progress through these stages at the same pace.

• Independence: Independence is the ultimate goal. Encourage clients to perform ADLs to the absolute limit of their ability. Never rush them! Allow ample time to complete each task. Master their self-help assistive devices and reinforce techniques taught by the therapy team.

• Individualized care: Tailor care strictly to the client's physical, cognitive, and psychosocial strengths and limitations. Adapt interventions to personal routines.

• Preferences: Honor personal choices. Freedom of choice restores a sense of internal control.

• Privacy: Many clients feel profound shame or embarrassment practicing basic skills (eating, walking, dressing) in front of others. Provide absolute visual and spatial privacy during skill practice, care procedures, and elimination. Maintain strict confidentiality.

• Safety: Illness, muscle weakness, and mental fatigue dramatically elevate accident risks. Never push clients beyond safe physical limits; schedule mandatory rest intervals. Maintain body alignment and safe transfer mechanics. Crucial safety mandate in facility settings: Ensure that the emergency call bell and overbed table are positioned on the client's UNAFFECTED (strong) side at all times!
• 尊严（Dignity）：给予案主无条件的尊重与接纳。失能案主需要的绝不是同情或可怜，而是发自内心的尊重、持续的肯定与对微小机能突破的热情赞美！当长者因认知受损无法理解指令时，严禁流露出半点焦躁或轻视！体会案主面对躯体失控时的深层绝望。深刻洞察案主及家属在遭遇残疾打击时必然经历的心理防御六大阶段：
1. 震惊麻木（Shock）；2. 否认现实（Denial）；3. 暴躁愤怒（Anger）；4. 抑郁消沉（Depression）；5. 反抗自立（Reaction against independence，消极依赖退行）；6. 最终接纳与重塑（Acceptance）。案主与家属往往处于不同步的心理周期中，需给予差异化同理陪伴。

• 独立性（Independence）：独立是康复的终极灵魂。鼓励案主在安全前提下自主完成个人自理活动（ADLs）。严禁催促抢步！留出充足时间让案主自行穿衣扣纽。熟练掌握案主专用的自理辅助用具，与治疗师保持步调一致。

• 个性化照护（Individualized Care）：依据案主的认知储备、残余肌力、心理韧性及既往起居习惯制定照护节奏，彻底摒弃“一刀切”的机械化护理。

• 个人偏好（Preferences）：充分尊重个人习惯与自决权，哪怕是极其微小的日程自主权，也能重新点燃长者对生命掌控的自信火种。

OVERALL BENEFITS OF REHABILITATION AND RESTORATIVE CARE

康复与恢复性照护对长者家庭及加拿大医疗体系的综合长远效益

Rehabilitation and restorative care can be very beneficial to clients and their families. These benefits include slowing a client's decline of functioning, maintaining or improving functioning, restoring the client to the previous functional level as much as possible, and preserving the client's quality of life and independence. However, there are a number of systemic socioeconomic benefits that rehabilitation and restorative care provide to Canadians and the healthcare system:

系统性的康复护理与恢复性照护为服务对象及其整个家庭带来了深远福祉：有效遏制全身机能发生雪崩式衰退、保全并挖掘残存代偿潜力、协助案主最大化找回病前独立机能，并持久捍卫其晚年生活品质与生命尊严。更具深远意义的是，康复与恢复性照护对整个加拿大社会经济与公共医疗体系（Medicare）展现了不可估量的系统性红利：

  • • Shorter or no hospital stays for clients: Rather than staying in an acute-care hospital for weeks or months, clients can transition home or to short-stay facilities in their communities. Respite programs provide temporary relief to family caregivers.
    显著缩短或消除住院周期：使案主无需在急性三甲医院中滞留数周乃至数月，而是迅速回归其原生社区或短期过渡机构。在加拿大各省，赋能自立计划（Reablement programs，重赋权方案）已成为公共卫生的前沿典范——专门面向那些已度过急性危重期、但出院后暂时无法独自料理生活或在居家环境中挣扎恢复体能的脆弱长者，通过多学科密集赋能服务使其能够继续长久居住在自己家中安享晚年。喘息照护计划（Respite programs）则为身心俱疲的家庭全职照料者提供临时替代托管照护，防范家庭系统崩溃。
  • • Telehealth in remote and northern regions: Health care providers provide remote clinical instructions and biomechanical assessments through videoconferencing, allowing clients in rural and northern communities to remain in their homes.
    边远原住民与北部地区远程医疗覆盖：在加拿大辽阔的北部极寒与偏远农村社区，高级治疗师利用高清视频会议远程医疗（Telehealth）开展动作评估与居家训练督导，彻底免除了长者跨越数千公里长途奔波就医的艰辛。
  • • Preserving true independence: Clients who live at home maintain functional autonomy and familiarity far superior to long-term institutionalization.
    最大化保全生命独立尊严：身处熟悉的家庭生活环境中，案主保留着高度的日常生活自决权，其神经突触重塑与生活机能恢复水准显著优于长期被动圈养在养老机构。
  • • Less disruption for families: Discharging clients closer to home enables families to visit frequently without draining financial resources on long-distance travel and lodging.
    极大降低对正常家庭生活的破坏：使长者重回家庭亲情环抱，子女亲属无需耗费巨额差旅住宿成本跨城市陪护探视，维系了健康稳定的家庭经济结构。
  • • Massive taxpayer cost savings: Acute hospital beds are extraordinarily expensive to taxpayers. Delivering high-quality restorative care in outpatient and home settings saves millions of healthcare dollars, allowing funds to be reallocated to other critical healthcare priorities.
    为公共财政节约天文数字般的医疗开支：在公费医疗体制下，急性综合医院病床的每日综合开销对纳税人而言是极其高昂的负担。将康复重心下沉至社区居家与日间门诊，能够为公共医疗基金节约数以亿计的宝贵财政资金，促使医疗资源重组再配置于急危重症等最核心领域。

  • • Rehabilitation care is the use of therapy, exercise, and other methods to restore a person to the highest possible level of functioning. It may address acute injuries (short-term) or chronic conditions (long-term), maintaining function or slowing functional decline.
    康复护理是通过物理治疗、针对性功能锻炼及辅助手段，促使案主恢复至最高可能生理机能的医疗过程。它既适用于急性外伤后的短期功能重建，也贯穿于慢性退行性疾病的长期管理中，起到延缓衰退、保留生活品质的核心作用。
  • • Restorative care is aimed at preventing health deterioration, maintaining or improving quality of life, and restoring previous functional levels as much as possible. It is primarily provided by nurses and support workers in daily care.
    恢复性照护旨在防止机体机能发生继发性恶化，维持并增进生命品质，并在最大限度内协助案主找回自主独立。这项照护主要由护士和支持工作者在日常饮食、洗漱、入厕及活动等基础护理场景中无缝开展。
  • • Every client has an individualized care plan with unique goals and multidisciplinary team members. Support workers have the most ongoing contact with clients, making their input at conferences and reports vital.
    每位服务对象都拥有高度定制化的个性化护理计划与康复目标。由于支持工作者在日常生活中与案主保持着最持久、最频繁的贴身接触，工作者对案主微小机能变化和情绪波动的观察汇报，构成了团队个案研讨会（Care Conferences）的最关键决策依据。
  • • Therapy and rehabilitation are essential to help clients reach their personal goal of independence. The care plan establishes the support worker's exact role in each step of functional training.
    系统性的功能疗法与适应性训练是助力案主迈向自立尊严的必由之路。护理计划严格界定了支持工作者在各个康复阶段中的协助边界与实操规范。
  • • Family members are vital members of the rehabilitation team. They must learn about the condition, acquire new caregiving skills, and receive continuous emotional and respite support from the healthcare team.
    家庭成员是康复团队不可或缺的基石核心。家属需要深入理解疾病病理，学习全新的沟通与协助技巧，并应当从医疗团队处持续获得心理疏导与喘息照护支持。
  • • Psychosocial elements deeply affect clients' reactions to their conditions. Emotional stages such as shock, denial, anger, depression, and gradual acceptance must be met with patience, compassion, and non-judgmental listening.
    社会心理因素深刻影响着案主对自身残疾的心理适应。从最初的震惊、否认、愤怒，到抑郁消沉乃至最终接纳，支持工作者必须以极大的同理心、深度的包容与倾听去陪伴案主走过心理阴霾。
