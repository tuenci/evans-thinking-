CHAPTER 29: Urinary Elimination

第 29 章：排尿照护与留置导尿管

Support workers assist clients with urinary elimination, promoting dignity, comfort, independence, and strict infection control.

支持人员在协助案主进行排尿排泄照护时，全力维护案主尊严、舒适感与自立能力，并严格执行院感防控与无菌原则。

OBJECTIVES / 本章核心学习目标

After reading this chapter, the learner should be able to do the following: / 研读本章后，学员应当能够全面掌握并达成以下专业执业技能：

  • • Identify the characteristics of normal urine.
    识别正常尿液的颜色、透明度、气味、比重及正常生理排尿量特征。
  • • Describe the guidelines for maintaining normal urinary elimination.
    阐述促进与维持正常泌尿排泄的护理指导原则（包括排尿习惯、饮水量及隐私照护）。
  • • List the observations to make about urine.
    列举在协助排尿过程中对尿液性状与排尿异常应当观察的关键临床特征。
  • • Describe urinary incontinence and the care required.
    描述尿失禁（Urinary Incontinence）的病理类型、身心影响及皮肤防护等专科照护要点。
  • • Explain why catheters are used.
    解释临床留置导尿管的医疗适应症与潜在感染高危风险。
  • • Explain the differences between straight, in-dwelling, suprapubic, and condom catheters.
    阐明单次导尿管、留置导尿管（Foley）、耻骨上膀胱穿刺造口管及安全套导尿管（Condom catheter）的区别与适用场景。
  • • Describe the guidelines for caring for clients with in-dwelling catheters.
    详述照护留置导尿管案主的规范指导准则（防逆流、防牵拉、维持封闭系统与无菌操作）。
  • • Describe the methods of bladder training.
    描述膀胱再训练（Bladder Training）的常用训练方案与促进自主排尿神经反射恢复的临床技巧。
  • • Describe the guidelines for collecting urine specimens.
    掌握常规随机尿标本、中段清洁无菌尿标本、24小时连续尿标本及滤尿查结石的规范采集流程。
  • • Explain how to care for a client with a ureterostomy.
    阐述接受输尿管造口术（Ureterostomy）或回肠膀胱术（Ileal conduit）案主的造口底盘及集尿袋更换照护规程。
  • • Apply the procedures described in this chapter to clinical practice properly.
    在临床实操中规范、严谨且富于同理心（DIPPS）地执行本章各项泌尿照护实操规程。

Eliminating Waste and Infection Prevention

机体排泄机制与感染预防原则
Eliminating waste is one of the important functions of the body. The body’s waste products are removed through various body systems—digestive, respiratory, integumentary, and urinary. The digestive system gets rid of solid wastes from the body; the lungs get rid of carbon dioxide; the skin sweats, thus getting rid of water and other substances; and the urinary system removes waste products from blood (which come from body cells after breaking down and metabolizing food for energy) and maintains the body’s water balance (see Chapter 32 to review the urinary system).
排泄代谢废物是人体至关重要的生理功能之一。人体的废物通过多个系统排出体外——消化系统、呼吸系统、皮肤外被系统以及泌尿系统。消化系统排出固体粪便；肺部排出二氧化碳；皮肤通过出汗排出水分及其他物质；而泌尿系统则清除血液中的代谢废物（这些废物源于细胞分解代谢食物以获取能量的生物化学过程），并维持机体的水盐与体液平衡（详见第 32 章泌尿系统解剖生理学回顾）。
When assisting a client with elimination, you may be exposed to urine or stool (feces) directly or indirectly—for example, on linen and clothing that are soiled with these bodily fluids. Protect yourself and your client by following the rules of medical asepsis and Standard Practices (see Chapter 18). Follow the infection control precautions in BOX 29.1 when assisting the client with elimination.
在协助案主进行排泄照护时，你可能会直接或间接接触到尿液或粪便——例如接触被排泄物污染的床单织物与衣物。请务必严格遵守医疗无菌规程（Medical Asepsis）与标准预防措施（Standard Practices，见第 18 章），以保护你自己和案主的安全。在协助案主排泄时，必须严格遵循专栏 29.1 中详述的感染防控预防措施。

Stress incontinence /stres ˌɪnˈkɒn.tɪ.nəns/ 压力性尿失禁 p. 648

压力性尿失禁：在腹腔压力突发增高时（如咳嗽、打喷嚏、大笑、跑步或提重物）发生的尿液不自主流出。
Suprapubic catheter /ˌsuː.prəˈpjuː.bɪk ˈkæθ.ə.tər/ 耻骨上膀胱造口导管 p. 650

耻骨上膀胱造口导管：通过下腹部耻骨上方腹壁穿刺或切口直接置入膀胱腔内的长期留置导管。
Ureterostomy /jʊˌriː.tərˈɒs.tə.mi/ 输尿管造口术 p. 673

输尿管造口术：外科手术将一侧或双侧输尿管直接移位引出并缝合固定于腹壁体表的人造造口。
Urge incontinence /ɜːdʒ ˌɪnˈkɒn.tɪ.nəns/ 急迫性尿失禁 p. 648
The loss of urine in response to a sudden, urgent need to void.
急迫性尿失禁：伴随强烈、突发且无法用意念克制的尿急感，无法及时抵达厕所便迅速发生的尿液不自主流出。
Urinary frequency /ˈjʊə.rɪ.nər.i ˈfriː.kwən.si/ 尿频 p. 640
The need to urinate at frequent intervals.
尿频：单位时间内排尿次数异常增多（白天排尿超过6–8次），但每次排尿量通常较少。
Urinary incontinence /ˈjʊə.rɪ.nər.i ˌɪnˈkɒn.tɪ.nəns/ 尿失禁 p. 648
The inability to control the passage of urine from the bladder; the loss of bladder control.
尿失禁：膀胱括约肌或神经控制功能受损，导致无法自主用意念控制尿液排出的病理状态。
Urinary output /ˈjʊə.rɪ.nər.i ˈaʊt.pʊt/ 尿量 p. 638
The amount of urine a person excretes. Depending on the care plan, the client’s urinary output is measured and recorded after every hour, at the end of each shift, at the end of each day, or not at all.
尿量：人体在特定时间内排出的尿液总毫升数。依据护理计划医嘱，可逐小时记录、按班次统计或计算24小时全天总量。
Urinary urgency /ˈjʊə.rɪ.nər.i ˈɜː.dʒən.si/ 尿急 p. 640
The need to void immediately.
尿急：一种突发、不可延误且迫切必须立即排尿的主观冲动与紧迫感。
Urination /ˌjʊə.rɪˈneɪ.ʃən/ 排尿 p. 638
The process of emptying urine from the bladder. Also known as micturition or voiding.
排尿：将储存在膀胱内的尿液经尿道排出体外的生理排空过程。亦称排尿反射（Micturition）或排空（Voiding）。

See urination.
排尿：参见排尿（Urination）。

Normal Urination

正常排尿生理与特征
Urinary output refers to the amount of urine a person excretes. Depending on the care plan, the client’s urinary output is measured and recorded after every hour, at the end of each shift, at the end of each day, or not at all. The healthy adult excretes about 1 500 mL (millilitres) of urine a day. Factors such as age, disease, the amount and kinds of fluid ingested, dietary salt, and medications affect urine production. Some substances such as coffee, tea, alcohol, and some medications increase urine production. A diet high in salt causes the body to retain water; when water is retained, less urine is produced. Dehydration is a decrease in the amount of water in body tissues, which occurs when fluid output exceeds intake (see Chapter 33). Common causes are low fluid intake, vomiting, diarrhea, bleeding, excess sweating, and increased urine production (see Chapter 28).
尿量（Urinary output）是指一个人所排出的尿液总量。根据护理计划的要求，案主的尿量可能会每小时测量记录一次、在每班交接时记录、在全天结束时记录，或者完全不需要测量记录。健康成年人每天排出的尿液约为 1 500 mL（毫升）。年龄、疾病、摄入液体的数量与种类、膳食中的盐分以及药物等因素均会影响尿液的生成量。某些物质如咖啡、茶、酒精和某些药物会增加尿液生成。高盐饮食会导致机体水钠潴留；当水分被滞留在体内时，尿液生成量就会减少。脱水（Dehydration）是指机体组织中水分含量的减少，当体液排出量超过摄入量时即可发生脱水（见第 33 章）。常见病因包括液体摄入不足、呕吐、腹泻、出血、大量出汗以及尿量病理性增多（见第 28 章）。
Urination, micturition, and voiding all mean the process of emptying urine from the bladder. The amount of fluid intake, personal habits, and available toilet facilities, as well as activity, work, and illness, affect frequency of urination. People usually void at bedtime, after getting up, and before meals. Some people void every 2 to 3 hours, and some others void less frequently. The need to void at night causes sleep disturbance in many people. As a support worker, you should be aware that although many of the clients in facilities are incontinent of urine, incontinence is not a normal part of aging.
排尿（Urination）、排尿反射（micturition）以及排空膀胱（voiding）均指将尿液从膀胱中排出的生理过程。液体摄入量、个人生活习惯、可用的如厕设施，以及案主的活动水平、工作性质与疾病状态，都会影响排尿的频率。人们通常在就寝前、晨起后以及餐前排尿。部分人每 2 至 3 小时排尿一次，而另一些人的排尿频率较低。夜间排尿需求常会导致许多案主睡眠中断。作为支持人员，你必须明确认识到：虽然照护机构中的许多案主存在尿失禁，但尿失禁绝不是人体正常衰老的必然结果。

• Practise medical asepsis and follow Standard Practices.

• 当案主提出如厕请求时，立即协助前往洗手间，或及时递送便椅、便盆或尿壶。排尿需求可能十分紧迫，必须予以充分理解与快速响应。
• Help the client assume a normal position for voiding, if possible. Women sit or squat, and most men stand.
• 尽可能协助案主采取符合生理习惯的正常排尿体位。女性通常采取坐位或蹲位，多数男性则习惯站立位排尿。
• For the client’s comfort, warm the bedpan or urinal under warm water.

• 确保严密隐私。拉严床周隔帘，关好卧室门与洗手间门，拉下窗帘或百叶窗。若案主身体状况允许独处，应礼貌离开房间。

• Remain nearby if the client is weak or unsteady.

• Place the call bell (in facilities) and toilet tissue within reach.

BOX 29.3 Common Causes of Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs) & Prevention · 专栏 29.3 尿路感染（UTI）常见病因机制与预防指南
The urinary system is structured to prevent infections, but occasionally (or frequently in some people), people do get UTIs. UTIs can occur when bacteria enter the urinary tract through the urethra, the body’s urine portal to the outside.
One type of UTI, specifically infection of the bladder (cystitis), is more common in women than men because the distance between the urethral opening to the anus is shorter in women, as is the distance from the urethral opening to the bladder.
Another type of UTI, infection of the urethra (urethritis), can occur when bacteria from the gastrointestinal system and anus are spread to the urethra, usually through sexual practices. Clients of all genders can get infections of the urethra.
In older persons, UTIs can be caused by inadequate emptying of the bladder, sometimes because of blockages in the urinary tract, neurological causes (such as stroke), certain medications, or the presence of a urinary drainage catheter that has introduced hostile bacteria into the bladder.
人体泌尿系统的解剖结构本身具备防感染机制，但细菌仍可能侵入引发尿路感染（UTI）。当外界细菌通过尿道外口逆行侵入尿路时即可发病。
第一种常见的 UTI 是膀胱炎（Cystitis），女性的发生率显著高于男性，因为女性尿道较短，且尿道外口与肛门的距离显著短于男性，细菌极易从会阴部逆行上行进入膀胱。
第二种 UTI 是尿道炎（Urethritis），通常由胃肠道与肛周的细菌蔓延播散至尿道引起，常发生于不洁性行为。任何性别的案主均可罹患尿道炎。
在老年人群中，UTI 常由膀胱排空不全引起，诱因包括尿路机械性梗阻、神经系统损伤（如脑卒中偏瘫）、特定药物反应，或者因留置导尿管直接将致病菌带入膀胱造成导管相关性尿路感染（CAUTI）。
Ways to Prevent UTIs:
For all people:
1. Ensure proper hydration. Water will keep urine diluted. Also drink antioxidant drinks such as cranberry or blueberry juice.
2. Empty the bladder at regular intervals.
3. Provide good perineal care after each voiding and especially after each bowel movement.
4. Follow agency or facility guidelines for care of catheters.
5. Avoid unnecessary urinary catheterizations.
For clients with female genitalia:
6. Wipe from front to back.
7. Urinate soon after vaginal intercourse to clear out bacteria that may have entered the urethra.
8. Do not use douches, sprays, or powders that can alter the normal flora in the vaginal area.
9. Avoid bubble baths, bath oils, and other perfumed products that can irritate the urethra.
10. Wear breathable fabrics and loose-fitting clothing that allow the vaginal area to stay dry.
预防尿路感染的核心策略：
全人群通用预防准则：
1. 确保充沛的水分摄入。充足饮水使尿液保持稀释并冲刷尿道。饮用蔓越莓汁或蓝莓汁等富含抗氧化物质的原汁亦有辅助保护作用。
2. 养成规律排空膀胱的习惯，切勿长时间憋尿。
3. 每次排尿后、尤其是每次排便后，提供彻底的会阴冲洗与清洁照护。
4. 严格遵守机构关于导尿管护理的无菌与维护指南。
5. 避免一切非必要的侵入性导尿操作。
女性解剖特征专用预防要点：
6. 便后清洁必须严格坚持“由前向后”（由尿道口向肛门方向）单向擦拭。
7. 阴道性生活后应尽快排尿，以及时冲出可能被推入尿道的细菌。
8. 切勿使用阴道灌洗剂、香精喷雾或爽身粉，以免破坏会阴阴道正常有益菌群平衡。
9. 避免使用泡泡浴、沐浴油及其他含人工香料的洗护用品，防止化学刺激尿道黏膜。
10. 穿着透气纯棉内裤与宽松衣裤，保持会阴部干爽透气。

Urinary Incontinence

尿失禁的分型与病理机制
Urinary incontinence—the loss of bladder control—may be temporary or permanent. The different types of incontinence are as follows: • Stress incontinence—the leaking of urine during exercise and certain movements. Urine loss is small (less than 50 mL). Often called dribbling, it occurs with laughing, sneezing, coughing, lifting, or other activities. Late-in-life pregnancy and obesity are other causes. The condition is common in individuals who have given birth, as pelvic muscles weaken due to pregnancies and aging. This is known as pelvic organ prolapse (POP) (see Chapter 33). • Urge incontinence—the loss of urine in response to a sudden, urgent need to void. The client cannot get to a toilet in time. Urinary frequency, urinary urgency, and nighttime voidings are common. Causes include UTIs, nervous system disorders, bladder cancer, and an enlarged prostate. • Overflow incontinence—the leaking of urine when the bladder is too full. The client feels as though the bladder is never completely empty and experiences only dribbling or a weak urine stream. Diabetes, enlarged prostate, and some medications are causes. • Functional incontinence—the loss of urine that occurs when the client has bladder control but cannot get to the toilet in time. Immobility, restraints, unanswered calls for help, lack of a call bell within reach, confusion, disorientation, difficulty removing clothing, and not knowing where to find the bathroom are all causes of functional incontinence. • Reflex incontinence—the loss of urine at predictable intervals. Urine is lost whenever the bladder becomes full, even when the client does not feel the need to void. Nervous system disorders and injuries are common causes. Sometimes incontinence results from surgery to the intestinal, rectal, or reproductive system. The presence of more than one type of incontinence is called mixed incontinence.
尿失禁（Urinary incontinence）——指膀胱括约肌控制排尿能力的丧失——可以是暂时性的，也可以是永久性的。临床主要分型如下： • 压力性尿失禁（Stress incontinence）——在剧烈运动或特定增加腹压的身体动作时发生漏尿。漏尿量通常较小（少于 50 mL），常被称为“点滴漏尿（dribbling）”，多在咳嗽、打喷嚏、大笑、提重物或下蹲时诱发。高龄妊娠和肥胖是其他诱因。经阴道分娩史的女性中尤为多见，因妊娠分娩与衰老导致盆底肌肉群松弛无力，常合并盆腔器官脱垂（POP，见第 33 章）。 • 急迫性尿失禁（Urge incontinence）——在产生突发强烈、不可遏制的排尿冲动时发生的尿液不自主流出。案主往往来不及走到马桶前便已失禁。常伴有尿频、尿急与夜尿频繁。诱因包括尿路感染（UTI）、中枢神经系统疾患、膀胱癌及前列腺增生肥大。 • 充溢性尿失禁（Overflow incontinence）——当膀胱过度充盈膨胀时尿液被动溢出。案主自觉膀胱永远无法完全排空，表现为持续点滴漏尿或尿线极其微弱无力。糖尿病神经病变、良性前列腺增生导致尿道梗阻及某些抗胆碱能药物是主要病因。 • 功能性尿失禁（Functional incontinence）——案主的膀胱自主控制功能本身完好，但由于身体活动受限或外部环境障碍而无法及时到达卫生间如厕导致的失禁。身体行动瘫痪、不当肢体约束、按铃求助无人响应、呼叫铃未放在伸手可及处、认知障碍定向力缺失、解扣脱衣困难或无法辨别卫生间位置均属功能性诱因。 • 反射性尿失禁（Reflex incontinence）——在可预测的时间间隔内发生的不自主漏尿。每当膀胱内尿液积蓄达到特定容量阈值时便会自动排空，而案主在此过程中毫无便意冲动。常见于脊髓损伤截瘫或神经传导通路受损的案主。 部分尿失禁由肠道、直肠或盆腔生殖外科手术损伤神经括约肌所致。同时合并存在上述两种或两种以上特征的失禁称为混合性尿失禁（Mixed incontinence）。

Challenges Related to Urinary Incontinence

尿失禁引发的多维身心困境与挑战
Incontinence is embarrassing for the client. Clothing and linens get wet, odours develop, and the client is usually cold and uncomfortable. The client can develop skin irritation, skin breakdown, and infection. The urge to urinate can be so strong that some clients may rush to the washroom and sustain injury by slipping or falling on the way. As a support worker you can minimize incontinence by ensuring the client has the opportunity to urinate regularly—for example, before and after each meal, before and after activities, and before going to sleep and upon wakening. Incontinence can affect clients’ pride, dignity, and self-esteem and cause them to avoid participating in activities, going out, or visiting with others, out of fear of being incontinent in public. These clients need your support, understanding, and compassion.
尿失禁对于案主而言往往伴随着极大的羞耻感与挫败感。衣物与被褥被尿液浸湿，弥漫难闻异味，案主常感到阴冷潮湿与身心极度不适。潮湿浸渍极易诱发局部皮肤发红发炎、表皮糜烂破损甚至继发严重真菌细菌感染（失禁性皮炎 IAD）。强烈的排尿紧迫感常促使部分案主匆忙慌张冲向洗手间，极易在途中滑倒跌伤骨折。 作为支持人员，你可以通过主动协助案主建立规律的如厕习惯来最大程度减少失禁的发生——例如，每餐前后、各项文娱活动前后、就寝前以及晨起清醒后立即协助如厕。尿失禁会重创案主的尊严、自尊心与社交信心，因害怕在公共场合失禁出丑而抗拒参加集体活动、拒绝外出或走亲访友，导致严重的社交隔离与抑郁。这些案主极度需要你专业、深切的同理心、尊重与慈爱关怀。

Stress for the Caregiver

照护者的心理压力管理与防范虐待忽视
Caring for people with incontinence can be stressful. Some family members who are caregivers cannot cope with difficulties associated with incontinence and often seek long-term care for the client. As a professional caregiver, even you may find caring for these clients stressful, as they may need frequent care that includes changing wet garments and linens and giving skin care. Do not lose patience. The client’s needs are great, and your role is to meet these needs. If you find yourself becoming short-tempered and impatient, discuss it with your supervisor immediately. Remember, the client has the right to be free from abuse, mistreatment, and neglect. The incontinence is beyond the client’s control and is not something they choose to let happen. Showing kindness, empathy, understanding, and patience is very important.
照护患有大小便失禁的案主可能会承受巨大的身心重负。许多承担照护职责的家属因无法应对失禁带来的无休止清理而心力交瘁，这常常成为将案主送入长期照护机构的主要原因。作为专业支持人员，面对需要频繁更换湿冷衣被、反复进行皮肤冲洗擦拭的案主，有时你也会感到劳累与压力。但切记绝不可丧失耐心！案主的痛苦与护理需求是极其沉重的，而你的崇高执业使命正是满足这些需求。若你觉察到自己情绪烦躁、缺乏耐心，必须立即向主管护士寻求支持与心理疏导。请时刻牢记：案主拥有不受任何形式虐待、苛待与忽视的法定人权！失禁是机体病理退化导致的不可自控的痛苦，绝不是案主故意制造的麻烦。展现仁慈、同理、宽容与无比的耐心，是顶级照护者的最高品格。

Catheters

导尿管临床分类与生理机制
A catheter is a tube (plastic, latex, or other material) used to drain or inject fluid through a body opening. A urinary catheter is a tube that is inserted into the bladder to drain urine. Catheter insertion (catheterization) is done by a nurse or physician or even by the client if the client learns how to do it. Occasionally, a physician might order a client to be catheterized after the client has voided, to determine how much urine is left in the bladder. A client who was catheterized for 300 mL but voided only 200 mL would need medical treatment for inadequate emptying of the bladder and urinary retention. • A straight catheter is a tube that drains the bladder and is removed after one use. It is inserted through the urethra into the bladder. • An in-dwelling catheter (also called a retention or Foley catheter) is a tube (plastic, latex, or other material) that is inserted into the bladder through the urinary meatus and urethra (FIGURE 29.9). A balloon within the catheter is inflated after insertion, preventing the catheter from slipping out of the bladder (FIGURE 29.10). Tubing connects the catheter to the drainage bag. • A suprapubic catheter is a catheter that is surgically inserted through the abdomen, above the pubic bone, into the bladder (FIGURE 29.11).
导管（Catheter）是一根通过人体天然孔道或手术造口置入体内用于引流体液或注入药液的软管（材质包括医用级聚氯乙烯塑料、天然乳胶或硅胶等）。导尿管（Urinary catheter）是特指经尿道插入膀胱用于引流尿液的管道。导尿术（Catheterization）必须由执业护士或医师严格执行无菌插管操作，某些掌握清洁间歇导尿技术的案主亦可在指导下自行插管。 在临床上，医生有时会开具医嘱要求案主在自主排尿后立即接受导尿插管，以精确测量膀胱内的残余尿量（Residual urine）。若案主自主排尿仅排出了 200 mL，但随后插管导出了多达 300 mL 的残余尿，则表明案主存在严重的膀胱排空不全与尿潴留（Urinary retention），必须立即接受专科医疗干预。 • 单次导尿管（Straight catheter）——是一种用于临时排空膀胱的直头软管，在尿液完全引流完毕后即刻拔除，一次性使用。 • 留置导尿管（In-dwelling catheter——亦称 Foley 导尿管或气囊滞留导尿管 retention catheter）——是通过尿道外口插入膀胱的导管（图 29.9）。导管头端内部设有一个微型气囊，置入膀胱腔后注入无菌注射用水将气囊充盈胀大，从而牢固卡在膀胱颈内口，防止导管滑脱出尿道（图 29.10）。引流接头与体外的封闭式集尿袋相连。 • 耻骨上导尿管（Suprapubic catheter）——是通过下腹壁下部（耻骨联合上方）经外科手术造瘘切口直接穿刺刺入膀胱腔内的长期留置导管（图 29.11）。

A suprapubic catheter is a catheter that is surgically inserted through the abdomen, above the pubic bone, into the bladder (FIGURE 29.11). It is a type of in-dwelling catheter; that is, it is left in the bladder and is attached to a drainage bag with tubing. Suprapubic catheters may be used in clients needing long-term catheterization or after certain types of surgeries. The insertion site (the opening on the abdomen) and the tubing must be cleaned daily with soap and water and covered with dry gauze. To do this, follow the care plan and your assignment sheet.
耻骨上导尿管（Suprapubic catheter）是通过下腹壁（耻骨联合上方）经外科手术造口直接置入膀胱腔内的管道（图 29.11）。它属于留置导尿管的一种，即长期滞留在膀胱内，并通过引流管连接至集尿袋。耻骨上导尿管通常用于需要终身或长期导尿的案主（如高位截瘫），或在特定复杂的泌尿与盆腔生殖外科手术后使用。腹壁穿刺造瘘口及导管管路必须每日使用温水和温和肥皂彻底清洗消毒，并覆盖无菌干燥医用纱布。执行该项照护时，必须严格遵照护理计划与任务分工指派单的明确要求。

Purposes of Catheters

导尿管的临床适应证与置管治疗目的
• Catheters often are used before, during, and after surgery to keep the bladder empty. Catheterization reduces the risk of accidental bladder injury during surgery, and after surgery, it prevents the pressure on nearby organs that a full bladder could cause. • Catheters allow hourly urinary output measurements in clients with critical illnesses. • Catheters are the last resort for managing incontinence, since they do not treat the cause of incontinence, and the risk of infection is high. However, some clients have wounds and pressure sores that must be protected from contamination by urine. • Clients who are extremely weak or have disabilities and are unable to use the bedpan, commode, or toilet—for example, those who are dying—can be made more comfortable by having incontinence managed by a catheter. • Catheters have diagnostic uses, such as collecting sterile urine specimens and measuring residual urine. For such purposes, the catheter is inserted after the client has voided. As a support worker, you will be required to care for clients with in-dwelling catheters. Follow the guidelines listed in the Think About Safety: Guidelines for Caring for Clients With In-Dwelling Catheters box, on page 654, to promote comfort and safety.

Drainage Systems

封闭式尿液引流系统的结构与院感防线
A closed drainage system is used for in-dwelling catheters (including suprapubic catheters) so that nothing enters the system from the catheter to the drainage bag. The urinary system must remain sterile; otherwise, infection can occur when microbes enter the drainage system and travel up the tubing or catheter into the bladder and kidneys. A UTI can threaten the client’s health and even life. The drainage system consists of tubing and a drainage bag (FIGURE 29.20). Tubing attaches to the catheter at one end and the drainage bag at the other end. The bag must not touch the floor. Clients may feel embarrassed if the drainage bag can be seen by visitors. Carefully drape a bed sheet or robe over the drainage bag when visitors are present. Some clients wear leg bags when not in bed. The bag is attached to the thigh or calf (FIGURE 29.21), and the pant leg or robe covers it. As stated earlier, it is important to never hang the drainage bag on a bed rail because when the bed rail is raised, the bag is at a higher level than the bladder. When the client walks, the bag must remain at a lower level than the bladder. Measuring Urine Drainage The urine drainage bag is emptied at routine times, according to your agency’s policy. If you are responsible for this task, follow the guidelines to measure the urine from a drainage bag:

Bladder Training

膀胱功能再训练方案与临床路径
Bladder training programs have been developed for clients with urinary incontinence. Some clients need bladder training after removal of an in-dwelling catheter. After having an in-dwelling catheter for some time, they may have lost muscle tone in the bladder, so the bladder may no longer be able to stretch sufficiently to hold urine. These clients may have urinary incontinence until bladder tone is restored. Control of urination is the goal of the bladder training program, which would be part of the client’s care plan. As a support worker, you are required to assist with bladder training, as directed by your supervisor and the care plan. There are two basic methods of bladder training: 1. The client uses the toilet, commode, bedpan, or urinal at scheduled times. The client is given 15 or 20 minutes to initiate voiding. The guidelines for maintaining normal urination (see page 639) are followed. The normal position for voiding is assumed if possible. Privacy is important. Help the client relax, and provide encouragement. These techniques can help the client succeed. 2. The client has a catheter. The catheter is clamped to prevent urine from draining out of the bladder (see FIGURE 29.14 on page 656). Usually, the catheter is clamped for 1 hour at first, and eventually it is clamped for 3 to 4 hours at a time. Urine drains from the bladder when the catheter is unclamped. When the catheter is removed, you encourage the client to void every 3 to 4 hours or as directed by your supervisor and the care plan.
膀胱再训练计划（Bladder training programs）专为帮助尿失禁案主重建自主排尿控制功能而设立。许多案主在拔除长期留置导尿管后必须立即接受膀胱训练。长期留置导尿管使膀胱逼尿肌长期处于被动引流通畅状态，导致膀胱肌张力严重萎缩退化，膀胱丧失正常的充盈扩张张力反射。在膀胱肌张力恢复之前，案主常发生暂时性尿失禁。重新获得自主控尿能力是训练的最高终极目标，也是案主护理计划的重要核心部分。支持人员必须在主管护士指导下严格协助执行。 临床膀胱再训练主要涵盖两大基本路径： 1. 定时如厕排尿法：安排案主在固定设定的时间（如每隔 2 小时）使用卫生间、便椅、便盆或尿壶排尿。给予案主 15 至 20 分钟的充裕时间以诱发排尿反射。遵循第 639 页促进正常排尿的指导原则，协助案主采取生理坐位或站位。严密维护隐私，帮助案主身心放松并给予持续正向鼓励，协助其重塑神经括约肌反射弧。 2. 导管夹闭训练法：在计划拔除留置导尿管之前，使用阻断夹定时夹闭导尿管（参见第 656 页图 29.14），阻断尿液引流，使尿液在膀胱内自然充盈积蓄。初始阶段每次夹闭 1 小时，逐步延长至每次夹闭 3 至 4 小时，模拟正常的生理性膀胱充盈过程。松开阻断夹排空膀胱。当导尿管最终拔除后，鼓励并协助案主每隔 3 至 4 小时主动如厕排尿一次，逐步恢复自主控尿。

The Random Urine Specimen

常规随机尿液标本的临床应用
The random urine specimen is collected for a urinalysis. No special measures are needed, and the specimen is collected at any time. Often, clients can collect the specimen themselves, but clients who are weak or very ill will need assistance. The Midstream Specimen

The midstream specimen is also called a clean-voided specimen or a clean-catch specimen. Testing Urine

尿液临床试纸化验技术
You may be asked to perform simple urine tests—for example, measuring pH, glucose, ketones, and blood—using reagent strips. Ensure that the reagent strips are not outdated by checking the expiry date on the container. Straining urine for stones is another simple test. A physician orders the type and frequency of urine tests, and you must ensure accuracy when performing the tests. Promptly report the results, according to employer policy. • Testing pH—Urine pH measures indicate whether urine is acidic or alkaline. Changes in normal pH (4.6 to 8.0) occur due to illness, foods, and medications. A routine urine specimen is needed. • Testing for glucose and ketones—In clients with diabetes, the pancreas does not secrete enough insulin (see Chapter 33), which is needed by the body to use sugar for energy. If not used by the body, sugar builds up in the blood, and some of it will appear in urine. Glucosuria means glucose, a type of sugar (glucos, glycos), in urine (uria). The client with diabetes may also have ketones in the urine. Ketones appear in urine because of the rapid breakdown of fat for energy, which happens when the body cannot use sugar. The client’s care plan will determine when the client’s urine needs to be tested for glucose (or if the urine needs to be tested for both glucose and ketones). It is usually done four times a day: 30 minutes before each meal (ac) and at bedtime (hs). The physician uses the test results to regulate the client’s medication and diet. To test for glucose and ketones, follow the instructions printed on the side of the reagent bottle. (See Using Reagent Strips below.)
在临床工作中，你可能会被委派执行床旁简易尿液快速筛查——例如使用化学试纸（Reagent strips）检测尿液 pH 酸碱度、尿糖、尿酮体以及潜血。每次测试前必须严格核验试纸瓶身上的有效使用期限，严禁使用过期试纸！滤尿筛查尿路结石是另一项简易测试。医生开具化验医嘱与测试频次，你必须确保操作精准读数无误，并按规程及时汇报化验数值。 • 检测尿液 pH 酸碱度——反映尿液呈酸性还是碱性。正常尿液 pH 波动范围为 4.6 至 8.0。全身性疾病、特定饮食结构与药物均会导致 pH 改变。采用常规新鲜尿标本。 • 检测尿糖与尿酮体——在糖尿病案主中，胰腺无法分泌足量的胰岛素（参见第 33 章），导致机体细胞无法利用葡萄糖供能。未被利用的糖分在血液中蓄积（高血糖），当超过肾糖阈时糖分便溢出进入尿液中，这被称为糖尿（Glucosuria——glucos/glycos 表示葡萄糖，uria 表示尿液）。糖尿病案主的尿液中亦常检出酮体（Ketones）。当机体无法利用糖分时，体内脂肪代偿性剧烈分解氧化供能，其副产物酮体在血中蓄积并排入尿中（酮尿症）。护理计划会详细规定测试时段，常规通常为每日 4 次：三餐前半小时（ac）及临睡前（hs）。医生依据试纸读数动态微调胰岛素剂量与饮食处方。

The Client With a Ureterostomy or an Ileal Conduit

尿路造口术：输尿管造口与回肠膀胱术案主的全人照护
The bladder is sometimes surgically removed as part of treatment for cancer or bladder injuries. Since provision must be made for urine to leave the body, a new pathway—called a urinary diversion—is created. Urinary diversions can be of various types. The most common one is an ostomy (see Chapter 28), which is the surgical creation of an artificial opening. A ureterostomy is an artificial opening (stomy) between the ureter (uretero) and the abdomen. The artificial opening is called a stoma (FIGURE 29.27). Stomas do not have nerve endings, so they are not painful. Some clients may have an ileal conduit, an artificial bladder fashioned out of a section of the ileum (part of the small intestine). Urine drains from the ureters into the newly created artificial bladder and then through the stoma. An ostomy appliance (which some call a “pouch”) is applied over the stoma. The ostomy appliance is a disposable plastic bag that adheres to the skin around the stoma and that urine drains into. The ostomy appliance is replaced any time it leaks, as leaking urine can cause skin irritation, skin breakdown, and infection. Leaks also often cause clients to worry about odours and embarrassing wetness. Immediately after this type of surgery, the client will receive care from a nurse, but you may be required to care for clients with long-standing ureterostomies. The client assists with care as much as possible. It is your responsibility to provide good skin care to the client with a ureterostomy. These clients are at risk for skin breakdown around the stoma, caused by the draining urine and by the adhesive of the ostomy appliance. You need to report and record changes in the client’s skin around the stoma.

• Dignity. Never do anything to increase the client’s embarrassment. Always be encouraging and positive when providing care. Do not show disgust at the sights or odours associated with elimination and ostomy care. Remember that your nonverbal behaviours and expressions reveal your feelings. For example, do not wrinkle your nose or grimace while you are assisting the client. Never refer to incontinence products as diapers, as this term can humiliate the client.
• 尊严（Dignity）：绝不做任何加重案主尴尬窘迫的言行。照护时始终展现鼓励与积极接纳。面对排泄物或造口异味时，绝对严禁流露出丝毫嫌弃、厌恶或恶心的面部表情！切记：你的非语言行为和神态微表情最易暴露内心想法，绝不可在案主面前皱鼻子、撇嘴或面露难色。严禁将成人失禁纸尿裤蔑称为“尿布（Diapers）”（必须使用“护理衬垫 briefs”或“失禁吸收裤 incontinence product”），该词汇会严重剥夺成年人的尊严与自尊心！
• Independence. Respond promptly to all requests for help with elimination, as the need may be urgent. Believe clients when they ask for help, even if they just used the toilet, bedpan, urinal, or commode. Your prompt response gives the client some sense of control.
• 自立（Independence）：对所有排泄求助呼叫予以最迅速的即刻响应，排尿需求往往刻不容缓。无条件信任案主的求助诉求，哪怕案主刚刚才上过厕所或刚用完便盆。你的即刻响应能赋予案主对自己身体的掌控感与安全感。
• Individualized care. For care to be truly client-centred, it must be planned or adapted to fit the client’s choices, physical and cognitive strengths and limitations, personality, and care plan.
• 个体化照护（Individualized care）：真正的以案主为中心，照护必须量身定制，精准契合案主的个人意愿、身心功能长处与局限、性格特质以及护理计划。
• Preferences. Respect the client’s preferences, whenever possible. For example, some male clients may want to use the urinal sitting up, and some others may prefer to use it lying down.
• 个人偏好（Preferences）：尽可能尊重案主的个人习惯偏好。例如，某些男性案主习惯坐起使用尿壶，而另一些案主则习惯平卧位使用。
• Privacy. Provide as much privacy as possible. Lack of privacy may inhibit the client and interfere with elimination. Pull privacy curtains and close doors, shades, and drapes. Ask visitors or family members to step out of the room. If allowed by the care plan, leave the room after assisting the client to the bathroom or providing a bedpan, urinal, or commode. Or, to be immediately available, stand just outside the door, stand on the other side of the privacy curtain, or stay by the client’s side but look away if safe to do so. Ask your supervisor for advice on ways to protect the client’s privacy.
• 隐私（Privacy）：提供最大程度的隐私屏障。隐私匮乏会直接抑制神经反射导致无法排尿。拉严床帘，关紧房门与窗帘百叶窗。礼貌请探视亲友暂时移步门外。若护理计划允许独处，安置好便盆后应礼貌退出房间关门等待；若需近距离监护，可守在门外、隔帘外，或侧身面向别处，在保障绝对防跌倒安全的同时将隐私保护做到极致。
• Safety. Do not leave clients who are weak or dizzy. If you can leave the client and a call bell is available, make sure it is always within reach. Stay close enough so that you can hear the client’s call for help. Remember to follow Standard Practices.
• 安全（Safety）：体质虚弱、头晕目眩的案主身边绝不可无人守护！若案主自理能力良好允许独处，呼叫铃必须始终置于其手中。守护在能清晰听见呼救的距离之内。时刻贯彻标准预防感染防控。

Critical Thinking in Practice: Mrs. Riley's Urinary Catheter Emergency · 临床思辨实战案例：莱利太太（Mrs. Riley）的导尿管报警评估
Mrs. Riley has a urinary catheter. She tells you, “I feel like I have to pee, and I feel pressure down there.” She points to her lower abdomen. There is no urine in her drainage bag. Is this normal? What do you do?
莱利太太（Mrs. Riley）留置有导尿管。她焦急地对你说：“我感觉好想尿尿，而且我下面那里胀得好厉害、压力好大。”她指着自己的下腹部耻骨联合处。你迅速查看集尿袋，发现集尿袋内空空如也，完全没有新鲜尿液流出。请问这属于正常现象吗？作为支持人员，你必须立刻采取哪些紧急处理与评估步骤？
Clinical Decision & Action Protocol:
1. Recognition: This is NOT normal! Mrs. Riley is experiencing acute bladder distension and catheter blockage. Severe lower abdominal pressure combined with an empty drainage bag indicates the urine cannot flow out and is backing up in the bladder, which can lead to bladder rupture, severe pain, or autonomic dysreflexia.
2. Immediate Physical Inspection:
- Check the entire length of the catheter and drainage tubing immediately for kinks, bends, twisting, or loops dangling below the bag.
- Ensure Mrs. Riley is not lying on the tubing.
- Check that the drainage bag is hanging below the level of her bladder and attached securely to the bed frame (not the bed rail).
3. Unclogging / Repositioning: If a kink or compression is found, gently straighten the tubing immediately to restore free gravitational flow and monitor if urine rapidly flows into the bag.
4. Immediate Nurse Reporting: If no external kinks are found, or if urine still does not drain within minutes, inform your supervising registered nurse immediately! The internal eyelets of the catheter may be blocked by blood clots, mucus plugs, or sediment, or the catheter tip may have migrated, requiring urgent nurse irrigation or replacement.
临床决策与紧急应对行动指南：
1. 敏锐判别：这绝对极不正常！莱利太太正经历急性膀胱膨胀与导尿管完全堵塞！强烈的下腹压迫感与空置无尿的集尿袋，明确表明尿液无法排出并大量憋在膀胱内，极易诱发急性膀胱破裂、剧烈疼痛甚至自主神经反射异常！
2. 立即管路物理核查：
- 第一时间顺着导管全程检查引流管路是否存在扭曲、折叠、成角打结，或下垂形成低于尿袋的U型弯折。
- 核实莱利太太是否平卧或翻身直接压在管路上。
- 检查集尿袋是否始终悬挂在低于膀胱水平的床架上（而非床栏上）。
3. 解除压迫：若发现管路受压打折，立即轻柔顺直管路恢复重力引流，观察是否有积存尿液快速涌入集尿袋。
4. 即刻汇报主管护士：若体外未发现任何管路折叠扭曲，或理顺后数分钟内仍无任何尿液流出，必须立即向主管责任护士紧急汇报！这提示导管内部可能被血块、黏液栓或沉淀物彻底堵塞，或导管气囊移位嵌顿，需护士立即进行无菌冲管或拔除重置。

Rationale / 精解：A healthy adult excretes about 1 500 mL of urine per day. Normal urine is clear, pale yellow/amber with a faint odour.
健康成年人每天排出的生理尿量约为 1 500 mL。正常尿液清澈透明，呈淡黄或草黄色，仅具淡淡微弱气味，绝无恶臭或浑浊。

Rationale / 精解：Prompt assistance when requested prevents accidents, respects urgency, and promotes normal elimination habits.
在案主提出如厕需求时立即快速响应协助，符合排尿急迫性规律，能消除失禁焦虑并维护正常排尿条件反射。

半坐卧位（抬高床头成坐位）借助重力增加腹内压，使盆底肌群与腹肌协调舒缩，最易于膀胱彻底排空与排便。

用毕的尿壶应稳固挂在床栏内侧或椅背上备取。严禁放在床旁餐桌或床头柜上（用于放置餐食与洁净物品）。
REVIEW QUESTION 5 (Official Answer: C)

尿失禁导致皮肤频繁接触潮湿与刺激性排泄物，极易诱发失禁性皮炎（IAD）与压疮，必须执行严格温和的皮肤屏障护理。

REVIEW QUESTION 6 (Official Answer: B)

B. Keep the drainage bag below the level of her bladder
C. Hide the tubing by placing it underneath the client
D. Try to comfort her by saying that her urge to void is all in her head
在照料留置导尿管的琼斯太太（Mrs. Jones）时，下列哪项属于支持人员的核心执业职责？
A. 将集尿袋悬挂在可活动的床边护栏上
B. 使集尿袋始终保持处于其膀胱水平以下
C. 将引流软管塞在案主臀部身下以遮蔽隐藏
D. 安慰案主说排尿冲动纯属心理作用无需理会
Rationale / 精解：Keeping the drainage bag below the bladder is essential to prevent backward urine reflux and life-threatening CAUTI.
集尿袋必须始终保持在膀胱水平以下，依靠重力单向引流，绝对防止尿液逆流引发导管相关性尿路感染（CAUTI）。
REVIEW QUESTION 7 (Official Answer: D)
Mr. Powers has a condom catheter. You should apply elastic tape:
A. Completely around the penis
B. To the inner thigh
C. To the abdomen
D. In a spiral fashion
鲍尔斯先生（Mr. Powers）使用安全套导尿管。在为其固定胶套时，专用弹性胶带应当如何缠绕贴敷：
A. 首尾相接完全环形闭合缠绕在阴茎周围
B. 粘贴在大腿内侧皮肤上
C. 粘贴在下腹部皮肤上
D. 以螺旋式（Spiral fashion）顺势缠绕固定
Rationale / 精解：Applying elastic tape spirally allows expansion during physiological changes without cutting off arterial circulation to the penis.
弹性胶带必须以螺旋式缠绕，允许阴茎充血自如扩张；环形缠绕会形成止血带效应截断动脉供血导致阴茎坏死。

Rationale / 精解：The overarching goal of bladder training is restoring voluntary bladder sphincter control and urinary continence.
膀胱功能再训练的核心目标是重建大脑皮层与脊髓排尿中枢反射弧，使案主重获自主自主控尿能力。

随机尿标本必须严格遵医嘱在特定时间采集送检，以确保检验结果真实反映案主当前代谢状态。

24小时尿标本需弃去起始日晨尿后，将后续整整24小时内产生的所有尿液完整无遗漏地收集于冷藏大瓶中。
