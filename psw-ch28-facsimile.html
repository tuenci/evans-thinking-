Clients may have special requirements in regard to nutrition and fluids. Because of illness, injury, or surgery, some clients have difficulty eating, drinking, chewing, or swallowing. For these clients, nutrition and fluids as well as medications are provided through tubes, as ordered by a physician. Only a qualified nurse or other professional administers the procedures. As a support worker, however, you may care for clients who require enteral nutrition or who have an intravenous infusion.
案主在营养和水分摄入方面可能存在特殊需求。由于疾病、创伤或外科手术，部分案主在进食、饮水、咀嚼或吞咽方面存在功能障碍。根据医生医嘱，可通过管道为这些案主提供必需的营养、体液及药物支持。置管与管道输注操作仅由具备资质的护士或其他专业医护人员执行。然而，作为支持人员，你经常需要照护接受肠内营养支持或正在接受静脉输液的案主。

ENTERAL NUTRITION

肠内营养治疗 (ENTERAL NUTRITION)
People who cannot chew or swallow often require enteral nutrition, a nutritional formula delivered into the gastrointestinal tract (enteral) through a feeding tube. Gavage is another term for tube feeding. Feeding tubes are used when food cannot pass normally from the mouth into the esophagus and then into the stomach. This need can arise due to cancers of the head, neck, or esophagus; trauma or surgery to the face, mouth, head, or neck; dysphagia caused by paralysis; and coma. As well, people with dementia who no longer know how to eat may require tube feedings. There are various types of feeding tubes:
无法自主咀嚼或吞咽的案主通常需要接受肠内营养（Enteral nutrition）——即通过喂养管将特定营养配方液直接输送到胃肠道（enteral）内。管饲法（Gavage）是鼻饲或管道喂养的专业医学术语。当食物无法通过口腔正常进入食管并流入胃部时，即需要使用喂养管。导致这种状况的常见原因包括：头颈部或食管恶性肿瘤；面部、口腔、头部或颈部严重创伤及外科大手术；神经瘫痪（如脑卒中后）引起的重度吞咽障碍；以及深度昏迷。此外，因重度认知症（痴呆）而丧失进食认知与动作协调能力的案主也可能需要管饲喂养。临床常见的喂养管道类型包括：
• A nasogastric (NG) tube is inserted through the nose (naso) into the stomach (gastro) (FIGURE 28.1). A physician or a nurse performs the procedure.
• 鼻胃管（NG tube）：经由鼻孔（naso）穿过咽喉及食管插入胃（gastro）腔内（图 28.1）。该置管操作由医生或注册护士严格执行。
• A nasointestinal tube is inserted through the nose (naso) into the small intestine (intestinal) (FIGURE 28.2). A physician or a nurse performs the procedure.
• 鼻肠管（Nasointestinal tube）：经由鼻腔（naso）插入，前端穿过胃部幽门并延伸进入小肠（十二指肠或空肠）（图 28.2）。该操作由医生或专科护士执行。
• A gastrostomy tube is inserted into the stomach. A surgically created opening (stomy) in the stomach (gastro) is needed for this procedure (FIGURE 28.3). It is also known as a G-tube.
• 胃造口管（Gastrostomy tube）：直接置入胃腔内。该操作需要在腹壁和胃（gastro）壁上通过外科手术创建造口（stomy）（图 28.3）。亦称为 G 管（G-tube）。
• A gastro-jejunostomy tube is a combination device that includes access to both the stomach and the jejunum (the middle part of the small intestine). It is also known as a GJ-tube. Typically, tubes are placed into a G-tube stoma (a surgically created opening—see ostomy), with a narrower long tube continuing through the stomach and small intestine. The GJ-tube is used if the client has a high risk for aspiration or when a tube cannot be placed into the client’s stomach. It must be inserted by a physician in a hospital setting.
• 胃-空肠造口复合管（Gastro-jejunostomy tube）：一种同时具备胃部与空肠（小肠中段）双重通道的联合导管装置，亦称为 GJ 管（GJ-tube）。通常，导管经由胃造口置入，其中一根较细的导管穿过胃部继续深达小肠内。当案主存在极高的误吸风险或胃部病变无法耐受胃内喂养时采用 GJ 管。该管道必须由专科医生在医院手术室或内镜室内置入。
• A jejunostomy tube is inserted into the intestines. A surgically created opening (stomy) in the middle part of the small intestine (jejunum) is needed for this procedure (FIGURE 28.4). It is also known as a J-tube.
• 空肠造口管（Jejunostomy tube）：直接置入小肠内。该操作需要在小肠中段（空肠 jejunum）外科手术创建造口（stomy）（图 28.4）。亦称为 J 管（J-tube）。

• A percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy (PEG) tube is a G-tube that is inserted with the use of an endoscope, which is a lighted instrument (scope) that allows the physician to see inside a body cavity or organ (endo), such as the stomach. The physician inserts the endoscope through the person’s mouth and esophagus and into the stomach. A stab or puncture wound (stomy) is made through (per) the skin (cutaneous) and into the stomach (gastro). A tube is inserted into the stomach through the puncture wound (FIGURE 28.5).
• 经皮内镜下胃造口管（PEG tube）：这是一种在纤维内窥镜辅助下置入的胃造口管。内窥镜是一种带有光源和镜头的精密光学仪器（scope），能让医生直接观察体内器官或体腔（endo，如胃腔）内部。医生将内窥镜经由案主口腔及食管伸入胃内。随后在腹壁皮肤（cutaneous）上穿刺（per）造孔（stomy）直通胃（gastro）腔，将喂养导管经由该穿刺孔置入胃内（图 28.5）。
Gastrostomy, jejunostomy, and PEG tubes are used for long-term enteral nutrition. An ostomy is a surgically created opening that is used to drain wastes or to feed the client. The ostomy may be temporary or permanent. In many facilities and agencies, it is the nurse who is responsible for running the formula through an enteral site. The physician orders the type of formula and the amount to be given to the client. Most formulas contain protein, carbohydrates, fat, vitamins, and minerals. Commercial formulas are commonly used, or sometimes formulas are prepared by a facility’s dietary department.

Preventing Problems Related to Tube Feedings

管饲喂养潜在并发症的预防与安全管控
Nasogastric and nasointestinal tubes pass through the esophagus and then into the stomach or small intestine. During insertion, the tube could accidentally slip into the respiratory tract, causing aspiration, which can cause pneumonia and even death. An X-ray is therefore usually taken after insertion to determine correct tube placement.
鼻胃管和鼻肠管经由食管下行进入胃或小肠。在插入过程中，导管有可能误入气管及呼吸道，引发急性误吸，进而诱发严重吸入性肺炎甚至危及生命。因此，在盲插置管完成后，通常必须立即拍摄 X 光胸部平片，以金标准影像学确认导管尖端位于胃肠道腔内的准确位置。
Coughing, sneezing, vomiting, suctioning, and poor positioning can move the tube out of place. A confused client can also move the feeding tube by pulling on it. The tube can move from the stomach or intestines into the esophagus and then into the airway. In a facility, a nurse checks tube placement before every scheduled tube feeding. In the case of continuous tube feedings, the nurse checks tube placement every 4 to 8 hours, depending on agency or facility policy. To do so, the nurse attaches a syringe to the tube and aspirates gastrointestinal secretions. Then the nurse measures the pH of the secretions. The pH of stomach contents is acidic. A pH reading of 0 to 4 indicates that the tube is in the stomach.
剧烈咳嗽、打喷嚏、呕吐、呼吸道吸痰以及不当的翻身体位都有可能导致喂养管发生移位或脱出。认知障碍（意识混乱）的案主还可能因不适而无意识撕扯导管。导管可能从胃或小肠脱出至食管并误入气道。在照护机构中，护士在每次间歇定时管饲前均必须核对管道位置；对于持续输注者，护士每隔 4 至 8 小时检查一次管道位置。护士使用注射器回抽胃肠道消化液，并用精密试纸测定抽取物的 pH 值。胃液呈强酸性，pH 值在 0 至 4 之间表明导管尖端仍稳固处于胃内。
Aspiration is a major risk for clients with feeding tubes. Regurgitation can occur when formula flows backward from the stomach into the esophagus and then into the mouth. If regurgitation occurs, formula can be inhaled into the lungs. To prevent regurgitation and aspiration, position the client in Fowler’s or Semi-Fowler’s position (head of bed elevated at least 30 to 45 degrees) during the feeding and maintain this position for 1 to 2 hours after feeding. This allows gravity to help move the formula down through the digestive tract. Avoid the left-side-lying position, as it prevents normal stomach emptying.
误吸是管饲案主面临的最严峻临床风险。当胃内配方液反向逆流回食管并涌入口腔时，便发生反流（Regurgitation）。一旦发生食物反流，配方液极易被呛入气管和肺部造成误吸。为了预防反流和误吸，管饲输注期间必须将案主安置于福氏卧位（Fowler's position）或半福氏卧位（Semi-Fowler's position，床头抬高至少 30 至 45 度），并在管饲结束后继续维持该高位体位 1 至 2 小时。利用重力生理学促进营养液向下排空进入肠道。严禁采取左侧卧位，因为左侧卧位会压迫胃幽门并阻碍胃内容物正常排空。

INTRAVENOUS THERAPY

静脉输液疗法 (INTRAVENOUS THERAPY)
Intravenous (IV) therapy—also referred to as IV or IV infusion—involves giving fluids through an injection port, needle (such as a butterfly needle used on infants or children), or a cannula or catheter into a vein. A cannula (sometimes called a catheter) is plastic tubing threaded over a needle. Once the cannula or catheter is inserted into the vein, the needle is withdrawn. Physicians order IV therapy for the following purposes:
静脉输液疗法（Intravenous therapy，通常简称为 IV 或静脉滴注）是指通过给药接头、穿刺针（如常用于婴幼儿的头皮针/蝴蝶针）或静脉套管/导管将液体直接滴注到静脉血管内的治疗手段。套管（Cannula，亦称留置导管 catheter）是套在金属穿刺针外周的柔软塑料管；一旦刺入静脉内，金属针芯被迅速抽出并废弃，留置软管保留在静脉腔内。医生开具静脉输液医嘱主要用于以下目的：
• To provide needed fluids when a client cannot take fluids by mouth • To reverse dehydration in the body • To replace minerals and vitamins lost because of illness or injury • To provide sugar for energy • To administer medications such as antibiotics • To administer blood and blood products • To provide hyperalimentation—the giving of a solution highly concentrated in nutrients (total parenteral nutrition, TPN)
• 当案主无法由口摄取足够液体时，供给机体必需的水分 • 纠正与治疗机体脱水状态 • 补充因疾病、呕吐、腹泻或创伤丢失的电解质矿物质与水溶性维生素 • 提供葡萄糖等糖类物质作为基础生理能量来源 • 经静脉途径注射抗生素、抗心律失常等关键急救及治疗药物 • 输注全血及红细胞、血浆、血小板等血液制品 • 实施全胃肠外高营养疗法（Hyperalimentation / TPN）——输入高浓度氨基酸、葡萄糖及脂肪乳剂等全营养高渗混合液
IV therapy is given in the hospital, outpatient care, subacute care, long-term care, and home care settings. Nurses are responsible for IV therapy—they start and maintain the infusion, according to the physician’s orders, and also give IV medications and administer blood.
静脉输液疗法广泛应用于急性期医院、门诊输液中心、亚急性医疗机构、长期照护中心以及居家上门照护中。注册护士承担静脉输液的核心专业职责——依据医生医嘱穿刺置管、建立静脉通路、调节维护滴速、经静脉给药以及执行输血操作。

Common Intravenous Solutions

临床常用静脉输液溶液
IV solutions vary in volume and contents, and are ordered by the client’s physician or other qualified health care provider. They may be ordered to run intermittently (periodically), such as only when a medication needs to be infused, or continuously, such as to rehydrate a person. Some may have electrolytes (such as potassium) added to replenish what the client’s body has lost. Specific types of IV solutions are ordered to meet the specific health needs of each client. If the client is to have a blood transfusion, they may require a 0.9% NaCl (normal saline) solution IV as this is isotonic to bodily fluids such as blood. Examples of common IV solutions include 5% dextrose in water (D5W), 0.9% NaCl (normal saline), or 5% dextrose in 0.45% NaCl. Whatever the IV solution ordered, it must be sterile, and sterile technique must be maintained to avoid introducing potentially life-threatening bacteria into the body.
静脉输液溶液的容量规格与化学成分各异，均由主管医生开具法定医嘱。输液可以按需间歇性（周期性）滴注（例如仅在滴注抗生素时连接），也可以全天持续滴注（如脱水补液）。某些溶液中额外添加了氯化钾等电解质以纠正电解质紊乱。开具的输液种类针对每位案主的具体生理病理状态。若案主需要输血，必须使用 0.9% 氯化钠（生理盐水）溶液进行通路冲洗与伴随输注，因为其与血液等机体体液完全呈等渗状态（Isotonic；参见第32章）。常用液体包括：5% 葡萄糖水溶液（D5W）、0.9% 生理盐水（NS）以及 5% 葡萄糖加 0.45% 半张盐水等。无论输注何种液体，都必须绝对无菌，并在操作中严格维持无菌操作技术，杜绝将可能致命的细菌带入血液循环。

Central Intravenous Sites

中心静脉通路穿刺解剖部位 (Central Venous Sites)
The subclavian vein and the internal jugular vein—located close to the heart—are central venous sites. A physician inserts a long catheter—called a central venous catheter or central line—into a central vein. The catheter tip is then threaded into the superior vena cava or right atrium (FIGURE 28.9, A and B). At times, the cephalic and basilic veins in the arm also are used for IV therapy. Catheters inserted into these sites are called peripherally inserted central catheters (PICCs). The catheter tip is threaded into the subclavian vein or the superior vena cava (FIGURE 28.9, C).

Clients receiving IV therapy in their homes often have central venous catheters. The nurse teaches the client and family about administering IV medications and managing the catheter. Because these catheters or cannulas are inserted directly into a vein, you must be very careful when providing care. Ensure that you do not do anything around the insertion point that could dislodge the catheter. Report immediately any redness around the site. If a catheter falls out, apply pressure with sterile gauze until bleeding stops, and immediately inform your supervisor. If you are giving a tub bath to the client, ensure that the IV tubing is kept dry; if your client has a pump, it must be unplugged before the client enters the water. If your client is having a shower, keep the IV site dry by covering it with plastic wrap. Your supervisor will give you directions for care.

BOX 28.1: Safety Measures for Intravenous Therapy · 框 28.1：静脉输液核心安全防护准则
• Follow Standard Practices.
• The position of the IV needle or catheter must be maintained. Do not move the needle, cannula, or catheter. If the needle or cannula is moved, it may come out of the vein; fluid will flow into the tissues (infiltration), or the flow will stop. If the needle or catheter becomes dislodged, the site will bleed; apply pressure with sterile gauze according to Standard Practices, and contact your supervisor immediately.
• Sometimes, the nurse may apply splints or restraints to prevent movement of the extremity (FIGURE 28.10) and, thus, the movement of the needle or cannula.
• Protect the IV bag, tubing, and needle or cannula when ambulating the client. Portable IV stands are rolled along next to the client. When assisting the client to change gowns, care must be taken to prevent dislodging the needle or cannula (FIGURE 28.11).
• Assist the client with turning and repositioning. Move the bag to the side of the bed where the client is lying. Always allow enough slack in the tubing to ensure the needle or cannula does not dislodge due to pressure on the tube.
• Notify your supervisor immediately if bleeding occurs from the insertion site. Follow Standard Practices.
• Notify your supervisor immediately if there is any leaking of fluid from the IV site.
• Notify your supervisor immediately of any signs and symptoms listed in BOX 28.2.
• Always be aware of your scope of practice for your province or territory and the policies of your facility or agency.
• 严格执行标准防护规程（Standard Practices，接触血液渗出时佩戴手套）。
• 必须保持静脉穿刺针或留置软管的原有体位与深度。切勿晃动、牵拉或移动针头、套管或导管。若针头移动，可能穿破或滑出血管壁，造成输入的液体漏入周围皮下软组织（液体外渗 Infiltration），或导致滴注完全停止。若穿刺针意外脱出，穿刺孔会出血；应立即佩戴手套使用无菌医用纱布在穿刺点直接按压止血，并即刻呼救主管护士！
• 有时护士会使用前臂固定夹板（Armboard，图 28.10）或约束装置限制关节弯曲，从而防止留置针受压折弯移位。
• 协助案主下床行走时，切实保护好输液袋、输液管及留置针。便携式立式输液架应由案主或协助者推行在其身侧。协助案主更换病员服时，必须极其谨慎，防止牵扯针具（图 28.11）。
• 协助案主床上翻身和调整体位时，先将输液袋移到案主翻身侧。输液管路必须始终预留出充裕的松弛度（Slack），严防管道紧绷牵拉导致脱管。
• 若穿刺部位发生渗血或持续出血，立即向主管护士报告并执行止血防护。
• 若穿刺点周围敷料浸湿或有药液向外渗漏，立即报告护士。
• 一旦发现框 28.2 中的任何异常体征或症状，即刻向主管护士汇报。
• 始终对所在省份/地区的法定职业执业范围（Scope of Practice）及所属机构的规章制度保持高度敬畏与严格遵从。

SUBCUTANEOUS INFUSION THERAPY

皮下输注疗法 (SUBCUTANEOUS INFUSION THERAPY)
For clients who require pain medication or hydration on a continuous basis, or frequent injections, some physicians order subcutaneous infusion therapy for their clients. Unlike an IV, in which a needle is inserted into the client’s vein, the medication is given through an injection port or small needle that has been inserted into the client’s subcutaneous tissue layer using Standard Practices. IV tubing is then connected to this injection port or needle, which is then securely taped into place onto the client’s skin (FIGURE 28.12). Pain medication can therefore be administered into the injection port instead of being injected into the skin.
对于需要持续补液、长期镇痛治疗，或需要频繁注射止痛药物的案主（常见于姑息舒缓照护及临终关怀案主），医生可能会开具皮下输注疗法（Subcutaneous infusion therapy，亦称皮下输液法 Hypodermoclysis）。与静脉输液必须刺入血管不同，皮下输注是在严格无菌防护下，将带有注射端口的细小微型钢针（如蝴蝶针）刺入案主的皮下疏松结缔组织层中。输液导管与该注射接头相连，并使用透明敷料牢固贴敷固定于案主皮肤上（图 28.12）。镇痛药物便可经由该预置注射端口平缓推注或恒速滴注，从而免除了案主遭受反复多次肌肉皮下扎针穿刺的剧烈痛苦。

In order for the medication to be effective, the position of the injection port or subcutaneous needle must be maintained. Do not attempt to move it. If it is dislodged (moved), it may come out of the subcutaneous tissue and, when injected, the pain medication will not be effective for pain relief. If the port or needle does become dislodged, apply pressure with sterile gauze, according to Standard Practices, and contact your supervisor immediately.
为了确保药物能够被皮下毛细血管微循环充分吸收发挥药效，注射接头或皮下留置针的原本位置必须受到严格保护。切勿自行试图挪动或调整针头深度。若针头被移位或脱出皮下组织，注入的镇痛药物将发生漏液外溢，无法达到镇痛效果。若注射接头或针头发生脱出，应立即佩戴手套使用无菌纱布覆盖按压穿刺点，并即刻向主管护士汇报重置。
PROVIDING COMPASSIONATE CARE: Enteral Nutrition and Intravenous Therapy · 倾注仁爱关怀：肠内营养与静脉输液中的 DIPPS 核心准则
• Dignity. Clients who have to rely on feeding tubes to supply their nutrition may be embarrassed by their tube. Others may feel like an outcast when they are required to watch others eat when they are unable to. Report if the client is embarrassed about having their feeding while they have visitors; visiting hours can be arranged around the client’s feeding times. Feeding tubes that are inserted into the client’s nose should be taped in the most discreet way possible, perhaps using transparent tape or dressings.
• 维护尊严（Dignity）：不得不依赖喂养管摄取营养的案主常对脸上的管子感到自卑与尴尬；在别人用餐而自己无法经口品尝时，有些人会产生被群体孤立抛弃的凄凉感。若案主在亲友探视时因当众管饲而感到难堪，应向护士报告，合理调整探视时间避开喂食时段。对于插入鼻腔的面部导管，应用最隐蔽美观的方式固定，优先使用超薄透明医用敷料贴敷。
• Independence. Encourage clients to be as independent as possible. If they are able to ambulate, provide a portable IV pole to hold the feeding bag so they can walk while receiving their feeding.
• 促进自主独立（Independence）：鼓励案主在安全范围内最大程度保持自理。若身体机能允许下床活动，提供带有静音滑轮的便携式输液架悬挂喂养袋，让其在输注期间仍能自主散步活动。
• Individualized care. For care to be truly client-centred, it must be planned or adapted to fit the client’s choices, physical and cognitive strengths and limitations, personality, and care plan. For example, if the client has any discomfort (see Chapter 23), try to determine the type of pain the person has, report it to the appropriate person, and document it in the client record.
• 个体化照护（Individualized care）：以案主为中心的照护必须契合其身心能力与性格特点。例如，若案主表现出任何疼痛或胀痛不适（参见第23章），细心观察评估疼痛性质与部位，迅速报告护士并记入护理记录。
• Preferences. Encourage personal choices whenever possible. Allow clients receiving an enteral feeding to choose clothing that would cover their tubing dressing or stoma but also provide access for routine checking.
• 尊重个人偏好（Preferences）：鼓励自主选择衣着。允许案主挑选既能遮盖腹部造口与敷料（维护外观体面），又便于医护人员拉开拉链进行常规巡视核对的适宜衣物。
• Privacy. Providing for privacy is important when checking the client’s tubing site, especially if the client has tubing that enters into their abdominal area. Close the curtains or the door when inspecting the tubing or when the nurse is providing care to their dressing or stoma site.
• 保障隐私（Privacy）：检查腹部置管造口或输液部位时，务必保护好身体隐私暴露。在查看腹部造口或护士更换敷料时，拉严病床隔帘或关好房门。
• Safety. To prevent regurgitation after an enteral feeding, ensure that the client is in the sitting or semi-Fowler’s position for the feeding. Keep the client in this position for 1 to 2 hours after the feeding as well to promote movement of the formula through the gastrointestinal system and prevent aspiration. Avoid the left-side-lying position, which prevents emptying of the stomach.
• 确保安全（Safety）：为了杜绝管饲后食物反流，必须确保在喂养全程及喂养结束后 1 至 2 小时内，案主始终处于高坐位或半福氏卧位（抬高床头30–45度），依靠重力生理学促进配方液向下消化排空，坚决预防误吸窒息。绝对避免左侧卧位，因为左侧卧位会严重阻碍胃排空。

CHAPTER REVIEW: KEY POINTS

• Some clients, because of illness, injury, or surgery, are not able to swallow, eat, or drink and need a feeding tube to provide nourishment and medications. • Enteral nutrition feeding may be done through a nasogastric tube, a nasointestinal tube, a gastrostomy tube, a gastro-jejunostomy tube, a jejunostomy tube, or a percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy tube. • Feeding-tube formulas may be commercially prepared or prepared by the facility where the client is. • The feeding tube can be moved out of place from coughing, sneezing, vomiting, suctioning, poor positioning, or the client pulling on the tube. The nurse checks on the position of the tubing, depending on your employer’s policy. • Aspiration is the major risk for clients with a feeding tube. You need to ensure that you do not move or dislodge the needle, catheter, or cannula. Re-insertion of an IV is painful for your client. • As a support worker, you are never responsible for starting or maintaining IV therapy, but you must report immediately any of the signs and symptoms of IV therapy complications.
• 由于疾病、创伤或手术，部分案主无法自主吞咽或进食，需要通过喂养管道供给营养和必需药物。 • 肠内营养途径包括鼻胃管（NG管）、鼻肠管、胃造口管（G管）、胃-空肠造口复合管（GJ管）、空肠造口管（J管）以及经皮内镜下胃造口管（PEG管）。 • 肠内营养配方可采用工业化商品配方制剂，或由机构营养科专门配制。 • 咳嗽、喷嚏、呕吐、吸痰、体位不当或案主抓扯均可导致管道移位。护士依据机构制度定期测试抽吸物 pH 值确认管道位置。 • 误吸（Aspiration）是管饲案主面临的最核心致命风险。其他必须即刻报告的症状包括恶心、不适、呕吐、腹泻、腹部膨隆、呛咳、消化不良、呼吸窘迫、脉搏增快及肠胀气。 • 医生开具静脉输液疗法用于补充体液、补充电解质/维生素/糖分，或给予药物与血液制品。 • 穿刺解剖部位取决于案主年龄、全身病情及输液目的。 • 作为支持人员，在照护输液案主时必须极其轻柔，严防牵扯或移位穿刺针、导管或套管。重新扎针置管对案主而言极其痛苦。 • 作为支持人员，你绝不承担穿刺起针或维护输液通路的责任，但你必须在发现任何输液并发症迹象时第一时间向主管护士报告。
CRITICAL THINKING IN PRACTICE · 临床实战批判性思维案例
Mrs. Stenson is receiving a continuous tube feeding. You hear her feeding pump alarm sound. You go to check on her. The head of her bed is flat and she says: “My mouth is dry.” What do you do first? What do you do next? What can you do within your role limits? What does the nurse need to do?
案例：斯滕森夫人（Mrs. Stenson）正在接受 24 小时持续管饲泵注。你听到病房内肠内喂养泵的报警蜂鸣声响起。你走进病房查看。发现她的病床床头完全处于水平平卧位（Flat），并且她对你说：“我的嘴巴好干啊。”
面对此紧急情境：
1. 你应当首先采取什么紧急行动？（立即暂停/关闭喂养泵输注，并迅速将床头摇高至半福氏卧位 30–45 度，防止平卧位引发食物反流和急性误吸窒息！）
2. 接下来做什么？（立即按下呼叫铃呼叫主管护士到场评估，告知护士喂养泵报警及案主处于平卧位的情况）。
3. 在你的职责权限内你能做什么？（评估案主有无咳嗽或呼吸困难；在护士评估确认安全前，绝对不能擅自给案主喝水，因为其处于 NPO 状态；可以使用温水棉签或湿润棉球为案主轻轻湿润嘴唇和口腔黏膜缓解口干，但绝不能让其咽下液体）。
4. 护士需要执行什么医疗处置？（护士负责检查导管体外标记刻度，抽吸胃液测定 pH 值以确认导管尖端是否依然在胃内，排查喂养泵报警原因（如管路打折、气泡或输完），并重新设定泵注速度启动喂养）。
