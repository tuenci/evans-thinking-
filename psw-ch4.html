ETHICS, MORALS, VALUES, AND BELIEFS

伦理、道德、价值观与信念的科学辨析

The term ethics refers to the rules of conduct that guide us when deciding what is right and what is wrong, and what is good and what is bad. These rules come from society's expectations of how people should conduct themselves. In contrast, morals are the fundamental principles of behaviour each person believes to be right or wrong for themselves, guided by personal conscience without strict regard to legalities or customs.

“伦理”（Ethics）是指指引我们在面对具体情境时甄别是非曲直（Right vs Wrong）以及善恶美丑的行为规范与价值准绳。这些规范源自整个人类社会对个体应当如何行事操守的共同期待。相比之下，“个人道德”（Morals）是指个体基于个人良知、在内心深处坚信对自己而言何为对、何为错的根本行为信念，它独立于具体的成文法律或民俗约定。

Values are what an individual considers to be worthwhile, desirable, and meaningful qualities in a person (such as honesty, compassion, loyalty, and fairness). A belief is a conviction that something is true, often rooted in faith, cultural upbringing, or lived experience. Ethics, morals, values, and beliefs continuously interact to guide professional and personal decision-making.

“价值观”（Values）是个体在内心深处视作至关重要、极具价值且值得毕生追求的人格品质与生活理想（如诚实守信、仁爱同理、忠诚尽责与公平公正）。“信念”（Belief）则是指个体对某种理念或事态为真实、存在的坚定认同，它往往深植于宗教信仰、文化熏陶或生活磨砺之中。伦理、道德、价值观与信念四者紧密交织，共同构筑了指引我们职业行为与临床决断的心灵罗盘。

THE INFLUENCE OF CULTURE ON BELIEFS, VALUES, MORALS, AND ETHICS

多元文化、历史创伤对伦理道德观念的深远塑造

Support workers must recognize that culture, religion, historical trauma, and personal lived experiences profoundly shape what a client considers acceptable, ethical, or honourable. Actions that seem obvious to a healthcare worker may conflict with a client's core cultural morals.

支持工作者必须清醒地认识到：文化传统、宗教教义、历史创伤以及案主过往的人生磨难，深刻塑造了当事人对何为得体、何为道德、何为尊严的认知。在医护从业者看来理所当然的常规医疗操作，在某些案主独特的文化道德世界中可能会引发强烈的心理冲突。

CASE STUDY: Supporting Mr. Medvjed: How Past Experiences Affect Beliefs

专栏案例研讨：支持梅德维杰德先生 —— 过往创伤如何深刻改变案主信念
SCENARIO:
Mr. Medvjed, age 86, is a resident in a long-term care facility. He fled war-torn Eastern Europe as a young man after enduring traumatic military interrogation and persecution. He hoards food beneath his mattress, becomes agitated when staff wear uniforms, and adamantly refuses to shower when bathroom doors are locked.

CLINICAL REFLECTION:
Mr. Medvjed's behaviour is not 'stubbornness' or 'deliberate non-compliance'; it is an adaptive survival response rooted in severe trauma. Forcing him into locked showers or confiscating his hoarded bread revictimizes him. A compassionate support worker accommodates his trauma: leaving bathroom doors unlocked, wearing non-clinical scrub patterns, and validating his need for food security.
实战情境：
梅德维杰德先生（Mr. Medvjed），86 岁，居住在长期照护院舍中。年轻时为了逃离战乱的东欧，他曾遭受过残酷的军事严刑拷打与政治迫害。在院舍生活中，他经常把未吃完的面包偷偷藏在床垫底下，一见到身穿白大褂或制服的员工就会神色恐慌，且一旦洗澡时关闭上锁浴室房门，他便会极度惊恐躁动、坚决抗拒洗澡。

临床深度反思：
梅德维杰德先生的行为绝非“固执任性”或“故意抗拒”，而是战后严重创伤后应激障碍（PTSD）深植于骨髓的生存防御机制！如果强行将其锁入浴室冲澡或粗暴没收其私藏的面包，将是对长者心灵的二次残酷伤害。富有同理心的支持工作者应当尊重其创伤背景：洗澡时虚掩房门消除其幽闭恐慌、改穿柔和的彩色便服工作服，并温和理解长者对食物安全的极度渴望。

CODES OF ETHICS

医疗卫生行业的职业伦理守则

Regulated healthcare professionals (such as physicians and nurses) are legally bound by formal codes of ethics published by their respective governing colleges. Support workers do not currently have a single provincial statutory college code; instead, each employer, health authority, or home care agency publishes an agency code of ethics outlining the fundamental values and standards of conduct expected of support workers.

受法律监管的医护人员（如注册护士与执业医师）受到各自法定专业监管学院发布的行业职业伦理守则的严格法律约束。支持工作者目前尚未通过省立法设立统属的专业监管学院；但各用人机构、卫生局或居家派遣中介均会制定并公布本单位的《员工职业伦理行为守则》，明确界定支持工作者必须誓死恪守的人本照护价值观与职业操守底线。

THE FOUR PRINCIPLES OF HEALTH CARE ETHICS

医疗卫生伦理四大根本基石原则

1. The Principle of Autonomy (Self-Determination)

第一基石：自主自决原则（Autonomy / Self-Determination）

Autonomy—also referred to as self-determination—is the fundamental right of a mentally competent individual to make their own choices and decisions about their own body, life, and healthcare. Autonomy affirms that a person does not lose their civil rights simply because they become ill, frail, or enter a healthcare facility.

“自主自决原则”（Autonomy，亦称自决权 Self-Determination）是指具有完全民事与知情同意心智能力的个体，拥有自主做出影响自身身体、生活方式以及医疗干预决策的神圣法定权利。自主权明确宣示：一个人绝不会仅仅因为生病衰老、失能或住进了医疗机构，就丧失了作为独立公民的基本人权！

In support work, autonomy requires honouring the client's preferences: asking what clothes they wish to wear, respecting their meal choices, and honouring their chosen daily routines. Crucially, autonomy includes the legal right to refuse medical care, assistance, or medications. If a competent client refuses a bath or assistance, the support worker must respect the refusal, never use coercion or force, explain the risks gently, and report the refusal immediately to the supervising nurse.

在日常支持工作中，践行自主权要求我们必须无条件尊重案主的个性化选择：主动征询其喜欢穿哪套衣服、尊重其饮食偏好、顺应其作息节奏。更关键的是，自主权赋予了案主合法拒绝医疗处置、拒绝翻身或拒绝服药的神圣法定权利！倘若一位神志清晰的案主坚决拒绝洗澡或拒绝护工搀扶，支持工作者必须坚决尊重其拒绝意愿，严禁使用任何形式的语言恐吓或肉体强迫，在耐心地用通俗语言告知潜在风险后，第一时间如实向主管护士汇报记录。

p. 52

2. The Principle of Justice

第二基石：公平正义原则（Justice）

The principle of justice means that all people should be treated in an equal, fair, and impartial manner. Healthcare resources, attention, and compassion must be distributed equitably according to need, without discrimination based on age, race, gender, religion, socioeconomic status, diagnosis, or temperament.

“公平正义原则”（Justice）是指所有受助个体均应当获得完全平等、公允且不偏不倚的对待与关照。医疗资源分配、护工的时间精力与同理心关怀，必须严格依据案主的实际照护需求进行公允分配，坚决杜绝因年龄老幼、种族肤色、性别取向、宗教信仰、贫富差距、病情轻重或脾气性格好坏而产生任何偏袒、冷落或歧视。

Support workers demonstrate justice by ensuring they do not spend excessive time with a pleasant, communicative resident while neglecting a withdrawn, agitated, or demanding resident. Every individual under your care deserves identical respect, thoroughness, and meticulous attention.

支持工作者在临床中体现正义原则的生动实践是：绝不能因为某位长者性格风趣和善、逢人便夸就整天泡在其房间聊天，而对隔壁性格孤僻沉闷、情绪暴躁或频繁拉铃的老人避之不及甚至消极怠工。在你负责轮班的每一个病榻前，每一位长者都享有获得同等细致、耐心与精湛照护的神圣正义权利。

3. The Principle of Beneficence

第三基石：慈惠行善原则（Beneficence）

The principle of beneficence means doing or promoting good. It requires healthcare providers to actively contribute to the client's health, safety, comfort, and well-being, striving to maximize clinical benefits in all interventions. Beneficence is not passive; it demands proactive kindness, empathy, and advocacy.

“慈惠行善原则”（Beneficence）是指积极主动地为案主行善利他并谋求福祉。它要求医护从业者必须主动采取建设性的积极行动，全力促进服务对象的身心健康、人身安全、机体舒适与心灵愉悦，在每一次临床照料中力求使案主的获益最大化。行善绝非消极等待，它要求从业者倾注主动的关爱、真挚的同理心与敢于为弱者发声的执业担当。

p. 53

4. The Principle of Nonmaleficence

第四基石：不伤害原则（Nonmaleficence / Primum Non Nocere）

The principle of nonmaleficence is the foundational duty to do no harm (derived from the ancient medical maxim primum non nocere). It requires healthcare workers to intentionally refrain from inflicting harm, to prevent harm whenever possible, and to eliminate existing risks of harm to clients.

“不伤害原则”（Nonmaleficence，源自古希腊希波克拉底医学名言“首先做到不伤害 Primum Non Nocere”）是整个医疗与护理伦理中最神圣、不可动摇的底线红线。它以法律铁律要求医护从业者：坚决蓄意不施加任何肉体或心理创伤、在任何可能的情境下竭力防范伤害的发生，并不遗余力地清除任何威胁案主安危的环境隐患。

In support work, nonmaleficence means rigorously following infection control protocols to prevent outbreaks, using transfer equipment correctly to avoid fractures or drops, testing bathwater temperatures before immersion to prevent third-degree scalds, locking wheelchair brakes, and reporting suspected physical, emotional, sexual, or financial abuse or neglect immediately.

在日常支持工作中，践行不伤害原则绝非空洞口号，它具体落地在极其严苛的操作规范中：洗手消毒防范院内超级细菌交叉感染、规范操作机械移位机杜绝长者坠床骨折、下水前必用手腕内侧测水温严防三度严重烫伤、轮椅停稳必锁死脚刹防溜车，以及一旦在案主身上发现可疑淤青擦伤或察觉虐待遗弃前兆，必须依法立即向上级护士进行紧急书面与口头双重汇报！

ETHICAL DILEMMAS AND CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

临床伦理两难困境与利益冲突的坚决防范

An ethical dilemma is an apparent conflict between opposing moral choices where choosing one moral obligation inevitably leads to violating another. In healthcare, ethical dilemmas rarely present a clear choice between 'good' and 'bad'; rather, they involve excruciating tensions between two competing 'goods' or choosing the lesser of two unavoidable evils.

“伦理两难困境”（Ethical Dilemma）是指在两个相互对立的崇高道德选择之间出现的尖锐冲突情境：选择履行其中一项道德职责，必然会在客观上导致背离另一项同样重要的道德义务。在复杂的医疗实践中，伦理两难极少呈现为非黑即白的“善与恶”对决，而往往是两个同样崇高的“善”之间的殊死博弈，或在两个无法避免的痛苦选择中艰难权衡并抉择“两害相权取其轻”。

Avoiding Conflict of Interest

坚决杜绝个人利益冲突（Conflict of Interest）

Support workers must maintain absolute impartiality and professional boundaries. A conflict of interest occurs when a worker's personal, financial, or familial interests clash with the professional duty owed to the client.

支持工作者必须时刻保持高度的职业纯洁性与严密的职业边界。当从业者个人的私利、财务状况或亲友利益与其对服务对象承担的崇高照护职责发生对立冲突时，即构成了极其严重的“利益冲突”（Conflict of Interest）。

Support workers must never accept personal loans, gifts of substantial value, tips, bequests in a client's will, or sell products to clients. Any decision regarding a client's care must be made strictly for the client's benefit, never for the worker's personal advantage.

支持工作者誓死严禁向案主借款、索取收受小费、接受贵重礼物、诱导长者在遗嘱中添加遗产赠予，或在工作场所推销个人微商副业。针对案主照护所做出的每一个临床决定，必须百分之百纯粹为了案主的最高福祉，绝不容许掺杂任何从业人员的私人私利算计！

CASE STUDY: AUTONOMY VERSUS NONMALEFICENCE

实战案例深研：当“案主自主自决权”激烈碰撞“防跌倒人身安全”

CASE STUDY: Mr. Ferguson's Refusal of a Walker

专栏实战案例：弗格森先生坚决拒绝使用四脚助行器
THE CLINICAL SITUATION:
Mr. Ferguson, age 79, lives in an assisted-living facility. He has Parkinson's disease with severe gait freezing and postural instability. Following two recent falls that caused scalp lacerations, the physiotherapist and nursing team assessed that he urgently requires a four-wheeled walker with handbrakes to ambulate safely.

However, Mr. Ferguson fiercely refuses to use the walker, stating: 'I am not an invalid! Walkers are for old, decrepit people. I will walk with my wooden cane or not at all.' He attempts to walk unassisted to meals, stumbling repeatedly.

THE ETHICAL DILEMMA:
• On one hand, the principle of AUTONOMY dictates that Mr. Ferguson is mentally competent and has the sovereign legal right to make his own mobility choices and refuse assistive devices.
• On the other hand, the principle of NONMALEFICENCE obligates the care team to protect him from foreseeable catastrophic harm (a fatal hip fracture, intracranial hemorrhage).

THE RESOLUTION:
Staff do not 'force' or physically restrain Mr. Ferguson (which would be illegal assault). Instead, the care team arranges an interprofessional family conference. The team validates his feelings about aging, explores stylish walking poles or low-profile rollators, pairs him with a support worker for standby assistance during peak dining hours, and establishes a dignity-preserving compromise.
临床真实情境：
弗格森先生（Mr. Ferguson），79 岁，居住在辅助生活照护公寓中。他患有进行性帕金森病，伴有严重的步态冻结与姿势平衡反射丧失。在最近连续发生两次导致头皮裂伤缝针的跌倒摔伤后，物理治疗师与责任护士经严格评估下达医嘱：他必须全程使用配备手刹的四轮助行车（Walker）方可下地活动。

然而，神志清晰的弗格森先生坚决抗拒使用助行车，愤怒抗议道：“我绝不是残废！那玩意儿是给快断气的废人用的。我要么拄我的木拐杖走，要么就不出门！”他固执地拒绝推助行车，摇摇晃晃踉跄走去餐厅就餐，途中险象环生。

尖锐的伦理两难冲突：
• 一方面，自主自决原则（AUTONOMY）要求必须尊重弗格森先生作为完全民事能力人的意愿与主权，他有权拒绝使用其痛恨的辅助器具；
• 另一方面，不伤害原则（NONMALEFICENCE）以生命至上原则要求医护团队必须履行防范其再次遭受灾难性髋关节骨折或颅内出血致命伤害的崇高职责。

科学卓越的临床化解路径：
团队绝不采取粗暴抢夺拐杖或身体约束硬推的违法侵权暴行；而是召开由案主、家属、主管护士与治疗师共同参加的三方伦理座谈会。团队充分倾听长者对衰老抗拒的心声，为其量身推荐更为轻巧美观的登山式平衡杖，并在用餐高峰期专门安排支持工作者贴身伴随步行保护，最终达成了既充分捍卫长者自尊、又确保绝对防摔安全的双赢折中方案。

A FOUR-STEP FRAMEWORK FOR RESOLVING ETHICAL DILEMMAS

解决临床伦理两难困境的四步科学决断框架

When confronted with complex ethical tensions, support workers should never act impulsively or make solitary decisions. They must follow a structured, collaborative four-step decision-making model:

当在病榻旁面临复杂的伦理道德冲突时，支持工作者绝不能凭一时冲动草率行事，更绝不能单枪匹马擅自做出武断决断。从业者必须严格遵照以下结构化、团队协作的四步决策科学模型：

  • • Step 1: Identify the Ethical Issue and Competing Principles. Clearly articulate what the ethical conflict is. Which fundamental principles (Autonomy vs Nonmaleficence, Justice vs Beneficence) are in direct tension?
    第一步：精准辨识核心伦理问题与对立原则。清晰透彻地界定伦理冲突的症结所在：究竟是哪两项根本原则（如“案主自主权”与“不伤害人身安全”、“公平正义”与“利他行善”）发生了激烈碰撞？
  • • Step 2: Gather Comprehensive Information and Perspectives. Collect all relevant medical facts, cognitive assessments, cultural beliefs, family viewpoints, and agency policies. Listen deeply to the client's own stated fears and desires.
    第二步：全面收集各方临床事实与多重视角。详尽梳理案主的病史诊断、认知能力评估报告、宗教文化禁忌、家属核心诉求以及机构法定管理规章，以极大的同理心深度倾听案主本人的内心真实渴望与恐惧。
  • • Step 3: Consider All Possible Options and Weigh Ethical Consequences. Brainstorm creative alternative compromises. Evaluate each option through the lens of the DIPPS model. What are the short-term and long-term harms and benefits of each choice?
    第三步：全面头脑风暴构思所有备选方案并权衡伦理后果。基于 DIPPS 人本照护模型系统审视每个折中方案：哪种选择能够将对案主尊严与自主权的损害降到最低？哪种选择能够最大限度保障其人身生命安全？
  • • Step 4: Decide on the Best Action, Implement Collaboratively, and Evaluate Outcome. Select the most ethical, compassionate course in concert with the supervising nurse and team. Document objectively, monitor results, and reassess as needs change.
    第四步：审慎确定最佳行动路径、团队协同执行并严密追踪评估成效。在责任主管护士与跨学科团队指导下选定最具人文同理心的处置方案。在病历中做出客观详尽记录，持续观察长者反馈并动态调整。

Seeking Professional Support

构建强大的职业伦理支持后盾

Remember: Support workers are never expected to resolve complex ethical or legal dilemmas in isolation. Always consult your supervising nurse, social worker, or your facility's clinical ethics committee. Open interprofessional dialogue protects both the client and the worker.

请时刻牢记：支持工作者绝非独行侠，没有任何人要求你孤军奋战独自承担复杂的法律与伦理重担！在遇到两难抉择时，必须第一时间向你的主管护士、责任组长、社会工作者或医院的临床医学伦理委员会（Ethics Committee）请示咨询。开放透明的跨学科专业探讨，是筑牢案主生命尊严与从业者执业安全的双重铠甲！

CHAPTER REVIEW

本章复习与临床思维修炼

KEY POINTS

全章核心知识要点精要清单
  • • Ethics refers to rules or values guiding right from wrong and good from bad, based on societal expectations.
    伦理是指基于人类社会共同期许、指引我们在日常行为中甄别是非曲直与善恶美丑的行为规范与价值体系。
  • • Morals are fundamental principles of behaviour an individual believes to be right or wrong for themselves, guided by personal conscience.
    个人道德是指个体基于自身良知在内心深处坚信对自己而言何为对错的根本准则，不受制于外部成文法律或世俗习俗。
  • • Values are qualities considered worthwhile in a person; beliefs are convictions that something is true.
    价值观是个体视作具有崇高价值的人格品质；信念则是个体内心深处坚信某事为真实存在的心理信仰。
  • • Culture, religion, and lived trauma influence ethical beliefs, morals, and values, which in turn drive personal actions.
    文化传统、宗教信仰及过往人生创伤深刻塑造了伦理信念与道德观，并最终主导着个体的实际行为取向。
  • • The principle of justice means all people should be treated in a fair, equal, and impartial manner.
    公平正义原则要求必须以完全公允、平等且不偏不倚的崇高态度对待每一个受助生命，杜绝偏袒与歧视。
  • • The principle of autonomy means having the freedom and sovereign right to make choices and direct one's own care.
    自主自决原则是指神志清晰的案主享有做出自主选择、主导自身日常生活并合法拒绝医疗处置的神圣自决主权。
  • • The principle of beneficence means actively doing or promoting good, maximizing well-being and positive outcomes.
    慈惠行善原则是指积极主动地为案主谋求福祉、行善利他并全面促进其身心健康的道德作为。
  • • The principle of nonmaleficence is the foundational duty to do no harm, preventing injury and removing hazards.
    不伤害原则是誓死追求“绝不造成伤害”的不可动摇底线，全力防范蓄意伤害、过失疏忽或意外跌倒创伤。
  • • An ethical dilemma is an apparent conflict between opposing moral choices; solving it requires considering the four principles, gathering facts, and consulting the team.
    伦理两难困境是对立道德职责之间的尖锐冲突；化解它必须熟练运用四大伦理基石、全面倾听各方心声并依靠团队协作。
  • • Support workers must strictly avoid conflicts of interest, refusing gifts, loans, or personal ventures that exploit the therapeutic relationship.
    从业者必须坚决防范个人利益冲突，誓死拒收昂贵礼物、借款小费，绝不利用照护关系谋求任何私人私利。

CRITICAL THINKING IN PRACTICE

临床实践中的批判性思维修炼

CRITICAL THINKING: The Family Demanding Force-Feeding

实战情境思维修炼：家属极度恐慌要求“强行将饭菜硬灌进嘴里”的伦理应对
THE CLINICAL SCENARIO:
You are assisting Mr. Zhang, age 82, in a long-term care facility with his evening meal. Mr. Zhang is cognitively intact but frail and terminal; he calmly shakes his head and refuses to eat after three spoonfuls. His wife, who is deeply distressed about his progressive weight loss and terrified of losing him, grabs your arm and demands: 'Force the food into him! Shove it in his mouth! He will starve to death if you don't!'

CRITICAL THINKING QUESTIONS:
1. Does Mr. Zhang have the legal and ethical right to refuse to eat? (Autonomy)
2. Does Mrs. Zhang have the legal right to demand that her husband be force-fed? (Substituted judgment limits)
3. What are the severe physical and legal dangers of attempting to force food into a resistant elder? (Nonmaleficence, aspiration pneumonia, choking, battery)
4. What is your immediate response to Mrs. Zhang with empathy and compassion? Who do you report to immediately?
实战临床情境：
你正在长期照护养老院内协助 82 岁的张老先生进食晚餐。张老先生神志完全清晰但处于肿瘤晚期极度衰竭；在勉强咽下三口粥后，他平静地摇了摇头，闭口坚决拒绝再进食。其守候在病榻旁的妻子见状情绪失控、极度恐慌于丈夫体重骤降与死亡逼近，一把抓住你的手臂厉声哭求道：“给我硬把饭灌进去！强行撬开他的嘴往里塞！你们不灌他就会活活饿死的！”

批判性思维修炼核心命题：
1. 神志清醒的张老先生是否拥有拒绝继续进食的神圣法律与伦理权利？（自主权 Autonomy 的神圣性）
2. 张太太作为家属，是否有权强令医护人员对其配偶实施强迫性硬塞喂食？（家属意志绝不能凌驾于具有心智能力当事人的明示拒绝之上）
3. 倘若照护者屈从家属压力试图强行撬嘴灌食，将面临何种可怕的身体与法律灾难？（不仅构成违法的身体侵害 Battery，更极易导致致命的食物误吸入气道、窒息或严重吸入性肺炎瞬间毙命！）
4. 你应当如何以充满慈爱同理的心情安抚极度悲恸的张太太，并将这一情况以何种规范流程向责任主管护士汇报？

Answers to these questions are at the bottom of page 58. Questions 1 through 5 are True/False questions testing the scope, function, and principles of ethics. Questions 6 through 10 test application of autonomy, justice, beneficence, nonmaleficence, and identifying conflicts of interest.
