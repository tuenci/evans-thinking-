    EN 原文Your instructor will probably assign chapters or partial chapters from the textbook to read before or after class. When given a reading assignment, do you read from the first page to the last page without stopping? How much of what you read do you remember? Using an efficient study system will help you understand and retain all the information that you read. A useful study system has these five essential steps:

    步骤 1：预习与全盘浏览 (Preview or Survey)

    EN 原文Before you start a reading assignment, preview or survey the assignment to get an idea of what it covers and to recall what you already know about the subject. Preview the chapter title, headings, subheadings, and terms or ideas in bold print or italics. Also, survey the learning objectives, key terms, introductory paragraph, boxes, key points, and the review questions at the end of the chapter. Previewing takes only a few minutes. Remember, previewing helps you become familiar with the material.

    CN 译文在正式精读某个章节之前，先花几分钟时间对该阅读任务进行全景预习与快速浏览，以建立整体知识框架，并唤醒大脑中已有的背景认知。浏览章节标题、各级小标题、黑体粗体字与斜体强调词。同时，仔细扫读本章的学习目标（Objectives）、核心术语表（Key Terms）、开篇导语、表格插框、核心要点（Key Points）以及章末的复习思考题（Review Questions）。预习只需耗时三至五分钟，但它能在大脑中建立起清晰的“认知地图”，让后续精读事半功倍。

    步骤 2：主动设问 (Question)

    EN 原文After previewing, you need to form a list of questions to be answered as you read the material. Questions should relate to what might be asked on a test or how the information applies to giving care. Use the title, headings, and subheadings to form questions. Avoid questions that have simple one-word answers. Questions that begin with what, how, or why are most helpful. While reading, if you find that a particular question does not help you understand and retain the material, change the question to make this step more useful.

    CN 译文完成预习浏览后，带着思考主动将章节各级标题转化为具体的问题清单。所拟定的问题应当紧密围绕两个核心维度：“考试可能会如何考察？”以及“在实际临床照护中我该如何应用此项知识？”。尽量避免能用单一词汇作答的浅层问题，而应当多使用“是什么（What）”、“如何操作（How）”以及“为什么（Why）”开头的深度思考题。在阅读过程中，如果您发现某个问题对理解核心操作并无助益，可随时调整设问，让思考更加聚焦临床实践。

    步骤 3：精读与主动记录 (Read and Record)

    EN 原文Reading, which is the next step, is more productive after you have determined what you already know and what you need to learn. The purpose of reading is to: - Gain new information - Connect the new information to what you already know

    Break the assignment into smaller parts, and as you read each part, try to find answers for the questions you had formulated earlier. Also, mark important information in the text by underlining, highlighting, or making notes, which will remind you later what you need to go back to in order to review and learn. Making notes helps you remember what you have learned. When making notes, write down important information in the text margins or in a notebook. Use words and summary statements that will jog your memory about the material.

    After reading the assignment, in order to retain the information, you need to organize it into a study guide—in the form of diagrams, flowcharts, or concept maps that show relationships or steps in a process. Note-taking in outline format is also very useful.

    CN 译文在明确了自身已知与未知之后，接下来的阅读过程将极富成效。专业精读的核心目的在于： - 获取全新的医学与照护理论及操作规程； - 将新知识与以往的医学常识及过往生命经验建立深层神经连接。

    将长章节分解为若干短小单元。在阅读每一小节时，专注寻找刚才所提问题的答案。同时，通过划线、高光或做旁注标记核心考点，以便日后复习时精准回溯。动笔做笔记能显著强化神经突触记忆。做笔记时，请用您自己的话语提炼浓缩性结论，记录在页边空白或专用笔记本中。

    读完一个章节后，为了长期保持记忆，建议将其整理为体系化的“学习指南”——例如绘制展示操作流程的前后步骤图、因果逻辑图或概念框架图。采用层级清晰的提纲式大纲（Outline Format）记录笔记同样极为高效： ` I. 一级主题大标题 (Main heading) 1. 二级分类标题 (Second-level heading) a. 三级要点与操作步骤 (Third-level heading) `

    步骤 4 与步骤 5：大声复述与周期复习 (Recite and Review)

    EN 原文Recite: Finally, recite and review, using your notes and the study guide, by finding the answers for the questions you formed earlier and any others that may have come up during the reading and as you answered the review questions at the end of the chapter. Answer all questions out loud (recite). If you are unsure about the answers to any of the questions, consult your instructor or re-check the text.

    Review: Reviewing is more about when to study than what to study. You already decided what to study during the preview, question, and reading steps. Your instructor may have emphasized key points from the reading assignment in class. The best times to review both the information in your text and your notes from class are: 1. The same day or evening of the class 2. Right after your first study session 3. One week later 4. Regularly before a quiz, test, midterm, or final exam

    Studying the information many times over spaced intervals will help transfer it into long-term clinical memory.

    CN 译文复述（Recite）： 借助您的提纲笔记与知识结构图，针对前面提出的问题以及章末的复习思考题，尝试脱离书本、大声口述回答（Recite）。用专业术语清晰、流利地解释照护原理与操作要领。口述能够立即暴露记忆中的模糊与漏洞。如果对某个操作步骤或病理概念无法清晰作答，必须立刻重翻书本核实，或向授课导师请教。

    通过这种“间隔重复（Spaced Repetition）”的方式反复回顾，医学知识才能真正化为您下意识的临床反应与肌肉记忆。

    二、 人本照护 DIPPS 五大基石原则 (DIPPS Core Principles)

    原则代号英文核心词 (Core Concept)中文标准译名临床照护实践指导要领 (Clinical Practice Meaning)
    DDignity维护尊严始终视服务对象为一个值得尊敬的完整人。杜绝将其当婴儿般对待（Baby Talk），操作前敲门礼貌称呼其尊称，充分聆听并接纳其情感。
    IIndependence促进独立自主鼓励服务对象尽最大可能自己动手完成力所能及的动作（哪怕动作极为缓慢），严禁照护者为了赶时间而剥夺其自主权。
    PPreferences尊重个人偏好允许并鼓励服务对象自主决定自己的日常事务，例如几点起床、今天想穿哪件衣服、想吃何种餐点等。

    三、 日常照护与日常生活活动能力 (Daily Living & Clinical Care)

    英文专业术语 (English Term)中文标准译名权威学术释义与临床内涵 (Definition & Clinical Context)
    Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)日常生活活动能力维持人类独立生活所必需的最基本自我照顾技能，主要包括：沐浴（Bathing）、穿衣修饰（Dressing）、进食（Eating/Feeding）、移动转移（Transferring/Mobility）、如厕（Toileting）及大小便控制（Continence）。
    Instrumental Activities of Daily Living (IADLs)工具性日常生活活动能力在复杂社会环境中独立生活所需的高阶复合能力，包括：做饭做餐、房屋日常清洁打扫、洗衣服、管理个人财务、遵医嘱服药、使用电话与交通工具、日常买菜购物。
    Care Plan (Nursing Care Plan)综合护理照护计划由医疗团队（主要由主管护士主导）评估服务对象的身心需求后制定的全面、个性化书面工作指引，详述其具体护理目标、干预措施、特殊注意事项及照护频次。
    Body Mechanics身体力学在进行各种身体动作、搬运与转移重物时，通过调整身体姿态、扩大支撑底面积（双脚分开与肩同宽）、降低重心、保持脊柱正常生理弯曲，从而最省力并防止背部与肌肉骨骼损伤的科学运动机制。
    Medical Asepsis (Clean Technique)内科无菌技术 / 清洁技术旨在减少微生物数量、阻断病原体从一个人传播到另一个人或环境中去的日常清洁与防感染措施（如手卫生、穿戴手套、环境表面消毒）。
    Surgical Asepsis (Sterile Technique)外科无菌技术旨在彻底消灭或清除某一区域或器械上所有微生物（包括细菌芽孢与病毒）的最高级别无菌操作规范。
    Standard Precautions / Routine Practices常规预防措施 / 标准预防针对所有服务对象普遍适用的基本防感染准则，基于“所有人的血液、体液、排泄物均具有潜在传染性”的假设，在接触时必须佩戴个人防护装备（PPE）。
    Restraints身体约束具 / 限制性措施用于限制、妨碍或阻碍服务对象自由身体移动的物理器械、衣物或化学药物。加拿大现代医疗倡导“无约束照护（Least-Restraint / Restraint-Free Environment）”。
    Responsive Behaviours应对行为反应失智症与认知障碍患者常出现的激越、徘徊、幻觉、日落综合征等行为。在加拿大护理学中不再使用“问题行为（Problem Behaviours）”，而是称为“应对行为”，强调其为患者应对内心恐惧或未被满足生理需求的外在反应。
    Palliative Care (End-of-Life Care)姑息照护 / 安宁疗护 / 临终关怀针对威胁生命的终末期疾病患者及其家属提供的一种整合性照护，其核心目标不是治愈疾病或盲目延长痛苦的生命，而是最大程度缓解疼痛与躯体不适，抚慰心理与灵性痛苦，提升临终生命质量。
    Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) ChecklistADL 评估考核清单临床评定患者独立程度的标准化量表（从完全自立、部分协助到完全依赖）。
    MAR (Medication Administration Record)药物给予记录单 / 用药单医疗机构中用于详细记录服务对象处方药名称、剂量、给药时间、途径及执行护士签名的法定法律文书。
    SBAR CommunicationSBAR 标准化沟通汇报模式临床医疗交班与病情恶化紧急汇报的国际通用结构化框架：Situation（现状）、Background（病史背景）、Assessment（评估发现）、Recommendation（处理建议）。

    第一节：支持工作的本质与崇高宗旨 (Pages 2 - 3)

    支持工作者的定义与服务对象 (What is a Support Worker & Client?)

    EN 原文Support workers provide care and assistance to clients of any age who have defined health care needs and who require help with their daily activities, whether they live in a facility or in the community. Client is a general term for any person receiving care or support services in a community setting, such as hospital patients, facility residents, and clients in the community.

    CN 译文支持工作者（Support Worker）为处于任何年龄段、具有明确医疗健康与照护需求，且在日常生活活动中需要协助的人士提供专业照料与支持服务，无论其身处机构环境还是社区之中。“服务对象”（Client）是对在各类照护场景中接受医疗照护或支持服务的所有个体的总称，涵盖医院中的患者（Patients）、照护机构中的院舍长者/居民（Residents）以及居住在社区家庭中的案主（Community Clients）。

    支持工作的终极目标 (The Ultimate Goal of Support Work)

    EN 原文The ultimate goal of support work is to improve the quality of life of the client and family by offering client-centred care and support in a safe, kind, sensitive, caring, and understanding manner. Support workers can make a difference in people's lives by alleviating loneliness, providing empathy, ensuring comfort, encouraging independence, and promoting the client's self-respect (FIGURE 1.1). They support the client and family in a holistic manner by addressing the client's physical, psychological, social, cognitive, cultural, and spiritual needs and advocate for the client by speaking or acting on the client's behalf.

    CN 译文支持工作的终极目标在于：通过以一种安全、仁善、敏锐、慈爱与理解的方式提供“以服务对象为中心的照护与支持”，全面提升服务对象及其家庭的生活质量。支持工作者通过排遣孤独、倾注同理心、确保身心舒适、鼓励自立自主并维护服务对象的自尊心，真正改变并丰盈人们的生命历程（参见图 1.1）。他们以“全人照护（Holistic Care）”的理念支持服务对象及其家庭，全面满足服务对象在身体生理、心理情绪、社交人际、智力认知、文化传统及精神灵性六大维度的多维需求，并在必要时代其发声或采取行动，忠实捍卫服务对象的合法权益。

    团队协作、执业规制与专业严谨性 (Teamwork, Legislation, and Professional Discretion)

    EN 原文Support workers may work as part of a health care team or work individually with a client. Legislation, employer policies, and a client's condition all influence how support workers function and how much supervision they need. They may collaborate with health care providers or with professionals outside of the health care sector, depending on the needs of their clients. Support workers must use discretion at all times and honour the client's right to confidentiality whenever discussing the client's case with co-workers. Support workers are expected to adapt their work style to the setting and to the needs and wishes of the client receiving care.

    CN 译文支持工作者既可以作为多学科医疗照护团队的一员协同工作，也可以一对一单独为某位服务对象提供照护。省级法律法规、雇主管理规章制度以及服务对象自身的健康状况，共同决定了支持工作者具体如何履行职责以及需要接受何种程度的临床督导。根据服务对象的实际需求，他们可能需要与专业医护人员协作，亦可能需要与医疗卫生体系之外的其他专业人士协作。在任何时候与同事探讨案情时，支持工作者都必须秉持高度的审慎明辨（Discretion），并恪守服务对象的隐私保密权（Right to Confidentiality）。支持工作者必须具备高度的专业灵活性，能够根据不同的工作环境以及受助个体的具体需求与意愿，敏锐调整自身的工作方式与服务风格。

    第二节：支持工作在加拿大全境的职业现状与场所 (Pages 3 - 4)

    加拿大各省职业称谓与现状差异 (Support Work Across Canada)

    EN 原文The nature of support work varies across the country. There are differences in educational programs, work settings, job responsibilities, and terms used to describe support workers. Some sections of this text may not apply to support work in your particular city, province, or territory. If you are unsure about which parts apply to your area, ask your instructor or employer.

    The term support worker refers to the worker who provides personal care and support services. However, assisted-living worker, personal support worker, personal attendant, patient care assistant, resident care aide, resident care attendant, health care aide, home care attendant, home support worker, nursing aide, nursing attendant, community health worker, or continuing care assistant—among other titles—may be used in your province or territory to describe similar (but not always equivalent) jobs. The Appendix at the end of this book lists the support worker's titles in each province and territory. Because of the wide variety of titles, the general public may not be aware of what support workers are called in their own province or territory or even within a facility they use. It is the role of support workers, their employing agencies, other health care providers, and their province's or territory's Ministry of Health to inform others of their role and how it fits in their current health care structure.

    This list may also include several titles that are not equivalent to one another. For example, in Ontario, developmental service workers (DSWs) play a different role and follow different educational standards than personal support workers (PSWs), and yet both are considered to be support workers within the health care team. In some parts of Canada, the term personal attendant refers to a worker who is supervised directly by the person for whom they provide support services; personal attendants support people with physical disabilities. Educational programs to become a personal attendant are generally shorter than those for support workers.

    CN 译文支持工作的性质在加拿大全境各省与地区之间存在一定差异。这种差异体现在培训教育大纲、工作场所类型、具体工作职责以及用于描述该职业的官方头衔上。本书中的某些章节可能并不完全适用于你所在的特定城市、省份或地区。如果你对自己所在地区适用的具体法规存在疑问，请及时向你的授课教师或雇主咨询。

    “支持工作者”（Support Worker）是一个统称，特指提供个人照护与支持服务的专业从业人员。然而，在各个省份或地区，可能会使用以下头衔来描述类似（但并非完全等同）的岗位：辅助生活护理员（Assisted-living worker）、个人支持工作者（Personal support worker, PSW）、私人照护员（Personal attendant）、病患照护助理（Patient care assistant）、院舍照护助理（Resident care aide）、院舍照护员（Resident care attendant）、医疗保健照料员（Health care aide, HCA）、居家护理员（Home care attendant）、居家支持工作者（Home support worker）、护理助理（Nursing aide）、护士助手（Nursing attendant）、社区卫生工作者（Community health worker）或持续照料助理（Continuing care assistant, CCA）等。本书文末的附录详细汇总列出了各个省与地区对该岗位的官方头衔。

    正因为头衔百花齐放，社会公众往往并不完全了解在自己所在的省份、地区乃至他们所使用的医疗机构中这些护理人员的正式称谓。支持工作者本人、其受雇机构、医疗团队中的其他医护人员以及各省和地区的卫生厅（Ministry of Health），均肩负着向社会大众普及该岗位职责及其在当前医疗卫生体系中定位的宣教责任。

    需要特别指出的是，上述列出的诸多头衔之间并不能直接划等号。例如在安大略省，“发育障碍服务工作者”（Developmental Service Worker, DSW）与“个人支持工作者”（PSW）承担不同的职责角色，遵循完全不同的教育培训标准，但两者均被视为跨学科医疗团队中的支持工作者。在加拿大的某些地区，“私人照护员”（Personal Attendant）特指直接由接受服务者本人进行管理与督导的工作人员；私人照护员专门为肢体残疾人士提供生活支持，其成为私人照护员的培训课程时长通常明显短于标准支持工作者。

    多元化工作场所与主管督导者的“眼睛和耳朵” (Workplace Settings & "Eyes and Ears")

    EN 原文Support workers work in a variety of settings (see Chapter 3 for definitions and details of workplace settings). In all settings, support workers ensure the safety of their clients, including those who are living with cognitive or mental health challenges. Services provided by support workers to clients in their homes can enable some clients to live independently or with their families. Support workers may also assist clients with social support, or social reintegration, as they prepare to move into an assisted-living facility or group home. In long-term care facilities, support workers assist clients with complex health challenges. Support workers also provide comfort and end-of-life care to dying clients so that they can die with dignity.

    They are often the supervisor's "eyes and ears," meaning that because they are the person who spends the most time with the client, they are most likely to observe or hear things that should be reported to the supervisor, who may be a nurse or another type of health care worker. Observing and reporting are very important parts of the support worker's role and can have a great impact on a client's care.

    Support workers may have to consult with other health care providers, such as social workers or physiotherapists, and may also have to consult with other professionals, such as the client's employers, clergy, or teachers, depending on the client's individual care plan.

    CN 译文支持工作者的执业场所极为广泛多样（有关各类工作场所的精准定义与详细规范，参见第 3 章）。在所有执业场所中，支持工作者都必须时刻筑牢服务对象的安全防线，特别是针对那些患有认知障碍或心理精神健康挑战的脆弱人群。支持工作者在案主家中提供的居家入户服务，能够赋能许多长者与病患在自己的家中独立生活或与家人共享天伦。当案主准备入住辅助生活设施（Assisted-living facility）或集体居所（Group home）时，支持工作者还会协助案主获得社交支持或实现社会功能重聚融入（Social reintegration）。在长期照护院舍（Long-term care facilities）中，支持工作者协助患有复杂重度健康挑战的长者；他们亦为处于生命弥留阶段的垂危病患提供安宁抚慰与临终关怀，确保长者能够怀抱尊严安详离世。

    支持工作者往往是临床督导护士的“眼睛和耳朵”（"Eyes and Ears"）：这意味着，由于支持工作者是与服务对象朝夕相处、陪伴时间最长的专业人员，他们最有可能第一时间观察到或听到需要向上级督导汇报的异常体征与病情变化——督导人员可能是注册护士或另一类专业医疗照护人员。敏锐的观察（Observing）与严谨的汇报（Reporting）是支持工作者角色中举足轻重的组成部分，对服务对象的照护质量与生命安全有着深远影响。

    根据每位服务对象个性化照护计划的具体要求，支持工作者在日常工作中可能需要与其他医护人员进行跨学科会商（例如社会工作者或物理治疗师），亦可能需要与医疗卫生体系之外的其他专业人士进行沟通协作（例如案主的受雇雇主、神职人员/牧师或学校教师）。

    第三节：支持工作者的六大核心工作职责 (Pages 4 - 5)

    支持工作者的六大职责范畴概述 (The Six Categories of Responsibilities)

    EN 原文A support worker's general responsibilities, depending on the jurisdiction in which they work, can be grouped into six categories: (1) personal care, (2) support for nurses and other health care providers, (3) family support, (4) social support, (5) housekeeping or home management, and (6) documenting and reporting any basic assessments, observations, and care provided in the client's record or chart.

    CN 译文根据支持工作者所执业的司法辖区（所在省份或地区），其日常通用工作职责大体可归纳为六大核心范畴： 1. 个人生活照料（Personal care）； 2. 协助护士及其他医疗卫生团队成员（Support for nurses and other health care providers）； 3. 家庭系统支持（Family support）； 4. 社交与人际支持（Social support）； 5. 家务清洁或家居管理（Housekeeping or home management）； 6. 在案主病历档案中记录并汇报所提供的基础评估、观察所见及照护措施（Documenting and reporting any basic assessments, observations, and care provided）。

    1. 个人生活照料：ADLs 与 IADLs (Personal Care: ADLs & IADLs)

    EN 原文Personal care responsibilities include assisting with activities of daily living (ADLs). These are the self-care actions that people perform every day to remain independent and to function effectively in society. Support workers help with daily activities such as eating, bathing, grooming, dressing, and toileting ("elimination"). They assist clients with limited mobility to change positions or move from one place to another and also help promote the client's safety and physical comfort.

    In community settings, support workers also assist clients with instrumental activities of daily living (IADLs), which are the complex and necessary skills needed to successfully live independently. These IADLs may include assisting the client with handling finances, assisting with management of medications, arranging transportation, shopping, preparing meals, assisting with using a telephone or other communication devices, and doing housework and basic home maintenance.

    The support worker is not responsible for deciding what should or should not be done for the client. However, while providing personal care, the support worker observes for and reports any changes in the client's behaviour or health. Such information is important to share with the appropriate person (either the charge nurse or the client's primary care nurse).

    CN 译文个人生活照护职责包括协助服务对象完成基础日常生活活动（Activities of Daily Living, ADLs）。这些是人们为了维持自立生存并在社会中正常行使机能而每天必须执行的基本自我照料行为。支持工作者协助完成诸如进食（Eating）、沐浴（Bathing）、梳理修饰（Grooming）、穿衣（Dressing）以及如厕排泄（Toileting / Elimination）等日常活动。他们协助活动受限的长者变换体位（Change positions）或在床、轮椅之间转移搬运，并全力促进长者的机体安全与生理舒适。

    在社区入户环境中，支持工作者还会协助案主完成工具性日常生活活动（Instrumental Activities of Daily Living, IADLs）。这些是维持在社区中独立生活所必需的复杂高阶生活技能，包括：协助案主管理日常财务收支、协助服药管理（按医嘱服药）、安排交通出行、外出采购食品杂物、烹饪配制健康餐食、协助使用电话或其他通讯设备，以及操持日常家务和基本房屋维护保养。

    支持工作者没有权力擅自决定应当为案主做什么或不该做什么（必须严格遵照医嘱与照护计划）。然而，在提供个人照护的过程中，支持工作者必须敏锐观察并及时汇报服务对象在行为表现或健康状态上的任何细微变化。将这些宝贵信息第一时间共享给对口负责人（病区主管护士 Charge Nurse 或案主的责任护士 Primary Care Nurse），对保障照护安全具有决定性意义。

    2. 协助护士与其他医护人员 (Support for Nurses and Other Providers)

    EN 原文Support workers assist nurses or other health care team members by following the established care plan for each client; for instance, they may be required to clean equipment, measure and report vital signs, or assist with simple wound care. Support workers might also assist with oxygen therapy, heat or cold applications, and range-of-motion (ROM) exercises.

    CN 译文支持工作者通过严格执行为每位服务对象量身定制的既定照护计划（Established Care Plan），全力协助护士及其他医疗团队成员；例如，他们可能会被要求清洗消毒医疗辅助器材、测量并记录汇报生命体征（体温、脉搏、呼吸、血压）、或协助进行简单无菌伤口换药照料。在经过专门技能培训并获授权委派的情形下，支持工作者还可能协助实施氧气吸入疗法（Oxygen therapy）、冷热敷物理治疗应用（Heat or cold applications）以及关节活动度康复训练（Range-of-motion / ROM exercises）。

    3. 家庭系统支持 (Family Support)

    EN 原文In many facilities, support workers assist with admissions and discharges by introducing the client and family to the facility and helping the client unpack and settle in. In private homes, support workers help families care for loved ones who are living with health issues or need assistance with ADLs. Some families may need help with preparing meals and doing household chores; other families may need help with child care. Support worker services often give family caregivers a break from their duties.

    CN 译文在诸多照护机构中，支持工作者通过向服务对象及其家属介绍机构的设施环境、帮助案主拆箱整理行李并安顿入住，积极协助办理入院（Admissions）与出院（Discharges）手续。在私人家庭中，支持工作者协助家庭照料患病或在日常生活活动中需要照护的挚爱亲人。有些家庭可能需要协助备餐与操持家务；有些家庭可能需要协助照料年幼儿童。支持工作者所提供的入户服务，往往能为长期处于高压状态下的家庭照护者（Family caregivers）提供极其珍贵的喘息休息机会（Respite break），防止家庭照料者身心崩溃。

    4. 社交与人际支持 (Social Support)

    EN 原文Support workers may help clients participate in social activities. These activities provide the client with enjoyment, recreation, and a chance to meet with friends. They may organize games and outings or may be hired privately to be a client's companion. Support workers may also be responsible for teaching clients to learn to live independently and to cook, clean, or shop by themselves.

    CN 译文支持工作者可以协助服务对象参与丰富多彩的社交活动。这些活动为长者带来身心愉悦、文娱享受以及与亲朋好友聚会交往的契机。他们可以组织益智桌游与户外远足郊游，亦可作为私人受雇的专业陪伴者（Companion）。支持工作者还可能负责对服务对象进行功能康复指导，培训他们重新掌握自立生活的技能，学会自己动手做饭烹饪、打扫清洁或独立去超市采购。

    5. 家务管理与家居环境维系 (Housekeeping or Home Management)

    EN 原文Support workers often do a variety of housekeeping tasks in a facility setting, including making beds, delivering meals, tidying up living areas, and maintaining supplies. In a private home, housekeeping is called home management. Services depend on the needs of the person and the resources available to provide these services. Duties may include assisting clients with their IADLs, doing light housekeeping, doing laundry, and preparing and serving nutritious meals.

    CN 译文在机构环境中，支持工作者经常执行一系列家务保洁任务，包括整理铺床更单、运送分发餐盘、整理收拾起居区域以及补充维护护理消耗用品。在私人家庭环境中，家务工作被称为“家居管理”（Home Management）。具体服务内容完全取决于案主的个体需求以及该家庭可调用的资源条件。日常职责可能包括：协助案主执行工具性日常生活活动、从事日常轻度家务清洁、洗涤晾晒与熨烫衣物，以及规划烹饪并端送符合膳食指南的营养餐食。

    6. 基础评估、观察记录与书面汇报 (Documenting and Reporting)

    EN 原文As part of their professional accountability, all health care workers are legally responsible for reporting and documenting any client care they provide, as well as basic assessments and important observations about their clients. Examples of basic assessments or observations include if the client complains of pain, or if they refuse to eat. Understanding what is important to report is part of the support worker's need to think critically (see page 13). Depending on the agency, documentation may be in a written or electronic format (see Chapter 26).

    The A Day in the Life boxes on pages 5-9 describe three support workers' typical workdays—in the community, in a long-term care facility, and in a hospital setting. In each box, the word nurse may mean registered nurse (RN), registered practical nurse (RPN), or licensed practical nurse (LPN), depending on the staffing policies of that particular agency.

    CN 译文作为其职业问责制（Professional Accountability）的法律组成部分，所有医疗卫生工作者对自己所提供的任何照护服务、所执行的基础临床评估以及对案主的重要观察所见，均承担严格的记录与汇报法律责任。基础评估或临床观察的典型范例包括：案主是否主动主诉疼痛、或者案主是否出现拒食不吃等反常征象。敏锐理解哪些信息属于必须紧急向上汇报的危急值与异常点，是支持工作者运用批判性思维的必备核心素养（参见第 13 页）。根据受雇机构的性质不同，文书记录既可以采用纸质手写表单，亦可采用电子病历系统（Electronic Format，详见第 26 章医学文书详解）。

    原书第 5 至 9 页所呈现的“支持工作者的一天”（A Day in the Life）真实案例模块，详尽生动地记录了三位支持工作者在不同临床场景下的典型一日实操记录——分别涵盖社区入户环境、长期照护院舍环境以及综合医院病区环境。在各个案例中，出现的“护士”（Nurse）一词根据具体机构的岗位编制政策，可能代表注册护士（RN）、注册实用护士（RPN）或执照实用护士（LPN）。

    受助人群的通用专业称谓 (People Who Receive Support Services)

    EN 原文People receiving health care and support services are known by different terms, depending on the setting. A person receiving care in a hospital is usually called a patient. A person living in a live-in facility is called a resident. A person receiving care or support services in the community is called a client. Client is also an all-encompassing term for people receiving health care or support services: hospital patients, facility residents, and clients in the community.

    Whether the individual receiving care is known as a client, patient, or resident, always remember that he or she is a person. Every person is unique, with unique life experiences and situations, desires and opinions. Each will have different needs and different abilities. Support workers work with people from a variety of cultures or backgrounds (see Chapter 7). Part of the support worker's job is to accept this diversity among people. The Respecting Diversity boxes that appear throughout this text outline the importance of diversity and how people's backgrounds influence who they are and what they do.

    CN 译文接受医疗保健与支持服务的人群，根据其所处的医疗照护场所不同，在专业上被冠以不同的称谓： - 在综合医院或急性照护机构中接受治疗护理的人士，通常被称为患者（Patient）； - 在全寄宿式照护机构（养老院、护理院、辅助生活院舍）中长住的人士，被称为院舍长者 / 居民（Resident）； - 在社区或私人家庭环境中接受支持照护服务的人士，被称为案主 / 客户（Client）； - 同时，“服务对象”（Client）也是一个统摄全局的尊称总称，泛指一切在医院、机构或社区中接受照护支持的个体。

    无论被照料的个体在术语上被称为案主、患者还是院舍长者，支持工作者都必须时刻铭记：他或她首先是一个活生生、有尊严的“人”（Person）。每一个人都是独一无二的，拥有独一无二的人生履历、生活境遇、内心愿望与观点立场。每个人都有截然不同的生理心理需求以及残存的机能水平。支持工作者需要与来自多元文化、不同种族、不同宗教与社会背景的人士共事（详见第 7 章多元文化照护）。包容并接纳人与人之间的文化多元性（Diversity），是支持工作者职业素养不可或缺的基石。全书中贯穿设立的“尊重多元文化”（Respecting Diversity）特色专栏，深刻阐释了多元文化包容的重要性，并剖析了文化背景如何深刻塑造个体的言行举止与价值取向。

    加拿大受助人群的九大核心类别 (Nine Categories of Clients Grouped by Issues, Needs, and Ages)

    EN 原文Clients can be grouped according to their issues, needs, and ages:

    - Older persons. Aging is a normal process and is not an illness or disease. Many older persons enjoy good health. However, body changes normally occur with the aging process. Social and emotional changes may also occur (see Chapter 43). The risks for contracting serious illnesses and becoming disabled increase with age. Most older persons remain at home as long as possible. Others are unable to manage even with assistance and move into a live-in facility. Throughout the text, issues relevant to older persons are discussed. - People with disabilities. Some people are living with disabilities due to illness, injury, or conditions present at birth. Disabilities may affect physical functioning, mental functioning, or both. Many adults with disabilities live in their own homes. Many work outside their homes. Support workers might help clients living with disabilities with their ADLs or might be responsible for teaching them how to perform the ADLs independently. - People with medical issues. Medical issues include illnesses, diseases, and injuries. These may include short-term (such as a broken bone), long-term (such as diabetes or multiple sclerosis), or progressive and life-threatening (such as some types of cancer) issues. - People having surgery. Surgical clients are those being prepared for surgery or who have recently had surgery. Preoperative care includes preparing the client for what to expect after surgery and addressing the client's fears and anxieties. Needs after surgery relate to relieving pain and discomfort, preventing complications, and helping the client adjust to body changes. People recover from surgeries in hospitals or in their homes. - People with mental health issues. Mental health issues range from mild to severe. Some people function normally but need help making decisions or coping with life stresses. Others are severely affected and need assistance with ADLs. - People needing rehabilitation. Rehabilitation is the process of restoring a person to the highest possible level of functioning through the use of therapy, exercise, or other methods. The person may need to regain functions lost due to surgery, illness, or accident. Some hospitals have special rehabilitation units. Many people receiving support at home and in long-term care settings require rehabilitation. - Children. When hospital care is needed, children are admitted to the pediatric unit. In some areas of Canada, support workers are hired to work in pediatric units (FIGURE 1.2). However, most support work for children occurs in community settings and long-term care facilities. Some children who receive care are living with physical or intellectual disabilities. Others need care because a parent has a medical challenge or has just had a new baby. The Focus on Children boxes and Chapter 42 discuss issues related to caring for children. - Birth parents and newborns. Complications and difficulties can occur at any time during pregnancy and even up to 6 to 8 weeks following childbirth. Some new birth parents need assistance with their own care or with their newborn's care. Most support work with birth parents and newborns takes place in the home (see Chapter 41). - People requiring special care. Some people who have serious and complex medical conditions need special care and equipment. Hospitals have special care units, including critical care units, coronary care units, kidney dialysis units, burn units, and emergency departments. In some areas of Canada, support workers are hired to work in these units. Support workers might transport people from one unit to another; take specimens to the lab; assist clients with bathing, feeding, mobility, or toileting; and assist other health care providers with special procedures. In some parts of Canada, support workers are not allowed to provide personal care to clients in unstable or critical conditions.

    CN 译文根据受助个体的健康问题、照护需求与年龄跨度，服务对象可清晰归纳为九大核心类别：

    - 老年人群（Older persons）：衰老是一个不可逆转的正常生理过程，其本身绝非一种病态或疾病。许多老年人身体康健、精神矍铄。然而，机体生理结构的衰退性改变会随着增龄自然发生，社会人际与心理情绪的变化亦往往随之而来（详见第 43 章老年照护）。随着年龄增长，罹患急慢性严重疾病与出现失能的风险显著上升。绝大多数长者都渴望尽可能长久地留在家中生活；一部分长者在即便有外力协助下仍无法安全维持自理时，才会选择入住寄宿型长照机构。全书各章节将对长者照护展开系统阐述。 - 残障 / 残疾人士（People with disabilities）：一部分群体因罹患重病、意外创伤或先天性发育异常而生活在残障状态之中。残疾可能损害其躯体运动机能、心理智力机能，或二者兼有。许多成年残障人士完全能够居住在自己的私人住所中，甚至正常外出工作。支持工作者可以协助残障案主完成其日常生活活动（ADLs），或承担康复训练职责，教会他们如何独立执行这些自我照护技能。 - 内外科医疗疾患人士（People with medical issues）：医疗疾患涵盖各种急性病、慢性疾病及外伤创伤。按病程可划分为短期突发性问题（如单处骨折）、长期慢性慢性病（如糖尿病、多发性硬化症），或是进行性恶化、危及生命的重症（如某些类型的晚期恶性肿瘤）。 - 围手术期外科手术患者（People having surgery）：外科手术患者包括正在接受术前准备的案主，或刚完成外科手术的术后病患。术前护理（Preoperative care）的核心在于让患者提前知晓术后康复流程、缓解其内心的恐惧与濒死焦虑；术后照护的重点则在于有效缓解疼痛与身体不适、积极预防肺栓塞及压疮等严重并发症，并协助患者逐步适应机体结构的术后改变。手术康复既可在医院病房内进行，亦可在患者家中展开。 - 心理与精神健康障碍人士（People with mental health issues）：心理精神健康问题的严重程度从轻微的情绪失调跨越到重度精神障碍。一部分案主在日常生活中能够维持正常社会机能，但在重大决策或应对生活极端压力事件时需要外力心理支持；另一部分严重受损的重症患者，则连最基本的进食穿衣等 ADLs 均需要全面协助。 - 需要康复治疗的人士（People needing rehabilitation）：康复（Rehabilitation）是指通过物理治疗、运动疗法、作业治疗或其他专科康复手段，将个体的身心功能恢复至其“所能达到的最高机能状态”的动态治疗过程。案主往往需要重新赢回因手术创伤、严重疾患或意外事故所丧失的肢体或言语功能。部分医院设立有专门的康复医学病区；在居家社区及长期照护机构中，大量长者与案主都需要持续接受康复支持。 - 儿童与儿科患者（Children）：当儿童患病需要住院救治时，会收治于医院儿科病房（Pediatric Unit）。在加拿大某些省份或地区，医院儿科病房会聘请支持工作者协助照料患儿（参见图 1.2）。然而，绝大多数针对儿童的支持服务发生在社区家庭或长期照护设施中。接受照护的儿童一部分患有先天性躯体或智力缺陷；另一部分儿童则是因为其父母突遭重病瘫痪，或家中刚刚迎来新生儿而需要协助照料大龄幼儿。全书设立的“聚焦儿童照护”（Focus on Children）专栏及第 42 章将对儿童照护展开深入剖析。 - 产妇与新生儿群体（Birth parents and newborns）：在整个妊娠期乃至产后 6 至 8 周的产褥期内，随时可能出现严重的产科并发症与育儿困难。一部分新手产妇在产后自我身体复原或新生儿哺乳抚育上亟需外力协助。支持工作者对产妇和新生儿的支持工作绝大部分在家庭环境中实施（详见第 41 章母婴照护）。 - 需要重症特级监护的特殊病患（People requiring special care）：患有极其严重且复杂的内外科病情的危重症个体，需要全天候严密监护与精密医疗设备支持。综合医院设立有一系列专科重症监护单元，包括重症监护病房（ICU/Critical Care Unit）、冠心病重症监护室（CCU）、血液透析室（Kidney Dialysis Unit）、烧伤救治病区（Burn Unit）及急诊抢救室（Emergency Department）。在加拿大某些省份，医院会聘请支持工作者在这些重症科室工作。支持工作者的职责可能包括：在科室之间安全转运重症病患、运送关键血标本至中心实验室、协助患者完成皮肤擦浴、喂食、体位移动或排泄，以及协助专科医生和护士开展复杂的专科诊疗操作。在加拿大某些管辖区，法律严格规定严禁支持工作者对处于生命体征极其不稳定或处于病危垂死状态下的患者独立提供个人照料。

    医疗卫生照护团队的本质 (The Health Care Team)

    EN 原文A team is a group of people working together toward a common goal. Health care teams include professionals with a variety of skills and knowledge who work together to meet the client's needs. Their goal is to provide quality care. Many professionals, including support workers, are involved in the care of one client. Which professionals are involved depends on the needs of the client.

    CN 译文团队是指一群为了达成共同目标而协同工作的人。医疗卫生照护团队汇聚了具备不同专业技能与知识背景的各类专业人士，他们彼此协作，全力满足服务对象的全面健康需求。他们的核心目标是提供高品质的临床照护（Quality Care）。在任何一位服务对象的照护流程中，往往都会有包括支持工作者在内的多位专业人员共同参与；具体有哪些专业人员介入，完全取决于服务对象的个体健康与照料需求。

    受监管专业人员与未受立法监管人员的法定界限 (Regulated and Unregulated Workers)

    EN 原文Health care professions are either regulated or unregulated. A regulated profession is self-governing. It has a professional organization called a college, which sets education and licence requirements. It also establishes the scope of practice, code of ethics, and standards of conduct for its members. In the case of a complaint about a member's conduct, the college investigates and, if necessary, disciplines members guilty of misconduct. Each regulated health care profession has legislation that details the roles and responsibilities of its members. Nursing is one of many regulated health care professions.

    Unregulated care providers (UCPs) are health care providers who perform clearly identified services under the direction and supervision of a client, family member, regulated health care provider, or employer. However, UCPs are not regulated through legislation and are not members of an organization or professional college that governs their role. While UCPs do not currently follow any official code of ethics, they must adhere to the codes of behaviour dictated by their employers (see Chapter 4, Box 4.1, for A Sample Code of Ethics for Support Workers, on page 51). At this point, support workers in most provinces and territories are considered to be UCPs.

    CN 译文医疗卫生保健行业中的工种被清晰划分为两大法定类别：受监管职业（Regulated Professions）与未受监管照护人员（Unregulated Care Providers, UCPs）。

    1. 受监管专业医护人员（Regulated Workers）： 受监管职业具备法定“行业自我管理权（Self-governing）”。每个受监管行业都依法设立有省级法定的专业行业公会（称为 College，如安大略省护士公会 CNO、内外科医师公会 CPSO 等），由公会统一步署该工种的教育准入大纲和执业发牌考试要求。行业公会负责制定其会员的法定执业范围（Scope of practice）、职业伦理准则（Code of ethics）以及执业行为规范（Standards of conduct）。一旦社会公众或患者投诉某位执业人员存在医疗过失或失当行为，行业公会有权依法介入调查；若查证属实，公会有权对违纪会员处以停牌、罚款、吊销执照或取消从业资格等纪律惩戒。每个受监管职业均有专门的省级法律法规（如《受监管健康职业法》RHPA）详尽规制其会员的权利与义务。护理专业（涵盖注册护士 RN、注册实用护士 RPN/LPN）便是最具代表性的受监管专业之一。

    2. 未受监管照护提供者（Unregulated Care Providers, UCPs）： 未受立法监管照护者是指在服务对象本人、家属、受监管医护人员（如督导护士）或受雇机构的管理与督导下，执行明确生活支持与照料任务的健康照料从业人员。然而，UCPs 并非由省级专门立法直接发牌监管，他们也不隶属于任何管理其从业资质的法定行业公会（College）。虽然目前绝大多数省份尚未为 UCPs 颁布强制性的法定职业伦理准则，但他们必须严格遵守受雇机构所制定的员工执业行为守则（Codes of Behaviour）（参见第 4 章第 51 页 Box 4.1《支持工作者职业伦理守则范本》）。在现阶段，加拿大绝大多数省份和地区的支持工作者（PSW / HCA）均属于未受监管照护人员（UCPs）。

    执业范围与严禁越权 (Scope of Practice and Limitations)

    EN 原文Support workers must understand what to do, what not to do, and the limit and extent of a health care worker's role as determined by their province's educational standards and employer's policies in order to protect clients from harm. The term scope of practice is not a copyrighted term, and it is not intended only for regulated professionals. Instead, it is used to identify what a support worker may and may not do in relation to client care.

    Never act beyond the legal limits of your role. Also, never perform a function or task that you have not been trained to do. If you perform a task that is outside these limits, you could harm a client and create serious legal problems for yourself and your employer.

    CN 译文为了切实保护服务对象免受人身伤害，支持工作者必须深刻理解自己“该做什么”、“绝不该做什么”，以及由其所在省份教育培训大纲和雇主管理规章制度所界定的职责边界与权限范畴。“法定执业范围”（Scope of Practice）并非受版权保护的专属词汇，它绝非仅适用于受监管的注册医护人员；恰恰相反，在全行业中，它被广泛用于精准界定支持工作者在照护案主时“被法律允许执行的操作”与“被法律严格禁止执行的行为”。

    绝不可越权执行超出你法定角色界限的任何操作！同时，绝对严禁执行你从未接受过正规院校临床技能培训的任何技术性任务！如果你胆敢越过这一红线擅自操作，你极有可能直接导致案主伤残甚至死亡，并为你自己以及你的受雇机构招致灾难性的重大法律诉讼追责与刑事责任。

    执业范围的三大决定性权威来源 (Three Sources of Scope of Practice)

    EN 原文The support worker's scope of practice in any province, territory, city, or municipality is determined from three important sources. Those sources include the following:

    1. Provincial/territorial educational standard of practice: Many provinces now follow either the national or provincial program standards, which dictate what support workers need to be taught in their educational programs. The support worker educational program includes information on the scope of practice for support work in that province or territory. Students will learn about the laws and the support worker's professional responsibilities in their part of the country. Students should ask their instructors if they require any clarification. (Please visit [Nova Scotia CCA Scope of Practice Document](http://www.novascotiacca.ca/DocumentViewer.aspx?elementId=106908&portalName=ha) to view an example of a scope of practice document for the province of Nova Scotia.) 2. Employer's policies: Every employer has written policies that establish what can and cannot be done by support workers. These policies should be read carefully before starting work. 3. Supervisor: On the job, the supervisor is the best source of clarification of the employer's policies. It is far better to ask for direction than risk harming a client, so never hesitate to ask questions or request clarification about any procedure.

    CN 译文在任何省份、地区、城市或市镇，支持工作者的执业范围均由以下三大核心支柱权威决定： 1. 各省/地区官方教育大纲与执业标准（Educational Standards）：加拿大许多省份目前均严格遵循国家级或省级教学大纲标准，明确规制院校在支持工作者培训项目中必须教授的理论与操作技能。学生将在学习中系统掌握所在地区的法律法规及法定职责界限。若有任何疑问，学生应当主动向教师请教。（读者可参阅加拿大新斯科舍省继续照料助理 CCA 官方执业范围文件范本）。 2. 雇主机构的管理规章制度（Employer's Policies）：每一家正规医疗机构或社区机构都拥有详尽的书面规章制度，黑纸白字地明确界定该机构内支持工作者被允许执行与严禁涉足的任务清单。在正式上岗提供照护前，必须逐条研读这些制度。 3. 临床主管督导（Supervisor）：在临床第一线，督导护士是解答与澄清雇主制度疑难的最权威来源。主动向上级护士请教指导，其安全价值远远高于冒险违规操作而致案主于险境！因此，在对任何临床操作的合规性产生哪怕一丝疑虑时，绝不可盲目臆断，必须毫不犹豫地向上级请示。

    职业素养提升、终身学习与反思性执业回顾 (Professional Development and Lifelong Learning)

    EN 原文Being a professional requires critical thinking and reflective practice review. Critical thinking is the ability to think clearly and logically, using reflection and reason, based on knowledge obtained from experience, observation, or education. A reflective practice review is a self-evaluation of care provided with the goal of identifying ways to improve clinical performance and caregiving.

    Health care is an ever-changing field. It is the responsibility of every professional in the field to ensure that all clinical skills and knowledge are up to date, since their own health and safety and the health and safety of co-workers and clients are at stake. Depending on the region, many postgraduate certificate courses and workshops may be available to support workers, such as advanced palliative care, conflict management courses, or medication management. Support workers also need to be aware of the scope of practice in their province or territory, since some, in particular situations, have been asked to perform tasks that are not within the support worker's scope.

    CN 译文成为一名真正的专业从业者，离不开临床批判性思维与持续的反思性执业回顾。批判性思维（Critical Thinking）是指立足于从实践经验、敏锐观察或正规教育中获取的专业知识，运用反思与理性逻辑进行清晰、严密推理思考的心智能力。反思性执业回顾（Reflective Practice Review）是指从业人员在完成临床照护后对自己所提供的服务进行自我剖析与自省评估，其终极目标在于发现自身不足并找出持续优化临床执业表现与照护技巧的具体途径。

    医疗保健是一个日新月异、知识技术快速迭代的领域。确保自身的临床技能与前沿照护知识时刻保持在最新状态，是该领域中每一位从业人员神圣不可推卸的职业责任——因为这不仅直接关乎服务对象的安危，亦直接牵涉到同事及支持工作者自身的健康与人身安全。根据所在地区不同，许多针对支持工作者的毕业后进修认证课程和专题工作坊可供深造选修，例如高级安宁舒缓疗护（Advanced Palliative Care）、职场冲突管理（Conflict Management）或药物协助管理（Medication Management）等。支持工作者还必须时刻保持法律敏锐度，时刻清晰知晓本省执业范围红线，因为在某些临床特殊情境下，个别机构或人员可能会违规要求支持工作者去执行并不在其法定执业范围内的危险技术性操作。

    支持工作者的临床督导机制 (The Supervision of Support Workers)

    EN 原文In facilities, in the community, or in private homes, support workers may be supervised by a nurse or other health care provider. A registered nurse (RN) is licensed and regulated by the province or territory to maintain overall responsibility for the planning and provision of client care. Some RNs have university degrees and even postgraduate education. Others have community college diplomas. RNs assess clients, make nursing diagnoses, develop care plans, and implement and evaluate nursing care. They also carry out physicians' orders. An RN is usually a team leader of the health care team, which includes RPNs or LPNs (see below), support workers, and other allied health care providers.

    A licensed practical nurse (LPN), also known as a registered practical nurse (RPN), is licensed and regulated by the province or territory to carry out basic nursing procedures and provide client care. RPNs and LPNs have a community college diploma, although many also have university degrees and even postgraduate education. Like RNs, they must hold a current nursing registration in the province that they practise in. RPNs or LPNs function in a decision-making position when caring for stable clients with uncomplicated health issues, but when providing care to clients with serious and unstable health issues, they assist RNs and help with complex procedures. RPNs and LPNs often supervise support workers.

    Some clients, particularly in a live-in environment, may be assigned to a primary care nurse, who is responsible for the ongoing management of the health of a client. Duties of this nurse include liaising with other health care team members, the client, and her or his family. In an acute-care environment, the primary care nurse may or may not be directly involved in providing the client care on a daily basis but will oversee the work of those who do, including the support worker.

    In some situations, a support worker may be supervised by a professional other than an RN, RPN, or LPN. For example, in the recreation department of a long-term care facility, support workers may report to a recreational therapist. In the community, a supervisor may be a social worker, a physiotherapist, or another health care provider.

    Some support workers are hired and supervised directly by clients. These support workers must be particularly aware of provincial or territorial legislation that limits the tasks and procedures they can perform.

    CN 译文无论是在院舍机构、社区中心还是私人家庭中，支持工作者通常都在护士或其他专业医护人员的督导下开展工作： 1. 注册护士（RN）督导：注册护士由省或地区法定发牌监管，对服务对象整体照护计划的制定与实施承担全面法律责任。RN 通常拥有四年制护理学士学位甚至研究生学历；他们负责全面评估案主病情、下达护理诊断、制定并动态调整照护计划、组织实施并评价护理成效，并严格执行医师医嘱。RN 通常是跨学科医疗团队的领队，统筹指挥 RPN/LPN、支持工作者及其他相关辅助医护人员。 2. 注册实用护士（RPN / LPN）督导：RPN/LPN 同样由省或地区依法发牌监管，具备公立学院大专文凭。他们与 RN 一样必须持有本省有效的年度注册护士执照。当面对病情稳定、预后明朗的慢性病案主时，RPN/LPN 处于独立的临床决策地位；而在照护病情凶险不稳定的危重患者时，他们协助 RN 并配合完成复杂操作。在长期照护院舍等机构中，RPN/LPN 经常直接担任支持工作者的现场主管护士。 3. 责任制护士（Primary Care Nurse）：在院舍或急重症环境中，案主常被指定由一名责任护士负责其长期的全面健康管理，负责与团队成员及家属保持全方位联络。在急性照护环境中，责任护士虽然不一定事必躬亲地处理每项基础擦洗，但负责全面监督一线护理人员（包括支持工作者）的实际操作质量。 4. 非护士专业人员的跨界督导：在某些特殊岗位上，支持工作者的主管可能并非护士。例如在养老院康乐文娱部，支持工作者可能直接向康乐治疗师（Recreational Therapist）汇报；在社区环境中，督导人员可能是注册社会工作者、物理治疗师或其他专业医护人员。 5. 案主本人直接雇佣与自主督导：一部分支持工作者是由案主本人（如高位截瘫但智力清晰的年轻残疾人）直接出资雇佣并由案主自行管理的私人护理员。在此类情形下，支持工作者更必须保持高度的法律警惕性，清醒界定本省限制未受监管人员执行的技术性医疗禁区，严防盲从案主要求而触犯法律底线。

    什么是真正的专业素养？(Being a Professional & Positive Attitude)

    EN 原文Professionalism is an approach to work that demonstrates respect for others, commitment, competence, and appropriate behaviour. Being cheerful and friendly, keeping work schedules, performing tasks competently, and being helpful are all part of a professional approach. To be a true professional, you must demonstrate the following:

    - Positive attitude: You need to show a good attitude about your job. The work you do is very important. People rely on you to give efficient care and support. You need to believe that you and your work are valuable and show that you enjoy your work. Be enthusiastic, considerate, and courteous.

    CN 译文专业素养（Professionalism）是一种在职业活动中充分展现对他人尊重、高度敬业承诺、精湛操作胜任力以及端庄合宜言行举止的工作方式与精神境界。保持乐观开朗与亲切友善、严格守时遵守排班、精益求精胜任操作任务、乐于助人并展现团队协作，皆是专业职业素养的具体体现。要成为一名名副其实的专业人士，你必须在工作中践行以下准则： - 积极阳光的职业态度（Positive attitude）：你必须对自己的本职工作展现出发自内心的积极态度。你所从事的支持工作对他人生命意义非凡，长者与病患完全依赖你所提供的高效照护与心理支撑。你必须坚信自己与自己的工作具有崇高的社会价值，并由衷热爱这份事业。始终保持热情、体贴与温良有礼。

    第十节：以服务对象为中心的照护与 DIPPS 核心原则 (Pages 16 - 17)

    以服务对象为中心的核心内涵与 DIPPS 六大基石 (Client-Centred Care & DIPPS Acronym)

    EN 原文THE GOAL OF SUPPORT WORK: IMPROVED QUALITY OF LIFE THROUGH CLIENT-CENTRED CARE

    Client-centred care (also known as compassionate or person-centred care) or caring means having concern for the dignity, independence, preferences, privacy, and safety of clients and their families at all times. It is important that any communication with or care given to a client be individualized for that client. The goal of support work is to demonstrate true client-centred care by following this principle and treating clients with kindness, honesty, sensitivity, comfort, discretion, respect, and understanding. Many people who require support are coping with serious illness, disability, personal issues, or challenges, and they depend on the care given to them for survival and improved quality of life. The acronym DIPPS is a reminder of the six principles of client-centred care—Dignity, Independence, Individualized care, Preferences, Privacy, and Safety—which is the goal of support work:

    - To preserve their dignity: Dignity is the state of feeling worthy, valued, and respected. People need to feel dignified. - To live independently: Clients need to do what they can for themselves. - To have all care individualized for each client: No person is identical to another person, so care should be personalized to each client. - To express their preferences: Clients need to make choices and explain how they want to have things done. - To preserve their privacy: Clients need to know that their bodies and their affairs are treated respectfully and protected from public view (FIGURE 1.4). - To be safe from harm: Clients need to live in an environment that is as hazard free as possible. They also need to feel secure about the care provided.

    When well and able-bodied, most people take the fulfillment of the six needs listed above for granted. When they are living with disabilities or suffer serious illnesses, however, these needs may be more difficult to fulfill. Those who rely on others for personal care may worry about losing their dignity, and they may not feel free to express their wishes. For example, clients living in long-term care facilities may have to eat what is provided and socialize only at prearranged times. They may share a room with another client and find that private moments are rare. Safety concerns are serious issues for clients with illnesses or disabilities. For example, they may worry about reaching the bathroom without falling.

    Clients with illnesses or disabilities do not all have the same needs. However, most have at least some of the needs just discussed. To help you recognize clients' needs, Providing Client-Centred Care boxes throughout the text discuss the goal of support work, which is to follow the principles of client-centred or compassionate care when performing procedures with clients (BOX 1.4).

    CN 译文支持工作的终极崇高宗旨：通过“以服务对象为中心的人本关怀”全面改善生活品质！

    “以服务对象为中心的照护”（Client-centred care，亦被称为慈爱关怀 Compassionate care 或以人为本的人性化照护 Person-centred care）的核心内涵在于：在任何时候，对长者及其家属的人格尊严、自立自理能力、个性化偏好、隐私权及生命安全始终怀抱深切真诚的关切。支持工作者与案主的每一次沟通交谈、所实施的每一项具体照护操作，都必须针对该个体的独特情况实现高度的“个性化定制”（Individualized）。支持工作的终极崇高使命，就是通过秉持这一原则，以仁善、诚实、敏锐、舒适、克制、尊重与深刻理解去善待每一位长者。许多需要支持照料的人士都在苦苦抵御重病折磨、严重残障或人生重大危机；他们完全依赖我们所给予的照料来维系生存并重获生命的尊严。

    首字母缩略词 DIPPS 是时刻警醒我们践行以服务对象为中心照护的六大根本伦理原则——即尊严、自立、个性化、偏好、隐私与安全： 1. 捍卫人格尊严（Dignity）：尊严是个体感受到自身有价值、受重视与受尊敬的精神心理状态。任何人都需要活得有尊严； 2. 激发自立生活（Independence）：案主需要尽其最大可能亲手完成自己尚有能力完成的事情，绝不包办替代； 3. 全程个性化定制（Individualized care）：世界上没有任何两个人是完全相同的，因此照护措施绝不可千篇一律，必须因人而异量身定制； 4. 尊重自主偏好与选择权（Preferences）：案主拥有自主做出选择并明确表达他们希望如何被照护的绝对权利； 5. 神圣捍卫身体与生活隐私（Privacy）：案主需要切实体会到自己的身体躯干与个人私人事务受到极度尊重，严防暴露在公众视线之下（参见图 1.4）； 6. 远离伤害确保安全（Safety）：案主需要在尽可能零危险隐患的环境中生活，且对所接受的照护怀有充分的安全信任感。

    在身体健全健康时，大多数人往往将这六项基本生存需要的满足视作理所当然；然而，一旦身患残疾瘫痪或遭遇重病打击，这些基本需要的满足便会变得异常艰难。那些连擦洗如厕都不得不依赖他人协助的长者，时刻深陷在丧失人格尊严的巨大恐慌之中，他们往往不敢自主表达内心的真实意愿。例如，长住长期照护机构的老人往往只能吃配餐提供的固定食物，只能在规定时间参加活动，往往需要与陌生病友共居一室而几乎毫无私密空间。跌倒等安全顾虑更是心腹大患——许多长者每天都在恐惧自己独自下床去洗手间时会不会再次跌倒骨折。

    具体必须严格执行以下十步标准化行动程序： 1. 操作前必核对护理计划（Check Care Plan）：在开始执行任何操作前，必须首先仔细查阅案主的个性化护理计划，全面熟悉其个性化需求、个人偏好及安全注意事项； 2. 进门前必须先敲门（Knock Before Entering）：进入案主居室前务必先轻声敲门，等待应允后再进入； 3. 亲切尊称姓名并主动自报家门（Address by Name & Introduce Yourself）：使用其偏好的尊称称呼长者，主动报出自己的全名及职业头衔（如：“李奶奶您好，我是您的支持工作者王明”）； 4. 操作前及操作全过程务必详细解释（Explain the Procedure）：在开始前详尽解释要做什么，并在操作全过程中解释每一步正在做什么。获得充分知情的长者通常更乐意主动配合，且更不易因惊恐而产生抗拒抵抗反应；因此，能够极大规避长者自伤或弄伤护理员的职业暴露风险； 5. 操作前必须获得长者知情同意（Obtain Consent）：在实施任何身体接触与照护操作前，必须首先征得长者的知情同意； 6. 依从文化多样性个性化施护（Respect Diversity）：确保所有照护细节均尊重每位长者的文化、信仰及个人生活习惯的多样性； 7. 全过程捍卫人权与隐私权（Protect Rights & Privacy）：在整个操作过程中严守隐私，拉合床帘、关闭房门，操作中仅暴露正在清洗或治疗的身体部位，严防身体裸露； 8. 评估自理能力并鼓励自主参与（Promote Independence）：精准评估长者残存的自理功能，鼓励并引导长者自己动手做力所能及的动作，维系其最大限度的自立机能； 9. 轻柔操作并严守安全规范（Handle Gently & Observe Safety）：动作轻柔敏捷，时刻遵守人体工效学与安全规范，锁定轮椅与床刹，将长者与自身的损伤风险降至最低； 10. 严格执行标准感染控制规程（Follow Infection Control Practices）：严格落实手部卫生、规范佩戴手套与防护用品，彻底切断病原体交叉传播途径。

    临床批判性思维与问题解决的四大决策支柱 (Critical Thinking & Four Pillars of Problem-Solving)

    EN 原文CRITICAL THINKING, DECISION MAKING, AND PROBLEM-SOLVING

    Support workers think critically and make many decisions in the course of a workday. For example, they estimate the time each task will take and plan the best way to complete their work on time, taking into account the various needs of the client. Many decisions involve critical thinking in order to solve problems.

    When solving problems, consider the following: - The goal of support work: Solutions to problems should not compromise the six principles of client-centred care: dignity, independence, individualized care, preferences, privacy, and safety, or DIPPS. - The client's viewpoint: Involve clients in solving problems that concern them. Examine the problem from the client's point of view. - Scope of practice: Learn and observe the rules of your workplace. Know the limits of your role. - Supervisor's viewpoint: Decide if the problem is one that you can handle on your own or one that your supervisor should handle. Your supervisor should provide guidance about which problems you can deal with on your own.

    CN 译文临床批判性思维、临床决策与照护问题解决

    在每天的工作流程中，支持工作者无时无刻不在运用批判性思维做出大量的临床决策。例如，在兼顾服务对象多维需求的前提下，合理预估每项护理任务所需的时间跨度，并科学规划出确保在排班时间内高质量完工的最优工作路径。许多日常决策都必须依托批判性思维来解决错综复杂的现场问题。

    在面对临床难题进行问题解决与决策时，支持工作者必须立足于以下四大核心支柱进行系统考量： 1. 始终对齐支持工作的终极宗旨（The Goal of Support Work）：任何问题的解决方案，都绝不可牺牲或损害“以服务对象为中心”的 DIPPS 六大根本基石（尊严、自立、个性化、偏好、隐私与安全）。 2. 充分站在服务对象的立场视角（The Client's Viewpoint）：只要该问题涉及长者自身，就必须主动邀请长者共同参与探讨解决；必须设身处地从长者的视角与切身利益来审视审判问题。 3. 严守法定执业范围红线（Scope of Practice）：熟练掌握并严格遵守所在机构的规章制度与省级法律界限，清醒知晓自己角色的权力极限，严禁越权擅断。 4. 深刻理解上级督导的专业视角（Supervisor's Viewpoint）：清醒辨析该问题究竟是属于自己有权独立现场解决的范畴，还是属于必须立即上报交由主管护士介入决策的范畴。主管督导护士应当就哪些问题支持工作者可以自主处置、哪些问题必须立即上报提供清晰的指引规范。

    核心知识要点总结 (CHAPTER REVIEW · KEY POINTS)

    EN 原文CHAPTER REVIEW · KEY POINTS - The ultimate goal of support work is to improve the client's quality of life by following the principles of client-centred care (DIPPS). The support worker tends to the person's needs, which include physical needs as well as helping to relieve loneliness, providing comfort, encouraging independence, and promoting the person's self-respect. Care should always be individualized to each client. A support worker's services to people in their homes help them remain independent and continue to live with their families. Support workers make a difference in people's lives. - Support workers can work in community-based or facility-based settings. Duties will vary according to the setting. - Most of a support worker's responsibilities can be grouped into six categories: (1) personal care, (2) support for nurses and other health care providers, (3) family support, (4) social support, (5) housekeeping or home management, and (6) documenting and reporting any basic assessments, observations, and care provided in the client's record or chart. - Depending on their setting, people receiving health care and support services are known by different terms, such as patient (in a hospital), resident (in a live-in facility), or client (in the community). - The people supported can be grouped according to their problems, needs, and ages. - At this point, support workers in most provinces and territories are still considered health care providers not regulated through legislation, or unregulated care providers (UCPs). Support workers are accountable to their supervisors, employers, and clients; they do not presently have an organization or college that governs their role. Regulated care providers are health care providers licensed and regulated by the province or territory to carry out care that is within their scope of practice. - There are many members of the health care team, with the client always at the centre of care. - It is important that support workers understand their scope of practice and never act beyond their role. - In many settings, support workers are supervised by nurses or other professionals. - Professionalism includes having a positive attitude, a sense of responsibility, a professional appearance, being discreet about client information, being a lifelong learner, advocating for the client, being discreet about personal matters, and using acceptable speech and language. - While discussing the client with co-workers, support workers must use discretion at all times and honour the client's right to confidentiality. Confidentiality means respecting and guarding personal and private information about another person. - Part of being a professional, reflective practice reviews consist of a review and self-evaluation of any care provided and identification of ways to improve clinical performance. - When thinking critically, making decisions, and solving problems, support workers must consider the principles of client-centred care (DIPPS), the client's viewpoint, their scope of practice, and their supervisor's viewpoint.

    CN 译文第 1 章核心知识要点权威回顾（KEY POINTS）： 1. 支持工作的终极使命：支持工作的根本宗旨在于践行 DIPPS 六大人本原则以提升长者的生命品质。支持工作者全方位满足长者的身心需求，排遣孤独、倾注温情抚慰、激发自主自立、维护人格尊严。照护必须全程个性化。入户服务能赋能长者在熟悉的家中自立生存，支持工作者真正改变了他人的生命轨迹。 2. 执业场所多样性：支持工作者既可执业于社区环境亦可执业于院舍机构，具体职责随场所特性动态调整。 3. 六大核心职责体系：(1) 个人生活照料；(2) 协助护士及其他医护人员；(3) 家庭系统支持；(4) 社交人际支持；(5) 家务管理；(6) 基础评估、观察所见与照护记录书面汇报。 4. 受助人群的三大称谓：医院中称为患者（Patient），全寄宿机构中称为院舍长者（Resident），社区家庭中称为案主（Client），通称为服务对象。 5. 受助人群九大分类：可依据健康问题、多维需求与年龄跨度清晰归纳为九大核心类别。 6. 未受监管照护者（UCPs）法定属性：目前在绝大多数省份支持工作者属于 UCPs，向督导、雇主及案主问责，暂无专门法定行业公会直接发牌管理；而受监管专业人员（如护士、医生）由省级专业公会直接发牌规制。 7. 以案主为中心的跨学科团队：医疗团队汇聚多学科专业力量，但服务对象始终处于整个照护体系的正中央核心。 8. 执业范围红线原则：深刻领悟所在省份教育标准与雇主制度所界定的执业范围（Scope of Practice），绝不擅自越权。 9. 多层级督导机制：在绝大多数场景下，支持工作者接受注册护士（RN）或实用护士（RPN/LPN）等专科人员的临床督导。 10. 专业素养八大支柱：专业素养涵盖积极阳光态度、高度责任心、专业规范仪表、案主信息保密审慎、终身学习、敢于为长者发声、将个人私事完全排除于职场外、文明合宜的语言谈吐。 11. 信息隐私保密权（Confidentiality）：与同事探讨案情时必须严守保密义务，尊重并全力捍卫服务对象的一切个人健康与私人隐私信息。 12. 反思性执业回顾（Reflective Practice Review）：作为专业素养的核心，从业者必须定期对自身实施的各项照护进行批判性自省评估，确保持续精进临床操作品质。 13. 临床决策四大维度：在运用批判性思维解决问题时，必须综合平衡 DIPPS 人本照护宗旨、案主切身视角、法定执业范围以及上级督导的专业指引。
