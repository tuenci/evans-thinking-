p. 1056 Key Terms: Core Pharmacology & Medication Concepts

Medications are drugs and other substances that may be prescribed for and taken by people in order to prevent or treat diseases or illnesses, or to obtain various desired results in the body. Many clients living in community settings engage in self-directed medication management, meaning that they take their medications independently. As a support worker, however, you may be responsible for managing a client’s medications, usually by assisting them with taking medications (see the Supporting Mr. Whitehill: Assisting a Client With Taking Medications box). Clients in a variety of situations and settings may need this kind of help, for different reasons.

药物（Medications）是人们为预防、治疗疾病或在机体内达成特定生理治疗目标而经由处方或自行服用的活性化学物质。在社区居家环境中，许多长者和康复期人士能够独立进行自主药物管理（Self-directed medication management）。然而，作为支持人员，你经常需要承担协助客户管理药物的职责，主要表现为‘协助服药’（见‘协助怀特希尔先生服药’案例专栏）。处于不同生理病理状态和生活环境的客户，出于各异的原因均可能需要此类专业协助。

p. 1057 The Support Worker's Role: Assisting vs. Administering Medications

THE SUPPORT WORKER’S ROLE IN MEDICATION MANAGEMENT

支持人员在药物管理中的职责边界与准则

As a support worker, your role in medication management is strictly defined by provincial legislation, employer policy, and each client’s individual care plan. In most community and facility settings, the support worker ASSISTS the client with taking medications. Assisting with medications means you help the client to self-administer medications that have already been prepared and measured.

作为支持人员，你在药物管理中的角色与行为边界由所在省份的法律法规、雇主机构规章以及每位客户的个性化护理计划严格界定。在绝大多数社区居家和长期照护机构中，支持人员的职责是‘协助服药’（Assisting with medications）。协助服药的本质是帮助客户自我服用已经由药剂师、护士或家属预先准备和准确计量的药物。

PRACTICE GUIDELINES · 执业常规

Typical Tasks in Assisting With Medications / 协助服药的常规职责范畴

  • Reminding the client to take a medication: Prompting the client at the scheduled time indicated on the care plan.
    按时提醒服药：依据护理计划规定的用药时间节点，及时温和提醒客户服药。
  • Bringing medication containers to the client: Bringing the original labelled pill container, blister pack, or dosette directly to the client.
    取送药物容器：将带有原装处方标签的药瓶、泡罩包装（水泡包）或分药盒直接递送至客户手中。
  • Reading the label: Reading the medication label aloud to the client to confirm drug name and dose.
    宣读标签：大声向客户宣读药瓶标签上的药名、规格与用法，供客户核实。
  • Opening containers: Loosening tight child-proof caps or peeling blister foils for clients with severe arthritis or physical weakness.
    开启药盒包装：为类风湿关节炎、肌力衰退或手指震颤的客户拧开紧固的儿童安全瓶盖或剥开锡箔纸。
  • Providing water and positioning: Providing a glass of water, placing the client in high-Fowler’s position, and ensuring the client has swallowed the medication.
    协助坐位与温水：协助客户采取高半坐卧位（防误吸），递上充足温水，并在旁确认药物已完全吞服。
  • Documenting and reporting: Recording on the Medication Administration Record (MAR) and reporting any refusal, difficulty swallowing, or adverse reactions immediately.
    签字记录与异常报告：在给药记录单（MAR）上签字留痕；遇拒服、吞咽呛咳或可疑不良反应立即向主管汇报。
CASE STUDY · 临床案例

Supporting Mr. Whitehill: Assisting a Client With Taking Medications / 协助怀特希尔先生安全服药

Mr. Whitehill, 78, has Parkinson’s disease and experiences severe tremors and rigidity in his hands. He is mentally alert and understands his medication regimen, but cannot physically open his pill bottles or pick up small pills without dropping them. As his support worker, you bring him his water, check his MAR, read the labels, and open the container. Mr. Whitehill takes the pill from the cap and swallows it with water. You observe him swallowing and document on his MAR. You have assisted Mr. Whitehill with taking his medications while respecting his autonomy and dignity.

怀特希尔先生（78岁）患有帕金森病，双手伴有严重的静止性震颤与齿轮样肌强直。老人的神智极为清醒，对自身的用药方案了如指掌，但在肢体上却完全无法自行拧开药瓶盖或捏取细小药片（极易掉落遗失）。作为他的支持人员，你为老人倒来温水、核对 MAR 单、大声宣读药名标签并帮他拧开瓶盖。怀特希尔先生用颤抖的手从瓶盖中取过药片，在温水协助下顺利吞服。你全程确认其完全咽下并在 MAR 单上签字。这一规范流程既提供了安全保障，又最大限度维护了长者的自主性与人格尊严。

p. 1058 Medication Packaging: Blister Packs & Dosettes

MEDICATION PACKAGING SYSTEMS

常见药物包装系统：泡罩包（水泡包）与多格式药盒

To enhance medication safety and reduce dosing errors, pharmacies and clients use specialized packaging systems in community settings. The two most common packaging methods are blister packs and dosettes.

为了提升用药安全性并降低社区居家环境中的服药差错，药房和客户普遍采用标准化的专门包装系统。其中最常见的两种形式是泡罩包（水泡包）与多格式分药盒。

FIGURE 45.1: Blister (bubble) packs are supplied by the client’s pharmacy. Each transparent plastic bubble contains the pre-measured dose for a specific time and date. (Photo credit: Can Stock Photo Inc./Wavebreakmedia)

Blister packs (also called bubble packs, FIGURE 45.1) are pre-packaged by pharmacists. Each blister contains the exact tablets or capsules scheduled for a specific day and time (e.g., Monday Morning, Monday Noon). Blister packs prevent double dosing, make it immediately visible if a dose was missed, and maintain medications in a clean, sealed environment.

泡罩包装（Blister packs / 水泡眼药包，图 45.1）由注册药剂师在药房无菌调配封装。每个透明小泡内装有在特定日期与时间节点（如‘周一早晨’、‘周一中午’）所需服用的全部准确药片或胶囊。水泡包能有效杜绝重复服药，使漏服情况一目了然，并使药品始终处于密封卫生的保护状态中。

FIGURE 45.2: Dosettes are pillboxes that help clients keep track of their daily medications by day and time. Support workers are strictly forbidden from filling dosettes. (Photo credit: Can Stock Photo Inc./lisafx)

Dosettes (pillboxes, FIGURE 45.2) are plastic organizers divided into days of the week and times of day. Dosettes help clients maintain independence with self-administration. CRITICAL SAFETY RULE: Support workers must NEVER fill a dosette. Filling dosettes is a complex medical task that must only be performed by a pharmacist, a nurse, or the client/family members.

多格式药盒（Dosettes / 分药盒，图 45.2）是按周一至周日及一天中各时段（早、中、晚、睡前）分格的塑料药盒，有助于长者维持自我服药的独立性。核心安全红线铁律：支持人员绝对禁止为客户分装药盒！分装药盒属于极易引发致命配药差错的高危操作，必须严格由药剂师、持牌护士或客户本人及家属完成。

FIGURE 45.3: A MedicAlert bracelet contains the client’s medical condition, severe drug allergies, and emergency phone numbers for paramedic access. (Photo credit: MedicAlert Foundation Canada)

Many clients with chronic medical conditions (such as diabetes, epilepsy, severe asthma, heart disease) or life-threatening drug allergies (such as penicillin or latex) wear a MedicAlert bracelet or necklace (FIGURE 45.3). In an emergency, first responders and paramedics immediately check this emblem to access the client’s critical health profile and 24-hour hotline information.

许多患有慢性基础疾病（如糖尿病、癫痫、严重哮喘、心脏病）或对特定药物存在致死性过敏史（如青霉素过敏、乳胶过敏）的客户，平时会佩戴医疗急救识别手环（MedicAlert bracelet）或项链（图 45.3）。在突发紧急医疗状况时，急救医护人员（Paramedics）会第一时间查阅该手环背面镌刻的关键健康档案与24小时应急热线，以制定精准挽救生命的急救方案。

TRADITIONAL MEDICINE VS. MODERN MEDICINE

传统医学与现代生物医学的互补与文化包容

Modern Western medicine (allopathic medicine) relies on scientific clinical trials, synthetic pharmaceuticals, and surgery to treat diseases. In contrast, traditional healing practices have been developed over centuries within diverse cultures. In Canada, many Indigenous, Asian, and holistic communities integrate traditional healing remedies alongside modern pharmaceuticals.

现代西方生物医学依赖严谨的循证临床试验、化学合成药物及外科手术来治疗疾病；与此相对，传统医疗实践则是各族裔文化在千百年经验中凝结而成的身心疗愈智慧。在加拿大，许多原住民（第一民族、因纽特人、梅蒂斯人）、亚裔社区及自然疗法倡导者，常常将传统草药疗法与现代西药处方结合应用。

Indigenous Medicine Practices & Sacred Healing Traditions / 加拿大原住民传统医药实践与神圣疗愈习俗

四大神圣植物：烟草（向造物主献礼感恩）、雪松（驱除病气与富含维C草药茶）、甜草（象征仁慈，编成三股代表身心灵用于净化仪式）、鼠尾草（强效净化驱除消极情绪）。

p. 1061 Pharmacokinetics: Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism & Excretion

HOW DRUGS WORK IN THE BODY (PHARMACOKINETICS)

药物在体内的运行历程（药代动力学四部曲）

Pharmacokinetics describes how the body processes a drug through four distinct physiological stages: absorption, distribution, metabolization, and excretion.

药代动力学（Pharmacokinetics）阐述机体如何通过四个紧密衔接的生理阶段处理摄入的药物：吸收、分布、代谢与排泄。

1. Absorption (药物吸收):
The process by which a drug enters the bloodstream from its site of administration. The route of administration determines the speed of absorption (e.g., intravenous drugs act immediately, whereas oral tablets must dissolve and absorb through the stomach and small intestine, taking 30 to 60 minutes).
药物从给药部位渗透进入血液循环的过程。给药途径直接决定吸收速度（静脉注射瞬间入血生效；口服药片需经胃肠道崩解溶解吸收，通常需30至60分钟）。
2. Distribution (药物体内分布):
The transport of the drug via the bloodstream to tissues, organs, and intended receptor sites throughout the body. Blood circulation, cardiac output, and protein binding (albumin) affect distribution.
药物随血流被运送至机体各组织、脏器及目标细胞受体靶点的过程。全身血液循环状态、心输出量及血浆蛋白结合率直接影响分布效能。
3. Metabolization / Biotransformation (药物代谢与生物转化):
The biochemical conversion of the active drug into inactive compounds and waste products. The liver is the primary organ of drug metabolism. Impaired liver function slows metabolization, allowing drugs to accumulate to toxic levels.
机体通过酶促生化反应将活性药物分解转化为无活性复合物及水溶性代谢废物的过程。肝脏是人体药物代谢的中心枢纽。肝功能不全会显著延缓药物降解，导致药物蓄积引发毒性反应。
4. Excretion (药物排泄与清除):
The elimination of drug metabolites from the body. The kidneys (urine) are the primary organ of excretion, followed by the gastrointestinal tract (feces), lungs (exhaled air), and sweat glands. Renal failure drastically impedes excretion.
药物及其代谢产物被彻底排出体外的清除过程。肾脏（经尿液）是排泄的首要器官，其次为肠道（粪便）、肺部（呼出气体）及皮肤汗腺。肾功能衰竭会严重阻碍药物排泄。
p. 1062 Factors Affecting Drug Action, Synergism & Antagonism

FACTORS AFFECTING DRUG ACTION & DRUG INTERACTIONS

影响药物疗效的多元因素与药物相互作用

Individuals respond differently to the same medication. Major factors influencing drug action include age, body size, sex, disease state, genetics, diet, and route of administration.

不同个体对同一种药物的反应存在显著差异。影响药物疗效与耐受性的核心因素包括：年龄、体型体重、性别、基础疾病、遗传基因、日常饮食及给药途径。

Age (年龄因素): Older persons and infants metabolize and excrete medications much more slowly than younger adults due to decreased liver and kidney function, making them far more vulnerable to drug toxicity and side effects.
老年人及婴幼儿由于肝脏微粒体酶活性与肾小球滤过率下降，代谢和排泄药物明显迟缓，极易发生药物蓄积中毒及严重副作用。
Body Size & Weight (体重与体型): Larger individuals require larger doses to achieve therapeutic concentration; malnourished or frail clients require reduced dosages.
体重大者需要更大量剂方能达到有效血药浓度；极度消瘦衰弱的长者则需减少剂量以防相对过量。
Disease State (基础病理状态): Liver disease impairs drug breakdown; kidney failure prevents excretion; heart failure diminishes circulation to target organs.
肝硬化阻碍药物代谢灭活；肾衰竭无法清除代谢毒物；心力衰竭使目标靶器官组织血流灌注大幅减少。
Diet & Nutrition (饮食与营养相互作用): Food in the stomach can delay absorption. Grapefruit juice dangerously inhibits CYP3A4 enzymes, causing blood pressure and cholesterol drugs to surge to toxic levels.
胃内食物可延缓药物吸收；西柚汁（葡萄柚）可强效抑制肝脏代谢酶，导致降压药及他汀类降脂药血浓度异常飙升至中毒浓度。

Drug Interactions: Synergism vs. Antagonism / 药物相互作用：协同增效与拮抗抵消

• Drug Synergism (协同作用): Occurs when the combined effect of two drugs is much greater than each drug alone. For example, combining sleeping pills with alcohol triggers severe CNS depression, respiratory arrest, and coma.
协同作用：两药合用产生的效应远大于单药之和。例如镇静安眠药与酒精合用会引发致命的超强中枢抑制、呼吸骤停与昏迷。

• Drug Antagonism (拮抗作用): Occurs when one drug cancels out or weakens the action of another drug. For example, antacids neutralize stomach acid and block the absorption of certain antibiotics.
拮抗作用：两药合用时相互抵消或削弱药效。例如抗酸铝镁制剂中和胃酸并阻碍特定四环素类抗生素的吸收。
p. 1063 Forms of Medications: Solid, Liquid, Topical & Inhaled

FORMS OF MEDICATIONS

现代药物的多样化物理剂型与给药方式

Medications are manufactured in various chemical and physical forms designed to facilitate entry, absorption, and targeted therapeutic delivery within the human body. As a support worker, understanding drug forms helps you assist clients correctly and avoid harmful errors (such as crushing an enteric-coated tablet).

为了便于人体摄入、优化吸收效率并实现目标组织的精准释放，药物被制成多种物理与化学剂型。作为支持人员，熟知各类药物剂型有助于正确协助客户服药，并能坚决避免灾难性错误（例如擅自研碎肠溶片导致胃黏膜灼伤或药效过早失效）。

1. Oral Solid Forms (口服固体制剂):
• Tablets (药片): Compressed powder. May be scored (cut in half along groove only). Enteric-coated tablets have a special coating to resist stomach acid and dissolve in the small intestine; they must NEVER be chewed, crushed, or cut.
• Capsules (胶囊): Gelatin shells containing powdered or liquid medication. Extended-release (XR, ER, SR) capsules release medicine slowly and must NEVER be opened or crushed.
• Caplets (囊形片): Oval tablets coated for easier swallowing.
• Lozenges (含片): Flavoured flat disks designed to dissolve slowly in the mouth without chewing.
片剂（有刻痕者方可掰开；肠溶片具耐酸保护层在小肠崩解，绝对禁止研碎咀嚼）、胶囊（缓释胶囊严禁拆开倾倒）、囊形片（椭圆糖衣易于吞咽）、含片（含于口中缓慢融化，禁咀嚼吞下）。
2. Oral Liquid Forms (口服液体制剂):
• Solutions (口服溶液剂): Completely dissolved drug in clear liquid.
• Suspensions (混悬剂): Insoluble drug particles suspended in liquid. MUST BE SHAKEN WELL immediately before pouring so dosage is evenly distributed.
• Syrups (糖浆剂): Medication dissolved in concentrated sugar solution.
• Elixirs (酏剂): Sweetened, aromatic liquid containing water and alcohol.
溶液剂、混悬液（倒出前必须彻底用力摇匀以使药粒均匀分散）、糖浆剂、酏剂（含酒精与芳香甜味剂）。
3. Topical & Transdermal Forms (外用与透皮制剂):
• Ointments & Creams (软膏与乳膏): Semi-solid preparations applied directly to skin.
• Transdermal Patches (透皮贴剂): Adhesive patches that deliver medication slowly through the skin into the bloodstream over hours or days (e.g., nitroglycerin, nicotine, fentanyl).
外用药膏与透皮贴片（贴于皮肤经毛细血管持续缓慢释药，如硝酸甘油贴片、芬太尼止痛贴片；换贴前必须先撕除旧贴片并更换贴敷部位）。
4. Inhaled, Drops & Suppositories (吸入、滴剂与栓剂):
• Metered-Dose Inhalers (MDIs) & Dry Powder Inhalers (DPIs): Aerosol canisters delivering medication directly into bronchial airways.
• Ophthalmic, Otic & Nasal Drops: Sterile solutions for eyes, ears, and nostrils.
• Suppositories (栓剂): Solid conical bases that melt at body temperature when inserted into the rectum or vagina.
定量吸入气雾剂（MDI/DPI）、滴眼液/滴耳液/滴鼻液、直肠与阴道栓剂（在体内体温下熔化吸收）。
p. 1064 Prescription vs. OTC & Generic vs. Trade Names

PRESCRIPTION VS. OVER-THE-COUNTER (OTC) MEDICATIONS

处方药（Rx）与非处方药（OTC）的法律界限

Prescription (Rx) medications are drugs that can only be obtained with a written order from a licensed healthcare provider (physician, dentist, nurse practitioner) and dispensed by a pharmacist. They are regulated by Health Canada because they carry potential hazards, side effects, or dependency risks.

处方药（Prescription / Rx）是指必须凭借持有合法行医执照的医疗执业者（医生、牙医、执业护士）出具的正式书面处方，并由注册药剂师审核后方可配发的药物。此类药品受加拿大卫生部严格管制，因其具有潜在毒副反应、配伍禁忌或药物成瘾依赖风险。

Over-the-counter (OTC) medications can be purchased without a prescription (e.g., acetaminophen, ibuprofen, antacids, cough syrups). However, OTC medications are real drugs with real risks! They can interact dangerously with prescription drugs, mask serious symptoms, or cause liver or kidney failure if misused. Support workers must never recommend or purchase OTC drugs for a client.

非处方药（OTC）无需处方即可在药房零售货架选购（如扑热息痛对乙酰氨基酚、布洛芬、胃药抗酸剂、止咳露）。然而，非处方药同样是具备生理效应的真实药物，绝非绝对安全！它们能与处方药产生致命的拮抗或协同反应，掩盖急性重症早期征兆，滥用更可致肝肾衰竭。支持人员绝不可擅自向客户推荐或代购非处方药。

DRUG NOMENCLATURE: GENERIC NAME VS. TRADE NAME

药物命名系统：通用名（法定学名）与商品名（商标名）

Every medication has at least two names that support workers must recognize to avoid catastrophic confusion: the generic name and the trade name.

每种上市药物通常具备至少两个名称，支持人员必须清晰辨识两者对应关系，防止在给药和核对 MAR 时发生危险混淆：

Generic Name (通用名 / 官方学名):
The official, non-proprietary chemical name approved by Health Canada. It is printed in lowercase letters on medication labels and remains the same regardless of which company manufactures the drug (e.g., acetaminophen, furosemide, metoprolol).
由加拿大卫生部核准的法定通用化学成分名称。标签上通常以小写字母呈现。无论哪家药厂生产，通用名恒定不变（如扑热息痛 acetaminophen、呋塞米 furosemide、美托洛尔 metoprolol）。
Trade Name / Brand Name (商品名 / 专利商标名):
The commercial brand name given to the drug by the pharmaceutical company that manufactures it. It is registered, trademarked (®), and usually capitalized (e.g., Tylenol® for acetaminophen, Lasix® for furosemide, Lopressor® for metoprolol).
由制药企业为其注册并拥有专属商标权的商业品牌名称。首字母通常大写并标注®（如扑热息痛的商品名为 Tylenol® 泰诺、呋塞米的商品名为 Lasix® 速尿、美托洛尔的商品名为 Lopressor® 诺压）。
p. 1065 Side Effects, Adverse Effects & Life-Threatening Anaphylaxis

DRUG RESPONSES: THERAPEUTIC, SIDE EFFECTS & ADVERSE EFFECTS

药物体内反应阶梯：疗效、副作用与危险不良反应

When a medication enters the body, it produces distinct physiological responses:

当药物进入体内起效时，会产生不同性质的生理应答反应：

  • Therapeutic Effect: The desired, beneficial, positive outcome of the drug (e.g., insulin lowering blood glucose, analgesics relieving chronic hip pain).
  • Side Effects: Common, unintended, usually predictable responses occurring in addition to therapeutic action. Many are mild and well-tolerated (e.g., slight drowsiness from antihistamines, dry mouth, mild nausea).
  • Adverse Effects: Severe, unexpected, and dangerous reactions that threaten client health and safety (e.g., internal bleeding from anticoagulants, severe cardiac arrhythmias, sudden hallucinations). Must be reported to your supervisor immediately!
MEDICAL EMERGENCY · 致死性急症警示

Recognizing Life-Threatening Anaphylaxis / 识别危及生命的全身性过敏性休克

Anaphylaxis is a severe, systemic, rapid-onset allergic reaction triggered when an antigen evokes an overwhelming immune release of histamine throughout the body. It can progress to fatal respiratory arrest and cardiovascular collapse within minutes!

严重过敏反应（过敏性休克 / Anaphylaxis）是一种突发性、全身暴发型的极危重变态反应。当外来抗原（如青霉素、磺胺类药物、坚果或蜂毒）进入机体后，免疫系统产生过量组胺爆发性释放，可在短短数分钟内迅速导致气道完全窒息与循环休克死亡！

Classic Clinical Signs of Anaphylaxis (过敏性休克五大特征征兆):
  • Skin: Severe generalized itching, widespread red hives (urticaria), facial flushing.
  • Respiratory: Swelling of the lips, tongue, and throat; stridor (high-pitched whistling sound); severe dyspnea, wheezing, and tight chest.
  • Cardiovascular: Tachycardia (rapid weak pulse), dizziness, dramatic plunge in blood pressure (hypotension), vascular collapse.
  • Gastrointestinal: Severe abdominal cramps, projectile vomiting, diarrhea.
  • Neurological: Extreme anxiety, sense of impending doom, loss of consciousness.
IMMEDIATE ACTION: Call 911 immediately! If client has a prescribed auto-injector (EpiPen®), assist with administration per care plan and notify supervisor immediately.
p. 1066 Cytotoxic & Antineoplastic Drugs: Chemotherapy Precautions

CYTOTOXIC & ANTINEOPLASTIC DRUGS: SPECIAL SAFETY PROTOCOLS

细胞毒性与抗肿瘤化疗药物：超高危防护标准规程

Cytotoxic drugs are specialized pharmaceuticals that inhibit, destroy, or prevent the division of human cells. The most common category is antineoplastic drugs (chemotherapy agents) used in cancer treatment, as well as certain medications prescribed for rheumatoid arthritis and severe psoriasis (e.g., methotrexate). Because these drugs kill healthy cells alongside diseased cells, exposure poses severe health hazards to support workers, including reproductive toxicity, mutagenicity, and secondary cancers.

细胞毒性药物（Cytotoxic drugs）是能够破坏、杀灭或阻断人体细胞有丝分裂与增殖的高毒性化合物。最典型的代表是用于恶性肿瘤化疗的抗肿瘤药物（Antineoplastic drugs），以及用于治疗难治性类风湿关节炎或重度牛皮癣的免疫抑制剂（如甲氨蝶呤 Methotrexate）。由于此类药物在杀灭病变细胞的同时会无差别毒害正常健康细胞，因此暴露于该类药物对支持人员构成严重的生殖毒性、致突变性与继发致癌风险。

SPECIAL PROTOCOL · 细胞毒性污染控制规范

Safe Handling of Chemotherapy Bodily Fluids / 处理化疗体液的安全法则

  • 7-Day Hazard Window: All bodily fluids (urine, stool, vomit, blood, sweat) of clients receiving chemotherapy are hazardous for up to 7 days after the last dose.
    7天毒性排泄危险期：受治客户的全部排泄物与体液（尿液、粪便、呕吐物、血液及汗液）在停药后长达7天内仍富集毒性代谢物。
  • Double Gloving (双层手套): Wear two pairs of vinyl or nitrile gloves when handling urinals, commodes, or linens. Change gloves immediately if torn.
    佩戴双层手套：接触便盆、坐便器或床单时必须同时戴两层丁腈或乙烯基手套，破损即刻更换。
  • Double Flushing (双次冲水): Client must sit on the toilet (even male clients) to avoid splashing. Close the toilet lid securely before flushing, and flush twice.
    盖盖双次冲水：长者必须采取坐姿如厕以杜绝尿滴飞溅。冲水前务必盖上马桶盖，且每次使用必须连续强力冲水两次。
  • Special Waste Containers: Cytotoxic waste (diapers, pads, gloves) must be sealed in heavy-duty yellow biohazard cytotoxic bags with warning labels.
    专用黄色医疗垃圾桶：所有接触过体液的纸尿裤、手套及耗材必须封存入带有细胞毒性警示标识的专用黄色防渗生物垃圾袋中。
p. 1067 The 10 Rights of Medication Management (Rights 1 to 4)

THE 10 RIGHTS OF MEDICATION MANAGEMENT

药物管理‘十大权益’（10 Rights）核心准则（第1至第4项）

The 10 Rights of medication management are the gold standard for clinical safety designed to prevent fatal errors whenever assisting or administering medications. Check each right thoroughly every single time!

药物管理‘十大权益’是保障用药安全的黄金临床金标准，旨在每次协助服药或给药时全流程阻断医疗差错。每次协助用药前，必须对每一项权益进行逐一严谨核验！

1. The Right Medication (正确的药物):
Check the medication label against the MAR and care plan. Perform the Three Checks of the Label: (1) when obtaining the container from the cupboard, (2) just before opening or pouring, and (3) when returning the container to storage.
严格将药瓶标签与 MAR 单核对。必须执行三查标签制度：① 从药柜取出药盒时查、② 准备开启包装前查、③ 将药盒归置原位前查。
2. The Right Person (正确的受药人):
Confirm identity using at least two unique client identifiers (e.g., full first and last name, date of birth, photo on MAR, ID wristband). Never rely on room numbers or assumption.
使用至少两项唯一标识核对长者身份（核实全名、出生年月日、MAR单彩色近照或手腕带）。绝不可仅凭房间号或主观印象猜测。
3. The Right Dose (正确的给药剂量):
Confirm that the exact prescribed dosage matches the MAR (e.g., 500 mg, not 250 mg or 1000 mg). If a tablet must be broken, ensure it is pre-cut along its scored line by the pharmacy.
核实标签剂量与 MAR 单完全一致（如 500mg 绝不可服成 250mg 或 1000mg）。若需半片，必须由药房或护士沿中间凹槽预先切好。
4. The Right Route (正确的给药途径):
Verify how the medication enters the body (oral swallowing, sublingual under the tongue, transdermal patch on skin, ophthalmic drops in eye). Never substitute an oral suspension for eye drops!
核对给药通道（吞服、舌下含服、皮肤贴剂、滴眼剂等）。绝对严禁将口服液滴入眼内或混淆给药通道！

5. The Right Time (正确的时间):
Medications must be taken at the exact scheduled hours (e.g., 08:00, 12:00, 20:00) or relative to meals (e.g., with food, 30 minutes before breakfast) to maintain therapeutic blood levels.
严格按预定钟点（如 08:00）或与进餐特定关联（如随餐或饭前30分钟）服药，维持平稳血液疗效浓度。
6. The Right Day (正确的给药日期):
Some drugs are ordered on specific days of the week (e.g., methotrexate once weekly on Thursdays) or on alternate days. Verify calendar date against MAR.
核对具体服药日期（某些药物仅限每周四服用一次，或隔日服用一次）。切忌将每周一次的药物误当成每日服用。
7. The Right Reason (正确的用药理由):
The client should understand why the medication is prescribed (e.g., blood pressure control, pain management, blood thinner). If client is confused about purpose, clarify before giving.
客户理应知晓该药的治疗意图（降压、止痛或抗凝）。若客户对该药功用提出严重质疑，必须在澄清确认后再服。
8. The Right Documentation (准确及时的记录):
Immediately after the client swallows the medication, record on the MAR with your official initials. NEVER document beforehand!
在长者确实完整咽下药物后，立即在 MAR 单对应空格签注工号全名或姓名首字母。绝对严禁在服药前提前打勾签字！
9. The Right Action / Evaluation (正确的药效评估):
Observe the client following administration. Did the analgesic relieve pain? Are there signs of allergic rash or hypotension?
服药后持续观察客户反应。止痛药是否起效？有无皮疹、头晕、恶心等异常不良表现？
10. The Right to Refuse (合法的拒绝服药权利):
Clients have the legal autonomy to refuse any medication. Never force or trick a client into taking pills. Document refusal on MAR and notify your supervisor/nurse immediately.
长者享有拒绝任何药物的完全法律自主权。严禁欺骗或强迫客户服药。若客户拒服，立即在 MAR 单上标注‘拒服（Refused）’并火速汇报主管。
p. 1069 Documentation & The Medication Administration Record (MAR)

THE MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION RECORD (MAR)

法定给药记录单（MAR）：医疗证据与安全闭环

The Medication Administration Record (MAR) is an official legal clinical document containing the complete list of all medications ordered for a client. In facilities and community home care agencies, the MAR is the central source of truth for medication management. The MAR specifies the client’s full legal name, allergies, room number, prescribing physician, drug generic and trade names, dosage, route, scheduled times, and start/stop dates.

给药记录单（Medication Administration Record / MAR）是一份具有法律效力的正式医疗临床凭证，详尽汇总了为客户开具的全部药物医嘱明细。在机构病房与社区居家照护中，MAR 单是用药协助的绝对基准。它清晰载明客户的全名、药物过敏警示、房间号、开具处方法定医师、药物通用名与商品名、规格剂量、给药途径、规定给药钟点及医嘱起止有效日期。

Mandatory Rules for MAR Documentation / MAR 单记录硬性铁律

  • Sign Immediately AFTER: Sign the MAR block only after the client has swallowed the pill or the procedure is completely finished.
  • Never Pre-Sign: Pre-signing is a severe professional violation that leads to false clinical assumptions if the client subsequently chokes or refuses.
  • Document Omissions & Refusals: If a medication is withheld, dropped, vomited, or refused, circle your initials and document the clinical reason on the back of the MAR, notifying supervisor.
  • Black or Blue Ink Only: Use indelible ink; never use pencil or erasable pens. Correction fluid (white-out) is strictly illegal on medical records.

p. 1072 Medication Errors: Prevention & Immediate Emergency Response

MEDICATION ERRORS: PREVENTION & IMMEDIATE RESPONSE

用药差错应急处置流程：首重生命安全与零隐瞒

A medication error is any preventable event that may cause or lead to inappropriate medication use or client harm. Errors include: wrong person, wrong drug, wrong dose, wrong route, wrong time, or omitted dose. If you realize or suspect that an error has occurred, NEVER conceal it! Prompt action can save the client’s life.

用药差错（Medication error）是指任何可防范的、导致或可能导致不当用药或患者伤害的医疗事件。常见差错包括：服错人、服错药、剂量错误、途径错误、时间错误或遗漏给药。一旦发现或怀疑发生了用药差错，绝对不可隐瞒！及时的急救干预能够挽救长者的生命。

CRITICAL PROTOCOL · 差错应急四步法

Immediate Steps Following a Medication Error / 发生用药差错时的紧急应对程序

  1. Check the Client Immediately: Assess the client's vital signs (pulse, respiration, blood pressure), level of consciousness, and skin condition immediately.
    立即评估长者生命体征：即刻测量脉搏、呼吸、血压，检查意识状态及有无皮疹窒息，确保气道通畅。
  2. Report to Supervisor / Nurse IMMEDIATELY: Phone your supervisor, agency nurse, or physician right away. State exactly what medication and dose were taken and the client’s current status.
    火速向主管或注册护士汇报：立即致电主管，清晰准确说明误服的药物名称、规格剂量、发生时间及长者当前生命体征。
  3. Stay and Monitor: Remain with the client. Follow the supervisor’s or physician’s emergency instructions (e.g., calling 911 or poison control).
    守候在旁持续监护：寸步不离守护长者，严格遵从主管指示（必要时呼叫911急救或中毒控制中心）。
  4. Complete Incident Report: Document the exact facts objectively on an agency Incident / Occurrence Report. Never guess or record opinions.
    详实填报意外事件报告单：依据机构政策客观记录事实经过，如实归档，为后续临床救治提供法律证据。
p. 1073 Safe Storage of Medications: Temperature, Moisture & Security

SAFE STORAGE OF MEDICATIONS

居家药品的科学储藏规范：避光、防潮、控温与上锁防误服

Improper storage degrades medications, rendering them ineffective or chemically toxic. Follow these storage standards:

不当的存放环境会加速药物水解变质，使药效丧失甚至分解产生化学毒物。必须恪守以下存放标准：

Original Labelled Containers (原装带标签保存): Keep all pills in original bottles with intact prescription labels. Never mix different pills in the same jar.
始终保存在原装药瓶中并确保处方标签清晰完整。严禁将不同药片混装在同一容器内。
Cool, Dry, Dark Place (阴凉干燥避光处): Store at room temperature (15°C–25°C) away from direct sunlight and humidity.
存放在常温（15°C–25°C）避光处。远离阳光直射。
PROHIBITED LOCATIONS (严禁存放的区域): NEVER store medications above a kitchen stove (extreme heat and grease) or in a bathroom medicine cabinet (extreme humidity and heat steam from showers destroy pills).
绝对严禁存放于厨房炉灶上方（高温油烟熏烤）或浴室镜柜中（淋浴产生的高温水蒸气与潮气会迅速破坏药片结构）。
Refrigeration (特定冷藏要求): Certain drugs (e.g., unopened insulin, specific antibiotic suspensions, eye drops) require refrigeration at 2°C–8°C. Do not freeze!
未开封的胰岛素或部分抗生素混悬液需在2°C–8°C冷藏保存，绝对禁止冷冻结冰！
Locked Away from Children & Dementia (安全上锁防护): Keep in a locked box or locked cabinet to prevent accidental poisoning by toddlers or clients with cognitive impairment.
妥善锁置于专用药箱或带锁柜中，防止幼儿误服或失智症长者因认知障碍反复抓服中毒。
p. 1074 Assisting With Oral Medications: Procedures & Safety Positioning

ASSISTING WITH ORAL MEDICATIONS: CLINICAL PROCEDURE

协助口服给药标准临床操作规程

Oral medications are swallowed through the mouth. Aspiration (medication or fluid entering the airway and lungs) is a catastrophic risk for frail older clients. Always ensure the client is fully alert and sitting upright (at least 60–90 degrees, high-Fowler’s position) before assisting with oral medications!

口服药物经口腔咽下。误吸（Aspiration / 药物或水误入气道和肺部引发吸入性肺炎甚至窒息）是高龄体弱长者的头号致命威胁。在协助口服药物前，必须确保长者神智清醒，并协助其采取**至少 60–90 度的高半坐卧位（High-Fowler's position）**！严禁平卧服药！

STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE · 标准步骤

Assisting With Solid Oral Medications / 协助口服固体制剂操作流程

  1. Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and warm water.
  2. Check the MAR: Verify client identity (2 identifiers) and check medication order.
  3. Check 1: Locate medication container and compare label to MAR.
  4. Provide privacy and assist client into an upright seated position.
  5. Check 2: Compare label to MAR again before opening bottle or blister.
  6. Open container: Pour correct number of pills into container lid or directly into medicine cup. Do not touch pills with your bare fingers!
  7. Hand medicine cup and fresh glass of water to client.
  8. Check 3: Compare label to MAR before returning container to storage.
  9. Remain with the client until you observe that every pill has been swallowed completely.
  10. Wash hands, and sign the MAR immediately.

Special Considerations for Liquid Oral Medications

口服液体制剂的精准量取与防浑浊细节
  • Shake Suspensions Thoroughly: Suspensions contain solid drug particles settled at the bottom. Shake vigorously for at least 15–30 seconds before opening so the dosage is uniform.

  • Palm the Label (手心护住标签): When pouring liquid, hold the bottle with the label against the palm of your hand. This prevents dribbles of syrup from running down and staining or obscuring the prescription label!
    手心护住药标：倒药液时手心紧贴包裹药瓶标签，防止残余药液顺着瓶壁流下腐蚀或污损处方文字。

Swallowing Observation Rule: Always stay with the client until the medication is swallowed! Some clients accidentally drop pills into blankets or pocket them in their cheeks (cheeking) due to dementia or intent to hoard. Look inside the client's mouth if you suspect cheeking.

确认吞咽观察铁律：必须在旁守候直至目睹长者将药完全吞下！部分长者可能因震颤将药片掉落在被褥缝隙中，或因失智症/攒药自杀倾向将药片藏在颊黏膜与牙龈之间（藏药 Cheeking）。若怀疑藏药，必须温和请长者张口发‘啊’音核实口腔已被清空。

p. 1076 Assisting With Eye Medications: Asepsis & Cross-Contamination

ASSISTING WITH EYE MEDICATIONS (OPHTHALMIC)

协助眼部给药：无菌操作与防交叉感染红线

The eye is extremely delicate and susceptible to bacterial and fungal infections. Medications for the eye must be labelled “sterile ophthalmic”. Follow strict asepsis to prevent blindness-causing infections.

眼球组织高度娇嫩敏感，极易受细菌和真菌感染侵害。所有眼部药物必须带有‘无菌眼用制剂（Sterile ophthalmic）’标识。在操作过程中必须严格恪守无菌原则，防止诱发致盲性眼内炎。

Cardinal Rules for Eye Medications / 眼部用药核心准则

  • Clean Eyelid First: If crust or discharge is present, wipe gently from the inner canthus (corner near nose) to outer canthus using a clean warm damp cotton ball for each eye.
  • NEVER Touch Dropper Tip: The tip of the dropper or tube must NEVER touch the client's eye, eyelashes, eyelids, or your fingers. Touching contaminates the entire bottle!
  • Never Drop on Cornea: Never drop medication directly onto the sensitive cornea (centre of eye), which triggers intense pain, corneal injury, and blink reflex. Drop into the lower conjunctival sac!

p. 1078 Assisting With Ear Medications: Adult vs. Child Ear Position

ASSISTING WITH EAR MEDICATIONS (OTIC)

协助耳部滴药：耳廓牵拉手法与防眩晕机理

Ear medications (otic drops) are used to treat ear infections or soften earwax. The ear canal is sensitive to temperature. CRITICAL RULE: Cold ear drops instilling onto the tympanic membrane cause severe vestibular stimulation, triggering acute vertigo (dizziness), nausea, and vomiting. Warm the medication bottle in your hands for several minutes before instilling!

滴耳液用于治疗外耳道感染或软化耵聍。内耳前庭迷路对温度极其敏感。关键安全铁律：若将冰冷的药液直接滴入耳内，低温会剧烈刺激鼓膜与半规管，诱发急性眩晕、恶心剧烈呕吐！滴药前必须用手掌将药瓶搓焐数分钟，使药液接近人体体温！

Straightening the Ear Canal / 顺直耳道解剖牵拉手法对比

FOR ADULTS & CHILDREN OVER 3 YEARS (成人及3岁以上儿童):
Gently pull the external ear (pinna) UP AND BACK (FIGURE 45.9). This straightens the adult S-shaped ear canal so drops flow directly to the tympanic membrane.
向后、向上轻轻牵拉耳廓（图 45.9）。成人外耳道呈S形弯曲，向后上方牵拉能拉直耳道，使药滴顺滑流向鼓膜。
FOR INFANTS & CHILDREN UNDER 3 YEARS (婴幼儿及3岁以下儿童):
Gently pull the earlobe DOWN AND BACK. In young children, the canal angles differently.
向后、向下轻轻牵拉耳垂。幼儿耳骨未闭合，向后下方牵拉方能拉直耳道。

ASSISTING WITH NASAL MEDICATIONS (DROPS & SPRAYS)

协助鼻腔给药：滴鼻剂与鼻腔喷雾剂规程

Nasal medications are used to reduce mucosal inflammation, allergies, and congestion. Have the client blow their nose gently before administration to clear the nasal passages. For nasal sprays, the client sits upright with head tilted slightly forward. Insert tip into nostril, occlude the opposite nostril with finger, and have client breathe in gently through the nose as the spray is compressed. Wipe the nozzle clean before recapping.

鼻用药物用于缓解过敏性鼻炎、黏膜水肿充血。用药前指导长者轻轻擤鼻涕以清理鼻腔通道。使用鼻腔喷雾剂时，长者采取坐姿、头部略微前倾；将喷嘴轻轻插入鼻孔，另一手指按闭对侧鼻孔，在按压喷雾的瞬间嘱长者用鼻腔轻缓吸气。用药后用洁净纸巾擦拭喷嘴并盖好瓶盖。

ASSISTING WITH TRANSDERMAL PATCHES

协助透皮贴剂应用：轮换部位与严禁过量红线
FIGURE 45.10: Ensure that the edges of the transdermal patch adhere firmly to clean, dry, hairless skin. Press firmly with palm for 10 to 30 seconds. (Photo credit: Can Stock Photo Inc./monkeybusiness)
SAFETY PROTOCOLS · 透皮贴剂六大安全法则
  • ALWAYS REMOVE THE OLD PATCH FIRST: Failing to remove the old patch before applying a new one causes double absorption and fatal drug toxicity (especially with fentanyl and nitroglycerin)!
    换新贴片前必须先撕除旧贴片：若忘记撕除旧贴片直接贴上新贴片，会导致双倍药物重叠蓄积吸收，诱发致死性药物过量中毒（尤其是芬太尼强效麻醉止痛贴与硝酸甘油贴片）！
  • Rotate Application Sites: Choose a different clean, dry, hairless site (upper chest, back, upper arms) with each change to prevent skin breakdown.
    轮换贴敷部位：每次更换必须轮换至不同部位（上胸部、肩背部、上臂外侧），防止局部皮肤过敏红肿溃破。
  • NEVER CUT A TRANSDERMAL PATCH: Cutting damages the specialized drug reservoir and rate-controlling membrane, releasing a lethal dose immediately into the skin!
    绝对严禁剪开贴片：剪裁会破坏控释膜微囊，导致储库中的全部高浓度药物瞬间倾仓释放，引发急性致死性休克！
  • Safe Disposal: Fold the used patch in half with sticky sides touching each other and dispose of per agency narcotic protocol.
    安全丢弃：将撕下的废贴片胶面对折粘贴封闭，依管制药品规程妥善处置。

ASSISTING WITH METERED-DOSE INHALERS (MDIS)

协助定量气雾吸入器（MDI）：精准深吸与屏气10秒规范
FIGURE 45.11: Client using metered-dose inhaler (MDI). Have client exhale fully, press canister once while inhaling slowly and deeply for 3 to 5 seconds, and hold breath for 10 seconds. (Photo credit: Can Stock Photo Inc./monkeybusiness)

Step-by-Step MDI Inhalation Technique / MDI 吸入标准操作步骤

  1. Remove cap and shake the inhaler vigorously 5 or 6 times.
  2. Have client sit fully upright in high-Fowler’s position.
  3. Instruct client to breathe out gently and completely, emptying the lungs.
  4. Client places mouthpiece between teeth and seals lips tightly around it.
  5. As client begins to breathe in slowly through the mouth, press the top of the canister down ONCE.
  6. Client continues slow, deep inhalation over 3 to 5 seconds.
  7. Hold breath for 10 seconds (or as long as comfortable) to allow fine drug droplets to settle deeply into alveoli.
  8. If a second puff of the SAME medication is ordered, wait 1 FULL MINUTE before repeating the procedure.
p. 1082 Sequential Inhalers: Bronchodilator First & Steroid Rinse Protocol
CRITICAL PHARMACOLOGY RULE · 呼吸给药核心黄金法则

Order of Inhalers: Bronchodilator FIRST, Corticosteroid SECOND / 两种吸入剂使用次序与漱口铁律

Many clients with asthma or COPD are prescribed two different inhalers: a bronchodilator (e.g., salbutamol / Ventolin®) and a corticosteroid (e.g., fluticasone / Flovent®). The sequence in which these are taken is extremely critical!

许多哮喘或慢性阻塞性肺疾病（COPD）长者同时开具了两种不同机制的气雾吸入剂：支气管舒张剂（Bronchodilator，如沙丁胺醇 Ventolin®）与糖皮质激素消炎吸入剂（Corticosteroid，如辅舒酮 Flovent®）。这两种吸入剂的使用先后次序至关重要：

1. ALWAYS INHALE THE BRONCHODILATOR FIRST (必须首先吸入支气管舒张剂):
The bronchodilator relaxes smooth muscle and opens up narrowed bronchial airways.
支气管舒张剂能够迅速松弛痉挛的气道平滑肌，使狭窄的气道充分扩张通畅。

2. WAIT 5 FULL MINUTES (必须等待整整 5 分钟):
Waiting 5 minutes allows time for the bronchioles to fully dilate.
静待5分钟，使肺部各级细支气管完成充分舒张。

3. INHALE THE CORTICOSTEROID SECOND (其次吸入糖皮质激素):
Because the airways are dilated, the steroid anti-inflammatory medication can penetrate deeply into lung tissues.
在气道已完全扩张的状态下吸入激素消炎药，药雾才能深达肺泡小气道黏膜发挥强效抗炎效果。

4. MANDATORY WATER RINSE AND SPIT (吸入激素后必须清水漱口并吐出):
After inhaling any steroid medication, the client MUST thoroughly rinse the mouth and gargle with water, and SPIT IT OUT. Rinsing removes deposited steroid residue and prevents fungal oral candidiasis (oral thrush) and hoarseness.
吸完激素后必须用清水漱口并吐出（切勿咽下）！漱口能冲洗掉口腔与咽部残留的激素药粉，坚决防范真菌性鹅口疮（口腔假丝酵母菌感染）与咽部声音嘶哑！
p. 1083 Figure 45.12: Holding Chambers / Spacers With MDIs

HOLDING CHAMBERS (SPACERS) WITH INHALERS

储雾罐（加药腔 Spacer）：解决老年手口协调困难的利器
FIGURE 45.12: A holding chamber (spacer) suspended between the MDI and the client enables slow, normal breathing to inhale the suspended mist without perfect hand-breath synchronization. (Photo credit: Lilley et al.)

Many older adults have difficulty coordinating pressing the MDI canister and inhaling simultaneously. A holding chamber (spacer) attaches to the MDI. When the canister is pressed, the medication mist is trapped inside the chamber, allowing the client to breathe the medication in naturally over 2 to 3 normal breaths. Wash the plastic spacer weekly in warm soapy water and let it air-dry completely (never rub with dry towels, which creates static electricity that traps medication particles on the walls!).

许多高龄长者或患有关节炎、帕金森病的老人，难以完成‘按压药罐’与‘深吸气’在同一瞬间的精准手口协调。储雾罐（Spacer / 储雾吸入腔，图 45.12）连接在气雾剂与口唇之间。按下药罐后，药雾会暂时悬浮储存在密闭腔体内，长者可通过单向气阀平静自主地吸入2至3口正常呼吸即可全量吸尽。储雾罐每周用温肥皂水浸泡洗净并**自然风干**（严禁用毛巾摩擦擦拭，以免产生静电吸附使药雾粘附在腔壁无法被吸入！）。

p. 1084 Chapter Review: Key Points, Critical Thinking Case & Quiz (Q1-Q11)
CHAPTER REVIEW · 本章总结回顾

KEY POINTS / 核心执业要点

  • Assisting vs. Administering: Support workers assist with medications (reminding, handing containers, opening lids, reading labels, providing water). Administering is a controlled medical act.
  • Filling Dosettes: Support workers are STRICTLY FORBIDDEN from filling pillboxes or dosettes.
  • The 10 Rights: Always verify: Right medication, person, dose, route, time, day, reason, documentation, action, and right to refuse.
  • Three Checks of Label: Check label (1) when obtaining, (2) before pouring/opening, and (3) when returning to storage.
  • Cytotoxic Drugs: Bodily fluids are hazardous for 7 days post-chemotherapy; wear double gloves and flush twice with lid down.
  • Medication Errors: If an error occurs: check client vitals immediately, report to supervisor at once, stay with client, document incident report.
  • Inhaler Rules: Inhale bronchodilator first, wait 5 minutes, inhale corticosteroid second; rinse mouth with water and spit to prevent thrush.
CRITICAL THINKING IN PRACTICE · 临床思辨实战

Clinical Decision-Making / 药物管理复杂情境决策

Scenario: You are visiting Mrs. Klein in her home. She complains of a severe throbbing headache and asks you to give her two tablets of an OTC pain reliever (ibuprofen) that she has in her medicine cabinet. Looking at Mrs. Klein’s MAR, you see that she takes a daily prescription blood thinner (warfarin) and that ibuprofen is NOT listed on her MAR. What should you do?

临床情境：你正在居家上门探视克莱因太太。老人抱怨前额剧烈搏动性头痛，请你从她浴室药箱里拿两片非处方止痛药（布洛芬 Ibuprofen）给她吃。在核对克莱因太太的 MAR 给药记录单时，你发现老人每天口服处方抗凝药（华法林 Warfarin），而布洛芬并没有列入她的 MAR 医嘱清单中。此时你应当采取何种行动？

Clinical Decision: Do NOT give Mrs. Klein the ibuprofen! Ibuprofen is an NSAID that causes severe gastrointestinal bleeding when combined with warfarin (a life-threatening synergism). Because ibuprofen is not on the MAR, you cannot assist with it. Explain this gently to Mrs. Klein, offer a cold compress to her forehead, and immediately telephone your agency supervisor or nurse to report her headache and seek physician orders.

临床权威决策应对：绝对不可给克莱因太太服用布洛芬！布洛芬属于非甾体抗炎药（NSAID），与华法林合用具有极高致命性的出血增效协同作用，极易诱发严重的消化道大出血或脑出血。此外，MAR 单上未授权的药物支持人员坚决不可协助提供。应温和地向老人说明安全风险，提供额部冷敷物理缓解，并立即致电机构主管或护士汇报其头痛症状，由执业医生评估开具安全医嘱。
