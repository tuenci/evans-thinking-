OBJECTIVES

本章学习目标
  • • Describe the communication process.
    详尽描述人际沟通的经典流转过程（发送者、接收者、信息载体与反馈循环）。
  • • Describe verbal and nonverbal communication.
    深入阐述言语沟通（Verbal）与非语言沟通（Nonverbal）的核心特征与临床表现。
  • • List common defence mechanisms and explain how they can interfere with effective communication.
    列举临床常见的心理防御机制，并深度剖析其如何阻碍人际间的有效良性沟通。
  • • Define professional communication and describe how it differs from other types of communication.
    界定专业沟通（Professional Communication）的本质内涵，阐明其与日常社交沟通的本质区别。
  • • Explain the methods of and barriers to effective communication.
    熟练掌握促进有效沟通的核心实操技巧，并警惕识别各类沟通障碍（Barriers）。
  • • Explain how messages can be misunderstood.
    深入剖析信息传递过程中发生误解、扭曲或脱节的深层主客观诱因。
  • • Explain the importance of workplace etiquette in maintaining professional relationships.
    阐述严格恪守职场礼仪（Workplace Etiquette）在维护专业团队和谐关系中的至高意义。
  • • Describe ways to prevent and deal with angry behaviours.
    熟练掌握防范、识别以及化解受助案主或家属愤怒应激行为的专业实操规程。
  • • Describe the principles of assertive communication.
    全面阐述坚定自信沟通风格（Assertive Communication）的核心准则与临床界限。
  • • Explain ways to effectively communicate with clients living with dementia.
    系统掌握与罹患认知症（阿尔茨海默病等）长者进行无障碍同理关怀沟通的实操黄金法则。

Key Terms · 核心专业术语表（全 26 词速查）

英文术语 / 国际音标 中文对照 / 出处 教材标准学术释义与临床界定
active listening /ˈæktɪv ˈlɪsnɪŋ/ 积极倾听 / 敏锐倾听 p. 134
A nonjudgemental communication technique that focuses not only on understanding the content of what is being said but also on the underlying emotions and feelings conveyed by the sender. Active listeners concentrate fully on what is being said and pay attention to the client's nonverbal cues.
一种完全秉持非评判态度的核心沟通技巧：不仅专注听懂对方言语的客观表面内容，更能深度敏锐捕捉并理解发送者所传递的底层情绪与深层心理感受。积极倾听者全神贯注于对话，时刻密切注视案主的非语言肢体线索。
assertiveness /əˈsɜːrtɪvnəs/ 坚定自信沟通 / 自我主张 p. 143
A style of communication in which thoughts and feelings are expressed positively and directly, without offending others.
一种健康、从容的专业沟通风格：以积极、坦诚、直接的方式清晰表达自身的思想、观点与内心需求，同时兼具专业修养与同理心，绝不冒犯、贬低或侵犯他人的法定尊严与权利。
body language /ˈbɒdi ˈlæŋɡwɪdʒ/ 肢体语言 / 体态语 p. 134
An important part of nonverbal communication that can reveal a person's physical, mental, or emotional state through powerful messages such as gestures, postures, and facial expressions.
非语言沟通的最关键组成部分：通过手势姿态、身体站立/坐卧体态、面部细微表情与眼神接触等极其强烈的潜意识信号，真实展露个体当前的生理健康状态、思维活动及真实情绪反应。
closed-ended questions /kləʊzd ˈɛndɪd ˈkwɛstʃənz/ 封闭式提问 p. 137
Questions that are structured so that the response can be restricted to one word such as yes or no or to a few words.
经过特定逻辑结构化设计的提问方式：将对方的回答范围严格限定于单字回答（如“是”或“否”），或极少数确切的事实性简短词汇。多用于精准采集特定临床数据或体征状态。
defence mechanisms /dɪˈfɛns ˈmɛkənɪzəmz/ 心理防御机制 p. 141
A subconscious practice intended to either delay - or avoid altogether - uncomfortable feelings such as anxiety, guilt, stress, or embarrassment. These practices include denial, displacement, fantasizing, projection, rationalization, regression, repression, suppression, passive-aggressiveness, and submissiveness.
个体潜意识中自发激活的自我保护心理应对行为：旨在延缓或彻底逃避、压抑内心所体验到的焦虑、内疚自责、巨大压力、恐慌或极度难堪等负面痛苦感受。
denial /dɪˈnaɪəl/ 否认机制 p. 142
An unwillingness to accept the truth or a certain reality.
心理防御机制的一种：当事人内心断然拒绝接受客观已经发生的残酷真相、临床诊断结果或特定客观危机现实。
displacement /dɪsˈpleɪsmənt/ 转移 / 移情发泄 p. 142
A transfer of feelings (usually of anger) onto another person or item instead of onto the person who is the source of the anger.
将内心积压的强烈负面情绪（通常为愤怒、屈辱或怨气），从真正的矛盾源头身上转移宣泄至另一个相对弱势、安全的第三方人（如无辜的前线支持工作者）或无生命物体之上。
electronic communication /ɪˌlɛkˈtrɒnɪk kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/ 电子信息通讯 p. 132
The transfer of information through electronic media.
依托数字化、网络化现代电子媒介设备进行信息、数据与视音频的瞬时跨空间流转传递。
empathetic listening /ˌɛmpəˈθɛtɪk ˈlɪsnɪŋ/ 同理心共情倾听 p. 136
A nonjudgemental technique that requires the listener to be attentive to the sender's feelings.
一种高度专注的高阶沟通技巧：要求倾听者放下任何评判妄断，全神贯注体察并共情发送者深层的情绪感受与生命体验，让对方切实感受到被接纳与被理解。
fantasizing /ˈfæntəsaɪzɪŋ/ 幻想 / 白日梦 p. 142
Daydreaming as an escape instead of dealing with reality.
一种心理防御退缩机制：当事人在脑海中沉溺于虚构的美好白日梦境，以此作为逃避严酷现实挫折、推卸实际行动的避风港。
focusing /ˈfəʊkəsɪŋ/ 聚焦探讨 / 聚焦法 p. 138
Limiting the conversation to a certain topic.
一种极具针对性的专业沟通把控技巧：当案主言语思维漫无边际、胡乱游移发散时，护工温和而坚定地将交谈主题重新引向并牢牢限定于当前最关键的照护焦点之上。
interpersonal communication /ˌɪntərˈpɜːrsənl kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/ 人际沟通 p. 131
The exchange of information between two people, usually face to face.
两人或多人之间所发生的信息、思想与情感的双向双向互动流转过程，在医疗临床现场通常体现为面对面交互。
nonverbal communication /ˌnɒnˈvɜːrbl kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/ 非语言沟通 p. 134
Messages sent without words.
不借助任何有声语言或书面文字符号、完全通过身体姿态、眼神、面部神情、空间距离、手势与抚慰性触碰所传递出的全部信息总和。
open-ended questions /ˈəʊpən ˈɛndɪd ˈkwɛstʃənz/ 开放式提问 p. 137
Questions that invite a person to share thoughts, feelings, or ideas.
无法用简单的“是”或“否”直接打发的提问方式：热情邀请并鼓励案主用自己的语言充分分享内心的真实想法、情感体验、病情细节或生活构想。
paraphrasing /ˈpærəfreɪzɪŋ/ 释义复述 / 意译重构 p. 136
Restating someone's message in one's own words.
用倾听者自己精炼、清晰的词汇重新表述对方刚才所传递的核心观点与信息，以此向案主证实信息已被准确接收与深度理解。
passive-aggressiveness /ˈpæsɪv əˈɡrɛsɪvnəs/ 被动攻击性行为 p. 142
The practice of expressing negative emotions, resentment, or anger toward others through passive, indirect ways (such as through procrastination or manipulation) instead of by verbalizing feelings in an assertive manner.
不通过坦诚坚定的成熟言语表达内心不满，而是采取消极拖延、隐蔽破坏、冷暴力疏离或心理操纵等拐弯抹角的间接方式宣泄怨恨与敌意的病态处事模式。
projection /prəˈdʒɛkʃn/ 投射机制 p. 142
Blaming someone else for one's own behaviour.
将自己内心无法容忍、难以启齿的缺点、过失、阴暗心理或失职行为，生硬归咎、转嫁并强加在他人身上并予以严厉指责。
rationalization /ˌræʃnəlaɪˈzeɪʃn/ 合理化借口 / 文饰作用 p. 142
Justifying a behaviour or action by explaining it as being less harmful or hurtful than it really is.
当事人为其不当行为、照护失误或道德过错编造出冠冕堂皇、看似合情合理的所谓“正当逻辑借口”，以此竭力淡化其实际造成的恶劣损害与内疚感。
reaction formation /riˈækʃn fɔːrˈmeɪʃn/ 反向形成机制 p. 142
Acting in a way that is opposite to how one truly feels.
个体表现出与其潜意识中真实感受与欲念完全背道而驰的极度夸张外在言行举止（例如内心极其厌恶某人，外在却表现得异常谄媚客套）。
regression /rɪˈɡrɛʃn/ 倒退 / 退行机制 p. 142
A return to an earlier developmental stage as a way to escape from stress.
面对无法承受的沉重心理压力或器质性重病危机时，心智或日常行为不可逆地退行倒退至幼年时期的不成熟阶段（如成年长者嚎啕大哭、耍小孩子脾气或撒泼尿床）。
repression /rɪˈprɛʃn/ 潜抑机制 p. 142
The involuntary action of blocking painful memories from the conscious mind.
机体防御机制不由自主的无意识生理动作：将极其痛苦的创伤记忆、恐慌体验强行封锁、压入潜意识底层，使其无法进入日常意识层面。
social media /ˈsəʊʃl ˈmiːdiə/ 社交媒体平台 p. 132
Websites and applications through which users create virtual communities or networks to share information, ideas, personal messages, and other content.
供用户自主创建虚拟社交圈并跨地域共享资讯、见解、个人动态与图文视音频内容的数字化网络站点与应用程序。
submissiveness /səbˈmɪsɪvnəs/ 顺从盲从性 p. 142
A show of obedience, meekness, and compliance in the face of conflict or a difference of opinion, instead of assertiveness.
面对意见分歧、权利侵害或利益冲突时，完全放弃合理的自我坚定主张，毫无底线地展现出唯唯诺诺、胆怯服从与过分顺从迎合的态度。
suppression /səˈprɛʃn/ 压制 / 意识性压抑 p. 142
A conscious refusal to acknowledge a painful or upsetting situation.
个体在完全清醒的理智意识支配下，主观上明确且决绝地拒绝去正视、提及或承认某一令其极其痛苦、焦虑烦躁的严峻现实情境。
verbal communication /ˈvɜːrbl kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/ 言语沟通 p. 132
Messages sent through the spoken word.
依托人类声带振动所发出的口头有声词汇语言，来传递信息、交换思想与指令的核心沟通途径。
workplace etiquette /ˈwɜːrkpleɪs ˈɛtɪkɛt/ 职场专业礼仪守则 p. 138
Professional, ethical, friendly, and respectful behaviour toward all staff and co-workers at all times.
支持工作者在执业环境中的立身之本：在任何工作时刻（包括工间休息期间），对所有跨学科团队成员与同事始终恪守高度专业、伦理自律、友好温和且相互尊重的崇高行为操守准则。

FACTORS THAT INFLUENCE COMMUNICATION

影响人际沟通的多元主客观核心因素

Interpersonal communication is the exchange of information between two people, usually face to face. A message is sent by one person (the sender) and is received and interpreted by another person (the receiver).

人际沟通本质上是两个人之间所进行的信息交互过程，在医疗临床现场通常体现为面对面的直接交流。信息由一人（发送者，Sender）发起编码并传递，由另一人（接收者，Receiver）接收并予以解码阐释。

Often, the receiver provides information in response to the message (feedback). During the exchange of information, each person usually acts as both a sender and a receiver.

通常情况下，接收者会针对接收到的信息给出明确的响应（反馈，Feedback）。在整个动态信息交互环路中，每一位参与者都在时刻交替扮演着发送者与接收者的双重角色。

Successful interpersonal communication occurs when the receiver understands the meaning of the message. However, sometimes the receiver does not interpret the message in the way the sender intended, and mistakes and hurt feelings can occur.

只有当接收者真正、完全领会了发送者所要传达的原汁原味意图时，一次人际沟通才算圆满成功！然而在现实中，接收者往往会对信息产生截然不同的主观解读，由此极易诱发严重的临床差错与心灵创伤。

At times, the sender may not be aware that the receiver does not understand the message as intended. As well, there is a possibility that the receiver has hearing difficulty or a cognitive problem.

有时发送者甚至根本未曾察觉接收者已经产生了严重误解。

The relationship between the sender and the receiver also affects their communication. Communication is easiest when the people involved understand and respect each other (FIGURE 9.1). 此外，接收者自身很可能罹患听力衰退或认知思维障碍。发送者与接收者之间的人际关系质量更是直接左右着沟通成败：当双方彼此尊重、互相信任时，沟通方能畅通无阻（图 9.1）。在跨学科医护团队、受助案主与其家属之间，存在着众多深刻左右信息解读的核心变量：

  • • Experience and confidence: Clients can often tell when support workers feel hesitant or uncertain about what they are instructing their clients to do. As a result, the client may hesitate to cooperate with the support worker's instructions and be resistant to care. It is important to try to communicate in an assertive and professional manner at all times.
    临床阅历与自信度（Experience and Confidence）：病榻上的案主具有极强的直觉，往往能瞬间嗅出护工言语中的迟疑、心虚或犹豫不决。一旦长者察觉护工底气不足，便会对护理指令产生严重疑虑，甚至拒绝配合照护。因此，你必须在任何时刻都努力展现出坚定自信与从容不迫的专业风范。

    心境与瞬时情绪（Mood and Emotions）：许多案主能敏锐感知到照护者内心的烦躁、愤怒或不悦，从而不敢开口求助。其直接恶果便是案主因害怕遭嫌弃而冒险独自下床，诱发致命跌倒重创！因此，护工必须时刻掌控自身情绪，理性应对案主的情绪骤变，并将关键观察如实汇报与记录。

    核心价值观差异（Values）：案主所秉持的人生信条、道德观念可能与你截然不同。作为受过正规法务培训的专业人员，你必须绝对尊重案主的言语表达方式，严禁以居高临下的姿态对案主的价值观品头论足或流露任何轻蔑。

To communicate effectively with clients, you need to understand, respect, and be sensitive to each client's unique situation and needs, including a client's culture and religion. Guidelines for Effective Verbal Communication

高效言语沟通实操黄金守则
  • • Choose your words carefully. Words must have the same meaning for both you and the other person. Try to avoid words with more than one meaning. For example, the words small, moderate, and large mean different things to different people. Is 'small' the size of a pea or the size of a walnut? Instead, use words that are specific and descriptive: Telling your supervisor that a client's temperature is 37.9°C is clearer than saying, 'His temperature is up.'
    字斟句酌，杜绝歧义：确保所选用的词汇对双方而言拥有完全一致的确切含义。坚决摈弃具有多重歧义的主观模糊词汇。例如“少量”、“中等”或“大量”，在不同人眼中有云泥之别——“少量伤口渗出物”究竟是豌豆大小还是核桃大小？必须使用高度精确量化的描述语言：向主管护士准确汇报“案主体温为 37.9°C”，远比含糊其辞地说“他体温升高了”要专业严谨千百倍！
  • • Be aware that emotions can affect your communication. A person who is angry or upset can unintentionally convey these feelings when communicating with others. This display of emotion may, in turn, confuse or upset the listener, who is not aware of the background context of why you are communicating in such a manner. It is important to maintain a positive attitude at all times when speaking to clients, their families, or co-workers.
    时刻觉察情绪对言语的潜意识污染：当一个人内心处于愤怒或烦躁状态时，即便言语内容本身客观，也极易在语调中不自觉地渗出敌意与不耐烦，令无辜的案主深感委屈恐慌。因此，在与案主、家属或同事交谈时，必须时刻保持积极从容的专业仪态。
  • • Use simple, everyday language. You will become familiar with medical terminology as you study and gain experience in health care. However, do not use these medical terms when communicating with clients and their families because these terms may be unfamiliar to them. Also, use correct grammar, and avoid vulgar words and slang. Be aware that culture may influence people's choice of words and how they use them. By using certain words, you may insult or confuse a person from another culture.
    使用通俗朴实的日常大白话：在专业研习中你将熟记海量医学术语，但面对案主与家属时严禁卖弄医学行话！坚持使用严谨规范的语法，彻底杜绝粗俗脏话与边缘俚语。警惕跨文化背景下的词汇陷阱，防范因词汇误用引发严重文化冒犯。
  • • Speak clearly, slowly, and distinctly. Do not mumble or speak quickly. Move your lips as you speak, slow down your speech, and pause between sentences. Do not, however, slow down your speech so much that your client feels embarrassed by it. Ensure the room in which you are communicating is well lit and quiet, so the client can see your facial expressions and body mannerisms. Do not shout or overexaggerate words; doing so will distort your speech.
    字正腔圆、语速沉稳缓慢：吐字清晰，严禁含糊咕哝或语速过快。说话时清晰转动唇形，在句意转换处适当自然停顿；但绝不能放慢到像在哄婴儿一般，否则会让成年案主感到尊严受辱。确保环境光线充足、室内静谧，使长者能清晰看清你的唇形神态。严禁大喊大叫或夸张嘴型，那只会彻底扭曲声音波形！
  • • Use visual clues. Write your message down for clients who cannot hear but can read, and use objects or pictures to help them understand. For example, show the client the meals they can choose from.
    充分依托视觉辅助媒介：面对有阅读能力的听障长者，主动将关键信息工整书写在白板上；善用实物或图谱菜单协助案主做决策（如将今日供应的两款实物餐盘直接呈现在长者眼前供其挑选）。
  • • Control the volume and tone of your voice. How your voice sounds sends a message. Do not shout, because shouting can suggest irritation or anger (FIGURE 9.3). Similarly, do not talk in a harsh or abrupt manner. As well, avoid speaking to adults in high-pitched tones as you might when speaking to children.
    严格把控嗓音声调与音量：嗓音的质感本身就在传递强烈的潜意识信号！严禁冲案主大声嚷嚷，呼喊往往等同于不耐烦与愤怒（图 9.3）。严禁使用生硬、尖刻、命令式的口吻。更要坚决杜绝用尖锐刺耳的“哄婴儿娃娃腔”（Elderspeak）同长者说话，那是对成年人生命的极大不敬！
  • • Be brief and concise. Do not add unrelated or unnecessary information. Focus on what you are saying, stay on the subject, and do not use too many words. Instructions that are too wordy may confuse the client and may reduce the possibility of the client understanding them. Speak in short sentences to emphasize your words, as short sentences are more clearly understood.
    简明扼要，直达核心：摒弃一切无关废话，紧扣当前主题。冗长复杂的复合长句只会让注意力受损的长者大脑过载陷入混乱。尽量使用清晰明了的精炼短句，逐句递进，确保信息接收准确无误。
  • • Present information in a logical manner. Organize your thoughts before you speak, and present them in sequence (in the right order). Think about what has happened, or what is going to happen, step by step.
    逻辑清晰，次序严谨：开口前在脑海中对信息进行条理化梳理，严格按照时间发生先后或临床处置步骤步步展开，引导长者思维顺畅跟进。
  • • Ask one question at a time. Give the client time to answer each question you ask, and do not rush them. Avoid providing the answer for the client.
    一次仅抛出一个问题：提出问题后，给予长者充足的时间组织语言作答，保持耐心绝不催促，更严禁迫不及待地替案主抢答！
  • • Determine understanding. Do not assume that the client understands what you are saying. Ask the client to repeat the message in their own words.
    闭环确认真实理解：绝不能一厢情愿地想当然认为长者已经听懂。温和邀请长者用他自己的话简要复述一遍，以确证信息没有发生走样偏差。
p. 134

Do not pretend to understand. If you do not understand what the client has said, ask them to restate or rephrase the message. Repeat the message, if needed, to indicate that you have understood it.

绝不不懂装懂、假装听懂：如果由于案主吐字含糊或语言障碍导致你没有听清，必须坦诚、礼貌地请长者重新复述或换一种说法。必要时主动向案主复述你所捕捉到的片段，向长者证实你确实在竭力理解他。

Humour

幽默艺术在医患沟通中的双刃剑效应

Humour is an important, but often underused, technique in communicating with clients and co-workers. Research has shown that laughter shared among clients, family members, and the support worker can reduce tension, increase trust, and promote bonding.

由衷的幽默是一门威力巨大却常被忽视的沟通法宝。现代临床医学研究表明，案主、家属与护工之间会心的欢笑，能显著瓦解内心的焦虑紧张，极大增进人际信任与情感联结。

When caring for clients whose first language is not the same as your own, however, you may not be able to use verbal humour, because it may be misunderstood or misinterpreted. Gauge whether the use of humour would be both comfortable for you as a support worker and appropriate for your client.

然而必须高度警惕：当案主与你的母语文化不同时，口头幽默极易因语境脱节演变为严重的冒犯或误解！护工必须敏锐评估现场情境，唯有在双方皆感到舒适且符合伦理的前提下方可适度施展幽默。

NONVERBAL COMMUNICATION

非语言沟通：体态语、触碰伦理与静默的崇高力量

In nonverbal communication, messages are sent without words through body language, touch, and the use of silence. The meaning and interpretation of messages sent through nonverbal communication varies, depending on both the sender's and receiver's age, gender, life experiences, and culture (see Chapter 7). Using active listening skills to observe your clients' body language can help you understand them better.

非语言沟通（Nonverbal Communication）完全摒弃文字语言，通过肢体体态、温情触碰以及深沉的静默（Silence）传递信息。非语言信号的内涵解读，极大取决于双方的代际年龄、性别差异、生活阅历与深层文化传统（详见第7章）。

Active listening is a nonjudgemental communication technique that focuses not only on understanding the content of what is being said but also on the underlying emotions and feelings conveyed by the sender. A person listens actively by concentrating fully on what is being said and paying attention to the client's nonverbal cues, as well as by conveying interest in the conversation by nodding, making eye contact, and responding when appropriate. This technique helps to develop rapport and fosters a trusting relationship.

运用积极倾听技巧全方位审视案主的肢体语言，能让你洞若观火：积极倾听绝非被动听声音，而是全神贯注凝视案主，通过适时的微微点头、真诚的目光平视接触与温和的响应，传递出无条件的关切与专注，由此与案主建立起牢不可破的信任羁绊。

For example, a slumped posture and a slow, shuffling walk may indicate that a client is not happy or is not feeling well. Sometimes, clients may say they feel fine, but their facial expressions may indicate that they are in pain. Nonverbal clues often reflect a person's true feelings, and because they are usually involuntary and unconscious, they may send messages more accurately than words can (see the Supporting Mr. Reyes box).

例如：案主耷拉着肩膀、双脚拖地缓慢挪步，往往是内心重度抑郁或躯体极度不适的写照。很多时候，长者口头上坚强地说“我没事，挺好的”，但紧锁的眉头与咬紧的牙关却无情出卖了其正遭受着剧痛折磨！非语言线索几乎完全由潜意识与植物神经自主支配，因而往往比精心修饰过的语言更能吐露一个人灵魂深处的真实状态（详见雷耶斯先生临床案例专栏）。

Supporting Mr. Reyes: Verbal and Nonverbal Behaviours Send Differing Messages

关怀雷耶斯先生：口头言语与非语言肢体行为的剧烈反差
You have been giving support care to Jim Reyes in his home for several months. His physical condition has deteriorated, and he now requires 24-hour care. You are making your last home visit today and ask him how he is doing. Mr. Reyes says, 'I am looking forward to moving to the nursing home. I am sure I will make some new friends.' However, you see tears in his eyes and he looks away from you. His verbal communication suggests that he is happy, but his nonverbal communication shows sadness. How can you give him support and comfort?
你在吉姆·雷耶斯先生（Jim Reyes）家中为其提供社区居家照护已有数月之久。近来老人的身体机能急剧恶化，已达到必须入住长期照护机构接受24小时全天候监护的法定标准。今天是你在老人搬离前的最后一次上门服务，当你温和关切地询问老人的状态时，雷耶斯先生强颜欢笑地说：‘我正眼巴巴盼着搬进养老院呢！我相信到了那里我肯定能结识不少新朋友！’然而，你分明看见老人眼眶里噙满了泪水，下意识地把头扭向窗外避开你的目光。老人的口头语言竭力表现出欢喜与期待，但其肢体语言却流露出无尽的凄凉、悲伤与对故居的深切眷恋！面对这种言行反差，你应当如何给予老人最有力的心灵抚慰？
Clinical Action Plan: 1. Acknowledge the underlying emotion rather than challenging the contradiction: Gently say, 'Mr. Reyes, moving away from a home full of memories is one of the hardest transitions in life. It's completely natural to feel heartbroken.' 2. Use silence and comforting touch: Sit beside him at eye level, place a warm, supportive hand on his forearm, and remain quietly present without forcing cheerful conversation.
【临床高阶实操指引】：1. 共情接纳深层情绪，绝不当面生硬拆穿矛盾：温和地对老人说：‘雷耶斯先生，告别自己居住了大半辈子、满载温暖回忆的家园，是人生中最艰难的关头。即便心里感到万分不舍与难过，也是最自然、最真实的情感。’ 2. 善用静默与肢体抚慰：在老人身旁端坐，保持平视眼神，将温暖的手掌轻轻托抚在老人的前臂上，静静陪伴，绝不用廉价轻浮的所谓乐观去否定老人真实的悲伤。

Body Language

肢体语言六大核心观察维度
  • • Posture: Standing tall conveys confidence, while slouching signals defeat or pain.
    躯体姿态（Posture）：昂首挺胸传递自信从容，而蜷缩伛偻往往表征身心耗竭、抑郁或内脏剧痛。
  • • Appearance: Dress, hygiene, and adornments (jewellery, perfume, tattoos, piercings, cosmetics).
    外貌着装仪容（Appearance）：包括个人衣着整洁度、卫生状况以及饰品（香水、纹身、穿孔与妆容）。
  • • Facial expressions: Smiles, grimaces, frowns, and raised eyebrows reveal instantaneous emotion.
    面部神情变化（Facial Expressions）：微笑、蹙眉、龇牙咧嘴或挑眉，瞬间折射出内心的喜怒哀乐。
  • • Body movements: Pacing, wringing hands, or rapid fidgeting show heightened anxiety.
    肢体动作（Body Movements）：踱步、搓手、抓挠衣角等动作，是焦虑失控的强烈躯体化表现。
  • • Eye contact: Direct eye contact signals honesty and focus, while avoidance conveys shame or evasion.
    眼神接触（Eye Contact）：适度的眼神凝视传递坦诚与专注，躲闪回避往往暗示自卑、羞愧或防卫。
  • • Gestures: Open hands invite trust, whereas clenching fists or crossed arms erect defensive barriers.
    手势动作（Gestures）：摊开双掌传递接纳包容，而紧握拳头或双手胸前交叉则构筑起森严的抗拒壁垒。
p. 135

Show respect to a client who is in a bed or wheelchair by sitting or squatting so that you are at eye level. Your general appearance also communicates information.

面对卧床或端坐轮椅的案主，必须主动拉过椅子端坐或单膝下蹲，使自己的视线与长者保持在同一水平高度（Eye Level），严禁以居高临下的压迫姿态俯视长者！

Some clients and their families may assume that you will provide poor care if you show up in a wrinkled or soiled uniform. Visible tattoos, especially with images such as skulls, dragons, or weapons, and body piercings may startle, frighten, or intimidate some clients.

你的整体仪容外表时刻都在无声传递专业信息：皱巴巴或沾染污渍的工服，会让案主和家属本能推定你的照护技能粗劣散漫。显眼的纹身图案（如骷髅、恶龙或武器）以及面部打孔饰品，极易惊吓或威慑某些体弱多病的高龄长者。

In some situations, you may need to consciously control your body language when providing care to clients. Do not react visibly, for example, to body odours; often such odours are beyond the control of clients. Your reaction is likely to increase a client's embarrassment and humiliation.

在特定特殊照护场景下，你必须高度自律地克制自身的本能肢体反应：例如遭遇人体排泄物或伤口腐败恶臭时，严禁当面捂鼻皱眉或流露恶心神态！长者往往对自己无法自控的体味深感无地自容，你任何一丝嫌弃的微表情都会瞬间击溃长者残存的全部自尊！

Touch

触碰伦理：温情抚慰与身体边界守护

Touch is a very important form of nonverbal communication. It can convey warmth, comfort, concern, affection, trust, and reassurance.

肢体触碰是非语言沟通中极具情感穿透力的载体。它能传递出言语无法企及的温情、抚慰、关切、深情、信任与定心骨。

For example, holding a person's hand can provide comfort, and gently stroking a person's shoulder or back can promote rest and relaxation. The use of touch is very personal. While most people respond well to a touch on their hand because it helps them feel less lonely, not everyone would feel comfortable being hugged or patted on the head.

例如，紧紧握住长者颤抖的手掌能瞬间驱散孤独，轻柔抚拍背部能促进身心松弛安眠。然而，触碰具有极强的个人私密属性！绝大多数案主乐于接受手部握持，但绝非人人都习惯被拥抱或抚摸头部。

Your touch should be gentle, not hurried or rushed, and should be restricted to 'public' areas of the body, such as the hands or shoulders. It is a good rule to ask permission from a client to hug them. Your employer and the care plan will direct your care.

你的触碰动作必须轻柔沉稳、绝无匆忙仓促，且必须严格限定于身体的“公开社交区域”（如手部或肩部）。在给予拥抱前主动征求案主知情同意是一项崇高的法务黄金法则。雇主机构规章与正式护理计划（Care Plan）将为你指明清晰的执业边界。

Touch can mean different things to different people. Pulling away or tensing of the body may indicate that the client does not want to be touched because they are in pain and it hurts to be touched.

触碰在不同案主心中承载着截然不同的心理重负。当案主身体骤然紧绷或本能向后瑟缩闪躲时，往往表明局部触碰引发了躯体剧痛，或者源于其文化传统中严苛的身体禁忌。

Or they may be uncomfortable with touch because of their cultural background. For some people who have experienced torture, spousal or child abuse (physical, sexual, or both), rape, or bullying, any touching from another person may be beyond uncomfortable. It is very important that you ask your clients for permission each time you touch their body or possessions.

更必须极其严肃对待的是：对于曾不幸遭受过战争酷刑、家庭暴力虐待、性侵犯或恶性霸凌的创伤后应激个案而言，任何来自外界未经许可的身体触碰，都会瞬间诱发噩梦般的重度惊恐发作！因此，在每一次触碰案主身体或挪动其贴身私人物品前，必须清晰开口征求许可！

Silence

静默相伴的疗愈力量

The use of silence can convey messages of acceptance, rejection, fear, or the need for quiet and time to think. As a support worker, you may find it difficult to accept silence when you want to comfort your client. Sometimes, however, especially during sad times, you do not need to say anything; just being there shows that you care.

静默（Silence）是一种极富张力的非语言表达：它既可能传递接纳与尊重，也可能表征恐慌或深思的诉求。许多初入职场的护工在面对案主的沉默时往往手足无措，急于用喋喋不休的言语去打破冷场。

Silence can give you and others time to organize thoughts and choose words. It is also useful when the client is making difficult decisions or is upset and is trying to regain control. In these situations, silence on your part shows respect and empathy for the client (see the Case Study: Silence and Touch During Sad Times box).

然而在案主遭遇重大丧亲噩耗或剧烈精神打击时，任何言语都是苍白无力的——静静守候在身旁本身，就是最伟大的关切！静默赋予彼此整理思绪的沉淀空间，在案主陷入悲痛竭力重构心理防线时，你的安静陪伴正是对案主至高无上的同理关怀与崇高敬意（详见悲痛时刻个案专栏）。

临床个案思维修炼：重大悲恸时刻的静默守候与温情触碰

You may have to be completely silent and comfort the client just with your touch if it is culturally allowed and the client gives permission. COMMUNICATION METHODS

专业沟通科学实操方法库：倾听、复述与同理心构建

Certain communication skills help you share information more effectively with others and result in better relationships with people.

掌握一系列经循证医学检验的高阶沟通技巧，能让你与案主的信息交互效能成倍提升，并在病榻前建立起深厚互信的疗愈性人际关系。

Active Listening Guidelines

积极倾听核心实操指引
  • • Face the client: Your client will often need to see your facial expressions and body language to be assured that you are listening.
    正面身体朝向案主：长者必须清晰看到你的面部表情与身体姿态，方能笃定确信你正在全神贯注倾听其倾诉。
  • • Make eye contact: Maintain appropriate, culturally sensitive eye contact to signal undivided attention.
    保持恰当目光接触：真诚平视，但必须审慎兼顾不同族裔对于眼神凝视的文化习俗与敏感边界。
  • • Lean toward the client: Do not sit back with your arms crossed. Crossed arms can be interpreted as an indication that you are not open to what the client is telling you.
    身体适度微微前倾：切忌向后深陷椅背、更严禁双臂交叉抱胸！双臂环抱在心理学上属于典型的封闭防御姿态，等于直接向案主宣告‘我对你说的话毫无兴趣’！
  • • Respond to your client: Nod, give verbal confirmations, but respect when a client wishes to remain silent.
    适时给予温和回应：通过微点头、简短呼应传递接纳，但当案主内心疲惫不愿交谈时，亦要懂得适时保持尊重性留白。
  • • Avoid communication barriers: Consciously eliminate all interruptions, premature advice, and distractions (see page 139).
    坚决肃清沟通壁垒：自觉克服随意打断、妄加说教、替案主下定论等恶习（详见第139页）。

Paraphrasing

释义复述（Paraphrasing）：三大临床价值与应用

Paraphrasing is restating another person's message in one's own words. A paraphrase usually has fewer words than the original message. Paraphrasing serves three purposes: 1. It shows that you are listening.

释义复述（Paraphrasing）是用倾听者自己精炼、清晰的词汇重新表述对方刚才所说的核心内容。成功的复述通常比原话更为精炼。

2. It lets both you and the sender know that you understood the message. 3. It promotes further communication. People usually respond well to a paraphrased statement.

复述在临床照护中承载着三大神圣目的：1. 向案主无可辩驳地证实你正在全神贯注倾听；2. 让双方当场闭环校验信息是否被完全准确理解；3. 极大地鼓舞并促使案主进一步敞开心扉深层表达。人们面对真诚的复述往往会产生深层的被认同感。

For example: Mrs. Cummings: 'I was a keen reader when I could see. I miss books so much. Those talking books are hard to follow.' You: 'You love stories, but talking books are not as good as real books.' Mrs. Cummings: 'Exactly. I wish you had time to read to me.'

【经典实战对话示例】：卡明斯太太叹息道：‘我以前眼睛好使的时候是个十足的书迷，现在我实在太想念捧着书阅读的感觉了。那些有声读物听起来真是让人难以跟上节奏。’ 护工运用释义复述回应：‘您非常热爱文学故事，但觉得有声书根本无法替代真正纸质书的质感与体验。’ 卡明斯太太眼前一亮连连称赞：‘完全没错！要是有空能有人给我读读书该有多好！’

Empathetic Listening

同理心共情倾听（Empathetic Listening）

Empathetic listening requires being attentive to the speaker's feelings. Empathy means being open to and trying to understand the experiences and feelings of others. It involves acknowledging the other person's point of view without judging.

同理心倾听要求照护者将全部注意力聚焦于诉说者的‘情绪感受’。同理心（Empathy）意味着全然敞开心扉，设身处地去体验并理解他人独特的生命遭遇与痛苦。它要求无条件承认对方看问题的视角，绝不妄加道德评判！

Clients need to know that they are understood. Empathy can help reduce feelings of loneliness and sadness and can create bonds of trust between a support worker and the clients they support. When paraphrasing, you acknowledge the other person's words. When empathizing, you acknowledge their feelings.

病榻上的长者迫切需要确信自己被理解、被看见。共情能大幅消融长者内心的孤独与哀恸，铸就医患互信的金石纽带。复述重在确认‘语言词汇’，而共情重在承接‘灵魂感受’！

Avoid quick, thoughtless responses such as the following: • 'I know how you feel.' (Nobody can ever know how another person feels.) • 'I feel sorry for you.' (This implies pity.) • 'I wouldn't want to be in your shoes.' (This suggests superiority and implies pity.) Consider these two responses to a complaint: Mr. Witowski: 'I can't believe they made me move to this new room. I felt settled in the other room, and now I feel completely disoriented.' Poor response: 'Don't worry, you'll get used to it.' Empathetic response: 'It must feel very frustrating and unsettling to be moved when you were finally feeling comfortable.'

坚决戒除轻率、廉价、不动脑子的口头敷衍套话：• 严禁说‘我完全理解你的感受’（人类悲欢并不相通，没有人能真正完全复制另一个人的深渊痛苦）；• 严禁说‘我真替你感到可怜’（这流露出高高在上的施舍与怜悯）；• 严禁说‘换成我可遭不住你这罪’（这带有隐蔽的优越感与无情施怜）。请对比维托夫斯基先生遭遇强制调换病房时的两则回应：老人愤慨抱怨：‘真不敢相信他们非逼我搬到这间陌生屋子，我在原先病房刚刚安顿顺当，现在整个人全蒙了！’ 粗暴回应：‘别愁了，过两天就习惯了。’ 同理共情回应：‘在好不容易适应了旧环境后突然被强行调换，心里一定觉得万分憋屈和局促不安。’

CASE STUDY: Using Skills of Paraphrasing and Questioning

Sophia provides personal care to 48-year-old Mr. Dupuis. He is severely disabled by multiple sclerosis. On her first visit, Sophia helped Mr. Dupuis shave and dress. On this second visit, she is to assist him with bathing and preparing breakfast.
支持工作者索菲亚（Sophia）负责照护48岁罹患重度多发性硬化症（MS）致残的杜普伊斯先生（Mr. Dupuis）。初次上门时索菲亚协助其完成了刮胡子与穿衣。今天第二次上门，其法定任务是协助老人完成擦浴与早餐制作。
Mr. Dupuis: 'Oh, it's you. I was still asleep. It's awfully early.'
Sophia: 'A 7:30 start is a little early for you. You're not ready for me.' (paraphrasing)
Mr. Dupuis: 'Yes, I've asked the case manager to start the morning care at 8:00 instead.'
Sophia: 'Perhaps she is working on the schedule change. I will check with the agency.'
Mr. Dupuis: 'Thanks.'
Sophia: 'The care plan calls for a bath today. Would you like to rest in bed until I prepare your breakfast and then do your bath after you have had your breakfast?' (closed question)
Mr. Dupuis: 'It doesn't matter much. I wish I didn't need to take a bath. Being bathed by someone else is not much fun.'
Sophia: 'Can you tell me what you dislike about your bath?' (open-ended question)
Mr. Dupuis: 'The lack of privacy really bothers me.'
Sophia: 'We can work together on giving you privacy.'
Mr. Dupuis: 'That would be a good idea. I don't think my last support worker cared much about my privacy.'
Sophia: 'We will work on this, and I will try to provide you with more privacy.'
杜普伊斯先生神色恹恹：‘哦，是你啊。我刚还在做梦呢，这来得实在太早了。’
索菲亚运用【释义复述】：‘早晨7:30对您来说确实早了点，您还没做好起床准备呢。’
杜普伊斯先生点头认可：‘可不是嘛，我已经跟个案经理申请把晨间护理顺延到8点整了。’
索菲亚积极跟进：‘个案经理可能正在统筹协调排班，今天回机构我一定帮您再次核实推进。’
老人语气稍缓：‘多谢了。’
索菲亚紧接着提出【封闭式提问】：‘护理计划安排今天擦浴。您看是先躺在床上再歇会儿、等我把早餐做好吃完再洗，还是现在洗？’
老人叹了口气：‘无所谓了，我真希望自己根本用不着洗澡。被别人扒光了伺候擦澡，可一点都不好受。’
索菲亚敏锐捕捉到抵触情绪，立即抛出【开放式提问】：‘能跟我聊聊在擦浴这件事上，究竟是哪一点让您心里最不舒服吗？’
老人终于吐露真言：‘完全丧失个人隐私，这真的让我尊严扫地、无比难堪。’
索菲亚郑重承诺：‘我们今天完全可以携手配合，把隐私防护做到极致！’
老人深受触动：‘这太好了！我觉得上一位护工根本不在乎我的隐私死活。’
索菲亚坚定回应：‘请您放心，我们一起改进，我一定尽全力守护您的绝对隐私！’

Closed-Ended Questions vs Open-Ended Questions

封闭式提问与开放式提问的临床定夺

Closed-ended questions are structured so that the response can be restricted to one word such as yes or no or to a few words. Use closed questions when you need specific, factual information quickly. For example: • 'Are you in pain?' • 'Did you take your medication?' • 'Do you prefer tea or coffee?' While closed questions save time, they limit conversation and do not encourage the client to share deeper feelings.

封闭式提问（Closed-Ended Questions）的逻辑结构将对方的回答严格收敛至‘是’、‘否’或极简短的客观字词。当急需快速排查危急体征或采集特定临床数据时，封闭式提问具有无可替代的决断价值：• ‘您现在伤口疼吗？’ • ‘降压药您刚才服下了吗？’ • ‘早餐饮品您更偏好红茶还是黑咖啡？’ 封闭式提问虽极其高效省时，但会切断对话深度，无法启发案主抒发深层情感。

Open-ended questions invite a person to share thoughts, feelings, or ideas. They encourage the client to talk and give you deeper insight into what the client is experiencing.

开放式提问（Open-Ended Questions）热情鼓励案主畅所欲言，分享其内在思想、情绪与见解，能让护工深度窥见案主所经历的心灵波澜。开放式提问通常以‘什么’、‘如何’、‘能否跟我多聊聊...’切入：• ‘您昨夜睡眠质量如何？’ • ‘关于回家休养后的生活，您心里都有哪些打算？’ • ‘关于这件事，您能再多跟我说说吗？

Open-ended questions usually begin with 'What,' 'How,' or 'Can you tell me about...'. For example: • 'How did you sleep last night?' • 'What are your plans for when you go home?' • 'Can you tell me more about that?' Avoid questions that begin with 'Why,' because 'Why' can sound demanding, judgmental, and accusatory, putting the client on the defensive.

’ 【执业避坑天条】：严禁以‘为什么’（Why）作为提问开头！‘你为什么不吃药？’、‘你为什么又哭了？’在心理学上带有强烈的审问、责难与指责意味，会瞬间激发长者的敌意防卫与心理封闭！

p. 138

Use open-ended questions in combination with closed questions to find out about a client's needs and preferences and to find out if a client is satisfied with your care. For example, a closed question ('Are you comfortable?') can give you the necessary information. An open-ended question ('Is there anything else I can do to make you more comfortable?') allows the client to expand and express unmet needs.

在临床实务中，应当将封闭式提问与开放式提问有机交织运用，以此全方位把脉案主的护理需求、个人偏好以及对今日服务的满意度。例如：以封闭式提问快速摸底（‘您现在的体位躺着舒适吗？’），随即衔接开放式提问（‘还有什么是我能为您微调、好让您更舒坦一些的吗？’），赋予长者充分表达未尽诉求的广阔空间。

Clarifying and Focusing

澄清确认（Clarifying）与聚焦引导（Focusing）

Clarifying lets you make sure that you understand the message. Ask the client to explain further if you do not understand.

澄清确认（Clarifying）是消除歧义的严密屏障：当你对案主的含糊言语哪怕有一丝疑虑时，绝不装懂，立即温和求证：‘我不太确定是否完全理解了您的意思，您能再换个方式跟我细说说吗？

For example: 'I'm not sure I understand what you mean. Could you explain that again?' Focusing is limiting the conversation to a certain topic. It is useful when a client rambles, wanders off the subject, or jumps from topic to topic. For example, if a client is talking about three different symptoms simultaneously, you can gently refocus: 'You mentioned having difficulty breathing this morning. Let's talk about that first.'

’ 聚焦引导（Focusing）则是将发散对话牢牢拉回正轨的罗盘：当长者因思维迟缓、焦虑而东拉西扯、漫无边际碎碎念时，护工必须温和而坚定地予以聚焦：‘您刚才提到了今天清晨胸口憋闷喘不上气，这件事关乎安全最重要，咱们先集中把这个情况彻底说明白。’

COMMUNICATION BARRIERS

临床沟通壁垒：打断、说教、轻视与冷漠的致命危害

Certain behaviours can also create communication barriers. Improve communication with clients by avoiding the following: interrupting, answering your own questions, giving advice, minimizing problems, using patronizing language, and failing to listen.

在临床人际交往中，许多照护者不良的行为习惯会筑起坚不可摧的隔阂高墙！想要打通与案主的心灵桥梁，必须严正杜绝以下六大沟通暗礁：随意打断插话、自问自答抢话、好为人师妄加说教、轻描淡写淡化案主痛苦、使用居高临下的蔑视语言、以及心不在焉拒绝倾听。

  • • Interrupting: Interrupting a person stops communication and conveys that you do not consider what the person is saying to be important. Wait patiently for the person to finish speaking before responding.
    粗暴打断插话（Interrupting）：在中途蛮横掐断对方的话头，无异于直接向对方宣判‘我认为你说的全是废话，一文不值’！必须保持高度修养，耐心等待案主完全表达完毕后再从容开口。
  • • Answering one's own questions: Asking a question and then immediately providing the answer yourself deprives the client of the opportunity to think and express their own thoughts.
    自问自答抢话（Answering One's Own Questions）：刚刚抛出问题，不等案主反应便迫不及待替其作答，彻底剥夺了思维迟缓长者独立思考与主张权利的生命机会。
  • • Giving advice: Telling a client what they should or should not do suggests that you know better than the client. Support workers are not permitted to give medical, legal, or personal life advice. Guide the client to consult the nurse or doctor.
    好为人师妄加说教（Giving Advice）：擅自指导长者‘你应该这样做、不应该那样做’，流露出狂妄自大的说教心态。支持工作者在法律上严禁跨越执业红线向案主提供任何医疗、法律或个人生活抉择指导，必须引导其咨询专科医生或主管护士。
  • • Minimizing problems: Making light of a client's concerns or responding with false reassurances (such as 'Don't worry, everything will be fine') dismisses the client's genuine fear and destroys trust.
    轻描淡写淡化痛苦（Minimizing Problems）：面对绝症长者的深重恐慌，随口甩出一句‘别瞎操心，一切都会好起来的’虚伪廉价安慰，是对长者真实苦难的残忍漠视与精神霸凌，会瞬间彻底摧毁医患互信！

Using patronizing language makes clients feel small, incompetent, and childlike. Failing to listen sends the message that you do not care about the person. Active, engaged presence is required in every clinical interaction.

居高临下的贬损语言会让成年案主感到自己微不足道、软弱无能且如同幼童般受人摆布。而心不在焉、左耳进右耳出的假倾听，更是在赤裸裸向长者传递‘我对你的死活毫无兴趣’的冷酷信号！在每一次临床交汇中，全身心的专注与敬意是支持工作者最基本的道德操守。

职场专业礼仪与多学科团队协作规范

PSYCHOLOGICAL DEFENCE MECHANISMS

心理防御机制：人类潜意识避风港与八大病理应对模式

People subconsciously use defence mechanisms to avoid uncomfortable feelings such as anxiety, guilt, stress, or embarrassment. Defence mechanisms are psychological strategies used to cope with or avoid stressful realities. As a support worker, understanding these mechanisms will help you interpret difficult client behaviours and maintain an empathetic, nonjudgmental approach.

当人类脆弱的心灵遭遇无法承受的重度焦虑、内疚自责、极度难堪或绝症危机时，大脑潜意识会自发启动心理防御机制（Defence Mechanisms）！这些无意识的心理应对策略旨在通过扭曲客观现实或转移焦点，延缓或逃避剧烈的心理痛苦。作为专业支持工作者，深入洞悉这些机制的病理根源，将帮助你在面对案主的无理取闹、恶语相向或冷漠抗拒时，穿透现象直抵本质，始终坚守不加评判的崇高同理心。

  • • Denial: Refusal to accept reality or fact. For example, a client newly diagnosed with terminal cancer insists, 'The lab made a mistake with my biopsy; I just have a slight cold.'
    否认机制（Denial）：断然拒绝承认客观存在的残酷事实。例如一位刚刚被确诊为终末期癌症晚期的长者，执拗地对所有人坚称：‘化验所肯定是把我的活检报告弄混了！我身体硬朗得很，不过是受了点风寒而已！’
  • • Displacement: Transferring angry feelings from the true source of stress to a safer, less threatening target. For example, a resident who is furious with his physician yells at the support worker for placing his water glass slightly too far away.
    转移宣泄机制（Displacement）：将针对某一令其恐惧或无法抗衡的对象（如主治专科医生）所积压的滔天怒火与屈辱，无端转移发泄至另一个弱势、安全的无辜第三方（如病榻前细心照料他的护工）身上，仅仅因为水杯摆放位置稍有偏差便破口大骂。
  • • Fantasizing: Using daydreaming or imaginary scenarios to escape from painful reality. For example, a bedridden client with severe spinal injury spends hours detailing how he will run a marathon next spring.
    幻想逃避机制（Fantasizing）：通过沉溺于虚构的极乐白日梦境来逃避严酷现实。例如一位因严重高位截瘫彻底卧床的案主，终日喋喋不休地向人描绘自己明年开春去参加全马长跑并夺冠的虚幻图景。
  • • Projection: Attributing one's own unacceptable feelings, impulses, or faults onto another person. For example, a client who feels intense hostility toward staff accuses the support worker: 'Why do you hate me so much?'
    投射机制（Projection）：把自己内心深处无法容忍、难以启齿的阴暗心理、过失或恶意，无端投射并强加在他人头上。例如一位内心对所有护士抱有强烈敌意的长者，反咬一口质问护工：‘你为什么成天这么恨我、想方设法害我？’
  • • Rationalization: Creating false but logical-sounding excuses to justify unacceptable behaviour or shortcomings. For example, a client who violates diabetic diet restrictions by eating cake says, 'If the dietary staff gave me better meals, I wouldn't be forced to buy treats.'
    合理化文饰（Rationalization）：为自身的不当过错、自控力崩塌编造冠冕堂皇、看似正当的逻辑借口。例如严重糖尿病案主偷吃高糖奶油蛋糕导致血糖飙升，却振振有词：‘要不是院里食堂饭菜难吃，我能被逼得自己买甜品充饥吗？’
  • • Reaction Formation: Acting in a way that is diametrically opposed to one's true, uncomfortable subconscious feelings. For example, a client who deeply resents having an assigned roommate behaves with over-the-top, exaggerated affection and flatter toward that roommate.
    反向形成机制（Reaction Formation）：外显言行与潜意识中的真实感受展现出180度的大倒转。例如一位内心对强制安排的同房病友极其厌恶、恨不得对方立即搬走的长者，表面上却表现出极其夸张的过分亲昵、肉麻奉承与殷勤讨好。
  • • Regression: Retreating to an earlier developmental stage of behaviour to cope with stress. For example, a 75-year-old client hospitalized for hip surgery throws temper tantrums, weeps inconsolably, and sucks his thumb.
    倒退退行机制（Regression）：面对难以承受的精神重压时，心智言行大幅倒退至幼儿期。例如一位75岁因髋关节骨折住院的老人，在病床上如同两岁幼童般肆意撒泼打滚、嚎啕大哭、甚至把大拇指含在嘴里用力吮吸。
  • • Repression: The involuntary, subconscious blocking of traumatic memories or unbearable anxiety from conscious awareness.
    潜抑机制（Repression）：中枢神经不由自主地将极其恐怖、引发巨大创伤的剧痛记忆强行封锁并深埋于潜意识深渊，使其无法进入日常意识显现。
  • • Suppression: The conscious, deliberate decision to push a painful thought or reality out of mind. For example, a client says, 'I will not think about my biopsy results until Monday morning.'
    压制机制（Suppression）：在理智清醒的状态下，主观上明确决定暂时将痛苦的客观现实强行搁置一旁。例如案主平静地说：‘在下周一医生正式找我谈话之前，我决不再去想肿瘤病理结果这件事。’

When a client uses a defence mechanism, remember that it is a subconscious response to fear or stress. Do not argue, contradict, or try to 'break through' the defence.

当受助案主启动心理防御机制时，支持工作者必须在内心时刻提醒自己：这是当事人潜意识在面对无法承受的恐惧与病痛打击时自发筑起的心灵保护壳！严禁当面与长者针锋相对地激烈争辩、当场无情戳穿谎言或企图生硬击碎其防御防线！

For example, if a client in denial insists they can walk when their legs are paralyzed, do not say, 'You can't walk, stop lying.' Report your observations to the supervisor.

BOX 9.2: Communicating With an Angry Client

• Recognize that the client is feeling frustrated or frightened. Put yourself in the client's situation. How would you feel? How would you want to be treated?

COMMUNICATING ASSERTIVELY

坚定自信沟通风格：在同理接纳与专业原则之间构筑定海神针

Assertiveness is a style of communication in which thoughts and feelings are expressed positively and directly without offending others. An assertive person appears confident, calm, and composed. Assertive communication stands in sharp contrast to aggressive communication (which attacks, demeans, or violates the rights of others) and passive communication (which involves hesitancy, submissiveness, and failing to stand up for professional standards).

坚定自信沟通（Assertiveness）是一种崇高而成熟的专业沟通境界：以坦诚、直接、从容的方式清晰陈述自己的观点、事实与伦理边界，同时全程倾注对他人人格尊严的最高敬意。坚定自信者外在展现出气定神闲、举止沉稳优雅与从容不迫的非凡定力。这一风格与具有毁灭性的“攻击型沟通”（Aggressive，肆意攻击谩骂、践踏他人权利）以及窝囊憋屈的“被动屈从型沟通”（Passive，唯唯诺诺、吞声忍气、丧失专业底线）形成了鲜明、尖锐的云泥之别！

CASE STUDY: Communicating Assertively

临床个案实战深度辨析：新人护工卡拉面对截肢案主暴怒挑衅的三种抉择
Kara just graduated as a support worker. Her first job is at a long-term care facility, where she was hired to replace Debbie. Mr. Beruti is a 28-year-old client who has had both his arms amputated. While Kara is shaving him, he shouts: 'Be careful! You almost nicked me. You obviously don't know what you're doing. The nurse told me this is your first job. You're not nearly as good as Debbie. If you don't get better at this, I'll report you!'
支持工作者卡拉（Kara）刚刚以优异成绩从专业院校毕业，在长期照护中心迎来了职业生涯的首份正式工作，接替此前离职的资深护工黛比。案主贝鲁蒂先生（Mr. Beruti）年仅28岁，在惨烈工伤中痛失双臂、接受了双侧高位截肢手术。清晨卡拉手持剃须刀耐心为其修面，贝鲁蒂先生突然暴跳如雷大声咆哮：‘你给我长点眼！刚才差点割破我的喉咙！你这一看就是个什么都不会的生瓜蛋子！护士早告诉我这是你第一天上班！你照黛比差了十万八千里！你要是手艺再这么烂，我立马向主管投诉举报开除你！’
Response Option 1 (Aggressive & Hostile): 'Report me if you like, Mr. Beruti. I wouldn't have been assigned to you if I were useless. You're just missing Debbie. I can assure you that I am just as qualified as she is. I won't tolerate your abuse.'
Analysis: This response is aggressive and hostile. It makes a judgment by assuming that Mr. Beruti misses Debbie. It shows no empathy or respect, and it completely disregards his legitimate safety fears.
【应对策略一：攻击对抗型（极其恶劣）】：卡拉柳眉倒竖反唇相讥：‘你想投诉就尽管去投，贝鲁蒂先生！要是我真没本事，机构能派我来伺候你吗？你无非就是心里放不下黛比罢了！我告诉你，我跟她拿一样的执业文凭！我可绝不惯着你这臭脾气！’
【专家剖析】：这一回应属于典型的人格攻击与敌意对抗。不仅妄自揣度案主心理，更毫无同理心与基本敬意，直接彻底粉碎了案主对剃刀抵住咽喉的安全感！
Response Option 2 (Passive & Submissive): 'I'm so sorry, Mr. Beruti. I am so clumsy and rough. I'll try to do better.'
Analysis: This response is passive and weak. It suggests that Kara completely lacks professional confidence and implies that she genuinely doubts her own clinical competence, which only intensifies the client's terror that he will be injured.
【应对策略二：被动屈从型（极其糟糕）】：卡拉吓得瑟瑟发抖带着哭腔：‘对不起对不起，贝鲁蒂先生，都怪我笨手笨脚、粗心大意，我以后一定尽力改，求您别投诉我...’
【专家剖析】：这一回应窝囊软弱至极！卡拉的主动贬低不仅暴露自身缺乏底气，更坐实了自己‘技术粗劣’的指控，只会让本就吓得半死的年轻双臂截肢案主陷入更大的绝望惊恐之中！
Response Option 3 (Assertive & Compassionate - PROFESSIONAL GOLD STANDARD): 'It's hard when caregivers do things in different ways. I can assure you that your safety and comfort are my top priorities. Can you tell me how you prefer to be shaved?'
Analysis: This response is compassionate yet assertive. It reassures Mr. Beruti immediately. It shows that Kara is entirely confident in her professional shaving skills, recognizes that every support worker has unique hand movements, and is genuinely open to adapting her technique to his precise comfort preferences.
【应对策略三：坚定自信同理型（行业崇高黄金典范）】：卡拉神色从容平稳，目光真诚坚定地注视着老人的双眼，微笑着柔声说道：‘当习惯的照护者换了新人、手法存在细微差异时，心里确实会感到万分悬空和不踏实。但我向您郑重承诺：您的绝对人身安全与极致舒适，是我心中不可动摇的最高准则！您能否指导指导我，平日里黛比在刮哪个部位时角度是怎样的、您最享受怎样的修面方式？’
【专家剖析】：这一回应堪称教科书级别的神来之笔！它瞬间瓦解了案主的防御敌意，既承认了手法差异的客观现实，又以坚如磐石的自信确立了安全担当，并将案主从被动的‘待宰受害者’升华为平等的‘指导合作者’！

• Put the client at ease: Relax and smile. Do not give the impression that you are in a hurry. If the client senses you are tense or rushed, learning will be difficult or impossible.

BOX 9.4: Tips for Communicating With People Living With Dementia

专栏 9.4：加拿大阿尔茨海默病协会认知症全方位沟通实操守则
What to Say:
• Try to be positive, cheerful, and encouraging at all times.
• Avoid asking too many direct questions. People with dementia can become frustrated if they can't find the answer. If you have to ask, ask questions one at a time, and phrase them in a way that allows for a 'yes' or 'no' answer.
• Try not to ask the person to make complicated decisions. Giving choices is important, but limit options to two clear items (e.g., 'Would you like the blue sweater or the green one?').
• If the person doesn't understand, rephrase the message in a different, simpler way rather than repeating the same words louder.
• Never contradict or argue over incorrect facts. If a resident says something factually wrong, steer the conversation around the topic rather than arguing. Look behind the content to the underlying feeling.
【言语表达黄金法则】：
• 始终保持积极、温和、富有生机的语调；
• 严禁连珠炮式抛出复杂的开放式提问！认知受损的长者在无法调取大脑记忆词汇时会陷入极度崩溃与狂躁。必须单一提问，尽量转化为简易的‘是/否’辨析；
• 坚决杜绝让长者做复杂的长远决策。提供选择至关重要，但必须严格限定于二选一（‘您今天穿这件蓝毛衣，还是这件绿毛衣？’）；
• 当长者听不懂时，切忌扯着嗓子大声重复原话！应迅速换用更浅白生动的日常短句或辅以实物手势重述；
• 绝对红线天条：严禁当面纠错或争辩客观事实！当长者坚称‘我妈妈在外面等我放学回家’时，严禁残忍驳斥‘你妈早死了几十年了’，而应温柔顺应：‘您一定非常想念妈妈做的热汤面吧？跟我讲讲妈妈的故事好吗？’，敏锐体察并共情其底层的思念与孤独。
Listening & Nonverbal Guidelines:
• Listen patiently without interrupting. Allow plenty of time for the person to find words.
• Maintain eye level. Sit beside or in front of the resident so you are not looming over them.
• Eliminate environmental distractions: Turn off blaring televisions, close doors, and ensure peaceful lighting before speaking.
• Use gentle touch appropriately: Holding a hand or placing a comforting hand on the shoulder reassures safety.
• Validate emotions: Acknowledge feelings rather than facts. Validation provides profound security.
【倾听与非语言环境实操规约】：
• 秉持圣徒般的耐心静默倾听，即便长者语无伦次也绝不粗暴打断；
• 严格保持平视（Eye Level），坚决杜绝像巨人般居高临下俯视卧床长者；
• 彻底肃清环境噪音：开口前先随手关闭嘈杂刺耳的电视广播，营造安详静谧的沟通气场；
• 适度施展温情肢体抚慰：轻轻托抚长者的小臂或双手，给予无可估量的安全感；
• 践行共情确认（Validation）：接纳情绪感受，淡化客观事实，为困顿于迷雾中的灵魂构筑温暖避风港。

CHAPTER REVIEW: KEY POINTS

本章核心知识点精要回顾
  • • The communication process is the means by which people exchange information. Communication skills are used in all environments, including at work, school, and home.
    沟通过程是人类社会交换思想、情报与情感的基本途径。沟通技能在一切生活场景中——包括职场、院校与家庭生活，皆发挥着决定性基石作用。
  • • Interpersonal communication is the exchange of information between two people, usually face to face.
    人际沟通是两人或多人之间所发生的信息交互，在照护实务中主要体现为面对面的直接互动。
  • • Messages sent are sometimes misunderstood by the receiver because factors such as perception, experience, physical and mental health, emotions, values, beliefs, culture, gender, and age can influence understanding.
    发出的信息之所以常遭接收者误解，是由于知觉偏好、过往阅历、身心健康状况、瞬时情绪、价值观、信仰文化、性别差异与代际年龄等海量变量的深刻干扰。
  • • To communicate effectively with words, you need to choose your words carefully; use simple, everyday language; speak clearly, slowly, and distinctly; control the volume and tone of your voice; be concise; present information in a logical manner; ask one question at a time; determine understanding; and do not pretend to understand.
    为了实现高效言语沟通，你必须：字斟句酌、使用通俗大白话、字正腔圆且语速从容、严格掌控嗓音音量与声调、简明扼要精炼表达、按逻辑条理推进、一次仅提一个问题、闭环确认真实理解、且绝不懂装懂假装听懂。
  • • Before starting and while doing a task, explain to the client what you are doing, regardless of how routine the task might feel to you.
    在开启任何照护操作之前以及执行全过程中，无论该操作在你眼中显得多么习以为常、日复一日，都必须向案主清晰解释每一步骤！
  • • Professional communication requires being courteous and ensuring confidentiality at all times.
    专业沟通严正要求在任何执业时刻始终保持温和谦逊的礼仪素养，并不折不扣坚守绝对的病历隐私保密天条。
  • • Workplace etiquette requires support workers to behave in a professional, ethical, friendly, and respectful manner to co-workers at all times, including while on breaks.
    职场礼仪守则要求支持工作者在任何时间（包括工间休息期间），对全体同事与团队成员始终恪守崇高专业、伦理自律、友好温和且相互尊重的崇高行为操守。
  • • Nonverbal communications are messages sent without words through body language, touch, and the use of silence.
    非语言沟通完全依托肢体语言（体态、眼神、手势）、温情触碰以及深沉的静默来传递情感信息。
  • • Communication methods include active listening, paraphrasing, empathetic listening, asking closed questions, asking open-ended questions, clarifying, and focusing.
    核心沟通科学实操技巧涵盖：积极倾听、释义复述、同理心共情倾听、封闭式提问、开放式提问、澄清确认以及聚焦引导。
  • • Communication barriers include interrupting, answering one's own questions, giving advice, minimizing problems, using patronizing language, and failing to listen.
    必须坚决杜绝的严重沟通壁垒包括：随意打断插话、自问自答抢话、好为人师妄加说教、轻描淡写淡化案主痛苦、使用居高临下的轻蔑贬损语言、以及心不在焉拒绝倾听。
  • • People subconsciously use defence mechanisms to avoid uncomfortable feelings such as anxiety, guilt, stress, or embarrassment. Defence mechanisms include denial, displacement, fantasizing, projection, rationalization, regression, repression, and suppression.
    人类潜意识启动心理防御机制旨在逃避焦虑、内疚、重压或极度难堪等负面痛苦感受。常见机制涵盖：否认、转移宣泄、幻想白日梦、投射、合理化借口、倒退退行、潜抑以及压制。
  • • At times, support workers will be faced with angry clients. Effective communication is important to preventing and dealing with anger.
    支持工作者必然会在一线遭遇处于狂怒暴躁中的案主，卓越高效的沟通技巧是防范冲突升级、平息怒火的唯一法宝。
  • • Assertiveness is a style of communication in which thoughts and feelings are expressed positively and directly without offending others.
    坚定自信沟通是一种从容境界：以积极、坦诚、直接的方式清晰表达自身观点与诉求，绝不退缩屈从，亦绝不冒犯凌辱他人。
  • • When teaching clients new tasks, describe to the client the steps in the task, show the client how to do each step, have the client try each step, and review the client's success with each step.
    在协助案主学习康复新机能时，严格依循四步教学模型：详尽描述分解动作、亲身示范规范步骤、鼓励案主亲自上手尝试、并由衷复盘肯定微小进步。
  • • It is especially important to pay attention to your verbal and nonverbal messages when communicating with people living with dementia. As clients lose their language skills, they rely more heavily on following nonverbal cues.
    在与罹患认知症（失智症）长者交流时，必须高度自律把控非语言体态信号：随着语言逻辑中枢的退化，长者将几乎全盘依托照护者的眼神、微笑与轻柔举止来感知世界！

临床批判性思维修炼：长期照护机构突发人手短缺时的伦理与排班抉择

You are employed at a long-term care facility. A co-worker did not show up for work. You and the other staff members have extra work.

【临床高阶困境推演】：你在一家大型长期照护中心担任前线专职支持工作者。今天清晨，同组的一名护工同事突发意外旷工未到岗。排班主管不得不将该名同事负责的一整条照护走廊的繁重任务，均摊给你和留守的其余同事。

How do you respond? Can you refuse to do the extra work assigned to you? Do you complain or keep silent about this situation, maintaining a positive attitude? How will you plan, prioritize, and manage the extra work?

面对陡然剧增的工作量，你内心将作何反应？在法律与劳动合同框架下，你能否当场断然拒绝主管委派给你的额外照护任务？你是应当当众喋喋不休愤怒抱怨，还是懂得克制情绪、保持积极专业的从容风范？你将依托何等科学的方法来重新制定计划、厘清轻重缓急优先级，从而在绝不牺牲长者绝对生命安全的前提下掌控这一突发危机？

Clinical Practice Guidelines: 1. Legal & Professional Duty of Care: In long-term care and healthcare settings, abandoning residents or refusing legally assigned emergency duties without just cause constitutes resident abandonment and professional misconduct. The safety and basic physiological needs of vulnerable residents must be met.

【支持工作者高阶实操指引】：1. 法务与崇高照护信托天条：在长期照护机构中，受助长者属于绝对弱势失能群体。在突发人手短缺紧急关头，无故当场拒绝履行合理委派的照护任务，在法律上属于触目惊心的渎职与遗弃服务对象（Resident Abandonment）！

2. Attitude & Etiquette: Venting anger or complaining loudly in front of residents or in hallway corridors terrifies frail residents and destroys team morale. Maintain composure and a supportive attitude.

长者的生命安全与基本生存需求拥有压倒一切的最高法律权重。2. 职业修养与团队礼仪：在病房走廊或案主面前长吁短叹、破口大骂或散布怨气，不仅会令体弱长者陷入大难临头的恐慌，更会彻底击溃团队防线。

3. Priority Triaging (SMART Planning): Meet immediately with the charge nurse to review the assignment. Triage tasks strictly: Vital safety care (toileting, positioning, scheduled medications assistance, meals, emergency call lights) MUST be done. Non-essential elective grooming or deep linen changes may be rescheduled with nurse authorization.

成熟的专业人员懂得深呼吸保持内心宁静。3. 科学优先级分类法（Triaging）：立即主动与主管注册护士碰头召开两分钟战术会议，对今日任务执行军规级优先级重组：第一优先级为长者绝对生命安全（按时协助如厕、翻身防褥疮、喂饭、防跌倒与即时响应呼叫铃）；第二优先级为一般身体擦浴；非紧急的指甲修剪、衣柜整理等可选项目，经主管护士签字同意后可合理顺延至明日，确保绝不发生医疗事故！
