# 《重生真意》（中英双语权威典藏版）
### The True Meaning of Being Born Again
**作者**：王明道（Wang Mingdao，1900–1991）
**著述时间**：一九三三年三月七日 · 北平（北京）
**初版印行**：北平《灵食季刊社》（Spiritual Food Quarterly）

> **全书导言**：
> 《重生真意》是中国教会二十世纪杰出领袖、北京基督徒会堂创办人王明道先生的里程碑式属灵经典著作。全书以圣经神学第一性原理为根基，以清晰明快的笔触、透彻有力的论理与痛彻心扉的牧者心肠，系统剖析了救恩神学中最关键的核心命题——“重生”。
> 
> 全书共分自序与五大核心篇章：
> 1. **自序**：剖析四等称为基督徒之人的属灵状态，确立检验真伪生命的试金石；
> 2. **一、重生是什么意思？**：解明从水和圣灵生、真道与圣灵的活泼大能，辨明“里面的人”与“外面的人”；
> 3. **二、为什么人必须重生？**：借神的至圣之眼洞察人类全然败坏的真面目，彻底粉碎“靠社会改良、道德教育可以自救”的人本幻梦；
> 4. **三、人得了重生有什么表现？**：借风之比喻，系统阐发新生命在恨罪、爱圣洁、爱神爱人、渴慕圣道上不可隐藏的活泼明证；
> 5. **四、怎样方能得重生？**：借旷野摩西举铜蛇之典故，揭示认罪、知死、绝望、仰望十架中保四步救恩真道，宣告即刻信靠、立时得救的赦免确据；
> 6. **五、得了重生的人还能犯罪吗？**：深刻剖析信徒内在“新人与旧人”的属灵交战、羊与猪的生命本质分野、三大仇敌之围困，以及靠常活元帅基督得胜有余的终极安慰。

### 【中文】
「重生」这个名词是大多数称为基督徒的人所熟悉的。但是真明白它的意义的人究竟有多少？真明白它的意义而且有过这种经验的人又究竟有多少？这却是一个严重的问题了。谈到重生的道理，我可以将所有称为基督徒的人分作四种：

### 【English】
The term "born again" (regeneration) is familiar to the majority of those who call themselves Christians. Yet how many truly comprehend its meaning? And how many truly understand its significance and have actually experienced it for themselves? This is indeed a sobering and weighty question. When speaking of the doctrine of the new birth, I would categorize all who are called Christians into four distinct classes:

---

### 【中文】
第一种是明白重生的道理而且有过重生经验的。这种信徒是最好的。他们知道自己所站的地位，明了他们所得的恩典。他们的人生是满有指望的。他们毫不疑惑他们自己得救的事。他们过着一种与世人不同的生活。他们向别人有可作的见证，也能清楚指导别人蒙恩得救。

### 【English】
The first class consists of those who understand the doctrine of regeneration and have also experienced the new birth. Such believers are in the most blessed condition. They know the standing they occupy and comprehend the grace they have received. Their lives are filled with living hope. They harbor not the slightest doubt concerning their own salvation. They live a life markedly distinct from that of the world. They possess a living testimony to bear before others, and they are able to guide others clearly into receiving grace and salvation.

---

### 【中文】
第二种是有过重生的经验，但不明白重生的道理的信徒。这种信徒在以往的一个时期已经得蒙重生，不过他们不明白重生的道理，他们不知道自己曾有过的那种经验就是重生的经验。一旦有人问他们是否已经得了重生，他们或是完全不知道怎样对答，或是以为自己未曾得蒙重生。他们自己虽然有过重生的经验，但他们因为不明白的缘故，自然不免常常怀疑自己得救的事，因而不能得着充分的平安与喜乐，也不能有坚强的盼望。他们常感忧虑，恐怕自己不能得救。他们过着一种战栗惧怕的生活。其实他们本来不必，且不应当这样，因为他们已经有过重生的经验，他们已经得了救。他们应当因所得的救恩欢欣，应当靠主常常喜乐。可惜他们因为不明白重生的道理，所以白白吃了许多无谓的苦。

### 【English】
The second class consists of believers who possess the experience of regeneration but do not understand its doctrine. At some past moment, they were truly born again; however, because they lack understanding of the biblical teaching on regeneration, they do not realize that the very spiritual experience they went through is in fact the new birth. Whenever someone asks whether they are born again, they either have no idea how to answer, or mistakenly assume they have not yet been regenerated. Although they have genuinely experienced the new birth, their lack of doctrinal understanding naturally causes them to frequently doubt their salvation. Consequently, they are deprived of full peace and joy, and they lack a steadfast hope. Beset by perpetual anxiety, they constantly dread that they might not be saved, living a life of trembling and fear. In reality, they need not and ought not to live in such a state, for they have already undergone the experience of regeneration; they are already saved. They ought to rejoice in the salvation they have received, and always rejoice in the Lord. Alas, because they do not comprehend the doctrine of regeneration, they endure so much needless suffering in vain.

---

### 【中文】
第三种是明白重生的道理，但却未曾有过重生经验的信徒。这种人本不应当称为信徒，因为真信的人必定有过重生的经验。未曾有过这种经验的人便是未曾真信。不过他们曾加入一个教会，自以为是信徒，别人又承认他们是信徒。我们在这里姑且称他们为信徒好了。他们听过一些人清清楚楚讲过重生的道理，或是读过什么讲解重生的道理的著作，因此明白了重生的道理。但是他们自己并末曾有过这种重生的经验。「重生」这一件事在他们身上不是事实，乃是理论。他们明白，他们也会讲述，但他们自己总得不着其中实在的好处，也不能了解这种道理的真意思。他们不能帮助别人，这不但是因为他们在重生的事上是门外汉，也因为别人在他们身上看不出一点重生的证据来，所以亳不羡慕他们所讲的这种亳无实用的理论。

### 【English】
The third class consists of those who understand the doctrine of regeneration, yet have never personally experienced the new birth. Strictly speaking, such persons ought not to be called believers at all, for whoever truly believes must inevitably experience regeneration. One who has never had this experience has never truly believed. Nevertheless, having joined a church, they consider themselves believers, and others acknowledge them as such; so for our present purpose, we may tentatively refer to them as "believers." Having heard the doctrine of regeneration clearly expounded by preachers, or having read books explaining it, they have acquired an intellectual grasp of the teaching. Yet they themselves have never tasted the actual experience of being born again. To them, "regeneration" is not a living reality, but mere abstract doctrine. They understand it with their minds and can articulate it with their tongues, but they never partake of its authentic spiritual benefits, nor do they fathom the genuine essence of this truth. They cannot help others—not only because they remain total outsiders to the reality of the new birth, but also because onlookers detect in them not a shred of evidence of a transformed life, and thus feel not the least attraction to the useless theories they profess.

---

### 【中文】
第四种是不明白重生的道理，也末曾有过重生经验的信徒。这种人本来也不应当称为信徒。不过他们曾加入一个教会，他们自己和别人都承认他们是信徒，我们也在这里姑且称他们为信徒就是了。

### 【English】
The fourth class consists of those who neither understand the doctrine of regeneration nor have ever experienced the new birth. Such individuals likewise ought never to be termed believers. Yet because they have joined a local church, and both they and others regard them as Christians, we will likewise tentatively refer to them here as "believers."

---

### 【中文】
在这四种信徒中，无疑第一种是最好的，我们都肯定他们是信徒。第二种信徒虽然不及第一种，但他们究竟是重生了的人，是神的孩子，是蒙恩得救的。不过他们因为不明白的缘故，失去许多信徒当享的权利，也不能多帮助人就是了。他们只要有一日明白了重生的道理，便可以与第一种信徒完全相同。第三种却与前两种大不相同。他们虽然明白重生的道理，但末曾有过重生的经验。他们是未曾得救的人，未曾得着新的生命。他们所明白的道理不但与他们亳无益处，而且反倒很容易使他们自满自欺。他们以为明白了重生的道理便可算已经得了重生，这种错误的观念或者竟致使他们不再追求重生的经验，这比不明白重生道理的，更多一些危险。第四种与第三种人所差无几，这两种人同是未曾重生的人，也站在同一的地位。所不同的不过是前一种人，头脑里多有一些圣经里的知识，后一种缺少这一些知识罢了。

### 【English】
Among these four classes, the first is indisputably the most blessed, and we affirm without reservation that they are genuine believers. The second class, though falling short of the first, are nonetheless truly regenerated souls—children of God who have received saving grace. However, through their lack of understanding, they forfeit many privileges that believers are entitled to enjoy, and their capacity to help others is severely hindered. Yet as soon as they come to understand the doctrine of regeneration, they will become entirely like the first class. The third class, however, stands in stark contrast to the former two. Although they comprehend the doctrine of regeneration, they have never had the experience of it. They remain unsaved, utterly destitute of the new life. The doctrines in their heads profit them nothing; on the contrary, such knowledge readily breeds complacency and self-deception. Imagining that an intellectual grasp of regeneration is equivalent to being born again, this fatal delusion may even prevent them from ever seeking the genuine experience of the new birth—a peril far greater than that facing those who are completely ignorant of the doctrine. The fourth class differs little from the third; both remain unregenerate and stand on the very same footing before God. The sole distinction is merely that the former possess a little more biblical knowledge in their heads, while the latter lack such knowledge.

---

### 【English】
The purpose of writing this book is certainly to help my readers understand the doctrine of regeneration; but more importantly, it is my earnest hope that after grasping this biblical truth, you will use it as a touchstone to examine your own experience and daily life, and see whether you are truly a born-again person.

### 【English】
If this little volume can help those who have experienced regeneration but have not yet grasped its doctrine to attain clear understanding; and if it can likewise lead those who have never experienced the new birth not only to comprehend the truth of regeneration, but to actually receive that living experience for themselves—then the author's purpose in writing this book will have been fully accomplished.

## 一、重生是什么意思？ / Chapter 1: What Does It Mean to Be Born Again?

---

### 【中文】
一、 重生是什么意思？

### 【English】
Chapter 1: What Does It Mean to Be Born Again? (What Is Regeneration?)

---

### 【中文】
「耶稣回答说：『我实实在在地告诉你，人若不重生，就不能见神的国。』」(约三3)

### 【English】
"Jesus answered him, 'Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born again he cannot see the kingdom of God.'" (John 3:3)

---

### 【中文】
「耶稣说：『我实实在在地告诉你，人若不是从水和圣灵生的，就不能进神的国。』」 (约三5)

### 【English】
"Jesus answered, 'Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God.'" (John 3:5)

---

### 【中文】
「重生」是什么意思呢？「重」就是「再」的意思。一个人以前作过一件事，现在他再作，我们就说重作。比方说一个人在若干年前曾到过一个地方，现在他又来到这个地方，我们就说他「旧地重游」。「生」这一个字在中文有许多的意思，就如「生产」、「生长」、「生命」、「生存」等等，但这里所说的「生」，乃是「生产」的意思。那样，我们把「重生」这两个字放在一处，就是「再生产一次」。每一个人都曾经过一次的生产。当他乍出母腹，呱呱落地的时侯，那是第一次的生产。每一个人都必须经过这第一次的生产，然后方能进入这个世界，方能成为一个人。但他要进入神的国，要成为神的孩子，却非经过第二次的生产不可。因为主耶稣的话明明说：「我实实在在地告诉你，人若不重生，就不能见神的国。」

### 【English】
What is the meaning of being "born again" (regeneration)? In Chinese, the character *chóng* (重) means "again" or "anew." When someone did something in the past and does it a second time, we say it is done "over again." For instance, if a person visited a place years ago and now returns, we say he is "revisiting an old haunt." The character *shēng* (生) carries many meanings in Chinese—such as "to give birth / be born," "to grow," "life," or "to exist"—but as used here, it specifically means "to be born" or "birth." Thus, when we combine these two characters into *chóng-shēng* (重生), it literally signifies "to be born once again." Every human being has already undergone one birth. When we emerged from our mother's womb and cried our first cry upon this earth, that was our first birth. Every individual must pass through this first birth in order to enter this world and become a human being. But if a person is to enter the kingdom of God and become a child of God, he must without exception undergo a second birth. For the Lord Jesus explicitly declared: "Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born again he cannot see the kingdom of God."

---

### 【中文】
必有人在听见这话以后要十分惊异的问道：「人怎能再生产一次呢？这样大的一个人还能回到母腹中再生一次吗？」人问这样的问题是不足为奇的，当日与耶稣谈话的尼哥底母听见耶稣讲论重生的道理，也曾问过这个问题说：「人已经老了，如何能重生呢？岂能再进母腹生出来吗？」「重生」的道理本是我们从来未曾想到的，乃是神的一个大奥秘，若非主耶稣清清楚楚的解明，无论什么人听见「重生」这句话都不能不感觉落在五里雾中一样。

### 【English】
Hearing these words, some will undoubtedly ask in utter bewilderment: "How can a person be born a second time? Can a grown adult re-enter his mother's womb and be born all over again?" It is hardly surprising that people should raise such a question. On that night long ago, when Nicodemus conversed with Jesus and heard Him expound the truth of regeneration, he asked that very question: "How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother's womb and be born?" The doctrine of the new birth is something that never would have entered the human mind; it is a profound mystery of God. Unless the Lord Jesus had explained it clearly, anyone hearing the phrase "born again" would inevitably feel lost in a dense fog.

---

### 【中文】
主耶稣怎样回答尼哥底母这个问题呢？祂说：「我实实在在地告诉你，人若不是从水和圣灵生的，就不能进神的国。从肉身生的就是肉身；从灵生的就是灵。我说『你们必须重生』，你不要以为希奇。风随着意思吹，你听见风的响声，却不晓得从哪里来，往哪里去；凡从圣灵生的，也是如此。」(约三5-8)

### 【English】
How did the Lord Jesus answer Nicodemus' perplexity? He said: "Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God. That which is born of the flesh is flesh, and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit. Do not marvel that I said to you, 'You must be born again.' The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it goes. So it is with everyone who is born of the Spirit." (John 3:5–8)

---

### 【中文】
祂叫尼哥底母知道，重生并不是进到母腹中，然后从母腹中再生产一次。这样的生产纵使再有几十次、几百次，仍是毫无补益，因为「从肉身生的就是肉身」。重生不是从肉身生，乃是从「水和圣灵」生。

### 【English】
He made Nicodemus realize that being born again does not mean entering back into a mother's womb to undergo physical birth once more. Even if such a physical birth could be repeated tens or hundreds of times, it would still profit nothing, for "that which is born of the flesh is flesh." Regeneration is not a birth from the flesh; it is a birth from "water and the Spirit."

---

### 【中文】
这里所说的水不是物质的水，乃是指着神的道。我们都知道一切有生命的物都不能离了水；照这样，属灵的生命也不能离了神的道。我们又知道，水能洁净一切污秽的东西；照这样，神的道也能洁净我们心身一切的污秽。圣经中用水代表神的道真是最合宜的(参弗五25-27；约一五3)。圣经中又有两段说话清清楚楚的告诉我们，这里所说的水是指着神的道：

### 【English】
The "water" spoken of here is not physical water, but refers to the Word of God. We all know that no living creature can survive without water; in the same manner, spiritual life cannot exist apart from the Word of God. Furthermore, we know that water cleanses all filth and defilement; likewise, the Word of God cleanses all impurities from our hearts and lives. Throughout the Scriptures, using water as an emblem of the Word of God is supremely fitting (cf. Eph 5:25–27; John 15:3). Moreover, two other passages in Scripture explicitly demonstrate to us that the "water" mentioned here signifies the Word of God:

---

### 【中文】
「我亲爱的弟兄们，不要看错了。各样美善的恩赐和各样全备的赏赐都是从上头来的，从众光之父那里降下来的；在祂并没有改变，也没有转动的影儿。祂按自己的旨意，用真道生了我们，叫我们在祂所造的万物中好象初熟的果子。」(雅一16-18)

### 【English】
"Do not be deceived, my beloved brothers. Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom there is no variation or shadow due to change. Of His own will He brought us forth by the word of truth, that we should be a kind of firstfruits of His creatures." (James 1:16–18)

---

### 【中文】
「你们蒙了重生，不是由于能坏的种子，乃是由于不能坏的种子，是借着神活泼常存的道。因为凡有血气的，尽都如草；他的美荣都像草上的花。草必枯干，花必凋谢；惟有主的道是永存的。所传给你们的福音就是这道。」(彼前一23-25)

### 【English】
"Since you have been born again, not of perishable seed but of imperishable, through the living and abiding word of God; for 'All flesh is like grass and all its glory like the flower of grass. The grass withers, and the flower falls, but the word of the Lord remains forever.' And this word is the good news that was preached to you." (1 Peter 1:23–25)

---

### 【中文】
第一次的生产是从肉身生的。肉身是污秽的、必朽坏的，所以凡从肉身生的也是污秽的、必朽坏的。第二次的生产是从神的道和神的灵生的。神的道和神的灵是圣洁的、是不朽坏的，所以凡从水和圣灵生的也是圣洁的、不朽坏的。「从肉身生的就是肉身；从灵生的就是灵。」

### 【English】
The first birth is of the flesh. The flesh is corrupt, polluted, and perishable; therefore, whatever is born of the flesh is likewise corrupt, polluted, and perishable. The second birth is of the Word of God and the Spirit of God. The Word and the Spirit of God are holy and imperishable; therefore, whoever is born of water and the Spirit is likewise holy and imperishable. "That which is born of the flesh is flesh, and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit."

---

### 【中文】
有人说：「重生这种说法并不是说真有一次生产的事实，不过是一个比喻，表明一个人悔悟以前的罪恶，决定心志要作一个好人，并且他真能洗心革面，痛改前非，这种心志和行为正好象一个人又重新生产了一次一样。先哲所说：『从前种种譬如昨日死，以后种种譬如今日生』，也就是这个意思。」

### 【English】
Some say: "The expression 'born again' does not denote an actual, factual birth; it is merely a metaphor indicating that an individual has repented of his past sins, resolved to become a good person, and genuinely turned over a new leaf. Such a resolve and moral reform are compared to a person being born afresh. This is just what the ancient sages meant when they said: 'Let all things past die as of yesterday, and all things to come be born as of today.'"

---

### 【中文】
说这种话的人明显有两个极大的错误：第一，他们擅自更改了主耶稣所讲的话。主耶稣未曾说重生是个比喻。祂以第二次的生产为一件事实，如同第一次的生产是一件事实一样。如果我们以第二次的生产为一个比喻，那样第一次的生产也必是一个比喻了。请问这样的说法通不通呢？

### 【English】
Those who make such assertions commit two glaring, fundamental errors: First, they take the liberty of distorting what the Lord Jesus explicitly taught. The Lord Jesus never said that regeneration is a metaphor. He treated the second birth as an objective reality, just as the first birth is an objective reality. If we reduce the second birth to a mere metaphor, then by the same logic, the first birth must also be regarded as a metaphor. Does such an assertion make any sense at all?

---

### 【中文】

正是因为这个缘故，保罗写信给以弗所的教会说：「因此，我在父面前屈膝，(天上地上的各家，都是从祂得名。)求祂按着祂丰盛的荣耀，借着祂的灵，叫你们心里的力量刚强起来。」(弗三14—16) 这是保罗为教会所奉献的祷告，这也应当作为我们每个信徒在神面前所发的呼求。

### 【English】
For this very reason, Paul wrote to the church at Ephesus: "For this reason I bow my knees before the Father, from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named, that according to the riches of His glory He may grant you to be strengthened with power through His Spirit in the inner man..." (Ephesians 3:14–16).

---

### 【中文】
属神的人在世界上生活的时侯，常常遭遇三种仇敌的攻击。一个仇敌是他们的肉体，或说外面的人；一个仇敌是空中掌权的恶魔；还有一个仇敌就是这伏在撒但权下的世界，或称为污秽邪恶的社会。

### 【English】
While living in this present world, the children of God are perpetually assaulted by three formidable enemies: One enemy is their own flesh—the outer man; another enemy is the host of demonic spirits ruling the air; and the third enemy is this wicked world that lies under the sway of Satan.

---

### 【中文】
从人事一方面看起来，我们真是毫无得胜的指望。这三个仇敌中的一个就足能制服我们有余，何况三个仇敌联合起来向我们进攻呢？这罪恶的世界在我们四面包围着我们，撒但从空中向我们进攻，我们的肉体在里面起来响应。这种情势拿现代战争来形容，正可以说四面被海陆军包围，空中又有飞机的轰炸，里面还加上第五部队的扰乱。似乎保守阵地尚且办不到，哪里还提得到胜利呢？

### 【English】
Looking at the battle purely from a human standpoint, we would appear to have not the faintest glimmer of hope for victory! Any one of these three enemies would be more than enough to crush us; how much more when all three join forces in a concerted assault against us! The sinful world surrounds us on every side; Satan launches fiery darts from the air; and our fallen flesh rises up from within in treacherous alliance! To borrow an analogy from modern warfare: it is as if an army were completely besieged on land and sea, subjected to relentless aerial bombardment from above, and undermined from within by a traitorous Fifth Column! It seems impossible even to hold the defensive lines, let alone dream of triumph!

---

### 【中文】
感谢神，我们现在借着祂的话，确实的知道我们虽然处在这种四面受敌的境况中，却仍有得胜的指望，因为我们的元帅基督已经战胜了一切的仇敌。祂永远活着，祂要扶助我们，增加我们的力量。祂要率领我们与那些凶猛的仇敌作战。我们靠着祂不但能得胜，而且能得胜有余。只要我们信祂、靠祂、亲近祂、听从祂的命令，我们必能看见祂的恩典是够我们用的，祂的能力是在我们的软弱上显得完全。

### 【English】
Thanks be to God, through His living Word we know with unshakeable certainty that although we are hard-pressed on every side, we possess glorious hope of total victory! For our Commander, Jesus Christ, has already vanquished every foe! He is alive forevermore; He stands by us to sustain and fortify our strength; He marches at our head to lead us against these ferocious enemies. Through Him, we are not only conquerors, but more than conquerors! If only we believe in Him, rely upon Him, draw near to Him, and obey His commands, we will surely discover that His grace is all-sufficient for us, and His power is made perfect in our weakness!

---

### 【中文】
那时我们必要像保罗那样说:「所以，我更喜欢夸自己的软弱，好叫基督的能力覆庇我。……因我什么时候软弱，什么时候就刚强了。」(林后一二9—10)

### 【English】
Then we shall cry out in triumphant joy with the Apostle Paul: "Therefore I will boast all the more gladly of my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may rest upon me... For when I am weak, then I am strong!" (2 Corinthians 12:9–10).
