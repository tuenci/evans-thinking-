PERSONAL HYGIENE

个人日常卫生照护之医学价值与全人关怀

Personal hygiene promotes comfort, safety, and health. It includes activities to clean the skin, teeth, mouth, genital area, and anus. Good hygiene helps keep teeth, skin, and mucous membranes intact and healthy.

个人日常卫生照护直接关乎案主的躯体舒适、生命安全与生理健康。它涵盖了清洁体表皮肤、牙齿、口腔黏膜、外生殖器区域以及肛周的全部日常活动。优良的卫生照护能有效维系牙齿健康、体表皮肤及黏膜组织的完整无损。

When microbes enter the body through breaks in skin or mucous membranes, infections can result. Therefore, intact skin and mucous membranes are the body's first line of defence against disease. As well as keeping the body clean and healthy, personal hygiene prevents body and breath odours, promotes relaxation, and increases circulation.

一旦微生物通过破损的皮肤或黏膜屏障侵入体内，极易诱发局部乃至全身性的严重感染。因此，完整健康的皮肤与黏膜组织是人体抵御外界致病性病原体入侵的第一道天然物理防线。除了保持身体洁净与健康之外，个人卫生还能消除汗臭与口气异味、抚慰神经系统促进身心放松，并显著改善全身微循环。

Support workers often assist clients with personal hygiene. Some clients need only minimal assistance, whereas some others may need hygiene care done for them.

支持工作者在日常照护中频繁协助案主开展个人卫生照料。部分案主仅需极少量的动作辅助或用物准备，而重度依赖案主则需要工作人员完全代为其完成全套卫生护理。

Illness, disability, and changes associated with aging affect a person's ability to practise hygiene for themselves. Many factors complicate hygiene and skin care, such as perspiration, elimination, vomiting, drainage from wounds or body openings, bed rest, and activity.

急慢性疾病、躯体伤残以及衰老带来的机能退化都会极大削弱案主自主维持个人卫生的能力。此外，大量出汗、大小便排泄、呕吐反流、手术创面或机体孔道引流分泌物渗出、长期卧床制动以及剧烈活动等诸多复杂生理病理因素，均使得卫生照护与皮肤防护工作极具挑战性。

As well, culture and personal choice affect hygiene practices (see the Respecting Diversity: Personal Hygiene Practices of Different Cultures box). Most clients have established hygiene routines—for example, a bath at bedtime or in the morning, brushing their teeth and washing their face and hands on awakening, and so on. Usually, these and other hygiene measures are done before and after meals and at bedtime.

同时，文化背景与个人生活偏好对卫生照护方式产生深远影响（参见专栏：尊重多元文化——不同文化背景下的个人卫生实务）。绝大多数案主在漫长生活中均形成了固定的卫生日常作息——例如每晚就寝前或清晨晨起沐浴、早起后立即刷牙洗脸洗手等。通常，这些卫生措施均规律安排在三餐前后及睡前就寝时段。

The care plan lists what personal hygiene measures are needed for a client and when to provide them. You are required to assist with personal hygiene whenever needed (see the Focus on Long-Term Care: Daily Care box). If you have questions, check with your supervisor.

照护计划详细列明了每位案主所需的具体卫生措施及标准执行时间表。支持工作者有责任在案主需要的任何时刻随时提供辅助（参见专栏：长期照护聚焦——日常例行照料）。如遇任何疑虑，务必及时向主管护士请示。

Respecting Diversity: Personal Hygiene Practices of Different Cultures

尊重多元文化：不同文化与宗教背景下的个人卫生实务规范
Personal hygiene is very important to Hindus, as their religion requires at least one bath a day. However, some Hindus believe that bathing right after a meal can cause harm to the body. They also believe that eye injuries can occur from bathwater that is too hot. When preparing a bath, hot water can be added to cold water. However, cold water is not added to hot water. After bathing, the body is carefully dried with a towel.
In some Asian cultures, females are very modest, so as a support worker, you must ensure that a woman's body is covered as much as possible. Ask your client or her family how best to provide a bath while respecting the culture.
Muslims are taught to pray five times a day and need to wash their hands, feet, and mouth before each prayer.
Remember, not all individuals within a group follow every belief and practice of their culture and religion. Each client is unique.
对于印度教徒而言，个人身体清洁极为神圣重要，其宗教教义要求信徒每日至少沐浴一次。然而，部分印度教徒坚信饭后立即沐浴会对机体元气造成严重损害；他们还认为过烫的洗澡水会导致眼部受损。在调节水温时，习俗允许将热水缓缓倒入冷水中调温，但严禁将冷水倒入热水中。沐浴完毕后，必须用软毛巾极其细致地将全身彻底擦干。
在许多亚洲文化传统中，女性案主具有极其强烈的羞怯感与身体自尊，作为支持工作者，你必须确保案主的身体在擦洗全过程中被大毛巾最大程度严密遮盖。主动向案主本人或家属请教如何在恪守其文化习俗的前提下最高标准完成沐浴。
伊斯兰教穆斯林信徒每日需严格遵照教规面向圣地礼拜五次，在每次祈祷前均需进行仪式性小净（彻底清洗双手、双足、口腔与面部）。
请牢记：并非所属特定族群的每个个体都会机械遵循其文化或宗教的全部习俗。每一位案主都是独一无二的独立个体，切忌以刻板印象行事。

FOCUS ON LONG-TERM CARE: Daily Care

长期照护机构聚焦：日常例行照料时段与专业工作流程
Facilities provide routine hygiene care at set times of the day. The amount of care given at each time and the terminology used vary from facility to facility, and each facility must adjust its procedures to address the various cultural needs of clients. The care plan will give you directions.
• AM care (early morning care or morning care)—routine care provided before lunch. The policies that dictate times when a client must be served breakfast and how the client must be dressed vary from province to province, and from one facility to another. Some facilities allow clients to continue sleeping if they so desire and offer them a late breakfast. Most clients are assisted to the bathroom; given a partial bath that includes washing the client's hands and face, axillae care, and perineal care; dressed and groomed; and provided mouth care before they go to the dining room for breakfast. Some facilities permit clients to go to the dining room for their breakfast in their pyjamas and robe after their partial bath. In some facilities, clients requiring a tub bath or shower would receive one before breakfast, depending on their care plan, while in other facilities, tub baths or showers are always given after breakfast.
• Afternoon care—routine care given after lunch and before the evening meal. Many clients like to have the afternoon care completed before naps, visiting time, or activity programs. Afternoon care usually includes assistance with going to the bathroom, oral hygiene, face washing, handwashing, and hair care.
• HS care (evening care or PM care)—care provided at bedtime. (HS stands for "hour of sleep.") HS care is aimed at relaxing the client and increasing comfort. It includes face washing, handwashing, oral hygiene, perineal care, back massages, and assistance with going to the bathroom. Bed linens and units are made neat and comfortable, and the client is helped into sleepwear.
照护机构在全天固定时段为案主提供标准化例行卫生照料。每次照护的具体内容及专业称谓因机构而异，且各机构均须灵活调整规程以满足案主多元的文化习俗与个体诉求。照护计划是支持工作者最权威的执行指引。
• 晨间照护（AM care，亦称清晨照护或早晨护理）：午餐前实施的例行照护。关于早餐供应时间及案主着装标准的法规政策在加拿大各省及不同机构间存在差异。部分机构允许渴望继续休息的长者自然睡醒，并为其提供延迟早餐。绝大多数长者在前往餐厅进餐前，会得到以下协助：协助如厕排空膀胱、实施局部擦浴（清洗面部与双手、双侧腋窝清洁、会阴部护理）、协助穿衣梳洗整理仪容，以及全套口腔护理。部分机构允许长者在完成局部擦浴后穿着整洁睡衣及晨袍前往餐厅进餐。在某些机构中，需进行盆浴或淋浴的长者会在早餐前完成沐浴（遵照照护计划），而在其他机构中沐浴则一律安排在早餐之后。
• 午后照护（Afternoon care）：午餐后至晚餐前实施的例行照护。许多长者偏好在午睡小憩、亲友探视或参加文娱活动之前完成午后清洁。通常包括协助如厕、口腔护理、洗脸洗手以及梳理头发。
• 睡前照料（HS care，亦称晚间护理或 PM care）：案主每晚就寝入睡前实施的照护（HS 为拉丁语 hora somni 缩写，意为入睡时刻）。其核心宗旨在于抚慰神经、促进身心深度放松并增进舒适度。包括洗脸、洗手、全套口腔清洁、会阴部排泄后护理、背部舒缓推拿按摩，以及协助如厕。将床单抚平拉紧、整理床单位使其整洁温馨，并协助案主换上舒适柔软的睡衣。

Clients may feel frustrated, angry, or embarrassed because they need help with such personal care. Remember the priorities of support work when assisting with hygiene. Promote DIPPS—dignity, independence, individualized care, preferences, privacy, and safety—to help the client feel more at ease during hygiene procedures (see the Providing Compassionate Care: Assisting With Personal Hygiene box).

案主由于在进食、穿衣、如厕洗浴等最基础的私密生活活动中必须依赖他人协助，内心常伴随深重的沮丧、愤怒甚至屈辱与尴尬。在协助卫生操作时，请时刻铭记支持照护工作的核心准则。全力贯彻 DIPPS 原则——尊严（Dignity）、独立（Independence）、个性化照护（Individualized Care）、偏好（Preferences）、隐私（Privacy）与安全（Safety）——帮助案主在整个卫生照护流程中感受到被尊重、放松与踏实（参见专栏：践行仁爱同理照护——协助个人日常卫生）。

Providing Compassionate Care: Assisting With Personal Hygiene (Part 1)

践行仁爱同理照护：协助个人日常卫生核心心法（第一部分）
• Dignity. Hygiene is a very personal and private matter. Most clients feel embarrassed that someone else needs to provide hygiene care for them, more so when the caregiver is of the opposite sex. Remember, personal hygiene is necessary for the client's health and well-being. Never show embarrassment or hesitation when assisting with hygiene procedures, as the client is likely to sense your discomfort. Talk to the client before and during the procedure. Let the client know what you will be doing. Listen to what the client has to say about the procedure, and address any concerns or questions. If appropriate, chat with the client about anything of interest. Your calm, professional manner will help put the client at ease. Minimizing exposure of the body during bathing promotes dignity.
• Independence. Allow and encourage clients to do as much self-care as possible. Sometimes, you only need to assist with a task. Give clients enough time to do what they can do independently. Check with your supervisor and the care plan to determine clients' abilities and limitations.
• 捍卫尊严（Dignity）：个人卫生是极其私密与个人的事情。绝大多数长者在必须由他人清洗私密部位时会产生极其强烈的尴尬与无地自容感，尤其是当照护者为异性时更为剧烈。请铭记：个人卫生是案主生命健康与身心福祉不可或缺的基础。在执行卫生操作时，工作人员自身坚决严禁流露出任何尴尬、勉强、嫌弃或迟疑犹豫的神色，因为长者极其敏感，会瞬间捕捉到你的不安。在操作前及操作全过程中持续与长者沟通，清晰告知每一个即将执行的动作。耐心地倾听长者对此项操作的感受与诉求，及时解答其任何疑虑。在恰当时候，可与长者轻松漫谈其感兴趣的日常话题。你沉稳、从容、自信而高度专业的举止风范能瞬间化解案主的紧张尴尬。沐浴擦洗期间，将身体暴露面积压缩至绝对最小化，是捍卫尊严的最有力武器。
• 赋能独立（Independence）：充分允许并热情鼓励案主尽可能多地自主完成自理动作。很多时候，你仅仅需要递送毛巾或挤好牙膏，扮演协助者角色。给予长者充裕的时间，让其按照自己的节奏完成能力所及的动作，切忌因赶时间而大包大揽。密切查阅照护计划并与主管护士沟通，精准界定案主的机能强项与活动受限红线。

Providing Compassionate Care: Assisting With Personal Hygiene (Part 2)

践行仁爱同理照护：协助个人日常卫生核心心法（第二部分）
• Individualized care. For care to be truly client-centred, it must be planned or adapted to fit the client's choices, physical and cognitive strengths and limitations, personality, and care plan. Clients should never be forced to receive personal care. If a client is not cooperative with their personal hygiene, gentle persuasive techniques such as re-approaching and redirecting should be attempted (see Dealing With Bathing Challenges on page 324 and Chapter 38).
• Preferences. Clients usually have hygiene preferences and routines. Routines provide a sense of order and control. Clients need to have a say about when and how a hygiene procedure is done. Personal choice is allowed in such matters as bath times and products used. The care plan should reflect the client's preferences and cultural practices. If there is a conflict between the care plan and the client's wishes, inform your supervisor.
• Privacy. Protecting the client's privacy is very important when assisting with hygiene. All procedures must be done in a private area with minimal exposure of the client's body. Close doors, privacy curtains, and drapes before giving care. Ask family members or visitors to leave the room when providing hygiene care. If a family member or friend wants to help, the client must consent. Do not unnecessarily expose the client's body when assisting with the bath. Provide appropriate covers to clients when taking them to and from tubs or shower rooms.
• Safety. Some clients have skin that can be easily damaged. Always be gentle; never scrub the skin or use rough sponges or cloths. Make sure your fingernails are trimmed, and remove rings before giving skin care so as to prevent scratching or tearing the client's skin. Remember to wash your hands with soap and warm water before and after every procedure. Wear gloves when giving perineal care and oral care and any time you may have contact with blood, body fluids, secretions, or excretions. As well, gloves should be worn when you have cuts or rashes on your hands. Always check the water temperature before bathing the client.
• 个性化照护（Individualized care）：真正的“以案主为中心”，必须根据案主的个性偏好、躯体与认知残存机能优势、性格特质以及照护计划量身定制。严禁采取任何威逼或强迫手段迫使案主接受卫生照护！若长者出现抗拒或不配合，应采用温和劝导技巧，如暂时离开片刻后以全新话题重新切入（Re-approaching）或注意力转移法（Redirecting）（详见第 324 页抗拒沐浴应对与第 38 章认知症实务）。
• 尊重偏好（Preferences）：案主在漫长人生中均形成了极具个人色彩的卫生偏好与固定习惯。习惯能为长者带来秩序感与对生活的掌控感。长者对何时洗澡、采取何种清洗方式拥有绝对的发言权。在洗澡时间点选择及护肤洗涤产品的选用上，必须最大化赋予案主自主选择权。照护计划必须充分吸纳案主的个人偏好与文化民俗。若案主临时的强烈意愿与照护计划产生冲突，务必及时向主管护士呈报沟通。
• 誓死守护隐私（Privacy）：在协助一切卫生照护时，隐私保护享有至高无上的地位。所有操作必须在封闭的私密空间内展开，严格将身体暴露降至最低限度。在开始操作前，必须首先关闭房门、拉严窗帘及床旁隐私隔帘。礼貌请房间内的家属或访客暂时移步室外回避。若家属表达协助意愿，必须首先征得案主本人的明确知情同意。在转运案主前往浴室或返回居室的途中，必须为其穿戴好大毛巾或保暖晨袍，坚决杜绝春光乍泄。
• 绝对安全底线（Safety）：高龄长者的表皮与真皮连接变平，皮肤薄如蝉翼脆如宣纸，极易发生表皮撕裂撕脱！手法必须极其轻柔缓慢；坚决严禁用力揉搓擦拭皮肤，坚决杜绝使用粗糙海绵或硬质毛巾。工作人员必须保持指甲剪短圆润，上岗前取下所有戒指首饰，杜绝抓破或撕裂长者皮肤。严格在每项操作前后用温水和肥皂规范洗手。在执行会阴部护理、口腔清洁以及任何可能接触血液、体液、排泄物分泌物的操作时，必须全程佩戴医用手套。若自己双手有伤口皮疹，亦必须佩戴手套隔离保护。在给案主洗浴前，必须严格复核测试水温！

ORAL HYGIENE

口腔卫生护理之深层生理机制与全身健康关联

Oral hygiene (mouth care) keeps the mouth and teeth clean; prevents mouth odours, infections, and cavities (dental caries); increases comfort; and makes food taste better. It also prevents periodontal disease (gum disease, pyorrhea), which is an inflammation of the tissues around teeth.

口腔卫生护理（简称口腔护理）能维持口腔黏膜、舌苔及牙列清洁湿润；彻底消除口臭异味、预防致病性口腔感染与龋齿蛀牙；大幅提升进食舒适度并使食物口感更佳。更为关键的是，它能有力阻断牙周疾病（牙龈炎与牙槽脓溢），即牙齿周围牙周支持组织的破坏性慢性炎症。

Oral care is an essential part of maintaining overall physical health and should never be overlooked! There are a number of excellent resources that are available on the internet that focus on ways to provide good oral care to clients who are ill, older, or in long-term care, such as Brushing Up on Mouth Care from Dalhousie University (http://brushingu__ABBR_7__ca).

口腔护理是维系人体整体全身生理健康的生命基石，在临床照护中绝不容有丝毫懈怠忽视！目前网络上有众多针对患病、高龄及长期照护机构长者的优秀专业口腔教参资源，例如加拿大达尔豪斯大学（Dalhousie University）倾力打造的“口腔护理进阶实务指南”（http://brushingup.ca）。

Poor oral hygiene allows the buildup of biofilm. Biofilm—which contains saliva, microbes, and other substances—is a thin film that sticks to teeth and leads to tooth decay or cavities. When biofilm hardens, it is called tartar.

不良的口腔卫生会导致牙菌斑生物薄膜（Biofilm）的极速附着积聚。生物薄膜是由唾液黏蛋白、脱落上皮细胞、活菌微生物群落及其代谢基质构成的黏性极强的薄膜，紧密贴附于牙面釉质上，细菌发酵产酸直接腐蚀牙釉质形成龋齿空洞。当生物薄膜吸收唾液中的钙盐发生矿化硬化后，便形成了坚硬的牙石（Tartar，牙垢）。

Tartar builds up at the gum line near the neck of the tooth, which leads to periodontal disease. Gums become red and swollen and bleed easily. As the disease progresses, bone is destroyed, and teeth become loosened, eventually resulting in tooth loss. The presence of bleeding gums during oral hygiene procedures is usually a sign of inflammation and means more mouth care is needed, not less.

牙石集中堆积在牙龈缘与牙颈部交界处，直接诱发牙周病。牙龈组织充血水肿、颜色鲜红发紫，在轻微触碰下极易出血。随着病变向深层牙周膜及牙槽骨蔓延，牙槽骨骨质发生不可逆吸收破坏，牙周袋变深，牙齿松动移位，最终难逃牙齿脱落的厄运！在执行口腔护理时若发现牙龈渗血，通常提示深层组织存在活动性炎症，这意味着案主需要更加频繁、更加轻柔细致的口腔专业护理，而不是减少或停止清洁！

Illness and disease often cause a bad taste in the mouth. Some medications and diseases cause a whitish coating on the mouth and tongue, and some others cause redness and swelling of the mouth and tongue.

急重症疾病常导致口腔内出现异味或苦涩金属味。某些广谱抗生素、免疫抑制剂或全身消耗性疾病会导致口腔黏膜及舌苔表面出现厚重的灰白色假膜或鹅口疮白斑（真菌感染），另一些疾病则会引起舌体和黏膜猩红光剥、干燥皲裂与剧烈肿痛。

Dry mouth is a common result of oxygen administration, smoking, low fluid intake, mouth breathing, and anxiety. Some medications also cause dry mouth.

口干症（Xerostomia）在临床极其普遍，常由持续高流量吸氧治疗、长期吸烟史、每日液体摄入严重不足、睡眠中张口呼吸以及重度焦虑恐惧所致。此外，精神类药物、降压药及利尿剂等数百种常用药物亦会显著抑制唾液腺分泌导致重度口干。

Recent research is identifying a relationship between periodontal disease and other diseases. Scientists have found that bacteria that grow in the oral cavity can be aspirated into the lungs and cause respiratory diseases such as pneumonia. Periodontal disease can also increase a person's risk of heart disease.

近现代循证医学研究强力揭示了牙周疾病与机体全身系统性致死疾病之间的直接因果联系。科学家与临床微生物学家证实：在口腔隐匿牙周袋内大量繁殖的厌氧菌与致病菌群，会随微量唾液或呼吸气流微量误吸坠入下呼吸道与支气管肺泡中，成为诱发致死性老年吸入性肺炎（Aspiration pneumonia）的罪魁祸首！此外，牙周炎引起的慢性持续性菌血症与炎性介质入血，会直接诱发血管内皮炎性损伤，使颈动脉内膜斑块增厚脱落，大幅攀升冠心病心肌梗死及脑卒中的暴发风险！

Some studies have shown that clients with oral disease are at greater risk for thickening of the carotid arteries, which leads to heart disease. Your supervisor and the care plan will tell you the type of mouth care and assistance needed for the client.

多项心血管临床研究证实，患有慢性牙周口腔感染的案主，其颈动脉内膜中层厚度（IMT）增厚的发生率显著升高，从而极易引发严重动脉粥样硬化及冠状动脉缺血性心脏病。主管护士及个案照护计划会清晰界定每位案主所需的具体口腔护理类型及辅助级别。

Oral hygiene is given to the client on awakening, after each meal, and at bedtime. Some medications may cause mouth dryness, which in turn, makes swallowing difficult.

标准口腔卫生通常在长者晨起苏醒后、三餐完毕后以及每晚就寝入睡前规范实施。某些药物导致的严重口腔干燥会使案主吞咽极为困难，甚至引发误吸呛咳。

Some clients practise oral hygiene before meals in order to promote salivation and to improve the taste of their food. When giving oral hygiene, you will come in contact with the client's mucous membranes. The mouth contains many microbes, so you need to wear gloves and follow Standard Practices.

因此，部分长者习惯在餐前进行口腔湿润清洁，以有效刺激唾液腺分泌、湿润黏膜并极大改善食物的味觉感知。在执行口腔护理时，工作人员的器械与双手会不可避免地直接触碰案主的口腔黏膜与唾液。口腔内寄生着数十亿计的微生态致病菌群，因此工作人员必须全程规范佩戴医用防护手套，严格恪守标准预防规程（Standard Practices）。

Equipment

口腔卫生护理标准化用物准备与材质安全红线

For oral hygiene, a toothbrush, toothpaste, dental floss, and mouthwash are needed. Ensure that the toothbrush has soft bristles, and replace toothbrushes every 3 months. Use toothpaste that contains fluoride.

实施标准化口腔护理需备齐以下专用器械与用品：医用/专业牙刷、含氟牙膏、牙线以及抗菌或生理盐水漱口液。必须确保牙刷刷毛质地柔软（Soft bristles），严禁用硬毛牙刷，且每隔 3 个月必须强制更换新牙刷。使用含氟牙膏能显著增强牙釉质抗酸防蛀能力。

Clients with dentures need a denture cleaner, a denture cup, and a denture brush or toothbrush. Use only products that are recommended for cleaning dentures to avoid damaging the dentures. For clients with sore, tender mouths and for unconscious clients, sponge swabs are used for mouth care.

佩戴活动假牙的案主需备齐专用义齿清洁剂、专用义齿存放杯以及义齿专用硬毛刷或软毛牙刷。仅可选用经牙医推荐的专业假牙清洁产品，坚决严禁使用普通研磨性极强的去污粉或粗颗粒牙膏，以免磨损刮花假牙基托树脂表面。对于患有口腔黏膜溃疡、牙周重度压痛以及昏迷无自主意识的案主，必须选用微湿的海绵医用棉签（Sponge swabs，Toothette）。

However, be careful to always check that the foam pad is tight on the stick. If the foam pad comes off the stick, the client could choke on it. Lip moisturizers are often used to relieve dry lips.

然而，临床存在一项致命的安全红线：在使用前必须用手指用力拽拉检查海绵泡沫垫是否紧密牢固地固定在塑料柄端！若海绵垫出现松动脱落，一旦在长者口腔内脱落，会瞬间滑入咽喉部诱发急性窒息窒亡！此外，护理完毕后必须及时在口唇涂抹润唇油或医用凡士林，以防口唇严重干裂渗血。

    3. 规范执行手卫生（流动水洗手或速干手消毒剂）。

FLOSSING

牙线剔牙预防医学机理与特殊人群专栏

Flossing the teeth is a preventive measure. It removes plaque and tartar, which cause serious gum disease that leads to loosening and loss of teeth.

使用牙线剔牙是一项极其关键的初级预防医学措施。它能强力清除普通牙刷刷毛完全无法探及的牙齿邻接面隐匿牙菌斑与早期牙结石，正是这些深层菌斑诱发了导致牙槽骨萎缩及牙齿脱落的破坏性牙周病。

Flossing also removes food that is lodged between teeth. It should be done at least daily, such as before or after meals, at bedtime, or at any other time.

牙线还能彻底剔除嵌塞在牙缝中的纤维性食物残渣。

The Canadian Dental Association suggests that getting into the habit of daily flossing may be easier while doing something else, like watching TV or listening to music, for example. You may be required to floss for clients who cannot do it themselves. Follow the care plan (see the Focus on Children: Flossing and Focus on Older Persons: Flossing boxes).

牙线清洁应至少每日坚持执行一次，通常安排在睡前就寝前、餐前或餐后。加拿大牙科协会（CDA）特别建议：在从事其他轻松休闲活动时（例如看电视或听音乐时）顺便剔牙，能极大帮助个人建立并维持每日规律剔牙的良好生活习惯。对于无法自主剔牙的案主，支持工作者须遵照照护计划代为实施（参见儿童与高龄长者专栏）。

FOCUS ON CHILDREN: Flossing

Just like adults, preschoolers and older children need to floss. Older children can floss by themselves, but you need to floss for preschoolers. Older children need reminding and some supervision.
与成年人完全一致，学龄前幼儿及大龄儿童同样必须每日进行牙线清洁。大龄儿童通常已具备自主剔牙的精细手部运动能力，但在操作时仍需要成人的适度督促与监督纠正；而对于学龄前幼儿，因其手部肌力尚未成熟，必须由支持工作者或家长亲自代为其完成牙线剔牙。

MOUTH CARE FOR AN UNCONSCIOUS CLIENT

昏迷案主口腔护理深度规程与防误吸致死铁律

Some agencies allow support workers to provide personal care to unconscious clients (see Chapter 47). It is important to follow the agency policy and your scope of practice if you are uncertain about the care you are allowed to provide.

部分医疗与长照机构允许经过高级考核认证的支持工作者为深昏迷案主提供生活护理（参见第 47 章）。若对自身能否实施此类高危护理存在任何不确定，必须严守机构规章与个人执业范围（Scope of Practice）。

Unconscious clients need special mouth care procedures. They cannot eat or drink, they may breathe with their mouths open, and they may receive oxygen therapy (see Chapter 39), all of which cause mouth dryness and crusting on the tongue and mucous membranes. Oral hygiene helps keep the mouth clean and moist and prevents infection.

昏迷嗜睡案主对口腔护理有着极其特殊而迫切的需求。由于完全丧失自主经口饮水进食能力，且常年处于张口呼吸状态，加之常常合并高流量吸氧治疗（参见第 39 章），导致其口腔内水分被急速抽干，舌苔、牙列与颊黏膜表面结满极其坚硬的深褐色脱水血痂与死皮污垢。及时的口腔专业护理能维持组织滋润洁净，杜绝严重的坏疽性继发感染。

The care plan will tell you what cleaning agent to use. Use sponge swabs to apply the cleaning agent. To prevent cracking of lips, apply a lubricant (check the care plan) to the lips after cleaning.

照护计划会清晰指定所采用的专用清洁漱口液。必须使用医用海绵棉签（Toothette）蘸取药液涂擦清洁。清洁完毕后，必须在口唇表面涂抹一层医用润唇凡士林以防干裂出血。昏迷长者通常已完全丧失自主咽下吞咽动作，必须在整个操作中誓死防范窒息与误吸（Aspiration，即任何液体或异物吸入气管肺泡内）！

Unconscious clients usually cannot swallow and need to be protected from choking and aspiration (inhaling fluid or an object into the lungs), which increases the risk for pneumonia and death. To prevent aspiration, position the client on one side, with their head turned well to the side (__ABBR_16__17.4) so that excess fluid runs out of the mouth, and when cleaning the mouth, use only a small amount of fluid. Sometimes, oral suctioning is needed (see Chapter 39).

一旦液体进入肺内，会瞬间诱发大叶性重症吸入性肺炎并导致感染性休克猝死！为彻底阻断误吸发生，必须协助案主取完全侧卧位，且将其头部向操作者一侧大幅度侧转偏斜（图 17.4），确保在重力学作用下，残存的任何微量液体能顺嘴角自然流向颌下弯盘！且擦拭时棉签必须彻底挤干水分，严禁残留滴落液体！在配备电动吸引器的病房，必要时需联合床旁负压吸痰（参见第 39 章）。

Keep the client's mouth open with a padded tongue blade. If one is not available, make a padded tongue blade, as shown in __ABBR_16__17.5. Do not use your fingers to hold the client's mouth open, as the client could bite down on them.

极为重要的是，沐浴为支持工作者提供了一个长达 20 至 30 分钟、能够自头至顶全面细致检视案主全身皮肤体征的绝佳临床评估窗口，同时也是与长者建立深厚互信同理关系的宝贵契机。依据案主的病情危重度、躯体自理能力及个人偏好，可采取全套床上擦浴、局部擦浴、盆浴或淋浴。在长期照护机构中，沐浴通常安排在早餐后或晚餐后。在排班允许的前提下，必须最大化尊重案主自主选择沐浴时段的权利。

Bathing frequency is a matter of personal choice or employer policy. For example, some clients who live in their own homes may prefer to bathe daily, but in long-term care facilities, legislation may call for clients to have a complete bath once or twice a week and a partial bath daily and as needed.

沐浴频次既受个人生活偏好决定，亦受机构规章及省级照护法规严格规约。例如在居家照护中，许多长者坚持每日洗澡；而在长期照护机构中，省级卫生法规通常规定案主每周接受 1 至 2 次全套大浴（盆浴或淋浴），其余日期则每日及按需实施局部擦浴。

Besides personal choice, bathing frequency is affected by weather, physical activity, and illness. Bathing may need to be more frequent because of increased perspiration caused by high environmental temperatures or a fever. Some illnesses and dry skin may limit bathing to once every 2 or 3 days.

除个人意愿外，气候酷热、体力活动量以及发热感染等病理状态会导致出汗激增，此时必须增加沐浴频次。反之，高龄极度干燥脱屑的脆弱皮肤则需将全身洗浴间隔拉长至每 2 至 3 天一次以保护天然皮脂膜。

Note that, in some cultures, only a family member is allowed to bathe a person. Bathwater that is too hot can scald a frail, thin person. Many agencies, therefore, require two people to check each client's bathwater temperature using a digital thermometer and then record the temperature in the client's chart.

特别需要注意的是，在某些特定文化传统中，严禁外人触碰身体，仅允许直系亲属为案主洗浴。过烫的洗澡水会使皮下脂肪菲薄、感觉退化的高龄虚弱长者在数秒内遭受深度烫伤！因此，许多照护机构已出台严格的机械门禁政策：必须由两名工作人员使用经过校准的数字水温计（Digital bath thermometer）共同复核测试水温，并在长者医疗记录单上由双人签字记录实测水温！

FOCUS ON OLDER PERSONS: Bathing

高龄长者聚焦：皮肤生理老化与香皂浴油安全禁忌
Aging and soap cause dry skin, which is easily damaged. Therefore, older persons usually need a complete bath or shower only two times a week and partial baths on other days. Many facilities no longer allow clients to be bathed using soap, as it may be too drying for their skin. If your facility does use soap, ensure thorough rinsing of the client's skin afterward. Bar soaps should always be rinsed and placed where the bar can dry. Some older clients may bathe daily but not always use soap. Others may find that bath gels are easier to use than soaps.
Bath oils should never be added to tub baths, as they can increase the risk of a client slipping and falling. As well, the oil can cause irritation in the delicate mucous membranes of the urethra and vagina.
Special skin-cleansing products that do not require rinsing are now available. Follow the manufacturers' guidelines for use. Lotions applied after a bath can help keep skin soft and moisturized.
生理性老化使得皮脂腺与汗腺分泌急剧萎缩，加之普通碱性肥皂的去脂作用，极易导致老年性干皮症（Xerosis），表皮干燥变脆极易发生裂伤撕脱。因此，高龄长者通常每周仅需进行两次全套盆浴或淋浴，其余日期进行局部擦浴即可。目前众多先进长照机构已明令禁止使用普通肥皂，改用弱酸性或免洗专用沐浴洁肤乳。若机构仍使用香皂，必须在擦洗后用大量洁净温水彻底冲洗掉所有皂垢残留！块状香皂用毕后必须冲洗干净并放置在能沥水的干燥皂盒中。部分长者偏好每日用温清水冲凉而不使用任何洗涤剂。
严禁在浴缸水中添加液体沐浴油！浴油会在浴缸底部及长者足底形成滑腻油膜，导致滑脱摔倒骨折的风险呈指数级攀升！此外，未乳化的矿物油直接接触黏膜会刺激尿道外口及阴道黏膜，诱发剧烈排尿灼痛与化学性前庭炎。
现代临床已广泛普及无需过水冲洗的专业免洗护肤清洁露（No-rinse cleansers），使用时遵照厂商说明书。沐浴擦干后，应立即在四肢及背部均匀涂抹润肤乳液，长效锁水滋润。

CLIENT OBSERVATIONS AND BASIC ASSESSMENTS DURING BATHING

沐浴全流程中全身体征敏锐评估与异常指标筛查

Observe the client's skin thoroughly during bathing procedures. Report and record the following essential clinical parameters:

在展开任何沐浴操作时，支持工作者必须以高度的专业警觉，细致审视案主每一寸暴露的皮肤与体表黏膜。一旦发现以下任何异常参数，必须在第一时间向主管护士呈报并在护理记录单上详尽登记：

  • • Skin temperature (warm, cool, hot, or cold).
    皮肤触感温度（温暖、湿冷、发烫高热或苍白冰冷）。
  • • Complaints of pain, discomfort, itching, or burning.
    案主在触碰移动时的主诉：疼痛、不适、顽固瘙痒或灼痛。
  • • Colour of the skin, lips, nail beds, and sclera (whites of the eyes) for pallor, cyanosis, or jaundice.
    全身肤色、口唇黏膜、甲床及巩膜（眼白）色泽：警惕苍白、缺氧发绀青紫或肝胆黄染。
  • • Presence of rashes, noting their exact anatomical location, size, and appearance.
    皮疹筛查：精确记录其发生解剖部位、形态分布及是否有水疱丘疹。
  • • Dry, flaky, cracked, or scaling skin.
    重度干燥、脱屑、皲裂或角化硬皮。
  • • Bruises (ecchymosis), skin tears, or open wounds (noting size, colour, and location).
    皮下瘀斑（青紫血肿）、皮肤撕裂伤或开放性破溃创面（详细记录长宽尺寸、基底颜色与位置）。
  • • Pale, reddened, or non-blanchable areas, particularly over bony prominences (sacrum, heels, hips).
    骨突受压部位（骶尾骨、大转子、双足跟、肩胛骨）的苍白或持续性受压不褪色红斑（压疮 1 期预警！）。
  • • Drainage or bleeding from surgical wounds, body cavities, or natural openings.
    手术切口、引流管口或身体自然孔道的渗血、异常分泌物与脓性渗出液。
  • • Swelling (edema) of feet, ankles, and legs; pedal pulse and capillary refill.
    双下肢、双足踝及双足背的水肿凹陷体征；足背动脉搏动与毛细血管再充盈时间。

THE COMPLETE BED BATH

全套床上擦浴（Complete Bed Bath）之适应人群与尊严准备

The complete bed bath involves washing the entire body while the client is still in bed. It is given to clients who cannot bathe themselves—clients who are unconscious, paralyzed, in casts or traction, or weak from severe illness or major surgery. Ask your supervisor about the client's ability to assist with the bath and about any activity or position limitations.

全套床上擦浴指在案主完全不离开病床的前提下，在床上为其洗净全身每一处体表解剖部位。此项操作专门针对完全丧失自主沐浴自理能力的案主——例如深度昏迷嗜睡、高位截瘫偏瘫、全身骨折打石膏或骨牵引制动、以及因心力衰竭、重症感染或大型外科术后极度虚弱濒临耗竭的长者。在操作前，务必向主管护士详细核实该案主是否能协助翻身抬臂、是否存在特定的体位禁忌（如全髋置换术后防脱位翻身角度限制）或肢体活动红线。

Remember to follow Standard Practices. Also, remember to allow the client to make choices during the bath. A bed bath is a new experience for many clients.

全程严格恪守标准感控预防。在擦洗全过程中，只要案主仍有认知，随时赋予其自主选择擦洗部位次序的权利。对于初次经历床上擦浴的案主而言，这往往是一次心理冲击极大的陌生体验。

Some may be embarrassed that another person can see their body, and some fear exposure and loss of privacy. Explain to the client how a bed bath is given and that you will cover the person's body for privacy. The bed bath procedure below applies to adults.

许多长者会因被迫在陌生人面前暴露胴体而感到极度无地自容、难堪自卑，甚至产生强烈的恐惧与被羞辱感。在掀开被褥前，必须以极其诚挚温和的语气向案主耐心解释整个擦浴流程，郑重承诺除了正在清洗的一小块区域外，身体其余全部部位将始终被温暖厚实的大浴巾严密包裹遮蔽，绝不会让其产生暴露感。下述标准化床上擦浴操作规程适用于成年案主。

TOWEL BATHS, BAG BATHS, AND PARTIAL BATHS

毛巾浴、免洗预湿袋浴与局部擦浴适应证解析

Towel Baths and Bag Baths

超大毛巾温浴与医用微波预湿袋浴（Bag Baths）创新技术

For a towel bath, usually an oversized towel is used, which is saturated with a warm, no-rinse skin cleansing solution. The saturated warm towel is placed over the client's body, and the client's skin is massaged through the towel.

在毛巾浴（Towel bath）中，通常采用一条特制超大号纯棉浴巾，将其完全浸泡在配制好的温热无刺激免洗护肤清洁液中。随后将拧至半干的温热大毛巾严密铺裹在长者裸露的躯体上，工作人员隔着毛巾施加温和有节奏的推拿抚摩。毛巾浴热汽蒸腾、极为舒适惬意且操作极速，对于患有重度类风湿性关节炎、全身骨痛或处于中晚期阿尔茨海默病认知症激越状态的长者而言，具有极佳的镇静止痛与抚慰安神功效。

Towel baths are soothing and quick, and they are particularly comforting for clients who suffer from joint pain, arthritis, or dementia. Bag baths are commercially prepared kits containing 8 to 10 disposable, pre-moistened washcloths that are saturated with a no-rinse, skin-softening, antimicrobial cleansing solution.

预湿袋浴（Bag baths）是现代医院与长期照护机构广泛普及的商业化无菌擦浴包。每包含有 8 至 10 块独立折叠、预先浸润了免水洗、软化角质且含抑菌成分的厚质医用湿巾。

The package is warmed in a special microwave or warming oven before use. A fresh, clean warm washcloth is used for each distinct anatomical part of the body and then discarded in the trash (never in the toilet). Bag baths eliminate the risk of pathogen transmission via contaminated wash basins and save significant nursing time.

使用前将密封包放入专用微波炉或恒温加热箱中预热至 40°C 左右。操作时，每一个身体解剖部位严格对应使用一块全新的温热湿巾，擦毕即扔入垃圾桶（严禁丢入马桶冲水！）。袋浴彻底消除了传统擦浴塑料盆反复使用滋生多重耐药菌（MRSA、VRE）的交叉感控死角，且无需端水倒水，极大地节约了宝贵的临床护理工时。

THE PARTIAL BATH

局部擦浴（Partial Bath）之临床界定与赋能自主原则

The partial bath involves bathing the face, hands, axillae (underarms), back, buttocks, and perineal area. These are the areas that accumulate the greatest amount of perspiration, bacteria, dead skin cells, and body odours. Other areas of the body are not bathed during a partial bath.

局部擦浴指在不进行全身大洗的前提下，专门针对最易积聚汗液排泄物、滋生细菌与散发体表恶臭的六大核心关键解剖部位展开的重点清洗：面部、双手、双侧腋窝、背部、臀部以及会阴部（Peri-area）。除上述部位外，躯干四肢其余皮肤在局部擦浴时不予擦洗。

Partial baths are given to clients who cannot tolerate a complete bath, to older persons whose skin is too dry for daily full bathing, and to clients on days when a complete bath or shower is not scheduled. Most clients can perform parts of the partial bath independently while sitting on the edge of the bed or in a sturdy chair in the bathroom. Support workers provide assistance as needed, ensuring privacy, maintaining safety, and promoting the client's independence.

局部擦浴专门适用于：无法耐受长时间全身搬动的心肺功能衰竭长者；皮肤严重干裂退化、每周仅能接受 1~2 次大浴的高龄长者；以及在两顿全套盆浴/淋浴排期之间的所有平时间歇工作日。绝大多数长者在协助下能够坐在床沿端坐或坐在卫生间稳固的靠背椅上，自主独立完成洗脸、擦手及前身清洗。支持工作者在旁提供物品递送、水温调控及盲区（背部、臀部与下身）的擦洗协助，全程誓死守护长者隐私、防范跌倒，最大化赋能长者的自主自尊。

TUB BATHS AND SHOWERS

盆浴与淋浴：安全防护铁律、防烫伤与防滑跌规程

Tub baths and showers are refreshing and relaxing. However, they carry significant safety risks for ill, weak, or older clients. Tubs and shower floors become slippery from water and soap, and falls are a constant danger.

全身盆浴（浸泡浴）与淋浴能带来极佳的舒适提神与身心放松体验。然而，对于体质虚弱、患病或高龄长者而言，浴室是全机构内跌倒致残率最高的极端高危场所！地面积聚的水渍与皂液会使瓷砖表面变得极度湿滑，滑跌骨折风险无处不在。

As well, the warm water causes peripheral blood vessels to dilate (open wider), which draws blood away from the brain and causes a sudden drop in blood pressure. This can make the client feel dizzy, faint (syncope), or weak. Therefore, STRICT SAFETY PRECAUTIONS must be followed at all times:

此外更为致命的病理生理机制在于：全身浸入温水或温水持续冲淋会促使全身外周骨骼肌毛细血管剧烈反射性扩张，大量循环血液瞬间淤滞在外周，导致回心血量与脑部供血急剧骤降！长者会瞬间感到头晕目眩、四肢无力、面色苍白，并发生突发性昏厥晕倒（Syncope）！因此，支持工作者在协助盆浴或淋浴时，必须誓死恪守以下各项安全铁律：

  • • NEVER leave a client unattended in a tub or shower! (Not even for a few seconds to fetch a forgotten towel!)
    绝对严禁将案主独自一人遗留在浴缸或淋浴间内！（即便为了去门口拿一条落下的毛巾，也绝不能离开长者视线半秒钟！）

DEALING WITH BATHING CHALLENGES

抗拒沐浴挑战应对：认知症心理恐惧剖析与温和沟通破局

Bathing can be a terrifying and disorienting ordeal for clients living with dementia, Alzheimer's disease, or severe cognitive impairment. They may not understand what is happening, why they are being undressed, or where they are being taken.

对于罹患阿尔茨海默病、各类血管性痴呆或重度认知障碍的长者而言，沐浴往往是一场极度恐怖、完全无法理解且充满身心折磨的灾难性事件。认知衰退使得长者完全丧失了时间与空间定向力，他们根本无法理解为什么有陌生人要强行脱光自己的衣服、要把自己带到充满水汽的陌生房间。

Rushing water, noisy exhaust fans, cold bathroom tiles, and forced exposure can trigger catastrophic fear reactions. As a result, clients may resist care, scream, become agitated, or strike out at caregivers.

花洒喷头哗哗的狂暴流水声、刺耳嘈杂的排气扇轰鸣、冰冷刺骨的瓷砖触感以及胴体被迫暴露的极端羞耻，会瞬间引爆大脑杏仁核的原始应激防御机制！长者会表现出极其顽固的拼死抗拒、惊恐尖叫呼救、极度焦虑激越，甚至出于本能自卫而挥拳踢打、抓挠咬噬照护人员。

Dementia Bathing Strategies and Adapting Care

认知症人性化温和沐浴实战策略与动作改良

The guidelines in BOX 17.1 apply when you are bathing clients with dementia. The client's care plan lists measures to help the client through the bathing procedure.

专栏 17.1 所列沐浴准则在为认知症长者沐浴时必须不折不扣严格执行。照护计划详细列明了化解恐惧的实操锦囊： • 坚决严禁催促长者！全流程保持缓慢、轻柔、从容不迫的节奏，给予长者充分的适应时间。• 营建安宁温暖的浴室环境：调高室温，避免强光直射眩目，关闭刺耳排气扇，可轻声播放长者年轻时挚爱的怀旧舒缓乐曲。• 始终保持温暖大毛巾严密包裹遮盖：洗哪暴露哪，洗完立即盖回，消除赤身裸体的失控恐惧。

Such measures may include: • Never rush the client; maintain a slow, gentle, unhurried pace. • Keep the bathroom warm, softly lit, and quiet (turn off noisy fans; play soothing familiar music). • Keep the client covered with warm towels at all times; expose and wash only one small body part at a time. • Introduce water gradually: place a warm washcloth on the client's chest or hands before using spray. • Hand the client a washcloth or small soft towel to hold; this provides security and keeps hands occupied. • If the client becomes combative or terrified, STOP IMMEDIATELY! Never argue, force, or restrain the client. Calmly step back, speak in soothing tones, redirect, and try again later (re-approaching technique). • Scale down to a partial bath, towel bath, or no-rinse bed bath if immersion causes extreme terror.

• 水流接触必须循序渐进：切忌直接拿花洒喷头冲淋面部！可先将一块温热毛巾敷在长者胸前或让其双手握住温湿布，随后自足部向上缓慢引导水流接触。• 让长者双手有所寄托：递给长者一块干燥柔软的小毛巾或海绵把玩握持，既能赋予其极大的心理安全感，又能有效防范其因惊慌失措而抓挠挥打工作人员。• 一旦长者表现出极度恐惧、尖叫或攻击攻击倾向，必须立即停手（STOP IMMEDIATELY）！绝对严禁与长者争辩、强行按压或施加任何肢体约束！温和退后半步，以极富同理心的温柔语调安抚情绪，巧妙转移注意力，稍作平复后换个时间再次温和切入（Re-approaching 重新切入技术）。• 若长者对踏入浴缸产生不可克服的深水恐惧，果断将沐浴降级为温和毛巾浴（Towel bath）、免洗袋浴或床旁局部擦浴，绝不强求！

BOX 17.1: Guidelines for Bathing Clients

专栏 17.1：协助案主沐浴核心执业准则与安全操作铁律
• Follow the care plan for bathing method and skin care products. Allow personal choices whenever possible.
• Follow Standard Practices (hand hygiene, gloves when risk of contact with blood/body fluids).
• Make sure the client fully understands the procedure before you begin. Talk with your supervisor if you are not sure that the client fully understands and agrees to the procedure.
• Collect all needed items before starting the procedure. For safety reasons, do not leave your client in the bathroom alone when you go to collect any equipment.
• Provide for privacy. Close doors, shades, drapes, or privacy curtains.
• Cover the client for warmth and privacy. Minimize body exposure at all times.
• Eliminate drafts. Close doors and windows, and ensure room temperature is warm.
• Protect the client from falling (use grab bars, non-slip mats, locked shower chairs).
• Use good body mechanics at all times to prevent back injury.
• Check water temperature with a bath thermometer, the inside of your wrist, or your elbow. Water temperature for a complete bed bath is usually 43.3°C to 46.1°C (110°F to 115°F) for adults. However, older persons may need lower temperatures. Water for tub baths and showers should be approximately 40.5°C (105°F).
• Keep bar soap in the soap dish between lathering to prevent slippery soapy water.
• Wash from the cleanest areas to the dirtiest areas (eyes and face first; anal area last).
• Encourage the client to help as much as is safely possible to promote independence.
• Rinse the skin thoroughly to ensure that all of the soap is removed, as soap residue causes severe itching and skin breakdown.
• Pat the skin dry to avoid irritating or breaking the skin. NEVER rub the skin dry!
• Dry under the breasts, between skin folds, in the perineal area, and between fingers and toes to prevent moisture maceration and fungal infections.

Incontinence During Bathing and Emergency Management

沐浴中突发大小便失禁处置与心脑血管虚脱应急救援

Some clients with urinary or fecal incontinence may urinate or defecate during a bath or shower. Warm water relaxes the pelvic floor muscles and sphincters, which can trigger voiding or a bowel movement. If this happens: • Remain completely calm, professional, and matter-of-fact.

患有尿失禁或便失禁的长者，在盆浴或淋浴过程中随时可能发生突发性排尿或排便。温水的温热效应会极大促使盆底肌群与直肠/膀胱括约肌深度松弛，从而反射性诱发不自主排泄。一旦发生此类意外： • 工作人员必须保持绝对的镇定、从容与专业！坚决严禁流露出丝毫嫌弃、厌恶、皱眉或不耐烦的神色，否则会长久摧毁长者的自尊心，使其陷入无地自容的绝望！

NEVER show disgust, irritation, or anger, as the client will feel deeply humiliated! • For Tub Baths: If feces are passed into the tub, the bath must be terminated immediately. Drain the tub, assist the client safely out, transfer to a shower chair, and wash and rinse them thoroughly in the shower stall. Disinfect the tub following agency biosafety protocols before any other use. • For Showers: Wash stool down the drain with the handheld spray, wash the client's perineal area thoroughly with soap and water, rinse, and complete the shower.

THE BACK MASSAGE

背部舒缓推拿按摩：神经生理疗效、解剖禁忌与手法力学

A back massage relaxes muscles and stimulates circulation. It is given after the bath and with HS care. Massages are particularly soothing for bedridden clients who have stiffness and discomfort from prolonged lying.

背部舒缓按摩能强力促使竖脊肌群深度松弛，改善背部及骨突受压区的微血管充盈循环。通常紧随全身沐浴之后或在每晚睡前照料（HS care）时规范执行。对于长期卧床、因姿势固定而产生肌肉僵直酸痛的长者而言，背部推拿具有极佳的镇痛、安神与抚慰功效。

The massage typically lasts 3 to 5 minutes. Check the care plan before giving a back massage. Back massages are CONTRAINDICATED in clients who have certain conditions, such as: • Fractured ribs or severe spinal disease/instability • Open skin wounds, burns, or healing surgical incisions on the back • Suspected deep vein thrombosis or pulmonary embolism • Severe cardiovascular diseases (heart attack or congestive heart failure), where systemic stimulation causes dangerous cardiac strain.

一次标准的临床背部按摩通常持续 3 至 5 分钟。在执行背部按摩前，必须严格查阅照护计划与医嘱禁忌。背部按摩在以下临床病理状态下属于绝对禁忌（CONTRAINDICATED）： • 肋骨骨折、脊柱压缩性骨折或脊柱不稳手术后； • 背部皮肤存在大面积破溃、开放性创面、烧伤或处于愈合期的外科手术切口； • 疑似下肢深静脉血栓（DVT）或肺栓塞高危期； • 急性心肌梗死急性期或严重充血性心力衰竭，此时任何过度的体表触觉刺激与循环负荷均会加重衰竭心脏的负担。

Position the client in the prone position (on the stomach) if tolerated. If the prone position is uncomfortable or contraindicated (as is common for older persons with respiratory illness or arthritis), place the client in a side-lying (lateral) position with the spine supported.

若案主生理耐受，可协助其取完全俯卧位（趴卧）。若俯卧位引发胸闷气短或因脊柱退化而极度不适（如患有慢性阻塞性肺疾病 COPD 或严重脊柱关节炎的高龄长者），必须协助其取完全侧卧位（Lateral position），背后及两膝之间垫软枕稳固支撑。

Always WARM THE LOTION before applying it to the client's back! Cold lotion applied directly to skin causes sudden chilling, shock, and muscle tensing. Warm the lotion container in warm water, or squirt lotion into your palms and rub your hands together vigorously to warm it before touching the client.

涂抹润肤乳前必须彻底预热（WARM THE LOTION）！若将冰冷的瓶装润肤露直接倒在长者裸露的背部皮肤上，冰冷的触感会瞬间引发躯体惊厥性打颤、血管骤缩与肌肉剧烈痉挛！可预先将润肤乳瓶浸泡在温水盆中加温，或挤出适量乳液于工作人员自己的手掌心，通过双手掌心快速用力对搓摩擦将其完全搓热温热后，再极其轻柔地接触长者的背部皮肤。

PERINEAL CARE (PERI-CARE)

会阴部专业清洁护理之医学原则、无菌防感染与自洁净向污染铁律

Perineal care involves cleansing the genital and anal areas. The perineal area is warm, moist, and dark, making it an ideal breeding ground for pathogenic microorganisms. Natural body secretions (sweat, smegma, vaginal discharge) and excretions (urine, feces) collect in the skin folds of the groin, labia, scrotum, and perianal region.

会阴部专业清洁护理（Perineal Care，临床简称 Peri-care）是指对男女外生殖器区域及肛周区域实施的专业卫生清洗。人体会阴部天然处于温热、潮湿且避光的微环境，加之汗腺皮脂腺密集、尿道口与肛门毗邻，构成了致病微生物极其完美的滋生温床！机体正常分泌物（汗液、皮脂、包皮垢 smegma、阴道分泌液）以及排泄物（残存尿碱、粪渣病菌）极易深陷积聚在腹股沟、阴唇褶皱、阴囊后侧以及肛周皱襞之中。

If not removed, they cause severe odor, skin breakdown (incontinence-associated dermatitis), and lethal ascending infections. Perineal care is given daily during the bath and whenever the area becomes soiled with urine or feces. It is particularly essential for clients who have indwelling urinary catheters, who have had rectal or genital surgery, who are in postpartum recovery, or who suffer from urinary or fecal incontinence.

若不进行规律彻底的清除，会急速诱发恶臭难闻、严重的排泄物相关性皮炎（失禁性皮炎 IAD，表皮浸渍剥脱红肿），更会成为引发致死性泌尿系统逆行感染与败血症的祸根！会阴护理必须在每日晨间沐浴时常规执行，且在任何时候一旦被尿液或粪便浸润污染，必须立即清洗！对于留置导尿管（Indwelling catheter）的长者、经历过直肠或生殖器外科手术者、产后恢复期产妇以及大小便失禁长者而言，规范的会阴护理直接是捍卫生命的防线！

STANDARD PRACTICES AND INFECTION CONTROL: When providing perineal care, you will come in contact with mucous membranes, urine, feces, and secretions. You MUST wear clean medical gloves and wash hands before and after care.

标准预防与感控铁律：在执行会阴护理全过程中，工作人员必然直接接触案主的生殖器黏膜、尿液、潜在粪便排泄物及分泌液。必须全程规范佩戴一次性医用检查手套，并在穿戴前及脱卸后严格执行流动水洗手！绝密工作方向原则：永远严格恪守自最洁净区域向最污染区域单向擦拭（WORK FROM CLEANEST TO DIRTIEST）！• 女性案主清洁黄金路径：严格自前向后（Front to Back）——从前方的尿道外口（最洁净），向后方擦向肛门直肠区（最污染）！

CRITICAL WORKING DIRECTION: ALWAYS WORK FROM THE CLEANEST AREA TO THE DIRTIEST AREA! • For Female Clients: Clean from front to back—from the urethral meatus (cleanest) toward the anus (dirtiest)! • For Male Clients: Clean the tip of the penis (urethral meatus) first in circular motions, then wash down the shaft, then the scrotum, and the anus last! • MEDICAL RATIONALE: Fecal matter contains high concentrations of Escherichia coli (E. coli) and enterococci. Wiping from back-to-front pushes these lethal enteric organisms directly into the sterile urethra and urinary bladder, causing catheter-associated urinary tract infections (CAUTIs), severe acute pyelonephritis (kidney infection), and life-threatening urosepsis!

CLINICAL RED-TEAM ANALYSIS:
1. Critical Mistakes Made:
a. Violation of Standard Practices: Failing to wear medical examination gloves when contacting mucous membranes, body secretions, urine, or feces exposes both the client and the worker to pathogen transmission.
b. Violation of Medical Asepsis Direction: Wiping from back-to-front (from the contaminated anus toward the sterile urethral meatus) directly transfers fecal microorganisms (especially Escherichia coli and Enterococcus) onto the urinary tract and indwelling catheter.
c. Re-using the Soiled Washcloth Surface: Failing to fold and switch to a fresh, clean section of the cloth for each downward stroke smears concentrated bacteria across the labia minora and vaginal orifice.
d. Aggressive Rubbing: Rubbing rather than gentle downward wiping traumatizes fragile, atrophic postmenopausal vaginal and perineal tissues.

2. Catastrophic Health Risks for Mrs. Gable:
a. Catheter-Associated Urinary Tract Infection (CAUTI): Fecal bacteria introduced onto the catheter tubing will rapidly ascend along the biofilm into the bladder, causing acute cystitis.
b. Pyelonephritis and Urosepsis: In frail older adults, ascending UTI rapidly leads to kidney infection (pyelonephritis) and life-threatening bloodstream sepsis (urosepsis), carrying high mortality.
c. Skin Breakdown and Severe Vaginitis.

3. Immediate Intervention and Professional Corrective Protocol:
a. Prompt, Tactful Intervention: Step in immediately with gentle authority: 'Excuse me, let me assist you with some fresh supplies for Mrs. Gable.' Hand the colleague a fresh pair of gloves and put on gloves yourself.
b. Immediate Remediation of the Area: Discard the contaminated washcloth in the soiled linen bag. Obtain warm water, a fresh gentle cleansing basin, and fresh washcloths. Re-cleanse Mrs. Gable's perineal area thoroughly following strict front-to-back, clean-to-dirty aseptic technique to remove all fecal contamination from around the urethral meatus and catheter insertion site.
c. Report to Charge Nurse: Promptly and objectively report the incident to the charge nurse so that Mrs. Gable can be monitored closely for early signs of fever, cloudy urine, altered mental status, and sepsis, and ensure the colleague receives mandatory supervised clinical retraining.
