Most Canadians believe that quality health care should be available to all Canadian citizens and permanent residents, regardless of their personal wealth or ability to pay for services. Canada's national health insurance system, known throughout the world as medicare, was developed to ensure that every resident receives medically necessary hospital and physician services at no direct out-of-pocket cost.

绝大多数加拿大人坚信：高品质的医疗卫生服务应当普惠全体加拿大公民与永久居民，无论其个人的经济财富状况或自费支付能力如何。享誉全球的加拿大国家全民医疗保险制度——“Medicare”，其建立的根本初衷正在于确保每一位居民在遭遇疾病时，均能享有医学上必需的医院住院及执业医师诊疗服务，而无需在就诊时自掏腰包支付昂贵费用。

Canada's publicly funded health care system is best described as an interlocking set of 10 provincial and 3 territorial health insurance plans, with the federal government and provincial/territorial governments sharing constitutional responsibilities. To receive their full share of federal funding, provincial and territorial medicare plans must meet the five fundamental criteria set out in the Canada Health Act.

加拿大由公共财政资助的医疗保健体制，最精准的定义是由 10 个省份和 3 个地区各自独立的医疗保险法案紧密衔接而成的一套全国联动网络。联邦政府与各省及地区政府在宪法框架下分工明确、共同履行治理职责。为了获得全额的联邦医疗财政转移支付，各省和地区的医保计划必须无条件遵循联邦《加拿大健康法》所确立的五大根本原则。

THE EVOLUTION OF MEDICARE IN CANADA

加拿大全民医保体系的历史演进

Prior to World War II, Canadian health care was largely privately funded. Individuals paid doctors directly or purchased private insurance.

在第二次世界大战爆发之前，加拿大的医疗服务基本上属于私人自费范畴。病患直接向医生支付诊疗费，或自行购买私营商业保险。

Those who could not afford medical care relied on charity, family support, or went without treatment. The Great Depression of the 1930s exposed the catastrophic vulnerabilities of private financing as millions were left destitute.

无力承担医疗开支的穷苦民众只能依赖教会慈善救济、亲友接济，甚至常常因为付不起医药费而听天由命、延误救治。二十世纪三十年代席卷全国的经济大萧条使数以百万计的家庭陷入赤贫，彻底暴露了纯商业医疗体制的残酷与弊端。

In 1947, Tommy Douglas, then Premier of Saskatchewan, introduced Canada's first publicly funded, universal hospital insurance program. Despite intense opposition, the Saskatchewan experiment demonstrated that public funding was viable and overwhelmingly popular. Encouraged by this success, the federal government enacted the Hospital Insurance and Diagnostic Services Act in 1957, offering to share 50% of the cost of hospital and diagnostic services with any province that established an insurance plan.

1947 年，时任萨斯喀彻温省省长汤米·道格拉斯（Tommy Douglas）在该省开天辟地推出了加拿大历史上第一个由政府财政全额资助的全民公费医院住院保险计划。尽管遭遇了来自传统势力的激烈阻挠，萨省的试点以无可辩驳的事实验证了公共医疗筹资的可行性，并赢得了公众排山倒海的拥护。受萨省成功的巨大鼓舞，联邦政府于 1957 年颁布了《医院保险与诊断服务法》（HIDS Act），承诺向任何建立符合国家标准的公费医院住院保险的省份提供 50% 的对等财政补贴。

In 1962, Saskatchewan once again led the nation by introducing public medical insurance covering physician services outside hospitals. Four years later, in 1966, the federal government passed the Medical Care Act, sharing costs for medical services outside hospitals nationwide. By 1972, all Canadian provinces and territories had established universal health insurance plans covering both hospital and physician care.

1962 年，萨斯喀彻温省再次敢为人先，将公费保险从医院病房进一步延伸至医院门诊及独立诊所医生服务。四年后的 1966 年，联邦议会正式通过了《医疗照护法》（Medical Care Act），在全联邦范围内对门诊医生诊疗费实施 50% 联邦财政分摊。至 1972 年，加拿大全部十个省和地区均已建立起覆盖医院住院及医生门诊诊疗的全民公费医疗保障体系。

THE CANADA HEALTH ACT (1984)

《加拿大健康法》(1984) 与全民医保五大原则

In 1984, the federal parliament unanimously passed the Canada Health Act, which consolidated previous legislation and banned extra-billing by physicians and user fees imposed by hospitals. The act affirms that the primary objective of Canadian health care policy is 'to protect, promote and restore the physical and mental well-being of residents of Canada and to facilitate reasonable access to health services without financial or other barriers.'

1984 年，加拿大联邦议会全票一致通过了里程碑式的《加拿大健康法》（Canada Health Act）。该法案整合了先前的分散立法，以铁律形式全面禁止医生向患者额外加收自费差价（Extra-billing），严禁公立医院收取门槛就诊费（User fees）。该法案庄严宣示：加拿大医疗卫生政策的根本宗旨是“保护、促进并恢复加拿大居民的身心健康福祉，并在毫无经济困境或其他门槛的前提下，保障所有人均享有合理获得医疗服务的权利”。

BOX 2.1 The Five Principles of the Canada Health Act

专栏 2.1：《加拿大健康法》确立的全民医保五大根本支柱原则
1. COMPREHENSIVENESS: All medically necessary hospital and physician services, as well as surgical-dental services performed in hospital, must be fully covered.
2. UNIVERSALITY: 100% of insured residents of a province or territory are entitled to the insured health services on uniform terms and conditions.
3. PORTABILITY: Residents continue to be entitled to coverage when traveling temporarily within Canada or abroad, or when moving between provinces until new provincial coverage takes effect.
4. ACCESSIBILITY: Insured persons must have reasonable access to insured services on uniform terms, unhindered by financial charges or user fees. Reasonable compensation must be provided to doctors and dentists.
5. PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION: The provincial/territorial health insurance plan must be administered on a non-profit basis by a public authority accountable to the provincial government.
1. 保障全面性原则（Comprehensiveness）：省医保必须百分之百全额报销所有医学必需的医院住院护理、执业医师门诊诊疗，以及在医院内执行的口腔颌面外科手术。
2. 全民普遍受保原则（Universality）：辖区内百分之百符合资格的居民，均享有在完全统一平等的条款和条件下获得受保医疗服务的神圣权利，绝不设立歧视门槛。
3. 异地便携性原则（Portability）：居民在加拿大境内跨省旅游、探亲、就医，或短期临时出国期间，原居住省份医保必须继续有效并报销必需急救费用；跨省搬迁时在过渡期内继续受保。
4. 合理可及性原则（Accessibility）：确保所有居民在平等的条件下享有获得受保医疗服务的合理可及性，严禁通过收取额外加价或自费就诊门槛费阻碍看病；确保医生及牙医获得合理公允报酬。
5. 非营利公办性原则（Public Administration）：各省和地区的医疗保险计划，必须由直接向省政府负责的法定公共行政机构在非营利性（Non-profit）基础上统筹管理与审计监督。

加拿大医疗卫生体系中的联邦与省/地区权责划分

Federal Responsibilities

联邦政府的核心专属职责
  • • Sets and administers national principles and standards for the health care system through the Canada Health Act.

  • • Regulate health care professionals through provincial professional regulatory colleges under health professions legislation.
    依据省《受监管健康职业法》设立护士公会、医师公会等专业自律学院，对全省医护从业人员实施执照考核、注册与纪律监管。

CATEGORIES OF HEALTH CARE DELIVERY

加拿大医疗卫生服务三级分级交付架构

Primary Health Care Delivery

1. 初级卫生保健（Primary Care）

Primary health care is the first point of contact between individuals and the health care system. It focuses on routine healthcare, health promotion, disease prevention, early diagnosis, chronic disease management, and coordination of care.

“初级卫生保健”（Primary Care）是公民与整个国家医疗卫生系统发生互动的“第一接触点”。其核心聚焦于常规健康体检、健康生活方式倡导、传染病疫苗接种、常见病初诊与确诊、慢病长期随访以及转诊协调。

Primary care is provided by family physicians, nurse practitioners, community health nurses, pharmacists, and walk-in clinics. Primary care also includes 24/7 telephone telehealth helplines (e.g., Telehealth Ontario, HealthLink BC 811) where registered nurses triage symptoms and advise callers on self-care or seeking urgent attention.

初级医疗主要由家庭医生（GPs）、执业护士诊所（NPs）、社区公共卫生护士、社区药剂师以及免预约免挂号普通门诊（Walk-in Clinics）提供。初级保健还涵盖全天候远程电话医疗热线（如安省 Telehealth、卑诗省 HealthLink BC 811），注册护士 24 小时在线提供精准分诊评估与自我护理指导。

Secondary Health Care Delivery

2. 二级专科医疗照护（Secondary Care）

Secondary health care delivery consists of specialized medical assessment and treatment provided upon referral from a primary care provider. It occurs primarily in community general hospitals, outpatient specialty clinics, and day-surgery units. Examples include consultations with cardiologists, orthopedic surgeons, dermatologists, diagnostic imaging (MRI, CT scans), and emergency department visits for acute crises.

“二级专科医疗照护”（Secondary Care）是指在初级家庭医生开具正式转诊信（Referral）后，由专科专家开展的高阶针对性检查与治疗。二级医疗主要在社区综合医院、专科门诊诊所以及日间微创手术中心开展。典型范例包括：心血管专科医生会诊、骨科关节置换手术、皮肤科专科活检、大型核磁共振（MRI）与增强 CT 影像检查，以及医院急诊科针对突发创伤的抢救处置。

Tertiary Health Care Delivery

3. 三级高精尖科研教学医疗（Tertiary Care）

Tertiary health care delivery is the most highly specialized, technologically advanced, and expensive level of care. It takes place in major university-affiliated teaching and research hospitals. Tertiary services include complex cardiac surgery, organ transplants, major neurological procedures, extensive burn management, trauma centers, and experimental clinical oncology trials.

“三级高精尖科研教学医疗”（Tertiary Care）是整个医疗层级中专业化程度最高、设备最尖端、技术最复杂且耗资最为昂贵的顶尖医疗救治。它通常集中在各省顶尖医科大学附属的大型科研教学型中心医院。三级医疗包括复杂体外循环心脏搭桥手术、异体大器官移植、高难度显微神经外科开颅手术、大面积重度三度烧伤专科救治、一级创伤急救中心以及前沿抗癌新药临床试验。

COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICES AND HOME CARE

社区卫生服务机构与居家照护的战略崛起

Community health services encompass a wide spectrum of health care and social support services provided outside of traditional hospital walls. These include community health centres (CHCs), public health units, walk-in clinics, mental health outreach, and home care. In recent years, healthcare reforms have shifted services from expensive inpatient beds into cost-effective community programs.

社区卫生服务涵盖了在传统综合医院围墙之外开展的极其广泛的医疗健康与社会支持服务。其中包括社区健康服务中心（CHCs）、公共卫生防疫局、免预约诊所、心理健康外展支持以及社区居家照护网络。近年来，加拿大医疗体制改革正在大刀阔斧地将资源从昂贵的住院病区转移至更具成本效益的社区卫生体系之中。

The Concept of Home Care

居家照护（Home Care）的科学内涵与使命

Home care involves health care and support services delivered directly to people in their personal residences. The primary goal of home care is to enable individuals with chronic illnesses, disabilities, or age-related limitations to remain living safely and independently in their own homes for as long as possible, avoiding or delaying premature admission to expensive long-term care facilities or hospitals.

“居家照护”（Home Care）是指直接送达案主私人住宅中的专业医疗护理与日常生活支持服务。居家照护最根本的战略宗旨在于：赋能患有慢性疾病、躯体残障或伴有高龄衰弱的长者，能够长期安全、舒适且自立地生活在自己熟悉的家中，最大程度避免或延缓过早入住高成本的公立长期照护机构或占用急症医院床位。

Home care services can be classified into four primary categories: (1) Personal care services (assisting with bathing, dressing, grooming, eating, toileting); (2) Home support services (light housekeeping, meal preparation, shopping); (3) Nursing and professional therapies (wound care, IV therapy, physiotherapy, occupational therapy); and (4) Support for IADLs and family caregiver relief (respite care).

社区居家照护服务通常系统划分为四大核心支柱：(1) 个人生活照料服务（协助沐浴更衣、梳理洗漱、进食喂饭、排泄护理）；(2) 家居支持保洁服务（轻度室内除尘吸尘、营养膳食烹饪、生活采买）；(3) 专科护士临床护理与康复理疗（复杂伤口换药、家庭静脉输液、物理与作业康复治疗）；以及 (4) 工具性生活能力（IADLs）协助与家庭照料者暂托喘息服务（Respite Care）。

HOME CARE PROGRAMS ACROSS CANADA

加拿大各省居家照护运行模式与资金筹措机制

Unlike hospital and physician services, home care is not formally protected as a mandatory universal entitlement under the Canada Health Act. Consequently, home care programs vary significantly from province to province in terms of funding models, eligibility criteria, service hours allocated, and scope of subsidized services. Every province and territory maintains a publicly funded home care program, but coverage levels depend on provincial budgetary allocations.

与医院住院和医生门诊不同，社区居家照护并未被白纸黑字列为《加拿大健康法》强制必须全额报销的五大普遍福利。因此，全国各省和地区的居家照护计划在筹资模式、准入门槛评估、获批服务工时以及免费自费界限上存在显著的地域差异。虽然每个省和地区均设立了由政府财政资助的公共居家照护项目，但每位案主能获得多少补贴工时，完全取决于该省卫生厅的年度财政预算政策。

In most provinces, home care services are delivered through a mixed economy of for-profit private agencies and not-for-profit community organizations contracted by regional health authorities (e.g., Home and Community Care Support Services in Ontario). Clients who require more assistance than provincial public plans provide may privately purchase additional hours of support worker care through their own financial resources or private extended health insurance.

在绝大多数省份，公立居家照护服务是由地方卫生主管局（如安省居家与社区照护支持机构 HCCSS）以政府采购服务的方式，依法招标委托给私立营利性家政护工中介机构或非营利性慈善社会组织具体执行。对于照护需求超出政府公立医保免费工时限额的长者，家庭可以通过自费（Out-of-Pocket）或雇主提供的商业补充医疗保险，向中介机构额外自费购买支持工作者的照护工时。

Support workers provide the vast majority (up to 80%) of direct care hours in home care settings, making them the indispensable backbone of the Canadian community care sector.

在所有的居家照护上门服务工时中，支持工作者（PSW / HCA）直接承担了高达 80% 以上的贴身照护与家政服务，是支撑整个加拿大社区医疗与居家养老事业名副其实的顶梁柱。

HEALTH CARE CHALLENGES IN CANADA

加拿大医疗卫生体系面临的四大严峻挑战

Although Canadians take immense pride in their publicly funded healthcare system, medicare faces mounting systemic stresses that threaten its sustainability. Four primary challenges dominate national healthcare policy:

尽管加拿大人对全民公费医疗体制抱有深沉的民族自豪感，但 Medicare 体系当前正面临着空前严峻的系统性重压，严重威胁着体制的可持续性。以下四大核心危机成为全国医疗改革的最紧迫课题：

  • • 1. Aging Population: The aging of the baby boomer generation has created an unprecedented demographic shift. Older adults utilize health services at higher rates due to multiple chronic conditions (multimorbidity), dementia, and functional dependency, placing massive strain on hospitals and long-term care homes.
    1. 银发海啸人口老龄化：二战后“婴儿潮一代”正全面跨入高龄阶段。由于多重慢性病缠身、阿尔茨海默病高发及自理能力衰退，长者对医疗资源的利用率呈指数级上升，使公立医院急诊与养老院床位不堪重负。
  • • 2. Escalating Technology and Drug Costs: Rapid developments in targeted gene therapies, biopharmaceuticals, advanced robotic surgery, and diagnostic imaging carry exorbitant price tags. Healthcare spending accounts for nearly 40% of provincial budgets, with rising costs outstripping government tax revenues.
    2. 医疗科技与专利药物成本飞速暴涨：高阶基因靶向抗癌药、生物制剂、手术机器人及超精影像设备的研发与应用价格极为昂贵。医疗开支已吞噬了各省近 40% 的全部财政预算，成本暴涨幅度远远跑赢政府税收增速。
  • • 3. Long Waiting Lists for Specialist Care and Surgery: Chronic bottlenecks in the healthcare continuum have led to lengthy wait times for elective orthopedic procedures, diagnostic scans, specialist consultations, and long-term care admissions.
    3. 专科转诊与择期手术候诊队伍漫长：医疗系统内各环节运转迟缓，导致关节置换择期手术、大型核磁共振检查、专科名医会诊以及养老院公费床位的候诊等待期长达数月乃至数年。
  • • 4. Severe Shortages of Healthcare Human Resources: Canada faces acute shortages of family physicians, registered nurses, and support workers, particularly in rural, remote, and northern Indigenous communities, resulting in emergency room closures and worker burnout.
    4. 医护人力资源严重短缺：加拿大正经历家庭医生、注册护士及支持工作者的全国性大短缺，在偏远乡村与北部原住民社区尤为致命，频频导致乡村医院急诊室被迫暂时关闭，从业人员深陷职业倦怠。

HEALTH CARE REFORMS: PROMOTION AND PREVENTION

国家医疗改革战略重心：健康促进与疾病预防

To safeguard the financial sustainability of the healthcare system, federal, provincial, and territorial governments are actively shifting from a 'reactive, disease-focused' illness model to a 'proactive, wellness-focused' model. Focus is placed on two vital strategies: (1) Health Promotion, which empowers citizens to adopt healthy lifestyle choices, nutritious eating, and physical activity; and (2) Disease Prevention, which targets upstream risk factors through immunization, cancer screenings, anti-smoking campaigns, and chronic disease self-management.

为了捍卫全民公费医疗的财政根基，各级政府正坚定不移地推动医疗范式从传统的“被动治病模式”向“前瞻预防维稳模式”根本性转变。改革牢牢锁定两大上游战略：(1)“健康促进”（Health Promotion）——赋能全体公民树立自主健康意识、践行科学运动、均衡膳食与控烟限酒；以及 (2)“疾病预防”（Disease Prevention）——通过全民免费疫苗接种、高危癌症早期筛查、控烟法规宣传及慢病自我管理干预，将疾病消灭在萌芽之中。

BOX 2.2 Routine Health Checkups and Screenings Across the Lifespan

专栏 2.2：全生命周期常规健康筛查与预防性体检指南
• Blood Pressure: Annually for all adults over 18; more frequent if hypertensive.
• Cholesterol and Lipid Profile: Every 5 years for men >40 and women >50, or earlier with cardiovascular risks.
• Colorectal Cancer Screening: Fecal immunochemical test (FIT) every 2 years for adults aged 50 to 74.
• Mammography: Breast cancer screening every 2 to 3 years for women aged 50 to 74.
• Cervical Cancer (Pap test): Every 3 years for individuals with a cervix aged 21 to 69.
• Bone Mineral Density (BMD): DEXA scan at age 65 for osteoporosis screening, earlier if fracture risk.
• Annual Influenza & Pneumococcal Vaccines: Crucial for older adults and individuals with chronic conditions.
• 血压测量：18 岁以上成年人每年至少测量一次；伴有高血压者应遵医嘱每月或每周规律自测。
• 血脂胆固醇全套检测：40 岁以上男性及 50 岁以上女性每 5 年化验一次；伴有心脑血管危险因素者应提前随访。
• 结直肠癌早期筛查：50 至 74 岁群体每 2 年进行一次免疫法粪便潜血检测（FIT），阳性者做结肠镜。
• 乳腺钼靶 X 线筛查（Mammogram）：50 至 74 岁女性建议每 2 至 3 年进行一次常规乳腺癌筛查。
• 宫颈癌筛查（巴氏涂片 Pap Test）：21 至 69 岁具有宫颈的女性每 3 年进行一次宫颈细胞学检查。
• 骨密度扫描（BMD）：65 岁以上长者建议进行双能 X 射线骨密度检测以筛查骨质疏松防骨折。
• 每年流感与肺炎球菌疫苗：高龄长者与慢病体弱群体每逢秋冬必打，建立稳固肺部免疫保护。

SUPPLEMENTARY HEALTH BENEFITS AND PRIVATE INSURANCE

补充医疗福利保险、私人商业保险与未来发展趋势

While the Canada Health Act guarantees coverage for hospital and doctor visits, it does not mandate full public coverage for outpatient prescription medications, routine dental care, vision care, medical equipment (wheelchairs, hearing aids), or allied therapies (physiotherapy, chiropractic, psychology). These are considered supplementary health benefits.

尽管《加拿大健康法》誓死捍卫了看医生和住院手术的零自费原则，但它并未将医院门诊外的处方药费、常规牙科治疗、眼科验光配镜、自理辅助医疗器材（轮椅、助听器）或辅助康复治疗（物理治疗、脊椎按摩、心理咨询）列为强制公费报销项目。这些服务在体制中被称为“补充医疗福利”（Supplementary Health Benefits）。

To cover these gaps, roughly 60% of Canadians hold private extended health insurance policies, often provided as an employee benefit by employers or unions. For low-income seniors and vulnerable populations, provincial governments operate targeted public drug and dental assistance programs (e.g., the Ontario Drug Benefit program for seniors over 65, and the newly established Canadian Dental Care Plan).

为了弥补公费医保的这一缺口，大约 60% 的加拿大人通过雇主福利或工会工商业计划持有私营商业补充医疗保险（Extended Health Care）。针对低收入长者与弱势群体，各省政府则设立了专门的公立处方药与牙科补助计划（如安省面向 65 岁以上长者的 ODB 公费处方药计划，以及联邦最新推行的加拿大全国牙科保健计划 CDCP）。

Future trends in the Canadian health care system focus on expansion of virtual care and AI triage, establishment of national universal pharmacare, closer integration of hospital and home care, and empowering support workers with advanced community training.

加拿大医疗体制未来的战略发展动向聚焦于：远程互联网门诊与 AI 智能分诊的大规模普及、国家全民全覆盖处方药保险（Universal Pharmacare）的立法破冰、医院与社区居家照护的深度数据互通无缝衔接，以及为基层支持工作者赋予更先进的社区照护专项技能。

KEY POINTS

全章核心知识要点精要清单
  • • Canada's publicly funded health care system is an interlocking set of 10 provincial and 3 territorial health insurance plans.
    加拿大公费医疗体制是由 10 个省和 3 个地区各自独立的医疗保险法案紧密衔接而成的全国性联动保障网络。
  • • Medicare provides universal, comprehensive coverage for medically necessary hospital and physician services at no direct cost.
    全民医保（Medicare）为全体合法居民提供医学必需的医院住院及执业医生诊疗服务的全额报销，就诊时完全免自费。
  • • Provincial and territorial health plans must meet the five Canada Health Act criteria: comprehensiveness, universality, portability, accessibility, and public administration.
    各省和地区的医保计划必须无条件遵循《加拿大健康法》五大原则：保障全面性、全民普遍性、异地便携性、合理可及性以及非营利公办性。
  • • The TRC identified seven Calls to Action (Actions 18-24) necessary to redress the legacy of residential schools and close Indigenous health disparities.
    真相与和解委员会（TRC）提出了针对医疗改革的七大行动呼吁（第 18 至 24 条），以弥合原住民触目惊心的健康不平等差距。
  • • Healthcare delivery is divided into primary care (prevention/first contact), secondary care (specialist referral/community hospital), and tertiary care (highly specialized teaching hospital).
    医疗服务划分为初级保健（预防与首诊接触点）、二级专科医疗（专科转诊与社区综合医院）以及三级医疗（大型大学附属科研教学型医院高精尖救治）。
  • • The healthcare system is challenged by the aging baby boomer population, long waitlists, physician/nurse shortages, and escalating technology/drug costs (the greatest challenge).
    医疗体制面临着四大严峻挑战：婴儿潮老龄化、候诊名单漫长、医护人员短缺以及最致命的高新科技与新药成本飞速飙升。
  • • Supplementary benefits (dental, prescription drugs, glasses, physiotherapy) are not mandated by medicare and are funded privately or via targeted public assistance.
    补充医疗福利（门诊处方药、牙科、配镜、理疗）不属于 Medicare 强制法定公费范畴，多由商业保险或政府长者专案资助。
  • • Governments are actively shifting funding and strategic focus to health promotion, disease prevention, and community-based home care.
    各级政府正全力将卫生政策与财政重心战略转移至“健康促进”、“疾病预防”以及“社区居家照护”三大领域。
  • • Support workers provide the majority of home care support services, serving as the essential frontline workforce.
    支持工作者（PSW）直接承担了绝大多数社区居家照护服务工时，是支撑社区养老的最核心基层主力军。
  • • Home care services encompass personal care, home support, nursing/therapies, and family caregiver respite.
    居家照护四大支柱包括：贴身个人照料、家居日常保洁、专科临床护理与康复理疗，以及家庭主要照料者喘息解困服务。
