OBJECTIVES

本章学习与执业考核目标 (10 大核心能力指标)
After reading this chapter, the learner should be able to do the following:
研读并掌握本章知识后，学习者应当能够全面达成以下临床核心技能与法律认知目标：
  • • Identify the legal act that is meant to protect all Canadians, new immigrants, and visitors from abuse.
    精准识别旨在保护全体加拿大公民、新移民及访客免受虐待与歧视的根本宪制法律基石。
  • • Describe the types of abuse.
    系统掌握并深入剖析身体虐待、性虐待、情感虐待、经济虐待、忽视与欺凌等主要虐待形态。
  • • Describe the cycle of abuse.
    清晰掌握虐待恶性循环的三大核心发展阶段（紧张积聚期、急性暴力发作期、蜜月宽恕期）。
  • • Describe spousal abuse, child abuse, and abuse of older persons.
    全面辨析配偶虐待（亲密伴侣暴力）、虐待儿童与长者虐待的临床特征与特殊脆弱性。
  • • Describe how clients and health care workers can be abused.
    深刻理解服务对象如何遭受医护人员不当对待，以及医护人员如何在临床执业中遭受服务对象的暴力与言语侵犯。
  • • Explain what to do if you have an abusive client.
    熟练掌握面对攻击性或虐待性服务对象时的自我防卫黄金安全规程与危机脱身法则。
  • • Identify signs of abuse.
    精准识别身体虐待、性侵、情感折磨、经济剥削与照料忽视的典型临床体征与行为警讯。
  • • Explain your legal responsibilities when reporting abuse.
    严格明晰在怀疑或目睹虐待时支持工作者所肩负的法定汇报义务、法律责任红线与保密边界。
  • • Describe what to do if a client tells you about being abused.
    熟练掌握当服务对象主动向你披露受虐经历时的专业倾听、情感支持与规范记录范式。
  • • Apply the information in this chapter to your clinical practice properly.
    将反虐待、防欺凌与维护人身尊严的法律伦理准则切实无缝转化应用于日常一线临床实践。

Abuse results when physical or mental harm is deliberately caused by another person or persons. Abuse can be caused by anyone, such as a family member, friend, partner, classmate, co-worker, supervisor, or caregiver.

当身体伤害、生理痛苦或精神心理创伤是由另一人或多人蓄意故意造成时，便构成了法律与医学意义上的虐待（Abuse）。虐待的施暴者可以是任何人，例如家庭成员、朋友、生活伴侣、同学、同事、主管乃至专职照料者。

Abuse can be caused by one person or by two or more people. Unlike random acts of violence, which take place in all societies, abuse implies a relationship between the abuser and the abused, and the abuser or abusers are able to exert power and control over the abused.

虐待可以由单一施暴者发起，亦可由两人或多人合谋实施。不同于在任何社会中都可能偶发的随机街头暴力犯罪，虐待的本质必然深植于施暴者与受害者之间的某种特定人际关系纽带之中，且施暴者能够凭借某种优势地位对受害者行使不对等的权力压制与人身控制。

Abuse occurs in all age groups, all genders, at all social and economic levels, and in all cultures and communities. Abuse can take place in private or in public, for example, through an information medium such as a phone or the Internet.

虐待可侵袭任何年龄阶段、任何性别取向、任何社会经济阶层以及任何文化信仰与族群社区。虐待既可以发生在隐秘的家庭私域，亦可公然暴露于公共场所，或通过手机、网络社交媒体等现代信息媒介广泛蔓延。

As a support worker, you must know how to recognize and report suspected abuse. You need to realize that abuse can occur in workplaces, homes, and community settings as well as in health care facilities. In some cases, the victim is physically, emotionally, or financially dependent on the abuser.

作为支持工作者，你必须熟练掌握识别疑似受虐体征与法定规范汇报的核心技能。你必须深刻清醒认识到：虐待既可能肆虐于工作场所、私人居家、社区日间中心，亦可能暗藏于医疗保健机构与长期照护院舍之中。在极多案例中，受害者在生理自理、情感归属或经济生存上对施暴者存在着极高程度的深度依赖。

In Chapter 7, we discussed each person's right to be treated with respect and the need to accept people's differences at all times. Showing respect for all clients is especially important in the field of support work.

在第 7 章中，我们深入探讨了每个人享有受人尊重的神圣权利，以及在任何时候无条件包容接纳个体文化差异的必要性。在支持工作实务中，对全体服务对象倾注由衷尊重尤为关键。

It is an unfortunate truth, however, that some people (and especially some support workers) do not recognize that all of us have the right to safe, respectful treatment. When clients' rights are ignored, the clients become victims of abuse.

然而令人痛心的是，现实中仍有某些人员（尤其是少数缺乏职业素养的支持工作者）未能真正领悟到：我们所有人——无论身心失能与否——均享有获得安全、充满尊严照护的不可剥夺的基本人权。当服务对象的合法权益遭受漠视甚至践踏时，他们便沦为了受虐的受害者。

CANADIAN CHARTER OF RIGHTS AND FREEDOMS

《加拿大权利与自由宪章》的法理基石

The Canadian government has enacted laws to protect all Canadians, immigrants, and even visitors to Canada, from abuse and discrimination of any kind (BOX 12.1). Since 1982, the most important protection has been the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms, which states that Canada values equality and diversity and that no one should be abused and no one has the right to treat others unfairly.

加拿大政府通过了一系列严密的法案，保护全体加拿大公民、新移民乃至抵达加拿大的所有访客免受任何形式的虐待与歧视（专栏 12.1）。自 1982 年起，最具权威的核心法制屏障当属《加拿大权利与自由宪章》（The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms）。

Knowingly failing to comply with the Charter is illegal and can be considered abuse. The Charter also requires everyone—including government, the police, the courts, and all Canadians—to respect the basic human rights of others.

该宪章庄严宣示：加拿大崇尚人人平等与多元文化包容，任何人都绝不应当遭受虐待，且没有任何人拥有不公正对待他人的特权。明知故犯违背宪章规定不仅违法，亦可直接被定性为虐待侵权行为。宪章强制要求包括政府各级部门、警务执法机关、各级法庭以及全体加拿大居民在内的所有人，必须无条件尊重他人的基本人权。

TYPES OF ABUSE

虐待的主要表现形态全解析

There are many ways in which people can abuse others of all ages, in various settings. It is also important to note that more than one type of abuse can occur at the same time. Abuse sometimes comes in other forms, such as bullying, or cyberbullying.

在不同的执业与生活场景中，针对不同年龄段个体的虐待行为有着繁多的表现形式。尤为重要的是：在真实的临床与生活情境中，往往不止一种虐待形态在同一受害者身上同时交织发生。虐待还经常以欺凌（Bullying）或网络暴力欺凌（Cyberbullying）等现代衍生形式蔓延。

  • • Physical abuse is force or violence that causes pain, injury, and sometimes death. Physical abuse includes pinching, hair pulling, pushing, slapping, hitting, shaking, choking, biting, or kicking. It also includes burning, poisoning, using weapons, or throwing things (e.g., a chair or a hammer) at someone.
    身体虐待（Physical abuse）：指蓄意施加的导致身体剧痛、组织创伤甚至死亡的物理暴力或强力。包括掐捏、扯头发、推搡、掌掴、击打、剧烈摇晃、锁喉勒颈、咬伤或脚踢；亦包括烫伤灼伤、投毒、动用器械武器攻击，或向他人猛力投掷重物（如椅子或铁锤等）。
  • • Sexual abuse is unwanted sexual activity such as rape and attempted rape. Unwanted touching, fondling, kissing, and exposure (i.e., the abuser showing their genitals to the victim) are also considered sexual abuse. Sexual harassment—another form of sexual abuse—is any conduct, comment, gesture, threat, or suggestion that is sexual in nature.
    性虐待（Sexual abuse）：指任何违背意愿、未经知情同意的强迫性行为，如强奸及强奸未遂。未经允许的身体触碰、猥亵抚摸、强吻以及暴露行为（即施暴者向受害者故意裸露生殖器）均构成性虐待。性骚扰（Sexual harassment）是性虐待的特定法律形态，指任何具有性意味、令人感到被侵犯的不受欢迎举止、言辞评论、肢体手势、威胁恐吓或暗示。
  • • Emotional abuse (psychological abuse) occurs through words or actions that cause mental harm. It usually involves an attack on a person's self-esteem—for example, constantly insulting, humiliating, or rejecting a person, or saying that the person is 'stupid' or 'ugly.' Insults can also be directed at the person's sexual orientation, weight, or ethnic identity (see Chapter 7). All of these can harm the person's sense of worth and self-confidence. Emotional abuse can happen to infants, children, teens, adults, and older adults. Emotional abuse lessens the victim's dignity and self-worth because they are called names, yelled at, insulted, mocked, threatened, or sworn at. The victim may be ignored for long periods or may not be allowed to do preferred activities or visit with family, friends, or others. Threatening to harm someone or something that the victim loves (such as a child or a pet) is another example of emotional abuse.
    情感虐待/心理虐待（Emotional / Psychological abuse）：指通过语言攻击或侮辱性行为造成精神心理创伤的暴行。它通常直接针对个体的自尊心展开毁灭性践踏——例如持续不断的人身侮辱、当众羞辱、冷酷排斥，或贬斥长者“愚蠢无能”或“丑陋恶心”。侮辱亦可针对当事人的性取向、体型体重或种族民族身份（参见第 7 章）。这一切都会毁灭个体的存在价值感与自信心。情感虐待可侵袭婴儿、儿童、青少年、成年人乃至年迈长者。当受害者被起侮辱性外号、疯狂咆哮、讥讽嘲弄、恐吓威胁或恶毒诅咒时，其人格尊严被剥夺殆尽。受害者还可能遭受长期精神冷暴力（被彻底视若空气），或被剥夺从事喜好活动的自由，被严禁与亲友会面交往。以伤害受害者所挚爱的人或事物（如子女或宠物）相要挟，同样是极其恶劣的情感虐待。
  • • Financial abuse is the misuse of a person's money or property, usually for the abuser's financial gain. Financial abuse includes stealing, forging signatures, selling property or possessions without permission, and persuading or tricking victims to change their will or give up control of their finances. Some abusers do not allow their victims access to their own money.
    经济虐待（Financial abuse）：指非法侵占、挪用或不当掌控他人的资金或财产，通常以谋取施暴者的经济私利为目的。包括盗窃现金、伪造签名、未经许可擅自变卖长者的房产与私人物品，以及通过欺诈、恐吓或诱骗迫使受害者修改遗嘱或交出财务控制权。某些施暴者甚至彻底切断受害者使用其自身合法退休金与银行账户的途径。
  • • Neglect is failure to meet the basic needs of a dependent person. Victims of neglect are usually children, people with disabilities, and frail older persons. Neglect occurs when a clean, comfortable, and safe environment is not provided. In some cases, the caregiver may even withhold or deny basic needs such as food, water, or clothing. Personal care, medical care, education, and attention and love may also be denied or withheld. The caregiver may knowingly neglect the victim or unintentionally neglect the person because of an inability to provide adequate care.
    照料忽视（Neglect）：指照护者未能满足生活依赖者的生存与发育基本需求。受害者通常为未成年儿童、残障人士以及极度衰弱的老年人。当未能为其提供清洁、舒适且安全的生存居住环境时便构成了忽视。在严重案例中，照护者甚至刻意剥夺或克扣诸如食物、饮用水或御寒衣物等生命必需品；生活护理、必要医疗、教育机会以及情感关切与爱抚亦被长期剥夺。忽视既可能是照护者明知故犯的主观遗弃，亦可能源于照护者能力严重不足、身心枯竭导致的非故意性照护失职。
  • • Bullying is a type of abuse in which one person or a group of people purposely and repeatedly says or does hurtful things to someone else. It is also known as harassment. The abuse can include (a) physical bullying, such as hitting, spitting, or breaking someone else's belongings; (b) verbal bullying, such as name calling, threats, and teasing; and (c) social bullying, such as spreading rumours, gossiping, or making others look foolish. It can occur anywhere: schools, nursing homes, and in the workplace (see Workplace Bullying on page 190).
    欺凌 / 霸凌（Bullying）：指一个人或一个群体有目的且反复多次对他人实施言语或肢体伤害的虐待行为，亦称骚扰。包括：(a) 肢体欺凌，如殴打、吐口水或故意损坏他人财物；(b) 言语欺凌，如侮辱性绰号、恶意恐吓与嘲讽；以及 (c) 社交欺凌，如散布恶意谣言、人际孤立或当众出丑。欺凌可发生于任何场所：学校、照护机构以及职场工作环境中（参见第 190 页职场欺凌）。
  • • Cyberbullying (also known as cyberabuse) is a type of abuse that uses communication technologies like the Internet, social networking sites, text messaging, and instant messaging with the purpose of repeatedly intimidating or harassing a person. Cyberbullying is not limited to any one age group. There are a number of types of cyberbullying (BOX 12.2). It is illegal to post untrue statements about a person (see discussion of libel and slander on page 72 in Chapter 5).
    网络欺凌（Cyberbullying，亦称网络虐待 Cyberabuse）：指利用互联网、社交网站、手机短信和即时通信软件，以反复恐吓、羞辱或骚扰特定个人为目的的现代虐待形态。网络欺凌绝不局限于某一特定年龄群体，涵盖多种衍生形态（专栏 12.2）。在网络上公开发布针对他人的不实虚假言论属于违法犯罪行为（参见第 5 章第 72 页关于文字诽谤与言语诽谤的法律界定）。

BOX 12.1 The Rights and Freedoms of All Canadians

专栏 12.1 加拿大全体居民法定人权与自由全貌
The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms (1982) ensures that all Canadians, new immigrants, permanent residents, and visitors to Canada have the following rights:

• Fundamental Freedoms. This section includes the rights to freedom of conscience and religion; freedom of thought, belief, opinion, and expression, including freedom of the press and other media of communications; and freedom of peaceful assembly and association. Therefore, Canadians, new immigrants, and visitors to Canada have the right to practise the religion of their choice and express their views without fear of being punished in any way. They can also voice their opinions in print, in person, or on the Internet, radio, or television. We all have the right to choose who we want to communicate with (or not), and we can join together in a peaceful manner to express our opinions.

• Equality Rights. This section includes the rights to equal treatment before and under the law and equal benefit and protection of the law without discrimination based on race, national or ethnic origin, colour, religion, gender, age, and mental or physical disability. Other grounds of discrimination that were not specifically set out in the Charter, such as sexual orientation and marital status, were recognized in 1985. We are all considered equal, and nobody can get poorer (or special) treatment because of their differences.

• Democratic Rights. These sections contain the right to vote, the right to run for public office, and the maximum duration of legislative bodies.

• Mobility. This section ensures the right to move from place to place within Canada and ensures that Canadian citizens are free to enter and leave Canada as they please. It also gives citizens and permanent residents the right to move to and live in any province.

• Legal Rights. These sections set out the rights that protect us in our dealings with the justice system. These rights ensure that individuals who are involved in legal proceedings are treated fairly, especially those charged with criminal offences, and include the rights:
- To have life, liberty, and security of the person
- To be secure from unreasonable search and seizure
- To be free from unnecessary detention or imprisonment
- To be informed promptly of reasons for any arrest or detention
- To retain and instruct counsel on arrest or detention
- To be tried within a reasonable time by an impartial tribunal
- To be presumed innocent
- To have protection against self-incrimination
- To be free from cruel and unusual treatment or punishment
- To have the assistance of an interpreter

• Language Rights. These sections confirm that English and French are the official languages of Canada and assert the equality of the French and English languages in particular situations. Federal laws must be published in both English and French.

• Minority Language Education Rights. This section establishes that provincial governments must provide certain English- or French-language minority schooling where numbers warrant.

BOX 12.2 Types of Cyberbullying, Its Impact, and How to Stop It

专栏 12.2 网络欺凌的常见形态、身心摧残与终结防范策略
Cyberbullying includes:
• Sending mean or threatening emails or text/instant messages
• Posting embarrassing photos of someone online
• Creating a website to make fun of others
• Pretending to be someone by using their name
• Tricking someone into revealing personal or embarrassing information and sending it to others

Cyberbullying can impact its victim in a number of ways, regardless of age, leading to:
• Stress-related health problems (headaches, stomachaches)
• Social anxiety, loneliness, and isolation
• Depression
• Low self-esteem
• Social withdrawal or aggressive behaviours
• Suicidal thoughts or attempts

If you or someone you know is a victim of bullying:
• Report criminal offences, such as threats, assaults, and sexual exploitation to the local police detachment.
• Report unwanted text messages to your telephone service provider.
• Report online bullying to the social media site and block the person responsible.
• Walk away or leave the online conversation.
• Keep track of the bullying (write it down and/or save a screenshot of the online message).

BOX 12.3 Cyberbullying Legislation

专栏 12.3 加拿大刑法针对非自愿分享私密图像的刑事立法
As of March 10, 2015, it is an offence under Canada's Criminal Code to share intimate images of a person without the consent of the person appearing in the image. This law applies to everyone, not only to people under age 18. The purpose of this offence is to protect the privacy a person has with regard to nudity or sexual activity. An 'intimate image' is defined as an image that depicts a person engaged in explicit sexual activity or that depicts a sexual organ, anal region, or breast. The image also has to be one in which the person depicted had a reasonable expectation of privacy at the time of the recording and never gave up any privacy interest at the time of the offence.

With digital technology rapidly changing, there has been an increase of cyberbullying in the form of distributing intimate or sexual images without the consent of the person in the photo or video. This type of behaviour can occur in a variety of situations. Often, it is used as a form of revenge: a person has willingly shared an intimate image of themselves with a partner and when the relationship ends, that partner distributes those photos publicly (this is sometimes called 'revenge porn').

Whatever the motivation, the impact of this kind of cyberbullying can be devastating to a person's self-esteem, reputation, and mental health. In some cases, these acts may have played a part in teens taking their own lives. Judges now have the authority to order the removal of intimate images from the Internet if the images were posted without the consent of the person or persons in the image.

• They could be imprisoned for up to 5 years;
• The computer, cell phone, or other device they used to share the image could be seized and forfeited; and
• They could be ordered to reimburse the victim for costs incurred in removing the intimate image from the Internet or elsewhere.

ABUSIVE RELATIONSHIPS

虐待关系的本质特征与权力控制

Abuse can occur in many different kinds of relationships. An abuser can be a caregiver, such as a parent, child, friend, grandparent, step-parent, or relative. Sometimes, the abuser is a nonrelative who is close to the person, such as a family friend or a parent's or adult child's partner.

虐待可潜伏并爆发于极其多元的人际关系形态之中。施暴者既可能是负有直接抚养监护职责的照料者（如亲生父母、成年子女、密友、祖父母、继父母或近亲属）；有时，施暴者亦可能是与家庭关系紧密的非血缘亲信人员（如家庭密友、父母或成年子女的新伴侣）。

All age groups are at risk and all are equally likely to be victims, although some people are at greater risk for abuse (BOX 12.4). Abuse occurs in all types of families: rich and poor, educated and uneducated. Any individuals can be abused by their spouses or partners; children can be abused by their classmates or by significant others; older persons can be abused by their caregivers or their adult children; or clients can be abused by health care workers. Health care workers can also be abused by their clients.

虽然理论上任何年龄群体均面临风险并可能沦为受害者，但特定人群具有显著更高的易感脆弱性（专栏 12.4）。虐待遍及各种类型的家庭环境：无论贫富贵贱、教育程度高低。任何人均可能遭受配偶或伴侣的家暴摧残；儿童可能沦为同学霸凌或监护人虐待的受害者；高龄长者可能遭受照料者或成年子女的欺凌侵占；服务对象可能遭受医护人员的失范侵犯；而医护工作者在临床执业中亦同样面临遭受服务对象暴力与言语攻击的严峻风险。

BOX 12.4 Persons at Highest Risk for Abuse

专栏 12.4 虐待极高危易感脆弱人群全景深度剖析
Even though anyone could potentially be abused (or, for that matter, be an abuser, given the right circumstances), there are certain groups of people who are especially at risk for being abused. Abuse may damage such persons' sense of self-worth and dignity and increase their social isolation, making them even more vulnerable to abuse. Also, abused persons may be unable to access (or not know about) support systems that are available to them. These people include:

• Marginalized persons (especially women). Those who (1) are unable to report abuse because they are not fluent enough in an official language, or (2) belong to a visible-minority group (for example, members of Indigenous communities or some ethnic groups, or perhaps members of the LGBTQ2 community), (3) are geographically isolated, (4) have a physical or intellectual disability, (5) live with a mental health disorder, (6) have (or had) a substance abuse problem, or (7) work in the sex trade, and are marginalized, which means they are usually shunned by mainstream society. People who are marginalized are usually treated like they are 'invisible' and they often feel powerless to report abuse, or they may not be understood or believed when they do report it, in which case authorities may not give as much attention to their reports as to other people's reports. Sexual assault against women is particularly prevalent in Northern Canada, where there is a much higher proportion of Indigenous people.

• Children. Children are usually dependent on the person who is caring for them. They are too young to know how to defend or protect themselves. Some parents or caregivers think that they have the right to 'discipline' their children by using abusive force or words. Others are overwhelmed with the stress and responsibilities of being a parent or guardian, or they very likely were abused themselves during their childhood. Some abuse is punitive, but it can also be sexual in nature.

• Socially or geographically isolated older persons. Older people who cannot get out of the places where they live may not have an opportunity to tell anyone that they are abused. Bruises and other obvious physical signs might also go unnoticed if these older persons are prevented from having contact with others. Older people may not know how to escape from an abuser or may lack the strength to seek safety and shelter.

• Older persons with reduced cognitive capacity. These people may not know or remember that they have been abused. They may no longer have control over their finances, so they may not know that their money or belongings are being taken.

• Dependent older persons with disabilities. If older persons do not have opportunities to interact with others, abuses can often go unrecognized. In the case of physical abuse, bruises are often explained away by the abuser as being caused by the disability, age, or general health condition of the older person.

• Older persons cared for by people with substance abuse problems. People with substance abuse problems may lack impulse control—that is, the ability to control their angry outbursts or to deal with the older person's challenging behaviours in a nonviolent manner. Others may have financial problems and steal from the older person they care for to support their own substance addiction.

Source: Adapted from Government of Canada. (2011). Elder abuse is wrong, Department of Justice.

• 边缘化弱势人群（尤其是边缘化女性）：包括：(1) 因官方语言（英/法）不流利而丧失对外求助能力的移民；(2) 属于少数族裔、原住民（Indigenous）社群或 LGBTQ2+ 群体的人员；(3) 地理位置极端隔绝偏僻的居民；(4) 罹患肢体失能或智力发育残障者；(5) 伴有精神心理疾病患者；(6) 存在或曾有物质滥用成瘾史的人员；以及 (7) 从事性工作者。他们长期遭受主流社会的冷漠排斥与忽视，在社会治理中往往沦为“隐形人”；他们感到无力报警或求助，即便主动报告也往往难以获得调查人员的充分信任与重视。针对女性的性侵犯与暴力在加拿大北部领地尤为高发，当地原住民人口比例高且医疗司法资源极度匮乏。

Abusive relationships are complex, and there is usually no single cause for the abuse. In most cases of abuse by a caregiver, the abuse has been triggered when the abuser felt overwhelmed and felt that no help was available to care for their physically or mentally challenged child or dependent parent. However, in all relationships, a person is more likely to be abusive if they are dealing with the following: Problems with alcohol or drugs; Mental illness (for example, major depression) or severe personality flaws (for example, an explosive temper, inability to control impulses, or lack of empathy for others); Abuse as a child; Going through a period of high stress, such as divorce, unemployment, poverty, or illness.

虐待关系的形成极其复杂，通常绝非由单一诱因所致。在大多数家庭照料者实施虐待的案例中，往往是由于照护者在面对身心失能的残障儿童或重病依赖的高龄父母时，承受了难以承受的长期重压，且深感外界没有任何援助可用，从而引发了绝望中的情绪崩溃与暴力外溢。然而，在所有人际关系中，施暴者往往具备以下高危特征：深陷严重的酒精或毒品物质滥用问题；伴有重度精神心理疾病（如重度抑郁症）或严重的人格缺陷（如暴躁易怒、冲动控制障碍或极端缺乏同理共情能力）；童年时期遭受过家庭虐待创伤；正在经历人生重大高应激变故（如婚姻破裂离婚、失业破产、极度贫困或恶性疾病）。

RECOGNIZING THE SIGNS OF ABUSE

敏锐识别虐待与忽视的临床客观指征

As a support worker, you are responsible for recognizing the signs of abuse. These signs generally apply to all abusive relationships, in all settings.

作为支持工作者，你的法定专业职责是敏锐识别出可能提示受虐的客观体征与行为警讯。这些临床指征广泛适用于各种场景下的虐待关系。

However, you are not qualified to judge whether or not your client is being abused. You may think you recognize one or some of the signs (see the Think About Safety: Signs and Symptoms of Abuse box), but such signs do not always mean that abuse has occurred. These signs might, however, suggest that abuse is a possibility.

然而，你必须清醒认识到：支持工作者没有法定资质去最终判定或裁决服务对象是否遭受了虐待。你或许观察到了某项或某些可疑体征（参见“安全前线：虐待与忽视的临床症状全景核查表”专栏），但单一指征并不绝对等同于虐待必然发生；然而，这些指征确凿无疑地提示着虐待的极大可能性。

Watch for signs of abuse or neglect in the course of your daily work with your clients. Immediately report any suspicions or observations to your supervisor.

在与服务对象日常接触的每分每秒中，请时刻保持高度的临床警惕。一旦察觉任何可疑的虐待或忽视线索，必须立即以事实为依据向你的临床主管汇报。

Your supervisor will ensure that the situation is investigated. Know your employer's procedures for reporting observations or suspicions of abuse.

主管将启动跨学科调查与法定处置程序。你必须熟练掌握所在机构关于观察记录与汇报疑似虐待的具体操作规程。

Victims of abuse can be subjected to one or more of the following types of abuse:

Physical Abuse
• Physical injuries (such as burns, bumps, bruises, scratches, cuts, or fractures) that frequently go untreated and either are unexplained or have unlikely explanations
• New injuries that appear while older injuries are still healing
• Frequent injuries (such as burns, bruises, welts, or cuts) on the face, neck, inner arms, back, upper arms, and inner thighs (people who are abused often try to hide their injuries under clothing or makeup)
• Welts, bruises, or burns that have the shape of the object that caused the injury; for example, the shape may be of a handprint, belt, or wooden spoon
• Unexplained missing or loose teeth
• A very groggy or overly sedated client

Sexual Abuse
• Irritation or injury (such as cuts and bruises or scarring) of the thighs, perineum, or breasts
• Intense fear of bathing or perineal care
• Torn, stained, or bloody underwear
• Vaginal discharge, genital odour, and painful urination
• Difficulty walking or sitting
• Avoidance of touching (see the Supporting Emmy Lou: One Abuse Survivor's Struggle box on page 182)

Emotional Abuse
• A change in behaviour, especially if the client seems depressed or unusually quiet or withdrawn
• Fearful behaviour, especially in the presence of the suspected abuser; for example, the client cowers, avoids eye contact, or trembles
• Unwillingness to talk or answer questions
• Change in behaviour when the suspected abuser enters and leaves the room
• Withholding of permission to socialize with family or friends or client's withdrawal from contacts
• Private conversations not allowed—the suspected abuser insists on being present or within hearing distance of all conversations
• Change in behaviour of the suspected abuser (who may be the caregiver)—for example, on one occasion she is pleasant and cooperative and, on another, is defensive or hostile

• Frequent statements by the caregiver that the person in their care is difficult or demanding

• 长者珍爱的贵重首饰、银行卡或私人物品莫名不翼而飞，且缺乏任何合理交代；
• 涉嫌施暴者长期处于无业状态，但对自身日常生活开销的经济来源极度隐瞒回避；
• 长者在动用自身合法退休金、书写支票或进行任何日常小额消费时，必须向施暴者屈辱地“申请批准”。

【五、照料忽视体征（Neglect Signs）】
• 个人卫生极度匮乏：如指甲过长深嵌甲沟感染（嵌甲）、满口重度龋齿残根、全身多处未处置的慢性溃疡与擦伤、头发严重板结油腻、体味刺鼻、衣物肮脏破旧；
• 严重的营养不良与脱水征象：进行性消瘦脱形、极度口渴、皮包骨样恶病质面容、眼窝深陷、面颊干瘪、皮肤干燥枯槁如羊皮纸；
• 骨隆突处出现难以愈合的压疮褥疮（Pressure Sores）；
• 长期断药：处方救命药物因未去药房配药购买而长期停滞；
• 长期缺乏基本生活监护，长者被反锁或孤立遗忘在床榻上数小时甚至整天无人问津。

【六、婴幼儿与儿童特异性受虐与忽视体征】
除上述全部指征外，儿童与婴儿出现以下异常高度警示受虐或忽视：
• 行为举止突发两极逆转：如已学会如厕的儿童突然重发夜间遗尿（Bed-wetting）、日间大小便失禁或食欲急剧丧失；
• 长期反复出现不明原因的剧烈呕吐或顽固性腹泻；
• 全面生长发育迟缓：体重不增反降，无法如期达成翻身、独坐、站立或语言发育里程碑；
• 婴儿精神萎靡淡漠：对周围环境缺乏最基本的好奇反应、极少哭泣发声，表现出反常的“过度温顺且从无需求”的病态退缩状态；
• 生活在极其脏乱、充满安全隐患或缺乏基本水暖设施的恶劣环境中。

SPOUSAL ABUSE

配偶虐待与亲密伴侣暴力

Spousal abuse is abuse that occurs between intimate partners in a marriage or common-law relationship and may be physical, sexual, emotional, or financial. Usually, more than one type of abuse is present in the relationship. Spousal abuse is abuse in any love-partner relationship, including same-sex relationships. Individuals of all genders can be abused by their partners.

配偶虐待（Spousal abuse，亦称亲密伴侣暴力 Intimate Partner Violence）是指在合法婚姻关系或事实同居伴侣关系中，一方对另一方所实施的侵害行为，涵盖身体暴力、性侵犯、情感精神折磨以及经济剥削等多重维度。在真实的家暴关系中，往往多种虐待形态交织并存。配偶虐待适用于任何形式的恋爱伴侣关系，包括同性恋伴侣关系。

However, women are statistically more severely injured than men are. The incidence of male injury from abuse in a man-woman relationship is not as well known because, in many cases, the male is less likely to report this abuse. Domestic violence rates among intimate couples usually increase when the woman is pregnant or caring for an infant.

任何性别的人群均有可能沦为亲密伴侣暴力的受害者。然而流行病学数据证实，女性在遭受家暴时受到的肉体创伤往往更为严重与致命。男性受暴者的统计数据常被严重低估，因为出于传统社会性别观念的羞耻感，男性往往极少主动报警求助。尤为触目惊心的是：临床数据显示，当女性处于妊娠期（怀孕期）或产后哺乳抚育新生儿的高压阶段时，家庭暴力的发生率与恶化程度往往显著飙升。

Why Do They Stay?

深度伦理剖析：受害者为何选择隐忍留守？

People who are abused by their partner often deny the abuse. They also often choose to stay with their partners in spite of serious physical violence.

遭受伴侣残酷虐待的受害者往往在外界询问时本能地否认受虐事实。

Some victims may stay because they are afraid of leaving their abuser, who might have threatened them with harm, death, or denial of child custody. In some circumstances, the victim may stay so that the children can continue to have the financial and other comforts of a two-parent home. Some victims stay because they do not know where to go or how to get help.

即便面对严重的肢体肉体暴力，他们亦常常做出令人费解的选择——继续留守在施暴者身边。深入剖析其心理与现实困境，受害者选择隐忍留守的核心诱因包括：极度恐惧离开施暴者后会招致毁灭性报复——施暴者常常凶残威胁称一旦敢离开便会杀害受害者本人、屠戮其父母全家或通过司法手段彻底剥夺孩子的抚养权；为了让年幼的子女能够继续享受双亲家庭的经济支撑与物质条件；受害者在长期的控制与孤立下完全丧失了社交资源，根本不知道能够去往何方寻求庇护，甚至完全不了解社会上存在着妇女庇护所与紧急法律救济机制。

THE CYCLE OF ABUSE

虐待的恶性循环三阶段模型

Abuse is rarely an isolated, one-time occurrence. Instead, it typically follows a predictable and destructive three-phase cycle that tightens over time:

家庭暴力与亲密关系虐待极少是一次性的偶然偶发事件。相反，它通常呈现出高度规律性、步步收紧且反复发作的恶性循环机制，贯穿于三大核心演进阶段之中：

  • • 1. Tension-Building Phase: The abuser experiences increasing tension, anger, and irritability over minor daily issues. The abuser may become overly critical, sullen, or verbally abusive. The victim recognizes the mounting danger and often feels as if they are 'walking on eggshells,' desperately trying to please, placate, or avoid antagonizing the abuser in a futile attempt to prevent violence.
    第一阶段：紧张积聚期（Tension-Building Phase）。施暴者因日常生活中的琐碎小事积蓄越来越强烈的烦躁、怨气与易怒情绪，开始频繁进行冷嘲热讽、刻意找茬或言语攻击。受害者能够敏锐嗅到空气中一触即发的危险气息，内心时刻处于“如履薄冰（Walking on eggshells）”的极度恐慌中，竭尽全力顺从、讨好或回避对方，试图以自我牺牲来延迟或阻止暴力的全面爆发。
  • • 2. Abusive Phase (Acute Battering Incident): The tension explodes into acute, uncontrollable physical, sexual, or emotional violence. The abuser deliberately exerts physical force, inflicts injuries, destroys property, or unleashes severe verbal terror. The victim is subjected to trauma, severe pain, and psychological devastation.
    第二阶段：急性暴力爆发期（Abusive Phase / Acute Battering Incident）。积聚的暴戾情绪如火山般爆发，升级为彻底失控的肢体暴力、性侵犯或极端精神摧残。施暴者毫无节制地拳打脚踢、动用器械殴打、砸碎财物或发起极尽恶毒的死亡威胁。受害者遭受严重的躯体创伤与不可逆的精神休克。
  • • 3. Honeymoon Phase (Calm and Remorse): Following the violent explosion, the abuser suddenly undergoes a dramatic behavioral reversal. The abuser appears deeply remorseful, weeps, begs for forgiveness, buys extravagant gifts, and promises to never hurt the victim again. The abuser becomes exceptionally loving, attentive, and charming, often blaming external stressors (like alcohol or work pressures) for the 'lapse.' Falsely believing that genuine transformation has occurred, the victim forgives the abuser and abandons plans to escape—until tension inexorably begins to build once again.
    第三阶段：蜜月宽恕期（Honeymoon Phase / Calm and Remorse）。在狂暴的发泄过后，施暴者态度发生令人咋舌的戏剧性逆转。施暴者展现出痛心疾首的悔恨，痛哭流涕地下跪道歉、恳求宽恕、购买贵重礼物，并信誓旦旦发誓“下不为例、绝不再犯”。在此阶段，施暴者表现出异乎寻常的温柔体贴与深情款款，并习惯性将暴行甩锅给外部压力（如醉酒或工作不顺）。受害者被这种虚假的悔改所迷惑，误以为对方已真正痛改前非，从而选择宽恕并放弃求助或出逃计划——直至紧张的阴云再度不可避免地重新笼罩。

Supporting Emmy Lou: One Abuse Survivor's Struggle

临床实务案例：支持艾米·卢——一位受虐幸存者的心理创伤与触碰回避
Emmy Lou is a 32-year-old woman who grew up in a loving family. One night, when Emmy Lou was in high school, she was babysitting her neighbour's two children, as she often did. She was upstairs putting the children to bed and did not hear the children's father come into the house and up the stairs. She greeted him warmly, but he suddenly grabbed her, covered her mouth, and sexually assaulted her. He threatened to kill her and her family if she ever told anyone.

Terrified, Emmy Lou kept the assault a secret for years. The trauma severely impacted her mental health, causing chronic anxiety, depression, and severe touch avoidance. Even now, during routine medical examinations or supportive personal care, any sudden or unexpected touch causes her extreme panic and triggers flashback memories of the assault.

As a support worker, understanding Emmy Lou's trauma history is critical. Always explain what you are about to do before touching her, ask for her permission, maintain eye contact, and allow her to set the pace of care.
艾米·卢（Emmy Lou）现年 32 岁，成长于一个充满关爱的温暖家庭。高中的一个夜晚，艾米·卢像往常一样在邻居家协助照看两个年幼的孩子。当她在二楼哄孩子们入睡时，完全没有听到孩子的父亲已经悄然进屋并走上楼梯。她礼貌地向他打招呼，但那名男子突然凶残地抓住她、捂住她的嘴，对她实施了严重的性侵犯。施暴者恶狠狠地威胁她：如果她敢向任何人透露半句，就将她及其全家彻底杀害。

在极度恐惧与绝望中，艾米·卢将这段梦魇般的受虐经历深埋心底多年。这一毁灭性的创伤严重摧毁了她的心理健康，导致她长期遭受慢性重度焦虑、抑郁以及极其严重的“触碰回避”（Touch Avoidance）。即便到了今天，在接受常规身体检查或支持性个人护理时，任何突如其来或未提前告知的肢体接触都会引发她极度的恐慌发作，并瞬间诱发对当年性侵场景的创伤后闪回（Flashback）。

作为支持工作者，深入理解艾米·卢的创伤背景是提供安全照护的前提。在进行任何肢体接触前，必须清晰告知下一步操作并征得其知情许可，保持温暖的目光交流，绝不突兀触碰，由长者自主掌控照料节奏。

CHILD ABUSE

儿童虐待与忽视的深远危害与法定分类

Child abuse occurs when a parent, guardian, or caregiver mistreats or neglects a child, which may result in: Significant emotional or psychological harm; Serious risk of harm to the child. Child abuse is a gross misuse of a caregiver's position of trust and authority over a child.

虐待儿童（Child abuse）是指父母、法定监护人或照料者对未成年儿童实施的虐待或照料忽视，直接导致：严重的心理创伤与精神损害；使儿童面临遭受严重身体与身心伤害的致命风险。虐待儿童是对照护者本应享有的信任地位与监护权威的极端滥用与背叛。

Children who are abused tend to experience more social problems and perform less well in school than other children. As a result, as adults, they can remain poorly adjusted socially and emotionally.

长期遭受虐待的儿童在人际交往中表现出极其严重的社交适应障碍，学业成绩普遍显著落后于健康同龄人。其毁灭性创伤甚至会延续终生，导致其成年后在情绪管理、人际依恋与社会融入中长期处于严重的心理失调状态。

Types of Child Abuse and Neglect

儿童受虐与被忽视的四维分类
  • • Physical abuse includes beating, shaking (such as in Shaken Baby Syndrome), burning, kicking, or suffocating a child.
    儿童身体虐待：涵盖对儿童进行毒打、掌掴脚踢、烫伤灼烧、窒息闷堵，以及极其致命的“摇晃婴儿综合征（Shaken Baby Syndrome）”——由于婴儿颈部肌肉与脑部血管极其脆弱，剧烈前后摇晃可直接引发致命性颅内出血与不可逆永久脑损伤。
  • • Sexual abuse includes any sexual exploitation, fondling, exposure, or involving a child in pornography.
    儿童性虐待与性剥削：包括针对未成年人的任何非自愿性接触、猥亵抚摸、性器暴露，以及强迫儿童参与拍摄任何色情淫秽影像制品。
  • • Emotional abuse includes constantly demeaning, ridiculing, terrorizing, or isolating the child, conveying that the child is worthless or unloved.
    儿童情感虐待：包括对孩子进行持续的人格侮辱、当众讥讽、心理恐吓或长期关禁闭孤立，向孩子灌输其一无是处、毫无价值且不配被爱的毁灭性心理暗示。
  • • Physical and emotional neglect includes failure to provide food, shelter, warm clothing, medical/dental care, hygiene, supervision, or maternal love and bonding.
    身体与情感忽视：表现为未能为儿童提供温饱饮食、御寒衣物、安全住所、必要的医疗与牙科救治、基础卫生自理，或长期剥夺亲子依恋关爱与情感滋养。

Failure to Thrive

儿童生长发育停滞与衰亡状态

Failure to thrive is a term used to describe infants, babies, or young children who are well below developmental norms for body weight, height, or cognitive milestones without an identified medical organic disease. While some causes are organic, a primary known etiology of failure to thrive is severe caregiver neglect and emotional deprivation.

生长发育迟缓与消退综合征（Failure to thrive）是一个严峻的儿科医学诊断术语，特指婴儿、幼儿在无明确器质性原发病的前提下，体重增长、身高发育或神经认知发展指标显著低于同龄人群正常生长曲线的最低阈值。尽管少数病例源于器质性吸收障碍，但在绝大多数临床实践中，该综合征的最主要病因正是照护者的极度生活照料忽视与长期情感冷暴力剥夺。

One of the many known causes of failure to thrive is caregiver neglect. In extreme cases, it can lead to permanent developmental delays and even the death of the client. Unfortunately, many of the mothers of infants described with failure to thrive were themselves likely also abused in the past.

照护者的长期忽视是诱发生长发育停滞的公认首恶。在极端恶劣的情形下，这种剥夺会导致患儿不可逆的永久性智力与身体残疾，甚至直接导致患儿衰竭死亡。更令人扼腕叹息的临床流行病学事实是：被诊断为生长发育迟缓的患儿的母亲们，其自身往往在童年时代亦遭受过极其严重的家庭虐待与忽视创伤，从而深陷跨代际创伤传递的悲剧死循环。

Supporting Taylor: Understanding Factors Contributing to Abuse

临床实务案例：支持泰勒——深刻剖析引发儿童受虐的高危复合诱因
Taylor is a 6-year-old boy who was born with fetal alcohol spectrum disorder (FASD; see Chapter 36). His weight and height are well below developmental norms, and he exhibits severe hyperactivity, impulsivity, and difficulty following simple instructions.

Taylor's mother, Sarah, is a 24-year-old single parent living in poverty. She has no family support nearby and works long hours at minimum-wage jobs. Sarah was herself severely physically and emotionally abused as a child and struggles with depression. Frequently overwhelmed by Taylor's challenging behaviours, Sarah often screams at him, locks him in his room for hours, and has on multiple occasions slapped him hard across the face, leaving visible finger welts.

A community support worker assigned to assist with family respite notices the bruises, Taylor's emaciated appearance, and Sarah's explosive temper. Recognizing the acute intersection of maternal trauma, developmental disability, poverty, and isolation, the worker immediately documents the objective findings and notifies both the supervisor and child welfare services to initiate emergency family support and protection intervention.
泰勒（Taylor）是一名 6 岁的男童，患有先天性胎儿酒精谱系障碍（Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorder, FASD，参见第 36 章）。他的身高和体重严重低于同龄儿童的发育标准，且表现出极端的活动过度、冲动攻击性以及难以遵循简单生活指令的行为障碍。

泰勒的母亲萨拉（Sarah）现年 24 岁，是一名在贫困线上挣扎的年轻单亲母亲。她没有任何亲属在身边协助，靠打零工维系微薄生计。萨拉在童年时代曾遭受过极其残酷的身体与情感虐待，长期深陷抑郁症的折磨。面对泰勒极为棘手的多动与哭闹行为，身心俱疲的萨拉常常陷入歇斯底里的情绪失控：疯狂对泰勒咆哮、将其反锁在卧室数小时，甚至多次狠抽他的耳光，在面部留下清晰的指痕与淤青红印。

受派上门协助提供家庭喘息服务的社区支持工作者敏锐察觉到了泰勒身上的淤伤、极度消瘦营养不良的体征以及萨拉一触即发的暴躁情绪。该支持工作者深刻洞察到：童年受虐创伤史、患儿发育残障、生活极度贫困与社会孤立构成了诱发虐待的致命复合温床。工作者立即以绝对客观的事实记录所有观察结果，并第一时间上报给主管与儿童保护福利机构（CAS），全面启动紧急家庭干预与法定儿童保护程序。

ABUSE OF OLDER PERSONS

长者虐待：临床高发诱因、易感性与法律保护

Abuse of older persons (often referred to as elder abuse) can occur in private homes or in health care institutions. Elder abuse can be physical, emotional, sexual, financial, or neglect. Often, financial abuse is combined with emotional or physical abuse—for example, an adult son who moves in with his aging parent after losing his job and takes control of the parent's home and pension cheques.

长者虐待（Abuse of older persons / Elder abuse）既频繁肆虐于私人居家环境，亦悄然滋生于长期照护院舍等医疗机构之中。长者虐待涵盖身体、情感、性虐待、经济侵占以及生活忽视五大维度。在现实案件中，经济虐待往往与身体暴力或精神恐吓高度捆绑——例如成年子女在失业破产后强行搬回年迈父母家中同住，随后一步步霸占父母的房产所有权并强行扣留老人的养老金支票以供自己挥霍。

Abused older persons sometimes choose not to complain about the abuse. Like abused spouses, they may fear the abuser but are also dependent on them, or may not know where or how to get help. They may fear being forced to move into a long-term care facility if they report their caregiver.

遭受虐待的老年人往往在面对外界时选择保持沉默、隐忍不报。如同受虐配偶一样，长者内心虽对施暴者充满了恐惧，但在生活自理上又彻底依赖对方，或者根本不知道去向何处求援。

Some older people feel deep shame that their own children are abusing them, or they may feel that they are somehow to blame. Social isolation, lack of language or literacy skills, and cognitive impairment make older adults uniquely vulnerable to abuse.

更为普遍的恐惧在于：长者极其害怕一旦举报了身为子女的照料者，自己便会被迫逐出熟悉的居所，被强行送入冰冷的长期照护机构。许多长者对被自己的亲生骨肉虐待深感奇耻大辱，甚至错误地认为是自己教子无方、愧对家庭。严重的社会隔绝、官方语言文字障碍以及伴随衰老而来的认知机能障碍，使老年群体具有独特的极高受害脆弱性。

ABUSE BY HEALTH CARE WORKERS

医护人员执业失范与机构虐待防范

Abuse can be very obvious, such as roughly handling or force-feeding a client. Or it can be very subtle (not obvious); for example, staff can demean or belittle clients by calling them 'grandma' or 'slugger' or by referring to their incontinence briefs as 'diapers.' These comments are embarrassing for the client and violate the DIPPS (dignity, independence, individualized care, preferences, privacy, and safety) principles. Staff may also neglect clients by ignoring call bells, withholding care, or failing to reposition immobile clients, leading to pressure ulcers.

WHAT TO DO WHEN CLIENTS SPEAK OF ABUSE

当服务对象向你披露受虐事实时的专业应对指南

You should be prepared for the possibility of a client or child telling you about being abused. In such situations, it is important that you support the person and know how to offer immediate help. Follow your employer's guidelines and policies. The following are some general guidelines on how to be supportive:

作为一线支持工作者，你必须随时做好心理准备：某位长者或儿童可能会在照护信任建立后，悄悄向你倾诉自己正在遭受的受虐惨剧。在此类极度敏感的危机时刻，懂得如何给予当事人坚定的情感支撑并启动即时专业援助至关重要。必须时刻严格遵循雇主规章与法定报告流程。以下是提供专业支持的核心执业黄金法则：

  • • Reassure the person. Reassure the client that they are not to blame for the abuse, that their safety is paramount, and that help is available.
    给予坚定的情感抚慰（Reassure the person）：真诚肯定地告知服务对象：遭受虐待绝不是他们的过错，他们的人身安全是最重要的事情，机构和专业团队一定会全力提供保护与支持。
  • • Maintain confidentiality within the care team. Inform the client gently that you must report this information to your supervisor/nurse so that proper protection and resources can be coordinated.

HOW TO REPORT ABUSE

法定虐待汇报程序的四大黄金铁律

Your employer will have specific rules regarding how to report your observations. It is very important to document your observations in writing as soon as possible after the event occurs. When reporting abuse, remember to follow these essential protocols:

你的雇主机构必将拥有关于如何规范汇报疑似虐待的详尽操作指南。在可疑事件发生后，第一时间以书面形式准确客观记录全部第一手见闻至关重要。在汇报涉嫌虐待时，必须且仅能严格恪守以下核心法纪程序：

  • • Record only objective facts—do not make assumptions or interpretations. Document exactly what you saw, heard, and smelled, and quote the exact words used by the client using quotation marks.

Think About Safety: What to Do When a Client Is Abusive

安全防卫专栏：遭遇攻击性或暴力倾向服务对象时的自我防护黄金法则
When confronted by an aggressive, volatile, or abusive client, your safety is paramount. Follow these proven clinical safety protocols:

• Stay calm. Take slow, deep breaths. Keep your facial expressions neutral and your voice calm, steady, and low-pitched.
• Stand up so that the client is not in a dominant physical position over you.
• Stand far enough away from the client—at least 2 m (6 ft)—so that the client cannot suddenly hit, kick, or grab you.
• Position yourself close to an open door or clear exit route so you can escape quickly without being trapped in a corner.
• Note the exact locations of call bells, emergency pull cords, alarms, closed-circuit monitors, and telephones.
• Keep your hands free and visible. Do not touch the client or make sudden movements that could be interpreted as threatening.
• Do not argue, defend yourself, or scold the client. Validate feelings while setting firm, calm boundaries.
• Protect yourself. If you believe you are in immediate physical danger, quickly leave the room or house, proceed to a safe area, and immediately call for emergency help or contact your supervisor.
当面对具有强烈攻击性、情绪失控或暴力虐待倾向的服务对象时，你自身的人身安全永远是第一位的最高法则。必须严格践行以下临床防卫黄金规程：

• 保持绝对冷静：进行深长缓慢的腹式呼吸；面部表情保持平静中立，语调平稳、低沉且坚定，绝不显露惊慌或愤怒。
• 立即站立起身：绝不要坐在椅子上或蹲下，防止失控长者在体格上处于居高临下的物理压制优势。
• 保持充足的安全防御距离：与当事人保持至少 2 米（6 英尺）以上的安全间隔，防止其突然伸手击打、脚踢或猛烈撕扯抓挠。
• 始终占据靠近门或逃生通道的位置：绝不要将自己置于被逼入墙角、床角或封闭死胡同的绝境，确保身后逃生动线畅通无阻。
• 敏锐锁定现场安全救援设施：进屋第一步即须查明呼叫铃、应急拉绳警报器、监控探头及座机电话的确切方位。
• 双手自然垂放并保持空置可见：绝不要将双手插入口袋，切勿伸手强行触碰或推搡长者，避免任何可能被误解为攻击敌意的突兀肢体动作。

WORKPLACE BULLYING AND HARASSMENT

职场欺凌与骚扰的法律界定与雇主法定防护责任

Workplace bullying involves acts or verbal comments that could mentally hurt or isolate a person in the workplace. Bullying can be carried out by a co-worker, a supervisor, or a group of employees.

职场欺凌（Workplace bullying）是指在工作场所中发生的足以对他人的精神心理造成严重创伤、羞辱或人际孤立的蓄意言行。欺凌的实施者既可能是同级同事、上级主管，亦可能是抱团排挤他人的员工小团体。

Harassment occurs when conduct, comments, or gestures offend or humiliate another person. Sexual harassment can be directed toward women, men, or individuals of all gender identities. Provincial Occupational Health and Safety legislation across Canada strictly mandates that employers protect staff from all forms of workplace harassment and violence (BOX 12.5).

当某些不受欢迎的言行举止或肢体手势冒犯、羞辱或贬低了当事人时，便构成了职场骚扰。性骚扰绝不仅针对女性，在女性占多数的医护职场中亦常针对男性员工，并侵犯所有性别身份群体。加拿大各省《职业健康与安全法案》（Occupational Health and Safety Act）严格确立：雇主负有法定义务保护全体员工免受职场骚扰与暴力的侵害（专栏 12.5）。

BOX 12.5 Workplace Bullying and Harassment: Examples and Worker Protections

专栏 12.5 职场欺凌与骚扰的典型表现形式及员工法定防护权益
Workplace bullying and harassment can take many toxic forms:
• Spreading malicious rumours, gossip, or innuendo
• Excluding or isolating someone socially or professionally
• Intimidating a person with yelling, swearing, or aggressive body language
• Undermining or deliberately impeding a person's work (e.g., hiding supplies or withholding critical patient care information)
• Constantly changing work guidelines or assigning unreasonable workloads
• Demeaning comments about a person's gender, race, culture, or personal life
• Unwelcome sexual jokes, suggestive remarks, or unwanted physical contact

Under Occupational Health and Safety legislation, Canadian employers MUST:
1. Develop and post written workplace harassment and violence policies.
2. Provide mandatory anti-harassment training to all employees.
3. Establish confidential reporting and fair investigation procedures.
4. Protect employees from retaliation for reporting harassment in good faith.
职场欺凌与骚扰在临床环境中常表现为以下剧毒失范行为：
• 恶意散布同事的恶毒谣言、八卦绯闻或不实中伤；
• 在社交交往或专业交接中故意冷落、孤立排挤特定同事；
• 通过咆哮、爆粗口、拍桌子或侵略性肢体动作实施精神威慑恐吓；
• 蓄意暗中搞破坏阻碍同事正常履行职责（如恶意藏匿护理耗材、故意隐瞒患者关键交班病情）；
• 频繁朝令夕改工作标准，或蓄意给特定员工指派非人道、不可能完成的惩罚性超负荷排班；
• 针对同事的性别、种族肤色、文化背景或个人私生活发表具有侮辱贬损意味的言论；
• 讲荤段子性暗示笑话、发表下流评论或进行未经许可的不当肢体触碰。

根据加拿大各省《职业健康与安全法案》，雇主必须强制履行以下法定职责：
1. 制定并在工作场所显眼位置张贴书面的《反职场骚扰与反暴力法定政策》；
2. 为全体新老员工提供系统规范的强制性反欺凌与防骚扰实操培训；
3. 设立严密保密的投诉渠道与客观公正的独立调查仲裁程序；

果断终结与维权反制职场欺凌的四大实操步骤

Access your organization's Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for confidential psychological counseling and trauma recovery support.
    第四步：正式提起书面申诉并启用员工援助计划（EAP）。严格依据雇主规程向人力资源部（HR）、高层管理层或工会正式提交书面投诉信。同时充分利用机构提供的“员工援助计划（Employee Assistance Program, EAP）”，获取免费且绝对隐私保密的专业临床心理疏导与创伤复原支持。

    全体医护人员必须高度警惕并熟练掌握成人与儿童受虐的典型生理体征、行为异样及情绪改变（详见教材第 180 至 181 页）。
  • • People at highest risk for abuse are (1) marginalized people, especially women; (2) children; (3) older persons; (4) socially or geographically isolated older persons; (5) older persons with reduced cognitive capacity; (6) dependent older persons with disabilities; and (7) older persons cared for by people with substance abuse problems.
    虐待极高危易感人群包括：(1) 边缘化群体（尤其是原住民女性、LGBTQ2+等）；(2) 未成年儿童；(3) 高龄长者；(4) 遭遇社会或地理隔绝的独居老人；(5) 认知机能衰退或痴呆症患者；(6) 严重肢体残障完全依赖照料的长者；以及 (7) 由物质滥用（酗酒/吸毒）人员照料的脆弱个体。
  • • Situations that increase the risk for child abuse include (1) family crisis, (2) presence of nonbiological or transient parental partners, (3) isolation, and (4) caring for children with special needs.
    显著增加儿童受虐风险的高危现实诱因包括：(1) 家庭重大变故或经济危机；(2) 家中存在频繁更换的非亲生伴侣；(3) 极端社会隔绝；以及 (4) 抚养患有先天残障或重症特殊照护需求的患儿。
  • • All health care workers should be aware of what to do if they suspect that one of their clients has been abused or if a client admits to being abused: (1) follow your employer's rules on how to report your observations; (2) record all your observations and make your reports in writing; and (3) keep all notes in case you are asked to remember details in the future.
    当怀疑服务对象受虐或当事人主动吐露受虐事实时，医护人员必须严格践行三原则：(1) 严格遵循雇主规章与法定程序上报观察所得；(2) 详实客观地以书面形式记录所有观察到的第一手事实证据；(3) 妥善永久保存全部原始笔记以备法律诉讼调查。
  • • When reporting abuse, record only the facts you know or were told by the alleged victim—do not make assumptions. Record the following: (1) the alleged victim's name, address, phone number, age, and gender; (2) the alleged abuser's name, address, phone number, and relationship to the victim; (3) your description of abuse or neglect, suspicions, and evidence obtained; (4) the date, time, and place of the abuse.
    汇报涉嫌虐待时，必须且仅能记录已知客观事实或受害者原话陈述——严禁任何主观臆断或推论猜测。记录要素必须完备：(1) 受害者姓名、住址、电话、年龄与性别；(2) 涉嫌施暴者姓名、联系方式及其与受害者的关系；(3) 涉嫌受虐或忽视的具体客观体征、嫌疑线索与获取的证据；(4) 案发的准确日期、具体时间与发生地点。
  • • When reporting abuse, it is essential to respect and protect your client's right to privacy. Tell only those who need to know. Do not gossip or tell anyone who is not directly involved.
    在汇报虐待全流程中，必须严格捍卫服务对象的法定隐私保密权利。仅能向法定必须知情的主管或执法专职人员汇报，严禁任何形式的同事八卦或向无关人员泄露案情。
  • • Health care workers should be aware of how to handle an aggressive, volatile, or abusive situation involving a client.
    医护人员必须受过专业培训，熟练掌握在面对具有攻击性、情绪失控或暴力虐待倾向的服务对象时的专业降温与自我防护脱身规程（详见教材第 188 至 190 页）。
  • • Bullying in the workplace can have very negative effects on staff. It is the employer's duty to protect employees from risks at work.
    职场欺凌与骚扰会对员工的身心健康造成严重不可逆创伤。根据各省职业健康与安全法案，雇主负有不可推卸的法定责任为员工创造并维护免受骚扰与暴力的安全执业环境。

CRITICAL THINKING IN PRACTICE: Preserving Adult Dignity and Responding to Institutional Mistreatment

临床批判性思维实战：捍卫成年长者尊严与抵制机构内贬损虐待行为
【CLINICAL SCENARIO】
Ms. Hild has experienced urinary incontinence and chewing and swallowing problems since her stroke. When speaking to Ms. Hild, a co-worker uses the term 'diaper' to describe her incontinence product and 'bib' for her clothing protector. The same co-worker wipes spilled food from Ms. Hild's face with a spoon and threatens to withhold privileges if she does not finish meals. How will you respond to your co-worker's statements and actions? What are your responsibilities if you witness this behaviour? Explain why it is important to treat the person as an adult and not a child.

【CLINICAL ETHICAL ANALYSIS】

1. Deconstruct the Abusive Behaviours:
- Infantizing Demeaning Language: Calling an adult's protective garment a 'diaper' or 'bib' is emotional/psychological abuse. It strips the client of adult dignity and violates DIPPS. The correct clinical terms are 'incontinence brief/product' and 'clothing protector'.
- Rough Physical Disrespect: Scraping food with a metal spoon can cause pain, skin tears, and humiliation. Staff should gently wipe the face with a warm, damp cloth or napkin.
- Threatening and Coercion: Threatening to withhold activities or privileges is emotional abuse and coercive intimidation.

2. Professional Direct Response to Co-Worker:
- Intervene calmly and advocate immediately for the client: 'Let's use a soft damp cloth to clean Ms. Hild's face so she feels more comfortable.'
- In private, address the professional standards firmly: 'Calling these products diapers and bibs makes Ms. Hild feel embarrassed. Our agency and professional guidelines require us to use respectful adult terminology like brief and clothing protector, and threatening to withhold activities violates her rights.'

3. Duty to Report Witnessed Abuse:
- Under professional codes of conduct and institutional policy, every healthcare worker has an absolute ethical and legal duty to protect clients from abuse. You must document the exact objective facts witnessed and report the incident directly to your supervisor/nurse.

4. The Core Principle of Adult Dignity:
- Ms. Hild is an adult with decades of life experience, wisdom, and intrinsic human worth. Illness and disability do not diminish adult personhood. Treating an adult like a helpless, misbehaving child induces severe shame, depression, and loss of self-esteem. Person-centred care demands unconditional respect.
【临床真实情境】
希尔德女士（Ms. Hild）在中风后遗症后出现了尿失禁以及咀嚼吞咽功能障碍。在与其沟通时，你的一位同事当着老人的面使用“尿布（Diaper）”来称呼成人失禁吸收垫，用“围嘴（Bib）”来称呼成人衣物围兜保护巾。不仅如此，该同事在老人嘴角溢出食物残渣时直接粗暴地用金属勺子在其脸上用力刮蹭擦拭，甚至严厉恐吓老人：“如果你今天不把饭全吃完，就取消你参加下午集体娱乐活动的资格！”你该如何得体应对同事的言行举止？目击此类行为时你肩负着怎样的法定与伦理职责？深刻阐述为何必须将服务对象视为拥有完整尊严的成年人、坚决摒弃婴儿化对待的极端重要性。

【临床伦理与界限剖析】

1. 深入剖析虐待与失范行为的本质：
- 婴儿化与贬损性语言（Emotional/Psychological Abuse）：对成年长者使用“尿布”、“围嘴”等幼儿词汇属于典型的情感与精神虐待，直接践踏长者的人格尊严，严重违背 DIPPS 准则。临床标准术语必须使用“失禁吸收裤/护理垫（Incontinence Brief/Product）”与“衣物防护围巾（Clothing Protector）”。
- 粗暴的物理侵犯与羞辱：用坚硬的金属勺子刮擦面部极易刮伤脆弱皮肤，且充满羞辱性。必须使用温热柔软的湿毛巾或餐巾轻柔拭去食物。
- 恐吓与剥夺权利：以取消活动资格相威胁属于严重的精神恐吓与霸凌，侵犯长者的基本自由。

2. 现场与事后对同事的专业应对策略：
- 现场即时温和介入保护长者：“我们换一条柔软温热的毛巾帮希尔德女士擦拭吧，这样她会更舒服。”
- 事后私下严肃沟通：“使用‘尿布’和‘围嘴’会让希尔德女士深感难堪。根据机构政策与执业标准，我们必须使用成人尊重术语；以取消活动恐吓她进食更是严重违规。”

3. 目击虐待时的法定汇报义务：
- 根据照护工作者职业守则与法律要求，医护人员对目击的任何虐待行为负有不可推卸的法定保护与汇报责任。必须如实记录目击时间、准确言行等客观事实，第一时间向临床主管护士书面报告。

4. 捍卫成年人尊严的核心伦理逻辑：
- 希尔德女士是一位拥有数十年丰富人生阅历、社会贡献与独立人格的成熟长者。突发的疾病与失能绝不会剥夺她作为成年人的崇高尊严。将失能长者当作不听话的幼童加以训斥与贬损，会诱发深重的屈辱感、无望感与绝望抑郁。全人照护要求无论其身体机能如何受损，均须以崇高敬意平等相待。
