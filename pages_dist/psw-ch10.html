学习目标 (Objectives)

▶

After reading this chapter, the learner should be able to do the following: • Describe the benefits of working as part of a multidisciplinary health care team. • Explain the differences between regulated health care professionals and unregulated care providers. • Differentiate between assigning and delegating tasks. • Explain the role of the nurse and the support worker in the delegation process. • Describe the five rights of delegation and when it is legally and clinically appropriate to refuse a delegated task.

完成本章研读后，学习者应当能够达成以下核心专业能力考核目标： • 深刻阐明作为跨学科多专业卫生保健团队（Multidisciplinary Team）一员协作执业的巨大临床优势。 • 严谨辨析法定受监管卫生专业人员（Regulated Health Care Professionals）与非受监管照护提供者（Unregulated Care Providers, UCPs）的法律界限与执业范畴。 • 准确鉴别日常“工作指派”（Assigning）与法定“任务委派”（Delegating）在法律效力与责任归属上的本质差异。 • 详尽阐释在工作委派全流程中，主管注册护士与支持工作者各自所承担的神圣法定职责。 • 熟练掌握“工作委派五大正确原则”（Five Rights of Delegation），并熟稔在何种法律与临床情形下有权并必须坚决拒绝接受委派。

第一节：跨学科医疗团队与全人照护协作 (Pages 151 - 153)

团队协作的临床优势与全人视角 (Benefits of Working on a Team)

▶

The goal of the health care team is to provide the client with the best possible care and support. When providing care, team members must consider the whole client and promote physical, emotional, social, cognitive, and spiritual well-being. Benefits of the multidisciplinary team approach include: • Comprehensive care: Different specialists provide expertise tailored to all dimensions of the client's life. • Improved safety and reduced errors: Open communication and collaborative double-checking catch potential risks early. • Continuity of care: When shifts change or a worker is ill, consistent care plans ensure smooth transitions. • Enhanced job satisfaction: Team members support one another, reducing stress and preventing burnout.

医疗卫生团队的崇高使命，在于为受助案主提供最高品质的全人关怀与支持。在团队照护中，全体成员必须贯彻整体论哲学，协同促进案主在身体生理、情绪心理、社会交往、智力认知以及精神信仰各大维度的全面安康。 跨学科团队协作模式的显著临床优势： • 全方位综合照护： 团队涵盖各领域专才，针对案主复杂的健康需求量身定制协同解决方案。 • 保障临床安全并降低失误率： 坦诚的交接班沟通与多专业交叉核对，能防患于未然，在萌芽状态截断用药失误或跌倒隐患。 • 照护流程的无缝连贯性： 当出现交接班轮替或员工突发休假时，标准化的照护计划与档案记录可确保服务不脱节。 • 提升执业认同感并分担压力： 团队成员在危急时刻守望相助，有效缓解心理孤立感，预防职业倦怠。

BOX 10.1: Working on a Team: Core Principles for High-Performing Collaboration

§ SECTION · 章节核心专题

专栏 10.1：医疗团队协作基石：卓越跨学科团队成员的黄金行为准则

▶

To be an effective team member: • Understand your role, scope of practice, and job description thoroughly. • Understand and respect the roles and responsibilities of other team members. • Communicate clearly, honestly, and promptly. Report any changes in the client's condition immediately. • Be positive, dependable, and cooperative. Complete your assigned tasks on time. • Respect confidentiality. Discuss client matters only with team members directly involved in the client's care. • Support your colleagues. Offer assistance when you have completed your own assignments.

成为一名受人尊重的卓越团队成员，必须恪守以下守则： • 彻底吃透自身法定的执业范畴（Scope of Practice）与岗位描述，清醒知晓“哪些能做，哪些绝对不能越界”。 • 深刻理解并真诚尊重其他团队专业成员（医生、护士、物理治疗师、营养师）的法定职责与专业权威。 • 沟通力求精准、透明与及时。一旦察觉案主生命体征或精神状态有丝毫异样，第一时间向主管护士交班。 • 严守信誉、协作度高。保质保量在规定时间内闭环完成派工单上的各项支持任务。 • 坚守保密防线。仅在负责该案主的直接医疗团队内部讨论病情，绝不在走廊、电梯或食堂高谈阔论。 • 发扬同侪互助精神。当高效完成自身工作后，主动向忙碌的同事伸出援手。

第二节：工作指派（Assigning）与任务委派（Delegating）的法律界限 (Pages 154 - 156)

指派与委派的本质法律分野 (Assigning vs Delegating)

▶

The difference between assigning and delegating is a critical legal and professional distinction for support workers: • Assigning: Handing over the responsibility for providing care that is already within the worker's recognized scope of practice and job description. - Example: A supervisor assigns you to assist Mr. Smith with a complete bed bath, morning dressing, and feeding. These are core personal support tasks. The supervisor assigns the task; you are responsible for carrying it out. • Delegating: Transferring the authority to perform a specific procedure that is normally beyond the support worker's recognized scope of practice (often a regulated, invasive, or controlled health act). - Example: Teaching and authorizing a support worker to administer eye drops, apply a medicated cream, or perform a clean urinary catheterization. - The supervising nurse retains ultimate accountability for ensuring the client's safety and determining whether delegation is appropriate. The support worker accepts responsibility for performing the task competently and strictly according to the nurse's training.

精准厘清“工作指派”（Assigning）与“任务委派”（Delegating）的法律界限，是捍卫支持工作者自身执业执照与人身安全的第一道防火墙： • 工作指派（Assigning）： 指主管护士安排支持工作者执行本就属于其法定岗位描述与执业范畴之内的常规照护操作。 - 典型范例：主管护士指派您协助史密斯先生完成晨间床上擦浴、协助穿衣以及辅助进食。这些纯属支持工作者的本职核心业务。护士拥有指派权，您独立对操作过程与结果承担责任。 • 任务委派（Delegating）： 指受监管护士将某项原本超出支持工作者法定执业范畴、属于受法律严格管制的专业医疗护理操作（Controlled Acts），依法单向授权委派由该支持工作者执行的法定程序。 - 典型范例：护士经过系统评估、针对特定案主手把手培训并考核通过后，授权委派支持工作者为该长者滴眼药水、涂抹处方药膏或实施清洁导尿。 - 核心法律责任分配： 主管护士始终承担终极问责性（Accountability）——必须为该委派决定是否恰当、案主是否安全承担全责；而支持工作者则承担执行职责（Responsibility）——必须严格按照护士培训的步骤精准、安全执行，绝不可自行发挥修改！

BOX 10.2: The Five Rights of Delegation

§ SECTION · 章节核心专题

专栏 10.2：工作委派五大正确原则（加拿大护士学会与执业法规黄金门禁）

▶

Before delegating any task to an unregulated care provider, the supervising nurse must verify the Five Rights of Delegation. The support worker must also use these five rights to determine whether it is safe to accept the task: 1. The Right Task: Is the task one that can be safely delegated for this specific client? (Must be a stable, predictable procedure). 2. The Right Circumstances: Is the client's condition stable and predictable? Has a thorough nursing assessment been completed? 3. The Right Person: Does the support worker have the specific training, demonstrated competence, and confidence to perform the task for this particular client? 4. The Right Direction and Communication: Has the nurse provided clear, concise, step-by-step instructions, including what to observe and what parameters to report immediately? 5. The Right Supervision and Evaluation: Is the nurse available to assist if needed? Will the nurse evaluate the client's response and the worker's performance?

在向非受监管人员委派任何护理操作之前，主管护士必须严格核实以下“五大正确原则”；支持工作者同样必须以这五大门禁自我审视是否能够安全接受委派： 1. 正确的任务（The Right Task）： 该项操作是否依法属于可委派范畴？（必须是操作步骤明确、风险可控且结果可预期的标准化操作）。 2. 正确的情境（The Right Circumstances）： 案主的生命体征与病情是否绝对稳定？护士是否已完成详尽的临床评估？若案主病情急转直下，绝不可委派！ 3. 正确的人员（The Right Person）： 该支持工作者是否接受过针对该案主的专门实操培训？是否已被护士面对面考核通关并记录在案？支持工作者内心是否具备充分信心？ 4. 正确的指导与沟通（The Right Direction & Communication）： 护士是否提供了清晰明确的分步操作指导？是否明确告知了重点观察指标以及何种异常参数必须立即上报？ 5. 正确的监督与评估（The Right Supervision & Evaluation）： 当出现突发意外时护士是否随时能够就近支援或电话联络？护士是否按时对案主的反应及支持工作者的操作质量进行实地核查评估？

BOX 10.3: Delegation Legislation in Canada (BC, Alberta, Nova Scotia, Ontario)

§ SECTION · 章节核心专题

专栏 10.3：加拿大各省工作委派法定规约（卑诗省、阿尔伯塔、新斯科舍与安大略省法规对比）

▶

Health care legislation across Canadian provinces sets strict boundaries on delegation: • British Columbia: The Health Professions Act governs delegation. Unregulated care providers may perform tasks that are normally considered nursing acts only when delegated by a registered nurse under strict agency policies and client-specific training. • Alberta: The College of Licensed Practical Nurses of Alberta (CLPNA) and the College of Registered Nurses of Alberta (CRNA) establish that delegation is client-specific and task-specific. A delegation to perform a task for Client A does not authorize the worker to perform it for Client B. • Ontario: The Regulated Health Professions Act (RHPA) specifies controlled acts. Nurses may delegate controlled acts (such as administering certain medications or wound care) to support workers only when the client's condition is stable and predictable and the procedure has an established, routine outcome. • Nova Scotia: Emphasizes that delegation is a transfer of authority, not a transfer of responsibility. The nurse remains legally accountable for monitoring the client.

加拿大各省医疗法规对护士委派工作设立了极其严密的法定防火墙： • 卑诗省（British Columbia）： 依据《卫生专业法案》（Health Professions Act），非受监管照护者仅在主管注册护士依照严密机构制度完成针对特定案主的技能培训后，方可代为执行特定的管制护理操作。 • 阿尔伯塔省（Alberta）： 省护士监管学院确立了“针对特定案主（Client-specific）且针对特定任务（Task-specific）”的委派铁律。护士委派您为 A 案主执行某项操作，绝对不等于授权您擅自为 B 案主执行相同操作！ • 安大略省（Ontario）： 依据《受监管卫生专业法案》（RHPA），护士仅在案主病情处于绝对稳定、预后清晰且操作步骤高度例行常规化时，方可将特定受管制行为（如给药、特定无菌换药）委派给支持工作者。 • 新斯科舍省（Nova Scotia）： 法规严正强调：委派仅仅是执行权的单项让渡，绝非终极责任的转嫁！委派护士始终在法律上对案主的生命安全承担连带监控问责。

第三节：拒绝委派任务的专业准则与沟通范式 (Pages 157 - 160)

何时有权并必须坚决拒绝委派？ (When to Refuse a Delegated Task)

▶

Support workers have a legal and ethical duty to refuse a delegated task under certain circumstances. You must refuse a task if: • The task is beyond your legal scope of practice or beyond what your agency's policies allow. • The task is not in your written job description and has not been officially delegated through proper training. • You have not been trained to perform the task for this specific client. • You were trained, but you do not feel comfortable or competent performing the task safely. • The client's condition is unstable, rapidly deteriorating, or unpredictable. • The nurse is not available for supervision or emergency assistance. • The directions from the nurse are unclear, incomplete, or contradictory.

How to Refuse Professionally: Refusing a task is not insubordination; it is a vital act of client safety. When refusing: • Never say "That's not my job" and walk away. • State clearly, calmly, and professionally why you cannot perform the task. • Example: "I cannot administer Mrs. Chen's insulin injection because I have not received the required certified training and agency authorization for subcutaneous injections." • Offer alternative support: "I am happy to assist Mrs. Chen with her breakfast while you prepare the medication."

在特定情形下，坚决拒绝接受委派是支持工作者不可推卸的法定职业操守与人权底线！出现以下任何一种情形，您必须坚决拒绝（Must Refuse）： • 该项操作公然违反省卫生立法，或直接违背雇主机构的书面执业政策。 • 该项任务未包含在岗位职责中，且此前未经过正规授权委派流程。 • 您此前从未接受过针对该特定案主执行该操作的手把手培训。 • 虽然曾经接受过培训，但由于长时间未操作，您感到手生心慌，无法确保 100% 胜任与安全。 • 案主病情出现剧烈波动、生命体征极不稳定或处于恶化边缘。 • 委派护士即将外出或下班，现场没有任何具有资质的护士能够提供实时指导或急救支援。 • 护士的口头或书面操作指令含糊其辞、残缺不全甚至前后矛盾。

专业拒绝的沟通典范： 专业拒绝绝不等于违抗上级命令或消极怠工，而是为了捍卫长者生命的专业负责之举！拒绝时必须恪守以下范式： • 严禁冷冰冰地扔下一句“这不关我的事”扭头就走！ • 态度沉着、冷静，用客观事实清晰阐明无法执行的法理与专业依据。 • 典范陈述：“护士长，我无法为陈太太注射胰岛素。因为根据省护理法规与我们机构的执业政策，我尚未接受并通过皮下注射的专业委派资质考核。” • 积极提供建设性替代方案：“在您为陈太太抽吸并注射胰岛素期间，我很乐意先协助隔壁房间的王先生洗漱进餐，以便为您节省宝贵时间。”

3. A nurse has trained and authorized you to apply a prescription medicated ointment to Mrs. Smith's leg. Mr. Brown, across the hall, has the identical prescription. Can you apply it to Mr. Brown?
中文题意：主管护士对您进行了专业培训并授权您为史密斯太太的腿部涂抹处方药膏。对门病房的布朗先生恰巧开具了完全相同的处方药膏。您能否直接顺手为布朗先生涂抹？
A Yes, because you are now trained in applying that ointment.
B No, delegation is client-specific and requires separate authorization.
C Yes, if Mr. Brown asks you to do so.
D Yes, as long as you document it afterwards.
标准权威答案： 选项 B
临床实操解析与知识点溯源：
在加拿大各省执业法规中，任务委派绝不是针对某项技术的泛化通票，而是“特定案主、特定任务、特定情境”（Client-specific and task-specific）。为 A 案主获得的授权，绝对不能私自迁移到 B 案主身上！

4. Which of the following is NOT one of the Five Rights of Delegation?
中文题意：下列哪一项不属于国际公认的“工作委派五大正确原则”？
A The Right Task
B The Right Circumstances

标准权威答案： 选项 C
临床实操解析与知识点溯源：
工作委派五大正确原则包括：正确的任务（Right Task）、正确的情境（Right Circumstances）、正确的人员（Right Person）、正确的指导与沟通（Right Direction/Communication）、正确的监督与评估（Right Supervision/Evaluation）。选项 C 纯属干扰项。

5. Under which circumstance MUST a support worker refuse a delegated task?
中文题意：在下列哪一种情形下，支持工作者必须坚决拒绝接受委派？
A You are tired at the end of a shift.
B The nurse is busy with another client.
C The task has never been taught to you and is not in your job description.
D The client is grumpy and uncooperative.
标准权威答案： 选项 C
临床实操解析与知识点溯源：
未经培训且超出法定执业范畴的操作（选项 C），若盲目逞能动手，将直接构成严重非法执业与医疗过失犯罪！支持工作者必须坚决拒绝，这是对案主生命安危与自身职业生命的严密护栏。
