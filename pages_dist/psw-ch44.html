Some clients need help keeping their living environment clean, orderly, healthy, and safe. While the primary role of any support worker is to keep the client safe in the home, as a support worker you might also be assigned to home management, which is the cleaning and organizing of a home. Duties might include light housekeeping tasks, such as straightening and cleaning parts of rooms, vacuuming, dusting, washing dishes, making beds, and doing laundry. Occasionally, you may also be required to take care of clients’ pets, such as walking the dog if the client is unable to. Whatever your tasks are, they will be specified on each client’s care plan.

部分客户需要协助以保持其居住环境的清洁、有序、健康与安全。虽然任何支持人员的首要职责始终是确保客户在居所中的生命与人身安全，但作为支持人员，你也可能会被指派负责家庭环境管理（Home management），即家庭居所的日常清洁与整理。具体职责通常包含轻度家务维护任务（Light housekeeping tasks），例如整理并清洁房间局部、吸尘、擦拭除尘、洗涤餐具、整理床铺以及洗涤衣物。在特定情况下，你还可能受托协助照料客户的宠物，例如在客户无法行动时代为遛狗。无论你的具体任务是什么，都必须在每位客户的护理计划中明确列出。

Some clients are on cytotoxic drugs (primarily used to treat cancer) such as antineoplastic drugs (see Chapter 45), so it is important to be aware of your responsibility for the safe handling of any such items that have come into direct contact with the client. See the Think About Safety: Guidelines for Safely Assisting Clients Who Are on Cytotoxic Drugs box.

部分客户可能正在服用细胞毒性药物（主要用于治疗癌症），例如抗肿瘤药物（详见第45章）。因此，了解并严格履行安全处理任何与客户发生直接接触的物品的职责至关重要。请参阅“安全考量：安全协助接受细胞毒性药物治疗客户的指南”专栏。

You will not be assigned heavy housecleaning tasks, such as washing windows or cleaning carpets. In some areas, clients must pay for any home management services unless they are also receiving personal support. Hospitals and long-term care facilities might also have support workers do basic housekeeping tasks. However, most facilities have a housekeeping staff. This chapter focuses on home management in community care settings.

你绝不会被指派执行重体力家务清洁任务（Heavy housecleaning tasks），例如清洗室外窗户或深度清洗地毯。在某些行政区域，客户必须为家政服务自费支付费用，除非他们同时接受个人生活照护支持。医院和长期照护机构有时也会要求支持人员分担基础保洁工作，但大多数机构都配备了专职的保洁人员队伍。本章重点阐述社区及居家照护环境中的家政管理实务。

THINK ABOUT SAFETY · 安全警示专栏

Guidelines for Safely Assisting Clients Who Are on Cytotoxic Drugs / 协助接受细胞毒性药物治疗客户的安全指引

It is possible that you will be responsible for a client who is being treated with a cytotoxic drug such as an antineoplastic (or chemotherapy) drug (see Chapter 45). Items that have come into direct contact with cytotoxic drugs or the body fluids from a client who is receiving them can potentially pose serious health risks to others for up to 7 days after discontinuation of the medication. This is known as cytotoxic contamination. Examples of items that can be contaminated include incontinence products, toileting surfaces, eating utensils, linen, and clothing.

你可能会负责照护正在接受细胞毒性药物（如抗肿瘤药或化疗药物，见第45章）治疗的客户。直接接触过细胞毒性药物的物品，或接触过用药客户体液的物品，在停药后长达 7天 的时间内，仍可能对他人构成潜在的严重健康风险。这被称为细胞毒性污染（Cytotoxic contamination）。容易受到此类污染的物品包括：成人失禁用品、卫浴坐便器表面、餐具、床单织物与贴身衣物。

Recommendations for providing safe care include the following / 提供安全照护的核心建议包括：
  • Wear protective gloves while handling waste containers: Cytotoxic waste must be handled differently than regular garbage, and must be disposed of according to provincial regulations.
    处理废弃物容器时必须佩戴防护手套：细胞毒性医疗废弃物必须区别于普通生活垃圾，且必须依据所在省份的环境法规进行专门隔离封闭处理。
  • Use proper personal protective equipment (PPE): This includes protective gloves made of vinyl or nitrile rubber. Gloves should be changed frequently, or immediately if punctured, cut, or torn. It is also recommended that workers wear two pairs of gloves at a time, for additional protection.
    佩戴符合标准的个人防护装备（PPE）：包括由乙烯基（Vinyl）或丁腈橡胶（Nitrile）制成的防化手套。手套应频繁更换，若发生穿刺、割破或撕裂必须立即更换。强烈建议支持人员同时佩戴两层手套以提供双重物理屏障保护。

THE SUPPORT WORKER’S ROLE IN HOME MANAGEMENT

支持人员在家庭环境管理中的执业职责

A clean and orderly setting is important for health and safety. Dust, dirt, and damp areas promote the growth of microbes, and clutter can cause falls and serious injury. People who have illnesses or disabilities, those recovering from surgery or injuries, new parents, caregivers tending to sick family members, and older persons may not be able to do regular housekeeping.

清洁且井然有序的环境对健康与安全至关重要。积尘、污垢与潮湿区域会助长微生物滋生繁殖，而地面散落的杂物则极易诱发跌倒并导致严重创伤。患病或残障人士、手术或创伤康复期患者、新手父母、照顾患病家属的家庭看护者以及高龄老人，往往无法独立完成常规家务保洁。

Who Pays for What Services?

服务费用的出资方与权责边界

The client, family, and case manager decide what household tasks need to be done and list them in the client’s care plan. Clients are usually charged for home management services according to their income, with government-funded subsidies covering part or all of the costs for those who qualify. In other situations, private insurance or the client directly covers the costs. Regardless of who pays, the support worker must strictly adhere to the tasks outlined in the care plan.

客户本人、家属以及个案经理共同商定需要完成哪些家务工作，并将这些任务详尽记录于客户的护理计划（Care plan）中。家政管理服务的收费标准通常依据客户的收入水平核定，符合资格者可由政府财政补贴全额或部分报销。在其他情形下，则由私人商业保险或由客户个人全额自费。无论由何方支付费用，支持人员都必须严格遵守护理计划中列明的任务清单，不得擅自增减。

p. 1036 Assisting Clients With Money & Compassionate Care (DIPPS)

Assisting the Client With Tasks Involving Their Money

协助客户处理涉及金钱的事务

As a support worker, you may occasionally be required to accompany your client to do personal banking, buy groceries, or pay bills. When assisting your client with these tasks, you should keep the following points in mind:

作为支持人员，你可能偶有需要陪同客户前往银行办理个人业务、采购日用食品杂货或代缴账单。在协助客户处理此类涉及金钱的任务时，必须时刻铭记以下职业行为守则：

  • Care Plan Authorization: This duty must be clearly written in the client’s care plan and must follow the policies of your employing agency. The care plan should specify exactly what your responsibilities are. Some clients with severe arthritis, for example, may just need assistance with handling their money because they cannot hold it without dropping it. Others may need you to pick up some groceries and provide an account of the purchases.
    护理计划明确授权：该项职责必须明确载入客户的护理计划中，且必须完全符合雇佣机构的相关政策。护理计划应当确切界定你的责任范围。例如，某些重度关节炎患者仅因无法握稳纸币或硬币而需要抓握协助；另一些客户则可能需要你代购食品并出具详细的采购账目对账单。
  • Agency Policies: Your supervisor should be able to answer any questions that you might have about your agency’s policies regarding financial responsibilities. You should not agree to anything that is not specified on your client’s care plan.
    严守机构财务政策：关于机构在财务经手方面的规章政策，若有任何疑虑应随时咨询主管。对于护理计划中未明确规定的任何金钱请求，绝不可私自应允。
PROVIDING COMPASSIONATE CARE · 仁爱照护践行

Home Management Through DIPPS Principles / 践行DIPPS原则的家庭环境管理

Dignity (尊严): All people deserve a neat, clean, peaceful setting, but illness, disability, surgery, or injury may limit a person’s ability to clean. Helping with home management promotes comfort, emotional well-being, and dignity. Treat your client’s home and belongings with respect and care.
每个人都理应生活在整洁、洁净、安详的环境中。但疾病、残障、术后恢复或创伤可能削弱个体的清扫能力。协助家政维护不仅能提升躯体舒适感与心理幸福感，更捍卫了人格尊严。务必以敬重与爱护之心对待客户的居所与私人物品。
Independence (独立自主): Respect your client’s wishes for independence and control. As they recover, some clients gradually assume responsibility for home management. Occupational therapists help some clients learn new ways to perform household tasks. For example, Mr. Ho, who had an arm amputated, is trying to make his bed using techniques he learned in occupational therapy. Encourage such independence.
高度尊重客户对自主权与生活掌控感的渴望。随着身体逐步恢复，部分客户会逐渐重新承担家政任务。作业治疗师（OT）会指导客户学习新的家务适应技巧。例如单臂截肢的何先生正尝试运用OT技巧独立铺床，支持人员应积极鼓励这一自主性。
Preferences (个性化偏好): Clients have different habits, standards of cleanliness, and routines. Ask about preferences before rearranging items, using specific cleaning agents, or changing their usual ways of doing things.
每位客户都有其独特的生活习惯、保洁标准与起居惯例。在挪动物品、使用特定清洁剂或改变其既有做事方式前，务必先征求其个人偏好。
Privacy (隐私保护): Knock before entering rooms. Keep financial documents, mail, and personal effects confidential and strictly untouched unless requested.
进入房间前务必敲门。对客户的财务单据、信件与私人物品恪守保密准则，除非客户主动明确要求，否则严禁翻看或触碰。
Safety (安全首位): Maintain clear pathways, wipe spills immediately, never leave mopped floors unattended while wet, store cleaning agents out of reach of children or persons with dementia, and use PPE.
始终保持通道畅通无杂物，打翻液体立即擦净，拖湿地面严禁无人看管，将清洁化学品妥善锁置于儿童或失智症患者接触不到之处，全程规范佩戴PPE。
p. 1037 Financial Records, PIN Privacy & Conflicting Demands

Financial Accounting & Record-Keeping Standards

财务对账与记录留痕规范
  • Documenting Transactions: All money that you handle should be recorded in the appropriate manner. Some agencies, for example, record all transactions on a page to which the support worker is required to attach receipts. When you are given money to purchase an item, you will be responsible to record how much cash was given to you and the amount of change that you are returning. Always provide receipts for any transaction that you did on your client’s behalf.
    完整账目留痕：经手的所有资金必须按规定程序登记。例如某些机构要求将所有交易登记在专用表单上并附上购物收据。当客户交给你现金购物时，你必须如实记录收到的初始现金金额以及退还的零钱差额。无论代办任何交易，务必向客户提供完整正规的商业收据。
  • PIN Confidentiality (Strict Prohibition): Remember that the client’s personal identification number (PIN) and bank account numbers are strictly private, and you should NOT have access to them. If the client has difficulty typing in the numbers, for example, a banking associate should be asked to do so. You should step far enough away that you do not see or hear the numbers.

Dealing With Conflicting Demands

妥善应对冲突性要求与边界维护

Many people have set routines and want things done in certain ways. However, because of time constraints or for health and safety reasons, it may not be possible for you to follow the set procedure. Or you may have to deal with conflicting demands if the care plan says one thing but the client (or the family) wants something different. If you are asked to do additional tasks that are not in the client’s care plan, it is important that you inform the client (or the family) in a professional, respectful, and calm manner that this task was not identified on the client’s care plan. However, without making any commitments, identify why the request was made and report this request to your supervisor.

许多人拥有固定的生活习惯，并希望事情按照其特定方式完成。然而，由于工时限制或出于健康与安全考量，支持人员可能无法完全照搬其既定流程。如果护理计划与客户（或其家属）的诉求发生分歧，你可能会面临冲突性要求。当被要求执行未包含在护理计划中的额外任务时，必须以专业、敬重且冷静的态度告知客户（或家属），该任务未被列入当前护理计划。同时，在不做任何承诺的前提下，探明客户提出该项请求的具体原因，并及时向主管汇报。

1. Clear away clutter (清除散落杂物): Clutter can cause trips, falls, and injuries. Keep hallways, stairs, and walking pathways completely unobstructed. Ask before discarding anything.

水质浑浊或抹布变脏时必须立即更换。使用脏水脏布不仅无法去污，反而会大面积播散细菌。

使用微湿抹布除尘。干抹布会使灰尘飞扬弥漫在空气中，刺激呼吸道并在片刻后重新降落。

USING CLEANING PRODUCTS SAFELY

安全使用清洁化学品的13项黄金法则

Cleaning products contain chemicals that can cause skin irritation, eye damage, respiratory problems, or poisoning if not handled properly. Follow these 13 safety rules strictly whenever using cleaning products:

家用清洁剂普遍含有化学物质，若使用不当可能导致皮肤灼伤、眼部损伤、呼吸道受损甚至急性中毒。在使用各类清洁产品时，必须严格遵守以下 13 项安全操作守则：

1. Read all labels carefully (仔细研读标签): Review directions, warnings, and first-aid instructions before use.
使用前详阅产品说明、警示标识及急救指南。
2. NEVER mix cleaning products (绝对严禁混用清洁剂): Mixing chemicals can trigger violent chemical reactions. In particular, NEVER mix chlorine bleach with ammonia or acid-based cleaners (or urine)—this combination instantly creates deadly poisonous chloramine gas that causes severe pulmonary damage or death.
化学品混用会引发剧烈毒性反应。尤为重要的是：绝对不能将含氯漂白剂与含有氨成分的清洁剂（包括尿液）或酸性清洁剂混合——两者混合会瞬间释放致命的氯胺剧毒气体，引发严重的急性肺损伤甚至死亡。
3. Wear utility gloves (佩戴家务橡胶手套): Protect skin from harsh detergents, corrosives, and pathogens.
佩戴耐用型橡胶手套，防止强效去污剂灼伤皮肤及接触病原体。
4. Never store products in unlabelled containers (严禁存放在无标签容器中): Accidental ingestion or misuse can occur.
绝对禁止将清洁剂倒入无标签容器中，以防误服或误用。
5. Store in original containers (保留原装容器存储): Keep lids tightly sealed.
始终保存在原厂包装瓶中，瓶盖拧紧密封。
6. Keep away from food (远离食品存放): Store cleaning chemicals in separate cupboards away from food items.
严禁与食品存放在同一橱柜内，杜绝交叉污染。
7. Prevent splashing (防止药液飞溅): Pour gently and keep bottles close to surfaces.
倒药液时轻缓平稳，靠近目标表面，严防药液飞溅入眼或皮肤。
8. Out of reach of children and dementia clients (远离儿童与失智者): Secure with safety latches.
存放于儿童或失智症老人触及不到的安全上锁柜中。
9. Use products only for intended purpose (严格按指定用途使用): Do not use toilet cleaners on dishes or countertops.
专剂专用，严禁用洁厕剂洗涤餐具或擦拭备餐台面。
10. Keep aerosol cans away from heat (气雾罐远离热源): Pressurized cans can explode near stoves or radiators.
高压气雾喷罐远离炉灶或暖气片，严防遇热受胀爆炸。
11. Ask permission before using strong cleansers (使用强效去污剂前征求许可): Harsh chemicals may etch stone or wood.
在贵重石材或高档家具上使用强力去污剂前必须征得客户同意。
12. Rinse off harsh cleansers (彻底漂洗残留药剂): Wipe with clean damp cloth after application.
使用强效药剂擦洗后必须用干净湿布彻底漂清，防止腐蚀或毒性残留。
13. Do not scrub vigorously (切勿粗暴暴力刮擦): Scrubbing can ruin delicate surface finishes.
切勿过度暴力刮擦，以免损坏家具或器皿表面的精细涂层。

Mould and Mildew: Health Hazards and Safe Removal / 霉菌与霉斑：健康危害与科学清除规范

Mould is a microscopic fungal parasite, and mildew is the black discoloration caused by mould that can produce a foul odour. People living in homes with mould and damp conditions are more likely to experience eye, nose, and throat irritation, coughing and phlegm build-up, wheezing, shortness of breath, and worsening of asthma symptoms.

霉菌（Mould）是一种微观真菌寄生物，而霉斑（Mildew）则是霉菌滋生所导致的黑色变色斑痕，常散发难闻霉味。长期居住在潮湿生霉环境中的人，极易出现眼部、鼻腔及咽喉刺激感、咳嗽痰多、喘鸣、气促呼吸困难以及哮喘急性加重等呼吸系统症状。

Protocol for Safe Removal of Mould / 科学清除霉菌的核心规程：
  • Dry surfaces thoroughly: Always dry surfaces well after bathing and cleaning to prevent mould and mildew growth.
    彻底擦干表面：沐浴和清洁后务必将墙面地面擦干，从源头杜绝霉菌滋生条件。

Blue Box / Blue Cart (可回收容器与纸张):
Paper, cardboard, newspapers, clean glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and recyclable plastics (coded #1 to #7, depending on municipal guidelines). Rinse food containers clean before placing in the blue box.
废纸、瓦楞纸板、旧报纸、干净玻璃瓶罐、金属易拉罐以及带有回收代码的塑料制品。食物容器在投入蓝箱前必须彻底用水冲洗干净。
Green Bin / Organics (绿箱有机易腐厨余):
Food scraps, fruit and vegetable peels, coffee grounds, tea bags, eggshells, soiled paper towels, and food-soiled pizza boxes. Store in biodegradable liner bags to control odours.
剩菜剩饭、果蔬皮核、咖啡渣、茶包、蛋壳、擦手用过的废纸巾及沾满油污的披萨盒。置于可生物降解专用内衬袋中扎紧以防异味扩散。
Hazardous & Electronic Waste (特种有害废弃物与电子垃圾):
Batteries, paint cans, fluorescent tubes, medications, and aerosol cans should NEVER go into regular household garbage. Ask your supervisor about designated community drop-off depots.
废旧电池、油漆桶、荧光灯管、过期药品及高压喷雾罐绝不可扔进普通生活垃圾桶。必须咨询主管送往市政指定的特种废品回收处理中心。

p. 1051 Tasks Not on Care Plan & Box 44.7: Stain Removal Protocols

PERFORMING TASKS NOT INCLUDED ON THE CLIENT’S CARE PLAN

执行护理计划外任务的执业风险与法律边界

You should remember that you will not be paid for performing any tasks that are not on the client’s care plan. Some employers frown on the support worker providing “free” services (i.e., services that the client would normally have to pay your agency for you to perform). Some agencies’ insurance companies will not compensate you if you are injured while performing a task that is not on your client’s care plan. You should also be aware that if you provide “free” services, the client will probably expect the same services from other support workers in the future!

你必须铭记：任何未载入客户护理计划的额外任务，你都无法获得相应的工时报酬。许多雇主明令禁止支持人员提供所谓的“免费人情服务”（即客户本应向派遣机构付费购买的服务）。更严重的是，若你在执行护理计划外的非授权任务时发生工伤事故，派遣机构的保险公司将拒绝予以赔偿。此外，若你擅自提供免费服务，客户往往会在未来对其他上门的支持人员提出同样不切实际的要求，造成严重的职业摩擦！

Consult With Your Supervisor

遇疑必报：咨询主管以裁定边界

Before performing a task that is not on the client’s care plan, it is always a good idea to consult your supervisor. Occasionally, your supervisor will say that it is “up to you,” especially if the request is not time-consuming or does not require special skills. For example, feeding the canary while you are near the cage is neither complicated nor time-consuming. Other favours, however, can be time-consuming and may put you behind schedule.

在被要求执行护理计划外的工作前，主动向主管咨询永远是明智之举。主管熟悉机构政策，有时对于耗时极短且不涉及专业技能的顺手小事（例如顺道给金丝雀鸟笼喂食）会授权你自行酌情决定。但若属于耗时冗长或有安全隐患的重任，则坚决不可答应，否则会导致延误下一位客户的既定行程。

BOX 44.7 · CLINICAL GUIDELINES

Removing Stains / 常见体液与顽固污渍科学去除指南

The directions in this box apply to both white and colour-fast clothes and linens. If possible, treat stains immediately, before they become set in the fabric.
本指南适用于白色与不褪色织物。一旦发现污渍必须立即处置，避免污渍深层渗入纤维干涸固化。

Urine Stains (尿液污渍处理规范):
• Rinse in cold water.
• Soak in a solution of 1 L of warm water, 2 mL liquid dishwashing detergent, and 15 mL of ammonia for 30 minutes.
• Rinse in cool water.
• Soak in a solution of 1 L warm water and 15 mL of vinegar for 1 hour.
• Machine-wash with chlorine bleach and detergent; dry as usual.
① 首先用冷水彻底冲洗；② 在1升温水加入2毫升洗洁精和15毫升氨水的溶液中浸泡30分钟；③ 冷水漂清；④ 在1升温水加15毫升白醋的溶液中浸泡1小时除味除碱；⑤ 机洗加入漂白剂与常规洗涤剂洗涤。
Blood Stains (血液污渍处理规范):
• Rinse in cold water immediately. (Hot water causes protein to coagulate into the fabric, making the stain permanent!).
• Rub with a paste of powdered detergent and water or 5 mL of liquid commercial stain remover, 2 mL dishwashing detergent, or bar of soap. Let sit for 15 minutes.
• Soak for another 15 minutes in 1 L of cold water with detergent.
• Wash in machine using chlorine bleach and detergent; dry as usual.
① 立即用冷水冲洗（绝对严禁用热水！热水会导致血红蛋白凝固变性永久锁定在纤维中！）；② 抹上洗衣粉与水调和的糊状物、或5毫升专用去渍液加2毫升洗洁精，静置15分钟；③ 在1升加有去渍剂的冷水中浸泡15分钟；④ 机洗并加漂白剂彻底洗净。
Fecal Stains (粪便污渍处理规范):
• Rinse in cold water.
• Soak in a solution of 1 L warm water, 2 mL liquid dishwashing detergent, and 15 mL ammonia for 30 minutes.
• Wash in machine using chlorine bleach and detergent; dry as usual.
① 先用冷水将粪便残渣冲洗干净；② 在1升温水加入2毫升洗洁精和15毫升氨水的溶液中浸泡30分钟；③ 随后投入洗衣机，加漂白剂与洗衣液进行高温消毒机洗。
p. 1052 Scope of Practice Boundaries & Case Conclusion

Scope of Practice Boundaries (Tasks Strictly Beyond PSW Scope)

执业范围严格硬边界：严禁越界医疗侵入性操作

Some “favours” asked by clients or family members may be beyond your scope of practice, and you must NEVER be persuaded to perform them. For example, if Mrs. Patel’s arthritis is acting up and she cannot handle her insulin syringe, and she asks you to administer her insulin injection, this is a dangerous medical procedure strictly outside your scope of practice. In this situation, call your supervisor immediately. There is considerable risk of severe harm or death from medication dosage errors. Your supervisor will arrange for a registered nurse to visit the home.

客户或家属提出的某些“请托”，可能完全超出了你的执业范畴（Scope of practice），你绝对不可被游说破例执行。例如，帕特尔太太由于关节炎发作手指僵硬无法握住胰岛素注射笔，恳请你代为注射胰岛素。这种侵入性药物给药属于严禁支持人员擅自执行的高危医疗操作。面对此种情境，必须立即致电主管汇报。胰岛素剂量差错可能导致严重的低血糖休克甚至死亡。主管会立即调派注册护士上门安全处置。

CHAPTER REVIEW · 本章总结回顾

KEY POINTS / 核心执业要点

  • Scope of Home Management: The support worker assists in light home management tasks (straightening, vacuuming, dusting, washing dishes, making beds, laundry). Support workers are never assigned heavy chores like washing windows or cleaning carpets.
    支持人员仅协助轻度家政任务（整理、吸尘、擦拭除尘、洗碗、铺床、洗衣）。绝对不承担清洗外窗或深度洗地毯等重体力劳动。
  • Cytotoxic Safety Precautions: Cytotoxic drugs pose health hazards for up to 7 days after discontinuation. Handle bodily fluids with double vinyl/nitrile gloves, protective gown, and face shield. Wash contaminated linens separately twice in hot water.
    细胞毒性药物停药后7天内仍具有潜在健康危害。接触体液必须佩戴双层手套、隔离衣与防护面罩。受污染织物需在热水中连续机洗两次。
  • 13 Cleaning Product Rules: Always read labels, never mix chemicals (especially bleach and ammonia/urine to prevent deadly chloramine gas), wear utility gloves, store in original containers away from children, dementia clients, and food.
    13项清洁剂安全守则：详读标签、绝对严禁混用化学品（尤其是含氯漂白剂与氨水/尿液混合会产生剧毒氯胺气体）、戴橡胶手套、原装密封存放、远离食品与儿童/失智老人。
  • Cleaning Guidelines: Clear clutter, work higher to lower, far to near (toward exit), dry to wet, cleanest to dirtiest, change cloths/water frequently, use damp cloth for dusting, and rinse/dry surfaces.
    保洁九项准则：清理散物、由高至低、由远及近退向房门、先干后湿、由净至污、勤换抹布与水、微湿布除尘、彻底漂洗擦干。
  • Mould Abatement: Dry all surfaces well. Clean small surface mould with water and dish detergent. Do not use bleach to clean mould. Extensive mould requires certified specialists.
    防霉除霉：表面彻底擦干。小面积霉斑用温水配洗洁精擦除。严禁用漂白剂除霉。严重霉变由持证专家处置。
  • Stain Removal: Always rinse blood, urine, and fecal stains in cold water first to prevent heat from setting proteins into fabric fibers.
    去渍铁律：血液、尿液与粪渍必须首先用冷水彻底冲洗，防止热力将蛋白质固化在纤维中。

Clinical Decision-Making / 复杂居家环境中的安全决策

Scenario: Working for a home care agency, you have been assigned to assist with bathing and dressing for Mrs. Soto, a new client. She was recently sent home from a rehabilitation hospital after falling and breaking her hip. You notice that Mrs. Soto’s family did not obtain the shower chair that was recommended. You do not know how to safely get her in and out of the tub without this chair, and she does not seem very steady on her feet. What should you do? What are your responsibilities in this situation?

临床情境：你作为居家机构的支持人员，被派往新客户索托太太家协助沐浴与更衣。她刚因摔倒导致髋部骨折从康复医院出院回家。你敏锐地注意到，索托太太的家属并未依出院医嘱购置推荐的专用淋浴防滑椅（Shower chair）。在缺乏淋浴椅的情况下，你无法安全协助她进出浴缸，而且老人站立步态极不稳定。面对这一情境，你应当采取何种行动？你的执业安全职责是什么？

Clinical Decision: Do NOT attempt to transfer Mrs. Soto into the bathtub without the necessary assistive equipment, as this presents an extreme fall and re-fracture hazard. Offer her a safe, soothing sponge/towel bed bath instead today to maintain hygiene and comfort. Immediately notify your supervisor regarding the missing shower chair so the case manager or occupational therapist can arrange urgent equipment delivery.

临床决策应对：在缺乏必备辅助用具的情况下，绝不可贸然强行协助索托太太跨入浴缸，否则极易发生二次跌倒与再次骨折惨剧。今日应向老人解释安全风险，改为在床上或平稳座椅上提供温水毛巾擦浴（Sponge bath），确保身体清洁与舒适。随后立即致电主管汇报淋浴椅缺失情况，由主管协同个案经理或作业治疗师（OT）紧急调配器材到位。
