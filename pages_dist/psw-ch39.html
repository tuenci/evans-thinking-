Oxygen and the Human Body
氧气与人体生命活动

Oxygen (O₂) is a gas that is part of the air we breathe. At room temperature, it has no taste, odour, or colour. Oxygen is the most important basic need for life, and without it, death will occur within minutes. Oxygen enters the body as part of the air that we breathe and enters our body through an airway. This is the pathway that air takes as it enters the body and goes into the lungs. An airway can be our nose, mouth, or through a breathing tube or tracheostomy.
氧气（O₂）是我们呼吸的空气的组成部分。在常温常压下，氧气无色、无嗅、无味。氧是维持生命最关键的基础生理需求；一旦缺氧，细胞坏死与生物学死亡将在数分钟之内发生。氧气随吸入的空气进入体内，其必经通道被称为气道（Airway）。气道是指气体进入体内并下行至肺泡的解剖途径，既包括我们天然的鼻、咽、喉及气管，也包括人工插入的呼吸导管或气管切开造口。

Airway
/ˈeəweɪ/ p.928 		The pathway that air takes as it enters the body and goes into the lungs. 气道：空气进入人体并到达肺部所经过的解剖通道（包括鼻、咽、喉、气管、支气管或人工气道）。
Apnea
/æpˈniːə/ p.932 		The lack or absence (a) of breathing (pnea). 呼吸暂停：呼吸运动的完全缺失或停滞（a-表示无，-pnea表示呼吸）。
Atelectasis
/ˌæt.əˈlek.tə.sɪs/ p.935 		When a portion of the lung collapses after mucus collects in a section of the airway, preventing air from entering that part of the lung. 肺不张：黏液痰栓积聚阻塞部分气道，阻碍气体进入，导致受累肺段或肺叶塌陷萎陷的病理状态。
Bradypnea
/ˌbræd.ɪpˈniːə/ p.932 		Slow (brady) breathing (pnea); respirations are fewer than 12 per minute. 呼吸过缓：呼吸频率异常缓慢（brady-表示缓慢），成人静息呼吸次数少于 12 次/分。
Chest percussion
/tʃest pəˈkʌʃ.ən/ p.946 		A technique that loosens thick, sticky mucus that is trapped in the lungs so it can move into the larger airways. It is done by holding the hands in a cupped position and clapping the client’s chest in a forceful but steady beat, moving from the distal areas of the chest toward the main bronchus (also known as chest clapping). 胸部叩击：手呈杯状弓起，有节奏且平稳地叩击患者胸壁，自肺外周向主支气管方向推进，促使肺深部黏稠痰液松动脱落并移向大支气管的胸部物理治疗技术（又称扣背/拍胸）。
Chest tube
/tʃest tjuːb/ p.954 		A hollow plastic tube surgically inserted into the chest cavity, which allows for the removal of trapped air (pneumothorax) and the drainage of blood or fluid (hemothorax or pleural effusion). 胸腔引流管：通过外科手术置入胸膜腔的医用软管，用于排出积聚的气体（气胸）或引流积血、积液（血胸或胸腔积液），以重建胸腔负压并使萎陷的肺复张。
Cheyne–Stokes
/ˈtʃeɪn ˈstoʊks/ p.932 		Respirations that gradually increase in rate and depth and then become shallow and slow; breathing may stop (apnea) for 10 to 20 seconds. 潮式呼吸（陈-施呼吸）：一种呼吸节律异常，呼吸由浅慢逐渐变为深快，达到顶峰后又由深快变浅慢，随后出现 10 至 20 秒的呼吸暂停，周而复始；多见于严重中枢神经损伤、心衰竭或临终状态。
Cyanosis
/ˌsaɪ.əˈnoʊ.sɪs/ p.933 		An abnormal condition, evidenced by bluish colour in the skin, lips, mucous membranes, and nail beds, caused by a lack of oxygenated blood in the visible tissues. 发绀（紫绀）：由于血液中还原血红蛋白绝对含量增多，导致患者皮肤、口唇黏膜及甲床呈现青紫色的缺氧体征。
Distilled water
/dɪˈstɪld ˈwɔː.tər/ p.943 		Sterile and pure water that has been boiled and allowed to cool and condense. 蒸馏水：经煮沸汽化并冷凝收集的无菌纯净水，常用于氧气湿化瓶，防止水垢沉积与气道刺激。
Eupnea
/juːpˈniːə/ p.932 		Normal breathing. 正常呼吸：成人平静状态下平稳、规律、省力的生理性呼吸运动（通常为 12–20 次/分）。
Face mask
/feɪs mæsk/ p.942 		A mask used to deliver oxygen that covers the client’s nose and mouth. 面罩：同时罩住患者口部与鼻部以输送中高浓度氧气的给氧器具。
Flowmeter
/ˈfloʊˌmiː.tər/ p.940 		A device that regulates the flow of oxygen to the nasal cannula or face mask. 氧气流量计：用于精确调节并显示输送给鼻导管或面罩的氧气流速（升/分钟，L/min）的减压调节仪表。
Flow rate
/floʊ reɪt/ p.942 		The volume of a substance, such as oxygen or fluids, administered over a prescribed period. 流速（给氧流量）：在规定时间内给予患者物质（如氧气或输液）的容积速率（氧疗中以 L/min 计量）。
Hemoptysis
/hɪˈmɒp.tɪ.sɪs/ p.931 		Bloody (hemo) sputum (ptysis, meaning “to spit”). 咯血：由气管、支气管或肺实质出血，随咳嗽咳出带血的痰液或整口纯鲜血（hemo-表示血，-ptysis表示吐出）。
Humidified oxygen
/ˈhjuː.mɪ.dɪ.faɪd ˈɒk.sɪ.dʒən/ p.943 		Oxygen that contains water vapour. 湿化氧气：通过蒸馏水湿化瓶增加水分蒸气的氧气，能预防高流量干燥氧气对呼吸道黏膜的脱水与损伤。
Hyperventilation
/ˌhaɪ.pəˌven.tɪˈleɪ.ʃən/ p.932 		Respirations that are more rapid (hyper) and deeper than normal. 换气过度（过度通气）：呼吸频率过快且深度显著增加，导致体内二氧化碳排出过多、引发呼吸性碱中毒。
Hypoventilation
/ˌhaɪ.poʊˌven.tɪˈleɪ.ʃən/ p.932 		Respirations that are slow (hypo), shallow, and sometimes irregular. 换气不足（低通气）：呼吸过慢（hypo-表示低下）、表浅且节律不齐，导致肺泡气体交换不足及体内二氧化碳潴留。
Hypoxia
/haɪˈpɒk.si.ə/ p.932 		A deficiency (hypo) of oxygen in the cells (oxia). 缺氧：机体组织细胞层面氧气供应不足或利用障碍的病理生理状态（hypo-表示不足，-oxia表示氧）。
Intubation
/ˌɪn.tjuːˈbeɪ.ʃən/ p.947 		The process of inserting an artificial airway. 气管插管术：将特制的人工气道导管经口或经鼻置入患者气管内的急救建立气道过程。
Kussmaul respiration
/ˈkʊs.maʊl ˌres.pɪˈreɪ.ʃən/ p.932 		Deep, rapid respiration characteristic of diabetic acidosis or other conditions causing acidosis. 库斯莫尔呼吸（酸中毒大呼吸）：一种深而快、呼吸音粗大的特征性呼吸形式，是机体代偿严重代谢性酸中毒（如糖尿病酮症酸中毒、尿毒症）的典型表现。
Mechanical ventilation
/mɪˈkæn.ɪ.kəl ˌven.tɪˈleɪ.ʃən/ p.953 		The use of a ventilator to move air into and out of the lungs. 机械通气：借助呼吸机机械辅助或完全控制气体进出肺部、维持肺泡通气与氧合的生命支持技术。
Nasal cannula
/ˈneɪ.zəl ˈkæn.jə.lə/ p.940 		A flexible tube inserted into the nares of the nose to deliver oxygen. 鼻导管：带有两个弯曲小尖端深入两侧鼻孔的柔韧塑料导管，是最常用的低流量给氧装置，允许患者自由说话与进食。
Orthopnea
/ˌɔː.θɒpˈniːə/ p.932 		Shortness of breath (dyspnea) when lying in a flat or supine position; ability to breathe comfortably (pnea) only when sitting or standing (ortho). 端坐呼吸：患者平卧时感到极度呼吸困难，被迫坐起或站立（ortho-表示直立）以减轻呼吸窘迫的临床症状（常由严重心衰或肺气肿引起）。
Orthopneic position
/ˌɔː.θɒpˈniː.ɪk pəˈzɪʃ.ən/ p.933 		Sitting up (ortho) and leaning over a table to breathe. 端坐卧位（端坐前倾位）：患者身体前倾、伏在床头过床桌（通常垫有枕头）上的特殊体位，利用重力下沉膈肌、最大限度扩张胸腔容积。
Oxygen-conserving devices
/ˈɒk.sɪ.dʒən kənˈsɜː.vɪŋ dɪˈvaɪ.sɪz/ p.939 		Devices that help reduce oxygen wastage. 节氧装置：仅在患者吸气瞬间触发输送氧气、呼气期间停止出气，以显著延长便携储氧设备使用时间的装置。
Pollutant
/pəˈluː.tənt/ p.931 		A harmful chemical or substance in air or water. 污染物：存在于空气或水体中对呼吸系统及人体健康产生刺激、破坏或中毒反应的有害化学物质或颗粒。
Respiratory arrest
/rɪˈspɪr.ə.tər.i əˈrest/ p.930 		Stoppage of breathing. 呼吸骤停：自主呼吸运动完全停止的极危重临床急症，若不立即进行人工通气可在数分钟内导致心跳骤停与脑死亡。
Respiratory depression
/rɪˈspɪr.ə.tər.i dɪˈpreʃ.ən/ p.930 		Slow, weak respirations at a rate of fewer than 12 per minute; respirations are not deep enough to bring enough air into the lungs. 呼吸抑制：呼吸中枢机能受到抑制导致呼吸异常缓慢（<12次/分）、微弱且表浅，无法满足肺部气体交换的基本通气需求（常见于麻醉药、麻醉性镇痛药过量）。
Respiratory therapist
/rɪˈspɪr.ə.tər.i ˈθer.ə.pɪst/ p.929 		A regulated professional who evaluates, treats, and maintains heart and lung function and helps the client to keep breathing. 呼吸治疗师（RT）：专门评估、治疗、维护患者心肺功能，调试呼吸机及气道管理设备的加拿大法定注册医疗卫生专业人员。
SOB
/ˌes.oʊˈbiː/ p.931 		Acronym for “short of breath.” 气促/呼吸短促（SOB）：临床常用缩写，代表“Short of breath”。
SOBOE
/ˌes.oʊ.biː.oʊˈiː/ p.931 		Acronym for “short of breath on exertion.” 劳力性气促（SOBOE）：临床常用缩写，代表“Short of breath on exertion”，指身体活动或用力时加剧的呼吸困难。
Sputum
/ˈspjuː.təm/ p.946 		Mucus from the respiratory system that is expectorated (expelled) through the mouth. 痰液：呼吸道（支气管与肺）黏膜分泌并经口腔咳出的病理黏液分泌物。
Suctioning
/ˈsʌk.ʃən.ɪŋ/ p.952 		The process of withdrawing or sucking up fluid (secretions). 吸痰术：利用负压吸引器与特制吸痰导管，抽吸清除气道内分泌物、痰液、呕吐物或异物的临床操作。
Tachypnea
/ˌtæk.ɪpˈniːə/ p.932 		Rapid (tachy) breathing (pnea); respirations are 24 or more per minute. 呼吸过速：呼吸频率异常增快（tachy-表示快速），成人静息呼吸次数达到或超过 24 次/分。
Tracheostomy
/ˌtreɪ.kiˈɒs.tə.mi/ p.949 		A surgically created opening (ostomy) through the neck into the trachea (tracheo). 气管切开造口术：经颈部外科切开颈段气管前壁并置入导管，用于解除上呼吸道梗阻或便于长期机械通气的人工通道。
Ventilator
/ˈven.tɪ.leɪ.tər/ p.953 		A machine used to provide airflow into and out of the lungs. 呼吸机：能够产生生理性气流驱动气体进出肺部、提供氧气并排出二氧化碳的机械设备。

Factors Affecting Oxygen Needs
影响人体氧气需求的各项因素

To survive, our cells must have an adequate supply of oxygen. Many factors can affect how much oxygen the body requires and how well the respiratory system functions. These factors include:
为了维持生存，人体组织细胞必须获得持续充足的氧气供应。多种生理、病理、环境与行为因素会深刻影响机体的需氧量及呼吸系统的摄氧效率。这些主要因素包括：
1. Respiratory system function: The airway must be open (patent) and clear of secretions, swelling, or foreign bodies. The lungs must be elastic and able to expand and contract. Any disease that narrows the airway (such as asthma or COPD) or damages lung tissue reduces oxygen intake.
1. 呼吸系统自身机能：气道必须保持通畅（Patent），无黏稠分泌物积聚、黏膜水肿或异物阻塞。肺组织必须具备良好的弹性回缩与舒张扩张能力。任何引起气道狭窄（如哮喘、慢性阻塞性肺疾病）或破坏肺泡实质的疾患都会急剧削弱机体的摄氧能力。
2. Cardiovascular system function: The heart pumps blood through the lungs, where red blood cells pick up oxygen and transport it to the body cells. Heart failure or impaired circulation prevents oxygen from reaching tissues even if the lungs are healthy.
2. 心血管循环机能：心脏如同水泵，将脱氧静脉血泵入肺毛细血管网，使红细胞摄取氧气并输送至全身组织。心力衰竭、低血压休克或外周循环障碍会导致组织灌注不足，即便是肺脏功能完全正常，细胞依然会发生严重缺氧。
3. Red blood cell count: Red blood cells contain hemoglobin, which binds to and carries oxygen. Clients with anemia or severe blood loss do not have enough hemoglobin to transport sufficient oxygen, leading to fatigue and breathlessness.
3. 红细胞数量与血红蛋白水平：红细胞富含血红蛋白（Hemoglobin），具有特异性结合并转运氧分子的功能。贫血患者或大出血伤员因红细胞及血红蛋白绝对量匮乏，血液携氧能力锐减，轻微活动即可出现极度疲乏与气促。
4. Nervous system function: The brainstem (medulla and pons) controls the rate and depth of respiration. Brain injury, stroke, spinal cord injury, or narcotics can cause respiratory depression (slow, weak breathing) or respiratory arrest (breathing stops completely).
4. 神经系统调节控制：脑干（延髓与脑桥）是调控呼吸节律与深浅度的生命中枢。脑外伤、中风卒中、颈髓高位损伤或过量中枢麻醉剂均可诱发呼吸抑制（呼吸变慢弱）甚至直接导致呼吸骤停。
5. Aging: As people age, lung tissue loses elasticity, chest wall muscles become weaker, and the cough reflex diminishes. Older adults are at higher risk for respiratory infections and hypoxia.
5. 老龄化退行性改变：随着年龄增长，老年人肺泡弹性纤维回缩力下降，胸廓骨骼与呼吸肌力量减退，气道黏膜纤毛清除能力和咳嗽反射减弱，导致老年群体呼吸道感染及缺氧的易感性显著上升。
6. Exercise, Fever, and Pain: Physical exertion and fever increase cellular metabolic rate, sharply raising oxygen demands. Severe pain often causes rapid, shallow breathing that prevents full lung aeration.
6. 剧烈运动、高热与疼痛：体力活动与体温升高会显著加速细胞基础代谢，使机体耗氧量呈倍数剧增。急性剧痛常迫使患者采用浅快呼吸代偿，阻碍肺底部肺泡的充分复张。
7. Smoking and Pollutants: Smoking damages cilia in the airway and destroys lung tissue, being the leading cause of chronic bronchitis, emphysema, and lung cancer. Environmental pollutants (dust, toxic chemicals, mold) also impair airway patency.
7. 吸烟与环境污染物暴露：吸烟会直接麻痹并摧毁气道表面纤毛，引起慢性炎症并破坏肺泡壁，是慢性支气管炎、肺气肿及肺癌的首要元凶。空气污染物（粉尘、工业烟尘、刺激性化学气体、霉菌孢子）同样严重损害呼吸道黏膜防御机制。

Altered Respiratory Function and Hypoxia
呼吸功能改变与机体缺氧

Normal adult breathing is quiet, effortless, and regular, with a rate of 12 to 20 respirations per minute. When illness or injury interferes with oxygen intake or transport, the client develops altered respiratory function. The most serious consequence of altered respiratory function is hypoxia—a deficiency of oxygen in the cells. Without oxygen, brain cells begin to die within 4 to 6 minutes, and other body organs suffer irreversible damage.
健康成年人在静息状态下的正常呼吸是平静、省力且节律规则的，呼吸频率通常在每分钟 12 至 20 次。当疾病或创伤阻碍了氧气的吸入或组织输送时，患者便会出现呼吸功能改变。呼吸功能受损最严重的危险后果是缺氧（Hypoxia）——即组织细胞层面的氧分子供应匮乏。如果完全断绝供氧，大脑神经元细胞在 4 至 6 分钟内便会发生不可逆死亡，其他重要生命脏器也将相继衰竭。
Early Signs of Hypoxia: The brain is the first organ to show signs of oxygen lack. The client becomes restless, irritable, anxious, and confused. Pulse rate and respiratory rate increase as the body attempts to compensate. In addition, the client may complain of headache or dizziness.
缺氧的早期预警体征：脑组织对缺氧最为敏锐脆弱。患者最早表现出烦躁不安（Restlessness）、情绪易怒、焦虑恐惧与意识混乱或注意力涣散。机体为了代偿缺氧，脉搏心率与呼吸频率会代偿性增快。此外，患者常诉头痛或头晕眼花。
Late Signs of Hypoxia: If hypoxia continues, the client becomes lethargic and unresponsive. The pulse rate slows, and respirations become slow and irregular. Cyanosis—a bluish discoloration of the skin, lips, mucous membranes, and nail beds—appears as deoxygenated blood accumulates. Cyanosis is a late and life-threatening sign! Another critical sign is hemoptysis (bloody sputum), which indicates bleeding within the lungs.
缺氧的晚期危险体征：若缺氧持续恶化未获纠正，患者将转入嗜睡、昏迷乃至无反应状态。心率转为心动过缓，呼吸变得微弱、缓慢且节律严重紊乱。随着还原血红蛋白在微循环中堆积，患者皮肤、口唇黏膜及甲床呈现发青青紫，即发绀（Cyanosis）。发绀属于晚期濒死预警体征！另一严重征象是咯血（Hemoptysis）（咳出带血痰液或纯血），提示肺泡或气道血管发生破裂出血。

Abnormal Respiratory Patterns
临床异常呼吸形态与波形解析

Struggling to breathe is both exhausting and dangerous. Breathing patterns can change depending on underlying diseases, metabolic states, or medications. Common abnormal respiratory patterns (FIGURE 39.2) include:
呼吸受阻竭力挣扎既耗尽患者体力，又具有致命危险。呼吸形态可因不同病因、机体代谢紊乱或药物作用而呈现特征性改变。常见的异常呼吸形态（图 39.2）包括：
l Tachypnea: Breathing faster than 24 breaths per minute. Causes include fever, exercise, asthma, hypoxemia, and anxiety.
l 呼吸过速（Tachypnea）：成人静息呼吸频率超过 24 次/分。常见于高热、剧烈活动、支气管哮喘发作、急性低氧血症及焦虑惊恐状态。
l Bradypnea: Breathing slower than 12 breaths per minute. Commonly caused by narcotics, alcohol overdose, brain injury, or hypothermia.
l 呼吸过缓（Bradypnea）：成人静息呼吸频率少于 12 次/分。常见于阿片类药物中毒、酒精过量、颅脑创伤及严重低体温。
l Apnea: The lack or absence of breathing. If apnea lasts longer than 4 to 6 minutes, cardiac arrest and biological death follow.
l 呼吸暂停（Apnea）：呼吸运动完全消失。若呼吸停滞持续超过 4 至 6 分钟，将迅速诱发心跳骤停与不可逆生物学死亡。
l Hypoventilation: Slow, shallow, and sometimes irregular respirations. The volume of air entering the lungs is inadequate, leading to carbon dioxide buildup.
l 低通气（换气不足，Hypoventilation）：呼吸浅慢且常不规则，进入肺泡的气体量严重不足，导致体内二氧化碳潴留与呼吸性酸中毒。
l Hyperventilation: Respirations that are more rapid and deeper than normal. It causes excessive carbon dioxide excretion, often triggered by severe anxiety or pain.
l 过度通气（换气过度，Hyperventilation）：呼吸频率过快且幅度显著加深，导致体内二氧化碳过度排出，多见于剧烈恐惧焦虑发作或急性重度疼痛。
l Cheyne–Stokes respirations: Respirations that gradually increase in rate and depth, then become shallow and slow, followed by an apnea period lasting 10 to 20 seconds. It often signals end-of-life or severe cerebral injury.
l 潮式呼吸（陈-施呼吸，Cheyne–Stokes）：呼吸节律呈波浪式变化，由浅慢逐渐变为深快，达到高峰后又由深快转为浅慢，随后出现持续 10 至 20 秒的呼吸暂停，周而复始。常见于中枢神经重创、严重心衰及濒死临终期。
l Kussmaul respirations: Deep, rapid, gasping respirations characteristic of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) or kidney failure, as the body attempts to blow off acid.
l 库斯莫尔呼吸（酸中毒大呼吸，Kussmaul）：深大、快速而节律整齐的代偿性深吸深呼大呼吸，是机体试图加速排出碳酸以代偿严重代谢性酸中毒（如糖尿病酮症酸中毒、尿毒症）的标志性呼吸。

Promoting Oxygenation and Positioning
促进氧合与治疗性体位管理

Promoting oxygenation is a key nursing goal. Several simple measures can help clients breathe more easily and expand their lung capacity:
改善并促进肺部氧合是临床照护的核心目标。采取多项针对性的支持干预能够显著减轻患者呼吸阻力并提高肺泡换气容积：
1. Positioning: Breathing is easier in semi-Fowler's and Fowler's positions. In these positions, gravity pulls the abdominal organs down, giving the lungs more room to expand. Clients with severe breathing difficulties often prefer the orthopneic position (FIGURE 39.3). The client sits up in bed or in a chair and leans forward over an overbed table that is padded with pillows. Leaning forward allows maximum chest expansion and eases diaphragm movement.
1. 舒适体位摆放：患者在半坐卧位（Semi-Fowler's）或高坐卧位（Fowler's）时呼吸明显较为省力。重力作用使腹腔内脏器自然下沉，减轻对膈肌的向上挤压，为肺底膨胀提供充裕空间。重度呼吸窘迫患者最常自发采取端坐前倾位（Orthopneic position，图 39.3）。患者在床上或扶手椅中端坐，身体前倾伏在垫有柔软枕头的床头跨床桌上。这种姿势能使胸腔达到最大程度的舒展，并借助胸肌与双臂支撑辅助呼吸肌群做功。

2. Frequent Position Changes: Clients who stay in one position for long periods allow respiratory secretions to pool in the dependent portions of the lungs. Pooling increases the risk of lung infections and atelectasis (collapse of a portion of the lung). Help clients reposition at least every 2 hours, or as directed by the care plan.
2. 定期协助变换体位：长期保持单一卧位的卧床患者，其呼吸道分泌物会在重力作用下在肺下垂部位滞留淤积。积痰不仅显著增加肺部细菌感染概率，还会直接导致肺不张（Atelectasis）（部分肺段因小气道阻塞而塌陷闭塞）。支持人员应至少每 2 小时协助患者翻身变换体位一次，或严格依照护理计划执行。

Deep Breathing, Coughing, and Spirometry
深呼吸锻炼、有效咳嗽与诱发性肺活量计

Deep breathing and coughing exercises help expand the lungs and clear secretions from the airway. They are commonly ordered for clients on bed rest, after surgery, or with respiratory disorders. Deep breathing moves air into the lower parts of the lungs, opening collapsed alveoli. Coughing pushes secretions up into the trachea and throat, where they can be expelled.
深呼吸与咳嗽锻炼有助于充分复张肺部肺泡并清除气道深部分泌物。这项练习通常广泛应用于术后恢复期患者、绝对卧床患者或呼吸系统疾患患者。深度吸气使新鲜空气深达肺底与肺外周，促使萎陷闭合的肺泡重新膨胀；而爆发性咳嗽动作则将分泌物推举至大气管与喉部，使其易于咳出体外。
Pursed-Lip Breathing: Clients with chronic lung diseases (such as COPD) are often taught pursed-lip breathing. The client inhales slowly through the nose and exhales slowly through pursed lips (as if whistling). Pursed-lip breathing creates back-pressure inside the airways, keeping small bronchioles open longer during exhalation so that trapped air can escape.
缩唇呼气法（Pursed-Lip Breathing）：患有慢性阻塞性肺疾病（COPD）的患者通常需接受缩唇呼气训练。患者经鼻孔平稳缓慢深吸气，随后将双唇噘起呈吹口哨状，缓慢将气体吹出。缩唇产生的气流阻力在气道内部形成轻度正压，防止细支气管在呼气末期过早塌陷闭合，从而有效排出滞留在肺泡内的残余气体。
Incentive Spirometry: An incentive spirometer is a handheld device that encourages slow, sustained deep breaths. The client inhales through a mouthpiece, creating suction that pulls a piston or ball upward inside a transparent chamber. Visual markings show the volume of air inhaled, providing positive reinforcement. The client is instructed to hold their breath for 3 to 5 seconds at peak inhalation before exhaling, ensuring full alveolar expansion.
诱发性肺活量计（Incentive Spirometry）：诱发性肺活量计是一种手持式视觉反馈呼吸训练仪，专门鼓励患者进行缓慢、持久的深度吸气。患者含住咬嘴深吸气，产生的负压吸力将透明腔体内的活塞或彩球托举升起。腔体外壁的刻度数值实时显示吸入的肺容积，给予患者直观的达标激励。练习要求患者在吸气至顶点后屏气 3 至 5 秒再缓慢呼出，以保证全肺肺泡的彻底复张。
Splinting an Incision: Coughing can cause sharp pain for clients who have had abdominal or chest surgery. To decrease pain, the incision is "splinted" by holding a pillow or the client's interlaced hands firmly against the wound during coughing. Splinting supports the surgical site and prevents strain on sutures.
切口支托保护（切口按压保护法，Splinting）：进行强力咳嗽排痰时，胸腹腔内压骤增，会对胸腹部手术切口造成剧烈牵拉剧痛。为了减轻疼痛，在患者咳嗽时应进行“切口支托”，即由患者或护理人员双手交叉按压切口，或用柔软的枕头紧紧抱压在伤口敷料外侧。支托保护能有效分散切口张力，避免缝线崩开并显著减轻创伤性疼痛。

Assisting with Oxygen Therapy Overview
协助氧气疗法概述与执业职责

Oxygen is treated as a medication. Because it can cause serious complications if administered incorrectly, oxygen therapy is strictly controlled. In Canada, oxygen must be prescribed by a physician or nurse practitioner. The prescription specifies: (1) the oxygen flow rate (in litres per minute, L/min) or percentage, (2) the delivery device (such as nasal cannula or face mask), and (3) when oxygen is to be given (continuous or PRN for SOB).
在临床医学与法律层面，氧气被严格视为处方药物。若给氧不当（如高浓度给氧导致慢阻肺患者呼吸中枢抑制），会引发严重的医源性并发症。在加拿大，氧气疗法必须由注册执业医师或执业护士开具明确医嘱。给氧处方必须详述：① 给氧流速（升/分钟，L/min）或氧浓度百分比；② 采用的给氧装置类型（如鼻导管、面罩）；③ 具体的治疗时段（持续吸氧还是仅在气促发作时按需吸氧）。
The Support Worker's Role and Safety Boundaries: As a support worker, you assist clients receiving oxygen, but your role is clearly defined:
  • YOU DO NOT start, stop, or adjust oxygen flow rates unless specifically trained, permitted by provincial legislation, and delegated under direct supervision.
  • YOU DO NOT decide what device or flow rate the client needs.
  • YOU DO ensure that tubing is secure, connected, and free of kinks or water traps.
  • YOU DO check that the nasal prongs are properly inserted into the nares pointing downward.
  • YOU DO provide frequent skin care behind the ears and around the nose to prevent pressure sores.
  • YOU DO immediately report any respiratory distress, cyanosis, change in behaviour, or equipment malfunction to your supervisor.
支持工作人员的职责定位与安全红线：作为支持工作人员，您负责协助照护吸氧患者，但您的操作权限有着严苛的法律与执业边界：
  • 绝对禁止擅自开启、切断或调节氧气流量计，除非属于省区法规特许、经过正规培训考核并在注册护士明确委托督导下的特别授权任务。
  • 绝对禁止自行决定更换给氧装置类型或调整给氧速度。
  • 必须做到确保氧气连接导管稳固、接头紧密，无压折、扭曲或积水阻塞。
  • 必须做到核查双孔鼻导管呈弯曲向下弧度正确置入两侧鼻孔。
  • 必须做到经常检查并清洁患者耳后及鼻翼周围受压皮肤，预防压力性损伤（压疮）。
  • 必须做到一旦发现患者出现呼吸窘迫、发绀、烦躁嗜睡或设备报警异常，必须立即向主管护士汇报。

⚠️ BOX 39.4: Oxygen Fire Hazards / 氧气助燃与防火严苛警示

Oxygen supports combustion. It makes things catch fire more easily and burn much faster and hotter!
  • NO SMOKING: Post "OXYGEN IN USE — NO SMOKING" warning signs on the door and near the bed. Ensure no matches, lighters, or open flames are anywhere near the client.
  • NO ELECTRICAL SPARKS: Avoid electrical equipment with frayed cords. Do not use electric razors, heating pads, or hair dryers near oxygen.
  • NO STATIC ELECTRICITY: Use 100% cotton blankets and clothing instead of wool or synthetic fibres that generate static sparks.
  • NO OIL-BASED PRODUCTS: Never apply petroleum jelly (Vaseline) or oil-based lip balms near the nose or face; petroleum is flammable in oxygen-enriched environments. Use water-soluble lubricants only!
氧气是极强的强氧化助燃剂，能使可燃物瞬间爆燃并产生致命高温！严禁室内吸烟与明火；禁止使用破损电器与可能产生电火花的电动剃须刀；避免化纤与羊毛织物摩擦静电；严禁使用凡士林等矿物油润唇膏（遇纯氧可自燃），仅可使用水溶性润滑凝胶！

Oxygen Supply Sources
临床与居家常用氧气供应用源

Oxygen can be supplied in different ways depending on whether the client is in a hospital, a long-term care facility, or at home. Common sources include oxygen concentrators, compressed gas cylinders, central piped wall outlets, and liquid oxygen systems.
根据患者身处急救医院、长期照护机构还是居家环境，其氧气供应形式各有不同。常见的氧源形式包括分子筛制氧机、压缩氧气钢瓶、中央管道壁式氧气插口以及液态氧系统。
1. Oxygen Concentrator (FIGURE 39.8): An oxygen concentrator is an electric machine that draws in ambient room air, filters out nitrogen, and delivers concentrated oxygen (about 90% to 95% pure). It is the most common system used in home care because it runs continuously without needing tank refills. However, because it runs on electricity, the client must always have a backup oxygen cylinder in case of a power outage.
1. 电动分子筛制氧机（图 39.8）：制氧机是一种靠电力驱动的医用设备，它吸入室内环境空气，利用分子筛滤除占空气 78% 的氮气，输出浓度高达 90% 至 95% 的医用浓缩氧。由于无需频繁运送更换笨重钢瓶，制氧机是目前居家护理最常用的氧源。然而，鉴于其对电力的绝对依赖，患者家中必须常备备用氧气钢瓶，以防电网突发断电停机。
2. Compressed Oxygen Cylinders (FIGURE 39.9 & FIGURE 39.10): Oxygen is compressed under high pressure (up to 2,000 to 2,200 psi) into heavy steel or aluminum tanks. Large cylinders are used for stationary bed rest, while small portable cylinders allow clients to walk, attend appointments, or exercise. The pressure gauge on top of the cylinder (FIGURE 39.10) shows how much oxygen remains. When the needle points into the red "Refill" section, the tank must be changed immediately! Cylinders must always be secured in a wheeled cart or chained to a wall so they cannot fall over. If a tank falls and its valve breaks, the pressurized gas turns it into a lethal rocket!
2. 压缩压缩氧气钢瓶（图 39.9 与图 39.10）：高纯度氧气在高压（高达 2000 至 2200 psi 磅/平方英寸）下压缩储存于特种钢质或铝质气瓶中。大型落地钢瓶用于卧床患者，而小型轻便便携式气瓶则装配在带轮小推车或肩背包中，供患者外出散步、就医或活动（图 39.9）。瓶头顶端的压力表（图 39.10）清晰显示剩余储气量；一旦指针进入红色“Refill（待充气）”刻度区，必须立即更换备用瓶！钢瓶必须始终使用安全推车固定或铁链锁在墙壁上，严防倒伏；如果重压钢瓶翻倒导致瓶阀断裂，瞬间喷射的高压气体能使气瓶化为致命的穿墙飞弹！

Central Outlets and Liquid Oxygen Systems
中央管道壁式氧源与液态氧供氧系统

3. Central Piped Wall Outlet (FIGURE 39.11): In hospitals and long-term care homes, oxygen is piped directly into rooms from a large central reservoir located outside the building. A wall outlet at the head of the client's bed is fitted with a flowmeter. The worker simply connects the tubing or humidifier bottle directly to the wall unit. Central systems are convenient, quiet, and eliminate the need to move heavy cylinders.
3. 中央管道壁式氧气插座（图 39.11）：在综合医院和养老机构中，氧气通常通过医用集中供气管道直接由室外大型液氧主储罐分流输送至每个病房。在患者床头的设备带上设有专用的氧气快插接口，直接插接流量计。护理人员只需将输氧导管或湿化瓶卡入插座即可开启使用。中央系统静音、便捷，彻底免去了搬运沉重钢瓶的体力和安全隐患。
4. Liquid Oxygen Systems (FIGURE 39.12): Oxygen gas can be cooled to -183°C (-297°F) to become a liquid. Liquid oxygen takes up much less space than compressed gas (1 litre of liquid oxygen expands to about 860 litres of gas!). A large stationary reservoir stays at home, and a small portable unit can be filled from it for ambulatory use. Liquid oxygen systems are lightweight and provide hours of portable use. However, liquid oxygen is extremely cold and can cause severe frostbite if skin comes into contact with the freezing fluid or frosted metal parts.

Oxygen Delivery Devices: Cannula and Simple Mask
给氧器具解析：双孔鼻导管与简易面罩

Different oxygen devices are used depending on the amount of oxygen the client needs, the client's comfort, and whether the client can breathe through the nose or mouth. Common devices include:
根据患者所需的吸氧浓度、舒适耐受度以及经鼻还是经口呼吸的习惯，临床选用不同结构特性的给氧装置：
1. Nasal Cannula (FIGURE 39.13): A nasal cannula is the most common and comfortable oxygen delivery device. It consists of plastic tubing with two curved prongs that insert into the client's nares. The prongs must curve downward following the natural curve of the nasal cavity. The tubing loops over both ears and is secured snugly under the chin with a plastic slide. Flow rates typically range from 1 to 6 litres per minute (L/min), providing an oxygen concentration between 24% and 44%.
Advantages: The client can talk, eat, drink, and take oral medications without removing the cannula. It is lightweight and well tolerated.
Nursing considerations: Check behind the ears and under the nose for pressure ulcers. Offer frequent oral and nasal hygiene. Ensure prongs do not dislodge when the client sleeps or turns.
1. 双孔鼻导管（Nasal Cannula，图 39.13）：鼻导管是临床最普及、患者接受度最高的低流量给氧器具。导管前端带有两个弯曲小尖塞，需顺应鼻腔自然解剖弧度弯曲向下插入两侧鼻孔内。两侧输氧软管绕经双侧耳廓上方，并在下颌中央通过滑块轻柔拉紧固定。常用给氧流量为 1 至 6 升/分钟（L/min），提供约 24% 至 44% 的吸入氧浓度。
临床优势：吸氧过程中患者可正常交谈、进食、饮水和服用口服药，无幽闭窒息感，轻巧舒适。
照护重点：严密巡视双耳廓后方及鼻小柱周围皮肤，警惕长期压迫形成压疮；勤做口腔与鼻腔清洁；防止夜间翻身时鼻塞滑脱。
2. Simple Face Mask (FIGURE 39.14): A simple face mask covers both the client's nose and mouth. It is held in place with an elastic strap around the back of the head, and a pliable metal strip pinches over the bridge of the nose for a snug fit. It delivers flow rates of 5 to 10 L/min (delivering 40% to 60% oxygen). The flow rate must NEVER be set below 5 L/min; a minimum flow of 5 L/min is required to flush exhaled carbon dioxide out through the holes in the sides of the mask, preventing the client from rebreathing CO₂.
Nursing considerations: A mask makes eating, drinking, and speaking difficult. If the client is ordered oxygen during meals, a physician order is obtained to temporarily switch to a nasal cannula while eating. Clean and dry the face regularly to prevent moisture buildup under the mask.
2. 简易面罩（Simple Face Mask，图 39.14）：简易面罩同时覆盖患者的口部与鼻部。利用弹性松紧带环绕固定于脑后，面罩鼻梁处配有可塑形金属片捏紧贴合。给氧流速通常为 5 至 10 L/min（提供 40% 至 60% 的中度氧浓度）。必须切记：简易面罩的氧流量绝不能低于 5 L/min！只有不低于 5 L/min 的持续气流，才能将面罩内蓄积的呼出二氧化碳从侧壁微孔中强力冲刷吹出，防止患者反复回吸二氧化碳造成酸中毒。

Humidification and Pulse Oximetry
氧气湿化处理与脉搏血氧监测原理

Humidified Oxygen (FIGURE 39.19): Medical oxygen stored in cylinders or piping is bone dry. Dry oxygen irritates and dries out the respiratory mucous membranes, leading to nasal crusting, nosebleeds, and thick mucus plugs. To prevent this, a humidifier bottle filled with sterile distilled water is attached between the flowmeter and the oxygen tubing. As oxygen passes through the water, it bubbles up and absorbs moisture before reaching the client. Check the water bottle frequently; never let it run completely dry!
湿化氧气（图 39.19）：储存于高压钢瓶或中心管道中的医用纯氧是极度干燥脱水的。干燥气体直接吹入气道会严重脱水灼伤呼吸道黏膜，引起鼻腔干痛结痂、鼻出血及痰液黏稠结栓。为防止黏膜干裂，必须在流量计与输氧管之间连接专用的湿化瓶，注入无菌蒸馏水。氧气从管底经水体产生细密气泡向上冒出，充分饱和吸收水分蒸气后输送给患者。必须频繁巡查湿化瓶水位，绝对不可干烧吹干！
Pulse Oximetry: Pulse oximetry is a non-invasive test used to measure the oxygen concentration in arterial blood. The device measures oxygen saturation (SpO₂)—the percentage of hemoglobin molecules that are carrying oxygen. A sensor is attached to a pulsating vascular bed (such as a finger, toe, or earlobe). Infrared and red light beams shine through the tissue to a photodetector on the other side. Oxygenated and deoxygenated hemoglobin absorb light differently. The machine calculates the ratio and displays both the SpO₂ percentage and the client's pulse rate.
脉搏血氧饱和度监测（Pulse Oximetry）：脉搏血氧测定是一种无创、连续监测动脉血氧合状态的关键临床技术。该仪器测定的是脉搏血氧饱和度（SpO₂）——即血液中与氧结合的氧合血红蛋白占全部可结合血红蛋白的百分比。传感器探头夹在富有搏动性微血管的解剖部位（如手指、脚趾或耳垂）。探头发射红光与红外光两束光波穿透人体组织，由对侧的光电探测器接收。氧合血红蛋白与还原血红蛋白对不同波长光线的吸收系数存在物理差异，微电脑芯片由此精准计算出 SpO₂ 饱和度百分比及实时脉搏跳动率。

Clinical Oximetry Principles and Precautions
血氧监测临床读数解读与干扰因素排除

Normal Range: A normal SpO₂ measurement in healthy individuals is 95% to 100%. A reading below 95% indicates potential hypoxia. For clients with chronic respiratory conditions such as COPD, the physician may order an acceptable lower range (such as 88% to 92%). Always check the specific care plan for individual target goals!
临床正常范围：健康成年人的生理性 SpO₂ 读数介于 95% 至 100% 之间。读数一旦跌破 95%，即提示可能存在组织缺氧。对于患有严重慢性阻塞性肺疾病（COPD）的患者，医生常会设立特定的临床靶向范围（如 88% 至 92%）。必须随时查对护理计划中的个体化靶向目标！
Factors that Can Cause Inaccurate Readings:
  • Nail Polish or Acrylic Nails: Dark nail polish (blue, black, purple, red) absorbs the red light emitted by the sensor, causing false low readings. Remove polish or place sensor on an earlobe or toe.
  • Cold Extremities / Poor Circulation: Severe vasoconstriction, hypothermia, low cardiac output, or peripheral vascular disease reduces blood flow to the fingers, preventing the detector from picking up a pulse signal. Warm the client's hands first.
  • Movement and Shivering: Shivering or restless movement creates motion artifact, confusing the sensor.
  • Bright Ambient Light: Direct sunlight, surgical lamps, or phototherapy lights shining onto the sensor can mimic infrared light. Cover the sensor with a blanket or towel.
  • Edema and Swelling: Fluid retention in the fingers scatters the light beams.
  • Carbon Monoxide (CO) Poisoning: Carbon monoxide binds to hemoglobin 200 times more tightly than oxygen. The pulse oximeter CANNOT tell the difference between carboxyhemoglobin and oxyhemoglobin and will show a falsely high 100% reading while the client is fatally suffocating!
导致仪器读数失真的常见干扰因素：
  • 指甲油与美甲贴片：深色指甲油（黑、蓝、深紫、暗红）会强力吸收光束，导致假性偏低读数。必须使用丙酮卸除指甲油或改测耳垂。
  • 肢端冰冷与末梢循环不良：严重低体温、休克、外周血管痉挛导致手指毛细血管血流微弱，探头无法识别搏动。测前应轻揉手部保暖。
  • 身体剧烈晃动与寒战：肢体抖动与寒战会产生强烈的机械运动伪影，导致读数剧烈跳变。
  • 强烈环境光线干扰：强烈的阳光直射、无影灯或高亮度荧光灯照射在探头上会干扰光敏接收，需用遮光毛巾覆盖。
  • 严重肢体水肿：组织间液过度积聚会散射光束，妨碍信号穿透。
  • 一氧化碳（CO）中毒警示：一氧化碳与血红蛋白的亲和力是氧气的 200 多倍。普通血氧仪无法区分碳氧血红蛋白与氧合血红蛋白，常显示“完美虚假的 100%”，而患者体内细胞已发生致命性窒息！
PULSE OXIMETRY DOES NOT REPLACE CLINICAL OBSERVATION! Never treat a number on a monitor instead of observing the human being. If the monitor reads 98% but the client is gasping, restless, sweaty, and pale, the client is in respiratory distress! Trust your eyes and call the nurse immediately.
血氧仪绝不能替代对患者的直接临床观察！切勿只看仪器屏幕上的数字而忽视有血有肉的患者。如果屏幕显示 98%，但患者出现大汗淋漓、面色苍白、大口喘气或极度烦躁，说明患者正处于急性呼吸窘迫危象中！相信您的临床直觉，立即召唤护士抢救。

Collecting Sputum Specimens: Sputum is respiratory mucus expectorated from the bronchi and trachea (it is NOT saliva from the mouth!). Sputum specimens are tested in the laboratory to identify causative bacteria, viruses, or fungi in lung infections, and to test for malignant cells.
留取痰液标本：痰液（Sputum）是由气管、支气管及肺泡分泌并咳出的病理分泌物（必须与口腔唾液 Saliva 严格区分！）。化验室通过痰液涂片与细菌培养，鉴定导致肺炎、肺结核的致病微生物，进行抗生素敏感性试验，或筛查异常癌细胞。
Key Rules for Collecting Sputum:
  • Best Time: Early morning upon awakening. Secretions pool in the lungs overnight, making morning sputum the most concentrated and representative.
  • Rinse Mouth with Water ONLY: Have the client rinse the mouth with clear water before expectorating. DO NOT use mouthwash! Mouthwash contains antiseptic agents that destroy microorganisms in the sample, invalidating culture results.
  • Maintain Sterility: The inside of the specimen cup and lid are strictly sterile. Never touch the rim or inside of the cup.
留取痰标本的关键准则：
  • 最佳留取时机：清晨刚醒时（晨起第一口痰）。夜间睡眠中气道纤毛运动减慢，深部痰液大量蓄积，此时的痰液细菌浓度最高、最具有诊断价值。
  • 仅可用清水漱口：咳痰前指导患者用温清水彻底漱口清除食物残渣。严禁使用药用漱口水或刷牙！漱口水中的杀菌剂会杀灭标本中的致病菌，导致培养阴性的严重假报告。

Postural Drainage and Chest Percussion

Some clients do not have the muscular strength or cough reflex to cough up deep mucus. In these cases, the physician orders postural drainage and chest percussion to mobilize secretions.
某些虚弱重症患者因呼吸肌无力或咳嗽反射减退，无法自主咳出肺深部黏稠痰栓。对于这类患者，医生常开具体位引流（Postural drainage）与胸部叩击（Chest percussion）的物理治疗医嘱以松动排痰。

Postural Drainage Principles: Gravity is used to drain secretions from specific lung segments into the trachea. The client is placed in special positions (FIGURE 39.21) where the affected lung area is higher than the main airway, allowing secretions to flow downward. The client usually remains in position for 15 to 20 minutes before coughing.
体位引流机制：利用重力作用使特定肺段内的痰液顺流排入大支气管与气管。根据病变肺叶的解剖走向，将患者安置在使受累肺部抬高并高于主气道的特殊体位（图 39.21）。患者通常需维持引流体位 15 至 20 分钟，使痰液在重力下流向中央气道后协助剧烈排痰。
Chest Percussion (Clapping): The therapist or nurse cups their hands and claps rhythmically against the client's chest wall over the affected area, moving from the bottom of the lung upward. The cupped hand traps air, creating vibrating shockwaves that loosen sticky mucus without bruising the skin. Support workers never perform chest percussion unless formally trained and delegated.
胸部叩击（扣背排痰）：操作者双手手指并拢、手掌呈空心杯状弓起，有节律地快速叩击患者病变肺部对应的胸壁，叩击顺序严格自下而上、由外周向中央推进。空心手掌内部的气垫产生深透震动波，促使黏附在气道壁上的痰液脱落，同时避免击伤皮肤软组织。支持人员未经专门技能培训与授权委托，严禁擅自实施胸部叩击。

Artificial Airways
人工气道分类与置管适应证

An artificial airway is inserted to maintain an open (patent) airway when the client's normal airway becomes obstructed by disease, injury, secretions, or the tongue falling backward, or when the client requires mechanical ventilation. Common types of artificial airways (FIGURE 39.23) include:
当患者自身气道因疾病狭窄、严重外伤、大量黏稠分泌物潴留或昏迷致舌后坠发生梗阻，或需要连接机械呼吸机辅助通气时，必须通过外科或介入手段置入人工气道（Artificial airway）以维持生命通道的通畅。临床最常用的人工气道（图 39.23）包括：
A. Oropharyngeal Airway (FIGURE 39.23, A): A curved plastic device inserted through the mouth into the pharynx. It holds the tongue forward, preventing it from falling back against the posterior pharyngeal wall. Commonly used for unconscious or sedated clients after surgery.
A. 口咽通气管（图 39.23 A）：经口腔置入咽后壁的弯曲硬质塑料导管。它将舌根向上向前托举，防止深昏迷或全身麻醉术后恢复期患者舌后坠完全阻塞咽部气道。仅用于意识不清或吞咽咽反射消失的患者。
B. Nasopharyngeal Airway (FIGURE 39.23, B): A soft, flexible rubber tube inserted through a nostril into the pharynx (also called a "nasal trumpet"). It can be used in conscious clients who require frequent suctioning.
B. 鼻咽通气管（图 39.23 B）：经一侧鼻孔深入咽腔的柔软橡皮或硅胶导管（又称鼻咽喇叭管）。适用于神志清醒但上呼吸道相对受阻、或需频繁经鼻抽吸深部分泌物的患者。
C. Endotracheal (ET) Tube (FIGURE 39.23, C): Inserted through the mouth or nose directly into the trachea. The procedure is called intubation. A small balloon (cuff) near the tip is inflated with air to seal the space between the tube and tracheal wall, ensuring that all ventilator-delivered air enters the lungs and preventing oral secretions from leaking into the lungs. An ET tube is usually temporary (for a few days up to 2 weeks).
C. 气管插管（ET tube，图 39.23 C）：经口腔或鼻腔直接插入气管内的特制导管，置管操作称为气管插管术（Intubation）。导管尖端附近配有微型气囊（Cuff），充气后紧密贴合封闭气管内壁与导管间隙，既确保呼吸机泵入的气体全部进入肺部，又阻断口腔分泌物逆流入肺造成误吸。气管插管通常用于短期急救（数天至 2 周）。
D. Tracheostomy Tube (FIGURE 39.23, D): A curved tube surgically inserted through a neck incision directly into the trachea below the larynx. It is used when an artificial airway is needed for a prolonged period.
D. 气管切开导管（图 39.23 D）：经颈部前方外科切口直接置入喉下段气管腔内的特制弯管。当患者需要长期甚至终身维持人工气道或长期依赖呼吸机时选用。

图 39.23 人工气道四大分类：A 口咽通气道；B 鼻咽通气道；C 带气囊气管插管；D 气管切开插管。

Parts of a Tracheostomy Tube and Stoma Care
气管切开导管构造解剖与造口皮肤护理

A tracheostomy is a surgically created opening (stoma) into the trachea. A tracheostomy tube consists of three distinct parts (FIGURE 39.24):
气管切开造口术是在颈前段气管切开的造口通道。标准的气管切开导管由三个精密配合的独立部件组成（图 39.24）：
1. Outer Cannula: The main curved tube inserted into the trachea. It has neck flanges with slots for fabric ties. The outer cannula remains in place continuously to keep the airway open. THE OUTER CANNULA IS NEVER REMOVED BY A SUPPORT WORKER! If the outer cannula is dislodged, the stoma can collapse and close within minutes, causing asphyxiation!
1. 外导管（Outer Cannula）：置入气管腔内的弧形主管。其两侧带有带孔凸缘（Flange），供系带穿过绑扎在颈部。外导管始终留置在体内以支撑造口保持通畅。支持工作人员绝对严禁取出或拔动外导管！外导管一旦滑脱，颈部软组织造口可能在数分钟内迅速闭合引起窒息！
2. Inner Cannula: Fits snugly inside the outer cannula and locks into place. It can be removed periodically for cleaning (to remove mucus buildup) or discarded if disposable. Removing and cleaning the inner cannula keeps the airway clear without having to remove the life-sustaining outer cannula.
2. 内导管（Inner Cannula）：紧密套入外导管内部并通过旋钮锁扣锁定。内导管可定期取出清洗消毒以彻底清除黏附的干结痰痂，或定期更换一次性内套管。清洁内导管无需拔动外管，保障了患者在气道维护期间的绝对通气安全。
3. Obturator: A bullet-shaped, smooth plastic guide used ONLY when the outer cannula is being inserted through the neck tissues. Its rounded tip prevents tearing of tracheal tissues. Once the outer cannula is in place, the obturator is immediately removed so the client can breathe. Safety Rule: The obturator must ALWAYS be taped to the head of the bed or kept at the client's bedside in case the tube accidentally falls out and must be urgently reinserted by the nurse or doctor!
3. 闭塞器（导引芯，Obturator）：带有光滑钝圆头部的塑料芯棒，仅在外导管插入气管造口的过程中使用。其圆头能保护气管壁黏膜免受导管硬边缘划伤。外导管一旦就位，必须立即拔出闭塞器以允许气体流通。安全铁律：拔出的闭塞器必须始终装在清洁袋中胶贴在床头或置于床边显眼处，以备外导管意外脱落时供医生护士紧急重新插管抢救使用！

Tracheostomy Care and Safety Measures
气管切开固定带安全更换与生活照护

Tracheostomy Ties Safety: The tracheostomy tube is held in place by fabric ties or a Velcro collar wrapped around the neck. Loose ties allow the tube to be coughed out; excessively tight ties compromise neck circulation and cause skin breakdown. The tie tightness is checked by sliding ONE finger under the ties (FIGURE 39.25, A). If you can easily insert two fingers, the ties are too loose; if one finger cannot slide under, they are too tight.
固定系带松紧度检查：气管切开导管依靠专用的布带或魔术贴颈带环绕颈部固定。系带过松易致患者咳嗽时整根套管被喷出体外；系带过紧则压迫颈部血管与气道造成皮肤溃烂。标准的松紧度校验法为：在系带与颈部皮肤之间仅能平整滑入一根手指（图 39.25 A）。若能轻松放入两指则表明过松；若连一指都无法塞入则表明过紧。
FIGURE 39.25 A, A finger is inserted under the ties to ensure proper fit. B, For safety, hold the outer cannula firmly while ties are being adjusted.
图 39.25 A 检查固定带松紧度（容纳一指为宜）；B 调整更换系带时必须由专人牢固按住外管凸缘防脱。
LIFESAVING RULE FOR CHANGING TIES: When tracheostomy ties become soiled, they must be changed. THE OLD TIES ARE NEVER REMOVED UNTIL THE NEW TIES ARE FIRMLY TIED IN PLACE, OR UNLESS A SECOND PERSON SECURELY HOLDS THE OUTER CANNULA IN PLACE! (FIGURE 39.25, B). If the client coughs while the ties are off and the tube is unheld, the tube will fly out across the room, causing acute airway closure.
更换系带的救命红线：固定系带被分泌物污损必须更换。在全新系带牢牢系紧打结之前、或有第二名医护人员用双手死死按住外导管凸缘之前，绝对禁止剪断或解开旧系带！（图 39.25 B）。如果在系带解开且无外力按压的瞬间患者突然爆发咳嗽，整个外导管会在气流冲击下瞬间飞出数米之外，引发窒息死亡！
Communication and Emotional Care: Because air bypasses the vocal cords through the neck stoma, the client cannot vocalize or speak words. This causes extreme frustration and panic. Always provide a paper and pen, picture board, or communication tablet. Place the call bell directly in the client's hand at all times and answer calls immediately!
沟通辅助与心理支持：由于呼出气流直接从颈部造口逸出而不再经过喉头声带，带气囊气管切开患者完全无法震动声带发音说话。这种失语状态会带来绝望与恐慌。必须为患者备齐写字板、画板或沟通平板电脑；呼叫铃必须始终放置在患者握力充足的手心，听到呼叫必须立即响应！

Suctioning an Airway and Emergency Bag
气道负压吸痰安全规约与人工简易呼吸器

Suctioning: When secretions accumulate in the airway and the client cannot cough them out, suctioning is necessary. Suctioning involves inserting a hollow plastic catheter into the airway and applying negative pressure to aspirate fluids and mucus. Suctioning can be performed orally, nasally, or directly into an ET or tracheostomy tube.
气道吸痰术：当气道深部痰液大量蓄积且患者无力自主咳出时，必须实施负压吸痰。吸痰操作是将无菌塑料吸痰软管深入气道，通过连接负压吸引机抽取清除积存的黏液与分泌物。吸痰可经口咽、经鼻咽或经气管插管/气管切开插管直接深入气管。
The Ambu Bag (FIGURE 39.26): An Ambu bag (also known as a bag-valve-mask resuscitator) is a self-inflating handheld balloon attached to an oxygen source. Compressing the bag forces oxygen-rich air into the client's lungs. The nurse uses the Ambu bag to hyperventilate and hyperoxygenate the client before and after deep suctioning. Hyperoxygenation ensures the client has high oxygen reserves to withstand the temporary oxygen loss during suctioning.
简易人工复苏气囊（Ambu 气囊，图 39.26）：Ambu 气囊（皮囊/气阀呼吸器）是一种连接高流量纯氧的手动自充气按压囊袋。挤压气囊能强力将高浓度氧气压入患者肺内。在实施深部气管吸痰前后，医护人员会使用 Ambu 气囊对患者进行过度通气与高浓度预充氧。预充氧能极大提升血液携氧储备，防止吸痰抽吸期间因缺氧诱发严重心律失常。

⏱️ SUCTIONING TIME LIMIT: 10 TO 15 SECONDS / 吸痰时间极限红线

Suctioning removes not only secretions but also OXYGEN from the lungs! A single suction pass must NEVER EXCEED 10 TO 15 SECONDS (only 5 seconds in infants and small children). Prolonged suctioning causes acute hypoxemia, cardiac dysrhythmias, and cardiac arrest! Suction is applied ONLY while withdrawing and rotating the catheter—never while inserting it! 吸痰泵在抽吸痰液的同时，以极高负压疯狂抽吸肺内赖以生存的氧气！单次抽吸时间绝对严禁超过 10 至 15 秒（婴幼儿严格限制在 5 秒以内）。时间过长会瞬间造成急性致死性低氧血症并诱发心跳骤停！必须牢记：吸痰管插入阶段绝不加负压，仅在由深部旋转退管时方可轻柔加压抽吸！

Suction Catheters: Different catheters are designed for different areas of the airway:
  • Yankauer Suction Catheter (FIGURE 39.27): A hard, rigid plastic catheter with a curved tip and large openings (also called a tonsil tip). It is designed specifically for suctioning thick secretions, vomitus, and saliva from the mouth and pharynx.   • 扬考尔硬质吸痰管（Yankauer Catheter，图 39.27）：一种带有弯曲圆滑前端与侧孔的坚硬塑料吸管（又称扁桃体吸引管）。专门用于经口腔抽吸积存的浓稠唾液、血块或呕吐物。在许多加拿大机构中，经过培训考核的支持人员可被授权执行经口 Yankauer 吸痰。

Mechanical Ventilation (FIGURE 39.28): A mechanical ventilator is a life-sustaining machine that moves air and oxygen into and out of the lungs for clients who cannot breathe adequately on their own. The client is connected to the ventilator via an ET tube or tracheostomy tube. The ventilator controls or assists respiratory rate, tidal volume, and oxygen concentration.

Ventilator Alarms and Chest Tubes Introduction
呼吸机报警紧急应对与胸腔引流基础

Ventilator Alarms and Immediate Worker Action: Ventilators are equipped with sophisticated alarms that alert staff to life-threatening emergencies.
  • Low-Pressure Alarm: Signals that air is escaping. Common causes: the tubing has completely disconnected from the client's airway!
  • High-Pressure Alarm: Signals an obstruction. Common causes: mucus plug blocking the tube, client biting the tube, coughing, or kinked tubing.
WHAT TO DO FIRST WHEN AN ALARM SOUNDS:
  1. FIRST: LOOK AT THE CLIENT IMMEDIATELY! Check if the tubing is still attached to the client's airway tube. If disconnected, reconnect it at once or call the nurse loudly!
  2. NEVER SILENCE, IGNORE, OR RESET AN ALARM! Support workers are legally forbidden from resetting ventilator alarms.
  3. CALL THE NURSE IMMEDIATELY using the emergency call bell.
呼吸机报警与紧急处置第一原则：呼吸机装配有高灵敏声光警报系统以指示危险：
  • 低压报警（Low-Pressure）：提示管路漏气或脱开。最常见原因：呼吸机螺纹管与患者气道接口彻底脱落！
  • 高压报警（High-Pressure）：提示气流通道受阻。常见原因：气道积痰阻塞、患者躁动咬管、剧烈咳嗽或管路扭折。
听到报警声时的第一动作与铁律：
  1. 第一反应：立即目测患者！迅速核查呼吸机管路是否仍牢固连接在患者的人工气道上；若脱开，立即接上并大声呼叫护士！
  2. 严禁擅自消音、忽视或重置报警！法律严格禁止支持人员触碰呼吸机控制面板复位键。
  3. 立即按下紧急呼叫铃召唤主管护士。
Introduction to Chest Tubes: The chest cavity (pleural space) normally has a negative pressure that keeps the lungs inflated against the chest wall. When chest injury, disease, or surgery causes air, blood, or fluid to leak into the pleural space, negative pressure is lost, and the lung collapses! To treat this, a surgeon inserts a chest tube into the chest cavity to remove the trapped air or fluid and re-expand the lung.
胸腔引流管导言：正常生理状态下，胸膜腔（脏层与壁层胸膜之间）维持着负压，如同吸盘牵拉着肺组织贴附胸壁舒展膨胀。当胸部创伤、肺部破裂或开胸手术导致空气、血液或炎性渗液漏入胸膜腔时，生理负压骤然丧失，肺泡受压萎陷！为挽救生命，胸外科医生需紧急置入胸腔引流管（Chest tube），将积存的气体或液体引流出体外，重建负压促使肺复张。

Chest Tube Anatomy and Drainage Mechanics
胸腔引流解剖机制与水封瓶原理

Conditions Requiring Chest Tubes:
  • Pneumothorax: Collection of air (pneumo) in the pleural space (thorax). Air enters the space from a punctured lung or chest wall stab wound, collapsing the lung. A tube is inserted high near the upper chest (second intercostal space) because air rises.
  • Hemothorax: Collection of blood (hemo) in the pleural space from ruptured blood vessels. A tube is placed lower in the chest (fifth or sixth intercostal space) because blood settles by gravity.
  • Pleural Effusion: Collection of serous fluid in the pleural space caused by heart failure, pneumonia, or cancer.
需要置入胸腔引流管的典型病症：
  • 气胸（Pneumothorax）：气体（pneumo-）积聚于胸膜腔内。肺破裂或胸壁穿透伤使空气漏入胸膜腔压迫肺叶。因气体比重轻自然向上升，引流管常置入胸壁上方较高位置（锁骨中线第 2 肋间）。
  • 血胸（Hemothorax）：血液（hemo-）积聚于胸膜腔。由于血液在重力下下沉，引流管置入胸壁中下段较低位置（腋中线第 5 或第 6 肋间）。
  • 胸腔积液（Pleural Effusion）：心力衰竭、严重肺感染或恶性肿瘤导致胸膜腔产生大量浆液性液体。

Water-Seal Drainage and Emergency Safety
水封引流瓶系统维护与脱管急救预案

FIGURE 39.30 Chest tubes attached to a disposable water-seal drainage system. Container must stay upright and below chest.
图 39.30 胸腔闭式一次性水封引流瓶系统（必须始终直立放置并低于患者胸腔平面）。
Water-Seal Drainage Mechanics (FIGURE 39.30): The chest tube connects to a drainage unit with a sterile water seal. The water seal acts as a one-way valve: it allows air and fluid to exit the chest with each breath or cough, but prevents outside atmospheric air from being sucked backwards into the chest cavity.
水封引流机制（图 39.30）：胸腔导管连接至注有无菌水的水封引流装置。水封层如同单向安全阀：患者每次呼吸或咳嗽时，胸膜腔内的积气与积液被挤出水封管并在水中鼓泡逸出；而在患者吸气胸腔负压增大时，水封管内的水柱随之上移，阻止外界空气逆向倒吸入胸腔。
Essential Nursing Rules for Chest Tubes:
  • ALWAYS KEEP DRAINAGE CONTAINER BELOW CHEST LEVEL: Never raise the container above the bed or chest! If raised, gravity will cause contaminated drainage fluid to rush backwards into the client's pleural cavity!
  • NO KINKS OR DEPENDENT LOOPS: Keep tubing coiled flat on the bed; never let it loop below the drainage unit.
胸腔闭式引流核心照护铁律：
  • 引流装置必须时刻低于患者胸腔平面：在任何情况下（包括翻身、下床、转运）绝对严禁将引流瓶抬高超过床面或胸部！一旦抬高，瓶内积聚的引流液将在重力下逆流灌入胸膜腔造成严重感染或致命性气胸！
  • 避免管道受压与低垂扭结：导管应平整盘绕在床单上，顺直向下引流，避免形成 U 型下垂积液弯管。

🚨 CHEST TUBE EMERGENCIES: LIFE-SAVING PROTOCOLS / 脱管危象紧急抢救规程

IF THE CHEST TUBE IS ACCIDENTALLY PULLED OUT OF THE CHEST: Immediately cover the insertion site with your gloved hand or sterile petrolatum (Vaseline) gauze and apply firm pressure to prevent air from rushing into the chest. Call the nurse STAT! Do NOT attempt to push the tube back in!

IF THE TUBING DISCONNECTS FROM THE DRAINAGE UNIT: Submerge the end of the chest tube 2 to 4 cm into a container of sterile water to re-create a temporary water seal, preventing air suction into the pleural space. Call the nurse immediately. Never clamp a chest tube without a direct physician order!

若胸腔引流管不慎自胸壁意外拔出：立即用戴无菌手套的手掌或无菌凡士林纱布用力按紧穿刺创口，封闭漏气，防止外界空气被胸膜腔负压抽吸形成张力性气胸！大声呼叫护士！严禁将脱出的管道插回！
若引流管与引流瓶接头处脱开：立即将引流管末端浸入 2 至 4 厘米深的无菌生理盐水瓶中建立临时水封，防止空气倒吸！立即召唤护士。未获医生直接指令，严禁擅自用血管钳死夹胸管！

CHAPTER REVIEW · KEY POINTS / 本章复习核心要点

  • Oxygen is a basic physiological necessity of life. Death occurs within minutes if breathing stops, and serious illness occurs without adequate oxygen.
    氧气是维持生命最根本的生理需求；呼吸停止数分钟即可发生生物学死亡，供氧不足会导致各脏器严重病变。
  • Factors affecting oxygen needs include respiratory and cardiovascular status, red blood cell count, nervous system regulation, aging, exercise, fever, pain, narcotics, smoking, allergies, pollutants, and alcohol.
    影响需氧量的关键因素涵盖呼吸与循环机能、红细胞计数、神经中枢调节、老龄退化、运动、发热、疼痛、镇痛麻醉药、吸烟、过敏、环境污染物及酒精。
  • Healthy adults take 12 to 20 breaths per minute. Tachypnea is >24/min; Bradypnea is <12/min; Apnea is absence of breathing.
    健康成年人呼吸为 12–20 次/分；>24次为呼吸过速，<12次为呼吸过缓，无呼吸为呼吸暂停。
  • Hypoxia is a deficiency of oxygen in the cells. Restlessness, anxiety, and tachycardia are early warning signs; cyanosis is a late and life-threatening sign.
    缺氧是组织细胞供氧不足。烦躁、焦虑、心动过速是早期警示；发绀是晚期危重体征。
  • Normal SpO₂ is 95% to 100%. Pulse oximetry does not replace vigilant clinical observation.
    正常 SpO₂ 为 95%–100%；仪器读数绝不可替代对患者的直接临床观察。
  • Deep breathing and coughing expand alveoli and clear secretions, preventing postoperative atelectasis and pneumonia. Incisions must be splinted with pillows during coughing.
    深呼吸与排痰可预防术后肺不张与肺炎；咳嗽时必须用软枕支托伤口。
  • Oxygen is a prescribed drug and a fire hazard. Strict no-smoking rules and water-soluble lubricants are mandatory.
    氧气属于处方药并具有极强助燃性；严禁烟火，禁用凡士林等矿物油润滑剂。
  • Never remove tracheostomy ties without a second person securing the outer cannula. Suctioning must not exceed 10 to 15 seconds.
    更换气切系带前必须牢固固定外管；单次吸痰时间严控在 10–15 秒以内。
  • Chest drainage units must ALWAYS be kept upright and BELOW the client's chest level.
    胸腔水封引流瓶必须始终直立放置且低于胸腔平面。

CRITICAL THINKING IN PRACTICE / 临床批判性思维案例

Mr. Henderson, 74, has severe COPD and a permanent tracheostomy. He uses a portable oxygen tank at 2 L/min via a tracheostomy collar. While assisting him with morning care, you notice he is extremely restless and breathing rapidly (30 breaths/min). When you check his pulse oximeter, it reads 86%. When you look at his oxygen tank, the pressure gauge needle is in the red "Refill" section, and his connecting tubing is kinked under the chair leg.
74 岁的亨德森先生患有重度 COPD 并留置永久性气管切开导管。他平时通过气切面罩接便携氧气瓶以 2 L/min 吸氧。早晨协助洗漱时，您发现他极度烦躁不安，呼吸急促达 30 次/分。血氧仪读数跌至 86%。仔细检查设备发现，其氧气钢瓶压力表指针已指在红色“Refill”归零区，且连接导管被压折在椅子腿下！

Discussion & Clinical Actions / 处置方案：
1. Immediately free the kinked tubing and unkink the line.
2. Immediately switch him to the full backup oxygen cylinder or home concentrator.
3. Recheck his SpO₂ and observe for easing of respirations.
4. Immediately alert your supervising nurse of the hypoxic episode and equipment issue.
