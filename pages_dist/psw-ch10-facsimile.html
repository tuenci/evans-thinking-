OBJECTIVES

本章学习目标
  • • Describe the multidisciplinary health care team, and state the benefits and challenges of working on a team.
    详尽描述跨学科医疗照护团队的运作架构，系统阐述团队协作的核心优势与潜在执业挑战。
  • • Describe the responsibilities of the support worker when working on a team in a facility and in the community.
    明确界定支持工作者分别在长期照护机构及社区居家照护团队中所肩负的法定职责与角色边界。
  • • Define delegation, and differentiate between a task, an authorized task (controlled act), and a delegated task.
    精准界定工作委派（Delegation）的法务定义，严格辨析常规照护任务、法定受控法定操作（Controlled Acts）与被委派任务三者之间的法律边界。
  • • Explain the responsibilities of the nurse and the support worker in delegation.
    深度剖析在工作委派全生命周期中，注册护士（委派方）与支持工作者（被委派方）各自不可推卸的法律与伦理责任。
  • • Describe the five rights of delegation.
    熟练掌握并践行护士委派决策的“五大正确黄金法则”（Five Rights of Delegation）。
  • • Explain the circumstances under which you have the right and duty to refuse a delegated task.
    系统掌握支持工作者拥有法定权利及不可推卸的法律义务坚决拒绝接受委派的特定红线情形与专业沟通范式。

THE HEALTH CARE TEAM

医疗照护团队：跨学科全人协作的基石体系

In most health care settings, support workers work on a team. A team is a group of people who work together toward a common goal (see Chapter 1). The goal of a health care team is to provide the client with the best possible care and support.

在几乎所有现代医疗卫生执业环境中，支持工作者绝非单打独斗的孤勇者，而是深度嵌入在一个高度协同的团队中。团队（Team）是指为了一个崇高的共同目标而紧密携手的一群人（详见第1章）。现代医疗照护团队的最高神圣宗旨，便是为受助案主提供当前医学所能企及的最高品质照护与生命支持！

When providing care, team members must consider the whole client and promote health in all five dimensions of the client's life: physical, emotional, social, cognitive, and spiritual (see Chapter 6). Health care team members depend on each other to perform their roles to the best of their abilities. It is also important that all members of the team conduct themselves professionally at all times and use correct terminology when reporting observations about the client (see Chapter 25).

在开展一切照护活动时，团队成员必须时刻树立“全人”（Whole Person）视角，全面涵养案主生命五大维度的全人福祉：生理身体、情绪心理、社交联结、认知智能以及灵性信仰（详见第6章）。全体团队成员休戚与共、高度互信依赖，各自发挥极限潜能。全体成员必须在任何时刻恪守崇高的职业修养，并在汇报临床观察时严谨规范运用标准专业术语（详见第25章）。

Health Care Team Members

医疗团队成员构成与多元执业阵型
  • • Independent work with off-site supervisor: You may work alone in a client's private home, with your registered supervisor reachable immediately via mobile calls, secure video chat, or clinical messaging.
    独立执业与远程监护阵型：你作为唯一登门人员在案主私密居所中独立开展照护，而主管护士通过手机通话、实时视频连线或加密临床移动终端提供全天候即时指导支援。
  • • Two-person direct team: You and a single supervisor (nurse, PT, OT, social worker, or facility owner) may constitute the entire clinical presence at the shift.
    二人极简核心阵型：轮班现场仅由你与直接主管二人组成。主管可能是注册护士、物理治疗师、作业治疗师或机构主理人。
  • • Comprehensive multidisciplinary team: A large matrix comprising physicians, nurse practitioners, RNs, RPNs, support workers, dietitians, physiotherapists, occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists, and spiritual counsellors.
    大型综合跨学科矩阵阵型：由主治医师、执业护士（NP）、注册护士（RN）、注册执照实用护士（RPN）、支持工作者（PSW）、临床营养师、物理治疗师（PT）、作业治疗师（OT）、言语病理学家（SLP）及灵性关怀师共同筑起的钢铁矩阵！

Benefits of Working on a Team

团队协同执业的无可比拟优势

There are many benefits to the team approach in health care. A group of people is more effective at making correct decisions and solving problems than one person is. The many benefits of a team approach to care include the following: • Opportunities for collaboration.

现代医学证明：跨学科团队在做出重大临床决策与化解疑难重症时的精准度与安全性，是任何单个个体所根本无法企及的！

A team provides opportunities for people with different perspectives and skills to collaborate and share information. • Opportunities for communication. Team members communicate with each other continuously. • A wide range of expertise. Diverse professional backgrounds ensure that no aspect of the client's care is missed.

团队模式的核心优势涵盖：• 跨学科碰撞与协同（Collaboration）：为不同专业视角的专家搭建了实时会商共享的广阔平台；• 高频无死角的紧密沟通（Communication）：团队成员全天候不间断流动互通情报；• 涵盖全领域的渊博专科智慧（Wide Range of Expertise）：确保从机体病变、心理创伤到康复辅具的任何一个细枝末节都绝不会被遗漏！

Principles of Effective Teamwork

卓越高效团队协作的核心基本原则
  • • Good teamwork requires clear communication: Team members must communicate honestly, clearly, and concisely, keeping each other informed of client progress and unexpected changes.
    清晰无障碍的坦诚沟通：成员之间必须以绝对诚实、透明、精炼的方式交流，时刻让协作伙伴知晓长者的最新体征波动与意外变化。
  • • Good teamwork requires trust and respect: Members must genuinely value the unique role and indispensable contribution of every colleague, regardless of title.
    深厚的互信与职业敬意：发自内心地珍视并敬重每一位同事独特的执业价值，绝无任何职业等级偏见与轻视。
  • • Good teamwork respects role boundaries: In successful teams, each member understands their exact scope of practice and never performs tasks prohibited by law or policy.
    神圣恪守法定角色边界：全体成员深谙自身法定职责范围（Scope of Practice），坚决不逾越雷池一步，绝不擅自越权执行法律与机构明令禁止的操作！
  • • Good teamwork requires active participation: Every member attends team conferences, shares observations, and actively contributes to problem-solving.
    全员全心全意的主动融入：每位成员积极参与个案研讨会，勇于发表客观一线观察，为化解临床瓶颈群策群力。
  • • Good teamwork resolves conflict constructively: Disagreements are tackled immediately, privately, and professionally rather than simmering into toxic resentment.
    建设性当面化解人际摩擦：发生分歧争端时立即在私下以成熟专业的方式协商解决，绝不任由矛盾发酵毒化团队安全防线！

BOX 10.1: Contributions to a Team Meeting (Mrs. Darby Hip Rehabilitation)

专栏 10.1：多学科个案研讨会实战实录：达比太太髋关节术后康复评估
A health care team in a long-term care facility is meeting to discuss a client's rehabilitation following her hip surgery. Mrs. Darby is 86 years old. The team consists of Mrs. Darby, her daughter, a nurse, two support workers, a social worker, and a physiotherapist. At the previous meeting, the physiotherapist had suggested exercises to help Mrs. Darby regain mobility. Since then, the physiotherapist has shown Mrs. Darby how to do the exercises and a support worker has helped her practise the exercises. Mrs. Darby has chosen not to attend the team meeting. The following team members are at the meeting:
• The nurse, who is also the team leader
• The support worker (Meredith)
• The physiotherapist
• The social worker
• Mrs. Darby's daughter (Sandra)

TEAMS IN HEALTH CARE FACILITIES

医疗照护机构团队运作架构：三大主流护理分工交付模式

Teams in facilities vary depending on the setting and the nursing care delivery model used by the employer. The three common nursing care models in Canadian healthcare facilities are Functional Nursing, Team Nursing, and Primary Nursing:

在加拿大各类医院与长期照护机构中，照护团队的编组与运作深度依托雇主机构所采纳的特定护理模式。临床一线最为常见的三大经典模式涵盖：功能制护理、团队制护理以及责任制主导护理：

  • • Functional Nursing: Focuses on tasks and jobs. Each team member is assigned specific tasks for all clients. For example, one nurse administers all medications, another nurse does all wound treatments, and support workers do all bathing and vital signs. While organized and efficient, care can become fragmented, and the client may feel treated like a set of tasks rather than a whole person.
    功能制流水线护理（Functional Nursing）：以‘任务单元’为核心导向。全院每位员工负责所有案主的某一专属特定操作——例如一名护士包揽病区全部发药打针，另一名护士专职全病区伤口换药，而支持工作者专职承担全员擦浴与生命体征测量。该模式如工厂流水线般极其高效省时，但最大弊端在于全人照护严重碎片化割裂，案主往往觉得自己像是流水线上被摆弄的零件，极度缺乏情感归属。
  • • Team Nursing: A team of healthcare providers, led by a registered nurse (the team leader), provides comprehensive care to a specific group of clients. The team leader assigns tasks based on each worker's skills and the clients' changing needs. Team conferences are held daily to discuss care. This model fosters excellent collaboration and responsive, person-centred care.
    团队制协同护理（Team Nursing）：由注册护士担任团队领队（Team Leader），率领数名护士与支持工作者共同为特定一组案主（如某病区20位长者）提供全方位包干式全人照护。领队根据每位成员的实操专长动态分工，每日召开交接研讨会。该模式不仅协同作战能力卓越，更能给予长者极高的连续性与人性化关怀。
  • • Primary Nursing: A registered nurse (the primary nurse) is officially responsible for the client's total care plan 24 hours a day throughout their stay. When the primary nurse is off duty, associate nurses and support workers follow the care plan developed by the primary nurse. This model ensures the highest continuity of care.
    责任制主干护理（Primary Nursing）：由一名专职注册护士（Primary Nurse）24小时全权负责某一特定案主从入院至出院全周期的整体护理计划制定与效果把控。在责任护士轮休工歇期间，由协理护士与支持工作者严格依循责任护士确立的计划开展照护，确保了临床照护的最高水准连续性与归属感。

TEAMS IN COMMUNITY CARE

社区居家照护团队：跨部门协作与个案经理统筹枢纽

In community care, team members do not work under the same roof. Instead, clients receive home care services from a diverse network of community health providers coordinated by a case manager (or care coordinator). The case manager assesses the client's needs, develops the initial care plan, coordinates multidisciplinary services, monitors client stability, and evaluates outcomes.

与大型医疗机构全员集中在同一座大楼内办公截然不同，社区居家照护（Community Care）的团队成员分散在不同区域。受助长者在自家私密居所中所享受到的海量医疗与生活支持服务，全盘由一位专职社区个案经理（Case Manager / Care Coordinator）作为总调度长实施统筹调度。个案经理亲自登门评估长者的全人需求，主导起草初始照护计划，精准核定工时配额，协调护士、护工、康复师跨机构协作，并动态监控病情与照护质量考核。

Because support workers in home care work independently, they must maintain constant, accurate communication with the case manager and nursing supervisor. Any change in the client's physical health, cognitive status, home safety hazards, or family dynamics must be reported immediately.

由于社区居家支持工作者是常态化单兵作战深入长者家中，护工必须与个案经理及主管护士保持绝对畅通、高频且严谨的书面与电话沟通闭环！案主在血压血糖等生命体征上的任何异常波动、思维神志的突发谵妄迷乱、居家物理环境中的致命跌倒隐患、乃至家庭看护者的情绪崩溃危机，都必须在第一时间据实火速汇报！

CHALLENGES TO EFFECTIVE TEAMWORK

团队协作面临的核心挑战：人际摩擦化解与正向职业操守

Working on a team is not always easy. Challenges arise when team members have personality clashes, misaligned expectations, poor communication, or negative attitudes.

多学科团队的高效运转绝非自然而然形成。当成员之间爆发个性冲突、工作预期脱节、交接班沟通含糊不清、或个别人滋生消极怠工的负面情绪时，团队协作将面临严峻考验！

未被及时扑灭的深层人际矛盾，不仅会彻底毒化职场执业氛围，更会引发恶性医疗差错，直接将长者推向致命险境！当与同组同事爆发分歧争执时，支持工作者必须以高度成熟自律的专业姿态，严格依循专栏 10.2 黄金规程予以即时妥善化解。

BOX 10.2: How to Effectively Resolve a Conflict With a Co-Worker

专栏 10.2：专业高效化解同事职场人际冲突八大核心法则
1. Address concerns promptly: If there is a perceived issue, address your concern with your co-worker as soon as possible following the incident. Do not let resentment fester.
1. 趁热打铁，即时沟通：一旦现场感知到分歧摩擦或不当言行，在事发后尽可能第一时间主动与当事同事进行当面交谈，绝不任由怨气在内心滋生发酵。
2. Choose a private, neutral location: Arrange to speak in a private, neutral area away from clients, family members, or other staff members.
2. 择选私密中立空间：主动约定在一处绝对不受外人侵扰的私密中立场所（如无人会议室）当面会谈，坚决远离案主、家属或其他无关同事的视线。
3. Identify specific behaviours respectfully: Describe the objective behaviours that caused concern rather than attacking the person's character or competence.
3. 就事论事，严禁人格攻击：以温和客观的口吻精准指出令你困扰的“具体客观行为”，坚决杜绝否定对方的人格品行或职业操守！
4. Provide precise factual details: State exactly when, where, and what took place, sticking strictly to documented clinical facts.
4. 呈现详实精确的事实细节：清晰陈述事情发生的具体时间、具体病房及事件全貌，完全依凭客观事实说话。
5. Prepare written notes if necessary: Make brief notes beforehand to keep your discussion focused and avoid emotional rambling.
5. 提前列出提纲要点备忘：在谈话前简要列出核心诉求清单，确保交谈始终聚焦于核心矛盾，防止被瞬时情绪带偏。
6. Strategize collaborative solutions: Brainstorm together on practical ways to prevent the same friction or miscommunication from recurring in future shifts.
6. 共同研商建设性规避策略：双方平等协商，共同确立一套切实可行的交接协同新规，从机制上彻底根除同类矛盾再次发生的土壤。
7. Maintain strict confidentiality: Never discuss the details of this private conflict resolution meeting with any uninvolved co-workers.
7. 恪守绝对私密闭环天条：严禁在事后向任何第三方未卷入的同事八卦嚼舌头、散布会谈细节，坚决杜绝拉帮结派！
8. Involve a neutral supervisor if unresolved: If the discussion reaches an impasse or hostility continues, formally request a neutral third party (supervisor or union representative) to mediate.
8. 协商未果及时引入主管调解：倘若当面沟通陷入僵局或对方态度极其恶劣，必须正式书面提请中立第三方（排班主管护士或工会代表）介入主持专业调解。

ASSIGNING AND DELEGATING

工作指派与工作委派：法律权责边界与受控医疗行为法典

Assigning and delegating are not the same thing. Assigning means appointing someone to take on a task that is already within that worker's legal scope of practice and established job description. Delegation is a formal legal process by which a regulated nurse authorizes an unregulated care provider (such as a support worker) to perform a specific task that is normally beyond the support worker's legal scope of practice-including controlled acts.

在医疗法学界，工作指派（Assigning）与工作委派（Delegation）存在着本质的法理分水岭！日常指派是指主管安排某位员工去完成一项‘本就属于该岗位法定执业范畴（Scope of Practice）与岗位说明书内’的常规照护任务（如擦浴、协助如厕）。而工作委派则是一项极为严肃的法定程序：是指受法律严格监管的注册护士，通过特定法定豁免机制，将其自身专属排他性执业权限内、原本完全超越支持工作者法定执业权限的高风险操作——包括法定受控医疗操作（Controlled Acts），正式授权转让给某位特定非受监管人员（如 PSW）执行！

Controlled Acts (Authorized Reserved Tasks)

法定受控医疗行为（安省 Controlled Acts / 加拿大各省明细）

In Canada, provincial legislation (such as Ontario's Regulated Health Professions Act [RHPA] and provincial Nursing Acts) designates certain high-risk medical procedures as controlled acts. Controlled acts are procedures that carry an inherent risk of serious harm or death if performed by untrained or unauthorized individuals. Unregulated workers are strictly prohibited from performing controlled acts UNLESS the procedure has been formally and legally delegated by a licensed nurse.

在加拿大各省法典中（如安大略省《受监管健康专业法案》RHPA 与《护士执业法案》），法律以不容置疑的条文将一系列极高危医疗操作锁定为‘法定受控行为’（Controlled Acts）。法律严正裁定：此类操作一旦由未经法务授权或技术不达标的闲杂人员擅自实施，极易诱发致命感染、大出血、器官穿孔乃至当场猝死！因此，非受监管工作者在常规状态下绝对禁止触碰受控操作，除非且仅当该项操作经过了执业护士合法、规范、逐案的法定委派！

BOX 10.3: Delegation in British Columbia, Alberta, Nova Scotia, and Ontario

专栏 10.3：加拿大四大省份工作委派立法对比与排他性受控操作清单
Provincial Terminology:
• In British Columbia: Authorized nursing tasks are called 'reserved acts,' and the delegation process is officially designated as 'transfer of function.'
• In Alberta: These high-risk tasks are officially classified as 'restricted activities.'
• In Ontario and Nova Scotia: They are governed under legislation as 'controlled acts.'
【加拿大各省法典专业术语对照】：
• 不列颠哥伦比亚省（BC省）：护士法定排他性操作被称为‘保留医疗行为’（Reserved Acts），而委派过程在法律上正式定名为‘职能转让’（Transfer of Function）；
• 阿尔伯塔省（AB省）：高风险专科医疗操作在法典中被称为‘限制性活动’（Restricted Activities）；
• 安大略省（ON省）与新斯科舍省（NS省）：在省级医疗法案中统一定名为‘受控医疗行为’（Controlled Acts）。
Tasks Authorized ONLY to Nurses (Strictly Prohibited to Support Workers Unless Delegated):
1. Performing a procedure below the dermis or mucous membrane (e.g., sterile dressing changes, deep wound irrigation, skin staging).
2. Administering a substance by injection or inhalation (e.g., subcutaneous insulin injections, intramuscular medications, aerosolized nebulizers).
3. Inserting an instrument, hand, or finger into a client's body openings, including bladder, esophagus, trachea, rectum, or surgically created stomas (e.g., Foley catheterization, rectal suppository insertion, deep tracheostomy suctioning).
【仅限注册护士执行、支持工作者常规绝对禁止触碰的三大排他性受控行为】：
1. 刺入真皮层或黏膜之下的侵入性操作（如深度开放性压疮换药清创、无菌创面冲洗、组织切除）；
2. 经皮注射或高阶吸入给药（如皮下胰岛素注射、肌内疫苗注射、高危雾化药物给药）；
3. 将器械、双手或手指插入案主人体自然孔道或人工造口孔道（包括膀胱导尿管插入、胃管置入、气管切开深部吸痰、直肠给栓剂）。
CRITICAL LEGAL PRINCIPLE: Non-Transferability of Delegated Tasks. In Canada, a delegated task is legally client-specific. If Nurse Sarah delegates and trains you to perform an enema or dressing change for Mr. Lau, you CANNOT legally perform that same procedure on another client, Mr. Davis! Every new client requires a separate assessment, specific training, and fresh delegation authorization.
【加拿大医疗法务不可动摇的最高铁律】：工作委派的‘绝对非可转让性’（Non-Transferability）！在加拿大全境，任何一项委派在法律上皆是且仅是‘特定案主专属’！即便主管护士萨拉手把手培训并考核授权你为刘先生执行深部开塞露灌肠或造口换药，你绝对不能、在任何情况下擅自将该项操作直接套用到隔壁同样需要灌肠的戴维斯先生身上！面对每一位全新案主，护士必须重新进行个案评估、重新对手把手指导考核、并重新签署全新的专属委派法律文书！

The Five Rights of Delegation (CNO Professional Standard)

护士工作委派决策的五大正确黄金法则（CNO 法定执业准则）
  • • 1. Right Task: The task must be one that can be safely delegated under provincial nursing legislation and agency policy.
    1. 正确的任务（Right Task）：该项操作在省护理立法及雇主书面执业政策框架下，属于法定明确允许由非受监管人员承担的范畴。
  • • 2. Right Circumstances: The client's clinical condition must be stable and predictable. Never delegate tasks for an unstable client.
    2. 正确的情境（Right Circumstances）：案主的临床病情必须高度平稳、体征完全可预测！面对病情突发剧烈波动、处于恶化边缘的危重长者，绝对严禁任何委派！
  • • 3. Right Person: The support worker must possess the verified training, manual competence, and confidence to perform the task safely.
    3. 正确的被委派人员（Right Person）：所选定的支持工作者必须经过实操培训、技术考核通关、具备绝对胜任力与充分自信。
  • • 4. Right Direction and Communication: The nurse must provide clear, concise, step-by-step written and verbal instructions and define limits.
    4. 正确的指导与沟通（Right Direction and Communication）：护士必须下达详尽透彻的书面与口头步骤指引，明确界定异常体征红线与汇报要求。
  • • 5. Right Supervision: The nurse must provide appropriate ongoing monitoring, remain reachable for assistance, and evaluate the final outcome.
    5. 正确的监护与督导（Right Supervision）：护士必须全程实施动态监护追踪，在发生意外时随叫随到提供急救支援，并对最终照护成效正式验收签字。

BOX 10.4: Factors Affecting Delegation Decisions for the Nurse

专栏 10.4：注册护士做出委派决断前必须审慎考量的五大核心要素
1. Is it appropriate that a support worker perform this task considering the client's condition, associated risks, and environmental supports?
1. 综合案主当前病情、潜在医疗风险及现场应急抢救支撑，委派给护工执行是否完全适宜？
2. Will the support worker be performing the task frequently enough to maintain competence?
2. 该名护工在未来的排班中是否有足够高的操作频次来维持该项特殊手艺的熟练度与安全性？
3. Can the support worker be adequately supervised in the setting?
3. 在当前的具体执业环境（机构或居家）中，护士能否提供法理要求的充分动态督导与监护？
4. Is a nurse readily available to help if the client's condition changes or problems arise?
4. 一旦长者病情突发危急恶化或操作现场出现崩盘事故，现场是否有资质完备的护士随叫随到提供急救？
5. Is this a task that can be delegated to a support worker, and is it included in her job description?
5. 该项操作在雇主机构的正式岗位职责说明书中是否被正式列入允许委派的操作目录？
Source: College of Nurses of Ontario (CNO). Practice Guideline: Working with Unregulated Care Providers.
【法定准则溯源】：安大略省护士学会（CNO）《与非受监管照护人员协作执业法定指引》（文件编号 41014）。

BOX 10.5: Should You Accept the Delegation? Support Worker's Safety Checklist

专栏 10.5：支持工作者在接手委派前必须灵魂自问的八大安全关卡
1. Is this task included in my job description and permitted under my agency's written policies?
关卡一：该操作在我的正式岗位职责说明书与机构书面制度中是否白纸黑字明确允许？
2. Is this task within my legal scope of practice?
关卡二：该操作是否完全处于我所在省份法律所界定的法定照护范畴之内？
3. Has the nurse personally taught and evaluated me on this specific client?
关卡三：主管护士是否已经针对‘这位特定案主’对我进行了面对面手把手培训并考核通关？
4. Am I aware of the client's usual baseline response to this procedure?
关卡四：我是否清楚掌握长者平日里接受该操作时的基线生理体征（以便比对今日是否异常）？
5. Is the client's current clinical condition stable and predictable?
关卡五：长者此时此刻的病情是否绝对平稳、呼吸心率血压是否完全处于安全阈值内？
6. Am I fully aware of all clinical risks and potential complications of this procedure?
关卡六：我是否详尽熟知该操作可能引发的全部严重并发症（如出血、穿孔、休克预警）？
7. Has the client (or substitute decision-maker) given informed consent for me to perform it?
关卡七：案主本人或其法定代理人是否对由我（而非护士）亲自操作签署了知情同意？
8. Do I feel 100% confident and competent, and is a nurse immediately accessible for assistance?
关卡八：我内心是否有绝对的把握与胜任底气？一旦现场突发意外，主管护士是否能随叫随到？

The Process of Delegation (Five Systematic Stages)

工作委派法定程序五大严密闭环阶段（图 10.2 权威解析）
  • • Stage 1: Assessment of Needs. The nurse assesses the client's condition, needs, and associated risks. The nurse determines if delegation is safe, legal, and clinically appropriate.
    第一阶段：全人需求与风险评估（Assessment of Needs）：注册护士亲自登门全面评估案主的生理病理指标与环境风险，严格确认该操作在法律与临床医学上是否适宜委派。
  • • Stage 2: Planning and Communication. The nurse and support worker discuss the task. The nurse explains the purpose, steps, and expected outcomes, and the support worker agrees to perform it after proper training.
    第二阶段：计划拟定与契约沟通（Planning & Communication）：护士与护工碰头深度会商，护士逐项阐述操作目的、规范动作与预期生理反应，护工在确认经过正规培训后签署同意。
  • • Stage 3: Teaching and Competency Demonstration. The nurse personally instructs and demonstrates the procedure for this specific client. The support worker performs return demonstrations until the nurse is fully satisfied with competency.
    第三阶段：手把手带教与考核通关（Teaching & Competency Demonstration）：护士针对该名特定案主，手把手进行床旁操作示范。护工进行回演示（Return Demonstration），直至护士完全确认其技术完美达标！
  • • Stage 4: Guidance, Assistance, and Ongoing Supervision. The nurse supervises the support worker, monitors care delivery, and remains readily available to intervene if complications or unexpected reactions arise.
    第四阶段：现场督导与动态支援（Supervision & Assistance）：在日常执行中，护士实施定期抽查与动态监护，在案主出现异常生命体征时随叫随到，提供即时技术指导与抢救支援。
  • • Stage 5: Follow-Up, Feedback, and Evaluation. The nurse collects clinical data, evaluates resident outcomes, provides feedback to the worker, and decides whether delegation should continue, be modified, or be terminated.
    第五阶段：复盘考核、反馈与成效验收（Evaluation & Feedback）：护士全面采集伤口或体征数据，考核案主康复成效，向护工反馈改进建议，并最终做出维持、微调或依法终止委派的决断。

Your Responsibilities When Performing Delegated Tasks

支持工作者在执行委派任务时的不可推卸法定义务
  • • Perform the task exactly as taught: Never cut corners, omit steps, or alter the delegated technique.
    分毫不差严格执行：严禁擅自删减步骤、凭想当然走捷径或篡改护士传授的标准化操作规程！
  • • Never perform a delegated task on another client: Delegation is client-specific; performing it on another client is an illegal controlled act.
    绝对专属，严禁套用：绝不能擅自为其他案主执行该操作！任何跨案主擅自操作在法律上属于违法执业重罪！
  • • Observe and report immediately: Monitor the client before, during, and after the task. Report any adverse reactions or unexpected changes to the nurse without delay.
    全程密切监护与即刻汇报：在操作前、操作中与操作后时刻审视长者反应，一旦发现剧痛、出血、发绀等异常必须立即停手火速汇报！
  • • Accurately document: Record what you did, the client's tolerance, and the exact time in the client's official record.
    严谨如实书面记录：准确记录执行的精准时间点、具体操作部位、案主的耐受程度及体征数据，签字归档。
  • • Know when to ask for help: If you ever feel uncertain, hesitation, or encounter resistance, stop and contact the nurse immediately.
    心怀敬畏，随时求援：一旦内心产生一丝一毫的不确定、心虚慌乱或遭遇案主抗拒，必须立即暂停并向主管护士求援！

REFUSING A TASK

坚决拒绝接受委派：支持工作者的法定权利、职业操守与人权底线

You have the right to say no. If you have a good reason for not doing a task, refusing to do it is not only your right-it is your duty!

在特定法定情形下，你拥有绝对的权利说‘不’！如果你拥有正当充分的专业法理理由，拒绝执行某项委派不仅是你的法定权利，更是你作为医护人员不可推卸的神圣法律与道德义务！

You have a legal and ethical duty to protect clients from harm. By using common sense and answering the questions in BOX 10.5, you protect your clients as well as yourself. You must NEVER refuse a task simply because you do not want to do it or dislike the client; refusing without just cause can be considered resident neglect or insubordination.

你的首要天职是依法保护弱势案主免遭人身重创！严谨运用专栏 10.5 关卡作为准绳，是对长者生命与你个人执业资格的双重崇高守护。必须极度严肃指出：支持工作者绝不能、在任何情况下仅仅因为‘嫌脏嫌累不想干’或‘讨厌某位长者’而任性拒不执行照护！无故擅自拒绝合理常规指派，在法律上属于触目惊心的渎职虐待（Neglect）与对抗管理（Insubordination），将直接导致开除！

Valid Grounds for Refusing a Delegated Task

支持工作者必须坚决拒绝（Must Refuse）委派的六大法定情形
  • • 1. The task is beyond the legal limits of your role or violates provincial legislation or agency policy.
    情形一：该项操作明显公然违反省卫生立法与护理法规，或违背雇主机构的书面执业制度政策；
  • • 2. The task is not in your official job description and has not been legally delegated.
    情形二：该项任务完全未包含在你的岗位职责说明书中，且此前未履行过任何合法委派授权流程；
  • • 3. You have not been trained to do the task on this specific client.
    情形三：主管护士此前从未针对‘眼前这位特定案主’对你进行过手把手面对面的培训与实操考核；
  • • 4. You were trained, but you have not performed the task in a long time and do not feel competent.
    情形四：虽然过去曾接受过培训，但由于长期未曾上手操作，你感到手生心慌，无法确保百分之百安全无虞；
  • • 5. The client's condition has changed, is unstable, or is deteriorating.
    情形五：案主病情突发意外剧烈波动、生命体征极不稳定或处于恶化边缘，不再满足委派的前提条件；
  • • 6. A nurse is not available to supervise, guide, or intervene if an emergency occurs.
    情形六：现场没有任何具有资质的护士能够提供实时监护，一旦突发意外大出血将陷入求助无门的绝境！

How to Refuse Professionally

专业坚定的拒绝沟通范式（典范回应）

When refusing a task, do not be angry, hostile, or defensive. Speak calmly, respectfully, and clearly explain the exact professional and legal reason for your refusal. For example: 'Nurse Anderson, I cannot change Mrs. Kowalski's surgical dressing.

在行使法定权利拒绝任务时，切忌态度恶劣、情绪激动或冷冰冰地甩下一句‘这不关我的事’！必须保持从容、平和、坚定的专业姿态，清晰陈述无法执行的客观法理依据。典范专业陈述：‘安德森护士长，我无法为科瓦尔斯基太太更换手术深部无菌敷料。

According to CNO guidelines and our agency policy, sterile dressing change is a controlled act, and I have not been officially delegated and trained for this procedure on Mrs. Kowalski. While you perform her dressing, I would be happy to assist Mr. Smith with his morning care to save you time.' This response shows professional accountability and collaborative teamwork.

因为根据安省护士学会（CNO）执业准则与我们机构的规章，无菌换药属于法定受控操作，我此前尚未接受针对科瓦尔斯基太太的专属委派与培训考核。在您亲自为她换药期间，我很乐意先协助隔壁史密斯先生完成晨间洗漱与进餐，好为您节省宝贵时间！’这一回应不仅彰显了坚如磐石的法治责任担当，更体现了兼济大局的高尚团队精神！

CHAPTER REVIEW: KEY POINTS

第10章核心知识点精要回顾
  • • A health care team is a group of providers with different skills who work together toward a common goal: providing quality, person-centred care.
    医疗照护团队是由具备多元技能的跨学科专业人员构成的协作组织，共同以提供最高品质的全人化照护为最高宗旨。
  • • Team members depend on each other and must communicate clearly, honestly, and respectfully at all times.
    团队成员彼此高度依存，在任何时刻都必须秉持清晰、诚实、透明且充满敬意的沟通操守。
  • • Support workers spend the most direct time with clients, making their documented observations critical for care planning.
    支持工作者占据着与长者直接接触的最长时长，其基于事实的书面床旁观察是多学科制定与调整护理计划的至高指南针。
  • • Common care delivery models in facilities include functional nursing, team nursing, and primary nursing.
    机构内主流照护分工交付模型涵盖功能制流水线护理、团队制协同护理以及责任制主干护理。
  • • In community care, the case manager assesses needs, coordinates services, and monitors client stability.
    在社区居家照护体系中，个案经理统筹承担需求评估、资源调度与长者稳定性全周期监护职能。
  • • Assigning is distributing tasks within a worker's existing scope of practice; delegation authorizes an unregulated worker to perform a task normally restricted to nurses.
    日常指派属于法定岗位职责内的常规分工；工作委派则是护士将其专属高风险或受控操作正式转让予非受监管人员的严肃法律行为。
  • • Controlled acts (reserved acts / restricted activities) are high-risk medical procedures restricted by law to regulated professionals.
    法定受控行为（保留医疗行为/限制性活动）是法律严格限定仅能由受监管专业人员实施的高危医疗操作。
  • • Delegation is strictly client-specific and non-transferable across clients.
    工作委派在法律上具有绝对的案主专属性，严禁擅自跨案主转让套用！
  • • The nurse must follow the Five Rights of Delegation: Right Task, Circumstances, Person, Direction/Communication, and Supervision.
    注册护士做出委派必须严格坚守五大正确黄金原则：正确的任务、情境、人员、指导沟通与动态督导。
  • • The support worker must evaluate their competence using the safety checklist in BOX 10.5 before accepting delegation.
    支持工作者在接手任何委派前，必须严格依凭专栏 10.5 八大关卡实施灵魂自审与安全校验。
  • • Support workers have the legal right and duty to refuse a delegated task if client safety or legal boundaries are compromised.
    当案主生命安全面临威胁或逾越法定边界时，支持工作者拥有不可推卸的法定权利与职业义务坚决拒绝接受委派！
  • • Refusing must be done professionally, respectfully, and based on objective legal and clinical grounds.
    拒绝必须秉持温和、坚定、专业的姿态，清晰陈述客观法理事实，绝不意气用事。

CRITICAL THINKING IN PRACTICE

临床批判性思维修炼：蒋先生居家换药请求与跨案主委派法律红线

You are assisting Mr. Chiang with personal care in his home. Mrs. Chiang asks you to change her husband's surgical dressing, stating, 'You do this every day in the hospital, and I don't want to wait for the home care nurse.' Nurses have delegated dressing changes to you for other clients in the past. What should you do?

【临床高阶困境推演】：你在社区案主蒋先生家中为其提供晨间日常生活照护。蒋太太神色焦急地恳求你顺手帮她丈夫更换腹部手术深部敷料，并催促道：‘你们护工在医院里天天给病人换药，手法肯定熟得很！社区换药护士每次都要等大半天，你现在顺便换一下，省得我们干等了！

Can you change Mr. Chiang's dressing? What legal and ethical consequences could occur if you perform this task?

’此前在其他案主身上，确实曾有主管护士正式委派并培训过你执行无菌换药。面对家属的苦苦恳求，你此刻能否答应为蒋先生换药？倘若你心存侥幸‘顺手帮了个忙’，在法律和伦理上将引发何等触目惊心的灾难性后果？

Clinical Practice Analysis: 1. Absolute Legal Prohibition: You CANNOT change Mr. Chiang's dressing! In Canada, delegation is strictly client-specific and non-transferable. The fact that a nurse delegated wound care to you for a previous client gives you ZERO legal authority to touch Mr. Chiang's wound.

【支持工作者高阶实操指引】：1. 法律绝对禁令：你绝对不能、在任何情况下擅自为蒋先生换药！在加拿大医疗法典下，工作委派具有不可动摇的案主专属性！此前其他护士针对其他案主赋予你的换药资质，在蒋先生身上在法律上等于零！2. 违法渎职后果：未经委派擅自触碰深部手术伤口属于非法行医（Illegal Practice of Nursing）、严重职业渎职！

2. Legal Consequences: Performing an unauthorized controlled act constitutes the illegal practice of medicine/nursing without a licence, gross professional misconduct, immediate termination of employment, and civil/criminal liability if Mr. Chiang develops a surgical site infection or hemorrhage. 3. Professional Action Plan: Politely and firmly explain the law to Mrs. Chiang: 'Mrs. Chiang, I care deeply about Mr. Chiang's safety.

一旦创面诱发金黄色葡萄球菌重度感染或大出血，你不仅将被立即开除、永久吊销执业文凭，更将面临巨额民事赔偿与过失伤人刑事指控！3. 典范实操应对：温和坚定地向家属普及法理：‘蒋太太，我万分理解您心疼蒋先生、不想漫长等待的心情。但在安省法律下，深部伤口换药属于法定受控医疗操作。

In Ontario, changing a surgical dressing is a controlled medical act. By law, a nurse must perform an individual clinical assessment and officially delegate that procedure for Mr. Chiang before I am permitted to do it. Performing it without authorization would put Mr. Chiang at serious risk of infection. I will call the home care nursing agency right now to check the nurse's arrival time.'

法律严格规定，必须由主管护士亲自对蒋先生的伤口愈合与感染指标进行个案评估、并正式签署委派文书后，我方可操作。我现在贸然动手，一旦引发伤口内部化脓大出血，后果不堪设想！我现在立即掏出手机致电社区护士站，为您核实换药护士的精准抵达时间！’
