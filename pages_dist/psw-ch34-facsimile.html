The Senses of Sight and Hearing

视觉与听觉感官的重要性
The senses of sight and hearing are important for communicating, learning, moving about, and performing activities of daily living (ADLs). They also help keep us safe by alerting us to danger. People who are dying are believed to be able to hear up until their death.
视觉与听觉感官对于人际沟通、知识学习、空间移动以及执行日常生活活动（ADL）至关重要。它们还能通过及时警示危险来保障人身安全。在临终关怀中，普遍认为听觉是濒死阶段最后消逝的感觉，服务对象在临终前直至死亡通常仍能保持听力。
Hearing and vision challenges are common among all age groups. Common causes include conditions present at birth, diseases, and accidents. Some hearing and vision impairment (and loss of other senses) is a natural part of aging. As a support worker, you will care for many clients with hearing and vision disorders. Some clients may have minor disorders that can be corrected with hearing aids and eyeglasses, whereas others may have severe impairments.
听力与视力问题在各个年龄段均十分常见。常见病因包括先天性畸形、器质性疾病以及外伤事故。一定程度的听力和视力减退（以及其他感觉的减退）也是机体衰老的自然生理组成部分。作为支持工作者，您将照料许多患有感官障碍的服务对象；部分对象的轻度减退可通过助听器和眼镜有效矫正，而另一些对象则可能存在重度永久性残障。

Ear Disorders and Hearing Impairment

耳部疾患与听力受损概述
The ear is needed for hearing and balance. Ear disorders range from slight hearing impairments to complete deafness. The most common causes of hearing impairment are aging and prolonged exposure to loud noise. However, hearing impairment can also result from a number of other things, including an accident, illness, exposure to certain drugs or chemicals. In some cases, genetics are responsible for causing impairment at birth.
耳朵是人体掌管听觉感知与身体平衡调节的关键器官。耳部病变的严重程度跨度很大，从轻微听力下降直到全聋。导致听力受损最常见的原因是生理衰老和长期暴露于高分贝强噪音环境中；此外，意外创伤、感染性疾病、接触特定耳毒性药物或化学工业毒物亦可诱发听障；某些情况下遗传因素则导致先天性听力缺损。
Hearing impairments may occur suddenly, but they are usually gradual in onset. To prevent hearing impairment, there needs to be more public awareness that ear protection should be used when using any loud machinery, even lawn mowers. Of greater concern are the consequences of children, teens, and young adults listening to music that is too loud—for example, blasting the music on car stereos or in their earphones—which can lead to future hearing impairment. Hearing impairment may affect one or both ears.
听力障碍有时可突发出现，但更多呈隐匿性缓慢渐进发展。为预防听力受损，公众亟需提高防范意识，在操作割草机等高噪音机械设备时应佩戴护耳隔音耳罩。更令人担忧的是儿童、青少年及年轻人群长期以过大音量收听音乐（例如车内音响震耳欲聋或佩戴入耳式耳机音量过高），这会导致不可逆的内耳毛细胞损伤与未来永久性听障。听力损害可波及单耳或双耳。

Otitis Media (Middle Ear Infection)

中耳炎（中耳感染）
Otitis media—infection (itis) of the middle (media) ear (ot)—is common in infants and children and may be acute or chronic. Normally, the ear's Eustachian tube allows fresh air to enter the middle ear and fluids to drain from it. However, a cold or flu, large adenoids or tonsils, or an allergy such as hay fever can block the Eustachian tube, causing otitis media. In very young children, symptoms like irritability, poor appetite, or ear pain may be mistaken for teething or post-immunization fever, allowing otitis media to go undetected. In chronic otitis media, fluid builds up slowly, thickens, and can damage the eardrum and ossicles, resulting in permanent hearing loss.
中耳炎（Otitis Media）即中耳腔黏膜的感染性炎症，在婴幼儿及儿童中高发，可分为急性与慢性。正常情况下，咽鼓管负责使空气进入中耳并排出渗出液体；然而感冒流感、腺样体肥大或花粉过敏常阻塞咽鼓管诱发感染。在婴幼儿中，烦躁哭闹、拒食或抓耳等症状常被误认为出牙期不适或疫苗接种发热而漏诊。慢性中耳炎积液黏稠积聚，可破坏鼓膜及听小骨链，遗留永久性传导性听力丧失。

Otosclerosis and Stapedectomy

耳硬化症与镫骨手术切除术
Otosclerosis is a hereditary condition in which abnormal bone growth causes hardening (sclero) and fixation of the ossicles in the middle ear (oto), especially the stapes (stirrup bone). The fixed bone can no longer vibrate to transmit sound waves to the inner ear, leading to progressive conductive hearing loss. It is more common in women and typically begins in early adulthood. Treatment often involves a surgical procedure called a stapedectomy, where the diseased stapes is replaced with a tiny wire-and-plastic synthetic prosthesis.
耳硬化症（Otosclerosis）是一种常染色体显性遗传性疾病，中耳听小骨（尤其是镫骨底板）发生海绵状骨质增生与骨化硬结，使原本活动的听小骨固定不能震动，阻断了声波向内耳淋巴液的传导，产生渐进性传导性耳聋。该病在青年女性中多发。临床根治性治疗通常采用镫骨切除术（Stapedectomy），用微型人造合成假体替换硬化固定的镫骨以重建听骨链传导。

Ménière's Disease

美尼尔病（梅尼埃病）：内耳膜迷路水肿
Ménière's disease is a chronic disorder of the inner ear characterized by fluid accumulation (endolymphatic hydrops) that affects both hearing and balance. Major symptoms include episodic severe vertigo (whirling dizziness that can cause falling), tinnitus (roaring or ringing sounds in the ear), fluctuating sensorineural hearing loss, and a feeling of fullness or pressure in the affected ear. Attacks can last from 20 minutes to 24 hours, often accompanied by severe nausea and vomiting. Care involves bed rest during acute attacks, anti-vertigo medications, and a low-sodium diet to reduce fluid retention.
美尼尔病（Ménière's Disease）是一种内耳特发性疾病，以膜迷路内淋巴积水为特征，同时损害听觉与前庭平衡功能。核心临床三联征包括突发剧烈旋转性眩晕（天旋地转极易跌倒）、耳鸣（轰鸣或刺耳鸣响）、波动性感音神经性听力下降以及患侧耳部明显的闷胀压迫感。急性发作可持续20分钟至24小时不等，常伴剧烈恶心呕吐。照护原则包括急性期绝对卧床休息、遵医嘱给予抗眩晕止吐药，并执行严格低钠饮食以减轻内耳体液潴留。

Presbycusis, Tinnitus & Cerumen Impaction

老年性耳聋、耳鸣与耵聍栓塞
Presbycusis is the gradual, symmetrical sensorineural hearing loss associated with normal aging. It begins around age 50 and typically impairs high-frequency sounds first, making it difficult for older adults to distinguish consonants (such as s, f, th) and understand speech in noisy backgrounds. Tinnitus is the perception of ringing, hissing, buzzing, or roaring in the ears without an external sound source. Cerumen (earwax) impaction occurs when earwax becomes hard and completely blocks the ear canal, causing sudden conductive hearing loss; clients should never insert cotton swabs or objects into the ear canal, as wax must be safely flushed or removed by a healthcare professional.
老年性耳聋（Presbycusis）是伴随机体自然衰老发生的双侧对称性、渐进性感音神经性听力衰退。通常自50岁左右开始，最先累及高频听力，使老年人极难分辨高频辅音（如s、f、th等），在嘈杂背景中言语分辨能力显著受损。耳鸣（Tinnitus）是指无外界声源时主观感知到的耳内鸣响、嘶嘶声或嗡嗡声。耵聍栓塞（Cerumen Impaction）指耳垢硬化完全阻塞外耳道引起的传导性听力减退；切记严禁服务对象自行用棉签或发卡挖耳以防刺破鼓膜，必须由医生或专业护士进行安全冲洗软化吸出。

助听器工作机制与日常照料红线
A hearing aid is a precision electronic device that fits inside the ear, behind the ear, or in the ear canal to amplify sounds (FIGURE 34.1). A hearing aid does not cure hearing loss or restore normal hearing—it makes sounds louder, but NOT necessarily clearer. Background noise is amplified along with voices, so support workers must minimize ambient environmental noise (such as shutting off TVs or closing doors) to assist the wearer in communication adjustment.

Modern digital aids can pair with smartphones via Bluetooth to stream phone calls, television audio, and music directly into the ear.
每只现代专业助听器的价格高达数千加元（甚至可达 5,000 美元/加元）。不慎将其摔落在大理石或硬地板上极易震碎内部微型电子元件，给服务对象及其家庭造成沉重经济负担。支持工作者在拿取时必须轻拿轻放；确保助听器机身及收纳盒上明确标有服务对象姓名。现代数字助听器支持蓝牙与智能手机连接，可将电话通话音、电视伴音及音乐无线流式传输直接送入耳内。

Cochlear Implants: Sound Transmission Biology

人工耳蜗（电子耳蜗）：跨越受损毛细胞的仿生听觉传导
A cochlear implant is an advanced electronic medical device for individuals with severe-to-profound sensorineural hearing loss who derive little or no benefit from traditional acoustic hearing aids. It consists of two parts: an external sound processor worn behind the ear, and an internal receiver-stimulator surgically embedded under the scalp with an electrode array threaded into the cochlea of the inner ear.
人工耳蜗（Cochlear Implant）是针对佩戴传统声学助听器无效或收效甚微的重度至极重度感音神经性聋人群的高科技医疗植入系统。它由两部分组成：耳外佩戴的声音言语处理器，以及经显微外科手术植入头皮下并穿入内耳耳蜗鼓阶的微电极阵列。
  • • 1. The external sound processor captures acoustic sounds via microphones and filters acoustic frequencies.
    1. 外部声音处理器通过麦克风捕获外界声学声波并执行频谱过滤分析。
  • • 2. The processor translates filtered sounds into coded digital information and radio-transmits it across the skin to the implant.
    2. 处理器将声音转化为经过编码的数字信息，通过射频线圈经皮无创发送给皮下植入体。
  • • 3. The internal implant decodes the digital radio signal into electrical current pulses and directs them to the electrode array inside the cochlea.
    3. 体内植入芯片将数字信号解码为微弱电脉冲，精准分派至耳蜗内的微型电极束。

    4. 电极脉冲直接刺激听神经节细胞，彻底跨越了坏死的内耳毛细胞，直接将听觉神经冲动传入大脑皮层产生听觉。

Auditory Processing Disorder (APD)

听觉处理障碍（APD）：听力阈值正常与大脑解码脱节
Children or adults labelled 'poor listeners' may possess perfectly normal peripheral hearing sensitivity on audiograms, yet experience a profound disconnect between what their ears detect and what their brain comprehends—this neurological deficit is known as Auditory Processing Disorder (APD). The central auditory nervous system distorts or delays sound processing, making it immensely exhausting to follow multi-step spoken instructions, understand conversation in background noise, or retain verbal memories. Specialized multidisciplinary therapy, FM listening systems, and quiet learning environments provide critical accommodations.
经常被误贴上‘心不在焉、不专心听讲’标签的儿童或成人，其纯音测听显示的周围耳部听阈可能完全处于正常范围，但在耳部感知声音与大脑中枢语义解码之间存在严重的病理脱节——这被称为听觉处理障碍（APD）。患者的中枢听觉神经系统在解析声音时出现处理延迟、频谱失真或信息丢失，导致其在嘈杂环境下极难听懂多步骤口头指令、学习记忆言语极其困难。需借助听觉训练、个人调频（FM）无线辅听系统及安静环境予以全面支持。

Psychological and Social Impact of Hearing Impairment

听力受损的深层心理创伤与社会隔离
Because hearing loss is an invisible disability, its emotional toll is frequently underappreciated. Clients struggling to hear experience profound fatigue from constant strain, embarrassment over inappropriate responses, fear of being perceived as cognitively declined, and frustration from asking others to repeat. Over time, many withdraw completely from social interactions, meals, and religious gatherings, entering devastating social isolation and clinical depression. Empathetic, respectful support is essential.
由于听力受损是一种隐性无形障碍，其给当事人造成的心理精神创伤往往被外界严重低估。听障人士整日处于费力倾听的极度疲惫之中，常因答非所问而深感羞愧，害怕被他人误解为‘智力衰退或老年痴呆’，更因不断恳求他人重复而充满挫败。随着时间推移，许多服务对象会彻底放弃家庭聚餐、朋友聚会和宗教活动，陷入深度的孤独退缩与重度抑郁。支持工作者必须给予全人共情与有尊严的扶持。

Eye Disorders and Vision Impairment Overview

眼睛结构功能、视力障碍分级与结膜炎
Vision loss ranges from mild refractive errors to total blindness (inability to perceive light). Legal blindness in Canada is defined as visual acuity of 20/200 (6/60) or less in the better eye with best possible correction, or a visual field of less than 20 degrees. While a person with normal vision sees an object clearly at 60 metres (200 feet), a legally blind person must be at 6 metres (20 feet) to discern that same object. Conjunctivitis (pink eye) is inflammation of the clear membrane lining eyelids and sclera, presenting with intense redness, itching, and purulent discharge requiring strict Contact Precautions.
视力损害的跨度从轻微屈光不正直至全盲（毫无光感）。在加拿大医学与法律界，法定盲（Legal Blindness）被严格界定为：在配戴最佳矫正镜片后，视力较好的一侧眼睛最佳视力仍等于或低于 20/200（6/60 米），或周边视野缺损缩小至 20 度以内。正常视力者在 60 米（200 英尺）远能看清的物体，法定盲患者必须走近至 6 米（20 英尺）方能辨认。结膜炎（俗称红眼病，Pink Eye）是覆盖眼白及眼睑内面的结膜发生感染性炎症，表现为眼红、奇痒及脓性分泌物，极具接触传染性，必须严格执行接触隔离防护。

Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD): Wet vs Dry

年龄相关性黄斑变性（AMD）：湿性与干性分型病理
The macula is the central, highly sensitive area of the retina responsible for sharp, central, high-resolution colour vision. Age-related macular degeneration (AMD) is the breakdown of the macula and stands as the leading cause of blindness in North America among adults over 55. AMD destroys central vision while preserving peripheral (side) vision: clients can see the frame of a doorway or clock face, but cannot discern facial features or read the clock hands.
黄斑位于视网膜后极部中央，是感光细胞最密集、负责高精细中心视力及敏锐色彩感知的核心解剖部位。年龄相关性黄斑变性（AMD）是黄斑部细胞的进行性萎缩衰亡，在北美是导致55岁以上老年人群不可逆致盲的首要病因。AMD特征性破坏中心视野，而保留周边周边视野：患者能够看到钟表的外轮廓或门框，却根本看不清表盘指针或熟人的面容五官。
  • • Dry AMD (Atrophic): Accounts for ~90% of cases. Photoreceptor cells in the macula slowly deteriorate over years. Symptoms include gradual blurring of central vision, needing brighter light to read, and a central blind spot. Both eyes are commonly affected.
    干性黄斑变性（萎缩型）：占所有病例约90%。黄斑区光敏细胞历经数年缓慢萎缩退化。表现为中央视力渐进性模糊、阅读需要极强照明、视野中央出现暗斑；通常双眼先后累及。
  • • Wet AMD (Neovascular): Accounts for ~10% of cases but causes 90% of severe blindness. Fragile abnormal new blood vessels proliferate beneath the retina and leak blood, serum, and lipids, causing rapid permanent scarring and central vision loss within days to weeks. The cardinal early warning sign is metamorphopsia: straight lines appearing distorted, bent, or wavy (tested via Amsler grid). Anti-VEGF intraocular injections help halt leakage.
    湿性黄斑变性（新生血管型）：占病例约10%，却导致90%的严重失明。视网膜深层异常增生脆弱新生血管，渗漏血液与浆液，在数天至数周内引发不可逆黄斑水肿机化结瘢与中心视力暴跌。标志性早期前驱警示体征为视物变形（Metamorphopsia）：直线看起来发生弯曲、扭曲或波浪状起伏（可用阿姆斯勒方格表筛查）。玻璃体腔注射抗VEGF药物可有效控制血管渗漏。

Diabetic Retinopathy

糖尿病视网膜病变：微血管病变与眼底出血
Diabetic retinopathy is a microvascular complication of diabetes in which prolonged chronic hyperglycemia damages tiny blood vessels feeding the retina. In non-proliferative retinopathy, capillaries develop microaneurysms, leak fluid, and cause macular edema. In advanced proliferative retinopathy, oxygen-deprived retina grows abnormal, fragile new vessels that bleed into the vitreous humour, creating fibrovascular traction scars that pull the retina off the posterior wall. Retinal detachment and severe blindness can result. Annual dilated funduscopic eye exams are mandatory for all persons with diabetes.
糖尿病视网膜病变是糖尿病引起的最严重的微血管并发症之一。长期慢性高血糖损害视网膜毛细血管内皮，早期形成微动脉瘤并渗漏浆液导致黄斑水肿；随着视网膜慢性缺血缺氧，眼底生长出异常脆弱的新生血管（增殖期），易自发破裂出血涌入玻璃体腔，形成的纤维机化条索持续牵拉视网膜导致牵拉性视网膜脱离与不可逆盲目。所有糖尿病患者每年必须常规接受散瞳眼底镜筛查。

Retinal Detachment: Medical Emergency

视网膜脱离：突发闪光暗影与急诊手术
Retinal detachment is the acute separation of the neurosensory retina from the underlying retinal pigment epithelium. As individuals age, the gel-like vitreous humour thickens, contracts, and separates from the retina. Sudden traction can tear the fragile retinal sheet, allowing liquefied vitreous to seep underneath and peel the retina away from its choroidal oxygen supply. Classic warning symptoms include sudden appearance of dark floaters (spots or cobwebs), unprovoked flashes of light (photopsia), and a dark shadow or veil resembling a 'curtain being drawn' across the field of vision. Immediate surgical reattachment is required to prevent permanent blindness.
视网膜脱离是指视网膜神经上皮层与色素上皮层之间发生病理性分离的眼科危重急症。随着年龄增长，凝胶状玻璃体发生液化和后脱离，牵拉视网膜形成裂孔，液化玻璃体经裂孔涌入视网膜神经上皮下，使其如同壁纸脱落般脱离脉络膜血液营养供应。其特征性警示体征包括：视野突然出现大量飞蚊黑影、眼前频发无声无光的闪光感、以及视野某侧出现如‘黑幕落下遮挡’般进行性扩大的视野缺损。一旦出现必须立即送眼科急诊手术复位，否则将造成不可逆失明。

Cataracts: Pathophysiology and Manifestations

白内障：晶状体蛋白质变性浑浊与光感衰退
A cataract is the gradual clouding of the eye's normally clear crystalline lens, which blocks light rays from focusing onto the retina. The term derives from the Greek word for 'waterfall', evoking the sensation of viewing the world through cascading water or a frosted windshield. Aging is the primary etiology (senile cataract), alongside diabetes, smoking, ocular trauma, and chronic corticosteroid use. Symptoms develop slowly and painlessly: progressive blurred or dim vision, faded colours, poor night vision, and glaring halos around oncoming headlights.
白内障（Cataract）是指眼球内原本透明的晶状体因蛋白质变性发生浑浊，阻碍光线聚焦于视网膜的眼病。该词源自希腊语意为‘瀑布’，形象地比喻患者看外界宛如隔着流淌的瀑布或磨砂玻璃般昏暗模糊。机体自然衰老是其最主要病因（老年性白内障），此外糖尿病、长期吸烟、眼外伤及长期服用糖皮质激素亦是常见诱因。其临床表现为缓慢无痛性视力进行性下降、色彩辨识暗淡退化、夜间视力极差以及直视车灯时出现刺眼的‘彩虹样光晕’（眩光）。

Cataract Surgical Management and Post-Op Care

白内障超声乳化手术与术后照料体位禁忌
When cataracts compromise ADLs and reading, surgery is the only definitive curative treatment. Under local anesthesia, modern phacoemulsification uses ultrasonic energy to fragment the cloudy lens, aspirate the debris, and insert a permanent artificial intraocular lens (IOL). Support workers must enforce critical post-operative precautions: wear the rigid protective eye shield while sleeping, avoid bending down from the waist, avoid coughing or sneezing forcefully, never lift heavy objects (>10 lbs), and avoid straining during defecation, as all these actions dangerously elevate intraocular pressure.
当白内障影响到阅读与日常生活时，手术置换是唯一确切的治疗手段。在局部麻醉下，现代超声乳化技术利用高频超声将浑浊的晶状体核碎解吸出，并在囊袋内植入永久性人工晶状体（IOL）。支持工作者在术后照护中必须恪守安全红线：夜间睡眠必须佩戴硬质塑料眼罩保护眼球；严格禁止弯腰低头提重物（大于10磅）；严防剧烈咳嗽或打喷嚏；排便时切忌用力屏气，以免引起眼内压急剧骤升造成手术切口裂开或出血。

Glaucoma: The Silent Thief of Sight

青光眼：进行性高眼压与视野管状缺损
Glaucoma is an eye disease characterized by increased intraocular pressure (IOP) resulting from impaired drainage of aqueous humour fluid. Chronic high pressure compresses and destroys optic nerve fibres. Glaucoma is often termed the 'silent thief of sight' because open-angle glaucoma develops painlessly with zero early symptoms: peripheral vision slowly constricts inward without the client realizing it, until only a narrow central area remains ('tunnel vision'). Once optic nerve fibres die, vision loss is permanent and irreversible. Lifelong medicated pressure-lowering eye drops (e.g. prostaglandin analogs, beta-blockers) are required daily.
青光眼（Glaucoma）是以病理性眼内压（IOP）升高为特征的严重致盲眼病，通常因眼前房角房水引流通道堵塞受阻所致。高眼压持续压迫并摧毁视神经节细胞与视网膜神经纤维层。开角型青光眼被称为‘无声的视力小偷’，因为其起病极其隐匿、全程完全无痛：周边视野由外向内缓慢悄无声息地缩小缺损，直至终末期只剩下正前方狭窄管状区域（‘管状视野’，Tunnel Vision），患者常在严重跌倒撞墙时才察觉。视神经坏死不可逆转，患者必须终生每日定时滴用降眼压眼药水以保全残存视力。

Effects of Vision Loss on Holistic Well-Being

视力障碍对身心社会全人维度的系统影响
Severe vision loss shatters autonomy and self-esteem. As illustrated in the textbook case of Mr. Barak (78, diagnosed with AMD), even simple morning routines like dressing and grooming become agonizingly time-consuming. Because he cannot read newspapers, drive, or safely navigate curbs, he isolates himself at home and ceases attending his mosque. Fear of tripping and falling creates immense anxiety. Support workers must restore dignity, promote remaining strengths, and maintain consistent environmental predictability.
严重的视力受损会重创服务对象的自理能力与自尊心。如教材中78岁患有黄斑变性的巴拉克先生（Mr. Barak）案例所示：原本简单的更衣梳洗耗时倍增；因无法阅读报纸、无法驾车、惧怕在马路牙子上摔倒迷路，他彻底不再去清真寺参与社交与宗教生活。对跌倒骨折的恐惧导致深度的焦虑孤立。支持工作者必须以最大的耐心维护其尊严，激发其残留代偿机能，并确保生活环境的高度稳定可预期。

Eyeglasses Handling, Cleaning, and Case Storage

眼镜取戴、温水清洗与专用硬盒存放
Eyeglasses correct refractive errors (myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism, presbyopia). Eyeglasses must be kept immaculately clean: wash lenses daily with warm tap water and a drop of mild soap (avoid abrasive chemicals or hot water that destroys anti-reflective coatings), then dry gently with a clean microfibre cloth or lens paper. Paper towels, tissues, and shirt sleeves contain wood fibres that scratch lenses permanently! When eyeglasses are not worn, they must be stored inside a hard protective case—never leave them loose on bedside tables, and NEVER place them on beds or chairs where they can be sat upon or broken.
眼镜用于矫正近视、远视、散光及老视等屈光不正。镜片必须保持高度清洁：每日用流动的温水冲洗，加微量中性洗洁精轻揉洗去油脂灰尘，再用专用超细纤维眼镜布轻轻吸干；严禁用面巾纸、餐巾纸、湿纸巾或衣服衣角擦拭镜片，因为纸浆粗糙木质纤维会在镜片表面刮出永久性细微划痕！眼镜未佩戴时，必须装入专用硬质眼镜盒内；绝对严禁随手丢在床铺、沙发或椅子上，以防被一屁股坐碎压折。

Contact Lenses Care and Microbial Keratitis Safety

隐形眼镜清洁消毒与角膜溃疡感染红线
Contact lenses sit directly on the cornea. Meticulous hand hygiene with soap and water before handling lenses is essential to prevent Acanthamoeba and bacterial keratitis (corneal ulcers that cause permanent scarring and blindness). Clean lenses only with commercial sterile contact lens solution—NEVER use tap water, saliva, or homemade saline! Unless specifically prescribed for extended overnight wear, lenses must be removed before sleeping. If a client's eye becomes red, painful, photophobic, or swollen, remove lenses immediately and notify the supervisor and optometrist.
隐形眼镜（角膜接触镜）直接贴附于角膜上皮。接触前必须严格使用肥皂流水洗手，严防棘阿米巴原虫及铜绿假单胞菌感染引发化脓性角膜炎（角膜溃疡可迅速穿孔留瘢失明）。只能使用专用的无菌隐形眼镜多功能护理液冲洗浸泡，绝对严禁用自来水、唾液或自制生理盐水冲洗！除非专为长戴型设计，睡前必须取下镜片；严禁戴镜游泳或泡温泉。一旦发现服务对象眼球充血发红、剧烈刺痛畏光或眼睑肿胀，必须立即协助摘除镜片并汇报主管护士安排眼科检查。

Braille Tactile Literacy System

布莱叶点字（盲文）触觉编码体系
Braille is a tactile system of reading and writing designed for blind and severely visually impaired persons, invented by Louis Braille in 1824. Characters are arranged within standardized 'cells' of six raised dots (two columns of three dots each). By feeling the pattern of raised dots with fingertips, readers decode letters of the alphabet, punctuation marks, contractions, musical notations, and mathematical formulas (FIGURE 34.4). Braille enables independent literacy, document review, and workplace autonomy.
盲文（Braille / 布莱叶点字）是由路易·布莱叶于1824年创立的专供盲人及重度视障人士使用的触觉拼音文字系统。每个盲文字符基于由 6 个凸出圆点组成的标准“盲文方”（由两列各3个点排列）。盲人通过灵敏的指腹触摸凸点点阵的不同排列组合，阅读识别26个英文字母、标点符号、缩写词、乐谱及复杂的数学公式（图 34.4）。盲文为视障人士开启了独立的阅读素养、文件审阅与职业自立大门。

  • • Optical and Electronic Magnifiers: Handheld magnifying glasses, illuminated stand magnifiers, and digital video magnifiers that zoom text up to 50x with high-contrast reverse-colour displays (white text on black background).

义眼（人工假体眼球）护理规范

The Human Sighted Guide Technique

人道领路导引技巧（Sighted Guide Technique）
When assisting a client with vision impairment to walk: NEVER grab the client's arm or push/drag them from behind! Instead, offer your elbow or forearm, and let the client take hold of your arm just above the elbow. Walk slightly ahead (about half a step) at a comfortable, normal walking pace. The client senses your body movements through your arm as you turn, ascend, or descend. Always pause briefly and verbally describe steps, curbs, stairs ('one step down'), doorways, and narrow passages.
在协助视力受损服务对象行走时：绝对严禁从身后推搡、生拉硬拽或强行抓住其手臂！正确规范的领路法是：照料者微屈前臂靠近患者，让服务对象的手指自然握住照料者肘部上方约一两寸处；照料者走在前方半步以正常从容步伐前行。患者通过肘部联动感知照料者身体的起伏与转弯。在遇到台阶、马路牙子、楼梯、门槛或狭窄通道前，提前半步停顿并用语言清晰提示（如‘前方有一级下行台阶’）。

Environmental Safety and Navigation Rules

室内环境绝对安全防跌倒守则

SAFETY ALERT: Environmental Predictability for the Visually Impaired / 安全警示：维护视障人士环境心智地图的铁律

  • NEVER rearrange furniture without the client's explicit permission and physical orientation. A moved chair is a severe tripping hazard.
    严禁擅自挪动家具：未征得服务对象同意并带领其亲自重新触摸确认前，绝对禁止变动桌椅床铺位置，防止撞击跌倒骨折。
  • Keep interior doors completely OPEN or completely CLOSED. Never leave a door half-open or ajar, which creates a dangerous edge impact.
    室内门要么完全敞开、要么完全闭紧：严禁将门半开半掩！半掩的门边锋利如刀，是视障人士头部撞伤最危险的隐患。
  • Remove all clutter from floors: Pick up electrical cords, throw rugs, shoes, and stray objects from hallways and pathways immediately.
    清除通道地面一切杂物：立即撤除散落在走廊和地面的电线、松散小块地毯、鞋子及玩具。
  • Ensure consistent, glare-free lighting: Hallways, stairwells, and bathrooms must be brightly lit at all times; use nightlights in bedrooms.
    保持充足无眩光照明：走廊、楼梯间及卫生间必须保持明亮通透，卧室通宵开启防跌倒小夜灯。

服务犬（导盲犬与听力犬）全人支持体系

Guide dogs (such as those partnered through CNIB) navigate outdoor travel, halt at curbs, bypass low-hanging tree branches, and guide handlers safely around obstacles.

    1. 始终与主人交谈，而非与犬对话：服务对象与工作犬是高度契合的工作搭档。交流时请一律注视服务对象并与其对话，切忌对着犬大呼小叫。
  • • 2. NEVER touch, pet, or whistle at a working dog: When a guide dog is wearing its harness or a hearing dog wears its vest, it is on duty. Petting or distracting the dog breaks its concentration and endangers the handler.
    2. 佩戴鞍具背心期间严禁抚摸或逗弄：工作犬在穿戴鞍具或工作背心时处于执勤状态。抚摸、呼唤或吹口哨会分散其注意力，直接陷主人于危险之中。
  • • 3. NEVER feed the service dog: Service dogs are on strict nutritional and relief schedules. Giving food scraps causes behavioural degradation.
    3. 严禁投喂任何食物零食：服务犬有严格的饮食与排泄作息表，外界投喂会严重破坏其纪律性。
  • • 4. Keep walkways wide and clear: Avoid blocking the dog's designated resting area near the client's bed or chair.
    4. 保持犬行通道畅通：切勿在服务对象床边或轮椅旁堆放推车障碍物，保障工作犬顺畅卧伏与起身领路。

Chapter 34 Review: Key Points

第 34 章复习重点：听力与视力障碍照护核心要义
  • • Hearing Disorders: Common causes include otitis media, otosclerosis, Ménière's disease, presbycusis, and cerumen impaction.
    耳部疾患：常见病因包括中耳炎、耳硬化症、美尼尔病、老年性耳聋及耵聍栓塞。
  • • Effects of Hearing Loss: Varies widely; includes embarrassment, answering inappropriately, fatigue from strain, and profound social isolation and depression.
    听障影响：因人而异；伴随答非所问、过度费力倾听疲劳、社交退缩回避及深层抑郁。
  • • Hearing Aids Care: Amplifies sound, not clarity. Keep dry and cool, inspect daily, clean cerumen, never wash electronics, and minimize background noise.
    助听器维护：放大音量而非清晰度；保持干燥阴凉、每日检查、清理耳垢、严禁水洗机身，并降低环境噪音。
  • • Cochlear Implants & APD: Implants bypass damaged inner ear hair cells to directly stimulate auditory nerve; APD involves normal hearing with impaired central brain decoding.
    人工耳蜗与APD：耳蜗植入体绕过受损毛细胞直接电刺激听神经；APD为听力正常但大脑中枢解码障碍。
  • • Vision Disorders: Conjunctivitis, cataracts (cloudy lens), glaucoma (high IOP optic nerve loss), AMD (loss of central vision), and retinal detachment (emergency).
    眼部疾患：结膜炎（红眼病）、白内障（晶状体混浊）、青光眼（高眼压视神经萎缩）、黄斑变性（中心视野破坏）及视网膜脱离（急诊）。
  • • Vision Assistance: Never rearrange furniture, keep doors fully open or closed, utilize sighted guide technique (client holds guide's elbow), and respect working service dogs.
    视障照料：绝不擅挪家具、门全开或全闭、运用人道领路法（扶照料者肘部）、严禁打扰执勤导盲犬。

  • Case 1: Mrs. Ho has severe vision impairment. While entering her room, what are your immediate verbal actions, and what environmental hazards must you assess?
    案例 1：何太太患有重度视力障碍。进入其房间时，您应立即采取哪些口头沟通行为？必须排查房间内哪些潜在危险隐患？
  • Clinical Analysis 1: Immediately state your name and role upon entering, speak in a normal tone, and announce when you leave. Inspect the room to ensure pathways are clear of cords and rugs, the door is completely open or closed, furniture has not been moved, lighting is adequate, and items on her nightstand remain in fixed clock positions.
    临床深度解析 1：进门立即自报姓名身份，用正常语调交谈，离开前明确告别。排查房间：地面无电线杂物地毯、房门完全闭合或完全开启、家具位置严禁变动、照明充足不刺眼、床头柜物品按时钟方位归位。

    案例 2：普林先生患有美尼尔病，突然发作严重眩晕伴恶心。您应如何确保其生命安全并开展有效沟通？
  • Clinical Analysis 2: Assist him into a safe, recumbent bed rest position immediately to prevent falls; keep the room quiet and dimly lit; position emesis basin for nausea; face him directly in good light, speak clearly and distinctly without shouting, and avoid abrupt head movements.
    临床深度解析 2：立即协助其就地平卧静卧休息以防摔倒；调暗室内光线保持绝对安静；备好呕吐盆防范误吸；交谈时面向光源、正对患者、清晰慢速吐字，切忌大喊大叫，避免剧烈转动头部。
