CHAPTER 31: Growth and Development

第 31 章：人类生长与发育全生命周期特征

Support workers care for clients across all developmental stages, understanding their evolving physiological, cognitive, and psychosocial needs.

支持人员为处于不同生命发展阶段的案主提供照护，深刻理解其随年龄演进的生理机能、认知思维与心理社会需求。

OBJECTIVES / 本章核心学习目标

After reading this chapter, the learner should be able to do the following: / 研读本章后，学员应当能够全面掌握并达成以下专业执业技能：

  • • Understand the principles of growth and development.
    全面理解并阐述人类生长（生理体格增长）与发育（心理社会功能演进）的核心基本原则。
  • • Explain Piaget’s stages of cognitive development.
    清晰解释皮亚杰（Jean Piaget）儿童认知功能发展的各个关键阶段及其思维特征。
  • • Describe Erikson’s developmental stages.
    熟练描述埃里克森（Erik Erikson）心理社会发展八大阶段的核心冲突与人生发展任务。
  • • Explain how understanding Maslow’s hierarchy of needs applies to support work.
    深刻阐释马斯洛人类基本需求层次理论在支持人员日常临床照护中的具体应用与优先次序。
  • • Identify the stages of growth and development and the normal age ranges for each stage.
    准确识别从新生儿期至老年期的全生命周期九大发展阶段及其对应的正常年龄跨度。
  • • Identify the developmental tasks for each age group.
    精准列举并掌握各个年龄阶段人群所必须掌握并达成的核心发展性任务（Developmental Tasks）。
  • • Describe the normal and typical growth and development for each age group.
    详尽描述各个生命阶段人群在身体形态、认知能力、情感心理及社会功能方面的典型常态特征。

Human growth and development is a continuous, lifelong journey from birth through old age.

人类的生长与发育是从出生贯穿至老年期、持续一生的生命演进历程。

As a support worker, you will care for clients in different stages of their development. Having a basic understanding of human growth and development will help you better understand the client’s needs and give better care. This chapter presents the basic changes that occur in most healthy people from birth through old age.
作为支持人员，你将为处于生命不同发展阶段的案主提供照护。具备关于人类生长与发育的基础理论知识，将有力帮助你更深刻地理解案主的真实身心需求，并提供更高质量的照护。本章系统阐述了绝大多数健康人在从出生至老年期的整个生命历程中所经历的基本身心转变。
Human growth and development are presented here in nine stages; for each stage, the age ranges and typical characteristics are outlined. The stages overlap, so it is hard to see clear-cut endings and beginnings of each. In addition, people vary in their rate of growth and development, especially if their health has affected their everyday activities. For example, you may care for young clients who are residents in a long-term care facility; they may exhibit very different developmental characteristics than other residents who are much older.
在此，人类的生长与发育被系统划分为九大阶段；针对每个阶段，均概括了其典型年龄跨度与核心身心特征。各个发展阶段之间彼此交织重叠，因此很难划定非黑即白的截然界限。此外，每个个体的生长发育速率存在显著差异，尤其当健康问题影响到其日常生活活动时更是如此。例如，你可能会在长期照护机构中照护年轻案主；他们所表现出的心理与行为发育特征，与同住该机构的其他高龄长者会有极其显著的差异。
One area of understanding around human growth and development that is being challenged in modern Western society is that of gender differences and identification—specifically, what society dictates versus what a child feels their personal gender affiliation to be. As researchers understand more about society’s influences on gender role assignments, our definitions of these may change and evolve.
在当代西方社会，关于人类生长与发育认知中正在经历深刻探讨与演变的一个重要领域是性别差异与性别认同（gender identification）——具体而言，即社会传统文化所规范的角色期望，与儿童自我内在感知的个人性别归属之间的相互作用。随着研究者对社会文化对性别角色指派影响的认识日益深化，我们对这些概念的界定也将不断与时俱进。

Increase in physical size and weight that occurs in a slow and steady manner.
生长：以稳定且可测量的方式持续发生的人体体格尺寸、身高与体重的生物学定量增长。
infancy /ˈɪn.fən.si/ 婴儿期 p. 703
The first year of life, during which the body undergoes rapid physical, psychological, and social growth and development.
婴儿期：出生后生命的第一年（0–1 岁）；在此阶段机体经历整个人生中最为迅猛的生理体格增长、大脑发育、心理活动萌芽与早期社会性依恋建立。
late adulthood /leɪt əˈdʌlt.hʊd/ 成年晚期（老年期） p. 714
Begins approximately at the age of 65 years. This stage is characterized by adjusting to decreased physical strength and loss of health, retirement, and reduced income; coping with the death of a partner; developing new friends and relationships; and preparing for one’s own death.
成年晚期（老年期）：约始于 65 岁。该阶段的核心特征是主动适应体力减退与健康状况变化、适应退休生活与收入减少；承受并应对伴侣离世的丧恸；维系并发展新的社交人际关系；以及安详从容地面对死亡的临终准备。
late childhood /leɪt ˈtʃaɪld.hʊd/ 学龄晚期（青春前期） p. 708
Ages 9 to 12 years; characterized by acquiring skills necessary for functioning in the community, developing friendships, and achieving independence.
学龄晚期（青春前期）：9 至 12 岁；以掌握参与学校与社区活动所必需的复杂技能、建立深度同伴友谊以及初步确立自主独立性为核心特征。
middle adulthood /ˈmɪd.əl əˈdʌlt.hʊd/ 成年中期（中年期） p. 713
Ages approximately 40 to 65 years; characterized by adjusting to physical changes, having grown children, developing leisure activities, and relating to aging parents.
成年中期（中年期）：约 40 至 65 岁；其特征是适应身体机能的渐进退行性转变、面对子女成年离巢独立、拓展业余闲暇爱好以及照料关怀年迈父母（“三明治世代”压力）。
middle childhood /ˈmɪd.əl ˈtʃaɪld.hʊd/ 学龄中期 p. 708
Ages 6 to 8 years; characterized by developing the social and physical skills normative to one’s gender; learning basic reading, writing, and arithmetic skills; developing a conscience and morals; and developing a good feeling and attitude about oneself.
学龄中期：6 至 8 岁；其特征包括掌握基本的社会交往与体能活动技能；系统学习阅读、书写与基础算术知识；开始形成是非良知与道德伦理准则；并建立积极健康的自我认同感与自尊心。
need /niːd/ 需求 p. 701
Something that is necessary or desirable for maintaining life and psychosocial well-being.
需求：维持机体生物学生存、保障生理功能运转以及达成心理社会安康状态所必需或渴望获得的内在与外在要素。
newborn /ˈnjuː.bɔːrn/ 新生儿 p. 703
A baby, up to 28 days after birth; also called a neonate.
新生儿：指从出生断脐至满 28 天以内的婴儿；医学专业术语亦称之为新生命体（neonate）。
preschool /ˈpriː.skuːl/ 学龄前期 p. 706
Ages 3 to 5 years; characterized by gaining an increasing ability to communicate and understand others, becoming able to perform self-care activities, learning the differences between the genders, learning to distinguish right from wrong, learning to play with others, and developing family relationships.
学龄前期：3 至 5 岁；其特征是言语沟通与理解他人能力显著增强、初步掌握自主生活自理技能、认知性别生理差异、学习区分是非善恶、学会与同伴合作游戏、并深化家庭依恋关系。
puberty /ˈpjuː.bɚ.t̬i/ 青春发育期（性成熟期） p. 710
The period when the reproductive organs begin to function and secondary sex characteristics appear.
青春发育期（性成熟期）：生殖系统器官开始发育成熟并具备生殖功能，且第二性征（乳房发育、喉结突出、体毛增生等）显著显现的发育时期。
reflex /ˈriː.fleks/ 反射 p. 704
An involuntary movement in response to a stimulus.
反射：机体神经系统受到内外环境特定刺激时所产生的非自主性、刻板性生理动作反应。
self-actualization /ˌself.æk.tʃu.ə.laɪˈzeɪ.ʃən/ 自我实现 p. 703
Realizing one’s full potential.
自我实现：马斯洛需求金字塔最高境界；指个体的全部天赋潜能、创造力与个人理想获得最大程度的发挥、实现与圆满。
sexual orientation /ˈsek.ʃu.əl ˌɔːr.i.enˈteɪ.ʃən/ 性取向 p. 711
Whether a person’s physical and emotional arousal is to people of the same or opposite sex, or both.
性取向：指个体在浪漫情感与身体性吸引方面，倾向于同性、异性、抑或两者皆有的持久生理心理取向。
toddlerhood /ˈtɑːd.lɚ.hʊd/ 幼儿期（学步期） p. 705
Ages 1 to 3 years; characterized by tolerating separation from the primary caregiver, gaining control of bowel and bladder functions, using words to communicate, and starting to assert independence.
幼儿期（学步期）：1 至 3 岁；其特征包括逐渐耐受与主要照料者的短暂分离、获得对大小便括约肌排泄功能的自主控制（如厕训练）、运用词汇句子进行表达、并开始强烈彰显自主自立意识（“第一反抗期”）。
young adulthood /jʌŋ əˈdʌlt.hʊd/ 成年早期（青年期） p. 712
Ages approximately 16 to 40 years; characterized by choosing an education and an occupation, selecting and learning to live with a partner, becoming a parent and raising children, and developing a satisfactory sex life.
成年早期（青年期）：约 16 至 40 岁；其核心特征是完成学业教育与确立终身职业、寻觅并学习与亲密伴侣共同生活、承担为人父母责任哺育教养下一代、并建立和谐满意的性生活。

PRINCIPLES OF GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT

生长与发育的基本生物学与心理社会学原则
Growth refers to the physical changes a person experiences that can be measured and that occur in a steady and orderly manner. Growth is measured by changes in height and weight, as well as by changes in physical appearance and body functions.
生长（Growth）是指个体所经历的、能够被客观测量且以稳定有序方式持续发生的身体生理形态变化。生长的衡量指标包括身高、体重的增长，以及体格外貌特征与器官生理机能的量化改变。
Development refers to changes in psychological and social functioning. A person behaves and thinks in certain ways at different stages of development. A 2-year-old thinks in simple terms and needs a primary caregiver for meeting many basic needs. A 40-year-old thinks in complex ways and meets most of their own basic needs without help from others.
发育（Development）是指个体在心理过程与社会人际交往功能方面所经历的质的演变与成熟。在不同的生命发展阶段，人的思维模式与行为举止截然不同。一个 2 岁的幼儿以极简的具象逻辑思考，必须完全依赖主要照料者来满足其大部分基本生存需求；而一个 40 岁的成年人则具备复杂的抽象辩证思维，无需他人协助即可独立满足自身的绝大部分生活需求。
  • • Growth and development occur from the moment of fertilization until death.
    全程连续性：人类的生长与发育从卵子受精的那一瞬间即刻启动，并不可逆转地持续推进直至生命终结。
  • • The process goes from the simple to the complex. A baby learns to sit before standing, to stand before walking, and to walk before running. An adolescent learns to develop from dependence to independence.
    由简入繁：发育遵循从简单动作向复杂技能的渐进规律。婴儿必须先学会独坐，然后学会扶站，站稳后学会迈步行走，走稳后才学会奔跑。青少年在心理社会功能上同样经历从全然依赖走向自主独立。
  • • Growth and development occur in certain directions: (1) From the head (cephalocaudal) to the foot (proximodistal)—babies learn to hold up their heads before they learn to sit. After learning to sit, they learn to stand. (2) From the centre of the body outward—babies learn to control shoulder movements before they can control hand movements.
    定向规律性：生理发育遵循特定解剖学轴向：（1）从头部向足底方向发展（头尾规律，Cephalocaudal）——婴儿必须先学会抬头稳颈，然后学会独坐，坐稳后才学会直立站立；（2）由身体躯干中心向远端四肢外周发展（近远规律，Proximodistal）——婴儿先学会控制大关节躯干与肩膀动作，随后才能精准控制手腕与手指抓握动作。
  • • Growth and development occur in a sequence and pattern. Certain developmental tasks must be completed during each stage, and no stage can be skipped, as each stage lays the foundation for the next stage. Children all over the world, whatever their culture or living conditions, must go through each stage of development before they can progress.
    阶段性与不可跳跃性：生长与发育遵循严格的生理阶梯时序。个体在每个阶段均有特定的发展任务（developmental tasks）必须完成，且任何阶段均不可被跨越跳过，因为每一个前序阶段都是构建下一个更高阶发展阶段不可或缺的基石。全世界各民族的儿童无论生活在何种文化环境或物质条件中，都必须按部就班地经历每一个发展阶段方能逐步成熟。

Piaget’s Theory of Cognitive Development

皮亚杰儿童认知发展阶段理论
Jean Piaget (1896–1980) was a Swiss psychologist who proposed that children actively construct knowledge as they explore and manipulate their environment. His theory highlights three fundamental tenets: (1) Children build their own knowledge based on their experiences; (2) Children learn things on their own without influence from adults or older children; (3) Children are instinctively motivated to learn and do not need external rewards as motivation.
瑞士著名发展心理学家让·皮亚杰（Jean Piaget，1896–1980）提出，儿童是在主动探索并操纵周围物理环境的过程中自主建构认知体系的。他的理论确立了三大核心基石：（1）儿童依据切身经验自主建构属于自己的知识经验；（2）儿童具备强大的自学能力，无需成年人或年长儿童的过度灌输干预；（3）儿童生来具有探索求知的本能动机，无需依靠外部物质奖励来激发学习。
Piaget identified four major cognitive developmental stages:
皮亚杰将认知发展划分为四大连续演进的核心阶段：
  • • Sensorimotor stage (birth to 18–24 months): Characteristics include motor activity without the use of symbols. All things learned are based on sensory experiences and motor trial and error. Object permanence develops toward the end of this stage.
    感知运动阶段（0 至 18–24 个月）：核心特征是没有符号表征系统的纯感觉与动作探索。婴儿所习得的一切知识完全依赖直接的感觉输入与动作试错。客体永久性（意识到物体看不见时依然存在）在此阶段晚期逐步确立。
  • • Preoperational stage (2 through 7 years): Memory and imagination develop rapidly. Children at this stage are egocentric, meaning they have cognitive difficulty seeing things from viewpoints other than their own. Symbolic thought and language flourish.
    前运算阶段（2 至 7 岁）：表象记忆与想象力迅猛发展。此阶段儿童表现出鲜明的“自我中心主义（egocentric）”，即难以从他人视角或立场来理解事物。符号思维与语言表达突飞猛进。
  • • Concrete operational stage (7 to 11 years): The main achievement is the ability to work things out mentally and solve concrete problems using logical operations without physically manipulating objects.
    具体运算阶段（7 至 11 岁）：核心里程碑是儿童开始能够在头脑中进行可逆的逻辑运算，无需借助实际物理摆弄即可在心理层面推导演绎并解决具体现实问题（守恒定律确立）。
  • • Formal operational stage (11 years and older): Individuals develop abstract intellectual reasoning, deductive hypotheses testing, and systematic problem solving, building knowledge without altering cognitive operational structures.
    形式运算阶段（11 岁及以上）：个体发展出成熟的高阶抽象逻辑思维、假设-演绎推理能力与系统化反思能力，能够脱离具体实物对纯理论与假设性命题进行严密逻辑论证。

Erikson’s Stages of Psychosocial Development

埃里克森心理社会发展阶段理论
Erik Erikson (1902–1994) was a developmental psychologist and psychoanalyst who studied the psychological development of human beings across the entire lifespan. He is famous for his eight stages of psychosocial development (TABLE 31.1). Each stage involves a crisis of two opposing forces (e.g., trust versus mistrust). For example, if an infant has developed trust, the infant successfully moves on to the next psychosocial task. But if that infant has developed mistrust, this can influence the infant’s ability to form trusting, intimate relationships while moving through the other psychosocial stages of life. Another example is a toddler who is not allowed to learn by doing, and thus develops doubt about their own abilities. This doubt can complicate the toddler’s later attempts at independence.
埃里克·埃里克森（Erik Erikson，1902–1994）是享誉全球的发展心理学家与精神分析学家，他系统考察了贯穿全生命周期的心理社会演变。他因提出著名的心理社会发展八大阶段理论而广为人知（见表 31.1）。每一个阶段都包含由两股对立力量构成的核心发展危机（如信任对不信任）。例如，若婴儿成功建立了对抚养者的基本信任感，便能信心百倍地迈向下一项心理社会任务；但若婴儿形成了根深蒂固的不信任感，这将在其整个人生后续发展中深刻妨碍其建立亲密互信的人际关系。又如，一个在幼儿期被过度包办替代、不被允许“在做中学”的孩子，会对自己产生深深的羞耻与怀疑，这种自卑怀疑将极大阻碍其未来的自立进程。
According to Erikson, factors that influence psychosocial health include: (1) Personality (blend of thought patterns, feelings, and unique traits); (2) Family background (loving families foster resilience, while neglect and abuse foster distrust); (3) Environment (school, media, peers, community systems); (4) Life circumstances (tragic losses or major life events).
埃里克森指出，深刻影响个体心理社会健康的核心要素包括：（1）个性特质（独特的思维模式、情感特质与行为风格）；（2）家庭成长背景（充满关爱支持的家庭培育积极心理资本，而暴力忽视与成瘾环境则埋下不信任与心理创伤）；（3）外部社会环境（学校教育、大众媒体、同伴圈子与社区支持系统）；（4）重大生活变故（如童年丧亲、严重创伤事件）。

Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs

马斯洛人类基本需求层次理论
Abraham Maslow (1908–1970) was an American psychologist who influenced ideas about psychosocial health. Maslow is best known for his theory of needs. A need is something that is necessary or desirable for maintaining life and psychosocial well-being. According to Maslow, certain basic needs must be met for a person to survive and function. These needs are arranged in a hierarchy, or order of importance (FIGURE 31.1). Lower-level needs are normally met before higher-level needs. However, some people will deliberately ignore a particular need for a certain period of time in order to meet another need. For example, a person with an addiction may choose to spend money on street drugs instead of spending it on food.
亚伯拉罕·马斯洛（Abraham Maslow，1908–1970）是人本主义心理学先驱。他最著名的学术贡献是需求层次理论。需求是指维持机体生命运转与保障心理社会安康所必需或渴望达成的要素。马斯洛认为，某些基础需求对于人的存活与基本机能运转必不可少。这些需求按照重要程度与生理优先级排列成阶梯金字塔（见图 31.1）。通常情况下，较低层次的需求必须优先得到满足，随后高层次需求才会浮现并驱动个体行为。然而，在特定极端情况下，某些人可能会暂时压抑甚至牺牲某项基本生理需求以满足另一项病理性需求——例如物质成瘾者可能会挪用赖以充饥的餐费去购买毒品。

Physical Needs

生理基础需求
Physical needs refer to basic needs that are the most essential of our needs for survival. Examples include the need to drink when thirsty, to get sleep when tired, or to eat when hungry. According to Maslow, some of these needs involve our efforts to meet the body’s need for homeostasis—that is, maintaining consistent levels in different bodily systems (such as maintaining a normal and constant body temperature). If someone is lacking in more than one need, they are likely to try to meet these physiological needs first. For example, if someone is extremely hungry, it is hard to focus on anything else besides food.
生理需求是指对于维持机体生物学生存最根本、最不可或缺的基础需求。例如口渴时饮水、疲惫时睡眠、饥饿时进食。根据马斯洛的阐述，其中许多生理需求直接服务于机体内环境稳态（homeostasis）的维持——即保持各器官系统生理指标的动态恒定（例如维持恒定的正常体温与水盐平衡）。当个体同时缺乏多种需求时，必然本能地最优先竭力满足生理需求。例如，当案主处于极度饥饿状态时，其注意力根本无法聚焦在食物以外的任何其他事物上。

Safety Needs

安全需求
Once people’s physiological requirements are met, the next need that arises is a safe environment. Our safety needs are apparent even early in childhood, as children have a need for safe and predictable environments and typically react with fear or anxiety when these needs are not met. Maslow pointed out that in adults living in developed nations, safety needs can be more apparent in emergency situations (e.g., during war and disasters), but this need can also explain why we tend to prefer the familiar or why we do things like purchase insurance and contribute to a savings account.
一旦个体的生理基础需求获得满足，紧接着浮现的核心需求便是对安全、稳定环境的渴求。人类对安全的需求早在童年早期便清晰显现，儿童天生需要安全且可预期的周围环境，一旦这种可预测性被打破，往往会本能地表现出强烈的恐惧与焦虑。马斯洛指出，在发达国家的成年人中，安全需求在战争、天灾等突发危急事件中表现得最为直观；但这一需求同样能解释人们为何在日常生活中更倾向于选择熟悉的事物、为何购置商业保险以及为何坚持在银行开设应急储蓄账户。

Love and Belonging Needs

爱与归属感需求
Love is a powerful human emotion that includes deep affection, tenderness, and devotion. Romantic love also involves physical desire. The need for belonging includes the need for a rightful place in society, in a peer group, and in a family. A peer group is a group of friends or acquaintances. Human beings are social creatures who need to be around others. When love and belonging needs are unfulfilled, people often feel lonely and rejected. Many cases have been reported of clients being slow to recover or dying because of lack of love and belonging. This risk is greatest among children and older people. Maslow believed that unfamiliar surroundings create a greater need for love and belonging. Clients in long-term care facilities have left their homes, friends, neighbours, pets, belongings, and familiar surroundings. As their support worker, you must be sensitive to the needs of those clients who are struggling with settling into their new environment.
爱是人类极其强大的崇高情感，包含深厚的情谊、温情关怀与全身心奉献。浪漫的爱情还涵盖身体层面的依恋与欲望。归属感需求则涵盖个体渴望在家庭、同伴群体以及整体社会中拥有属于自己的一席之地。同伴群体是指年龄相近的朋友或熟人圈子。人类作为高度社会化的生物，天生具有与他人结伴共处的心理本能。当爱与归属感长期缺失时，个体会体验到深重的孤独、被孤立感与被遗弃感。临床医学上有大量明确记录表明，缺乏关爱与归属感的案主其康复进程极度缓慢，甚至因抑郁衰竭而加速离世；这种风险在年幼儿童与高龄长者中格外致命。马斯洛强调，陌生孤立的环境会成倍放大对爱与归属的渴望。入住长期照护机构的案主不得不抛下居住了大半生的家园、挚友邻里、宠物与熟悉器物；作为支持人员，你必须格外体察并抚慰那些在新环境中艰难适应的案主的心灵创伤。

Self-Esteem Needs

自尊与受人尊重需求
Esteem is the worth, value, or opinion one has of a person. Self-esteem is thinking well of oneself and being well thought of by others. When self-esteem needs are fulfilled, a person feels confident, adequate, and useful. Unmet self-esteem needs can result in feelings of inferiority, worthlessness, helplessness, and, possibly, depression. Clients often lose their self-esteem when they become ill or injured. Think about the following: (1) How do ill parents feel when they cannot support or care for their children? (2) Does a woman feel whole and attractive after a breast has been removed? (3) Does a person who underwent a leg amputation feel complete, useful, and attractive? As a support worker you can help meet clients’ self-esteem needs by being sensitive to their feelings and encouraging them to be as independent as possible.
尊重代表着对一个人的价值认同、评价与肯定。自尊则是对自己持有积极正面的自我评价，并同时获得来自他人的由衷尊重与认可。当自尊需求得到满足时，个体表现得自信自强、觉得自己有能力且对社会有用；而自尊受挫则会迅速诱发严重的自卑感、无价值感、习得性无助乃至重度抑郁。当案主遭受严重伤病摧残时，往往会经历毁灭性的自尊崩塌。请深入思考以下临床情境：（1）当患重病的父母连抚养照料自己年幼儿女的能力都彻底丧失时，其内心该有多么煎熬破碎？（2）一位因乳腺癌切除乳房的女性案主，还会觉得自己身体是完整的、富有女性魅力的吗？（3）一个被迫施行下肢截肢术的案主，还会觉得自己是个有用、完整且具有吸引力的人吗？作为支持人员，你必须设身处地呵护其自尊脆弱点，并通过全力鼓励并协助案主实现最大程度的生活自理自立，来重塑其内在尊严与价值感。

Self-Actualization Needs

自我实现需求
Self-actualization means realizing one’s full potential. It involves learning, understanding, and creating to the best of one’s ability. It is the highest need in the hierarchy and is rarely met. Most people constantly try to learn and understand more. However, the need for self-actualization can be postponed, and life will continue.
自我实现是指一个人实现其全部个人潜能、理想抱负与创造力。它涵盖最大限度地学习新知、深刻洞察世界并发挥自身所长开展创造性活动。这是马斯洛体系中的最高境界，纯粹达成自我实现的人在现实中凤毛麟角。绝大多数人毕生都在不断探索并追求理解更多。然而，自我实现的需求在客观条件不具备时可以被延缓搁置，个体的基本生理生存依然能够延续。

生长发育九大阶段之第一阶段：婴儿期（出生至 1 岁）

婴儿期是指生命最初的一整年，此阶段是个体经历极其迅猛的体格增长、大脑发育与早期心理社会发展的关键奠基期。婴儿期的核心发展任务包括：
  • • Learning to walk

  • • Learning to eat solid foods
    学习咀嚼并耐受固体辅食
  • • Beginning to talk and communicate with others
    开始运用简单字词和咿呀学语与他人交流
  • • Beginning to have emotional relationships with primary caregivers and siblings
    开始与主要抚养照料者及兄弟姐妹建立深厚的情感依恋
  • • Developing stable sleep and feeding patterns
    逐步建立规律稳定的睡眠作息与定时进食节律

The neonatal period of infancy is the first 28 days after birth, and during this time, a baby is called a neonate or a newborn. The average newborn is 48 to 53 cm (19 to 21 in.) long and weighs 3 200 to 3 600 g (7 to 8 lb.) at birth. The newborn’s head is large compared with the rest of the body, and the skin is wrinkled. Arms and legs seem short compared with the trunk. The abdomen is large and round. Skin and eye colour vary, depending on the baby’s genetic background, and may change during the first year. The newborn has fat, pudgy cheeks; a flat nose; and a receding chin (FIGURE 31.2).
与身体其他部位相比，新生儿的头部显得格外粗大，皮肤呈现红润多皱褶状态。四肢相对躯干显得短小且呈生理性屈曲。腹部膨隆圆滚。皮肤与眼睛的颜色因遗传背景而异，且在出生后第一年内可能会发生微妙变化。新生儿面颊圆润丰满（富含颊脂垫以利吸吮）、鼻梁平扁、下颌轻度后缩（见图 31.2）。
The infant’s central nervous system is not well developed. Movements are uncoordinated and lack purpose. Infants can see at birth, although vision is not clear. They seem attracted to patterns, and as they develop, they prefer colours. Infants can hear well—they are startled by loud noises and soothed by soft sounds. They respond more often to higher-pitched voices than to lower-pitched voices. Infants react to touch, and the senses of smell and taste are well developed.
新生儿的中枢神经系统发育尚不完善，神经纤维髓鞘化尚未完成。身体动作多不协调且缺乏目的性。婴儿一出生就具备视觉能力，但视野聚焦模糊；他们天生对明暗对比分明的几何图案更感兴趣，随着视力发育逐渐对鲜艳色彩表现出明显偏好。婴儿的听觉发育相当成熟——突发巨响会使其受到惊吓，而柔和低语能使其迅速平静；相比低沉粗犷的语调，他们对高频尖细的说话声更具反应性。婴儿对触觉极其敏感，嗅觉和味觉在出生时已高度发育成熟。

Newborn Reflexes

新生儿原始神经反射
Newborns have certain reflexes (involuntary movements in response to a stimulus). These reflexes decline and then disappear as the central nervous system develops:
新生儿具备一系列先天的原始无条件神经反射（即受到特定刺激时的非自主动作反应）。随着大脑皮层中枢神经系统的快速发育成熟，这些原始反射会逐渐衰退并在几个月内彻底消退：
  • • The Moro reflex (startle reflex): Occurs when an infant is frightened by a loud noise or a sudden movement. The arms are thrown apart, the legs extend, and the head is thrown back.
    莫罗反射（惊跳反射，Moro / Startle reflex）：当婴儿受到突发巨响刺激或突然身体失去平衡失重时触发。表现为双臂对称性向外猛烈张开伸展、双腿伸直、头部后仰，随后双臂向胸前环抱收拢成拥抱状。
  • • The rooting reflex: Stimulated when the infant’s cheek is touched at or near the mouth. The infant’s head turns toward the touch. The rooting reflex is necessary for feeding; it helps guide the infant’s mouth to the nipple.
    觅食反射（Rooting reflex）：当轻触婴儿嘴角或面颊皮肤时诱发。婴儿本能地将头转向受触碰一侧并张开嘴唇搜寻。觅食反射对摄食维系生存生死攸关，它能精准引导婴儿口唇快速寻获母亲乳头。
  • • The sucking reflex: Produced by touching the cheeks or the sides of the lips, prompting vigorous rhythmic sucking movements.
    吸吮反射（Sucking reflex）：任何物体（乳头、奶嘴、手指）一旦触碰婴儿唇部或放入口腔，即刻诱发本能的、强有力的有节律吸吮吞咽动作。
  • • The grasping reflex (palmar grasp): Occurs when the infant’s palm is stimulated, causing the fingers to close tightly around the object. This reflex begins to decline around the second month and disappears by the third month.
    抓握反射（掌抓握，Grasping reflex）：当手指或细棒触碰婴儿手掌心时，婴儿五指会立即紧紧握拢并死死抓住不放，其抓握力量甚至能短暂承受自身体重。该反射在满 2 个月前后逐渐减弱，并在满 3 个月时彻底消失。
Developmental milestones during the first 6 months: Newborns cannot hold their heads up; diet is exclusively breast milk or formula. By 1 month, infants lift and turn heads when lying on stomachs. At 2 months, infants smile socially and track moving objects. At 3 months, they raise heads and shoulders, sit supported briefly, and hold a rattle. At 4 months, they roll over, sit supported, sleep through the night, and Moro/rooting reflexes disappear. At 5 months, they grasp objects, play with toes, and teeth begin erupting. Solid foods are added between 5 to 7 months under medical guidance. At 6 months, two lower central incisors appear and infants begin chewing finger foods.
婴儿前 6 个月的核心发育里程碑：新生儿颈部肌肉松弛无力，完全无法自主抬头；前 6 个月的饮食以母乳或婴儿配方奶为主。满 1 个月时：俯卧时能轻微抬头并转动面部。满 2 个月时：出现具有社会性意义的主动微笑，并能用眼睛追随移动物体。满 3 个月时：俯卧时能用前臂支撑抬起头部和胸部，在有支撑下能短暂独坐，并能握住拨浪鼓。满 4 个月时：能够自主翻身、在支撑下坐稳，多数能安睡整夜；此时莫罗反射与觅食反射基本消退，能用双手握物并开始咿呀发音。满 5 个月时：能主动伸手抓物并用脚趾玩耍，乳牙开始萌出；在 5 至 7 个月龄期间遵医嘱开始引入果泥、蔬菜泥等固体辅食。满 6 个月时：下颌两颗乳中切牙通常萌出，开始尝试咀嚼咬嚼手指食物。

Toddlerhood (1 to 3 Years)

发育九大阶段之第二阶段：幼儿期 / 学步期（1 至 3 岁）
Physical growth during the second year of life is not as rapid as during infancy. The developmental tasks during toddlerhood are as follows:
进入生命第二年后，幼儿的体格生理增长速度较婴儿期明显放缓。幼儿期的核心发展任务包括：
  • • Tolerating separation from the primary caregiver
    耐受与主要抚养照料者的短暂分离（克服严重分离焦虑）
  • • Gaining control of bowel and bladder functions (toilet training)
    获得对肠道排便与膀胱排尿括约肌功能的自主控制（完成如厕训练）
  • • Using words and phrases to communicate with others
    运用词汇与简短语句与他人进行言语交流
  • • Becoming less dependent on the primary caregiver and asserting autonomy
    逐步减少对主要照料者的绝对依附，主动彰显自主自立意识
Toddlers have a powerful need to assert independence. With their increasing mobility and avid curiosity, they explore everything within reach: touching, tasting, and smelling. By age 3 years, toddlers can run, jump, climb stairs, and ride a tricycle. Hand coordination improves: they progress from eating with fingers to using a spoon (FIGURE 31.4) and drinking from cups. Right- or left-handedness emerges during the second year.
幼儿具有极其强烈的彰显自我独立自主的内在心理需求。凭借日益精进的行走能力与旺盛的好奇心，他们穿梭于家中每一个角落：触摸、嗅闻并用嘴啃咬一切能够触及的物品。到 3 岁时，幼儿已能自如奔跑、跳跃、上下楼梯并蹬踏三轮脚踏车。手部精细动作协调性大幅飞跃：进食方式从用手抓握食物逐步进阶为熟练使用勺子进食（见图 31.4）并手捧水杯饮水；左利手（左撇子）或右利手倾向通常在两岁前后基本确立。

Toilet training is a major developmental task. Children must be psychologically and physically ready, as control depends on central nervous system myelination. Speech and language skills advance: Vocabulary expands rapidly by imitating adults, and 3-year-olds speak in short two- or three-word sentences. In play, 2-year-olds engage in parallel play—playing alongside other children without interactive cooperation. Toddlers are intensely possessive, shouting 'Mine!' and struggling to share.
言语表达能力突飞猛进：幼儿通过高频模仿成人迅速扩充词汇量，3 岁儿童已能说出由两三个词组成的简短完整句子。在游戏行为中，2 岁儿童多进行“平行游戏（parallel play）”——即与其他孩子并排坐在一起玩耍，但各自沉浸在自己的玩具世界中缺乏协同互动。幼儿具有极强的物权占有欲，经常大喊“我的！”，尚无法理解主动分享的抽象概念。
Temper tantrums and saying 'No!' are classic developmental features of this stage. When their autonomy is challenged or frustrated, toddlers kick and scream. These power struggles between child and caregiver characterize the developmental phase known as the 'terrible twos.' Tolerating brief separations from caregivers is another milestone; consistent parental presence when comfort is needed fosters profound emotional security.
大发脾气（哭闹撒泼，temper tantrums）以及动辄拒绝说“不！（No！）”是该阶段经典的心理发展常态。每当其自主愿望受挫或被成人强行管制时，幼儿会通过踢打、尖叫在地上打滚来发泄抗议。这种在主要照料者与幼儿之间频繁上演的权力拉锯战，被心理学界通俗称为“可怕的两岁（terrible twos）”。耐受与照料者的短暂分离是另一项关键任务；每当幼儿受到挫折或惊吓跑回时，抚养者及时且温暖的回应能帮助孩子建立终身稳固的依恋安全感。

Preschool (3 to 5 Years)

发育九大阶段之第三阶段：学龄前期 / 幼童期（3 至 5 岁）
Ages 3 to 5 years comprise the preschool period (early childhood). Children become taller, leaner, and more physically graceful and coordinated. The developmental tasks of the preschool years include:
3 至 5 岁被界定为学龄前期（早期童年）。此时期儿童身高拔高迅速，婴儿肥逐渐消退，体态显得更加修长苗条，身体动作愈发灵动优雅且协调。学龄前期的核心发展任务包括：
  • • Communicating effectively and understanding complex language
    有效进行言语沟通并深度理解复杂的日常语言
  • • Performing personal self-care activities (dressing, toileting, handwashing)
    独立完成基本的生活自理活动（穿脱衣物、自主如厕、洗手擦脸）
  • • Learning and exploring gender differences
    认知并探索两性生理差异与社会性别角色
  • • Distinguishing right from wrong and developing an early moral conscience
    初步明辨是非善恶，萌发早期的道德规则意识
  • • Engaging in cooperative play and sharing with peers
    学会与同伴合作游戏并逐步建立玩具分享机制
  • • Deepening family relationships and attachments
    进一步深化家庭内部亲情与多重人际依恋关系

The 3-Year-Old

3 岁儿童的发育特征
Three-year-olds have increased coordination: they can walk up stairs alternating feet, pedal tricycles, jump from low steps, put on their shoes, wash hands, and manage simple buttons. Their language vocabulary reaches approximately 900 words. They engage in vivid imaginative role-play (such as playing 'house' or 'dress-up,' FIGURE 31.5). The 7-Year-Old

7 岁儿童的发育特征
The average 7-year-old weighs 22 to 25 kg (49 to 55 lb.) and stands 119 to 124 cm (47 to 49 in.) tall. Fine motor coordination permits fluid cursive handwriting. Seven-year-olds are more introspective, serious, and quieter than 6-year-olds, spending more time alone. They are acutely sensitive to criticism, teasing, and peer approval. The 8-Year-Old

8 岁儿童的发育特征
Entering Grade 3, 8-year-olds display faster, more graceful movements. Peer-group conformity and fads become vital: acceptance by peers directly influences self-esteem and belonging. Eight-year-olds freely give advice to others but resist personal criticism. They assist capably with household chores (cooking, vacuuming) while demanding increased privileges over younger siblings.
步入三年级的 8 岁儿童动作更为迅捷灵敏、姿态优美。同伴群体的潮流风尚、流行用语与群体规范在其心目中占据至高无上的地位：被同伴小圈子接纳认可直接决定其自尊心与归属感的成败。8 岁孩子极爱对别人指点江山给建议，但自身却极难听进批评意见。他们已能在家庭中承担实质性的家务劳动（如吸尘打扫、简单烹饪帮厨），但同时也要求享有比年幼弟妹更多的主权和零花钱特权。

Adolescence (12 to 18 Years)

发育九大阶段之第六阶段：青春期（12 至 18 岁）
Adolescence is a profound period of rapid physical growth and emotional, psychological, and social maturation. The stage begins with puberty—the biological period during which reproductive organs begin to function and secondary sex characteristics emerge. Girls typically enter puberty between 10 and 14 years, and boys between 12 and 16 years. Developmental tasks of adolescence include:
青春期是个体经历翻天覆地的体格飞跃、性发育成熟以及剧烈心理社会重构的关键黄金期。青春期以青春发育期（Puberty）的生理启动为开端——在此期间生殖系统器官全面成熟并具备生育功能，第二性征显著外露。女孩通常在 10 至 14 岁之间率先进入青春发育，男孩则多在 12 至 16 岁紧随其后。青春期的核心发展任务包括：
  • • Accepting changes in the body and physical appearance
    坦然接纳并适应自身体型、外貌与第二性征的剧烈生理变化
  • • Developing mature peer relationships with both genders
    与同性及异性同龄伙伴建立成熟健康的平等社交关系
  • • Exploring and resolving personal sexual and gender identity
    探索、认知并最终确立个人的性别认同与性取向定位
  • • Achieving emotional independence from parents and authority figures
    实现脱离父母过度庇护的心理断乳与独立自主
  • • Developing moral values, ethical standards, and social responsibility
    全面建立健全的社会道德准则、公民责任感与人生价值观
  • • Navigating electronic communication and social media safely
    健康理性地使用互联网社交媒体并建立批判性网络防线

During the adolescent growth spurt, girls grow 5 to 23 cm (2 to 9 in.) and gain 7 to 23 kg (15 to 50 lb.), typically finishing skeletal growth around age 17 to 18 (some until 21). Adolescence is commonly termed the 'awkward stage' because bones and muscles grow at different rates, producing temporary clumsiness until coordinated neuromuscular balance catches up. Body image anxiety is prevalent: girls often fret over breast development or weight (heightening the risk for eating disorders), while boys worry about height and muscular build. Mood swings are intense and mercurial, although older adolescents (15–18 years) gradually gain emotional equilibrium.
在青春期体格突增高峰中，女孩身高可激增 5 至 23 厘米（2 至 9 英寸），体重猛增 7 至 23 公斤（15 至 50 磅），骨骼发育通常在 17 至 18 岁（部分延续至 21 岁）趋于定型。青春期常被称为“笨拙尴尬期（awkward stage）”，因为四肢长骨生长速度远快于躯干与肌腱，导致机体出现暂时的动作失调与肢体笨拙，待肌肉力量匹配后方重现协调。身材容貌焦虑普遍弥漫：女孩常为乳房发育大小或体重增加而寝食难安（极易诱发神经性厌食症或贪食症等进食障碍），男孩则常为身高矮小或肌肉单薄而自卑。情绪如过山车般剧烈波动、喜怒无常，直至青春期晚期（15 至 18 岁）情绪调控能力才逐步成熟平稳。

Sexual orientation refers to whether a person’s physical and emotional romantic arousal is in response to people of the same gender, the opposite gender, or both (heterosexual, gay, lesbian, bisexual).
性取向（Sexual orientation）是指个体在身体性吸引与浪漫情感共鸣方面，倾向于对同性、异性、抑或两者皆产生强烈吸引（异性恋、男同性恋、女同性恋、双性恋等）。
One does not have to be sexually active to have a sexual orientation.
一个人根本不需要经历过实际的性生活，其内在的性取向便已客观存在。
Teens who will eventually identify as gay, lesbian, or bisexual do not always do so during their adolescence.
日后认同自身为同性恋或双性恋的个体，在青少年时期并不一定会立即做出明确的自我宣告。

曾经与同性发生过性接触并不等同于该青少年必然是同性恋；同样，许多同性恋青少年在其整个青春期可能从未发生过任何同性性接触。
The process of declaring a non-heterosexual identity is often termed 'coming out.' It can begin with romantic fantasies, an inner realization of same-sex attraction, or a distinct feeling of being different from peers.
向外界公开宣告自身非异性恋身份的过程通常被称为“出柜（coming out）”。这一过程可以萌发于情感幻想、对同性吸引力的内在觉醒、或一种察觉自身与同龄异性恋同伴截然不同的深刻感觉。

Adolescents become increasingly able to use logic and reasoning, and they often form passionate opinions on many issues. However, their judgement and reasoning are not always sound. Guidance, discipline, and emotional and financial support from parents are still needed. Disagreements with parents are common, especially about behaviour and activity restrictions and limits. Adolescents would rather be with peers than do things with parents and other family members. Similarly, they tend to confide in and seek advice from peers or from adults other than their parents.
青少年的逻辑思维与推理推导能力日益增强，常对各类社会议题形成极为强烈的个人观点。然而，其判断力与理性思维尚未完全成熟稳健。来自父母的正确引导、纪律约束以及情感和经济支持依然不可或缺。他们与父母之间产生分歧十分普遍，尤其是针对行为举止规范以及各项活动的限制与禁令。青少年更倾向于与同龄伙伴待在一起，而非同父母及其他家庭成员共处。同样地，他们更乐于向同龄朋友或除父母以外的信任成人倾诉心事并寻求建议。
Teenage interests and activities reflect the need to become independent and to develop intimate relationships. All genders are often interested in parties, dances, and other social activities. Clothing, makeup, and hairstyles are often important to teenagers of all genders because their appearance enables them to define their identity and fit in with a group. Parents and teenagers often disagree about clothing and hairstyles. Teenagers may spend a lot of time with friends or converse with them through email, text messaging, or social media or on the phone. They also spend time participating in sports or in club activities, listening to music, or playing video games. Dating begins during adolescence, although the age of its onset varies from individual to individual.
青少年的兴趣与课外活动集中反映了建立独立性以及发展亲密人际关系的内在心理需求。不同性别的青少年通常都对派对聚会、舞会及其他社交活动抱有浓厚兴趣。服装搭配、妆容修饰与发型设计对所有性别的青少年都至关重要，因为外表不仅帮助他们确立自我身份认同，更是融入特定同伴群体的关键标识。父母与青少年经常在穿衣风格和发型审美上发生争执。青少年会花费大量时间与朋友聚在一起，或通过电子邮件、短信、社交媒体及电话密切交流。他们还会将大量时间投入到体育运动、社团活动、听音乐或玩电子游戏中。约会行为通常始于青春发育期，但各人的起始年龄存在显著个体差异。
Difficult decisions and conflicts result as the adolescent matures physically, psychologically, and emotionally. Parents and teenagers often disagree about dating because parents worry that dating may lead to sexual activities, pregnancy, and sexually transmitted infections. Teenagers usually do not understand or appreciate these concerns. Dating one person exclusively helps the teenager meet the needs for security, love and belonging, self-esteem, and, for some, gender identification or exploration (see BOX 31.1). Teenagers sometimes have difficulty controlling sexual urges and considering the consequences of sexual activity (including “cyber-dating,” in which they send pictures of themselves to other people). Unfortunately, when images such as these are sent through an electronic communication network, there is a high likelihood for those images to be misused (see Chapter 12).
随着青少年在生理、心理和情感层面逐步走向成熟，往往会面临艰难的抉择与激烈的心里冲突。父母与青少年常常在约会问题上产生严重分歧，主要原因在于父母深切担忧约会可能引发过早性行为、意外怀孕以及性传播感染（STIs）。青少年通常无法理解甚至体会不到父母的这层顾虑。与特定单一人选确立专属约会关系，有助于满足青少年对安全感、爱与归属感、自尊心的渴望，并对某些个体而言有助于进行性别认同或性取向探索（参见专栏 31.1）。青少年有时难以克制冲动的性冲动，未能深思熟虑性行为的严重后果（包括“网络约会/赛博约会”，即向他人发送自身私密裸露照片）。不幸的是，一旦此类影像通过电子通讯网络发出，极有可能遭遇恶意传播与滥用（参见第 12 章）。
Adolescents begin to think about careers and what to do after high school graduation. Interests, skills, and talents are some of the factors that influence the choice of further education or a job. Adolescents also need to develop morals, values, and attitudes for living in society. They need to develop a sense about what is good and bad, right and wrong, and important and unimportant. Parents, peers, culture, religion, television, school, and movies are some of the factors that influence teenagers. Drug abuse, unwanted pregnancy, alcoholism, suicidal ideation, and criminal acts (see Chapter 37) are common difficulties among adolescents who are troubled.
青少年开始深入思考未来的职业方向以及高中毕业后的出路规划。个人兴趣爱好、掌握的技能与天赋禀赋是影响其选择升学深造或直接就业的关键考量因素。此外，青少年还必须建立起立足社会所必需的道德伦理、价值观念与处世态度。他们需要清晰界定何为善恶、对错以及孰轻孰重。父母、同伴群体、文化背景、宗教信仰、电视传媒、学校教育以及影视网络是深刻塑造青少年的重要因素。在深陷困境与危机的青少年群体中，药物滥用、意外受孕、酗酒成瘾、自杀意念以及违法犯罪行为（参见第 37 章）属于普遍存在的严重社会心理危机。

Young Adulthood (18 to 40 Years)

青年期（18 至 40 岁）
Psychological and social development continues during young adulthood. There is little physical growth because adult height has already been reached by this time, and body systems are fully developed. Developmental tasks of young adulthood include the following:
在青年期，心理与社会功能的发展仍在持续深化推进。此时生理层面的形态增长已微乎其微，因为成年期的身体最终身高已完全定型，各系统器官的解剖与生理功能已全面发育成熟。青年期的核心发育任务包括以下各项：
  • • Choosing further education and a career
    选择高等教育/职业培训进修方向与终身职业生涯
  • • Selecting a partner
    物色并选择人生伴侣
  • • Learning to live with a partner
    学会与伴侣共同生活、磨合相处
  • • Becoming a parent and raising children
    为人父母并承担抚养教育子女的责任
  • • Developing a satisfactory sex life
    建立协调、健康且令人满意的性生活

Education and occupation are so closely related that they can rarely be separated. Most jobs require specific knowledge and skills, so the amount and kind of education needed depend on a person’s career choice. Most adults find that their job choices are greater with adequate educational preparation and that employment is necessary for economic independence and for supporting a family.
教育背景与职业选择息息相关、密不可分。绝大多数工作岗位均需要特定的专业知识与实操技能，因此所需受教育的年限与类型直接取决于个人的职业抉择。绝大多数成年人深切体会到：拥有充分完备的教育储备，就业选择面更为宽广；而稳定的工作就业则是实现经济完全独立以及供养维系家庭的基本前提。
At this stage of development, young adults may choose to marry their partner, remain single, or live in a common-law relationship. Some of the reasons for marriage or common-law relationship are being in love, wanting emotional security, wanting a family, having a sexual relationship, wanting to leave an unhappy home life, looking for a certain social status, wanting companionship, and sharing financial security. Some marry to feel wanted, needed, and desirable. Factors such as age, religion, interests, education, race, personality, and love affect the selection of a spouse. Some marriages are happy and successful, whereas some others are not. There are no guarantees that a relationship will work, so the couple must work together to build a marriage or partnership based on trust, respect, caring, and friendship.
在此发育阶段，青年人可以选择与伴侣登记结婚、保持单身，或选择同居伴侣关系（common-law relationship）。步入婚姻或事实同居关系的常见动因包括：坠入爱河、渴望获得情感安全感、渴望组建家庭、建立合法稳定的性关系、希望脱离原生家庭的不幸生活环境、谋求特定社会地位、寻求长久陪伴以及分担经济开销实现财务安全。某些人结婚则是为了感受被需要、被渴望与自身价值。年龄、宗教信仰、共同兴趣、受教育程度、种族背景、个性特征以及彼此爱慕等诸多因素均深刻影响配偶的挑选。有些婚姻幸福美满且地久天长，有些则以破裂告终。没有任何法则能保证一段亲密关系必然成功，因此伴侣双方必须齐心协力，共同构建并维系建立在信任、尊重、关怀与深厚友谊基础之上的婚姻或伴侣关系。
Partners (married or unmarried) must learn to live with each other, which may require habits, routines, household management, meal preparation, and leisure activities to be changed or adjusted to “fit” each other’s needs. They must learn to solve problems, make decisions together, and work toward the same goals. Open and honest communication is essential to a successful partnership (FIGURE 31.11).
无论是否登记结婚，伴侣双方必须学会共同朝夕相处，这往往要求生活习惯、作息规律、家务管理、三餐烹饪以及闲暇休闲活动进行全方位调整改变，以磨合适应彼此的需求。他们必须学会化解矛盾冲突、共同协商做出决断，并朝着共同的目标携手奋斗。开诚布公、坦诚相见的有效沟通是维系成功伴侣关系的绝对基石（见图 31.11）。

Young adults also need to develop a satisfactory sex life. Frequency of sex, desires, practices, and preferences vary among couples. Understanding and accepting a partner’s needs are necessary for a satisfying and intimate relationship.
青年人还需要建立协调融洽的性生活。在不同伴侣之间，性生活频次、性欲强度、实践方式以及具体偏好存在极大差异。相互深入理解并包容接纳伴侣的性心理与生理需求，是构筑令人满意、深度亲密关系的必要条件。
Statistically, most heterosexual couples decide to have children, and modern birth control methods allow them to plan how many children they want to have and when to have them. However, some pregnancies are unplanned. Many couples have a child during the first few years of marriage or partnership; some wait several years before starting a family. Some couples decide not to have children at all. Today, couples of any gender who wish to have children but may be unable to become pregnant or who experience difficulty having children may choose to adopt a child, have in vitro (medically assisted) fertilization (sometimes with donor sperm), or arrange to have a surrogate carry the child. The availability of surrogacy and evolving adoption laws have enabled couples to choose the option that best fits their family. In these cases, there may be costly legal costs involved. Those couples deciding to have children must agree on methods of child-rearing and discipline and must adjust to their roles in raising children and to the children’s needs for time, energy, and parental attention.
统计显示，绝大多数异性伴侣最终会选择生育子女，现代避孕节育手段使得他们能够科学规划生育子女的数量与受孕时机。然而，仍有部分妊娠属于计划外意外怀孕。许多伴侣在结婚或共同生活的前几年便迎来新生命的降临；有的则推迟数年再建立亲子家庭。亦有部分伴侣选择丁克、完全不生育子女。如今，渴望拥有子女但在生理上无法受孕或面临受孕困难的任何性别的伴侣，均可选择合法收养孤儿、进行体外受精/试管婴儿（IVF，医学辅助生殖技术，有时采用供精）、或通过合法代孕完成生育。代孕服务的可及性与收养法规的健全演进，使得家庭能够根据自身情况挑选最合适的育儿路径；在此类情形下往往涉及不菲的法律与医疗费用。凡决定抚育子女的伴侣，必须在育儿理念与家教管教方式上达成高度共识，并迅速适应抚育子女的全新角色定位，倾注大量时间、精力与亲子陪伴以满足孩子的成长发育需求。

Middle Adulthood (40 to 65 Years)

中年期（40 至 65 岁）
For some adults, middle adulthood is a stable and comfortable stage of development, a time when children have grown up and moved away and partners are able to spend time alone with each other, enjoy financial security, and have fewer worries about children. However, some adults in this stage have to deal with divorce, ailing parents, children moving back home or not moving out at all, and caring for grandchildren. The developmental tasks of middle adulthood include the following:
对部分成年人而言，中年期是一个稳定惬意的发育阶段：子女已经长大成人、成家立业搬出家门，伴侣之间得以重温二人世界、坐享财务宽裕安全，对子女的牵挂担忧也大幅减少。然而，亦有许多中年人必须应对婚姻离异、父母年迈多病、成年子女失业回迁啃老甚至从未搬离、以及协助照料第三代孙辈等繁重压力。中年期的核心发育任务包括以下各项：
  • • Adjusting to physical changes
    适应并接纳生理机能与身体形态的衰退性变化
  • • Having grown-up children
    妥善处理与成年子女的关系（应对空巢期）
  • • Developing leisure activities
    积极发展丰富多样的业余休闲爱好
  • • Relating to and perhaps eventually caring for aging parents
    维系与年迈父母的关系，并在日后承担起照护衰老父母的重任
Of the physical changes that occur in this period, several are gradual and go unnoticed, whereas some others become obvious early in this stage (see Chapter 43).
在此阶段发生的各项生理退行性变化中，有数项是渐进发展且起初往往不易察觉的，而另一些变化则在步入中年初期便显露无疑（详见第 43 章）。

People in their early 40s may feel energetic and able to function as they did in their 20s. However, energy and endurance begin to decrease. People in this age group can develop any number of diseases and illnesses, which may be chronic or life-threatening. Weight control becomes a problem as metabolism and physical activities slow down. Facial wrinkles and grey hair appear (although grey hair can also naturally occur in people in their 20s). The need for eyeglasses is common at this stage, and there may be hearing impairment or deterioration. In individuals with female genitalia, menstruation stops between the ages of 42 and 55 years (menopause). This means that the ovaries stop secreting female hormones (estrogen and progesterone), ovulation ends, and pregnancy is no longer possible. Many individuals in menopause experience hot flashes (a sudden warm sensation), night sweats, trouble sleeping, and mood changes.
刚跨入 40 岁出头的人可能仍觉得自己精力充沛、各项功能不减二十岁当年。然而，体力储备与耐力已开始出现生理性下降。处于该年龄段的人群易罹患多种疾病，其中不乏慢性进行性疾患或危及生命的重症。随着基础代谢率下降与身体活动量减少，体重管理往往成为一大挑战。面部皱纹开始显现，白发丛生（尽管部分人在 20 多岁时也可能出现少白头）。在此阶段佩戴老花镜（矫正老视）极为普遍，且可能伴随轻重不一的听力下降或退化。在具有女性外生殖解剖特征的个体中，月经会在 42 至 55 岁之间停止（绝经/围绝经期）。这意味着卵巢停止分泌女性激素（雌激素与孕激素），排卵彻底终止，不再具备受孕生理机能。许多处于更年期阶段的个体会经历潮热（突然袭来的燥热感）、夜间盗汗、睡眠障碍以及情绪起伏波动。
Middle-aged adults must adjust to their children leaving home. This is often referred to as the empty nest stage. Some adults cope well with this change, but others may experience feelings of loneliness, loss, and purposelessness. Adults also must cope with letting children go, being in-laws, and becoming grandparents. Parents must let their adult children lead their own lives while also remaining available to their children for emotional support in times of need.
中年人必须调整心态适应子女长大离巢。这一时期常被称为“空巢期（empty nest stage）”。部分成年人能泰然处之并良好适应这一转变，而另一些人则可能深受孤独感、失落感与无价值感的困扰。中年人还必须学会放手让孩子独立展翅，学会扮演岳父母/公婆的新角色，并接纳晋升为祖父母的身份转换。父母必须给予成年子女充分自主权过自己的生活，同时在子女面临困境需要时，始终作为坚实的情感后盾提供支持。
Middle-aged adults often discover that they have a lot more spare time once the demands of parenthood have decreased significantly. Hobbies and leisure activities such as gardening, fishing, painting, golfing, volunteer work, and membership in clubs and organizations can be sources of pleasure (FIGURE 31.12). These activities become even more important after retirement and during late adulthood.
一旦为人父母的繁重教养负担显著减轻，中年人常会发现自己拥有了充裕的闲暇时光。园艺劳作、垂钓、绘画、高尔夫球运动、义工志愿服务以及参加各类社团组织等兴趣爱好，能够成为丰盈精神世界、带来愉悦感的源泉（见图 31.12）。这些业余活动在退休后以及进入老年期后将发挥更为举足轻重的身心保健作用。

某些中年人的父母年事已高且体弱多病；他们必须毅然扛起照护年迈病患父母的重担，并常常不得不直面并承受双亲离世带来的丧亲之痛。

Late Adulthood (65 Years and Older)

老年期（65 岁及以上）
Certain physical changes are a normal part of aging and occur in everyone in late adulthood (see BOX 43.1 on page 1026). The rate and degree of change vary with each person. Some of these changes are a result of lifestyle choices, including diet, general health, exercise, stress, environment, and heredity. Normal aging does not mean loss of health, so quality of life does not have to change with aging.
某些生理变化是机体自然衰老的正常组成部分，会发生在老年期的每一个人身上（参见第 1026 页专栏 43.1）。衰老退化的速率与程度因人而异。部分退行性变化受到个体生活方式抉择的显著影响，包括饮食营养、全身健康状况、体育锻炼习惯、应激压力水平、居住生活环境以及遗传遗传基因等。正常的生理衰老绝不等于丧失健康；因此，晚年的生活质量并不必然随着年龄增长而恶化衰退。
The developmental tasks of this stage are as follows:
老年期的核心发育任务包括以下各项：
  • • Adjusting to decreased physical strength and loss of health
    适应并接纳体力减退与健康状况的下滑
  • • Adjusting to retirement and reduced income
    适应退休生活及退休金所带来的家庭收入缩减
  • • Coping with the death of one’s partner
    应对并承受丧偶之痛与丧亲调适
  • • Developing new friends and relationships
    结识新朋友并拓展建立新型晚年社交圈
  • • Preparing for one’s own death
    坦然面对并为自身终有一死的结局做好心理与现实准备
See Chapter 43 for a detailed discussion of the numerous physical, psychological, and social changes that occur during late adulthood, as well as the specific care that older persons may need.

CHAPTER REVIEW

本章权威复习指南

KEY POINTS

核心要点总结（一）
  • • Growth and development are ongoing from the moment of fertilization until death. They occur in a sequence, order, and pattern. Certain developmental tasks must be completed during each stage, and no stage can be skipped, as each stage lays the foundation for the next stage.
    生长与发育是从受精那一刻起直至生命终结持续不断的终身过程。它们遵循特定的先后顺序、规律和模式进行。在每一发育阶段都必须完成特定的发育任务，且任何阶段均不可逾越跳过，因为前一阶段始终是后续阶段不可或缺的基石。
  • • Psychosocial health is well-being in the social, emotional, intellectual, and spiritual dimensions of one’s life.
    心理社会健康是指个体在人际社交、情绪情感、智力认知以及精神信仰四个维度上的全面良好健全状态。
  • • Factors that influence psychosocial health include personality, family background, environment, and life’s circumstances.
    深刻影响个体心理社会健康的因素包括：个性人格特质、家庭成长背景、所处生活环境以及人生际遇境况。
  • • Two common theories that address a client’s psychosocial health are Erikson’s theory of psychosocial development and Maslow’s hierarchy of needs.
    临床阐释案主心理社会健康的两个最核心经典理论是：埃里克森（Erikson）的心理社会发育阶段理论，以及马斯洛（Maslow）的人类需要层次理论。
  • • Infant stage (birth to 1 year): Rapid physical, psychological, and social growth and development occur. Birth weight usually doubles by the age of 5 to 6 months and triples by the first birthday. Newborns have certain reflexes (Moro, rooting, sucking, and grasping) that are present at birth and disappear as the central nervous system develops. Developmental tasks for infancy are learning to walk, talk, and communicate with others.
    婴儿期（出生至 1 岁）：机体经历极其迅速的生理、心理与社会性生长发育。出生体重通常在 5 至 6 个月大时翻倍，满周岁时增加至出生时的三倍。新生儿具备出生时即存在的特征性原始反射（莫罗反射、觅食反射、吸吮反射和抓握反射），随着中枢神经系统髓鞘化成熟而逐步消失。婴儿期的核心发育任务包括：学会直立行走、开口说话并与他人建立交流。
  • • Toddlers (1 to 3 years): Physical growth during the second year of life is not as rapid as during infancy. Developmental tasks for toddlers are tolerating separation from the primary caregiver, gaining control of bowel and bladder functions, using words to communicate with others, and becoming less dependent on the primary caregiver.
    幼儿期（1 至 3 岁）：生命第二年的体格生长速度较婴儿期有所放缓。幼儿期的核心发育任务包括：耐受与主要照料者的分离、建立对肠道大便与膀胱排尿功能的自主控制、运用词汇语句与他人沟通交流、以及减轻对主要照料者的绝对依赖。
  • • Preschool (3 to 5 years): Children grow taller but gain little weight. They are more coordinated than toddlers. Developmental tasks for preschool children are improving the ability to communicate with and understand others, performing self-care activities, learning about the differences between the genders and identifying with a gender, distinguishing right from wrong, learning to play with others, and developing family relationships.
    学龄前期（3 至 5 岁）：儿童身躯迅速抽长但体重增长相对缓慢。其肢体运动协调性显著超越幼儿。学龄前儿童的发育任务包括：提高理解他人与表达自我的沟通能力、掌握基本个人自理技能、认识性别差异并确立自身性别认同、辨别是非对错、学会与同伴合作互动游戏、以及深化家庭亲情关系。
  • • Middle childhood (6 to 8 years): Children usually grow 5 cm each year during this stage; weight gain will vary, depending on such factors as level of activity. Developmental tasks include acquiring the social and physical skills needed for physical games; developing a conscience and morals; having a good feeling and attitude about self; developing the ability to get along with other children; learning behaviours and attitudes considered normative to one’s gender; and acquiring basic reading, writing, and arithmetic skills.
    童年中期（6 至 8 岁）：在此阶段儿童每年通常增高约 5 厘米；体重增长因活动水平等因素而异。核心发育任务包括：掌握开展体育游戏所需的社交与动作技能；形成良知与道德感；对自我建立积极良好的评价与态度；培养与同龄伙伴友好相处的能力；习得符合自身性别的规范化行为与态度；以及牢固掌握基础读、写、算三门学业技能。
  • • Late childhood (9 to 12 years): Boys grow about 2.5 cm and gain 1.8 kg of weight per year. Girls grow about 5 cm and gain 2.3 kg of weight per year. Body changes begin to occur. Developmental tasks are developing moral and ethical behaviour; developing and keeping friendships; becoming independent of adults and learning to depend on oneself; understanding the physical, psychological, and social roles of one’s identified gender; learning how to study; and developing greater muscular strength, coordination, and balance.
    童年后期（9 至 12 岁）：男童每年平均长高约 2.5 厘米、增重约 1.8 公斤；女童每年平均长高约 5 厘米、增重约 2.3 公斤。第二性征与身体形态开始萌芽变化。发育任务包括：形成符合道德伦理的行为习惯；建立并长久维护真挚友谊；摆脱对成人的依附学会自立自强；理解自身认同性别的生理、心理与社会角色；掌握自主学习方法；以及提升骨骼肌肉力量、肢体协调性与平衡力。
  • • Adolescence (12 to 18 years): Rapid growth and psychological and social maturing occur. Boys grow about 10 to 41 cm and gain 7 to 27 kg in weight. Girls grow about 5 to 23 cm and gain 7 to 23 kg in weight. Developmental tasks are accepting changes in one’s body and appearance; developing relationships with others of the same age; accepting the role considered normative for one’s age and identified gender; becoming independent from parents and adults; and developing morals, attitudes, and values needed for functioning in society.
    青春期（12 至 18 岁）：体格经历青春期突增，心理与社会功能迅速走向成熟。男孩在整个青春期身长增高约 10 至 41 厘米、体重增加 7 至 27 公斤；女孩身长增高约 5 至 23 厘米、体重增加 7 至 23 公斤。核心发育任务包括：接纳自身身体形态与外貌发生的剧烈变化；与同龄伙伴建立深入人际关系；接纳符合自身年龄与认同性别的社会规范角色；实现与父母及成人的心理断乳与独立自主；并树立立足社会所必需的道德伦理、处世态度与价值体系。
  • • Young adulthood (18 to 40 years): Adult height has been reached, and body systems are fully developed. Developmental tasks include choosing further education and a career; selecting a partner; becoming a parent and raising children; and developing a satisfactory sex life. Individuals will make choices depending on their goals.
    青年期（18 至 40 岁）：成年终身高已定型，全身器官系统发育完全成熟。发育任务包括：确定高等教育方向与职业生涯路径；挑选人生伴侣；为人父母并承担教养抚育子女职责；建立协调满意的性生活。个体将依据各自的人生理想与抱负做出具体选择。
  • • Middle adulthood (40 to 65 years): Of the physical changes that occur, some are gradual and go unnoticed at first, whereas others may be more obvious—for example, grey hair, wrinkles, and a decrease in energy. Developmental tasks include adjusting to physical changes, having grown-up children, developing leisure activities, and relating to aging parents.
    中年期（40 至 65 岁）：所发生的各项生理机能变化中，有些是渐进式起初不易察觉的，而另一些则较为醒目——如白发、皮肤皱纹以及体能耐力明显减退。发育任务包括：适应生理退行性变化、妥善应对子女成年离巢的空巢期、培养丰富的业余休闲爱好、以及维系并照料年迈多病的双亲。

CRITICAL THINKING IN PRACTICE
临床实操批判性思维剖析
Scenario: You are caring for a 2-year-old boy. You take the child to another room to weigh him. The child’s mother stays in his room with her infant. You ask the child to stand on the scale. He says: “No.” You reach for him to place him on the scale. He cries and runs from you. Is this response normal? What could have been done to avoid this?

Clinical Analysis:
1. Is this response normal? YES. For a 2-year-old toddler, negativism ('No!') is a hallmark characteristic of the 'terrible twos' and reflects healthy, normal psychosocial development as the child strives for autonomy (Erikson's stage of Autonomy vs. Shame and Doubt). In addition, 2-year-olds experience separation anxiety when removed from their primary caregiver into an unfamiliar environment with strange equipment.
2. Prevention & Best Clinical Practice:
- Keep the child with the primary caregiver: Weighing should occur in the mother's presence or the mother should accompany the toddler to the scale room. A frightened toddler should never be forcibly separated from a mother for routine measurement.
- Give choices rather than yes/no commands: Never ask 'Do you want to step on the scale?' (which invites an inevitable 'No'). Instead, say: 'Would you like to step on the big numbers with your shoes off, or should mommy hold your hands?'
- Use playful desensitization: Weigh a stuffed toy first, or let the mother hold the toddler while stepping on the scale and then subtracting the mother's weight.
临床情境：你正在照护一名 2 岁男童。你将孩子带到另一个房间测量体重。孩子的母亲带着婴儿留在病房中。你要求男童站上体重秤。他斩钉截铁地说：“不要！”当你伸手去抱他想强行放上秤台时，孩子大哭着转身跑开了。这种反应属于正常现象吗？本可以采取哪些照护措施来避免这一局面？

临床深度剖析：
1. 该反应是否正常？完全正常。对于 2 岁幼儿，反抗抗拒（“不要！”是“可怕的两岁”最典型标志）是心理社会发育健康的正常表现，反映了儿童在埃里克森“自主性对羞怯怀疑”发育阶段中极力争取自我掌控与自主权。此外，在陌生环境和面对冷冰冰的器械时，脱离母亲的主要看护会直接诱发强烈的“分离性焦虑”。
2. 预防措施与优选临床实践：
- 确保主要照料者在场陪伴：称重应在母亲视野内进行，或由母亲抱其一同前往测量室。绝不应为了常规体检而强行将惊恐的幼儿与母亲分离。
- 提供选择权而非发布命令：切勿询问“你想不想站上秤？”（这必然会招致“不要”的回答）；而应温和询问：“你想脱掉鞋子自己站上大数字，还是想让妈妈牵着你的手一起站上去？”
- 运用游戏脱敏技巧：可先给小玩具玩偶“称体重”，或者让母亲抱着孩子一起站上体重秤，随后减去母亲体重计算净重，最大限度消解患儿恐惧。

6. (D) 婴儿期是指从出生起至满 1 周岁（12 个月）的生命发育阶段。
7. (B) 觅食反射（触碰面颊即转向寻找乳头）与吸吮反射（触碰唇部即开始有力吸吮）是新生儿赖以摄食和生存所必需的原始中枢反射。

13. (B) 大多数女孩在 10 至 14 岁之间率先进入青春期，男孩通常比女孩晚约两年（12 至 16 岁）跨入青春期。

16. (C) 规划受教育方向并挑选确定终身职业与工作（Choosing an occupation），是青年期（18 至 40 岁）最重要的核心发展任务。
17. (D) 中年期横跨的时间界限约为 40 至 65 岁。
18. (C) 中年期最为典型的家庭特征是子女长大成人并搬离家庭独立自理，父母步入“空巢期（empty nest stage）”。
